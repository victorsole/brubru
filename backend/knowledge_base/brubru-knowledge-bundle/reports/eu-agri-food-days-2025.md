# Comprehensive Research Brief: EU Agri-Food Days 2025

## Executive Summary

I attended the third edition of the “EU Agri-Food Days 2025”, from Monday 15 to Wednesday 17 December 2025, in the European Commission’s premises in Brussels. It marked a pivotal moment for European agricultural policy, introducing the post-2027 CAP framework with **€300 billion ringfenced for income support** and unveiling the EU Agricultural Outlook 2025-2035.

The three-day event at the Charlemagne Building brought together farmers, policymakers, and digital experts under the theme "Securing Europe's Food and Agriculture." I went representing Bo, my startup focusing on linking local producers (whom we call Hosts, in Bo), and consumers (aka Visitors).

## Part 1: EU Agri-Food Days 2025 Event Content
### Major Announcements and Communications
#### 1. CAP Post-2027 Proposal (2028-2034)
Commissioner Christophe Hansen presented the post-2027 CAP vision within the 2028-2034 Multiannual Financial Framework:
- **Income Support Ringfencing:** at least **€300 billion** (80% of CAP envelope) ringfenced for income support and crisis management – protected from flexibility adjustments
- **National and Regional Partnership Fund:** **€865 billion** envelope giving Member States flexibility
- **Unity Safety Net:** **€6.3 billion** for market crisis management 2028-2034
- **Payment Cap:** **€100.000** per farm annually
- **Pensioner Phase-out:** by 2032, pensioners will be ineligible, facilitating entry for younger farmers
- **Small Farm Support:** optional annual payment of up to **€3,000** with simplified access.


#### 2. EU Agricultural Outlook Report 2025-2035 (Released on 16 December 2025)
Key findings from this landmark report:
- **Productivity Growth:** EU agriculture expected to maintain high productivity until 2035, though at slower rate due to climate change and input costs
- **Labour Productivity:** **1% annual growth** expected (compared to 1.6% from 2015-2025), primary driver of overall EU agricultural productivity
- **Agricultural Output Value:** projected to reach **€605 billion by 2035**, growing 0.8% per year
- **Self-Sufficiency:** EU projected to maintain self-sufficiency in cereals, meat, and dairy
- **Real Income:** **35% increase** in nominal factor income per agricultural worker projected over the outlook period
- **Innovation:** for the first time, includes disaggregated analysis by farm size and type using the IFM-CAP farm-level model.


#### 3. EU-FarmBook Platform Launch (also on 16 December 2025)
- Free, open-access online platform to share agricultural and forestry knowledge from EU-funded R&I projects
- Developed from Horizon Europe funding (EURAKNOS and EUREKA projects)
- Available in all EU languages
- Quote from Prof. Pieter Spanoghe (Project Coordinator): "Across Europe, billions are invested in agricultural research. Without robust mechanisms to translate findings into practice, much of this knowledge remains locked in reports, inaccessible to those who could benefit."


#### 4. EIB €3 Billion Agriculture Loan Package
Announced by Nadia Calviño (EIB President):
- **€3 billion** new loan package for agriculture and bioeconomy activities
- Prioritises young farmers and gender equality
- Collaborating with Commission on innovative agricultural insurance and risk mitigation schemes.


#### 5. EU-Mercosur Safeguard Agreement (17 December 2025)
Council and European Parliament reached provisional agreement on bilateral safeguard clauses:
- **Sensitive Products:** poultry, beef, citrus fruits, rice, sugar protected
- **Investigation Triggers:** 5% import volume increase (vs. 10% in original proposal) or 8% price decrease
- **Faster Procedures:** 3 months for general investigations (down from 6), 2 months for sensitive products
- **Reciprocity Mechanism:** investigation required if imports don't meet equivalent EU environmental, animal welfare, health, food safety, or labour standards.


*Note: The Mercosur deal itself was delayed until January 2026 amid significant farmer protests (10,000+ protesters, 150+ tractors) and opposition from France, Italy, and Poland.*
You can read Beresol’s open report on the EU-Mercosur Partnership Agreement here: https://beresol.eu/reports

### Thematic coverage
#### 1. Strong and resilient CAP and food system
- CAP post-2027 positions the Common Agricultural Policy as "a key priority for EU funding"
- New National and Regional Partnership Plans approach provides Member States more flexibility while maintaining core EU standards
- Commissioner Hansen emphasized: "Simplifying our policy is one of my main priorities. Farmers spend too much time on paperwork, which costs them valuable time and money."
- Quote from opening: "We kicked off the EU Agri-Food Days with a simple conviction: Europe's security starts with food security".


#### 2. Data and innovation for food security
- EU-FarmBook Platform launch as central knowledge-sharing infrastructure
- AgriDataSpace partnership developments discussed
- Horizon Europe investments continuing to support R&I
- Focus on translating research findings into practical farm applications.


#### 3. Productivity outlook for EU agriculture
Presented by Mauro Vigani, Socio-Economic Analyst, DG AGRI:
- **Labour productivity:** key driver at 1% annual growth
- **Land productivity:** 0.3% annual growth (down from 0.9% 2011-2021)
- **Capital productivity:** flat (investment efficiency unchanged)
- **Total factor productivity (innovation gains):** 0.3% growth
- 2025 EU agricultural labour productivity increased 9.2% compared to 2024 (Eurostat).


#### 4. Digital innovation and AI in agriculture
Day 2 theme: "Shaping together the future of digital innovation and AI in agriculture"
Key speakers and topics:
- **Daniela di Gianantonio** (FAO Head of Digital Agriculture): Keynote on digital innovation and AI
- **Dr. Carsten Stöcker** (CEO, Spherity): Making EU Business Wallet work for farmers
- **Catherine Geslain-Lanéelle** (Director, DG AGRI): AI's potential to enhance productivity and sustainability
- Discussions on precision agriculture, satellite monitoring, and data-driven decision making.


#### 5. Farmer challenges (administrative burden, digital tools, time constraints)
- Commissioner Hansen explicitly prioritised reducing paperwork
- CAP simplification package agreement reached between Parliament and Council
- Digital gap between younger and older farmers identified as pressing challenge
- Day 3 featured real-world farmer experiences:
 - **Trevor Boland** (Suckler Beef Farmer, Ireland)
 - **Clara Benito Pacheco** (Founder, "Entrelobas-Pastosalvaje")
 - **Wanda Verdonck** (Farmer using DjustConnect to reduce administrative burden)
 - **Vagelis Kyriakou** (Lead Agronomist, Anyfon Bio Producers Group, Greece).


#### 6. CAP reform and future direction (2028 and beyond)
Key elements from Commissioner Hansen's presentation:
- Separate legal proposal for CAP post-2027 confirmed
- Horizon Europe and European Competitiveness Fund will continue supporting agriculture
- National co-financing increases total public support potential
- Hansen's statement: "I don't want a revolution, but I do want a policy that is simpler, more targeted, modern and more impactful".


#### 7. Young farmers and generational renewal
Critical statistics and initiatives:
- **Only 12% of EU farmers are currently under age 40**
- **Only 3% of CAP funds** currently reach young farmers (per user notes)
- Strategy for generational renewal in agriculture being developed
- Pensioners to be phased out of payment eligibility by 2032 to facilitate new farmer entry
- Youth Policy Dialogue held alongside EU Agri-Food Days
- Vision for Agriculture and Food commits to generational renewal strategy to help family farms and young farmers access capital.


#### 8. Gender equality in agriculture
- Day 3 session: "How digital innovations are shaping the future of agri-food through the lenses of generational renewal and women's entrepreneurship"
- Speaker: **Lorena van de Kolk**, Gender Alliance for Innovation in Agriculture (GAIA)
- **GRASS Ceiling project:** EU-funded project launched to recognize rural women as drivers of agricultural change
- EIB €3 billion loan package explicitly "promotes gender equality in agriculture".


#### 9. Water Resilience Strategy
**European Water Resilience Strategy adopted on 4 June 2025**, a significant context for agriculture discussions:
- Three pillars: Restoring/protecting water cycle, developing water-smart economy, ensuring clean affordable water
- Agriculture accounts for **51% of total EU water consumption**
- **34% of EU territory** affected by water scarcity
- Target: **10% water efficiency improvement by 2030**
- **€15 billion** EIB commitment for 2025-2027 water resilience projects
- CAP identified as key instrument for promoting sustainable water practices.


#### 10. Protein Strategy
- Commissioner Hansen confirmed commitment to "comprehensive plan to address challenges that protein supply faces in the EU"
- EU produces only **77% of feed protein** used; only **29% of high-protein feedstock** is domestic
- EU heavily reliant on soybean imports from Argentina, Brazil, US
- 25 MEPs (led by Anna Strolenberg and Sigrid Friis) called for protein diversification strategy
- Focus areas: Increasing domestic production, diversifying protein sources (including microbial, insect, seaweed), greater circularity
- Commissioner Wojciechowski proposed Ukraine as potential protein supplier.


#### 11. Food affordability and security
Global context discussed at the event:
- **2.5 billion adults** globally overweight or obese (nutrition crisis)
- **2.3 billion people** experiencing moderate to severe food insecurity (2024)
- **295.3 million people** in 53 countries faced high levels of acute food insecurity in 2024
- **2.60 billion people** couldn't afford a healthy diet in 2024
- **30 million people in EU** face food insecurity (per user notes)
- EU commitment: **€18 billion** for global food security (2020-2024)
- EU supporting food systems in ~70 partner countries in 2021-2027.


#### 12. European Compass for Agri Food
- Referenced within the Vision for Agriculture and Food framework
- Part of strategic direction-setting for EU agricultural policy
- Connected to the Strategic Dialogue on the Future of EU Agriculture recommendations.


#### 13. Horizon Europe Funding
- **Cluster 6 (Food, Bioeconomy, Natural Resources, Agriculture and Environment):** €9 billion budget for 2021-2027
- **2025 Work Programme:** €7.3 billion total, including **€833 million** specifically for food, bioeconomy, agriculture and environment
- **2026-27 Work Programme:** €14 billion allocated
- **Proposal for 2028-2034:** Commission proposing to **double** the R&I framework programme to **€175 billion**
- Agriculture explicitly included in health, biotech, agriculture and bioeconomy window of European Competitiveness Fund.


#### 14. New EU Agri-Food Strategy Communication (Expected Mid-2026)
- Vision for Agriculture and Food already presented by Commissioner Hansen in early 2025
- Follows Strategic Dialogue on the Future of EU Agriculture recommendations
- Based on three pillars: **competitiveness, resilience, sustainability**
- Comprehensive communication expected to include:
 - Protein strategy
 - Action Plan for plant-based foods
 - Generational renewal strategy
 - Updated CAP framework for 2028+.


#### 15. Mercosur Agreement Concerns
Significant opposition documented at the event:
- **ECVC (European Coordination Via Campesina) statement:** "The EU-Mercosur FTA is incompatible with goals for generational renewal and fair prices for farmers"
- Key concerns: Production standards gap, animal welfare differences, pesticide regulations, deforestation links
- Deal would create world's largest free-trade area (780 million people, quarter of global GDP)
- Protests during December 18 EU Summit: Police used tear gas and water cannon against thousands of farmers in Brussels
- Copa-Cogeca, EFFAT, environmental groups issued joint statement calling the agreement a "betrayal of European farmers, workers, consumers and the environment"
- France, Italy, Poland lead opposition; Germany, Spain support.


#### 16. Ukraine’s EU membership agricultural implications
- Commissioner Wojciechowski proposed Ukraine as potential protein supplier
- Commissioner Hansen acknowledged Ukraine's potential to meet some EU protein needs
- Ukraine covers ~30% of world's maize and wheat supply (with Russia)
- EU Solidarity Lanes enabled export of over 68 million tonnes of Ukrainian agricultural goods
- Integration into CAP framework under discussion for enlargement planning
Some farmers’ representatives were very skeptical about seeing Ukraine in the EU as a Member State. We had a look at how the EU would look like with a Member State Ukraine in an open report: https://beresol.eu/reports/ukraine-in-eu 


#### 17. Interoperability funding in next CAP from 2028
Day 3 Session: "Breaking silos in agriculture: Bridging gaps through data sharing and interoperability"
Key speakers:
- **Evangelia Mourmoura** (Team Leader for Digitalisation, DG AGRI)
- **Leontina Sandu** (Head of Unit for Interoperability and Digital Government, DG Digital Services)
- **Jesper Stendal Sørensen** (Coordinator, European Partnership "Agriculture of Data", Denmark)
- **Prof. Dr. Engel Arkenau** (Deputy Director General Digital and Data Policy, German Ministry)
- **Jürgen Vangeyte** (Director, ILVO; Ambassador of Flemish data space DjustConnect).


Focus areas:
- Reducing administrative burden through data interoperability
- DjustConnect case study from Belgium
- AgriDataSpace partnership developments
- **Plans for interoperability funding in next CAP from 2028**.


#### 19. Data sharing challenges for farmers
- Only **20-30% of farmers** use digital tools (EU figures)
- **Two-thirds lack financial resources** to invest in digital solutions
- Privacy and data ownership concerns remain barriers
- Lack of standards and interoperability between systems
- Digital divide between younger and older farmers
- Day 3 specifically addressed real-world experiences and solutions.
