## ERA Policy Agenda: Knowledge Valorisation

### European Research Area (ERA) Context

The ERA Policy Agenda (Council Recommendation, November 2021) identifies 20 priority actions. Knowledge valorisation spans several actions:

| Action | Title | Relevance |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Action 7** | Upgrade EU guidance for the better valorisation of knowledge | Central: Guiding Principles |
| **Action 8** | Strengthen research infrastructures and technology infrastructures | Research-industry cooperation |
| **Action 12** | Accelerate green and digital transitions | Demand-side for valorised knowledge |
| **Action 14** | Bring science closer to citizens | Public engagement, responsible innovation |

### Commission Recommendation C(2022) 2391: Guiding Principles

Five principles for knowledge valorisation:

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **1. Collaborative knowledge creation** | Involve end-users and societal actors from the outset; co-design research agendas |
| **2. Skills and competences** | Train researchers in entrepreneurship, IP management, negotiation; establish Technology Transfer Office (TTO) professional development |
| **3. IP management** | Adopt institutional IP policies; ensure fair distribution of returns; use model clauses; distinguish background from foreground IP |
| **4. Open science and access** | Balance open access obligations with commercialisation potential; use "as open as possible, as closed as necessary" approach |
| **5. Cooperative and inclusive framework** | Share best practices across institutions; support less-experienced research organisations; foster regional innovation ecosystems |

## Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

### TRL Scale

| TRL | Description | Phase | Key Activities |
|-----|-------------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Basic principles observed | Research | Literature review, initial concept |
| 2 | Technology concept formulated | Research | Analytical studies, patent search |
| 3 | Experimental proof of concept | Research | Lab experiments, feasibility study |
| 4 | Technology validated in lab | Development | Component testing, prototype in lab |
| 5 | Technology validated in relevant environment | Development | Prototype in simulated environment |
| 6 | Technology demonstrated in relevant environment | Demonstration | Pilot system in relevant conditions |
| 7 | System prototype demonstration in operational environment | Demonstration | Full-scale prototype, field testing |
| 8 | System complete and qualified | Deployment | Certification, manufacturing readiness |
| 9 | Actual system proven in operational environment | Market | Commercial deployment |

### Valley of Death (TRL 4-6)

The transition from lab to market (TRL 4-6) is the critical gap where many promising technologies fail:

| Challenge | Description | Support Mechanism |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Funding gap** | Too mature for research funding, too risky for venture capital | EIC Transition, InvestEU, EIF |
| **Skills gap** | Researchers lack business development, marketing, regulatory skills | TTO support, EIT KIC bootcamps |
| **Market validation** | No evidence of product-market fit | Living labs, testbeds, procurement pilots |
| **IP uncertainty** | Unclear freedom to operate, competing patents | Patent landscaping, prior art searches |
| **Regulatory pathway** | Unknown or lengthy approval process | Regulatory sandboxes, pre-submission meetings |

## IP Management in Publicly Funded Research

### Horizon Europe MGA IP Provisions (Art. 16-17)

#### Background IP

- **Definition**: IP existing before the project or generated independently during the project
- **Listing**: Beneficiaries should list background IP in the consortium agreement (not mandatory in MGA but strongly recommended)
- **Access rights**: Other beneficiaries have access rights to background IP needed to implement the project (royalty-free) or to exploit their own results (fair and reasonable conditions)
- **Protection**: Background IP remains with the original owner; no automatic transfer

#### Results (Foreground IP)

- **Ownership**: Results are owned by the beneficiary that generates them
- **Joint ownership**: If jointly generated and shares cannot be distinguished, joint ownership applies (default: equal shares)
- **Protection obligation**: Beneficiaries must protect results that can be commercially exploited (within 4 months of generation; otherwise, other beneficiaries or the Commission may assume protection)
- **Exploitation obligation**: Beneficiaries must make best efforts to exploit results (directly or through licensing, transfer, or spin-off)

#### Access Rights

| Type | Purpose | Conditions |
|------|---------|------------|
| **For implementation** | Other beneficiaries need access to perform project tasks | Royalty-free |
| **For exploitation** | Other beneficiaries need access to exploit own results | Fair and reasonable conditions |
| **Affiliated entities** | Entities linked to a beneficiary | Same conditions as the beneficiary |
| **Third parties** | Entities not in the consortium | No automatic rights; negotiated separately |

### Model Clauses for IP Agreements

Best practice clauses for consortium agreements and university-industry partnerships:

| Clause | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Ownership allocation** | Who owns results generated under different scenarios (sole, joint, subcontracted) |
| **Licensing terms** | Exclusive vs non-exclusive; field of use; territory; duration; royalty rate |
| **Revenue sharing** | Distribution of licensing income among inventor, department, institution |
| **Publication rights** | Right to publish balanced with patent filing requirements (typically 60-90 day delay for patentability review) |
| **Spin-off provisions** | Equity split, IP licensing to spin-off, non-compete clauses |

## Technology Transfer Office (TTO) Benchmarking

### ASTP Data (Association of European Science and Technology Transfer Professionals)

Key metrics for TTO performance:

| Metric | European Average | Top Quartile |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Invention disclosures per EUR 100M research spend | 15-25 | > 30 |
| Patent applications per EUR 100M research spend | 5-10 | > 15 |
| Licence agreements per EUR 100M research spend | 3-7 | > 10 |
| Spin-offs per EUR 100M research spend | 1-3 | > 5 |
| Licence income per EUR 100M research spend | EUR 0.5-2M | > EUR 3M |

### TTO Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Invention disclosure management** | Assess commercial potential of research results |
| **Patent prosecution** | File and manage patent applications |
| **Technology marketing** | Promote technologies to industry (databases, events, one-on-one) |
| **Licensing negotiation** | Negotiate terms with licensees |
| **Spin-off support** | Advise on company creation, business planning, investor introduction |
| **Contract research** | Negotiate and manage industry-funded research agreements |
| **Material transfer** | Manage material transfer agreements (MTAs) for biological/chemical materials |

## European Innovation Council (EIC)

### EIC Instruments

| Instrument | TRL Range | Funding | Type |
|-----------|-----------|---------|------|
| **EIC Pathfinder** | TRL 1-4 | Up to EUR 3-4M per project | Grant (100% funding rate) |
| **EIC Transition** | TRL 4-6 | Up to EUR 2.5M per project | Grant (100% funding rate) |
| **EIC Accelerator** | TRL 5-9 | Up to EUR 2.5M grant + EUR 15M equity | Blended (grant + equity) |

### EIC Pathfinder

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Visionary interdisciplinary research for breakthrough technologies |
| **Open calls** | Bottom-up, any deep-tech topic |
| **Challenges** | Top-down, specific technology challenges defined annually |
| **Consortium** | Minimum 3 independent entities from 3 Member States/Associated Countries |
| **Duration** | Up to 4 years |
| **Evaluation** | Excellence, Impact, Quality and Efficiency of Implementation |

### EIC Transition

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Bridge lab to market: technology validation, business model development |
| **Eligibility** | Results from EIC Pathfinder, FET, ERC Proof of Concept |
| **Activities** | Demonstration, prototyping, business planning, IP strategy, regulatory pathway |
| **Duration** | Up to 3 years |

### EIC Accelerator

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Scale-up of deep-tech start-ups and SMEs |
| **Applicant** | Single SME (incl. start-ups) |
| **Grant component** | Innovation activities (up to EUR 2.5M) |
| **Equity component** | EIC Fund investment (up to EUR 15M) via managed fund |
| **Process** | Short application > Full application > Interview (jury panel) |
| **IPO/exit** | EIC Fund aims for 10-15 year horizon; co-investment with private investors encouraged |

## Standardisation as a Valorisation Tool

### EU Standardisation Strategy (COM(2022) 31)

Key elements for R&I:
- **Pre-normative research**: Research aimed at producing standards (test methods, reference materials)
- **Standards-essential patents (SEPs)**: Patents covering standards (FRAND licensing)
- **Participation in standards bodies**: CEN, CENELEC, ETSI at EU level; ISO, IEC, ITU at international level
- **Code of Practice on standardisation**: Encourages inclusion of standardisation plans in research projects

### From Research to Standard

1. Research produces validated methodology or measurement technique
2. Researcher engages with relevant CEN/CENELEC technical committee
3. New Work Item Proposal submitted
4. Drafting of European Standard (EN) or Technical Specification (TS)
5. Public consultation and vote
6. Publication and adoption as national standard in all EU/EEA countries

## University-Industry Cooperation Frameworks

### Models

| Model | Description | IP Approach |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Collaborative research** | University + company co-fund and co-execute research | Typically shared ownership of joint results |
| **Contract research** | Company funds research at university on specific topic | Company typically owns results |
| **Consulting** | Academic provides expert advice to company | No IP generated; know-how transfer |
| **Staff exchange** | Researchers spend time in company (or vice versa) | IP governed by secondment agreement |
| **Shared infrastructure** | Joint use of research facilities | IP follows user rules of the facility |
| **Knowledge Transfer Partnership** | Graduate placed in company with academic supervision | Company usually owns project results |
