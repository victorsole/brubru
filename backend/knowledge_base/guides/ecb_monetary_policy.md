# ECB Monetary Policy and Interest Rates

## QUICK FACTS
Institution: European Central Bank (ECB), Frankfurt
Primary mandate: Price stability (inflation target: 2% over the medium term)
Key rate: Deposit Facility Rate (DFR), currently 2.00% (since 11 June 2025)
Main refinancing rate (MRO): 2.15% (since 11 June 2025)
Marginal lending facility: 2.40% (since 11 June 2025)
Interest rate corridor: DFR to MRO = 15 bps; MRO to MLF = 25 bps (asymmetric)
Current cycle: Easing -- five consecutive 25 bps cuts from December 2024 to June 2025
Treaty basis: Article 127 TFEU, Protocol No. 4 (Statute of the ESCB and ECB)
President: Christine Lagarde (since November 2019)
Governing Council: 6 Executive Board members + 20 national central bank governors (euro area)
Legal framework: CELEX 12016E127 (TFEU Art. 127), CELEX 12016HP04 (Statute)

## Key ECB Instruments

- **Main Refinancing Operations (MRO)**: Weekly lending to banks against collateral
- **Longer-Term Refinancing Operations (LTRO)**: 3-month lending; targeted versions (TLTRO) incentivise bank lending to the real economy
- **Asset Purchase Programme (APP)**: Bond buying (reinvestments ended July 2023, portfolio run-off ongoing)
- **Pandemic Emergency Purchase Programme (PEPP)**: COVID-era bond buying (reinvestments ended December 2024)
- **Transmission Protection Instrument (TPI)**: Activated if needed to counter unwarranted spread widening

## Euro Reference Exchange Rates

- Published daily at ~16:00 CET based on concertation procedure at ~14:10 CET
- 32 currencies covered (USD, JPY, GBP, CHF, and 28 others)
- Russian ruble (RUB) suspended since 1 March 2022 (sanctions)
- Bulgarian lev (BGN) removed 1 January 2026 (Bulgaria joined euro area)
- For information purposes only, not for transaction settlement

## ECB Decision-Making

| Body | Composition | Role |
|------|-------------|------|
| Governing Council | 6 EB + 20 NCB governors | Monetary policy decisions, rate setting |
| Executive Board | President + VP + 4 members | Day-to-day management, implementing decisions |
| General Council | President + VP + all 27 NCB governors | Advisory, statistical, convergence reports |

## Geopolitical Context (March 2026)

The Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz crisis have pushed oil prices above $100/barrel, creating a tension between the ECB's easing cycle (supporting growth) and renewed inflationary pressures from energy costs. The ECB Governing Council meets 18 March 2026 to assess the monetary policy stance.
