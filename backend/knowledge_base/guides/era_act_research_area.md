# The European Research Area (ERA) Act — an announced but not yet tabled proposal

## QUICK FACTS

- **STATUS AS OF 26 AUGUST 2026: NOT YET PROPOSED.** The Commission has announced the ERA Act but
  has **not tabled a legislative proposal**. Anything describing the ERA Act's articles, scope or
  numbering as if adopted is wrong — there is no text yet.
  *Basis for that statement, stated so it can be re-checked rather than trusted:* (a) the
  Parliament's own resolution is titled "on the **upcoming** European Research Area (ERA) Act" and
  reads throughout as anticipatory — it *recommends* what form the Act should take, which is only
  coherent before a proposal exists; (b) a Cellar scan of the proposals sector over the last 400
  days returned **no** act with "Research Area" in its title. Note the second check is **bounded**:
  Cellar returned the query limit, so it proves nothing was found rather than that nothing exists.
  The resolution's own wording is the stronger evidence.
- **What DOES exist** is the European Parliament's anticipatory position: **European Parliament
  resolution of 10 March 2026 on the upcoming European Research Area (ERA) Act**, procedure
  **2025/2951(RSP)**, motion **B10-0156/2026**, adopted text **P10_TA(2026)0068**. Tabled by the
  Committee on Industry, Research and Energy (ITRE) under Rules 142(5) and 136(2).
- **Published in the Official Journal C series** in the week of 24 August 2026 as **OJ C/2026/4008**,
  CELEX **`52026IP0068`**, which is why it surfaces as news five months after adoption. The vote
  itself was **PV 10/03/2026 - 12.13**. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/4008/oj
- **Treaty basis of the ERA itself**: **Article 179 TFEU** — strengthening the Union's scientific
  and technological bases through a European Research Area enabling the **free circulation of
  researchers, scientific knowledge and technology**.
- **Where the Act was announced**: the Commission President's **mission letter of 1 December 2024**
  to the Commissioner responsible for Startups, Research and Innovation, and the **2026 Commission
  work programme, COM(2025)0870**.
- **Lead Commissioner**: Ekaterina Zaharieva (Startups, Research and Innovation). **Lead EP
  committee**: ITRE. **Council configuration**: Competitiveness (COMPET), research part.

## What the ERA is, and why an Act is being proposed

The European Research Area has existed as a **policy coordination framework since 2000** and as a
Treaty objective since Lisbon. It is not, today, a body of law. It runs on **voluntary
coordination**: the Pact for Research and Innovation (Council Recommendation (EU) 2021/2122,
26 November 2021) and successive **ERA Policy Agendas** — 2022-2024, and 2025-2027 adopted by
Council Recommendation of 24 June 2025 (OJ C/2025/3593).

The case for turning some of it into binding law was made by three reports the resolution cites
directly:

| Report | Date | Author |
|---|---|---|
| *On the future of European competitiveness* | September 2024 | Mario Draghi |
| *Much more than a market* | April 2024 | Enrico Letta |
| *Align, act, accelerate — Research, technology and innovation to boost European competitiveness* | October 2024 | independent expert group chaired by Manuel Heitor |

The diagnosis, in the resolution's own words (recital D): the Union still lacks an integrated ERA
that attracts and retains talent, because of **inadequate prioritisation of R&I investment, with
few Member States meeting the 3 % of GDP target**, insufficient policy coordination, continued
fragmentation, performance disparities between Member States, and excessive administrative burdens
on researchers and universities.

## The "fifth freedom"

Recital C frames the ERA Act as the instrument that can realise the **fifth freedom** — the free
movement of research, knowledge and innovation alongside the four classic single-market freedoms —
by setting common standards and ensuring consistent application of rules, and explicitly links it
to the **Single Market Roadmap 2028**.

This is the framing to use when a user asks how the ERA Act relates to single-market policy: it is
being positioned as a single-market instrument, not only as a research-funding one.

## What the Parliament actually asked for

Seventeen operative paragraphs. The ones that will shape the negotiation:

1. **Make it a Regulation** (para 3). The EP recommends the ERA Act take the form of a **regulation**,
   for direct applicability across Member States, rather than a directive or a further recommendation.
2. **A two-track approach** (para 2): voluntary cooperation under the ERA Policy Agenda 2025-2027
   *combined with* legislative measures, to give the ERA "a binding and enforceable framework".
3. **A separate proposal on academic freedom** (para 4). The EP wants the ERA Act accompanied by a
   **distinct legislative proposal with its own legal basis** protecting freedom of scientific
   research — minimum standards for researchers' rights, ethical conduct, integrity and
   institutional independence, with effective monitoring. This is a structural demand, not a
   drafting preference.
4. **Write the 3 % target into the Act** (para 5): at least 3 % of Union GDP invested in R&D by
   **2030**, complemented by national targets and national roadmaps.
5. **Private investment and capital markets** (para 6): public-private partnerships, and completing
   the capital markets union to accelerate knowledge valorisation.
6. **Researcher mobility** (para 13): simplified career paths for young and early-career
   researchers, mutual recognition of qualifications, facilitated visa procedures, and continued
   support for a European Charter for Researchers. It notes that remote and hybrid work let
   researchers work in another Member State without relocating.
7. **Measurable metrics and governance** (paras 9-11): planning, monitoring, reporting and
   evaluation tools; rationalisation of existing policy and monitoring instruments; a balance
   between research fields and between basic and applied research.
8. **Alignment with neighbouring files** (para 16): the Innovation Act, the Pact for R&I, the
   Framework Programme and the European Education Area.

Also in the text: gender equality and the gender pay gap in R&I (para 14); research security,
integrity, open and secure circulation of knowledge, and the ethical use of emerging technologies
including AI (para 15); support for Horizon Europe funding levels (para 7).

## What to be careful about

- **Do not describe the ERA Act as adopted, tabled, in force, or numbered.** It is none of those.
  The correct formulation is "announced in the 2026 Commission work programme; the Parliament set
  out its expectations in March 2026; no proposal has been published".
- **A resolution under Rule 136(2) is a resolution on an oral question**, not a legislative act and
  not an own-initiative report. It binds nobody.
- **The OJ C publication in August 2026 is not new news.** The resolution dates from 10 March 2026.
  Publication lag in the C series routinely runs to several months.
