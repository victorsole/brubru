## What the 2026 Recommendation does

The Recommendation operationalises Principle 19 of the European Pillar of Social Rights and the **Lisbon Declaration on the European Platform on Combatting Homelessness** (21 June 2021), which committed Member States to ending homelessness by 2030 through:

1. No one sleeping rough for lack of accessible, safe and appropriate emergency accommodation
2. No one living in emergency or transitional accommodation longer than required for successful move-on
3. No one discharged from institutions (prison, hospital, care) without an offer of appropriate housing
4. Evictions prevented whenever possible (and no homelessness as a consequence of unjustified eviction)
5. No one discriminated against due to homeless status

The 2026 Council Recommendation translates these political commitments into operational guidance for Member States, including:

- Common indicators for measuring homelessness (ETHOS-Light typology — European Typology of Homelessness and Housing Exclusion)
- Cohesion Policy + ESF+ + ERDF programming alignment
- Housing First model promotion (proven cost-effective; ~80%+ tenancy retention rate vs 30-50% for staircase models)
- Eviction prevention frameworks
- Affordable-housing supply measures (cross-link with Cohesion Policy + 28th Regime / EU Inc on construction-sector single market)
- Energy-poverty + housing-adequacy linkage (cross-link with `accelerateeu_fossil_energy_crisis`, EPBD Reg 2024/1275)
- Migrant + refugee + Roma + youth + women-fleeing-domestic-violence specific provisions

The instrument is **a Recommendation, not a Directive** — non-binding but normative; Member State competence under Article 153 TFEU (housing is shared / national competence).

## Pillar of Social Rights Action Plan 2.0 connection

The Action Plan 2.0 is scheduled for College adoption on **22 July 2026**. The 6 May 2026 Recommendation:

1. Sets the operational baseline for the housing-exclusion target inside Action Plan 2.0
2. Aligns with the Anti-Poverty Strategy (`eu_anti_poverty_strategy_2026`) on housing-cost-overburden as a poverty driver
3. Cross-references the Child Guarantee strengthening (`eu_child_guarantee_strengthened_2026`) on housing-for-families-with-children
4. Cross-references the disability rights enhancement (`eu_disability_rights_post2024`) on accessible housing

## Key figures (Eurostat SILC + ETHOS-Light proxies, 2024)

- 8.2% of EU households face **housing cost overburden** (>40% of disposable income on housing)
- ~1 million people experiencing homelessness on a given night (best-effort estimate; ETHOS-Light not fully harmonised across MS)
- ~895,000 people in emergency or transitional accommodation (latest FEANTSA estimate, 2024)
- Severe housing deprivation rate: 4.0% of EU population (overcrowded + lacking basic facilities)
- Highest housing-cost-overburden: Greece (28.6%), Bulgaria (15.4%), Denmark (14.2%); lowest: Cyprus, Malta, Czechia (~3-5%)
- Housing First success rate: 70-90% sustained tenancy across 5+ pilot countries (Finland is the leading evidence base — homelessness fell ~50% 2008-2024)

## Treaty basis

- **Article 153(1)(j) TFEU** — combatting social exclusion (shared competence; Council acts on EP consultation)
- **Article 175 TFEU** — economic, social and territorial cohesion
- **Article 9 TFEU** — horizontal social clause
- **Article 292 TFEU** — Council Recommendation procedure (Commission proposal, qualified majority Council vote, EP consulted)
- **Article 31 EU Charter** — fair and just working conditions (housing as related decent-living dimension)
- **Article 34(3) EU Charter** — right to social and housing assistance (Union recognises and respects)

## Member State implementation roadmap

Following adoption (Q3 2026 expected):

1. Member States designate **National Coordinators on Housing Exclusion** (typical pattern for EU social Recommendations)
2. National Plans (or update existing Action Plans on Combatting Homelessness) submitted by Q1 2027
3. Cohesion Policy + ESF+ + ERDF programming alignment by mid-term review window
4. Annual reporting via European Semester + EPSCO Council monitoring
5. ETHOS-Light data submission to Eurostat (improving comparability)

## Cohesion Policy + ESF+ delivery

Housing-related EU funding flows:

- **ERDF + Cohesion Fund**: urban regeneration, social housing renovation, EPBD Reg 2024/1275 alignment, integrated urban development
- **ESF+** (Reg (EU) 2021/1057): Article 4(1)(l) social inclusion + Article 7(5) child poverty earmark — supports services for homeless and at-risk individuals
- **REACT-EU** (legacy)
- **RRF national plans** (Reg (EU) 2021/241): housing renovation + social housing investments
- **AMIF** (Asylum, Migration and Integration Fund): migrant integration housing
- **Just Transition Fund** (where housing exclusion is region-specific)

The Recommendation is **not financed through a new envelope** — same constraint as the parent Anti-Poverty Strategy.

## Why this matters

1. **First dedicated EU housing-exclusion instrument** — prior policy in this space was Lisbon Declaration commitments + scattered Cohesion Policy actions
2. **Operational anchor** for ETHOS-Light data harmonisation across MS
3. **Cohesion Policy 2028+ leverage** — sets the case for housing-investment line in the post-2027 framework
4. **Cross-portfolio crosswalk** — DG EMPL (lead) + DG REGIO + DG ENER (energy poverty + EPBD) + DG HOME (migrant integration)
5. **Brussels-Strasbourg politics**: housing has emerged as the top citizen concern in 2024-2026 Eurobarometers; voters want EU action; Member States retain competence — the Recommendation balances both pressures
