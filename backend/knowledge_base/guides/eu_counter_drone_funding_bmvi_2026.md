# EU funding for drone and counter-drone capabilities: EUR 130 million top-up under the Border Management and Visa Instrument (October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** the European Commission is adding **EUR 130 million** to support Member States' **drone and counter-drone capabilities** under the **Border Management and Visa Instrument (BMVI)**. The top-up was announced at the **Justice and Home Affairs Council on 1 October 2026** and reported in the Commission's Daily News of 5 October 2026 (MEX/26/2073). Commissioner Andrius Kubilius highlighted it on 8 October 2026 ("Hostile drone activity is increasing across Europe").
- **How much in total (three tranches):** EUR 250 million announced in December 2025, a further **EUR 169 million** made available earlier in 2026 (for surveillance drones and related equipment for use by Frontex, the European Border and Coast Guard Agency, and Member States), and now **EUR 130 million**. Together this brings the funding for surveillance and counter-drone technology under the Home Affairs funds to **more than half a billion euro**.
- **Purpose:** help Member States reinforce situational awareness, better protect the external borders and address risks linked to unauthorised or hostile drone activity.
- **Related EU initiatives named by the Commission:** the **EU Action Plan on Drone and Counter Drone Security** launched in **February 2026** (protecting airspace and critical infrastructure; includes a **Counter-Drone Centre of Excellence**); funding from the **European Defence Fund (EDF)** for advanced drone and counter-drone technologies; and Member States' joint procurement of drone and counter-drone systems through the **Security Action for Europe (SAFE)** programme.
- **BMVI, NOT the defence budget:** this money comes from the Home Affairs funds (border management), separate from the European Defence Fund and SAFE.
- Source (Commission Daily News, 5 October 2026): https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/mex_26_2073
