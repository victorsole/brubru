# European Court of Auditors special report 24/2026: Horizon 2020 "industrial leadership" did too little for close-to-market innovation

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** **ECA special report 24/2026**, "EU funding for innovation and competitiveness: limited focus on close-to-market measures under Horizon 2020's 'industrial leadership' priority", published **15 October 2026**; notice in the **Official Journal C/2026/5434 of 16 October 2026**.
- **Scope:** whether Horizon 2020 funding under the **"industrial leadership"** priority supported innovation, technology transfer, market uptake and the scaling-up of innovative companies, particularly **SMEs**, and whether the Commission's monitoring and evaluation supported those aims.
- **Figures (the ECA's):** Horizon 2020 (2014-2020) had a total budget of **EUR 79 billion** (EUR 77 billion baseline plus Euratom), **54%** of the MFF heading "Competitiveness for growth and jobs"; the "industrial leadership" priority had **around EUR 17 billion (22% of Horizon 2020)**.
- **Findings:** EU strategic priorities were reflected in work programmes, but the link between participants' needs and call design was unclear; support went mainly to **mid-TRL** projects, with **insufficient emphasis on close-to-market actions, commercialisation and scale-up**; monitoring lacked systematic follow-up, robust participant data and methods to measure **lasting economic value**.
- **Recommendations:** strengthen technology transfer, market uptake and scaling-up; streamline monitoring; improve assessment of participants' economic performance and innovation capacity. The Commission's replies are published with the report (**not read** here).
- **Context the ECA gives:** the proposed **European Competitiveness Fund and Horizon Europe** for 2028-2034 together plan **around EUR 409 billion (EUR 362 billion in 2025 prices)**, about 21% of the proposed budget.
- **Not read:** the observations chapter, the annexes and the Commission's replies.
