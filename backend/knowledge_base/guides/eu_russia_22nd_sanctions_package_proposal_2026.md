# EU 22nd sanctions package against Russia: PROPOSED 23 November 2026, NOT adopted

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status: PROPOSED on Monday 23 November 2026** by the Commission and the High Representative, Kaja Kallas. **NOT adopted as of 25 November 2026**: the Council must adopt it unanimously (27 Member States). Nothing in it applies yet.
- **Not the same thing as the listings of 16 November 2026** under five existing regimes (guide "EU sanctions listings of 16 November 2026"): those are in force; the 22nd package is NOT.
- **Banks and payments** (President von der Leyen's statement, 23 Nov): transaction ban extended to about **60 Russian banks**, all 60 removed from SWIFT; crypto and payment networks targeted, including third-country ones that help Russia evade sanctions; a **Crypto Sanctions Task Force** launched.
- **Energy:** the energy services ban is widened so that no EU company can help Russia build, operate or repair its energy infrastructure (refineries named); for the first time it covers **electricity services**. A proposed ban on **tankers insured in Russia**.
- **Shadow fleet, two figures from two officials:** "over 70 more ships" (von der Leyen) and "over 60 shadow fleet vessels" (Kallas). The legal proposal is not public, so give both with their owner, never one as "the" number.
- **Military industry** (Kallas, 23 Nov): **"over 100 designations"** of Russian and Belarusian military-industrial companies, including circumvention; export controls tightened on **over 20 entities**; wider export bans.
- **People:** activation of the **visa ban for anyone serving or who served in Russia's armed forces since 24 February 2022** (foreseen in the 21st package). Same day the Commission adopted **revised visa guidelines** (IP/26/2521): very strict approach to tourist visas, non-biometric Russian passports no longer recognised, new supporting documents (military-service questionnaire, self-declaration, military booklet).
- **Business:** Europeans barred from sitting on governing bodies of companies in Russia.
- **Sport and culture:** Russian teams and athletes may not compete under the Russian flag at sports events in the EU; official Russian symbols banned; money from Russian sponsors to EU sports and cultural associations restricted; Russians restricted from governing bodies of EU-based sports federations.
- **Context:** the EU has adopted 21 packages since 2022. On 18 November 2026 the Commission created an **EU Sanctions Office** and appointed a new **EU Sanctions Envoy** (IP/26/2499).
- **Sources:** statement by President von der Leyen, STATEMENT/26/2563, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/statement_26_2563 ; visa guidelines, IP/26/2521, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2521 ; Kaja Kallas on Bluesky, 23 Nov.
- **What was NOT read:** the legal proposals themselves (not published). Counts beyond the officials' own figures are not known: give none.

## Where it goes next
Unanimity in the Council (Foreign Affairs Council or written procedure), then publication in the Official Journal, which is the date it applies. Hungary and Slovakia have blocked or delayed earlier packages: until the Official Journal publishes it, say "proposed".
