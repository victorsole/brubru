# EU accession negotiations, October 2026: Montenegro closes chapters 17 to 19, accession conferences of 13 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **Montenegro, 12 October 2026 (Council press release, 18:50):** at the second Intergovernmental Conference for Montenegro under the Irish presidency, the EU and Montenegro **provisionally closed three more chapters: 17 (economic and monetary policy), 18 (statistics) and 19 (social policy and employment)**. Montenegro has **opened all 33 chapters; 21 of 33 are now provisionally closed**. The previous conference, 14 July 2026, closed chapter 8 (competition policy) and chapter 29 (customs union).
- **"Provisionally" is the Council's word and it matters:** agreement on single chapters is **not final until an overall agreement on all chapters**; never say a country has "closed" chapters without "provisionally".
- **13 October 2026, Luxembourg: the three accession conferences.** Albania's release was read in full; Ukraine's was read on 15 October in its EEAS copy; the Moldova and GAC conclusions releases were NOT read (Council bot check):
  - **Albania (release 18:35, read):** second conference with Albania under the Irish presidency; **chapter 20 (enterprise and industrial policy) provisionally closed**, after chapters 25, 26 and 30 on 14 July 2026. Albania has opened all 33 screened chapters and has **provisionally closed 4** (**21 of 33 is Montenegro's figure, never Albania's**).
  - **Ukraine and Moldova:** each **opened cluster 2 (internal market, 9 chapters) and cluster 3 (competitiveness and inclusive growth, 8 chapters)**; nothing closed; Ukraine opened external relations in July 2026 (`ukraine_accession_clusters_internal_market_competitiveness_2026`).
  - European Parliament President Metsola wrote that day: "Ukraine and Moldova ... [open] two more negotiation clusters. Montenegro has closed three additional chapters, while Albania has closed another." That is her statement, not the Council's text.
- **General Affairs Council, 13 October:** the programme (Council advisory of 12 October) listed preparation of the 15-16 October European Council, the MFF 2028-2034 in public session, the Article 7 procedure concerning Hungary, omnibus simplification packages, conclusions on inclusive democratic participation and societal and community resilience, a Slavkov-3 ministerial with Western Balkan partners and an informal lunch on the pre-enlargement policy reviews. **Published result: the Council approved the conclusions on inclusive democratic participation, and societal and community resilience** (release 12:35). The other items' results were not read.
- **Partner association (Commission news, 13 October):** Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia and Ukraine, with Kosovo (status-neutral designation), are associated with the **European Centre for Democratic Resilience** (`european_democracy_shield`): cooperation, not accession progress.

## How to answer
Montenegro: 21 of 33 chapters provisionally closed, all 33 opened, last closures 12 October (17, 18, 19). 13 October: Albania chapter 20 provisionally closed (4 closed in all); Ukraine and Moldova opened the internal market, competitiveness and inclusive growth negotiations. Accession needs unanimity (Article 49 TEU); the 6 October pre-enlargement reviews fix no dates.
