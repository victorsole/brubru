# EU accession negotiations, October 2026: Montenegro closes chapters 17 to 19, accession conferences of 13 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **Montenegro, 12 October 2026 (Council press release of 12 October, 18:50):** at the **second Intergovernmental Conference for Montenegro under the Irish presidency**, the EU and Montenegro **provisionally closed three more negotiating chapters**: **chapter 17 (economic and monetary policy), chapter 18 (statistics) and chapter 19 (social policy and employment)**. Montenegro has **opened all 33 negotiating chapters** and **21 of the 33 are now provisionally closed**. The previous accession conference, on **14 July 2026** (the 28th meeting of the Accession Conference with Montenegro), had closed chapter 8 (competition policy) and chapter 29 (customs union).
- **"Provisionally" is the Council's word and it matters:** under the negotiating principles, agreements on single chapters are **not final until an overall agreement is reached on all chapters**, and the EU may return to a closed chapter "at an appropriate moment". Do not say Montenegro has "closed" 21 chapters without "provisionally".
- **Ireland holds the rotating presidency of the Council in the second half of 2026**; Thomas Byrne, Minister of State for European Affairs and Defence of Ireland, chaired the conference and called enlargement "a geopolitical necessity" and a top priority of the Irish presidency.
- **13 October 2026, Luxembourg: the General Affairs Council and THREE accession conferences.** Council media advisory of 12 October (17:51), approximate times: the General Affairs Council from about 10:00; **Accession Conference with Albania about 17:00; with Ukraine about 18:30; with the Republic of Moldova about 20:00**, each followed by a press conference. The Council's own agenda for the day: preparation of the European Council of 15-16 October, the MFF 2028-2034 (public session), the Article 7 procedure concerning Hungary, omnibus simplification packages (state of play), Council conclusions on inclusive democratic participation and societal and community resilience, a High-Level Ministerial meeting of Slavkov-3 with Western Balkan partners, and an informal lunch discussion on the **pre-enlargement policy reviews** (see `pre_enlargement_policy_reviews_2026`).
- **OUTCOMES OF THE THREE 13 OCTOBER CONFERENCES WERE NOT PUBLISHED when this guide was written (13 October 2026, about 12:30 CEST).** Do not state which clusters or chapters were opened or closed for Albania, Moldova or Ukraine on 13 October; say the Council publishes a press release after each conference and that this guide will be updated. Anything you know about those three countries from before 13 October is background, not the outcome.
- **Partner association with a new EU body (Commission news item of 13 October):** in the margins of the General Affairs Council, ministers endorsed the association of **Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia and Ukraine, together with Kosovo** (designation without prejudice to positions on status, in line with UNSCR 1244/1999 and the ICJ opinion) with the **European Centre for Democratic Resilience** (see `european_democracy_shield`). That is cooperation on projects, not accession progress.

## How to answer
For "where is Montenegro", give 21 of 33 chapters provisionally closed, all 33 opened, last closures 12 October 2026 (chapters 17, 18, 19). For Albania, Moldova and Ukraine on 13 October, say the accession conferences were scheduled and the outcomes are published by the Council afterwards; never invent a chapter count. Accession needs unanimity in the Council (Article 49 TEU); the Commission's pre-enlargement reviews of 6 October do not fix dates.
