Guide covering EU trade defence instruments: anti-dumping duties, countervailing duties, and safeguard measures. Procedures, legal basis, calculation methods, and key cases.

## Overview

EU trade defence instruments (TDIs) protect EU producers against unfair international trade practices. The EU is one of the most active users of TDIs globally, with approximately 150 active anti-dumping measures and 25 countervailing duty measures. The European Commission (DG TRADE) investigates and imposes measures following WTO-compliant procedures.

## Anti-Dumping Measures

### What is Dumping?

Dumping occurs when a foreign producer exports a product to the EU at a price **below its normal value** (typically the domestic market price in the exporting country). If this causes injury to EU industry, anti-dumping duties can be imposed.

### Legal Framework

- **Basic Regulation:** Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 (CELEX: 32016R1036)
- **New methodology (2017):** Regulation (EU) 2017/2321 amended the basic regulation to introduce a country-neutral methodology for calculating normal value when "significant distortions" exist in the exporting country (replacing the old "analogue country" method for non-market economies)
- **WTO basis:** WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement (Article VI GATT 1994)

### Investigation Procedure

| Phase | Duration | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Complaint** | - | EU industry files complaint (representing 25%+ of EU production) |
| **Initiation** | 45 days from complaint | Commission assesses sufficiency of evidence |
| **Investigation** | 9 months (provisional), 15 months max (definitive) | Dumping margin calculation, injury analysis, EU interest test |
| **Provisional measures** | 6-9 months from initiation | Provisional duties (max 6 months, extendable to 9) |
| **Definitive measures** | Within 13-14 months | Council/Commission adopts definitive duties (5-year duration) |
| **Expiry review** | Before 5-year expiry | Measures renewed if dumping/injury likely to continue |
| **Interim review** | Any time after 1 year | Measures adjusted if circumstances changed |

### Types of Anti-Dumping Duties

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Ad valorem** | Percentage of CIF (cost, insurance, freight) price at EU border. Most common form. |
| **Specific duty** | Fixed amount per unit (e.g. EUR 100 per tonne) |
| **Variable duty (MIP)** | Minimum import price: no duty if export price exceeds MIP |

### Price Undertakings

Exporters can offer to raise their export prices to an agreed minimum level instead of paying duties. The Commission must monitor compliance. Undertakings can be withdrawn if the exporter violates terms.

## Countervailing Duties (Anti-Subsidy)

### What are Countervailable Subsidies?

Government subsidies that confer a specific benefit on producers/exporters, distorting trade. Types include:
- Direct grants or tax exemptions
- Below-market loans or loan guarantees
- Provision of goods/services below market price (e.g. cheap energy, land, raw materials)
- Export subsidies

### Legal Framework

- **Basic Regulation:** Regulation (EU) 2016/1037 (CELEX: 32016R1037)
- **WTO basis:** WTO Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures (SCM Agreement)

### Investigation Procedure

Similar to anti-dumping but with additional step: must demonstrate that the subsidy is **specific** (targeted at certain enterprises, industries, or regions) and **countervailable** under WTO rules.

Duration: same timelines as anti-dumping (provisional within 9 months, definitive within 13 months).

## Safeguard Measures

### When are Safeguards Used?

Safeguard measures protect EU industry against a **sudden surge of imports** causing or threatening serious injury, regardless of whether the trade is "fair" or "unfair." These are temporary, non-discriminatory (apply to all countries), and must include a liberalisation schedule.

### Legal Framework

- **Basic Regulation:** Regulation (EU) 2015/478 (CELEX: 32015R0478)
- **WTO basis:** WTO Agreement on Safeguards (Article XIX GATT 1994)
- **Duration:** Maximum 4 years (extendable to 8 years)

### Current Safeguard: Steel

The EU's most significant active safeguard measure covers **steel imports** (26 product categories). Originally imposed in 2018 (Regulation (EU) 2019/159), extended multiple times. Tariff-rate quotas (TRQs) allow duty-free imports up to a certain volume, with 25% duty above the quota.

**Steel safeguard amendment (9 April 2026):** Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/846 (CELEX 32026R0846) amends Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/159 imposing a definitive safeguard measure on imports of certain steel products.

### Recent Anti-Dumping: Terephthalic Acid (April 2026)

**Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/801** (CELEX 32026R0801), published 9 April 2026: Provisional anti-dumping duties on imports of terephthalic acid originating in the Republic of Korea and the United States. Terephthalic acid (purified terephthalic acid, PTA) is a key input for PET plastic, polyester fibres, and packaging. This case is notable for targeting both a key US and Korean industry simultaneously.

## Key Sectors and Countries

### Most Affected Sectors

| Sector | Typical Products | Main Exporters |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Steel and metals** | Hot-rolled coils, stainless steel, aluminium, wire rod | China, Russia, Turkey, India |
| **Chemicals** | Phosphoric acid, melamine, citric acid | China, Russia |
| **Ceramics and glass** | Tiles, tableware, glass fibre | China |
| **Solar/renewable** | Solar panels, wind towers | China |
| **Electric vehicles** | Battery electric vehicles (BEVs) | China |
| **Textiles** | Certain fabrics and yarns | China, India, Pakistan |

### China: Dominant Target

China is subject to approximately 50% of all active EU anti-dumping measures. The 2017 methodology reform (Regulation 2017/2321) was specifically designed to address "significant distortions" in economies where state intervention prevents reliable domestic price data.

### Notable Recent Cases

- **Electric vehicles from China (2024-2025):** Commission investigation into subsidised BEV imports, provisional countervailing duties of 17-38% imposed
- **Steel safeguard:** Ongoing since 2018, TRQs for 26 steel product categories
- **Biodiesel from China:** Anti-dumping and countervailing duties
- **Grain-oriented electrical steel:** Investigation ongoing

## EU Interest Test

Unique to the EU: even if dumping/subsidisation and injury are proven, the Commission must verify that imposing measures is **in the overall EU interest**. This balances:
- Injury to EU producers
- Impact on importers, downstream users, and consumers
- Impact on competition in the EU market
- Broader economic effects

This test can result in measures NOT being imposed even when all technical conditions are met.
