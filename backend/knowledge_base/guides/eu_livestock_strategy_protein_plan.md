# EU Livestock Strategy and Protein Action Plan (July 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Tuesday 7 July 2026 — COLLEGE ADOPTED THE LIVESTOCK STRATEGY + PROTEIN ACTION PLAN)**: The College of Commissioners adopted a paired agri-food package: an **EU strategy on livestock** (Communication, CELEX **52026DC0576**, with accompanying Staff Working Document **52026SC0576** "The EU livestock sector") and an **Action Plan for resilience, strategic autonomy and sustainability of the EU protein system** (Communication, CELEX **52026DC0355**). Presented jointly by **Executive Vice-President Raffaele Fitto** (Cohesion and Reforms), **Commissioner Christophe Hansen** (Agriculture and Food) and **Commissioner Olivér Várhelyi** (Health and Animal Welfare). Source: EC Press Corner SPEECH "Remarks by Executive Vice-President Fitto, Commissioner Hansen and Commissioner Várhelyi on the Livestock Strategy and Protein Action Plan" (7 July 2026) + OJ Cellar metadata (52026DC0576, 52026DC0355, 52026SC0576, dated 7 July 2026).
- **Instrument type**: Two **Commission Communications** (soft-law strategy documents, not legislative proposals) — they set direction and announce follow-up actions rather than creating binding obligations. Both are non-legislative "own-initiative" Commission acts (sector 5, DC descriptor).
- **Lead DG**: DG AGRI (Agriculture and Rural Development), with DG SANTE (animal health and welfare) on the livestock file.
- **Policy families**: Common Agricultural Policy; food security and open strategic autonomy; sustainability and climate; animal health and welfare.
- **Why now**: feed-protein import dependency (the EU imports a large share of its high-protein feed, historically soybean/soymeal from the Americas), livestock-sector viability and generational renewal, methane and sustainability pressures, and the wider "resilience and strategic autonomy" framing that runs through the von der Leyen II Commission.

## What the Livestock Strategy (52026DC0576) does

The Communication sets out the Commission's approach to keeping the EU livestock sector **economically viable, sustainable and socially valued**. Typical strands for a strategy of this kind (confirmed against the Communication + its SWD "The EU livestock sector"):
- Support for the **long-term viability** of livestock farming (fair incomes, position in the food-supply chain, generational renewal).
- **Sustainability and climate** dimension (emissions including methane, nutrient management, animal welfare), balanced against competitiveness so as not to offshore production.
- **Animal health and welfare** modernisation (the SANTE angle; links to the Animal Health Law, Regulation (EU) 2016/429).
- Simplification and predictability for farmers, consistent with the Commission's wider agri-food simplification and Omnibus agenda.

## What the Protein Action Plan (52026DC0355) does

"Action Plan for resilience, strategic autonomy and sustainability of the EU protein system" — reduces the EU's dependence on **imported feed protein** and strengthens the whole protein value chain:
- Boost EU-grown **plant proteins** (legumes, pulses, oilseeds) for feed and food.
- Improve **feed autonomy** and diversify protein sources (including novel and alternative proteins where relevant).
- Support research, breeding, and market development across the protein system.
- Frame protein supply as a **strategic-autonomy / food-security** question, not only an agronomic one.

## Answer guidance for Brubru

- Both documents are **strategies / action plans (soft law), not regulations** — do not describe them as adopted legislation or attribute binding obligations. Say "the Commission adopted a strategy / action plan on 7 July 2026".
- Cite the CELEX numbers (52026DC0576 livestock, 52026DC0355 protein) and the three-Commissioner presentation.
- For "what will change for farmers" questions, be explicit that concrete effects depend on later legislative or CAP-programming follow-up, not on the Communications themselves.
- Cross-link to `common_agricultural_policy` and, for the animal-health angle, the Animal Health Law evaluation published the day before (6 July 2026, 52026DC0352).
