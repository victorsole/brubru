# EU chemical weapons sanctions regime: renewed on 12 October 2026 until 30 October 2031

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **12 October 2026** the Council adopted **Decision (CFSP) 2026/2286**, which amends **Decision (CFSP) 2018/1544** concerning restrictive measures against the **proliferation and use of chemical weapons**. It was published in the Official Journal on **13 October 2026** (ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/2286/oj) and **entered into force on the day following publication, 14 October 2026 (NOT 13 October)** (Article 2).
- **Two different end dates, do not mix them up:** (1) the **Decision itself** now applies **until 30 October 2031** (five years; it had applied until 16 October 2026); (2) the **measures against the listed persons and entities** (asset freeze and the ban on making funds or economic resources available, Articles 2 and 3, plus the travel ban for natural persons) now apply **until 30 October 2027** (one year). The Decision "shall be kept under constant review" (new Article 8(1)).
- **First of its kind:** the Council states this is the **first time ever that a sanctions regime has been renewed for such a long period** (five years).
- **Who is listed:** **31 persons and 6 entities in total.** On **3 July 2026** the Council added **six Russian individuals** involved in the development of chemical weapons, notably epibatidine (the Council's press release of that date is headed "Chemical weapons: EU sanctions six individuals involved in Navalny's poisoning and death").
- **Purpose:** to contribute to EU efforts to counter the proliferation and use of chemical weapons and to support the implementation of the **Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC)**. The legal framework dates from **15 October 2018** (Decision (CFSP) 2018/1544, OJ L 259, 16.10.2018). The Official Journal notice to listed persons of 13 October 2026 refers to Decision (CFSP) 2018/1544 and **Council Regulation (EU) 2018/1542**.
- **Not the same regime as:** the Russia territorial-integrity regime (Decision 2014/145/CFSP), the Russia hybrid-threats regime or the Russia human-rights regime: those are separate frameworks with their own renewal dates (see `eu_sanctions_implementation_framework`).

## How to answer
The chemical weapons regime did not lapse in October 2026: it was renewed on 12 October 2026, the framework to 30 October 2031 and the individual listings to 30 October 2027. Give both dates and say which is which.
