# Gas capacity allocation (CAM) network code revision: draft and call for feedback until 10 November 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **14 October 2026** the Commission (DG Energy) published a **draft implementing regulation revising Regulation (EU) 2017/459**, the network code on **capacity allocation mechanisms in gas transmission systems** (the "**CAM network code**"), and opened a **4-week call for feedback**.
- **Deadline for feedback: 10 November 2026** (Have Your Say, open to stakeholders, citizens and organisations).
- **What the code does:** sets how adjacent transmission system operators cooperate to sell cross-border capacity, for **existing and incremental capacity**.
- **Why revise:** last amended in 2017; needs updating for technical, physical and legislative changes, notably alignment with the **2024 Hydrogen and Decarbonised Gas Market Package**, and for the changes in gas supplies since 2022.
- **Next steps:** feedback is considered before the text goes to a **vote in the relevant comitology committee**; **formal adoption is expected in 2027**. It is an implementing act, not a co-decision file: Parliament and Council do not amend it.
- **What was NOT read:** the draft text itself; give no article-level changes.

## How to answer
"Is the EU changing the gas capacity allocation rules?": yes, a draft revision of the CAM network code (Regulation (EU) 2017/459) was published on 14 October 2026 with a call for feedback until 10 November 2026; adoption by implementing act after a comitology vote is expected in 2027.
