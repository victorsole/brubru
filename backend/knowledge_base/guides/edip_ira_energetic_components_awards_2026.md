# EDIP: EUR 168.2 million for eight energetic-components projects, first Industrial Reinforcement Action results (14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **14 October 2026** the Commission (DG Defence Industry and Space) allocated **EUR 168.2 million** to the **first eight projects** under the **Industrial Reinforcement Action (IRA)** pillar of the **European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP)**.
- **The call:** the first IRA call, dedicated to **energetic components (IRA-EC)**, was published in **June 2026** and drew **83 proposals** from companies in **23 Member States and Norway**.
- **What the projects do:** accelerate and expand production of **energetic components** used in **missiles, drones and ammunition**. They leverage **EUR 1.26 billion** of industrial investment.
- **Expected output from 2027 (Commission's figures):** manufacturing times cut by up to 90%; annual production of more than **20,000 tons of energetic materials**, **3,500 drone and missile propulsion systems**, **200,000 loitering munitions warheads** and **60 million ammunitions**.
- **Where:** **17 sites in 14 EU Member States and Norway**; funding also helps protect the sites against physical and cyber threats.
- **Programme figures:** EDIP has a total budget of **EUR 1.5 billion**; **more than EUR 700 million** is allocated to the IRA pillar to expand defence production in Member States, Norway and Ukraine. IRA-EC builds on **ASAP**, the Act in Support of Ammunition Production.
- **Label the numbers:** 168.2 million = EU money for these eight; 1.26 billion = industrial investment leveraged; 1.5 billion = all of EDIP.
- **The eight projects (factsheet "EDIP Industrial Reinforcement Actions 2026 call results", October 2026; EU funding in EUR):**
  - **ROCMAN** (Nammo, Diehl, Micam, Arceon, Magnaghi Aero, Fast Forward Arch & Eng; NO, DE, IE, NL, IT, BE): solid fuel rocket motors for missiles, 30,000,000.
  - **PBS-PROPEL** (Prvni Brnenska Strojirna Velka Bites; CZ): small turbojet engines for missiles and drones, 8,688,057.
  - **E-WARP** (KNDS FR, Zaklady Niewiadow, KNDS BE, Valsts Aizsardzibas, Wolfram; FR, PL, BE, LV): loitering munition warheads and explosive, 25,828,760.
  - **RAGNARASA** (UAB RSI, Odin Defence; LT, EE): loitering munition warheads and explosive, 29,500,811.
  - **IPC-BBFW** (Rheinmetall Expal; ES): 155mm base bleed and loitering munition warheads, 8,034,672.
  - **THUNDER** (Forcit Pori; FI): military-grade TNT, 30,000,000.
  - **SECURE-NC** (MSM Walsrode; DE): military-grade nitrocellulose, 29,312,757.
  - **FIKA** (Norma Precision; SE): small-calibre cartridges, 6,810,155.
- **Partner names as the factsheet prints them; name no other company. Countries are listed per project as a set, not per partner: never pair a company with a country.**

## How to answer
"What did EDIP fund in October 2026?": EUR 168.2 million for the first eight projects (largest: ROCMAN rocket motors and THUNDER TNT, EUR 30 million each) of its Industrial Reinforcement Action (call IRA-EC, energetic components for missiles, drones and ammunition), leveraging EUR 1.26 billion across 17 sites in 14 Member States and Norway, out of 83 proposals.
