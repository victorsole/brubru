# EU AgRI 2040 Conference: the nine thematic breakout sessions (24-25 September 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Nine breakout sessions** gathered views on the AgRI 2040 thematic R&I priorities: **the eight AgRI 2040 areas of action plus "climate change and biodiversity"** (which the outline calls a key overarching priority; it is NOT a ninth area of action in COM(2026) 453). They ran in three rounds: **24 September 14:00-15:45:** (1) cropping systems, (2) digitalisation, artificial intelligence and robotics, (3) climate change and biodiversity; **24 September 16:15-17:30:** (4) stronger agri-food value chain, (5) multi-functional forests, (6) rural areas; **25 September 09:00-10:45:** (7) livestock systems, (8) soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital, (9) bioeconomy in agriculture, forestry and rural areas.
- **Three elements each session discussed:** the thematic R&I needs and priorities; how to support **innovators, startups, scaleups** and market deployment; how to foster **dissemination and uptake** by farmers, foresters and rural actors. Overarching: skills, social sciences and humanities, agroecological approaches including organic farming, digitalisation, international cooperation. Each session had a DG AGRI (Unit F.2) moderator, speakers and a rapporteur reporting in plenary.
- **1 Cropping systems:** future-ready crop production with sustainable crop protection, plant breeding, biotechnology, **protein crops**; speakers included COPA-COGECA, the AdvisoryNetPest, COLIBRI and VALPRO Path projects and the Protein Project.
- **2 Digitalisation, AI and robotics:** how R&I can support data and digital technologies for farmers; speakers from AgData, EIT Food, the Common European Agriculture Data Space, the JRC, COPA-COGECA.
- **3 Climate change and biodiversity:** keeping agriculture productive and resilient while adapting, cutting pressure on resources and restoring biodiversity; Climate Smart Research, Pathways and SpongeWorks projects.
- **4 Stronger agri-food value chain:** farmers' fair share of value; two focus aspects: **new business models** and **on-farm and local food processing and commercialisation**; GI SMART, Strength2food, Strength2farm and FOX projects.
- **5 Multi-functional forests:** sustainable forest management for multiple benefits, resilience, **rewarding foresters for ecosystem services**; FOREST partnership, European Forest Institute, CEJA.
- **6 Rural areas:** competitiveness and attractiveness, living and working conditions, **generational renewal**, digitalisation; RURACTIVE and FLIARA projects.
- **7 Livestock systems:** declining livestock and farm numbers, economic instability, climate and animal health threats; supports the **EU Livestock Strategy and Protein Action Plan**; SCAR Animal Health and Welfare chair, Teagasc, SLU.
- **8 Soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital:** an integrated soil-water-nutrient capital approach, resilience, fewer external dependencies; BENCHMARKS, SOLO, NUTRI-KNOW, RAINS projects.
- **9 Bioeconomy:** links the 2025 EU Bioeconomy Strategy, the 2026 **Fertiliser Action Plan** (bio-based fertilisers) and the 2026 Protein Plan and Livestock Strategy; cascading use of biomass; BIOEAST, the CBE-JU primary producers group, MANUREFINERY.
- **Outcome:** reports were given in plenary; no written conclusions had been read on 13 October 2026.
