## What the Ukraine Support Loan is

The USL is the EU's 2026-2027 financing vehicle for Ukraine, replacing the year-to-year improvisation of earlier support with a single, conditional, multi-instrument loan facility. It is **enhanced cooperation** (Regulation (EU) 2026/467), meaning not all Member States participate, which is why the legal title carries "implementing enhanced cooperation."

Unlike the 2024-2025 **Ukraine Facility** (Regulation (EU) 2024/792, the €50bn 2024-2027 grants-and-loans instrument tied to the Ukraine Plan) and the **G7 ERA loans** (Extraordinary Revenue Acceleration, serviced by windfall profits on immobilised Russian sovereign assets), the USL is a distinct 2026 instrument focused on **budget support + defence industrial capacity**. When a user asks about "EU money for Ukraine," disambiguate which instrument they mean:

| Instrument | CELEX / ref | Scope | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ukraine Support Loan (USL)** | 32026R0467 | €90bn loan: budget support + defence | 2026-2027 |
| Ukraine Facility | 32024R0792 | €50bn grants + loans, tied to Ukraine Plan | 2024-2027 |
| G7 ERA loans | n/a (Council Decisions) | ~€45bn, serviced by Russian-asset windfall profits | from 2025 |
| Macro-Financial Assistance (MFA) | per-operation | the disbursement channel used for USL budget-support tranches | rolling |

## Conditionality

Payments are released in instalments, each conditional on Ukraine's satisfactory fulfilment of agreed reforms designed to:
- mobilise domestic revenues,
- improve the sustainability and quality of public expenditure,
- strengthen public financial management systems.

The reform conditions are set out in the Memorandum of Understanding (signed May 2026) and tracked against the Council Implementing Decision of 23 April 2026.

## Ukraine Recovery Conference 2026

The first disbursement was announced at the **Ukraine Recovery Conference 2026**, where the EU also set out wider recovery and reconstruction commitments (DG NEAR / DG ENEST: "EU steps up support for Ukraine's defence, recovery and reconstruction"). The conference is the annual political checkpoint for international reconstruction financing; the EU uses it to anchor headline disbursement announcements.

## Hedges / what NOT to assert

- The USL is a **loan**, not a grant — do not describe the €90bn as EU grant money.
- The €90bn is the **2026-2027 envelope**; only €45bn is allocated for 2026, and only €8.35bn of that is MFA scheduled for 2026. Do not imply the full €90bn is disbursed.
- The **€6 billion drone-procurement defence package** is a separate, flagged-but-future disbursement — hedge until confirmed.
- The press release does **not** detail repayment terms or an explicit frozen-Russian-assets servicing line for the USL specifically; do not assert that USL repayment is backed by immobilised Russian assets unless a primary source confirms it (that mechanism is documented for the G7 ERA loans, a different instrument).
- Member-State participation is partial (enhanced cooperation) — do not assert all 27 participate.
