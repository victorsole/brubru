# European Semester and Economic Communication

Guide for DG ECFIN communication officers and economic policy professionals covering the Semester cycle, economic forecasts, RRF implementation, and institutional communication.

## European Semester Annual Cycle

### Full Timeline

| Period | Event | Actor | Key Outputs |
|--------|-------|-------|-------------|
| **November** | Autumn Package | Commission | Annual Sustainable Growth Survey (ASGS), Alert Mechanism Report (AMR), Joint Employment Report (JER draft), Euro Area Recommendation, opinions on Draft Budgetary Plans |
| **December** | European Council | European Council | Endorses economic priorities |
| **February** | Country Reports | Commission | 27 in-depth country analyses; In-Depth Reviews for countries with macroeconomic imbalances |
| **February** | Winter Economic Forecast | DG ECFIN | Updated GDP, inflation, employment projections |
| **March** | European Council | European Council | Spring guidance on reform priorities |
| **April** | National Reform Programmes (NRPs) | Member States | National plans for structural reforms |
| **April** | Stability/Convergence Programmes | Member States | Medium-term fiscal plans |
| **May** | Spring Economic Forecast | DG ECFIN | Comprehensive economic projections (2 years) |
| **May-June** | Spring Package | Commission | Country-Specific Recommendations (CSRs) |
| **June-July** | ECOFIN/European Council | Council | Endorses CSRs |
| **September** | Autumn Economic Forecast | DG ECFIN | Updated projections |
| **October** | Draft Budgetary Plans (Euro area) | Member States | Next year's budget plans |

### Key Documents

#### Annual Sustainable Growth Survey (ASGS)

Sets economic and social priorities for the EU. Since 2022, structured around four dimensions:
1. Environmental sustainability
2. Productivity
3. Macroeconomic stability
4. Fairness

#### Alert Mechanism Report (AMR)

Screens all Member States for macroeconomic imbalances using a scoreboard of 14 headline indicators (current account, net international investment position, unit labour costs, house prices, private credit flow, unemployment, etc.). Countries flagged receive In-Depth Reviews.

#### Country-Specific Recommendations (CSRs)

Formal Council recommendations to each Member State on:
- Fiscal policy (deficit, debt, expenditure trajectory)
- Structural reforms (labour market, business environment, innovation)
- Investment priorities
- Social policies

## Economic Forecasts

### Publication Schedule

| Forecast | Month | Scope | Horizon |
|----------|-------|-------|---------|
| **Winter** | February | EU/Euro area/Member States | Current + next year |
| **Spring** | May | Comprehensive: all indicators | Current + next year |
| **Summer** | July | EU/Euro area only (limited) | Current + next year |
| **Autumn** | November | Comprehensive: all indicators | Current + 2 years |

### Key Variables

| Variable | Source | Methodology |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| GDP growth | DG ECFIN econometric models | Bottom-up (country desks) + model cross-checks |
| Inflation (HICP) | Component-based forecasting | Energy, food, core components separately |
| Unemployment | Labour market analysis | Okun's law relationship with GDP |
| Government balance | Fiscal policy analysis | Policy measures + cyclical component |
| Government debt | Debt dynamics equation | Primary balance + interest-growth differential |
| Current account | Trade and income analysis | Trade volumes, terms of trade, income flows |

### Communication Protocol

1. **Embargo**: Forecasts shared under embargo (typically 24-48 hours before publication)
2. **Press conference**: Commissioner for Economy presents key findings
3. **Country factsheets**: One-page summaries per Member State
4. **Statistical annex**: Full tables published simultaneously online
5. **Social media**: Thread on key findings (X/Twitter, LinkedIn)
6. **Press release**: Distributed via Rapid (Commission press service)

## Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF)

### Framework

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Legal basis** | Regulation (EU) 2021/241 |
| **Total envelope** | EUR 723.8 billion (EUR 338 billion grants + EUR 385.8 billion loans) |
| **Duration** | Commitments by end 2023; payments by end 2026 |
| **Conditionality** | Milestone and target achievement |

### National Recovery and Resilience Plans (NRRPs)

Each Member State submitted a plan with:
- Reforms and investments addressing CSRs
- 37% climate spending (minimum)
- 20% digital spending (minimum)
- Milestones (qualitative) and targets (quantitative) for each measure

### Payment Process

1. Member State submits **payment request** (declaring milestones/targets achieved)
2. Commission **preliminary assessment** (within 2 months)
3. **Economic and Financial Committee (EFC)** opinion
4. **Council Implementing Decision** (within 4 weeks of EFC opinion)
5. Commission **authorises payment** (pre-financing or instalment)

### RRF Scoreboard

Public dashboard tracking:
- Common indicators (renewable energy capacity, classrooms renovated, SMEs supported, etc.)
- Milestones and targets achieved per Member State
- Disbursements to date

## Digital Euro

### Legislative Proposal

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Proposal** | COM(2023) 369 (Regulation on the digital euro) |
| **Issuer** | European Central Bank |
| **Legal tender status** | Proposed as legal tender in the euro area |
| **Privacy** | Offline payments fully private; online payments with privacy similar to card payments |
| **Holdings limit** | To be set by ECB (expected EUR 3,000 initially) |
| **Compensation** | Merchants compensated through interchange-like fees |

### Communication Challenges

| Challenge | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| Public confusion with crypto | Emphasise central bank backing, stability, no speculation |
| Privacy concerns | Explain privacy-by-design and offline functionality |
| Bank disintermediation fears | Explain holdings limits and waterfall mechanism |
| Digital divide | Emphasise inclusion features and offline availability |

## DG ECFIN Communication Strategy

### Target Audiences

| Audience | Channels | Key Messages |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Financial markets** | Forecasts, press conferences, Economic Bulletin | Stability, predictability, data-driven |
| **National governments** | Country Reports, CSRs, bilateral meetings | Reform implementation, fiscal sustainability |
| **EU institutions** | Inter-service, ECOFIN Council, EP ECON Committee | Policy coherence, legal certainty |
| **General public** | Social media, factsheets, BEF | Economic recovery, jobs, prices |
| **Media** | Press releases, press conferences, backgrounders | Clear narrative, key figures, human impact |
| **Academia** | Economic papers, discussion papers, conferences | Methodology, data, peer review |

### Brussels Economic Forum (BEF)

Annual flagship conference organised by DG ECFIN:
- Typically held in June in Brussels (Charlemagne building)
- High-level speakers: Commissioners, Finance Ministers, central bankers, economists
- Themes aligned with Commission economic priorities
- Live-streamed with social media engagement
- Side events and networking reception

### Commission Visual Identity

All DG ECFIN communications must follow:
- **European Commission Visual Identity Guidelines** (corporate templates, logos, colours)
- EU emblem usage rules (Annex A of the Interinstitutional Style Guide)
- Graph and chart standards (accessible colours, source attribution)
- Language rules: publish in English first, French and German for major publications

## Stability and Growth Pact Communication

### Reformed Framework (2024) Communication Points

| Topic | Key Message |
|-------|-------------|
| **Simplification** | Single operational indicator (net expenditure benchmark) replaces multiple fiscal rules |
| **Country ownership** | National fiscal-structural plans designed by Member States, not imposed |
| **Flexibility** | 4-7 year adjustment paths depending on reform commitments |
| **Enforcement** | EDP remains operational; financial sanctions possible but last resort |
| **Debt sustainability** | Country-specific analysis replaces one-size-fits-all numerical rules |
