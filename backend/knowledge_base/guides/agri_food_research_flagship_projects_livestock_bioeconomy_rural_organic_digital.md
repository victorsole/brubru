# EU agri-food research flagships (2): livestock, bioeconomy, rural, organic, socio-economics, digital

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** the "flagship stories" and project lists of SWD(2026) 271 (8 September 2026): examples chosen by the Commission, not a ranking.
- **Livestock:** the European Partnership for Animal Health and Welfare (EUR 360 million EU over ten years, 55 projects). Disease and vaccines: DELTA-FLU, PALE-Blu, PIGSs, DEFEND, VACDIVA, SAPHIR; antimicrobial resistance: DISARM, ROADMAP, HealthyLivestock, AVANT, NeoGiANT; welfare: Hennovation, PPILOW, ClearFarm, TechCare, aWISH; emissions and feed: Re-Livestock, NUTRITIVE, Feed-a-Gene. **African swine fever:** ASFaVIP and VAX4ASF (vaccines with a DIVA strategy).
- **Bioeconomy and business models:** CBE-JU projects for primary producers (V-FOREST, MANUREFINERY, SUSTAINEXT, PACE, RUBBIO, ReLeaf, BioCordelia, LANDFEED, ZELDA, CROPSAFE, INSOIL, pHYBi, Rural BioReFarmeries, ROBOCOOP-EU). **ALLIANCE** (2022-2025, IA): digital integrity and traceability for organic and **PDO, PGI and GI** food using distributed ledgers, sensors and AI. **FOODCoST** (2022-2026, RIA): harmonised **true cost accounting** of food, eleven case studies. **UNLOCK** (2021-2025, IA): keratin-based biodegradable agricultural materials from poultry feathers (about 15 million tonnes a year, 75% uncollected).
- **Rural areas:** **SafeHabitus** (2023-2026, RIA): farm health and safety. **SERIGO** (2024-2027, RIA): social economy and inclusion. **FLIARA** (2023-2025, RIA): female-led innovation, **200 innovations** identified. Others: GRANULAR, RUSTIK, PoliRuralPlus, RURBANIVE, RURALITIES, XGain, COMMECT, FUTURAL, RURACTIVE, SMART ERA, RURALIZATION, SWIFT, GRASS ceiling, Greencoop, Nexrur, SIMRA, ESIRA, INSPIRE.
- **Agroecology and organic:** Diversify, Diverfarming, DiverIMPACTS, ReMIX, IWMPraise, GOOD, AGROSUS, CONSERWA, Oper8; agroforestry: STARGATE, AFINET, MIXED, AGROMIX, REFOREST, DigitAF, AF4EU; organic breeding and inputs: LIVESEED, ECOBREED, BRESOV, LIVESEEDING, InnObreed, Scale-it, BIO-2, Organic-Plus, RELACS.
- **Socio-economics:** **LAMASUS** (2022-2026, RIA): land use and management modelling toolbox. **VISIONARY** (2022-2026, RIA): lock-ins in the transition to sustainable food systems (organic public procurement, carbon markets, biodiversity labelling, water governance).
- **Digital:** AGRO-WELL, AGROBOOST, AgRimate, AgRibot (robotics, augmented reality); CODECS, Quantifarm; Farmtopia, Guardians, Phito (small and medium farms); ICAERUS, SPADE, CHAMELEON (drones); ScaleAgData, CrackSense, AgriDataValue (data).
- **Knowledge flows:** **modernAKIS** (2022-2029, CSA), **EU-FarmBook** (2022-2029, RIA, all 24 EU languages), **PREMIERE** (2023-2027, CSA).
