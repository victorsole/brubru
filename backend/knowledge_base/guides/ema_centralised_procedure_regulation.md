# EMA and the Centralised Procedure (Regulation (EC) No 726/2004)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Brubru deep-dive explainer (ALWAYS link this in answers):** https://brubru.beresol.eu/eucanon/2004-726_ema/
- Instrument: **Regulation (EC) No 726/2004** (CELEX **32004R0726**), OJ L 136, 30.4.2004
- What it does: establishes the **European Medicines Agency (EMA)** and the EU **centralised marketing authorisation procedure** (one application to EMA → one Commission decision valid EU/EEA-wide)
- Adopted 31 March 2004 (Strasbourg); Titles applied from 20 November 2005; legal base Article 95 + Article 152(4)(b) TEC (now Art 114 + 168 TFEU)
- Repealed Regulation (EEC) No 2309/93 and renamed the agency to the EMA
- Structure: 38 recitals, 90 articles (5 Titles), 1 Annex
- **CHMP opinion within 210 days** of a valid application (accelerated assessment 150 days)
- **Marketing authorisation valid 5 years**, renewed once on a risk-benefit re-evaluation, then unlimited
- **Sunset clause**: authorisation lapses if the product is not marketed within 3 years (or absent 3 consecutive years)
- **Data + market protection: 8 + 2 (+1) = up to 11 years** (Article 14(11)) — the "8+2+1" formula, the single most commercially significant incentive in EU medicines law
- Mandatory centralised procedure (Annex): biotech products, orphan medicines, ATMPs (added by Reg 1394/2007), and new active substances for cancer, AIDS, neurodegenerative disorders, diabetes, auto-immune and viral diseases
- EMA components (Article 56): CHMP, CVMP, COMP, HMPC at adoption; now **7 scientific committees** (added PRAC, PDCO, CAT)
- European Public Assessment Report (EPAR) published for every authorised product (Article 13)
- Adverse-reaction reporting within 15 days; EudraVigilance database (Article 57); PSURs
- Management Board: one representative per Member State + 2 Commission + 2 European Parliament + 2 patients' + 1 doctors' + 1 veterinarians' representatives
- EMA headquarters: **Amsterdam** (relocated from London after Brexit); Executive Director: Emer Cooke
- As amended by: Reg (EU) 1235/2010 (pharmacovigilance, created PRAC), Reg (EU) 1027/2012, Reg (EU) 2019/5, **Reg (EU) 2022/123** (reinforced EMA mandate — shortages, MSSG, Emergency Task Force). Veterinary medicines carved out into Reg (EU) 2019/6 (from 28 Jan 2022). Fees now under Reg (EU) 2024/568.
- Responsible DG: DG SANTE. EP lead committee: SANT.

## Context
Regulation 726/2004 is the institutional spine of EU medicines law: it created the EMA and the centralised route, and it is the authorisation base into which the orphan (141/2000), paediatric (1901/2006) and advanced-therapy (1394/2007) regulations plug. The 2023 pharmaceutical reform package (provisional agreement 11 December 2025, regulation rapporteur Tiemo Wölken) proposes to replace it. See `eu_pharmaceutical_framework.md` and `eu_pharmaceutical_legislation_reform.md`.
