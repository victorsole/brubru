# EU borrowing: the 9th syndicated transaction of 2026 (13 October 2026) and the H2 2026 funding plan

## QUICK FACTS
- **Transaction:** on **13 October 2026** the Commission raised **EUR 10 billion of EU-Bonds** in its **9th syndicated transaction of 2026** (press release IP/26/2142): a **EUR 5 billion tap of the 7-year EU-Bond maturing 12 October 2033** and a **EUR 5 billion new 15-year EU-Bond maturing 12 December 2041**. Both were priced against reference points on the EU-Bond curve, which the Commission says reflects the growing liquidity of the curve.
- **7-year tap:** coupon **3.125%**, re-offer yield **3.775%** (price 96.076%); spread to the EU-Bond due 13 December 2032 of 9 basis points; **order book above EUR 73 billion**, oversubscribed about **14.6 times**.
- **15-year new bond:** coupon **4.25%**, re-offer yield **4.353%** (price 98.886%); spread to the EU-Bond due 12 December 2040 of 7 basis points; **order book above EUR 87 billion**, about **17.4 times** covered.
- **Funding plan:** the transaction is part of the Commission's **EUR 80 billion funding target for the second half of 2026**.
- **Debt outstanding:** about **EUR 856.65 billion**, of which **EUR 47.98 billion in EU-Bills** and **EUR 86.24 billion in NextGenerationEU Green Bonds**.
- **Use of funds:** the Commission borrows on behalf of the EU to finance policy priorities including a more competitive and resilient Europe, **support to Ukraine**, **defence investment** (including the **SAFE** instrument) and the NextGenerationEU recovery programmes.
- **Joint lead managers:** Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, LBBW, Morgan Stanley and Santander.
- **Different measure:** the European Court of Auditors counted EU debt at **EUR 738.9 billion at end-2025** in its own annual report (see `eca_annual_report_2025_eu_budget_and_rrf`); the Commission's figure here is for October 2026; the two are for different dates and may use different definitions. Do not mix them.

## How to answer
"How much did the EU raise on 13 October 2026?": EUR 10 billion in two EUR 5 billion tranches (a tap of the 2033 bond and a new 2041 bond), heavily oversubscribed; part of the EUR 80 billion H2 2026 target.
