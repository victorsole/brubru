# AgRI 2040 open consultation (2025-2026): 701 replies, who answered, headline results

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it was:** the Commission's **open consultation** (online survey) to prepare the new EU strategic approach to R&I for **agriculture, forestry and rural areas**, adopted as COM(2026) 453 (AgRI 2040 and Food 2040). **Opened 15 December 2025** at the **EU Agri-Food Days 2025** (session "The future of EU agri-food: data and innovation for food security"); **closed 25 January 2026**.
- **Response:** **701 replies** after data cleaning. Published by **DG AGRI Unit F.2 Research and Innovation** in April 2026: *Future-proofing EU Agri-Food through research and innovation: Summary of results of the open consultation* (ISBN 978-92-68-38223-3, doi 10.2762/2404939). It is a summary of contributions and "cannot in any circumstances be regarded as an official position of the Commission".
- **Headline numbers:** up to **94%** back a broader, more forward-looking scope; only **31%** think support for a **seamless investment journey** from research to market is adequate and up to **82%** back the proposed actions; only up to **36%** think dissemination and uptake of R&I results currently work, up to **88%** back the proposed solutions; up to **88%** back four forms of cooperation; nearly all confirm the **13 priority areas** of the 2023 AgriResearch Conference. Details by area are in `agri_2040_open_consultation_findings_by_area`.
- **Who answered:** academic and research institutions **269 (38.4%)**; citizens **104 (14.8%)**; companies **63** and business associations **17** (together 11.4%); farmer, forester and rural organisations **57** and farmer, forester and rural actors **37** (together 13.4%); public authorities **55 (7.8%)**; NGOs **54 (7.7%)**; one consumer association; **44** other (6.3%).
- **Organisation size (589 organisations):** large (250+ staff) **263 (44.7%)**, mainly academia and public authorities; micro (1-9) **154 (26.1%)**, mainly farmers, foresters and rural actors; medium **92 (15.6%)**; small **80 (13.6%)**.
- **Where from:** EU-27 **643 replies (92%)**: Germany **14%**, Poland **11%**, Belgium **10%** (including Brussels-based EU and global bodies); France, Portugal, Spain and Italy together **31%**. **17** replies from Horizon Europe associated countries (the report says "including 11 EU Candidate Countries") and **13** from non-European, non-associated countries (Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, Niger, Ethiopia, Malawi, Central African Republic, Brazil, Cuba, USA, Pakistan, India).
- **Not to be confused with** the **Food 2040 survey** (631 responses, March 2026) for food systems.
- **Survey structure:** five areas: R&I as enabler of the Vision; the R&I ecosystem for startups, scaleups and SMEs; uptake of knowledge and innovation; cooperation in Europe and beyond; thematic priorities.
