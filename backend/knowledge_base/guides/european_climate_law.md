## Original Climate Law (2021)

Regulation (EU) 2021/1119, adopted 30 June 2021, established:
- Legally binding EU climate neutrality objective by 2050
- 2030 target of at least 55% net GHG reduction vs 1990 (raised from 40%)
- Requirement for the Commission to propose an intermediate 2040 target
- European Scientific Advisory Board on Climate Change (ESABCC) established
- Carbon budget trajectory: linear pathway from 2030 to 2050
- Obligation for EU institutions and Member States to take necessary measures

## 2040 Target Amendment (2025-2026)

### Commission Proposal (2 July 2025)
- Net 90% emissions reduction target for 2040 vs 1990, as recommended by ESABCC
- Flexibility across sectors
- International carbon credits allowed up to 3% of 1990 net emissions

### Council General Approach (5 November 2025)
- Increased international carbon credits cap from 3% to 5% of 1990 net emissions
- Added provisions on post-2030 climate framework
- Introduced biennial assessments with possibility of revising the Climate Law
- Postponed ETS2 (buildings, road transport, additional sectors) entry into force from 2027 to 2028

### European Parliament Position
- ENVI committee voted against rapporteur's draft report (which called for rejection of Commission proposal)
- ENVI adopted text aligned with Council approach with stronger safeguards on carbon credit environmental integrity (10 November 2025)
- Provisional agreement at first trilogue: 9 December 2025
- Coreper approval: 19 December 2025
- ENVI final approval: 19 January 2026 (50 for, 26 against, 1 abstention)
- EP plenary vote: February 2026 (413 for, 226 against, 12 abstentions)

### Key Provisions of the Amendment
- **Binding 2040 target**: 90% net GHG emissions reduction compared to 1990
- **International carbon credits**: Up to 5 percentage points of reductions can come from high-quality international credits (from 2036). Safeguards against projects conflicting with EU interests.
- **Domestic flexibility**: Permanent carbon removals allowed to offset hard-to-abate emissions. Enhanced flexibility within and across sectors.
- **ETS2 postponement**: Delayed from 2027 to 2028 for buildings and road transport fuel combustion
- **Biennial assessments**: Commission reviews considering scientific data, technological developments, industrial competitiveness, energy prices, and net removal status. May propose amendments to the 2040 target or strengthen supporting frameworks.

### Publication and Entry into Force
- Regulation (EU) 2026/667 published in the Official Journal on 18 March 2026
- Enters into force 20 days after publication (7 April 2026)

## ESABCC (European Scientific Advisory Board on Climate Change)
- Established by the European Climate Law (Article 10a)
- 15 independent scientific experts
- June 2025 updated recommendation: maintained 90-95% target as realistic and cost-efficient
- Emphasised that international carbon credits were "neither needed nor recommended" and would risk diverting investments outside the EU
