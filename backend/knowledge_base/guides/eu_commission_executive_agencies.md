# EU Commission Executive Agencies — Who Runs Which Funding Programme

## QUICK FACTS
- **What this is**: the six Commission **executive agencies** and exactly which EU funding programmes each one runs. They are **implementing bodies** — they manage calls, grants and payments **on behalf of policy DGs**; they do **not** make policy.
- **The six**: **CINEA** (climate/infrastructure/environment), **HADEA** (health/digital), **EISMEA** (innovation/SMEs), **ERCEA** (European Research Council), **REA** (research), **EACEA** (education/culture).
- **Why this matters for Brubru**: this is the funding universe behind **Tenderator**. Knowing the agency tells you where the call/grant lives. Horizon Europe's clusters are **split across three agencies** — see the table.
- **Not the same as a DG**: "who sets climate policy" = DG CLIMA; "who pays the LIFE grant" = CINEA. Keep them distinct.
- **Source**: each agency's `programmes_en` / `index_en` page (read 31 May 2026).

## The six agencies in detail

### CINEA — Climate, Infrastructure & Environment Executive Agency
Manages: **Connecting Europe Facility** (energy + transport), **LIFE** (environment & climate), **Horizon Europe Cluster 5** (Climate, Energy, Mobility), the **Innovation Fund**, **EMFAF** (European Maritime, Fisheries & Aquaculture Fund), the **Just Transition Mechanism**.
Serves: DG ENER, DG MOVE, DG CLIMA, DG ENV, DG MARE.

### HADEA — Health & Digital Executive Agency
Manages: **EU4Health** (the EU's largest health programme), the **Single Market Programme — Food** strand, **Horizon Europe Cluster 1 (Health)** and **Cluster 4 (Digital, Industry, Space)**, **CEF Digital**, the **Digital Europe Programme**.
Serves: DG SANTE, DG CNECT, DG GROW, DG RTD.

### EISMEA — European Innovation Council & SMEs Executive Agency
Manages: the **European Innovation Council (EIC)** (breakthrough/scale-up innovation), **European Innovation Ecosystems**, the **Single Market Programme** (enterprise competitiveness, internal-market governance, standardisation, consumers), the **Interregional Innovation Investments (I3)** instrument (part of the ERDF).
Serves: DG GROW, DG RTD, DG REGIO.

### ERCEA — European Research Council Executive Agency
Manages: the **European Research Council (ERC)** — bottom-up, investigator-driven frontier research grants (Starting, Consolidator, Advanced, Synergy, Proof of Concept), under Horizon Europe Pillar I.
Serves: DG RTD.

### REA — Research Executive Agency
Manages: **Horizon Europe Clusters 2, 3 and 6**; the **Widening participation & spreading excellence** part; **reforming/enhancing the European R&I system**; **Research Infrastructures**; **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA)**; the **Research Fund for Coal & Steel**; **Promotion of Agricultural Products**; the EU Mission **"A Soil Deal for Europe"**. REA is the Commission's largest grant-managing agency by volume.
Serves: DG RTD, DG AGRI (agri-promotion).

### EACEA — European Education & Culture Executive Agency
Manages: **Erasmus+**, **Creative Europe**, **CERV** (Citizens, Equality, Rights & Values), the **European Solidarity Corps**, the **Intra-Africa Academic Mobility** scheme, and Pilot Projects/Preparatory Actions in these fields.
Serves: DG EAC, DG JUST (CERV), DG COMM.

## Caveats (truthfulness)
- Agency pages list the programmes they manage but typically **do not name the parent DG**. The DG attributions above follow the **policy ownership of each programme** (e.g. EU4Health belongs to DG SANTE), which is standard and stable — but if a user needs the exact delegation act, point them to the agency's establishing decision rather than asserting a single owner.
- Programme allocations between agencies can be re-shuffled between Multiannual Financial Framework periods; the split above reflects the **2021–2027** period as published.
