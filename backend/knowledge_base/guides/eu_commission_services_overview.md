# EU Commission — Functional DGs, Service Departments & Offices

## QUICK FACTS
- **What this is**: the Commission bodies **beyond the 25 policy DGs** — the **functional/horizontal Directorates-General**, the **4 genuine service departments**, and the **offices/interinstitutional bodies**. Verified body-by-body (31 May 2026).
- **Correction to a common error**: many of these are **Directorates-General**, NOT "services" — Translation (DGT), Interpretation (SCIC), Human Resources (HR), Digital Services (DIGIT), Communication (COMM), Eurostat (ESTAT), the Joint Research Centre (JRC), Civil Protection & Humanitarian Aid (ECHO), the Anti-Fraud Office (OLAF) and the Health Emergency Authority (HERA) are all **DGs**.
- **The only genuine service departments** are **DPO, EC Library, FPI and the Historical Archives Service**.
- **Why Brubru cares**: users ask "who publishes the Official Journal?" (Publications Office), "who investigates EU fraud?" (OLAF — a DG), "who handles health emergencies?" (HERA — a DG). Getting the type right keeps answers credible.
- **Source**: each body's `commission.europa.eu/about/departments-and-executive-agencies/{slug}_en` page. Full map: `european_commission_who_does_what.md`.

## Functional / horizontal Directorates-General
These are full **Directorates-General** (or DG-level departments the Commission lists among its DGs):

| DG (code) | What it does |
|---|---|
| **COMM** — Communication | Explains EU policies to the public; runs the **Spokesperson's Service** and the **Representations** in Member States |
| **DIGIT** — Digital Services | The Commission's **own IT**: corporate systems, **Interoperable Europe**, services for institutions and national administrations. (Not digital *policy* — that is DG CNECT.) |
| **DGT** — Translation | High-quality translation of Commission texts in the **24 official languages**, plus editing and AI-based language services |
| **SCIC** — Interpretation | The Commission's **interpreting service** and conference organiser |
| **HR** — Human Resources & Security | Recruitment, careers, social policy, working conditions and **security** for Commission staff |
| **ESTAT** — Eurostat | The EU's **statistical office**: high-quality, comparable European statistics (see `eurostat_statistics_production.md`) |
| **JRC** — Joint Research Centre | The Commission's in-house **science service**: independent, evidence-based knowledge for policy |
| **ECHO** — Civil Protection & Humanitarian Aid Operations | **Humanitarian aid** and **civil protection**; coordinated EU crisis response worldwide |
| **OLAF** — European Anti-Fraud Office | **Investigates fraud** against the EU budget, corruption and serious misconduct; develops anti-fraud policy; the annual PIF report; works with the **EPPO** |
| **HERA** — Health Emergency Preparedness & Response Authority | **Health emergencies**: medical countermeasures, the **Critical Medicines Alliance**, preparedness (with DG SANTE and the EMA) |
| **SG** — Secretariat-General | The Commission's **central coordinating department**: the College, the Work Programme, Better Regulation, interinstitutional relations |
| **SJ** — Legal Service | The Commission's lawyer (an **internal department**): legal advice; represents the Commission before the **Court of Justice** |
| **IAS** — Internal Audit Service | An **independent central service**: internal audit of the Commission |
| **IDEA** — Inspire, Debate, Engage & Accelerate Action | An **advisory service**: ideas and interdisciplinary research on core Commission priorities |
| **SG REFORM** — Reform & Investment Task Force | Manages the **Recovery & Resilience Facility** and implements the **Technical Support Instrument** for Member State reforms |

## The four service departments
| Service (code) | What it does |
|---|---|
| **DPO** — Data Protection Officer | Independently ensures the Commission applies **data-protection law**; keeps the public register of processing operations |
| **EC Library** | Connects Commission staff with **data, information and knowledge**; curates a historical collection on EU integration |
| **FPI** — Service for Foreign Policy Instruments | The financial & operational arm of EU foreign policy: the **European Peace Facility**, **election observation**, the **Kimberley Process**, anti-torture measures (works with the **EEAS**) |
| **HAS** — Historical Archives Service | Manages the **archives** of the Commission and its predecessor organisations |

## Offices & interinstitutional bodies
| Body | What it does |
|---|---|
| **OP** — Publications Office | Publishes the **Official Journal**; runs **EUR-Lex**, the **EU Vocabularies** and the **WhoisWho** directory (the corporate-body codes) |
| **OIB / OIL** — Offices for Infrastructure & Logistics | Buildings, facilities and logistics in **Brussels (OIB)** and **Luxembourg (OIL)** |
| **EPSO** — European Personnel Selection Office | **Recruitment** for all EU institutions (reserve lists; AD/AST competitions, CAST, temporary agents, traineeships) |
| **ESA** — European School of Administration | **Training** for staff of all EU institutions and agencies |

## Deciding the type when a user asks "who does X"
1. Is X a **policy area**? → a policy DG (`european_commission_who_does_what.md`).
2. Is X a **horizontal function** (translation, IT, statistics, audit, legal, anti-fraud, communication, health-emergency)? → a **functional DG** above.
3. Is X **funding delivery**? → an **executive agency** (`eu_commission_executive_agencies.md`).
4. Is X data-protection / library / foreign-policy-instruments / archives? → a **service department**.
5. Is X publishing the OJ, recruitment, buildings, staff training? → an **office** (OP, EPSO, OIB/OIL, ESA).
