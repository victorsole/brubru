# Strategy measures: experimentation, regulatory learning, partnerships and the JRC Science for Policy Centre

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** COM(2026) 453 final, section 2.1 (second half). Intentions of the Commission, not obligations; without prejudging the next MFF.
- **Experimentation:** funding for access to **testing and experimentation infrastructures** (Living Labs, innovation hubs) and for **public procurement of innovation**, so startups and SMEs can validate prototypes in real conditions.
- **Risk assessment and regulatory learning:** more R&I on **risk assessment methodologies**, wider use of **regulatory sandboxes** and closer work with EU risk assessment bodies such as the **European Food Safety Authority**, to "harvest regulatory learnings" that support adjustments (data requirements, alternative requirements) for pre-market approval of agri-food innovations, **including plant protection products, feed and food, including food derived from genetically modified organisms**. Regular input will be sought from EU and national risk assessment bodies (action 3, **Q4 2027**).
- **Regulatory sandboxes in the food chain:** the Communication points to the proposal for a European Biotech Act, **COM(2025) 1022**, which would amend the **General Food Law Regulation** to set the conditions for sandboxes in the food chain.
- **Partnerships:** long-term collaborative ecosystems have shown added value (the Mission "A Soil Deal for Europe", Joint Undertakings, co-funded partnerships). In line with an ongoing assessment (a public consultation on Joint Undertakings, Article 185 initiatives and thematic priorities for European Partnerships under Horizon Europe 2028-2034 is cited with a news item of 10 July 2026), the Commission will explore how partnerships can attract more private and public investment; the Standing Committee on Agricultural Research (SCAR) could act as **knowledge broker** on EU R&I capacities.
- **JRC Science for Policy Centre:** the Commission will launch a **"Science for Policy Centre on Agriculture, Forestry, Rural Development and Food System Innovation and Transformation"**, managed by the **Joint Research Centre** (action 5, **Q4 2027**): identify innovation opportunities, analyse economic impacts and market and deployment gaps, give stakeholders evidence-based, actionable knowledge.
- **Member States:** encouraged to use their future **national and regional partnership plans** to invest in capacity-building, human resources and innovation-enabling infrastructure such as testing facilities (action 7, **Q4 2026**).
