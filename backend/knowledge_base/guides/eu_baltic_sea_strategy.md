# EU Strategy for the Baltic Sea Region (EUSBSR)

- **LATEST (Thursday 21 May 2026 — NORWAY JOINS AS FULL MEMBER)**: DG REGIO announced that **Norway becomes a full member of the EU Strategy for the Baltic Sea Region (EUSBSR)**. Source: DG REGIO news (21 May 2026). Norway becomes the **first non-EU country with full participation rights**, joining the 8 EU Member States already in the strategy. The membership consolidates a long-running observer/cooperation status into full standing — recognising Norway's Baltic-area Schengen integration, energy-grid interconnections (NordLink, NorNed), and shared security concerns in the High North + Baltic.
- **EUSBSR launched**: 2009 — the **first** of the EU's four macroregional strategies (predates EU Strategy for the Danube Region 2011, EU Strategy for the Adriatic and Ionian Region 2014, EU Strategy for the Alpine Region 2016)
- **Foundational document**: Commission Communication **COM(2009) 248** + Council Conclusions 26 October 2009; refreshed Action Plan **SWD(2017) 118**
- **Coordinating body**: National Coordinators (one per participating country) + Policy Area Coordinators + Horizontal Action Leaders; Commission acts as facilitator
- **Lead DG**: DG REGIO (Regional and Urban Policy)
- **Commissioner / EVP**: Raffaele Fitto (Cohesion and Reforms, Italy)
- **Funding**: No dedicated budget envelope — funded via Cohesion Policy (ERDF + ESF+ + Cohesion Fund + Interreg programmes) + Horizon Europe + LIFE + LEADER + EMFAF; the strategy is a **coordination framework** not a fund

## Participating countries (as of 21 May 2026)

- **EU Member States (8)**: Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Sweden
- **Full-status non-EU (1, NEW 21 May 2026)**: **Norway**
- **Cooperation partners**: Iceland, Russia (suspended 2022 over Ukraine invasion), Belarus (suspended), Ukraine (associated)

## Three EUSBSR objectives

1. **Save the Sea** — environmental priorities: eutrophication, biodiversity, marine pollution (microplastics, munitions, oil spills), maritime safety
2. **Connect the Region** — infrastructure: transport (TEN-T Baltic-Adriatic + North Sea-Baltic corridors), energy (BEMIP — Baltic Energy Market Interconnection Plan), digital
3. **Increase Prosperity** — economy: innovation, single-market deepening, SMEs, skills, blue economy, tourism, culture

## Why Norway's full membership matters

Norway is not an EU Member State but is:
- Member of the **EEA** (European Economic Area) + **EFTA**
- Member of **Schengen**
- A major **energy interconnector hub** with North Sea + Baltic Sea grid investments (NordLink to Germany 2020, NorNed to Netherlands 2008, planned interconnectors to UK + Poland)
- A **security partner** under the EU-Norway Security and Defence Partnership (2024)
- Part of the **CBSS** (Council of the Baltic Sea States) since 1992

Full EUSBSR membership recognises Norway's already-substantive integration into the Baltic regional system and gives it formal voice in coordinator meetings, action-plan revisions, and joint stakeholder forums.

## Key policy interactions

### Energy
- **BEMIP** (Baltic Energy Market Interconnection Plan) — flagship initiative to integrate the Baltic States with the Nordic + Central European electricity market and end energy isolation
- **CEF Energy synchronisation** — the Baltic States' grid was synchronised with **Continental Europe in February 2025** (Russian/Belarusian BRELL grid disconnection); ongoing monitoring under CINEA — see `energy_grids_package`
- **Gas LNG capacity** — multiple LNG terminals (Klaipėda, Świnoujście, Inkoo, Eemshaven) anchor Baltic gas security post-2022
- **Cross-link**: `eu_energy_policy`, `accelerateeu_fossil_energy_crisis`

### Maritime
- **Marine pollution** — micro-plastics, munitions (post-war WWII dumping), oil-tanker shadow-fleet risks
- **Eutrophication** — agricultural runoff via Polish and Baltic states' rivers
- **HELCOM** (Helsinki Commission) cooperation
- **Cross-link**: `eu_marine_strategy_framework_directive`, `eu_oceans_policy`

### Transport
- **TEN-T Rail Baltica** — high-speed rail Tallinn-Riga-Kaunas-Warsaw; EU co-financed flagship
- **Cross-link**: `eu_ten_t_revision_2024`, `cef_transport_funding`

### Defence and security
- Post-2022 Russia + Belarus aggression has shifted the EUSBSR towards stronger security cooperation
- **NATO + EU joint** activities on resilience, hybrid threats, undersea cable security (3 Baltic Sea undersea cable incidents 2023-2024)
- **Cross-link**: `european_defence_union`, `eu_uk_security_defence_partnership`

### Digital and innovation
- **Smart Specialisation strategies** of the 8+1 participating regions
- **BSR Stars** programme — joint innovation calls

## How the EUSBSR is governed

- **Annual Forum** — large stakeholder event (rotating chair)
- **Policy Area Coordinators** + **Horizontal Action Leaders** — operational owners of action lines
- **National Coordinators** — Member State + Norway interface
- **Commission facilitation** — DG REGIO provides secretariat support
- **Action Plan** — strategic document, revised periodically (last full revision 2017, light updates 2021 + 2025)
- **Reporting** — Commission report to Council on macro-regional strategies (latest 2023)

## What Norway's full membership changes

- **Decision-making weight** in coordinator meetings and forum agendas
- **Eligibility for full-participation** in Interreg Baltic Sea Region 2027+ programming (currently Norway is a 3rd-country participant under specific arrangements)
- **Joint Norway-EU programmes** in energy interconnections, undersea-cable security, Arctic + maritime safety
- **Political signal** post-2022 — closer EEA-EU integration on security-relevant cooperation tracks
