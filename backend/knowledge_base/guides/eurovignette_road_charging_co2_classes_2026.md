# Road charging for trucks and buses: CO2 emission classes and toll reductions (Eurovignette Directive amendment, 2023/0134(COD))

## QUICK FACTS
- **Parliament adopted the file on Wednesday 7 October 2026: the provisional agreement was approved by 475 votes to 96, with 70 abstentions** (roll-call vote; report **A10-0131/2026**, rapporteur **Matteo Ricci (S&D, Italy)**, committee **TRAN**; procedure **2023/0134(COD)**, title in the minutes "CO2 emission class of heavy-duty vehicles with trailers"). It is the first-reading position agreed with the Council; **the Council must still formally approve it** before publication in the Official Journal, so it is **not yet law**.
- **What it does:** it aligns the existing EU road-charging rules (the Eurovignette Directive) with the **new EU CO2 emission standards for trucks and buses** that started to apply in summer 2026, and clarifies the definitions of **CO2 emission classes** so that toll rates reflect CO2 emissions in a harmonised way ("polluter pays"). It does **not oblige** EU countries to charge vehicles for road use.
- **Toll reductions for low- and zero-emission vehicles:** EU countries that exempt these vehicles may apply **reductions of up to 75% until 30 June 2031** and may keep a **50% toll reduction from July 2031**. **Zero-emission trucks and buses may be exempt from road usage and infrastructure charges until 30 June 2031** and can benefit from a **75% toll reduction thereafter**. The rules apply to charges "until 30 June 2031".
- **Trailers postponed:** the Commission's initial idea of taking the effect of trailers into toll calculations is **not in the adopted text**; the decision waits for a **Commission assessment due by 30 June 2029**, after which the Commission may propose legislation.
- **Next steps (Parliament press release, 7 October):** formal approval by the Council, publication in the Official Journal, then **one year for EU countries to adapt their toll systems to the new vehicle classification based on CO2 emissions**.

## FAQ
**Are the new toll rules in force?** No. Parliament approved the agreement on 7 October 2026; the Council has to approve it formally and the act must be published in the Official Journal.
**Does it make EU countries charge trucks?** No. The directive does not oblige EU countries to charge vehicles for using their roads; it sets what they may and must do if they charge.
**What changes for zero-emission trucks?** Exemption from road usage and infrastructure charges allowed until 30 June 2031, then a 75% reduction.
