## Goal of the high-level dialogue (30 April 2026)

Per the DG ENV announcement, the Commission held a stakeholder dialogue to gather input ahead of College adoption. The five priorities surfaced:

1. **Unlocking the Single Market for the circular economy** — harmonising rules on secondary raw materials (SRM) so they can move across MS borders without new national restrictions (cross-link: SMTD work, Terrible Ten)
2. **Recovery of critical raw materials from waste** — supporting EU economic security by sourcing CRMs domestically via recycling instead of importing
3. **Building stronger markets for SRM** — public procurement, demand-side measures, taxonomy alignment
4. **Reducing EU dependence on critical raw material imports** — coordinated with the CRM Act (Reg 2024/1252)
5. **Simplifying regulatory frameworks for SRM** — End-of-Waste status harmonisation, one-stop notifications

## What the CEA is expected to do

The 30 April announcement does NOT enumerate the CEA's articles. Until the proposal is published, treat the policy direction as anchor and verify each provision when the proposal text lands. Likely scope (consistent with Compass and prior signals):

- A horizontal regulation rather than a directive — directly applicable, no transposition window
- End-of-Waste criteria harmonised at EU level (replacing fragmented national approaches under Directive 2008/98/EC)
- Mandatory recycled-content thresholds (sectoral, scaling over time)
- Secondary raw materials free-movement rules (Single Market angle)
- Possibly: recycled-content public-procurement minima
- Coordination layer with ESPR, Batteries Reg, WSR, CRM Act

This is illustrative — confirm against the published text.

## Why this matters

The CEA is a horizontal vehicle to consolidate fragmented circular-economy rules into a single framework with Single Market force. It pairs with:
- The Terrible Ten + Better Regulation push (DG GROW) on the Single Market side
- The Batteries Regulation removability consultation (28 April 2026, DG ENV) on the implementation side
- The Critical Raw Materials Act (Reg 2024/1252) on the supply-security side

Companies in waste management, recycling, EV battery, raw-material processing, and procurement-heavy sectors should track the proposal text immediately on publication.
