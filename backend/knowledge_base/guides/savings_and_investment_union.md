## Why the SIU rebrand from CMU matters

The Letta Report (Enrico Letta, "Much more than a market — Speed, security, solidarity", April 2024) and the **Draghi Report on European Competitiveness** (September 2024) both diagnosed the EU's structural under-utilisation of household savings as a single biggest drag on European competitiveness. Eurostat-based estimates (Draghi Report, Part 2): EU household financial savings are roughly **EUR 35 trillion**, of which a disproportionate share sits in low-yielding deposits and pension claims locked into nationally-fragmented systems.

The CMU branding emphasised **wholesale capital markets** (issuers, intermediaries, post-trade). The SIU adds two distinctive thrusts:
1. **Retail savings activation** — pan-EU savings products, simplified taxation, financial literacy harmonisation
2. **Pension integration** — bringing IORPs (Institutions for Occupational Retirement Provision), PEPP (Pan-European Personal Pension Product), and national second-pillar schemes into a single-market investment perimeter

## SIU four-pillar agenda (Communication March 2025)

### Pillar 1 — Retail savings & investments
- Pan-EU **personal investment savings accounts** — feasibility study + targeted Commission proposal (2025-2026)
- **Simplified retail investment product disclosure** — building on the Retail Investment Strategy package (2023-2024) and the PRIIPs Regulation review
- **Financial literacy benchmarks** — Council Recommendation track, leveraging the joint EC/OECD financial literacy framework
- Targeted tax-incentive harmonisation — Council unanimity requirement, slow track

### Pillar 2 — Investment frameworks
- **Securitisation review** — targeted revision of the Securitisation Regulation (EU) 2017/2402 (CELEX 32017R2402) — Commission consultation launched October 2025
- **ELTIF 2.0 implementation** — Regulation (EU) 2023/606 already in force; technical RTS being finalised
- **Pan-European venture capital and growth equity** — feasibility for an EU-level fund-of-funds backed by EIF + private LPs
- **Listing rules simplification** — building on the Listing Act package adopted October 2024

### Pillar 3 — Market structure & supervisory convergence
- **Consolidated tape** for equities + bonds — implementing the MIFIR review (Regulation (EU) 2024/791); ESMA tender process underway
- **Central counterparty (CCP) post-trade consolidation** — EMIR 3.0 (Reg (EU) 2024/2987) implementation
- **ESMA direct supervisory powers** — gradual expansion to cover large cross-border market participants (data reporting, benchmarks, ESG ratings)
- **Convergence on supervisory practices** — peer reviews + breach-of-Union-law procedures

### Pillar 4 — Integration of pension capital
- **PEPP review** — uptake of the existing Pan-European Personal Pension Product has been below expectations (under 100,000 holders by end-2025); review under way to relax product caps and simplify cross-border portability
- **IORP II review** — building on the existing Directive (EU) 2016/2341 (CELEX 32016L2341)
- **Pension dashboards** — national pension tracking systems interoperability

## Stakeholder map

- **AFME** (Association for Financial Markets in Europe) — wholesale capital markets industry body
- **EFAMA** (European Fund and Asset Management Association) — buy-side
- **Insurance Europe + PensionsEurope** — retail savings and pensions
- **ESBG, EBF, EAPB** — banking
- **BETTER FINANCE** — retail investor advocacy NGO
- **Eurogroup + ECOFIN Working Party on Financial Services** — Member State coordination
- **ECB** (Frankfurt) — euro-area monetary anchor; supports SIU on financial-stability grounds

## Member State political map

- **France**: champions SIU as a single-market completion file; supports ESMA powers expansion
- **Germany**: cautious on pension integration; protects national pension architecture
- **Netherlands**: supports market-structure pillar but resists tax harmonisation
- **Italy**: open to pan-EU savings products; cautious on supervisory transfer
- **Poland + CEE**: catching-up logic; pushes for capital-market deepening
- **Ireland + Luxembourg**: hosts of major fund infrastructure; defend existing competitive position
