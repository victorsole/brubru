## Overview

The Tobacco Products Directive (TPD), Directive 2014/40/EU, is the EU's core health-and-internal-market instrument regulating how tobacco and related products are made, presented and sold across the Union. It harmonises health warnings, ingredient reporting, flavour bans, anti-illicit-trade tracking, and — through Article 20 — the first EU-level rules on electronic cigarettes. It applies alongside, but is legally distinct from, the Tobacco Taxation Directive (which sets minimum excise duties).

Since 2014 the nicotine market has changed faster than the rules. Heated tobacco products, disposable vapes and nicotine pouches have grown rapidly, and Member States have diverged in how they treat them — fragmenting the internal market and, in the Commission's evaluation, weakening tobacco control. The 2 April 2026 evaluation Staff Working Document concluded that the largest framework gaps come from the absence of harmonised rules for these novel products.

## The 2026 revision

The Commission opened the formal revision in two steps in 2026: a **call for evidence** on 18 May 2026 (feedback window to 15 June 2026) and a broader **public consultation open until 14 August 2026**, both gathering input on whether and how to update the TPD (and the companion Tobacco Advertising Directive 2003/33/EC). Expected themes include:

- Harmonised rules for **novel nicotine products** (HTPs, e-cigarettes/vapes, nicotine pouches), including disposables
- **Flavours** in novel products and the scope of existing flavour bans
- Packaging, labelling and health-warning rules across product categories
- Advertising and cross-border promotion (via the Advertising Directive review)
- Alignment with the EU's wider tobacco-control and cancer objectives

The file is politically contested: public-health bodies (and the SmokeFree Partnership, ENSP) press for tighter harmonisation, while industry actors (PMI, BAT, JTI, Imperial) and harm-reduction advocates contest the evidence base and scope. The public consultation reportedly drew a high volume of submissions.

**Status caveat:** as of June 2026 the revision is at the evidence/consultation stage. There is **no tabled Commission proposal, no COM number, no rapporteur, no lead EP committee, and no OEIL procedure reference** for the TPD revision yet. Brubru will not assert any of those until the proposal text is published.
