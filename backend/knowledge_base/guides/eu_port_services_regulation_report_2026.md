# Port Services Regulation: Commission report of 8 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** Commission report **COM(2026) 535 final of 8 October 2026** to Parliament and Council "on the functioning and effect of the Port Services Regulation", i.e. **Regulation (EU) 2017/352** (framework for the provision of port services and common rules on the financial transparency of ports). The report is required by **Article 20** of the Regulation, which set a deadline of **24 March 2023**: the Commission says it can only report now because Member States were late providing information and stakeholders answered its questionnaire slowly. **It is a snapshot of implementation, "not an evaluation of the Regulation".**
- **The ports covered:** the EU has over 1,000 commercial maritime ports along 60,000 km of coastline; **327 are part of the trans-European transport network (TEN-T)**: 283 seaports and 44 mixed sea and inland ports, of which **97 are "core"** and **230 "comprehensive"**.
- **The Regulation's three objectives (as the report restates them):** a framework for the provision of port services; an appropriate framework to attract the investment needed in TEN-T maritime ports; and a European level playing field within and among ports. The Regulation imposes no specific port-management model.
- **The Commission's conclusion:** implementation has been gradual, "with some delays"; there were only a limited number of complaints and infringement procedures; enforcement concentrated on Member States' **notification obligations**, where the infringement procedures "all produced the desired effect"; the temporary possibility created by **Regulation (EU) 2020/697** to waive, suspend, reduce or defer port-infrastructure charges for 1 March to 31 October 2020 (COVID-19) was "hardly used" and not prolonged. Overall the Commission considers implementation "in line with the objectives set out in the Regulation and ... satisfactory", and says it will keep monitoring the 327 TEN-T ports. The conclusion announces continued monitoring, not an amendment of the Regulation.
- The report draws on Member State notifications, a support study annexed to it, exchanges in the **European Ports Forum** (a Commission expert group set up in May 2017) and information from port authorities; it also takes account of the EU Sectoral Social Dialogue Committee in the Port Sector.

## How to answer
Say that this is a Commission report under Article 20, late by more than three years, and that it concludes the Regulation is working satisfactorily without proposing changes. Do not call it an evaluation or a revision.
