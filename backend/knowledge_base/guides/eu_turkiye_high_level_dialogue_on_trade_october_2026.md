# Third EU-Türkiye High-Level Dialogue on Trade, 13 October 2026: TAREKS barrier removed

## QUICK FACTS
- **Meeting:** the **3rd EU-Türkiye High Level Dialogue on Trade**, **13 October 2026, Brussels**, co-chaired by Commissioner **Maroš Šefčovič** (Trade and Economic Security) and Türkiye's Minister of Trade **Ömer Bolat**; preceded by a business roundtable. Trade between the EU and Türkiye, a candidate country, reached **EUR 217.6 billion in 2025**. Source: Commission joint statement **STATEMENT/26/2135** (remarks: SPEECH/26/2136).
- **Concrete outcome:** the parties announced **the removal of the barrier on import controls under Türkiye's TAREKS system** (a risk-based foreign trade control system for importers), with continued cooperation on practical issues raised by EU operators.
- **Customs Union and alignment:** they welcomed **Türkiye completing its alignment to the new EU Machinery Regulation**, progress on **customs reform including e-commerce**, and confirmed the intention to advance implementation of the **mutual recognition agreement on Authorised Economic Operators (AEO)**. The Customs Cooperation Committee's Decision No 1/2026 on AEO mutual recognition was published in the OJ on 13 October 2026 (CELEX 22026D2303).
- **Still open (technical engagement continues):** **export taxes on hides, skins and wet-blue leather**; the import surveillance regime; **surveillance by registration of imports of textiles** and inputs; alcoholic beverages; Türkiye's access to the EU **energy labelling registry (EPREL)**; technical talks on access to **CPNP** (cosmetics notification portal) and on **batch release of pharmaceuticals**.
- **Other areas covered:** alignment of technical legislation, public procurement, circular economy, business mobility, **digital trade (technical work on mutual recognition of e-signatures)**, trade defence, WTO cooperation and **anti-circumvention of EU sanctions**.
- **Not in the statement:** no modernisation of the Customs Union, no date for the next Dialogue, no tariff change.

## How to answer
"What did the EU and Türkiye agree on trade on 13 October 2026?": removal of the TAREKS import-control barrier, welcome for Türkiye's alignment with the Machinery Regulation, and further technical work on leather export taxes, textile surveillance, EPREL, CPNP and e-signatures. No new trade deal.
