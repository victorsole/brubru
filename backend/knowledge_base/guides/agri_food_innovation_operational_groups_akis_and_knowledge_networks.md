# How agri-food innovation reaches farmers (2): EIP-AGRI Operational Groups, AKIS, knowledge networks, barriers

## QUICK FACTS
- **AKIS (agricultural knowledge and innovation systems):** "the combined organisation and knowledge flows between individuals, organisations, and institutions who use and produce knowledge for agriculture and interrelated fields" (definition from Regulation (EU) 2021/2115). Farmers sit in the "knowledge triangle" of **research, education and extension (advisory) services**. **Setting up an integrated AKIS is mandatory in the CAP 2023-2027**: Member States develop national AKIS strategies.
- **EIP-AGRI Operational Groups:** farmer-led bottom-up innovation projects under the CAP (the "interactive innovation model", Article 127 of Regulation (EU) 2021/2115). **Over 5,200 groups since 2014; more than 6,600 planned for 2023-2027.** The **European Court of Auditors** (special report 09/2026) called the European Innovation Partnership in the CAP "a key tool for innovation in EU agriculture falling short of its full potential". Reverse integration (testing Horizon results in Operational Groups) "appears to need more work".
- **Practice abstracts:** short practitioner summaries in a common EIP-AGRI format; **over 14,000**; the strategy will revise the format (action 11, Q4 2027).
- **Networks:** **more than 60 thematic networks** (examples: BROILERNET, EUNetHorse, OH-FINE, CLIMED-FRUIT, FORADVISE, NUTRI-KNOW); **advisory networks** (AdvisoryNetPEST, OrganicAdviceNetwork, STRATUS, FAIRshare, COREnet); **EU Thematic Knowledge Hubs** funded under the Horizon Europe Cluster 6 work programme 2026-2027 as go-to sources that reduce adviser fatigue.
- **Platforms:** **EU-FarmBook** (2022-2029): multilingual platform in **all 24 official EU languages** with real-time translation and AI-assisted search, consolidating practice abstracts; **modernAKIS** (2022-2029): a European AKIS network with a Member State benchmarking tool. The **EU CAP Network** connects actors across Member States.
- **Demonstration farms:** set up under Horizon and the CAP; they "strongly influence farmers' decision to innovate"; **Climate Farm Demo** runs **1,460 pilot demo farms in 28 countries**.
- **Barriers to uptake (SWD conclusion):** economic risk, uncertainty, compatibility with existing practices, trust in knowledge sources, reluctance to change; diverse farm structures, small actors, **ageing and gender imbalance**, limited investment capacity, **weaker rural broadband**.
- **Search words (other languages):** systèmes de connaissances et d'innovation agricoles, groupes opérationnels, PEI-AGRI (FR); agrarische kennis- en innovatiesystemen, operationele groepen (NL); sistemas de conocimiento e innovación agrarios, grupos operativos (ES); sistemes de coneixement i innovació agraris, grups operatius (CA); sistemi della conoscenza e dell'innovazione in agricoltura, gruppi operativi (IT).
