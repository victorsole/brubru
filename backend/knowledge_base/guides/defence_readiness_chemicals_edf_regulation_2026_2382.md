# Defence readiness Regulation (EU) 2026/2382 (Omnibus V): defence exemptions in REACH, CLP and biocides, and European Defence Fund changes

## QUICK FACTS
- **NOT YET in force on 3 November 2026; it will enter into force on 23 November 2026** (Article 6). OJ L of 3 November 2026; signed 21 October 2026. CELEX 32026R2382, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2382/oj , procedure 2025/0176(COD), COM(2025) 822.
- **Exactly one retroactive rule:** Article 9(6), third subparagraph, of the European Defence Fund Regulation as amended applies from **1 January 2025** (Article 6): costs of using Ukrainian-linked infrastructure for testing can be eligible under the conditions stated there.
- **REACH, Article 2(3) as replaced:** "Member States may allow for exemptions from this Regulation for substances, on their own, in a mixture or in an article, where necessary in the interests of defence."
- **CLP, Article 1(4) as replaced:** same test, for substances and mixtures, and articles under section 2.1 of Annex I. **Biocides, Article 2(8) as replaced:** same test, for biocidal products on their own or in a treated article.
- **What changed, in all three:** the earlier texts said "in specific cases for certain" substances, mixtures or products (read in each original Regulation; later consolidations of REACH were not compared). Those words go on 23 November 2026.
- **It is NOT an automatic exemption:** each Member State decides ("may allow"). It does NOT exempt companies by itself, and it is limited to the interests of defence.
- **Persistent Organic Pollutants, Regulation (EU) 2019/1021:** adds a definition of "defence readiness"; the Commission and Member States must take defence readiness into account in socio-economic information under the Stockholm Convention; Member States may exempt from Article 13 to protect sensitive defence information, without undermining reporting duties (Article 4).
- **European Defence Fund, Regulation (EU) 2021/697 (Article 5), among others:** new definition of cross-border SMEs; support for disruptive technologies and SME research and development; new award criteria; a 10-percentage-point increase for actions in a PESCO project (Permanent Structured Cooperation) or a Structure for European Armament Programme; an SME-based increase, up to an additional 10 percentage points; only the coordinator's financial capacity is verified; Article 22 deleted.
- **Not read in detail:** the remaining Fund amendments (Article 5, later points). **Who is bound:** Member States and the Commission.
