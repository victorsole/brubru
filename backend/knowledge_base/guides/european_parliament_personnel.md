# European Parliament -- Personnel Directory

## EP Presidency

President: Roberta Metsola
President's Office staff: 19 persons

## EP Bureau

The Bureau consists of the President and 14 Vice-Presidents. It takes decisions on matters of internal organisation.

## EP Secretariat-General

Secretary-General: Alessandro Chiocchetti
The Secretariat-General supports the work of the Parliament through 14 Directorates-General, a Legal Service, and several horizontal units.

### DG Presidency (EP_PRES)
Director-General: Markus Winkler
Covers: Plenary sittings, legislative coordination, presidency support.

### DG Economy, Transformation and Industry (EP_DGECTI)
Director-General: Michael Alexander Speiser
Covers: Internal market, industry, competition, research, energy, digital.

### DG Cohesion, Agriculture and Social Policies (EP_DGCASP)
Director-General: Agnieszka Walter-Drop
Covers: Regional development, agriculture, fisheries, employment, social affairs.

### DG Citizens' Rights, Justice and Institutional Affairs (EP_DGIUST)
Director-General: Leticia Zuleta de Reales Ansaldo
Covers: Civil liberties, justice, home affairs, constitutional affairs, petitions, gender equality.

### DG Budgetary Affairs (EP_DGBUDG)
Director-General: Monika Strasser
Covers: EU budget, budgetary control, own resources.

### DG External Policies (EP_EXTERN_POL)
Director-General: Pietro Ducci
Covers: Foreign affairs, security, defence, development, international trade, delegations.

### DG Parliamentary Research Services -- EPRS (EP_RESEARCH)
Director-General: Anders Rasmussen (also Deputy SG)
Covers: Research briefings, impact assessments, European Added Value, ex-post evaluations.

### DG Communication (EP_COMMU)
Director-General: Christian Mangold
Covers: Media, press, visitors, liaison offices, digital communication.

### DG Parliamentary Democracy Partnerships (EP_DGPART)
Director-General: Sannaleena Lepola-Honig
Covers: Democracy support, election observation, capacity building.

### DG Personnel (EP_PERS)
Director-General: Ellen Robson
Covers: Staff management, recruitment, training, social services.

### DG Infrastructure and Logistics (EP_INFRA_LOGIS)
Director-General: Anna Grzybowska
Covers: Buildings, technical services, logistics, catering.

### DG Translation and Clear Language (EP_TRANSL)
Director-General: Valter Mavric
Covers: Translation into 24 EU languages, clear language, terminology.

### DG Logistics and Interpretation for Conferences (EP_INTERPRET)
Director-General: Juan Carlos Jimenez Marin
Covers: Conference organisation, interpretation, meeting rooms.

### DG Finance (EP_FINANCE)
Director-General: Didier Klethi
Covers: Financial management, accounting, Members' allowances.

### DG Information Technologies and Cybersecurity (EP_SAFE)
Director-General: Lorenzo Mannelli
Covers: IT systems, cybersecurity, digital workplace.

### DG Security and Safety
Director-General: Guy Mols
Covers: Physical security, access control, fire safety.

### Legal Service (EP_LS)
Director: Freddy Drexler
Covers: Legal advice to Parliament, representation before CJEU.

## Interparliamentary Delegations

31 delegations covering bilateral and multilateral parliamentary relations. 1,065 MEP seats across all delegations.

Major delegations include: EU-US, EU-UK, EU-China, EU-Western Balkans (individual country delegations), EU-Latin America, ACP-EU, EuroNest (Eastern Partnership), EU-Turkey, OSCE PA, NATO PA.
