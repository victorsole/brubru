# Welfare of Dogs and Cats and Traceability (EU Regulation)

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU framework on the welfare of dogs and cats kept for commercial trade and on traceability of companion animals (microchipping, registration, cross-border movement)
- **Commission proposal**: **Proposal for a Regulation on the welfare of dogs and cats and their traceability** -- COM(2023) 769 final, 7 December 2023
- **Procedure**: 2023/0421(COD) -- ordinary legislative procedure
- **Lead DG**: DG SANTE (Health and Food Safety, Unit G5 -- Animal Welfare)
- **Responsible Commissioner**: **Oliver Várhelyi** (Health and Animal Welfare) since December 2024 -- "Animal Welfare" addition to portfolio name signals legislative push
- **Lead EP committee**: AGRI (Agriculture and Rural Development); ENVI opinion (public health), IMCO opinion (online sales)
- **EP rapporteur**: **Veronika Vrecionová** (ECR, Czechia, AGRI) -- former AGRI vice-chair, Mittelstand farming background
- **Council configuration**: AGRIFISH (Agriculture and Fisheries Council)
- **Legal basis**: Article 43(2) TFEU (CAP) + Article 114 TFEU (internal market harmonisation -- online sales)
- Covered animals: dogs and cats kept for commercial breeding, sale, transport, in establishments. Excludes pets owned by private individuals not for sale, or animals in scientific research (covered by Directive 2010/63/EU)

## Why this matters

Dogs and cats account for an estimated **127 million companion animals** in EU households (FEDIAF 2024 figures). Cross-border trade is significant: organised illegal puppy trafficking is a multi-billion-euro grey market, often linked to organised crime (Europol 2023 SOCTA). Welfare conditions in commercial breeding establishments vary wildly across Member States, and the absence of harmonised traceability rules makes enforcement near-impossible.

The 2023 Commission proposal addresses three gaps:

1. **Welfare standards in commercial establishments**: minimum requirements on housing, husbandry, health, breeding practices (e.g. limits on litters per breeding female, minimum weaning age, prohibited mutilations like tail docking and ear cropping)
2. **Identification and registration**: mandatory microchipping of all dogs and cats traded commercially; interconnected national databases interoperable across Member States
3. **Online sales rules**: identity verification of sellers, mandatory disclosure of microchip number + breeding-establishment registration, platform liability under DSA-aligned framework

## Key Provisions of the Proposal

### Article 4 -- Authorisation of breeding establishments
Member States must authorise commercial breeders meeting welfare + biosecurity criteria; authorisation valid 5 years; on-site inspections mandatory.

### Article 6 -- Husbandry standards
Floor space, environmental enrichment, social interaction, exercise, indoor climate, ventilation -- species-specific minima.

### Article 11 -- Identification and registration
- Microchipping with ISO 11784/11785 transponder before any commercial transaction
- Registration in a national database operationally interoperable with other MS databases via Europetnet or successor system
- Master microchip + database number recorded on commercial documents (cross-border movement, online sales)

### Article 14 -- Online sales
Online sale of dogs/cats only allowed where:
- Seller's identity verified to platform's KYC standard
- Microchip number disclosed in listing
- Breeding establishment authorisation number disclosed
- Platform takes down listings non-compliant; cooperation with national authorities

### Article 18 -- Penalties
Effective, proportionate, dissuasive; serious-offence threshold for organised illegal trafficking aligned with Directive 2008/99/EC environmental crimes / proceeds of crime confiscation.

## Stakeholder Positions

- **Eurogroup for Animals** (Brussels-based welfare NGO coalition): supportive, push for stricter mutilation bans, breeding-female limits
- **FEDIAF** (European Pet Food Industry Federation): supportive of traceability + welfare floor; concerns on SME burden
- **FECAVA + FVE** (vets associations): supportive; advocate veterinary inspection role
- **National kennel clubs / breeder federations**: split -- mainstream supportive, niche-breed federations argue for derogations
- **Member States (Council)**: France + Germany + Spain pushing harmonisation; Eastern + Southern MS more cautious due to enforcement-cost concerns
