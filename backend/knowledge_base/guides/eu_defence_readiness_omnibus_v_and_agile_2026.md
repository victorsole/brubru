# Defence simplification (Omnibus V) and the AGILE programme: four acts published in the Official Journal on 3 November 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status (read 3 November 2026): PUBLISHED in the Official Journal, L series, on 3 November 2026; all four signed in Strasbourg on 21 October 2026.** The act numbers are known. Each act has its OWN dates, read from its final article:
  1. **Regulation (EU) 2026/2387, AGILE programme.** In force? NO as of 3 November, YES from **4 November 2026** (Article 25: the day after publication, NOT the twentieth day; the Council's press release of 12 October said twentieth, the act prevails). Procedure 2026/0078(COD), COM(2026) 135. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2387/oj
  2. **Regulation (EU) 2026/2381, permits for defence readiness projects.** NOT YET in force on 3 November; it will enter into force on **23 November 2026** and applies ONLY to permit applications submitted from **24 February 2027** (Article 14). Procedure 2025/0172(COD), COM(2025) 821. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2381/oj
  3. **Regulation (EU) 2026/2382, defence readiness and defence investments.** Amends REACH, CLP, Biocides, Persistent Organic Pollutants and the European Defence Fund Regulation. NOT YET in force on 3 November; it will enter into force on **23 November 2026** (Article 6). Procedure 2025/0176(COD), COM(2025) 822. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2382/oj
  4. **Directive (EU) 2026/2383, intra-EU defence transfers and defence procurement.** NOT YET in force on 3 November; it will enter into force on **23 November 2026** (Article 4). Member States transpose by **24 November 2027** and apply by **24 February 2028** (Article 3), so its thresholds do NOT apply on 23 November 2026. Procedure 2025/0177(COD), COM(2025) 823. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/2383/oj
- **"Omnibus V" is exactly THREE acts: numbers 2, 3 and 4.** AGILE is a separate programme proposed in March 2026 and published the same day.
- **Votes:** Parliament voted Omnibus V at first reading on **16 September 2026** (T10-0291, T10-0292, T10-0293/2026) and AGILE on **6 October 2026** (T10-0320/2026). The Council adopted Omnibus V on **9 October** and AGILE on **12 October 2026**.
- **Companion text:** Joint statement by Parliament and the Council, C/2026/5559 (OJ C, 3 November 2026): further efforts are needed to reach defence readiness by 2030; they call on the Commission to consider further initiatives. It is a political statement, NOT a legal obligation.
- **Who is bound:** these acts bind Member States, public authorities and contracting authorities; companies are beneficiaries (faster permits, funding, fewer licences). Do not describe them as new compliance duties on companies.
