# Defence simplification (Omnibus V) and the AGILE programme: adopted by the Council on 9 and 12 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **AGILE in the Commission's words (press release IP/26/2132 of 12 October 2026, welcoming the adoption):** a **EUR 115 million** funding tool for **SMEs, start-ups and scale-ups** developing innovative defence solutions; **time-to-grant of four months**; technologies to reach defence forces in **1 to 3 years**; it will support **between 20 and 30 projects**, with **up to 100% funding** of eligible costs, and expenses can be claimed retroactively up to **three months before the closing of the call**. The work programme has two activity types: mission-driven development of disruptive defence products and technologies, and market delivery. The Commission says AGILE was adopted "in record time", in **less than seven months** after its March 2026 proposal. Source: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2132
- **Status (12 October 2026): ADOPTED BY THE COUNCIL, not yet in the Official Journal.** The Council gave its final green light to the **defence readiness simplification omnibus ("Omnibus V")** on **9 October 2026** and to the **AGILE programme** on **12 October 2026**. The Council says the acts "will be published in the EU's official journal in the coming days" and enter into force **on the twentieth day after publication** (AGILE: 20 days after publication). As of 13 October 2026 none of these acts had appeared in the Official Journal L index (20 acts that day, none of them from this package), so no final act number is known to this guide: give the procedure numbers, and say the act numbers come with publication.
- **Omnibus V is exactly three acts** (all Commission proposals of **17 June 2025**; all voted by Parliament at first reading on **16 September 2026**):
  1. **Regulation on defence readiness and facilitating defence investments and conditions for the defence industry**: procedure **2025/0176(COD)**, COM(2025) 822, Parliament text T10-0293/2026. It simplifies the management of the **European Defence Fund (EDF)**, raises the funding-rate bonus for projects involving SMEs, keeps European public bodies such as the European Defence Agency able to act as central purchasing bodies, and clarifies how EU environmental and chemicals law applies to defence readiness activities. The Legislative Observatory lists it as amending Regulations (EC) 1907/2006 (REACH), 1272/2008 (CLP), (EU) 528/2012 (biocides), (EU) 2019/1021 (persistent organic pollutants) and (EU) 2021/697 (EDF).
  2. **Regulation on the acceleration of permit-granting for defence readiness projects**: procedure **2025/0172(COD)**, COM(2025) 821, Parliament text T10-0291/2026. The permit-granting process is capped at **102 working days**; a request is **tacitly approved** if the authority has not decided by then; national law may still provide derogations where there is a grave risk to human health or national security.
  3. **Directive on the simplification of intra-EU transfers of defence-related products and of security and defence procurement**: procedure **2025/0177(COD)**, COM(2025) 823, Parliament text T10-0292/2026. It amends Directives **2009/43/EC** (transfers) and **2009/81/EC** (defence and security procurement). It raises the thresholds in the defence procurement directive, allows occasional joint procurement, adds a de minimis rule for contract modifications, makes framework agreements more flexible and introduces **two new mandatory general transfer licences**: one between certified suppliers and certified recipients, one for transfers within an intra-EU industrial partnership.
- **AGILE (Programme for agile and rapid defence innovation)**: Regulation, procedure **2026/0078(COD)**, COM(2026) 135 of 25 March 2026; provisional agreement with Parliament on **15 July 2026**; Parliament voted on **6 October 2026** (T10-0320/2026); Council adopted **12 October 2026**. **EUR 115 million** for SMEs, start-ups and scale-ups developing emerging and disruptive defence products: **time-to-grant of four months**, products expected to reach defence forces within one to three years, mission-driven challenges set with Member States, fast-track access to testing facilities, matchmaking with large defence companies, simplified procurement of AGILE-supported products. It amends Regulations (EU) 2024/795 and (EU) 2026/467. **The first call for proposals is expected at the beginning of 2027.**
- The Council presents Omnibus V as part of the EU simplification agenda; Minister of State Thomas Byrne calls it "an important achievement for the Irish presidency".

## How to answer
Say that the Council has adopted the acts and that Official Journal publication follows, so the dates of application depend on the publication date. Give the three procedure numbers when asked which acts make up the package. Do not describe AGILE as open for applications: the call is expected in early 2027.
