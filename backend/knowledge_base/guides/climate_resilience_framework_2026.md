# EU Climate Resilience Framework and European Climate Resilience Law proposal (adopted 27 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **STATUS (read 12:00 CET, Tuesday 27 October 2026): ADOPTED by the College on 27 October 2026 as a PROPOSAL and a Communication. It is NOT law, NOT in force: the Regulation is a Commission proposal that Parliament and Council must still negotiate under the ordinary legislative procedure 2026/0336(COD).** Press release IP/26/2244 https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2244 ; Q&A QANDA/26/2245 ; factsheet FS/26/2246.
- **Exactly TWO documents, both dated Brussels 27.10.2026 (owner: the documents' own headers):** (1) the Communication **COM(2026) 720 final** "A resilient Europe for a changing climate: Communication on the EU Framework for Climate Resilience" (16 pages) https://climate.ec.europa.eu/document/download/476ef20a-0c66-4be2-a104-70ba96622477_en?filename=com_2026_720_en.pdf ; (2) the proposal **COM(2026) 730 final, procedure 2026/0336(COD)**, "Regulation establishing a framework to enhance climate resilience and amending Regulation (EU) 2018/1999 (European Climate Resilience Law)", text with EEA relevance, legal basis **Article 192(1) TFEU**, 16 articles plus annexes https://climate.ec.europa.eu/document/download/dc70f56e-0b44-4a7a-b3ba-5cda2d378016_en?filename=com_2026_730_en.pdf . Accompanied by SEC(2026) 730, SWD(2026) 730, the impact assessment **SWD(2026) 731** (278 pages) https://climate.ec.europa.eu/document/download/7798a694-fda3-4bd1-9f84-ce5a385722fd_en?filename=swd_2026_731_en.pdf and SWD(2026) 732. Cellar 404 for both COM numbers and no OEIL page at 12:00 CET (same day).
- **What the proposed Regulation does (operative articles read in full):** Art. 3 a common climate resilience objective; **Art. 4 + Annex I a reference global warming trajectory of 1.5 °C by 2030, 2 °C by 2050 and 3 °C by 2100** versus 1850-1900, the common baseline for every risk assessment (updatable by delegated act, Art. 12); **Art. 5 the EEA carries out a European climate risk assessment by 30 June 2028, each Member State a national one by 30 June 2029, both every five years**; **Art. 6 a Union strategic plan on climate resilience by 30 September 2028**; **Art. 7 ONE national strategic plan per Member State by 31 December 2030**, replacing the planning obligations of the European Climate Law and the Governance Regulation (Art. 14 amends Regulation (EU) 2018/1999); Art. 8 consultation of neighbouring Member States on transboundary risks; Art. 9 an online platform with the European Climate Viewer; **Art. 10 Eurostat statistics on deaths and economic losses attributable to climate change by 31 December 2030, then yearly**; Art. 11 the EEA as centre of excellence; Art. 15 review by 30 September 2033; Art. 16 in force 20 days after OJ publication.
- **Figures (owner: IP/26/2244 and the Q&A, NOT the Regulation):** climate damages 2022-2025 about EUR 200 billion (EUR 50 billion a year); adaptation investment needed about EUR 70 billion a year; return on adaptation over 10 times; global adaptation-solutions market EUR 3.5 trillion by 2050; Europe warms at double the global rate; 75% of Europeans live in urban areas; over 35,000 heat-related deaths (preliminary, Q&A).
- **Tools:** European Climate Viewer (ECMWF), Navigator of Climate Resilience Tools (EEA), Mission Solutions Explorer. **Announced for later, NOT adopted today:** a European Heatwave Plan, a Climate Insurance Alliance, a European Water initiative, a PreparEU Advisory Group, an initiative on the 100 most climate-vulnerable territories, and an EU strategic plan on climate resilience in 2028.
- **Origin:** Have Your Say 14770 (consultation closed 23 February 2026); College agenda SEC(2026) 2581, EVP Teresa Ribera. **Not** the EU Adaptation Strategy COM(2021) 82 nor the European Climate Law 2021/1119, both still in force.
- **NOT read:** SWD(2026) 730, 731 and 732, SEC(2026) 730, Annex II onwards of the proposal in full, and sections 3 to 5 of the Communication in full. No rapporteur, committee or Council working-party step exists yet.
