# EU Disability Rights Strategy Post-2024

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Successor to the EU Strategy for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities 2021-2030 (mid-term review and post-2024 update)
- Current strategy: COM(2021) 101 final -- "Union of Equality: Strategy for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities 2021-2030" (3 March 2021)
- Legal foundation: UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (UN CRPD), ratified by the EU in 2010 (Decision 2010/48/EC)
- Treaty basis: Article 19 TFEU (non-discrimination), Article 21 Charter of Fundamental Rights, Article 26 Charter (integration of persons with disabilities)
- Responsible Commissioner: Hadja Lahbib (Equality, Preparedness and Crisis Management). EVP Minzatu (Social Rights) for cross-cutting social package 6 May 2026.
- PETI own-initiative report: 2025/2057(INI) "EU strategy for the rights of persons with disabilities post-2024" -- PETI committee opinion PE773.084 by Pal Szekeres (PfE, Hungary), adopted 17 July 2025; lead committee EMPL
- Upcoming policy product: "Enhancing disability rights strategy to 2030" -- part of Social Package scheduled for College of Commissioners 6 May 2026
- EU Disability Card: Directive (EU) 2024/2841 -- mutual recognition of disability status for short stays in other Member States (adopted October 2024)
- EU Parking Card: Directive (EU) 2024/2842 -- mutual recognition of parking rights for persons with disabilities
- European Accessibility Act: Directive (EU) 2019/882 -- applies from 28 June 2025
- AccessibleEU: EU Resource Centre launched June 2023 to support implementation of accessibility legislation
- EU population with disabilities: approx. 101 million people aged 16+ (roughly 27% of EU population), per Eurostat SILC data
- Focus areas in the current strategy: accessibility, participation, equality, employment, education, social protection, health, external action

## The 2021-2030 Strategy (Context)

The "Union of Equality" strategy launched 3 March 2021 structures EU action around:
1. Accessibility (physical, digital, and services -- anchored by the European Accessibility Act)
2. Enjoying EU rights (disability and parking cards)
3. Decent quality of life and independent living
4. Equal participation and non-discrimination
5. Protection from violence, abuse and neglect
6. Equal opportunities in education, training and employment

The mid-term review (2024-2025) identified persistent gaps in employment (disability employment gap ~25 percentage points), education transitions, deinstitutionalisation progress, and poverty risk.

## PETI Draft Report 2025/2057(INI) -- April 2026

The Committee on Petitions has posted (15 April 2026) a draft INI report on the post-2024 update of the disability rights strategy. PETI receives a large share of all EU petitions on disability access and treatment, giving the committee a unique evidence base.

## Social Package 6 May 2026

EVP Minzatu is preparing the "Social Package" for College adoption on 6 May 2026, which bundles:
- Anti-poverty strategy
- Strengthening of the Child Guarantee
- Council Recommendation on fighting housing exclusion
- Enhancing disability rights strategy to 2030

The enhanced disability rights component is expected to focus on independent living, employment, and access to essential services, responding to UN CRPD Committee concluding observations to the EU.
