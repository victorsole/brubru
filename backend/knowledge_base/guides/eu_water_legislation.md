# EU Water Legislation

## QUICK FACTS
Water Framework Directive (WFD): Directive 2000/60/EC, CELEX 32000L0060
WFD adopted: 23 October 2000
WFD objective: Good ecological and chemical status for all EU water bodies
Current performance: Only 37% of surface waters at good ecological status
Drinking Water Directive (recast): Directive (EU) 2020/2184, CELEX 32020L2184, procedure 2017/0332(COD)
Groundwater Directive: Directive 2006/118/EC, CELEX 32006L0118

Floods Directive: Directive 2007/60/EC, CELEX 32007L0060

Responsible DG: DG ENV (Environment)
Responsible Commissioner: Jessika Roswall (Environment)

## Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC)

### Core Provisions
- Establishes EU-wide framework for water protection and management
- Covers inland, transitional, and coastal surface waters + groundwaters
- Requires River Basin Management Plans (RBMPs) in 6-year cycles
- Programmes of Measures (PoMs) to achieve good status
- Common Implementation Strategy (CIS) with technical working groups
- Priority substances regulation with Environmental Quality Standards (EQS)
- Principle: "polluter pays" and cost recovery for water services

### 2019 Fitness Check
- Commission found WFD directives "broadly fit for purpose"
- Improvements needed in: investments, implementation, chemical pollution controls, digitalisation
- Did NOT propose repeal or major overhaul

### October 2022 Revision Proposal
- Commission proposed revising pollutant lists for surface and groundwater
- More frequent monitoring and data reporting requirements

### March 2026 Targeted Revision
- Commission launched evidence-gathering for "targeted revision of EU water legislation"
- Concerns raised by environmental organisations about closed-door consultations
- Tension between administrative burden reduction/mining expansion vs environmental protection
- Risk of weakening WFD standards under guise of simplification

## Drinking Water Directive (Recast)

- Directive (EU) 2020/2184, CELEX 32020L2184
- Procedure: 2017/0332(COD), completed
- Rapporteurs: Michel Dantin (EPP, France), Christophe Hansen (EPP, Luxembourg)
- Entered into force: 12 January 2021
- Updated quality standards, risk-based approach, improved access to water

## Water Resilience Strategy (December 2025)

COM(2025)280, launched 8 December 2025:
- Over 50 key actions across 3 pillars:
  1. Restore water cycles source to sea
  2. Build water-smart economy
  3. Secure clean affordable water for all
- Recommendation on Water Efficiency First (2025/1179)
- EU-wide goal to improve water efficiency by 2030
- Water Resilience Forum (biennial, first held December 2025)
