# EU safeguard on hybrid car imports: provisional import quotas from 1 December 2026 (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2614)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Act:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2614 of 23 November 2026 imposing a provisional safeguard measure on imports of certain NEW hybrid vehicles. CELEX 32026R2614, http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/2614/oj . Published in the Official Journal on **24 November 2026**.
- **In force? NOT before 1 December 2026.** Article 7: it enters into force on **1 December 2026**. Provisional: **200 days, until 18 June 2027**. Definitive measures may follow within nine months of the investigation's start (30 October 2026), extendable by two months.
- **What it does:** a quantitative restriction. Each country above 2% of EU imports in 2025 gets its own quota (2025 imports plus a little over 15%); all others share a residual quota. **Imports beyond the quotas are NOT allowed** (this is a quota, NOT a tariff or an anti-dumping duty).
- **Products:** full hybrids (HEV) and plug-in hybrids (PHEV), mild hybrids included; new vehicles only (CN 8703 40 10, 8703 50 00, 8703 60 10, 8703 70 00, TARIC subdivisions). Micro hybrids and petrol or diesel cars are NOT under quota; they are under retrospective surveillance (Annex III).
- **Quotas in units (Annex II), three periods: 1 Dec 2026 to 28 Feb 2027 / 1 Mar to 31 May 2027 / 1 to 18 Jun 2027:** China 86,301 / 88,219 / 17,260; Japan 76,900 / 78,609 / 15,380; Türkiye 56,844 / 58,107 / 11,369; United Kingdom 47,431 / 48,485 / 9,486; South Korea 30,566 / 31,245 / 6,113; Mexico 15,640 / 15,987 / 3,128; Morocco 10,779 / 11,019 / 2,156; Serbia 8,709 / 8,902 / 1,742; United States 8,317 / 8,502 / 1,663; all other countries 370 / 378 / 74. South Africa is exempt (developing-country rule).
- **China only:** each import needs an **export quota certificate** from the China Chamber of Commerce for Import and Export of Machinery and Electronic Products (CCCME) (Article 5, Annex IV).
- **Why:** the Commission found an imminent threat of serious injury to EU hybrid-car makers (lost market share in a growing market), driven by global overcapacity and other markets closing (the act cites Canada's quota and Türkiye's 40% duty). It implements the EU-China understanding of 9 October 2026 to moderate Chinese hybrid exports, but applies to ALL origins.
- **Who is affected:** importers of new hybrid cars into the EU; carmakers exporting from Japan, Türkiye, the UK, Korea, Mexico, Morocco, Serbia and the US as well as China. Vehicles brought from Great Britain and sold and registered in Northern Ireland are not subject to the quotas.
- **Related, separate measures:** countervailing duties on battery-electric cars from China (2024, guide on China BEV duties) are a different instrument; a separate safeguard investigation on composite input materials was opened on 23 November 2026.
- **Sources:** the act (read, Articles 5 and 7, recital 126, Annexes II-IV); IP/26/2564, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2564 .
- **What was NOT read:** recitals on injury figures per company. How the quota is managed day to day (first come, first served at customs) follows the EU tariff-quota rules: check Article 4 before answering detail.
