## Overview

The Commission Communication on Integrated Wildfire Risk Management, adopted on 25 March 2026, presents a comprehensive EU strategy to address the rising wildfire threat driven by climate change. 2025 was the worst wildfire season in Europe since records began, with over 1 million hectares burned -- the highest since 2006. The communication brings together civil protection, environmental, agricultural, research, and climate policy in a whole-of-government approach.

## Four Pillars

### 1. Prevention
- Ecosystem restoration and landscape management to reduce fuel loads
- Integration of wildfire prevention into spatial planning
- Guidance on climate adaptation within Natura 2000 protected areas
- Support for prescribed burning and traditional land management practices
- Cooperation with farmers, foresters, and private landowners
- Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) instruments for fire-resilient landscapes

### 2. Preparedness
- Enhanced European Forest Fire Information System (EFFIS) with improved early warning
- AI-assisted wildfire modelling tools
- Updated risk assessment guidelines for Member States
- Pan-European exchange of young firefighters under UCPM by 2027
- Toolkit for resilience building in the wildland-urban interface by 2027
- Pre-positioned firefighting personnel in vulnerable southern European regions
- Awareness campaigns and community involvement (UCPM Knowledge Network)
- Addressing disinformation linked to disasters and wildfires

### 3. Response
- Union Civil Protection Mechanism (UCPM) coordination via Emergency Response Coordination Centre (ERCC)
- RescEU firefighting fleet: 18 airplanes + 4 helicopters (2025 season)
- Fleet expansion: 12 new planes + 5 helicopters on order (Portugal, Spain, France, Italy, Croatia, Greece, Slovakia, Czechia, Romania)
- European firefighting hub in Cyprus (centre of excellence)
- Twinning model for UCPM exchange of experts (2027)
- Evaluation of innovative EU-based technologies for strategic autonomy in wildfire detection and response
- Supporting start-ups in the wildfire technology investment journey

### 4. Recovery
- Health monitoring for firefighters exposed to wildfire smoke and toxins (study by 2028)
- Best practice knowledge sharing on ecosystem restoration post-fire
- Support for climate-resilient ecosystem recovery
- Integration with cohesion policy funds for post-disaster reconstruction

## EFFIS -- European Forest Fire Information System

EFFIS is the EU's wildfire monitoring and information system, operated by the Joint Research Centre (JRC) under the Copernicus Emergency Management Service since 2015. Supported by an expert group of 43 countries (European, Middle East, North African).

Applications:
- Current Situation Viewer 2.0 (real-time fire monitoring)
- Current Statistics Portal (national-level statistics)
- Long-term fire weather forecast (monthly/seasonal temperature and rainfall anomalies)
- Wildfire Risk Viewer
- Data Request Form for researchers

Key findings from EFFIS: Wildfire season now lasts longer than historically. Extreme fire danger conditions in central Europe and Mediterranean are increasing. Most area burned in southern Europe (Portugal, Spain, France, Italy, Greece) but fires increasingly occurring in northern and central Europe.
