# EU Digital Omnibus Package (19 November 2025)

Guide for professionals working on EU digital simplification, AI regulation amendments, GDPR reform, data governance consolidation, cybersecurity reporting, and the broader Digital Fitness Check.

## Overview

On 19 November 2025, the European Commission published a package of four proposals to simplify and consolidate the EU digital rulebook:

| Component | Reference | What It Does |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Digital Omnibus on AI** | COM(2025) 836, procedure 2025/0359(COD) | Amends AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) + civil aviation regulation (Regulation (EU) 2018/1139) |
| **Digital Omnibus on Data & Cybersecurity** | COM(2025) 837 | Amends GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, Data Act, NIS2, CER, Single Digital Gateway, EU institutions data protection |
| **European Data Union Strategy** | -- | Strategy communication |
| **European Business Wallets Regulation** | -- | New regulation proposal |

**IMPORTANT**: The Omnibus proposals do NOT create new standalone regulations. They propose amendments to existing legislation. When users ask about "Digital Omnibus articles", specify WHICH underlying regulation is being amended.

**EPRS Briefing:** BRI(2026)782651, "Digital Omnibus on AI", by Maria Niestadt (9 February 2026)

## Digital Omnibus on AI -- COM(2025) 836

Procedure: 2025/0359(COD) (Ordinary Legislative Procedure)

Amends two regulations: the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) and the civil aviation regulation (Regulation (EU) 2018/1139).

### Key Policy Changes

| Change | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **High-risk AI timeline** | Postponed until harmonised standards available. Annex III: 6 months after Commission confirmation (backstop 2 December 2027). Annex I: 12 months after (backstop 2 August 2028) |
| **AI Office enforcement expanded** | Exclusively competent for: (a) AI systems based on GPAI models (same provider); (b) AI systems in VLOPs/VLOSEs under DSA |
| **AI literacy softened** | From mandatory provider/deployer obligation to Commission/Member State promotional duty |
| **SME regime extended to SMCs** | Small mid-cap enterprises (250-500 employees) get SME-like simplifications throughout |
| **Bias detection data** | New Art. 4a: providers/deployers of ALL AI systems (not just high-risk) may process special category personal data for bias detection, subject to 6 safeguards |
| **EU-level regulatory sandbox** | AI Office to operate an EU-wide sandbox (expected 2028). National sandboxes enhanced with cross-border cooperation |
| **Conformity assessment streamlined** | Single assessment for AI + sectoral legislation. Existing notified bodies get 18-month transition |
| **Agentic AI recognised** | New Annex XIV includes notified body code 0401 for agentic AI systems |
| **Estimated savings** | Up to 429.5 million euro/year in administrative costs |

### Civil Aviation Amendments (7 total)

Arts 27(3), 31(3), 32(3), 36(3), 39(3), 50(3), 53(3) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 -- all require that implementing/delegated acts on airworthiness, air operations, flight crew, aerodromes, ATM/ANS, unmanned aircraft, and other aviation safety account for AI Act Chapter III Section 2 requirements when AI systems are safety components.

## Digital Omnibus on Data & Cybersecurity -- COM(2025) 837

### Regulations Amended

| Regulation | Key Changes |
|-----------|-------------|
| **GDPR** (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) | Clarified personal data definition ("means reasonably likely" to identify); pseudonymisation/anonymisation implementing acts; scientific research as legitimate interest; AI model training on publicly available social media data confirmed as legitimate use (with opt-out); reduced data breach notification burden |
| **ePrivacy Directive** (Directive 2002/58/EC) | ~60% of cookies no longer require consent; mandatory single-click consent/refusal buttons; 6-month re-ask prohibition; machine-readable cookie preference standards; media services exempted from automated cookie preferences |
| **Data Act** (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854) | Consolidates provisions from repealed regulations; scope narrowing for B2G data sharing |
| **NIS2 Directive** (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) | Single Entry Point (SEP) for incident reporting operated by ENISA |
| **CER Directive** (Directive (EU) 2022/2557) | Aligned with NIS2 reporting changes |
| **Single Digital Gateway** (Regulation (EU) 2018/1724) | Updated references |
| **EU institutions data protection** (Regulation (EU) 2018/1725) | Aligned with GDPR amendments |

### Regulations Repealed

| Repealed Regulation | Provisions Moved To |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| Free Flow of Non-Personal Data (Regulation (EU) 2018/1807) | Data Act |
| Platform-to-Business (Regulation (EU) 2019/1150) | Data Act |
| Data Governance Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/868) | Data Act |
| Open Data Directive (Directive (EU) 2019/1024) | Data Act |

### GDPR Amendments Detail

| GDPR Article | Change |
|-------------|--------|
| Art. 4 | Clarified personal data definition: information not personal when entity lacks "means reasonably likely" to identify (reflecting CJEU case law). New "scientific research" definition |
| Art. 5(1)(b) | Further processing for scientific purposes confirmed compatible with initial purpose |
| Art. 6(1)(f) | Scientific research confirmed as legitimate interest; AI model development/operation may use legitimate interest (except where consent required, e.g., DMA Art. 5(2) gatekeepers) |
| Art. 13 | Extended exceptions from information obligation for disproportionate effort in scientific research |

### Cookie/ePrivacy Changes

- ~60% of cookies will no longer require consent
- Lawful processing without consent for: transmission, service provision, audience measurement by media providers, website/app usage statistics, security maintenance
- Browser extensions for automated consent management (opt-out as default); Commission may make these mandatory
- Media services exempted from automated cookie preference provisions (Recital 46 and Art. 88b of COM(2025)837)
- Estimated savings: 820 million euro/year (private sector) + 320 million euro/year (public sector) + 500 million euro/year (user productivity)

### Cybersecurity Single Entry Point

- Single Entry Point (SEP) operated by ENISA at EU level
- Consolidates reporting under NIS2, GDPR, DORA, eIDAS, CER
- Current reporting: ~1,340 incidents at 440 euro per notification
- Estimated savings: up to 41.5 million euro/year (businesses) + 9 million euro one-off + 3.2-7 million euro/year (public authorities)

## Stakeholder Positions

| Actor | Position |
|-------|---------|
| **European Commission** | Targeted simplification preserving policy objectives; phased approach (Omnibus now, Fitness Check later) |
| **European Council** | 20 March, 26 June, 23 October 2025 conclusions call for "ambitious simplification" maintaining "predictability, policy goals, and high standards" |
| **European Parliament** | 11 September 2025 plenary resolution supporting simplification without compromising core objectives |
| **EDPB/EDPS** | Joint Opinion 2/2026 (February 2026): support simplification with reservations on fundamental rights |
| **DIGITALEUROPE** | Welcomes as "necessary first step" but calls it "largely an administrative clean-up". Wants ePrivacy folded into GDPR entirely. Data Act core obligations need revision |
| **Bruegel** | AI omnibus risks prolonging regulatory uncertainty. Sandbox provision should be broadened. Data Act anti-competitive provisions untouched. "EU needs more than a digital omnibus" |
| **EFF / Civil society** | "Guts GDPR Privacy Rights" (4 December 2025). Oppose loosening personal data definition |

## Child Safety Online (March 2026)

On 5 March 2026, the Commission held the **first meeting of the Special Panel on Child Safety Online**, hosted by President von der Leyen (announced in the 2025 State of the Union).

The Panel provides expert recommendations on:
- Better protecting and empowering children online
- Potential harmonised age restrictions to access social media
- Links to the DSA Guidelines on Protection of Minors, the BIK+ Strategy, and the Cyberbullying Action Plan
- EU Age Verification solution development

This complements the proposed regulation on preventing and combating child sexual abuse online (CSAM regulation, 2022/0155(COD)), which is still under negotiation between Parliament and Council.
