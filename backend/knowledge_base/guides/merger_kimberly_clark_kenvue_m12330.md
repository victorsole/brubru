# Kimberly-Clark / Kenvue: Commission clears the acquisition with conditions (13 October 2026, case M.12330)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Decision:** on **13 October 2026** the European Commission approved, under the **EU Merger Regulation**, the proposed acquisition of **Kenvue Inc** by **Kimberly-Clark Corporation**, **conditional on full compliance with Kimberly-Clark's commitments** (press release IP/26/2137; case number **M.12330**).
- **Procedure:** notified on **25 August 2026**; cleared in **Phase I with commitments** after **35 working days** (25 working days plus 10 when commitments are offered). No in-depth (Phase II) investigation.
- **Competition concern:** the transaction, as notified, would reduce competition in **tampons**, **in particular in Czechia, Estonia and Slovakia**, by removing an important competitor where few suppliers are present.
- **Remedy:** Kimberly-Clark will **divest all its tampon and other feminine hygiene brands in the European Economic Area**, with intellectual property, know-how and key employees and relationships: **Kotex (including the Lidie sub-brand), Camelia and Record**. This removes the horizontal overlap in the EEA.
- **Purchaser:** the Commission approved **Dilek Gıda Üretim ve Pazarlama Ticaret A.Ş. (Turkey)** as buyer of the divested business after reviewing its business plan with an independent adviser. An **independent trustee** monitors implementation.
- **Companies:** Kimberly-Clark (US) makes personal care products; brands include **Huggies and Kotex**. Kenvue (US) makes consumer health products; brands include **Listerine, Nicorette, Neutrogena and o.b**.
- **The press release gives no deal value:** do not state one.
- **Public record:** the decision text and case file appear in the Commission's competition case register under M.12330.

## How to answer
"Was the Kimberly-Clark / Kenvue deal approved?": yes, on 13 October 2026, subject to divesting the Kotex, Camelia and Record feminine hygiene brands in the EEA to Dilek. It concerned tampons in Czechia, Estonia and Slovakia. Not blocked, not a Phase II case.
