# EIC Pathfinder — Administrative Process

## QUICK FACTS
- Funds **breakthrough deep-tech research** at **TRL 1-4** (proof of concept stage, "science-towards-technology").
- Two strands:
  - **Pathfinder Open** — any field of science / technology, no thematic priorities. 2026 budget **EUR 166M**. Deadline **12 May 2026** (single-stage).
  - **Pathfinder Challenges** — predefined thematic areas set by EIC Programme Managers. 2026 budget **EUR 96M**. Deadline **28 October 2026**.
- Total 2026 Pathfinder budget: **EUR 262M**.
- Max grant: **up to EUR 4 million** per project (more if justified) + optional **Booster grants of fixed EUR 50,000** for additional commercial/IP exploration.
- Project duration: typically **up to 36-48 months**.
- Funding rate: **100% of eligible costs** + 25% indirect-cost flat rate (Horizon Europe RIA standard).
- Topic IDs: `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PATHFINDEROPEN-01`, `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PATHFINDERCHALLENGES-01..n`.

## Eligibility

**Pathfinder Open** requires a consortium of **at least 3 independent legal entities** established in 3 different Member States / Associated Countries:
- at least 1 entity established in a Member State
- at least 2 others in different MS or Associated Countries
- Beneficiary types: research organisations, universities, SMEs, large companies, NGOs, etc.

**Pathfinder Challenges** allows additionally:
- Single applicants (mono-beneficiary)
- Small consortia of 2 partners

This single-applicant carve-out makes Challenges accessible to deeptech start-ups, university spinouts and individual research labs.

## Evaluation criteria

Proposals are evaluated on the standard Horizon Europe **Excellence / Impact / Implementation** framework, with Pathfinder-specific emphasis on:

**Essential characteristics:**
- Convincing long-term vision of a radically new technology
- Concrete, novel and ambitious science-towards-technology breakthrough
- High-risk / high-gain research approach and methodology with concrete, plausible objectives

For Challenges, an additional criterion checks alignment with the Programme Manager's challenge scope.

Scoring: 0-5 per criterion (half-points allowed). 12/15 typical threshold.

## Application — single-stage process

Unlike Accelerator and AIC, Pathfinder is a **single-stage submission**:

1. **Proposal submitted via Funding & Tenders Portal** by the deadline (5pm Brussels CET).
2. **Eligibility + admissibility check** by EISMEA (≤ 1 month).
3. **Remote evaluation** by 3-5 independent experts. Consensus meeting produces an Evaluation Summary Report (ESR).
4. **Ranking + selection** based on ESR scores.
5. **Information letter** (above / below threshold) issued to coordinator.
6. **Grant Agreement Preparation (GAP)** — 3-5 months from invitation to signature.
7. **Grant Agreement signature** under the Horizon Europe Model Grant Agreement (MGA).
8. **Pre-financing** disbursed (typically 50%) within 30 days of signature.

Typical "time to grant" KPI: 8 months from deadline to GA signature.

## Templates to prepare

Standard Horizon Europe RIA templates apply:

- **Application Form Part A** — administrative info, generated by the Submission Service portal
- **Application Form Part B** — technical proposal (template DOCX from the call topic page). Standard Pathfinder Part B is ~45 pages (Excellence / Impact / Implementation sections).
- **Ethics Self-Assessment** annex (if research involves humans, animals, personal data, dual-use, etc.)
- **Security Self-Assessment** (if dual-use considerations apply)
- **Letters of support** (optional but recommended for Challenges)
- For **Booster grants**: a short supplementary application (post-GA) describing the additional exploitation activity (typically a 2-page form)

## Pathfinder Challenges — 2026 themes

The Programme Managers set 4-6 Challenges per year. 2026 Challenge themes are listed on the call topic page (Topic IDs `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PATHFINDERCHALLENGES-01..06`). Historical examples include:
- Solar-to-X (renewable hydrogen, ammonia, methanol)
- AI for scientific modelling
- Cellular and gene therapy
- Quantum sensing & metrology
- Carbon dioxide and nitrogen management
- AI-enhanced biological and chemical screening

Current 2026 Challenge list is published at the topic page; check the 2026 Work Programme decision (Annex I) for full descriptions.

## Pathfinder Booster

Once a Pathfinder project is underway (or completed), beneficiaries can apply for a **Booster grant** (fixed EUR 50,000 lump sum) to:
- Conduct additional market analysis
- Run IP / FTO exploration
- Engage with investors
- Develop a technology-transfer plan

Boosters are submitted via the portal under a continuously-open call. Decision typically within 6 weeks.

## Eligible costs

- Personnel
- Subcontracting (max 30% of eligible direct costs)
- Travel, equipment depreciation, consumables
- Indirect costs: 25% flat rate
- Lump sum option in some calls (single payment on completion of work packages)

## After the grant

- Pathfinder beneficiaries are encouraged to **graduate to Transition** (TRL 4 → 5/6 maturation) once results are sufficiently advanced.
- Pathfinder Beneficiaries automatically eligible for the **EIC Transition Open** call (one of the eligible upstream programmes alongside ERC PoC, RIA, EDF civil research).
- Booster grants available throughout for IP / market exploration.
