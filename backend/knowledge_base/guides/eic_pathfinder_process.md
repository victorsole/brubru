# EIC Pathfinder — Administrative Process (2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- Funds **high-risk, science-towards-technology breakthrough research** at low TRLs (1 to 4): the output is a proof of principle that validates the scientific basis of a future technology.
- **Pathfinder Open:** any field. 2026 budget **EUR 166 million**. Deadline **12 May 2026** (closed). Portal topic `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PATHFINDEROPEN`.
- **Pathfinder Challenges:** three 2026 Challenges, budget **EUR 96 million** in roughly equal shares: **Advanced Materials for Miniaturised Energy Harvesting Systems**; **Biotechnology for Healthy Ageing**; **DeepRAP: Deep Reasoning, Abstraction & Planning towards trustworthy Cognitive AI Systems**. Deadline **28 October 2026, 17:00 Brussels**. Portal topics `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PATHFINDERCHALLENGES-01-01`, `-01-02`, `-01-03`.
- **Grant:** up to **EUR 4 million** per project considered appropriate (more if justified); **100% funding rate**; the grant is a **LUMP SUM** fixed during evaluation from the applicant's own cost estimate (NOT an actual-cost grant with a 25% flat rate).
- **Who:** Open = consortium of **at least 3 independent entities in 3 different countries** (at least one in a Member State, the others in Member States or Associated Countries). Challenges = single entities **or** consortia (2 entities from 2 different Member States/Associated Countries, or 3+ under the Open rule); single-beneficiary projects exclude mid-caps and larger companies.
- **Page limit (Part B, sections 1-3):** **22 pages for Open, 30 pages for Challenges.**
- **Scoring:** each criterion out of 5 with thresholds **Excellence 4/5 (weight 50%), Impact 3.5/5 (weight 30%), Implementation 3/5 (weight 20%)**; at least three individual evaluators, then an evaluation committee (Open: different experts; Challenges: experts + EIC Programme Managers, plus a portfolio step).
- **Timing:** result about **5 months** after the deadline; grant agreement signed about **8 months** after the deadline (Challenges: grant preparation expected within three months of the selection letter).
- Pathfinder projects can get **Booster grants of up to EUR 50,000**, apply to EIC Transition, and use the **Fast Track** to the EIC Accelerator full proposal.

## Pathfinder Open in detail
- **Gatekeepers** (check before applying): a convincing long-term vision of a radically new technology; a concrete, novel and ambitious science-towards-technology breakthrough; a high-risk/high-gain approach with concrete, plausible objectives. Incremental work is not supported.
- **Expected:** proof of principle, top-level open-access publications, formal IP protection, an exploitation plan and an assessment of regulation, certification and standardisation. Involving early-career researchers and high-tech SMEs/start-ups is encouraged, as is gender balance among work-package leaders.
- **Award criteria:** Excellence (long-term vision; breakthrough; objectives and methodology including gender dimension and open science; interdisciplinarity), Impact (long-term impact; innovation potential and IP protection; communication and dissemination), Implementation (work plan and risks; resources; consortium). Ties: Excellence score, then Impact, then gender balance of work-package leaders, number of SMEs, number of countries.
- Proposals within Euratom scope go to Euratom calls. Restrictions apply to topics on European communication networks.

## Pathfinder Challenges in detail
- Each Challenge builds a **portfolio** steered by an EIC Programme Manager with a common roadmap; projects must interact. Advised: a dedicated portfolio work package of at least 10 person-months.
- Typical starting point TRL 2, ending at proof of concept or lab validation (TRL 3-4).
- **Two-step evaluation:** (1) each proposal scored against the criteria (Excellence: objectives and relevance to the Challenge, novelty, methodology; Impact: pathways to the Challenge outcomes, innovation potential, communication; Implementation); (2) the evaluation committee selects a coherent portfolio among proposals above threshold (categories such as building blocks, competing technologies, application areas, risk level).
- Read the topic's **Challenge Guide** on the EIC website before applying.

## Booster grants
Fixed amounts not exceeding **EUR 50,000** for Pathfinder (and Transition) projects, to explore pathways to commercialisation or for portfolio activities (Work Programme Annex 5).

## After Pathfinder
- **EIC Transition:** Pathfinder results at TRL 3-4 are eligible base projects (see `eic_transition_process`).
- **EIC Accelerator Fast Track:** Pathfinder and Transition projects can be reviewed for direct access to the Accelerator full proposal.
- **Next Generation Innovation Talents:** researchers' internships in EIC/EIT start-ups; their personnel costs are eligible under the Pathfinder grant.
