# Vision 2040 for fisheries and aquaculture, COM(2026) 701 (adopted 20 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **ADOPTED (Tuesday 20 October 2026): the Commission Communication "Vision 2040 for fisheries and aquaculture", COM(2026) 701 (listed on the Commission's document register under DG MARE on 20 October; Cellar did not yet serve CELEX 52026DC0701 that day, so there is no EUR-Lex link until it lands).** Press release IP/26/2200, questions and answers QANDA/26/2199, factsheet FS/26/2201. Commissioner: Costas Kadis (fisheries and oceans). It is a strategy, not a statute: the Communication itself creates no obligation on fishers, farms or companies.
- **Origin:** a deliverable of the European Ocean Pact (June 2025) and of Commissioner Kadis's mission letter ("a vision for the fisheries sector with a 2040 perspective"); it incorporates the Energy Transition Roadmap for fisheries and aquaculture and the EU strategy for fisheries external action, and builds on the evaluation of the Common Fisheries Policy (CFP) Regulation, which found some progress on overfishing but no improvement in fleet economic performance and stagnating aquaculture.
- **Sector size (press release):** more than 200 000 direct jobs, nearly 124 500 in fisheries and almost 76 000 in aquaculture.
- **Priorities, as the press release states them:** (1) healthy ecosystems: the landing obligation "has not delivered the expected results" in monitoring unwanted catches; the Commission will explore technological innovation and fully documented fisheries through an implementation dialogue with stakeholders later in 2026; (2) energy transition: until 2030 energy and resource efficiency, new technologies and available clean fuels; from 2030 scaling low and zero-emission technologies; better definition of effective harvesting capacity and an update of the 2014 guidelines on the balance between fishing capacity and opportunities; (3) supply: support for more efficient and safe vessels, innovation and income diversification (pescatourism); for aquaculture, a legislative instrument and a more coherent regulatory framework; (4) demand: digital traceability for all products across the supply chain by 2029; (5) conditions for success: a proposed EUR 2 billion minimum reserved allocation for core CFP functions in the 2028-2034 long-term budget under National and Regional Partnership Plans, support from the European Competitiveness Fund, the future Ocean Act for small-scale fishers, harmonised EU training and certification standards, and a Blue Generational Renewal Strategy to be launched in 2027; (6) external action: fight against illegal, unreported and unregulated (IUU) fishing, stronger international rules, a new generation of Sustainable Fisheries Partnership Agreements (SFPAs), a level playing field against unfair competition.
- **Next step named by the Commission:** Member States and national and regional authorities are invited to translate the Vision into tailored measures.
