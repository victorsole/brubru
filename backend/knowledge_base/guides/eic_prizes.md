# EIC Prizes — Recognition Programmes

## QUICK FACTS
- Two active EIC Prize streams in 2026:
  - **European Capital of Innovation Awards** ("iCapital") — annual recognition for European cities most effectively promoting innovation
  - **European Prize for Women Innovators (powered by EIC + EIT)** — annual recognition for women entrepreneurs
- Cash prizes funded from the Horizon Europe budget under the EIC Pillar III envelope.
- Submitted via the Funding & Tenders Portal.
- Topic ID prefix: `HORIZON-EIC-2026-PRIZE-*`.

## European Capital of Innovation Awards ("iCapital")

### Prizes (2027 edition, opening 5 May 2026)

**European Capital of Innovation** (cities with population ≥ 250,000):
- Winner: **EUR 1,000,000**
- 2 Runners-up: **EUR 100,000** each

**European Rising Innovative City** (cities with population 50,000 – 249,999):
- Winner: **EUR 500,000**
- 2 Runners-up: **EUR 50,000** each

### Eligibility

- Minimum 50,000 inhabitants
- Located in an EU Member State or Horizon Europe Associated Country
- Apply to ONE category only (Capital OR Rising)
- Previous winners and 2023 runners-up NOT eligible
- Previous finalists (other than 2023 runners-up) may apply

### Six award criteria

1. **Experimenting** — testing innovation policies, regulatory sandboxes, pilots
2. **Escalating** — scaling proven innovations
3. **Ecosystem building** — collaboration between sectors, public-private-academic
4. **Expanding** — outreach to underrepresented groups
5. **City innovative vision** — strategic positioning + long-term roadmap
6. **Citizens' rights** — democratic participation, digital + social inclusion

### Jury

Composed of senior EU innovation policy experts, mayors of past winning cities, academics, and ecosystem leaders. Specific 2026 jury composition published with the call documentation.

### Templates

## European Prize for Women Innovators (EIC + EIT)

### Three categories + prize money

**EIC Women Innovators** (open):
- 1st: EUR 100,000 · 2nd: EUR 70,000 · 3rd: EUR 50,000

**EIC Rising Innovators** (under 35 at call start):
- 1st: EUR 50,000 · 2nd: EUR 30,000 · 3rd: EUR 20,000

**EIT Women Leadership Award** (powered by EIT, separate stream):
- Winner: EUR 50,000 · Runner-up: EUR 30,000 · Runner-up: EUR 20,000

### Eligibility

- **Female natural person** (not a legal entity)
- Legally established in an EU Member State, OCT, or Horizon Europe Associated Country
- **Founder OR co-founder** of the company/organisation
- Company/organisation must be established in the EU/OCT/AC and **registered or incorporated at least 2 years before the call year**
- Cannot have previously received an EU or Euratom prize for identical activities
- Rising Innovators applicants: **under 35** at call start

### Evaluation criteria (3 dimensions)

1. **Breakthrough innovation** — in deep-tech / STEM fields
2. **Impact** — addressing genuine societal/environmental challenges
3. **Inspiration** — pivotal leadership, role-model status
