## About the ART

The Analysis and Research Team sits within the General Secretariat of the Council of the European Union. It produces independent strategic analysis to support the Council's deliberations. Unlike EPRS (which serves Parliament) or JRC (which serves the Commission), ART papers support the Council Presidency and Member State delegations.

ART papers are often more geopolitically focused than EPRS briefings, reflecting the Council's role in foreign affairs and strategic direction-setting.

## Recent Publications

### Forward Look 2026: Playing by new rules?
- Explores how established assumptions in global power dynamics, economic stability, and security are being challenged
- Analyses how the EU may need to adapt its playbook: from conflict and hybrid threats to competitiveness and climate change
- Annual flagship publication (published each January/February)

### Bridging Water Extremes: Can floods alleviate droughts?
- Examines the intersection of flood and drought management in the EU
- Water moves up the strategic agenda as extreme events increase
- Practical assessment of integrated water management approaches

### Prospects for 2026: what others are saying
- Compiles key findings from 35 international outlook reports
- Covers: society, technology, economy, environment, geopolitics
- Highlights 10 conflicts expected to shape 2026
- Companion document to the Forward Look
