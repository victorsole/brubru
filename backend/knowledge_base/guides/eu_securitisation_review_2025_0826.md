# EU Securitisation Review 2025/0826(COD) and 2025/0825(COD): provisional agreement of 21 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Wednesday 21 October 2026, 23:17, Council press release "Savings and investments union: Council and Parliament strike deal to revitalise the EU's securitisation market"): provisional political agreement between the Council and the European Parliament on the two securitisation proposals.** NOT yet law: the legal texts must still be finalised and formally endorsed by both co-legislators, then adopted and published in the Official Journal. The agreed text was NOT read (not published); this guide carries the Council release and the Legislative Observatory (OEIL) files only.
- **Two procedures, one package (Commission proposals of 17 June 2025):** (1) **2025/0826(COD)**, COM(2025) 826, amending **Regulation (EU) 2017/2402** (the Securitisation Regulation, CELEX 32017R2402, with the simple, transparent and standardised (STS) framework); (2) **2025/0825(COD)**, COM(2025) 825, amending **Regulation (EU) No 575/2013** (the Capital Requirements Regulation, CELEX 32013R0575) for banks' securitisation exposures. Both files have the same ECON rapporteur and timetable (OEIL): committee vote 5 May 2026, reports A10-0137/2026 (2025/0825) and A10-0138/2026 (2025/0826), mandates confirmed by plenary 21 May 2026.
- **Parliament, 2025/0826(COD):** lead committee **ECON**, rapporteur **Ralf Seekatz (EPP, Germany)**, appointed 1 July 2025; committee vote **5 May 2026**; report **A10-0138/2026** tabled 8 May 2026; mandate for negotiations confirmed by plenary under Rule 72 on **21 May 2026**. OEIL status on 22 October 2026: "Awaiting Parliament's position in 1st reading" (the plenary vote on the final text comes after the agreement).
- **What the deal does (Council release, 21 Oct 2026):** re-calibrates banks' capital requirements for securitisation positions on a risk-sensitive basis (materially lower for low-risk positions, higher kept for riskier ones); creates a new **"resilient" category** of securitisations alongside the strengthened STS label; sets **EU-level supervision of third-party STS verifiers**; cuts administrative burdens for investors; **raises UCITS investment limits** in securitisations; targeted STS changes to allow insurers and reinsurers into synthetic securitisation and to finance projects before their operational phase. Figures (capital relief in euros, thresholds) were NOT given in the release.
- **Why:** part of the Savings and Investments Union (SIU) and of the "One Europe, One Market" roadmap; aim: free up bank capital and channel lending to households, SMEs and infrastructure.
- **Next steps:** technical finalisation, Coreper endorsement, ECON vote on the agreed text, plenary first-reading vote, Council adoption, OJ publication. No dates announced.

## Context
The Securitisation Regulation (EU) 2017/2402 has applied since 1 January 2019. The Commission's June 2025 package answered the Draghi and Letta reports' call to revive the EU securitisation market, which stayed far below its pre-2008 size.
