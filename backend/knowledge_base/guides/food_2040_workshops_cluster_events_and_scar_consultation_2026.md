# Food 2040: stakeholder workshop, project cluster workshops and the SCAR consultation (2025-2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** SWD(2026) 271 final, section 5 (and 5.2.1 to 5.3). These steps fed Food 2040 alongside the survey in `building_food_2040_stakeholder_input_2026`.
- **Food 2040 stakeholder workshop, 25 March 2026, Brussels:** **80 participants** (academia and research organisations, public institutions, interest groups including European Technology Platforms, private sector especially SMEs and startups). Conclusions: Food 2030 is "**essential but no longer sufficient**"; keep the systems logic but make it **operational, measurable and aligned with current risks**; **governance** was dominant (standalone priority or foundational layer, debated); digitalisation and AI are key enablers but should not be vague solutions; merge, broaden or reframe pathways; reinforce the **territorial** perspective (rural, regional, local alongside urban); more on implementation, **scale-up and competitiveness**, **food manufacturing and processing**, market uptake, demonstration infrastructure and skills; stronger **health, diets, food environments, protein diversification**, circularity, zero pollution, strategic autonomy, resilience, global role.
- **Project cluster workshops (Food 2030 projects):** food safety and food fraud, **10 September 2025**; food waste and resource-efficient food systems, **24 October 2025**; **urban food systems, 10 March 2026** (nearly 20 projects; needs: multi-level governance with political commitment, links between local innovation and national or EU policy, monitoring, trustworthy citizen-centred digitalisation); **food from the ocean and freshwater, 16 March 2026** (17 projects; needs: traceability and fully digitalised catch documentation, research on climate and natural risks to aquatic value chains). Further workshops: healthy diet and nutrition, alternative proteins, microbiome.
- **EU survey:** more than 600 responses and 63 written contributions; research and academia 52%; **57.6%** unaware of Food 2030 (citizens 44.1%); 59 of 63 written submissions flagged areas beyond the existing pathways.
- **SCAR (Standing Committee on Agricultural Research):** Member States and associated countries (more than 35); plenary twice a year; working groups 2016-2026 include Agroecology, AKIS (since 2010), Bioeconomy, Food Systems, Forest, Sustainable Animal Production, Animal Health and Welfare, Proteins, Foresight, Startup and Scaleup. **Consultation workshop 27 February 2026** (four breakouts: thematic priorities; uptake; Food 2040 priorities and pathways; valorisation into startups and scaleups). **Task force on startups and scaleups set up January 2026** (15 countries). The **5th SCAR Foresight Report** is "Resilience and Transformation".
