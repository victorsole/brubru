# AgRI 2040 area 3 of 8: livestock systems (animal health and welfare)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** third of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). The Commission web page words it "livestock systems, animal health and welfare". Preliminary, no obligation. It **contributes to the objectives of the livestock strategy**.
- **Purpose:** sustainability, competitiveness, excellence, **territoriality** and resilience of livestock systems; safeguard **animal health and welfare**, reduce the environmental impact of the sector, promote **precision farming**.
- **Breeding and feed:** impact-driven animal breeding for multiple purposes "with due consideration of the legal and policy context"; sustainable **feed and nutrients** including **vitamins and amino acids**.
- **Health:** **biosecurity** through a multidisciplinary approach; new interventions, treatments and **vaccines** for priority animal diseases including **zoonoses**; **prudent use of antimicrobials**; integrated approaches combining prevention, control and emergency preparedness, including the socio-economic dimension.
- **Resource efficiency:** innovative feed measures such as **grass-based and pasture systems** and better feed conversion; safeguarding biodiversity while reducing land pressure, for example **agropastoralism**; mitigating emissions and waste through circular-economy principles.
- **Structural changes to address systemically:** new **alternative proteins**, climate change, geopolitical change.
- **Related research:** the European Partnership for Animal Health and Welfare (EUPAHW; EUR 360 million EU over ten years, 55 projects in 2026).
