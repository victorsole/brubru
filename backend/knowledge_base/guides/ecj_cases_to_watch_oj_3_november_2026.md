# EU Court cases to watch, from the Official Journal of 3 November 2026: Temu, Bank of Russia, Hungary, Romania, Austria v Germany, Italian citizenship

## QUICK FACTS
All are **PENDING actions or references, read from their OJ C notices of 3 November 2026. A notice gives the applicant's claims, NOT the Court's view: say "argues", never "the Court found".** This is a selection of six from about 60 Court notices that day.
1. **Temu against its DSA fine: Cases T-535/26 (PDD Holdings Inc., Cayman Islands, which argues it was never designated under the DSA) and T-536/26 (Whaleco Technology Ltd, Dublin), General Court, lodged 12 August 2026.** Whaleco is NOT described as a parent company. They seek annulment of the Commission decision of 28 May 2026, C(2026) 3646 final, or annulment in full or in part of the **EUR 200 million** fine. Seven pleas each: rights of defence and access to file; liability of a parent never designated under the DSA; the "single economic unit"; Article 34 turned into an outcome duty and de facto general monitoring; burden of proof; errors of fact; a disproportionate fine. No judgment. See `dsa_enforcement`.
2. **Bank of Russia v Council: Case T-613/26, lodged 15 September 2026.** Seeks annulment of Article 1(28) of Regulation (EU) 2026/1848 of 23 July 2026, the new Article 11ca of Regulation 833/2014 on injunctions against recognising certain foreign rulings. Eight pleas, including ultra vires under Article 215(2) TFEU, extraterritorial effect, misuse of power, effective remedy, property. (The notice's form of order misprints the number as "2016/1848"; its pleas and footnote say 2026/1848.)
3. **Commission v Hungary: Case C-825/26, lodged 27 July 2026.** Hungary makes refusal mandatory where Article 4(1) of the European arrest warrant Framework Decision gives courts a discretion. See `european_arrest_warrant`.
4. **Commission v Romania: Case C-1018/26, lodged 16 September 2026.** The national health insurance fund allegedly pays pharmacies late, systematically and persistently, against Article 4(4)(b) of Directive 2011/7/EU. See `late_payments_regulation`.
5. **Austria v Germany: Case C-962/26, lodged 27 August 2026.** A dispute between two Member States under their double taxation convention, on the capital allocated to BTV's German branch. Neither the Commission nor a company is the party.
6. **Picuso: Case C-816/26, from the Italian Constitutional Court, lodged 23 July 2026.** Do Article 9 TEU and Article 20 TFEU preclude Italy's 2025 rule (Article 3-bis of Law 91/1992), which bars from the outset Italian nationality for people born abroad who hold another nationality, unless listed conditions apply?
- **Judgments announced for 5 November 2026 (the Court's own week-ahead post of 30 October; NOT read, no outcome known):** C-395/25, liquid detergent packaging that looks like a drink (Sweden); C-495/25, CBD products and organic certification (France).
