# Border and migration package of 27 October 2026: Frontex recast COM(2026) 1030 and return digitalisation COM(2026) 1037

## QUICK FACTS
- **STATUS (read 12:40 CET, Tuesday 27 October 2026): ADOPTED by the College on 27 October 2026 as TWO PROPOSALS for Regulations. Neither is law, neither is in force: both go to Parliament and Council under the ordinary legislative procedure.** Press release IP/26/2260 https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2260 ; Q&A QANDA/26/2261 ; factsheet FS/26/2263. Commissioner: Magnus Brunner (Internal Affairs and Migration); EVP Henna Virkkunen's cluster.
- **Proposal 1 (owner: the Commission document register, read 12:38 CET): COM(2026) 1030, "Regulation on the European Border and Coast Guard (recast)", DG HOME, main document plus one annex. It RECASTS the Frontex Regulation (EU) 2019/1896, NOT a mere amendment.** Its procedure number is NOT yet known (2026/0340(COD) belongs to COM(2026) 1037 below, NOT to the Frontex recast); its text was NOT read: DG HOME page 403, Cellar 404, EUR-Lex wall, register file not attached. Register entry: https://ec.europa.eu/transparency/documents-register/detail?ref=COM(2026)1030 . Frontex facts below come from IP/26/2260 and QANDA/26/2261, NOT from the articles.
- **What the press release says the Frontex recast does:** standing corps gradually raised to **30,000** = Frontex's own staff doubled to **6,000** + a new **European Border and Return Reserve of 17,000** national officers at Member States' disposal + the existing **7,000** seconded officers, phased to 2041; Frontex may acquire, lease, co-own and jointly procure equipment and act as a central purchasing body (also AI tools); a **Return Coordination Centre** (Member States report indicative return planning by 1 November each year); Frontex may organise returns from a third country to another third country, only under an EU-level agreement with that country and subject to non-refoulement; an **Online Monitoring and Response Centre** monitoring public social media, Frontex as a trusted flagger under the Digital Services Act; a **Quick Reaction Force** deployed by the Executive Director on a Member State's request for at most one month; Member States report events of significant impact within 24 hours in EUROSUR. Budget: EUR 11.8 billion proposed for Frontex in the 2028-2034 MFF (about EUR 6 billion in 2021-2027).
- **Proposal 2 (read from its PDF, 87 pages): COM(2026) 1037 final, procedure 2026/0340(COD), "Regulation on the digitalisation of case management in the area of return, readmission and reintegration, and amending Regulations (EU) 2018/1726, 2018/1860, 2019/817 and 2019/818"**, legal basis Articles 79(2)(c) and 77(2)(b) and (d) TFEU, 38 articles, with SEC(2026) 1037 and SWD(2026) 1037-1039. PDF: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/611b2106-f71a-456a-acee-55c5b960a747_en . Art. 3: one **national return case management system (national RECAMAS)** per Member State, an integrated Union **readmission** system (iRCMS) and a Union **reintegration** system (RIAT), the European Search Portal and the Central Repository for Reporting and Statistics of the interoperability Regulations, and a **communication portal** (Art. 14) built by eu-LISA; Frontex leads the development (Art. 15), eu-LISA the portal; **Art. 33: national RECAMAS in place four years after entry into force**; Art. 31 Member States bear national costs, the EU budget the Frontex systems; Art. 38 in force on the twentieth day after OJ publication. It operationalises the Return Regulation (EU) 2026/2280, applied since 16 October 2026 (guide `return_regulation_2026_2280_entry_into_force`).
- **Cellar had neither COM number at 12:38 CET; OEIL listed no procedure page.** In force today: Frontex Regulation (EU) 2019/1896 (`frontex_agency_overview`), the Pact on Migration and Asylum (`eu_migration_asylum_pact`), the Return Regulation 2026/2280.
- **NOT read:** the Frontex recast itself (COM(2026) 1030) and its annex; SWD/SEC documents of both proposals; Annexes of COM(2026) 1037; the factsheet FS/26/2263 (API body empty).
