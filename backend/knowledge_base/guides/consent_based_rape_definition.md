- Article 5 (Rape) -- the flashpoint: the Commission's 2022 proposal (COM(2022) 105) included a consent-based definition. Final text: Article 5 criminalises cyber-offences but rape definition remains national competence

## Member State Practice (Consent-Based Divergence)

As of April 2026, consent-based rape definitions exist in:
- **Consent-based (yes-means-yes / affirmative consent)**: Sweden (2018), Spain (2022), Greece (2019), Ireland, UK (England+Wales), Belgium (2022 penal code revision), Germany (2016 reform -- "No means No"), Netherlands (2024), Slovenia, Croatia, Luxembourg, Finland (2023), Malta (partial), Denmark (2021)
- **Coercion-based (still requires force/threat)**: Bulgaria, Czechia, Hungary, Italy (consent reform debated), Latvia, Lithuania, Poland (reform debated 2024-), Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Estonia, Austria (partial)
- **Hybrid / evolving**: France (consent debate active, 2025 legislative proposal), Cyprus

## EPRS Key Arguments for Consent-Based Definition

1. **Istanbul Convention compliance**: Article 36 explicitly requires consent-based approach; several MS technically non-compliant despite ratification
2. **Victim-centred justice**: removes burden of proving force/resistance; reflects psychological immobilisation ("frozen fright" response)
3. **Conviction rates**: jurisdictions that moved to consent-based saw significant increases (Sweden: +75% reporting; Spain: +27% convictions in first year post-2022 reform)
4. **Fundamental rights**: Article 1 (human dignity), Article 3 (right to integrity), Article 21 (non-discrimination) of Charter of Fundamental Rights
5. **ECHR case law**: M.C. v. Bulgaria (2003) already requires consent-focused investigation and prosecution
6. **EU competence**: while Article 83(1) TFEU list does not currently include rape, EP has called for its addition via Article 83(1)(3) procedure (requires unanimity in Council)

## Path Forward

- **Short term**: Commission monitoring transposition of Directive (EU) 2024/1385 (deadline 14 June 2027); infringement procedures possible for MS with deficient consent protection
- **Medium term**: EP FEMM is expected to push for Council to activate Article 83(1)(3) TFEU to add rape to EU crimes list (unanimity required)
- **Long term**: revision of Directive (EU) 2024/1385 once EU crime list updated; consent-based definition via Article 5 amendment

## Brubru User Questions Addressed

- "Is rape defined consent-based at EU level?" -- No. Currently Member State competence; Directive (EU) 2024/1385 does not harmonise definition
- "Why was the consent-based definition removed from the Directive?" -- Council legal service found Article 83(1) TFEU list does not include rape
- "Which MS use consent-based definition?" -- 14+ MS including Sweden, Spain, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands
- "Is the EU compliant with Istanbul Convention?" -- EU itself is a party since 2023; MS must comply via Article 36 of the Convention
- "Can the EU add rape to the list of EU crimes?" -- Yes, via Article 83(1)(3) TFEU, which requires Council unanimity
