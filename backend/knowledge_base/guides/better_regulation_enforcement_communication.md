## The "Better Regulation and Enforcement" Communication

Von der Leyen's second Commission positioned **simplification + enforcement** as a unified pillar of its "Competitiveness Compass" (29 January 2025, COM(2025) 30). Dombrovskis's expanded portfolio -- announced in the November 2024 hearings -- combines three legacy responsibilities previously split across Maros Sefcovic, Valdis Dombrovskis's own economic brief, and Didier Reynders's rule of law cluster:

1. **Regulatory design**: Better Regulation Guidelines, Toolbox, impact assessments, public consultation governance (legacy: Sefcovic).
2. **Stock simplification**: REFIT, Omnibus files, regulatory burden reduction, SME test (legacy: Breton + Dombrovskis).
3. **Implementation + enforcement**: Article 258 TFEU infringement procedures, transposition monitoring, annual EU law application report (legacy: Reynders's cluster -- now consolidated).

The 28 April Communication is expected to set targets for all three strands and explain how simplification pledges (e.g. "25% administrative burden reduction" and "35% for SMEs" repeated in Council conclusions since December 2024) will be measured and delivered.
