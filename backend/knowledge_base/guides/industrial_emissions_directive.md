# Industrial Emissions Directive 2.0 (Directive (EU) 2024/1785) and the Commission Q&A

## QUICK FACTS
- **The act**: Directive (EU) 2024/1785 of 24 April 2024 **amending Directive 2010/75/EU** on industrial emissions and **Council Directive 1999/31/EC** on the landfill of waste. CELEX `32024L1785`, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1785/oj . Published OJ 15 July 2024, **in force 4 August 2024**. It is an amending directive: the rules live in the consolidated Directive 2010/75/EU, now titled the **"Industrial and Livestock Rearing Emissions Directive"**.
- **Transposition deadline: 1 July 2026** (Article 4). Most new obligations then bite **installation by installation**, triggered by new BAT conclusions published after 1 July 2026, a permit update, or long-stop dates (**1 September 2034** for newly covered activities, **1 September 2036** for existing ones).
- **LATEST (Tuesday 15 September 2026): Commission Notice "Questions and Answers on Directive (EU) 2024/1785"**, OJ C/2026/4834, CELEX `52026XC04834`, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/4834/oj . **55 questions in ten chapters**: emission levels, environmental performance levels and benchmarks, environmental management system, indirect releases to water, innovation, registration, poultry and pigs, transitional provisions, transposition. **Not legally binding**: only the Court of Justice interprets EU law authoritatively. It deliberately **does not cover** provisions that the pending simplification proposal **COM(2025) 986** (10 December 2025, CELEX `52025PC0986`) would change, and will be updated once that file is adopted.
- **Scope widened**: battery manufacturing (**15,000 tonnes of cells a year**), extraction and on-site treatment of **15 metal ores** (bauxite, chromium, cobalt, copper, gold, iron, lead, lithium, manganese, nickel, palladium, platinum, tin, tungsten, zinc) **on an industrial scale**, water electrolysis for hydrogen above **50 tonnes a day**, cold-rolling mills above 10 tonnes of crude steel an hour, forging presses above 30 MN, pyrolysis, and textile finishing.
- **Livestock moved to a new Annex Ia in livestock units (LSU)**: pigs **350 LSU**, laying hens **300 LSU**, other poultry **280 LSU**, mixed pig and poultry farms **380 LSU**. **Cattle are not covered**; the Commission must report on livestock emissions, in particular cattle, **by 31 December 2026** (Article 73(3)). Organic and extensive pig farms below 2 LSU per hectare are excluded.
- **Stricter permits**: emission limit values must be the **strictest achievable** by applying BAT across the whole BAT-AEL range (Article 15(3)). This does **not** mean always the lowest end of the range (Q&A Q5).
- **New environmental performance limit values** for water and binding ranges for waste and resources, derived from BAT-AEPLs in future BAT conclusions (Article 15(4)).
- **Environmental management system** mandatory for every Annex I installation (Article 14a), including a **chemicals inventory** and, from **30 June 2030**, an **indicative transformation plan** for the heaviest sectors (Article 27d).
- **Penalties**: for the most serious infringements by a legal person, maximum fines of **at least 3 per cent of the operator's annual Union turnover** (Article 79). **Right to compensation** for individuals whose health is damaged by an infringement (Article 79a).

## What changed, in the Directive's own terms

### Scope (Annex I and Annex Ia)
- **Batteries** (Annex I, point 2.7): manufacture other than exclusively assembling, **15,000 tonnes of battery cells** (cathode, anode, electrolyte, separator, capsule) or more per year. Recital 46 links this to the expected wave of electric vehicle gigafactories.
- **Mining** (point 3.6): extraction including on-site treatment (comminution, size control, beneficiation, upgrading) of the 15 listed ores. The Commission must also review whether to cover **onshore and offshore oil and gas** and **industrial minerals** (Article 73(4)).
- **Hydrogen** (point 6.6): electrolysis of water above 50 tonnes a day; the threshold is itself under review.
- **Landfill**: Article 2 deletes Article 1(2) of the Landfill Directive, the clause that deemed landfills compliant with the IED.
- **Livestock**: special regime in **Chapter VIa** with simpler procedures. Member States may use **registration instead of permits** and general binding rules. The Commission adopts **uniform conditions for operating rules** by implementing act, due **by 1 September 2026** under Article 70i(2); national rules then apply **4 years** after that act for farms of **600 LSU or more**, **5 years** for **400 LSU or more**, **6 years** for the rest. Brubru has not verified whether that implementing act has been adopted: check before asserting it.

### Emission limits and performance
- **Article 15(3)**: strictest achievable ELVs, based on an **operator assessment** of the full BAT-AEL range, with cross-media effects.
- **Article 15(4)**: binding ranges and **environmental performance limit values for water**; indicative levels for waste and resources other than water. **Benchmarks** go into the EMS, not the permit.
- **Article 15(5)**: derogations from BAT-AELs on disproportionate cost, with tightened principles in the new Annex II.
- **Article 27b to 27e**: up to **30 months** to test emerging techniques; up to **6 years** to apply an emerging technique listed in BAT conclusions; up to **8 years** for **deep industrial transformation** set out in a transformation plan.
- **INCITE**, the EU Innovation Centre for Industrial Transformation and Emissions (Article 27a), collects data on innovative techniques, including for livestock.
- **Electronic permitting** systems and procedures by **31 December 2035** (Article 5(4)); permit decisions and updates published online, free of charge and without registration (Article 24(2)).
- **EMS deadline**: operators prepare and implement the EMS in line with the sector's BAT conclusions **by 1 July 2027**, except newly covered activities under Article 3(4) of the amending Directive (Article 14a(4)).

### Transformation plans (Article 27d)
Operators in Annex I activities 1 (energy), 2 (metals), 3 (minerals), 4 (chemicals) and 6.1(a) and (b)
(pulp and paper) must include an **indicative transformation plan** in their EMS **by 30 June 2030**,
explaining how the installation transforms over **2030 to 2050** towards a clean, circular and
climate-neutral economy. Other Annex I activities follow when permits are reviewed after BAT conclusions
published after 1 January 2030. Plans are **public** and checked by the EMS auditor within a year. The
Commission was to adopt a delegated act on their content **by 30 June 2026**; Brubru has not verified
its adoption.

### Enforcement
- **Article 79**: effective, proportionate, dissuasive penalties that strip the economic benefit; **at least 3 per cent of annual Union turnover** as the ceiling floor for the most serious infringements by legal persons; criminal penalties allowed as an alternative if equivalently effective. Factors: gravity, population or environment affected, repetition.
- **Article 79a**: individuals whose **health** is damaged can **claim and obtain compensation**; limitation periods cannot start before the infringement ends and the victim knows of the damage.

### Reporting and the Industrial Emissions Portal
The Directive works alongside **Regulation (EU) 2024/1244** (CELEX `32024R1244`), which establishes the
**Industrial Emissions Portal** and repeals the E-PRTR Regulation. Its Annex II pollutant list is the
reference for the pollutants that permits must cover (Article 14(1)(a), Q&A Q7).

## What the Q&A (15 September 2026) clarifies

| Question | Clarification |
|---|---|
| Q1, Q5 | "Strictest achievable" ELVs do not mean the lowest end of the BAT-AEL range in every case |
| Q3, Q47 | Article 15(3) applies to existing installations only at the earliest of: permit update within 4 years of BAT conclusions published after 1 July 2026, a new permit after such conclusions, an Article 21(5) update, or **1 September 2036** |
| Q6 | Article 15(4) derogations granted under the 2010 text **remain valid** until reassessed, at the latest **30 June 2030** |
| Q7 | ELVs may be needed even where BAT conclusions set no BAT-AEL, including for substances of emerging concern (Court judgment **C-626/22**) |
| Q9, Q10 | Exceeding a binding range or water performance limit value is non-compliance; exceeding an indicative level or an EMS benchmark is not, but triggers corrective assessment |
| Q13 | Setting only water EPLVs is not enough: binding ranges for waste and resources are also required |
| Q14 to Q16 | The EMS itself need not be in the permit; permits set its characteristics. **EMAS or ISO 14001 not required**, though they can be referred to |
| Q15 | The EMS requirement alone does not trigger a permit update |
| Q18 | ELVs for **indirect releases to water** are mandatory; the load after an external treatment plant must not exceed the load of a compliant direct release |
| Q22 to Q27 | Testing flexibility (Article 27b) does not require a listing in BAT conclusions; the 8-year transformation extension cannot be stretched further |
| Q30, Q31 | Member States may mix permits for large farms and registration for small ones, and use general binding rules with registration |
| Q33, Q34 | Permits may still apply to farms above the 2010 thresholds (40,000 poultry places, 2,000 production pigs, 750 sows), but national scope must be defined in **LSU** |
| Q35 | Guidelines on **aggregating farms** into a single unit due **by 5 August 2028** |
| Q44 | A farm can fall into several Annex Ia categories: 750 sows plus 50 broilers is covered as a pig farm |
| Q38 | The livestock phase-in does **not** postpone the 1 July 2026 transposition deadline |
| Q53 | Newly covered activities already inside the IED through another activity stay under the 2010 rules until the earlier of BAT-triggered update or **1 September 2034** |
| Q54 | IED transposition measures **need not be notified under TRIS** (Directive (EU) 2015/1535): the IED regulates activities, not products |
| Q55 | Article 34a (small isolated systems) need not be transposed if a Member State does not use it |

## What to say when asked
- **"Does IED 2.0 already apply to my plant?"** The national law must exist from 1 July 2026, but most new permit conditions reach an existing installation only at its next BAT conclusions, permit update, or the 2034 or 2036 long-stop dates.
- **"Are cattle farms covered?"** No. The Commission reports on cattle by 31 December 2026, possibly with a proposal.
- **"Is the fine 3 per cent?"** The Directive requires Member States to set a maximum of **at least** 3 per cent of annual Union turnover for the most serious infringements by legal persons. The actual fine is national.
- **"Is the Q&A binding?"** No. It is Commission guidance.
- Hedge anything touching provisions in the simplification proposal COM(2025) 986: it is still in the legislative process.
