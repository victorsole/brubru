# CBE-JU, EIT Food, the EIC and regional tools for agri-food innovation (SWD(2026) 271)

## QUICK FACTS
- **CBE-JU (Circular Bio-based Europe Joint Undertaking):** a **EUR 2 billion** partnership between the EU and the Bio-based Industries Consortium, **2021-2031**, successor of BBI JU (2014-2020); legal base **Council Regulation (EU) 2021/2085** (CELEX 32021R2085, title checked). It accelerates bio-based innovation and market deployment; **17 of its 26 flagship projects** (high TRL) concern food and feed ingredients. It does **not** fund direct food production.
- **CBE-JU food and feed ingredient portfolio:** **48 projects, EUR 291.4 million (21% of EU contributions)**: **5 flagship biorefineries (TRL 8) EUR 74.5 million**, **24 demonstration pilots (TRL 6-7) EUR 144.3 million**, **19 research actions (TRL 5) EUR 72.7 million**.
- **CBE-JU and primary producers:** about **EUR 185 million in 2022-2025** for projects on primary producers' challenges, involving **76 primary-sector entities (farmers about 73%)**; a **working group on primary producers** set up in **2025** (about 79 representatives; action plan; support project RootLinks).
- **EIT Food:** the EIT knowledge and innovation community for food: startups, scaleups, education and innovation programmes on regenerative agriculture, food waste, protein diversification, food biotechnology, circularity, healthier diets. The strategy's action 4 (Q4 2026) strengthens links between Horizon Europe agri-food research, EIT Food and the EIC.
- **European Innovation Council (EIC):** an Agriculture and Food portfolio and accelerator challenges: novel technologies for resilient agriculture (2023), food from **precision fermentation and algae** (2024; **EUR 50 million** in 2025), biotechnology-driven low-emission food and feed (2025), biotech for regenerating agricultural soils (2026).
- **Regional tools:** the **Thematic Smart Specialisation Platform on Agri-Food** (cohesion policy), the **European Regional Development Fund**, the **Interregional Innovation Investments (I3) instrument** (over 70 projects, for example AquaLoops4Med, Biotech4Food, VALUE4PACK) and the **Regional Innovation Valleys for Bioeconomy and Food Systems** concept (2023).
- **Other Horizon tools:** the **LIFE** programme for demonstration; **WIDERA** widening actions (digital biology centre in Estonia, higher-education alliances in digital agriculture, excellence hubs).
