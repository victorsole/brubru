# EDIC for Agri-Food: Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1978 of 8 September 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **Act:** *Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1978 of 8 September 2026 setting up the European Digital Infrastructure Consortium for Agri-Food (EDIC for Agri-Food)*, CELEX **32026D1978**, published in **OJ L 2026/1978 of 9 September 2026**. Adopted the same day as the AgRI 2040 and Food 2040 Communication COM(2026) 453.
- **Legal basis:** Article 14(3)(a) of **Decision (EU) 2022/2481** (the Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030, CELEX 32022D2481), which lets the Commission set up **European Digital Infrastructure Consortia (EDICs)**.
- **Applicants (13 February 2026):** Austria, Croatia, Finland, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Romania, Slovenia and the region of Flanders. **Poland joined on 8 June 2026.** **Observers:** Germany, Ireland, Latvia, Spain.
- **Host Member State: France**, which recognises the EDIC as an international body for VAT under Articles 143(1)(g) and 151(1)(b) of Directive 2006/112/EC and as an international organisation under Article 11(1)(b) of Directive (EU) 2020/262.
- **Mission (per its Statutes):** enhance the European agri-food digital ecosystem through EU-wide interoperable, use-case-based deployment actions.
- **What it is NOT:** not a funding programme and not a regulator; an implementation vehicle for a multi-country project under the Digital Decade. The Communication COM(2026) 453 lists EDICs among the instruments its technologies enabler should be built in complementarity with.
