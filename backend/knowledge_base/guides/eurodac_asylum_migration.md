# Eurodac and the New Pact on Migration and Asylum

## QUICK FACTS
- Full name: Eurodac Regulation (recast) -- Regulation (EU) 2024/1358
- CELEX: 32024R1358
- Type: Regulation (directly applicable)
- Procedure: 2016/0132(COD) (Ordinary Legislative Procedure)
- Adopted: 14 May 2024 (part of the New Pact on Migration and Asylum)
- Entry into force: 11 June 2024; application date: 12 June 2026
- Implementing Decision: Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/533 (application rules, published 12 March 2026)
- Purpose: EU biometric database for asylum seekers, irregular migrants, and persons apprehended crossing external borders
- Biometrics: Fingerprints (10 fingers) + facial images (new under recast)
- Age threshold: Lowered from 14 to 6 years old (new under recast)
- Data retention: 10 years (asylum applicants), 5 years (irregular border crossings)
- Responsible DG: DG HOME
- Responsible Commissioner: Magnus Brunner (Migration and Internal Affairs)
- Lead agency: eu-LISA (manages the database)
- EP lead committee: LIBE
- Related: Dublin IV replacement (AMMR), Screening Regulation, Crisis Regulation, APR

## Overview

Eurodac (European Dactyloscopy) is the EU's centralised biometric database used to identify asylum seekers and irregular migrants. Originally established in 2003, the system was substantially overhauled as part of the New Pact on Migration and Asylum adopted in May 2024.

The recast Regulation (EU) 2024/1358 significantly expands Eurodac's scope: facial images alongside fingerprints, lowered age threshold (from 14 to 6), new data categories, and interoperability with other EU large-scale IT systems (SIS, VIS, ETIAS, ECRIS-TCN).

## Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/533

Published 12 March 2026, this Commission Implementing Decision lays down the application rules for the recast Eurodac Regulation. It covers:

- Technical specifications for biometric data collection (fingerprint quality, facial image standards)
- Data transmission protocols between Member States and eu-LISA central system
- Interoperability frameworks with SIS II, VIS, ETIAS, and ECRIS-TCN
- Operational procedures for the common identity repository (CIR)
- Transition timeline for Member State systems to comply by 12 June 2026

## New Pact on Migration and Asylum (May 2024)

Eurodac is one of 10 legislative instruments adopted as part of the Pact:

| Instrument | CELEX | Key change |
|------------|-------|-----------|
| Eurodac Regulation (recast) | 32024R1358 | Biometric database expansion |
| Asylum and Migration Management Regulation (AMMR) | 32024R1351 | Replaces Dublin III |
| Screening Regulation | 32024R1356 | Pre-entry screening at borders |
| Asylum Procedure Regulation (APR) | 32024R1348 | Harmonised asylum procedures |
| Crisis and Force Majeure Regulation | 32024R1359 | Emergency solidarity mechanism |
| Reception Conditions Directive (recast) | 32024L1346 | Standards for asylum seekers |
| Qualification Regulation | 32024R1347 | Refugee/subsidiary protection |
| Resettlement and Humanitarian Admission Regulation | 32024R1350 | Legal pathways |
| Long-term Residents Directive (recast) | 32024L1233 | Integration of TCNs |
| Single Permit Directive (recast) | 32024L1232 | Work permits for TCNs |
