# EU Spring 2026 Economic Forecast (DG ECFIN)

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Thursday 21 May 2026 — SPRING FORECAST PUBLISHED)**: The European Commission's **Directorate-General for Economic and Financial Affairs (DG ECFIN)** published its **Spring 2026 European Economic Forecast**. EVP **Valdis Dombrovskis** (Economy and Productivity) delivered remarks at the press conference presenting the forecast. Source: EC Press Corner SPEECH (21 May 2026, "Remarks by Commissioner Dombrovskis at the press conference presenting the Spring 2026 Economic Forecast").
- **Type**: Commission staff working document (regular cyclical publication) — twice yearly (Spring + Autumn) plus a Winter interim forecast in February each year
- **Lead DG**: **DG ECFIN** (Economic and Financial Affairs)
- **Commissioner / EVP**: **Valdis Dombrovskis** (Economy and Productivity, Latvia — von der Leyen II Commission, since 1 December 2024)
- **Cycle role**: Anchors the **European Semester** Spring Package (typically May-June), which feeds into the Council's adoption of Country-Specific Recommendations (CSRs) under the Stability and Growth Pact reformed framework (Regulations (EU) 2024/1263 and 2024/1264)
- **Methodology**: forecast horizon typically the year of publication + 2 years (so Spring 2026 → 2026, 2027, 2028 projections); cut-off date for input data normally 7-10 days before publication
- **Public access**: Forecasts and underlying datasets published at economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-surveillance-eu-economies/european-economic-forecast_en

## The European Semester cycle anchor

The Spring forecast lands in the European Semester at this point in the cycle:

| Month | Step |
|---|---|
| November (Annual Sustainable Growth Survey) | Commission sets policy priorities for the next Semester cycle |
| February (Winter forecast) | Mid-cycle update on growth + inflation |
| March (European Semester reports + IDR) | Country Reports + In-Depth Reviews under MIP |
| **May (Spring forecast — this publication)** | Updated baseline informs the Spring Package |
| **May-June (Spring Package)** | Commission proposes CSRs to Council |
| July (Council adopts CSRs) | ECOFIN endorses, then EUCO endorses |
| September-October | National budgetary plans submitted (euro area) |
| November (Autumn forecast) | Confirmation/revision; opinions on draft budgetary plans |

The Spring 2026 forecast is the first full forecast under the **reformed economic governance framework** (Regulations (EU) 2024/1263 + 2024/1264, applying from 30 April 2024). It serves as the input to:

1. The Commission's recommendations on **Council Opinions on National Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plans (NMTPs)** — submitted by Member States in late 2024 and amended where needed
2. The **Article 126(3) TFEU reports** identifying Member States with potential excessive deficits — Commission published the 2026 Article 126(3) reports in parallel with the Spring forecast
3. The **Macroeconomic Imbalance Procedure (MIP)** — In-Depth Reviews for selected Member States

## What the forecast covers (structure of every Spring forecast)

The published document is structured in five parts:

- **Part I — Outlook for the EU and euro area**: aggregate GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, current account, fiscal balance
- **Part II — Country chapters**: 27 Member States + candidate countries (Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Türkiye, Ukraine) + Norway + Switzerland (selected)
- **Part III — Statistical annex**: harmonised tables, methodology notes
- **Part IV — Special features / boxes**: thematic deep-dives (e.g. impact of energy prices, defence spending, US tariffs, demographic transition)
- **Part V — Key assumptions**: oil price assumption, euro-USD exchange rate assumption, monetary policy stance

## How to use the Spring forecast in EU policy work

- For position papers: cite the **EU-27 and euro-area aggregate numbers** (Part I), not just national lines — Commission methodology applies harmonised assumptions across all MS
- For amendments to MFF + budgetary files: use the **debt-to-GDP and deficit projections** for the relevant MS
- For competitiveness arguments: cross-reference with the **JRC Foresight reports** and the **Draghi Report** baseline
- For CSR consultation: the Spring forecast underpins the **In-Depth Reviews** under the MIP and the **Article 126(3) reports**
