# Food 2030 flagship projects: alternative proteins, nutrition, food waste, microbiome, food safety, aquatic, urban

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** the flagship stories of SWD(2026) 271, section 4.2 (8 September 2026): examples chosen by the Commission. Food 2030 funded about EUR 770 million; successor: Food 2040.
- **Alternative proteins:** **Giant Leaps** (2022-2026, RIA, 30 partners; food consumption analysed in 28 European countries; six prototype categories). **LIKE-A-PRO** (2022-2026, IA, 45 partners; **63 pilot-scale prototypes in 22 product types**; living labs in **11 countries**).
- **Nutrition and health:** **CoDiet** (2023-2026, RIA; pilot study in the UK, Greece, Ireland and Spain). **FEAST** (2022-2027, RIA; 15 countries, 35 partners, 13 living labs). Also NUTRISHIELD, STANCE4HEALTH, PREVENTOMICS, PROTEIN, DOMINO, HealthFerm, FOODLAND.
- **Food waste and circularity:** **ZeroW** (2022-2025, IA; nine living labs, seven chose commercialisation); **REFRESH** (fed the EU Platform on Food Losses and Food Waste, which informed the Waste Framework Directive update); PRECIOUS and WASTEWISE (2024-2028); **MAGNO** (2024-2027, food packaging).
- **Microbiome:** **MASTER** (2019-2023, IA, over twenty innovations, CuratedFoodMetagenomic database); **DOMINO** (2023-2028, RIA); **MicrobiomeSupport** (2018-2022, CSA, 33 countries).
- **Food safety:** **WATSON** (2023-2026, RIA): traceability against fraud in wine, honey, olive oil, meat, dairy and fish; the JRC consolidated **2,445 food fraud incidents since 2016** (to January 2026). **FoodSafety4EU** (2021-2023, CSA): 42 formal members, annual EU Food Safety Forum.
- **Aquatic food:** **AWARE** (2022-2026): fish farming in recycled water, a case study at Castellana Grotte (Puglia). **IMPRESS** (2023-2027, IA): zero-waste aquatic value chains.
- **Urban and community:** **Cities2030** (2020-2024, IA): **42 policy and living labs in 18 countries**, 296 partnerships, 12,000 citizens, 115 follower cities. FoodE, CULTIVATE (Food Sharing Compass), **CLEVERFOOD** (2023-2026; nine countries; a platform of over 70 initiatives with over EUR 450 million of investment), FoodPathS (2022-2025). The Flemish Food Strategy was based on the Food 2030 priorities.
- **Data and Africa:** FOODITY (12 projects, 2023-2025), DRG4Food (European Food Register); **HealthyFoodAfrica** (2020-2025, ten African cities).
