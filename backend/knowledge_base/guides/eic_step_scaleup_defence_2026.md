# EIC STEP Scale Up Defence — 2026 call

## QUICK FACTS
- **New EIC call (Work Programme amendment of 17 June 2026)**: **equity-only** investments of **EUR 10 million minimum, EUR 30 million maximum** from the EIC Fund for scale-ups in **critical defence technologies**; NO grant. The award decision may add up to EUR 5 million of flexibility.
- **Budget: EUR 100 million in 2026** (and EUR 100 million planned for 2027, subject to the 2027 Work Programme); unused money goes to STEP Scale Up.
- **Deadline: 28 October 2026, 17:00 Brussels time**, single batch (opened 30 June 2026). Portal topic `HORIZON-EIC-2026-DEFENCE-01`.
- **Who:** a single SME or small mid-cap (up to 499 staff) established in an **EU Member State, Ukraine, or an EEA country associated to Horizon Europe**, or an investor applying on its behalf. Companies controlled from any other third country are **excluded unless the national authorities approve guarantees** (self-assessment of control is a mandatory document).
- **Scope:** products with a **PRIMARY defence application** in the STEP defence areas: air and missile defence; artillery and precision strike; missiles and ammunition; drones and counter-drones; strategic enablers (space, critical infrastructure, energy security); cyber, AI and electronic warfare; military mobility; ground combat; maritime; air combat; military medicine and CBRN. **Mainly civilian or dual-use products go to STEP Scale Up or the EIC Accelerator, NOT here.**
- **Pre-commitment (eligibility condition):** ONE qualified investor, equity only, at least **20%** of a target round of **EUR 50-150 million or more** that is at least **3-5 times** the EIC investment; the 20% must be uninvested on the letter's date.
- **Evaluation:** eligibility check, then an **interview with an EIC jury of at most six members** scoring GO / NO GO (invitation about 4-6 weeks after evaluation starts; result about 2 weeks after the interview session). Outcomes: GO + Sovereignty (STEP) Seal; NO GO + Seal; NO GO.
- **Submission limit (from 1 April 2026):** three unsuccessful submissions to STEP Scale Up or STEP Defence together, then no further application under Horizon Europe. No concurrent submissions with the Accelerator or STEP Scale Up.
- Legal basis: Article 48 of the Horizon Europe Regulation (EU) 2021/695 as amended by **Regulation (EU) 2025/2653 of 19 December 2025**; defence technologies as defined in the STEP Regulation (EU) 2024/795.

EIC Work Programme 2026, Commission Decision C(2026) 4080 (17 June 2026), Section VII, the Introduction (economic security) and Annex 1; EIC STEP Scale Up Defence page on eic.ec.europa.eu (read 8 October 2026); Funding & Tenders Portal topic `HORIZON-EIC-2026-DEFENCE-01` (search API, 8 October 2026).

## Application package
- Full business plan (max 50 pages) with ownership, financial structure and justification against the STEP defence objectives.
- Pitch deck (max 15 pages, PDF; shown to the jury).
- Equity pre-commitment from one qualified investor (official template).
- Financial plan (EIC template).
- Freedom-to-operate results or a 2-page note.
- CVs of key personnel.
- **Self-assessment of third-country control**, plus the guarantee approved under national procedures where control exists. Guarantees not supplied at application must follow within two weeks of the end of the interview session (or of a later request during due diligence), otherwise the application becomes ineligible.
- **Classified information** may support the proposal but NEVER through the Portal: contact EISMEA at least 30 working days before the deadline to arrange delivery (Commission Decision 2015/444).
- Optional interest in InvestEU venture debt if awarded the Seal.

## Award criteria (Work Programme Table 10, GO/NO GO)
- **Excellence:** defence technology breakthrough (deep tech, clear defence application, better cost, performance or capability); technological feasibility and substantiated TRL; IP strategy for defence markets and freedom to operate **without undue third-country export control (such as ITAR/EAR)**.
- **Impact:** size and realistic share of the defence market; credible traction with defence end-users; business model adapted to procurement cycles, classification, export control and certification; STEP Defence impact (strengthening the European Defence Technological and Industrial Base, or reducing strategic dependencies).
- **Level of risk, implementation and need for Union support:** team with defence expertise and security clearances where needed; risk level (longer procurement cycles, limited exits) that markets will not finance alone; leverage of 3-5 times; defence-specific risk management (export licensing, clearances, certification, procurement timing).

## Conditions after selection
- Award decision by the Commission; EIB due diligence and EIC Fund Investment Committee decision; amounts may be reduced or rejected for justified reasons.
- At least one **economic-security safeguard** in each investment agreement (ownership changes, governance, IP, location of activities, supply chain); companies keep most value and IP in the EU, associated EEA countries and Ukraine.
- **Extra ethics rules:** respect for international humanitarian law, and AI-enabled weapon functions must keep meaningful human control.
- Access to Business Acceleration Services and fast-track access to the EUDIS Business Accelerator.

## How it fits with the rest of the EIC
- **Dual-use innovation** (credible civilian AND defence markets) may apply to the **EIC Accelerator** (from 9 July 2026; first evaluated at the 2 September 2026 batch) and to **STEP Scale Up** (first evaluated at the 9 September 2026 batch), under the same criteria and with no preference. Civil-security buyers (police, border management, civil protection) count as civilian.
- Pathfinder, Transition and Advanced Innovation Challenges stay **civil-only**.
- Complementary EU defence instruments: European Defence Fund (EDF) and European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP).
