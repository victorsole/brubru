# Defence Directive (EU) 2026/2383 (Omnibus V): new defence procurement thresholds and simpler intra-EU transfers

## QUICK FACTS
- **NOT YET in force on 3 November 2026; it will enter into force on 23 November 2026** (Article 4). **The new thresholds do NOT apply on that date: Member States transpose by 24 November 2027 and apply by 24 February 2028** (Article 3). Until national law applies, the current rules and thresholds apply. OJ L of 3 November 2026; signed 21 October 2026. CELEX 32026L2383, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/2383/oj , procedure 2025/0177(COD), COM(2025) 823.
- **Procurement thresholds, Directive 2009/81/EC Article 8:** supply and service contracts **EUR 432 000 becomes EUR 2 000 000**; works contracts **EUR 5 404 000 becomes EUR 10 000 000**. Below a threshold the general principles still apply: transparency, non-discrimination, equal treatment, proportionality (recital 16).
- **Other procurement changes (Article 2):** procurement between authorities from different Member States (new Article 10a); occasional joint procurement (new Article 10b); centralised and ancillary purchasing definitions; innovation partnership (new Article 27a); dynamic purchasing system (new Article 29a). Article 28 on negotiated procedures without publication is rewritten: the differences from the old text were not analysed here.
- **Statistics suspended:** the annual statistical report is suspended until **31 December 2030**.
- **Transfers, Directive 2009/43/EC Article 4(2):** Member States **may** exempt transfers from prior authorisation in more cases: EU institutions and the European Defence Agency as recipients; both parties certified undertakings; cooperative programmes (PESCO, Structures for European Armament Programmes, European Defence Projects of Common Interest); projects funded under a Union defence industrial programme; structured intra-EU industrial partnerships; urgency from a crisis; assistance under Article 42(7) of the Treaty on European Union.
- **General licences:** Member States **shall** publish general transfer licences for transfers necessary for projects funded under a Union defence industrial programme (new Article 5a); a further general licence between certified undertakings (Article 5(2), point (ba)). The Commission gets delegated powers to add cases (Article 4(3)).
- **Who is bound:** Member States and contracting authorities or entities, NOT companies directly. Suppliers benefit through licences once national law applies.
- **Review:** the Commission reviews both Directives by **1 January 2031**.
- **Not read in detail:** new Article 5b, and the remaining procurement points. **Companion:** Joint statement C/2026/5559. **Overview:** `eu_defence_readiness_omnibus_v_and_agile_2026`; `eu_defence_procurement`.
