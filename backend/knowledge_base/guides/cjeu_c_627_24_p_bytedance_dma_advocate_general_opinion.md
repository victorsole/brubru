# ByteDance (TikTok) v Commission, C-627/24 P: Advocate General Biondi's Opinion of 15 October 2026 on the DMA gatekeeper designation

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **15 October 2026** Advocate General **Andrea Biondi** delivered his Opinion in **Case C-627/24 P, Bytedance v Commission**, the **first Digital Markets Act case to reach the Court of Justice** (press release No 151/26). He proposes that the Court **dismiss** the parts of ByteDance's appeal he examined.
- **An Opinion is NOT the judgment.** It does not bind the Court; the judges now deliberate and judgment comes later. Never say the Court has ruled.
- **Background:** in **September 2023** the Commission designated ByteDance as a **gatekeeper** for **TikTok** (online social networking service) under the **Digital Markets Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/1925**, without opening a market investigation. The **General Court dismissed ByteDance's action on 17 July 2024 (T-1077/23)**; ByteDance appealed.
- **Market investigation:** the Commission has **no discretion** where a company's arguments **manifestly call the presumption into question**: it must investigate, unless it is clear the company does not meet the qualitative requirements. Arguments that are not sufficiently substantiated may be rejected without investigation.
- **Standard of proof:** merely casting doubt is not enough; the company must put forward sufficiently convincing arguments that seriously challenge the presumptions, but need not prove conclusively at this stage that it is not a gatekeeper. The General Court applied the right standard.
- **Important gateway:** that users also use other social networks does not, by itself, show TikTok is not an important gateway.
- **Right to be heard:** ByteDance could submit arguments and have them examined, but the Commission did **not** have to let it comment on its provisional assessment. The General Court was **wrong** to find that right infringed, though **right to dismiss** the claim; the AG proposes **substituting different reasons** on that point.
