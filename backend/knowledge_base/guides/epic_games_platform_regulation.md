## CORE REGULATORY STACK — gaming-sector specifics on top of horizontal platform rules
- **Digital Markets Act** (Reg 2022/1925, CELEX 32022R1925) — Epic is **named complainant** driving gatekeeper enforcement against Apple + Google. Epic Games Store alternative distribution on iOS is DMA-enabled. Epic's regulatory strategy anchors on DMA.
- **Digital Services Act** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — Epic Games Store + Fortnite's Creative/UGC hub = hosting provider. Fortnite borderline VLOP on MAU.
- **Product Liability Directive recast** (Dir 2024/2853) — software product liability expanded; Unreal Engine + EOS in scope.
- **Copyright in DSM Art 17** (Dir 2019/790, CELEX 32019L0790) — UEFN user uploads make Epic an OCSSP.
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — Unreal Engine AI assets, MetaHuman generative features (GPAI deployment disclosures); Annex III classification for educational Epic Online Services.
- **Consumer protection / loot boxes** — 2022 US FTC $245M settlement; EU CPC network inquiry on loot boxes (Dec 2022 Norwegian CC + 2023 BEUC report). Commission 2023 report on gaming. Council conclusions on gaming minors 2024.
- **Digital Fairness Act** — V-Bucks, Battle Pass dark patterns, FOMO mechanics, whale-farming in scope.
- **AVMSD** (Dir 2018/1808, CELEX 32018L1808) — Fortnite tournaments/esports events bring VSP elements.
- **Digital Content Directive** (Dir 2019/770, CELEX 32019L0770) — Fortnite Crew subscription, microtransactions conformity.
- **P2B Regulation** (Reg 2019/1150, CELEX 32019R1150) — Fortnite creator economy; UEFN developer relationships.
- **GDPR Art 8** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679) — child gamer data; parental consent thresholds (13-16 by MS). Epic implemented "Cabined Accounts" for under-13s post-FTC settlement.
- **CSAM / Combating Sexual Abuse Directive** (Dir 2011/92, CELEX 32011L0092) — Fortnite party/voice chat, reporting.
- **CRA** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847) — Unreal Engine, EOS SDK backend, Epic Games Launcher = products with digital elements.
- **NIS2** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555) — online game platforms increasingly in scope.
- **Representative Actions** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828) — loot box / gambling-adjacent claims.
- **PEGI + AVMSD Art 28b** — PEGI is industry self-reg; AVMSD Art 28b is law.

## URGENT COMPLIANCE

## ENFORCEMENT / LITIGATION HISTORY
- **Epic v Apple** (US + EU-adjacent DMA complaint) — ongoing.
- **Epic v Google** (US, jury verdict Epic 2023).
- **FTC settlement Dec 2022:** $245M for dark patterns; $275M COPPA violation. Cabined Accounts for under-13s as remedy.
- No published EU enforcement action at company level as of April 2026.
