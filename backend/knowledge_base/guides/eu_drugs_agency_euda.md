# EU Drugs Agency (EUDA, formerly EMCDDA)

## QUICK FACTS
- Full name: European Union Drugs Agency (EUDA)
- Former name: European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug Addiction (EMCDDA)
- Founding regulation: Regulation (EU) 2023/1322 of 27 June 2023 (repealing Regulation (EC) No 1920/2006)
- CELEX: 32023R1322
- In force: 2 July 2024 (new mandate operational)
- Location: Lisbon, Portugal (Praca Europa 1, Cais do Sodre)
- Responsible DG: DG HOME (Migration and Home Affairs) / DG SANTE (Health and Food Safety, for health aspects)
- Responsible Commissioner: Magnus Brunner (Internal Affairs and Migration)
- EP lead committee: LIBE (Civil Liberties, Justice and Home Affairs)
- Budget: Approximately EUR 23 million/year
- Staff: ~100
- Network: Reitox (27 EU Member States + Norway and Turkiye)
- Key publication: European Drug Report (annual)

## Overview

The European Union Drugs Agency (EUDA) is the EU's decentralised agency responsible for providing independent, science-based information on illicit drugs and drug addiction. It was recast from the EMCDDA (established in 1993) to the EUDA in 2023 through Regulation (EU) 2023/1322, which significantly expanded its mandate to include threat assessment, preparedness, and direct monitoring capabilities.

The recast was driven by the evolving drug landscape in Europe: the rise of synthetic opioids (fentanyl analogues), new psychoactive substances (NPS), polydrug use, and the increasing role of darknet markets and encrypted communication in drug supply chains. The European Drug Report 2025 confirms that availability remains high across all substance types.

## Core Functions

### 1. Monitoring and Analysis
- Collects, analyses, and disseminates data on drug use, supply, and consequences across the EU
- Operates the Reitox network of national focal points (one per Member State plus Norway and Turkiye)
- Publishes the annual European Drug Report and Statistical Bulletin

### 2. Threat Assessment (New under 2023 Regulation)
- Issues alerts on new psychoactive substances posing public health risks
- Conducts risk assessments that can trigger EU-wide control measures (Council implementing decisions)
- Monitors emerging drug threats and early warning signals

### 3. Preparedness and Response (New under 2023 Regulation)
- Develops preparedness plans for drug-related health crises
- Supports Member States in responding to emerging drug threats
- Can issue public health alerts when substances pose immediate danger

### 4. Best Practice and Technical Assistance
- Develops quality standards for drug prevention, treatment, harm reduction, and recovery
- Supports drug policy evaluation at national and EU level
- Provides technical assistance to EU candidate and partner countries

## Governance

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Management Board | Strategic oversight, work programme and budget approval (1 representative per Member State + 2 Commission + 2 EP observers) |
| Executive Director | Day-to-day management |
| Scientific Committee | Independent scientific advice (max 15 members) |
| Budget Committee | Financial oversight |
| Executive Committee | Preparatory work for Management Board |

## Key Publications
- **European Drug Report**: Annual flagship publication analysing the drug situation in Europe
- **Statistical Bulletin**: Comprehensive dataset accompanying the Drug Report
- **EU Drug Markets: In-depth Analysis**: Joint publication with Europol
- **Drug profiles**: Substance-specific factsheets
- **Precursor assessment reports**: Supporting EU drug precursor legislation
- **European Responses Guide**: Best practices in drug policy implementation

## Drug Substances Covered
- Cannabis (most commonly used illicit drug in Europe)
- Cocaine (second most prevalent)
- Opioids (heroin, synthetic opioids including fentanyl)
- Amphetamine, methamphetamine, MDMA
- New psychoactive substances (NPS) including synthetic cathinones, synthetic cannabinoids
- Other: LSD, ketamine, GHB, nitrous oxide

## EU Drug Policy Framework

The EUDA operates within the broader EU drug policy framework:
- **EU Drugs Strategy 2021-2025** (Council document 14178/20): evidence-based, integrated, balanced approach
- **EU Action Plan on Drugs 2021-2025**: 84 actions across 4 priorities
- **Council Framework Decision 2004/757/JHA**: minimum provisions on drug trafficking offences
- **Regulation (EU) 2021/1436**: drug precursor controls (internal trade)
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/XXX**: upcoming regulation on new psychoactive substances risk assessment procedures

## Relevance for Advocacy

The EUDA is a key evidence provider for organisations working on drug policy, public health, harm reduction, and criminal justice reform. Entry points:
- Public consultations during risk assessments of new substances
- Management Board (via national government representatives)
- Scientific Committee (academic and research community)
- Reitox national focal points (national level)
- Joint publications with Europol (law enforcement dimension)
- EP LIBE committee hearings on drug policy
