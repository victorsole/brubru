# 2026 Enlargement Package (4 November 2026): Montenegro ready to close, roadmaps for four frontrunners

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted Wednesday 4 November 2026: the Commission's annual Enlargement Package**, communication **COM(2026) 690 final** (CELEX 52026DC0690) with country reports SWD(2026) 690 to 700 (Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, Türkiye, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine, plus one on gradual integration strands). Press release IP/26/2325; presented by High Representative Kaja Kallas and Commissioner Marta Kos. **An assessment, NOT a Council decision:** opening or closing chapters still needs unanimity in the Council.
- **Montenegro (the Commission's assessment):** **21 chapters provisionally closed** so far (Montenegro's count, not another country's); "subject to Member States' agreement, Montenegro **could provisionally close the remaining chapters by the end of this year**" (2026); the **financial package for its accession** has been presented and work on the **accession treaty** is well under way. The EU Ambassador in Podgorica called it the first Enlargement Package in 15 years to say a candidate is ready to conclude negotiations (his words, 5 November).
- **Indicative roadmaps (new, announced in the 2026 State of the Union) for the four most advanced: Montenegro, Albania, Moldova and Ukraine**, with milestones for the year ahead. Roadmaps are indicative, NOT binding dates.
- **Albania:** opened the last remaining cluster; met the interim benchmarks on the rule-of-law chapters, paving the way for provisional closure of four chapters; its own objective is to **conclude negotiations by 2027**.
- **Moldova:** opened four clusters in the past year; the Commission says it meets the conditions to open all remaining clusters; objective **close negotiations by early 2028**.
- **Ukraine:** opened four clusters in the past year; meets the conditions to open all remaining clusters; anti-corruption institutions "continue to operate overall independently and effectively"; objective **close negotiations by the end of 2028**.
- **Serbia:** "did not make any substantive progress"; low CFSP alignment. **North Macedonia:** constitutional amendments still not adopted. **Bosnia and Herzegovina:** "increasingly disengaging" from its EU path. **Kosovo:** reforms stalled; the Commission lifted its measures after the transfer of local governance in the north and stands ready to prepare an Opinion on its membership application.
- **Also new:** a common framework for **gradual integration** in energy, transport, digital and defence before accession.
- **What was NOT read:** the country reports themselves and the Georgia and Türkiye conclusions; the dates above are each country's stated objective as reported by the Commission.
