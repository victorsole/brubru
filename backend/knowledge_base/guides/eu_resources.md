## Legislative Tracking

### EUR-Lex (eur-lex.europa.eu)
Official EU law database.
- All treaties, regulations, directives, decisions
- Search by CELEX number, title, or keywords
- Consolidated versions showing all amendments
- Procedure files linking to preparatory documents
- Available in all 24 EU languages

### Legislative Observatory - OEIL (oeil.europarl.europa.eu)
EP's database for monitoring EU decision-making.
- Procedure tracking with timeline and status
- Links to all related documents
- Committee assignments and rapporteurs
- Upcoming plenary votes
- New procedures added in last 7 days

### Legislative Train Schedule (europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train)
Visual tracker of Commission priorities.
- Each priority = a "train", each file = a "carriage"
- Status: Announced, Tabled, Blocked (9+ months), Close to adoption, Adopted, Withdrawn
- Searchable by committee or policy area
- Quick summaries of each initiative

## European Parliament

### EP Plenary (europarl.europa.eu/plenary)
- Agendas and voting lists
- Live streaming and video archives
- Adopted texts
- Minutes of proceedings

### MEPs Directory (europarl.europa.eu/meps)
- All 720 MEPs with photos
- Search by name, country, political group
- Committee memberships
- Contact information

### EP Assistants Database (europarl.europa.eu/meps/en/assistants)
- All parliamentary assistants
- Searchable by name or MEP
- 6 categories: APAs, Local, Service Providers, Paying Agents, Leadership Assistants, Trainees

### EP Committees (europarl.europa.eu/committees)
- 25+ committees
- Meeting calendars and agendas
- Documents, reports, amendments
- Webstreaming of meetings
- Newsletter subscriptions available

### EPRS Think Tank (europarl.europa.eu/thinktank)
- Briefings, studies, infographics
- EU Legislation in Progress series
- At a Glance summaries
- Research by policy area

## Council of the EU

### Council Meeting Calendar (consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/calendar)
- All ministerial meetings
- COREPER and working party schedules
- European Council summits

### Council Document Register (consilium.europa.eu/en/documents-publications/public-register)
- Agendas, minutes, conclusions
- Voting records
- Documents from 1999 onwards

### Voting Results (consilium.europa.eu/en/general-secretariat/corporate-policies/voting-results)
- How each member state voted
- Search by date, policy area, or country
- Statements and reservations

### Council Preparatory Bodies (consilium.europa.eu/en/council-eu/preparatory-bodies)
- List of 150+ working parties
- Organised by Council configuration
- Composition and mandate
