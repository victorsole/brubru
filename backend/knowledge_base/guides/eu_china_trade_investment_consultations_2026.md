# EU-China Trade and Investment Consultations: second meeting in Beijing, 8-9 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** the **second meeting of the EU-China Trade and Investment Consultations (TIC)** took place in **Beijing on 8-9 October 2026** between **Wang Wentao**, China's Minister of Commerce, and **Maroš Šefčovič**, Commissioner for Trade and Economic Security, Interinstitutional Relations and Transparency. The two sides published a **joint statement** with an annex of "deliverables and understandings" (Commission STATEMENT/26/2122).
- **What Šefčovič said was achieved (his closing remarks, STATEMENT/26/2124, "check against delivery"):** three "shared understandings":
  1. **moderate China's exports of hybrids and plug-in hybrids to the EU**: "the prospect of cutting China's exports by more than a half";
  2. **better access to the Chinese market** for EU products through lower MFN tariffs, from car parts to olive oil and footwear: almost **EUR 4 billion** in current export value and at least **EUR 225 million** in duty savings;
  3. **easier export licensing for rare earths and permanent magnets** from China.
  He called it "a crucial first step, but only a first step", said the EU's trade deficit with China is "unsustainable", and said that where dialogue cannot deliver "the EU will use its tools to ensure a rebalancing".
- **In the joint statement's annex:** an understanding on trade in hybrid vehicles "in a WTO compliant manner"; procedures on price undertakings and reviews in the electric-vehicle anti-subsidy case continue; a dialogue on market access for medical devices; China to speed up recognition of the regionalisation principle for eligible Member States on animal disease; the export-control dialogue (China willing to keep facilitating licences for rare earths and permanent magnets through the fast-track mechanism, advance warning and more transparency on listing and delisting); the EU-China Intellectual Property Working Group; WTO reform; a technical dialogue on the EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation; and the EU clarifying that its guidance on EU funds for clean-energy projects involving certain inverters is country neutral.
- **These are understandings, not legal acts.** No tariff change, quota or licence rule takes effect from the statements themselves; each side must implement them.
- **Next steps:** a ministerial video-conference in **January 2027** and the **third TIC ministerial meeting in March 2027**. Šefčovič said President von der Leyen will assess the outcome and discuss next steps with leaders at the upcoming European Council (due 15-16 October 2026): "that is where the final decision lies". China is not an agenda item named in President Costa's invitation letter.

## How to answer
Attribute the three headline outcomes to Šefčovič ("shared understanding") and the annex items to the joint statement. Do not state that exports "will be cut by half": his words are "the prospect of cutting". For the wider relationship (Parliament's 7 October recommendation, trade defence cases, rare-earth controls) point to `eu_china_relations`.
