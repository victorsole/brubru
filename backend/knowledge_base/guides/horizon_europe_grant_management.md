# Horizon Europe Grant Management

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Horizon Europe grant lifecycle -- from proposal selection to project closure
- Programme regulation: Regulation (EU) 2021/695
- Financial basis: Financial Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046
- Grant Agreement Preparation (GAP): 3-5 months; target 8 months from call deadline to signature
- Indirect costs: 25% flat rate of eligible direct costs
- CFS threshold: Certificate on Financial Statements required if EU contribution >EUR 430,000
- Pre-financing: Up to 50% of maximum grant amount within 30 days of signature
- Lump sum grants: Payment triggered by work package completion (no detailed cost reporting)
- Open science: Mandatory open access to publications (CC-BY), FAIR data principles
- Audit window: Up to 2 years after final payment (OLAF and ECA have independent audit rights)
- Key portal: Funding & Tenders Portal (ec.europa.eu)
- Common audit findings: Ineligible personnel costs, double funding, unapproved subcontracting, insufficient documentation

Comprehensive guide to managing Horizon Europe grants from a Joint Undertaking or DG RTD perspective, covering the full grant lifecycle from proposal selection to project closure.

## Grant Agreement Preparation (GAP)

### GAP Process Phases

After a proposal is selected following evaluation, the Grant Agreement Preparation (GAP) phase begins:

1. **Invitation to GAP**: Coordinator receives invitation via the Funding & Tenders Portal
2. **Consortium validation**: All beneficiaries must have a validated PIC (Participant Identification Code) and designated LEAR (Legal Entity Appointed Representative)
3. **Technical annex review**: Description of action (Annex 1) is reviewed and may be adjusted based on evaluation panel comments
4. **Budget revision**: Cost estimates are reviewed; the granting authority may request adjustments to ensure value for money
5. **Ethics review**: If flagged during evaluation, an ethics review is conducted (can add 2-4 weeks)
6. **Grant Agreement signature**: Electronic signature via the portal by the coordinator and the granting authority

### GAP Timeline

Standard GAP takes 3-5 months from invitation. The granting authority targets 8 months from call deadline to grant signature (the "time to grant" KPI).

## Model Grant Agreement (MGA) Structure

The Horizon Europe MGA is a standardised template with the following key provisions:

### Core Articles

| Article | Topic | Key Provisions |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Art. 5 | Duration and starting date | Project duration, start date (usually first day of month after signature) |
| Art. 6 | Eligible costs | Direct costs, indirect costs (25% flat rate), subcontracting, financial support to third parties |
| Art. 7 | Budget | Budget breakdown by beneficiary and cost category |
| Art. 8 | Forms of funding | Actual costs, unit costs, lump sums, flat-rate financing |
| Art. 9 | Reporting and payment | Periodic reporting (every 12-18 months), final report within 60 days of end |
| Art. 11 | Liability and damage | Beneficiary liability, force majeure |
| Art. 12 | Termination | Grounds for termination by the granting authority |
| Art. 13 | Checks, reviews, audits, investigations | Ex-post audits, OLAF and ECA access rights |
| Art. 16 | Intellectual property rights | Background and results, access rights |
| Art. 17 | Communication, dissemination, open science | Open access obligations, visibility requirements |
| Art. 18 | Specific rules for action types | RIA, IA, CSA-specific provisions |

### Key Annexes

- **Annex 1**: Description of the action (technical annex)
- **Annex 2**: Estimated budget
- **Annex 3**: Accession forms for beneficiaries
- **Annex 5**: Specific rules (ethical requirements, security aspects)

## Amendment Procedures

### Types of Amendments

| Type | Process | Examples |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Minor** | Notification to granting authority | Change of bank account, change of contact person |
| **Major (non-financial)** | Amendment request via portal | Adding/removing work packages, significant scope changes |
| **Major (financial)** | Amendment request + justification | Budget transfers >20% between cost categories |
| **Adding beneficiaries** | Full amendment with accession form | New partner joining the consortium |
| **Removing beneficiaries** | Amendment + redistribution of tasks | Partner leaving, tasks reassigned |
| **Extension of duration** | Amendment request with justification | Maximum 6 months extension without additional funding |

### Amendment Process

1. Coordinator submits amendment request via Funding & Tenders Portal
2. Project officer reviews within the granting authority
3. If approved, amended grant agreement is issued for signature
4. Timeline: typically 30-60 days for straightforward amendments

## Eligible Cost Categories

### Direct Costs

| Category | Rules | Key Requirements |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| **Personnel** | Actual salary costs + statutory charges | Timesheets required; unit cost option available |
| **Subcontracting** | Must be identified in Annex 1 | Best value for money; no conflict of interest |
| **Purchase costs** | Equipment, consumables, other goods/services | Equipment depreciated over useful life |
| **Travel** | Actual travel and subsistence costs | Must be necessary for the action |
| **Financial support to third parties** | Cascading grants (max EUR 60,000 per third party unless justified) | Must be foreseen in Annex 1 |
| **Internally invoiced goods/services** | Internal charges for shared resources | Usual accounting practices apply |

### Indirect Costs

Flat rate of **25% of eligible direct costs** (excluding subcontracting, financial support to third parties, and unit costs that include indirect costs).

### Ineligible Costs

- Costs not incurred during the action duration
- Costs not necessary for the action
- VAT (if recoverable)
- Interest on debt, provisions for losses
- Excessive or reckless expenditure

## Financial Reporting

### Form C (Financial Statement)

Each beneficiary submits a Form C at each reporting period:
- Declaration of eligible direct and indirect costs
- Receipts (if any revenue generated)
- Interest on pre-financing (if applicable)
- Certificate on Financial Statements (CFS) required if EU contribution claimed by beneficiary exceeds EUR 430,000 for the entire project

### Payment Schedule

| Payment | Timing | Amount |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Pre-financing | Within 30 days of grant signature | Up to 50% of maximum grant amount |
| Interim payment | After approval of periodic report | Based on accepted costs minus pre-financing share |
| Final payment | After approval of final report | Remaining balance (5% retained until final review) |

## Lump Sum Grants

Horizon Europe introduced **lump sum grants** as an alternative to actual-cost reporting:

- Pre-defined lump sums per work package
- Payment triggered by completion of work package (deliverables and milestones)
- No detailed cost reporting or audit of actual costs
- Simplifies administration but requires clear deliverable definitions
- Currently used in selected calls (pillar II and some pillar I calls)

## Open Science Obligations (Art. 17 MGA)

### Mandatory

- **Open access to publications**: Immediate open access under CC-BY (or CC-BY-NC for specific cases)
- **Research data management plan**: Delivered within first 6 months
- **Open access to data**: FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)

### Encouraged

- Pre-registration of studies
- Open peer review
- Citizen science
- Open-source software

## Ethics Review

### When Required

- Research involving human participants or human cells/tissues
- Research with animals
- Research in countries outside the EU
- Potential dual-use or misuse
- Environmental or security concerns
- Use of AI/machine learning on personal data

### Ethics Deliverables

Ethics requirements are listed in Annex 5 and may include:
- Ethics committee approvals
- Informed consent procedures
- Data protection impact assessments
- Security clearances

## Consortium Agreement vs MGA

| Aspect | MGA | Consortium Agreement |
|--------|-----|---------------------|
| Parties | Beneficiaries + granting authority | Beneficiaries only |
| Legal basis | EU Financial Regulation | Private contract |
| Mandatory | Yes | Yes (for multi-beneficiary actions) |
| IP provisions | Access rights, background/results | Detailed IP management, licensing terms |
| Dispute resolution | EU courts | Arbitration/mediation |
| Decision-making | Not covered | Consortium governance structure |

## Audit and Compliance

### Ex-Post Audits

- The granting authority may audit any beneficiary up to 2 years after final payment
- OLAF (European Anti-Fraud Office) and ECA (European Court of Auditors) have independent audit rights
- Audit certificates from independent auditors required if EU contribution exceeds EUR 430,000

### Common Audit Findings

| Finding | Risk | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| Ineligible personnel costs | Timesheets incomplete or unsigned | Implement robust timesheet system |
| Double funding | Same costs charged to another project | Clear cost allocation methodology |
| Subcontracting without approval | Subcontract not foreseen in Annex 1 | Request amendment before engaging subcontractor |
| Insufficient documentation | Missing invoices, contracts | Maintain project filing system |

## FP10 and the Post-2027 Landscape

The current Horizon Europe programme (2021-2027) will be succeeded by FP10 (Horizon Europe 2028-2034). The Commission proposed a €175 billion budget for FP10 on 16 July 2025, nearly doubling the current €95.5B. FP10 will be "tightly connected" to the new European Competitiveness Fund (ECF, COM(2025)555), which consolidates 14 current MFF instruments into one framework. The ECF focuses on scaling up and deployment, while FP10 retains the mission of excellence-driven research and innovation.

For full details on FP10, the ECF, their linkage, and the ongoing legislative debate, see: `fp10_ecf_competitiveness.md`.
