## What It Is

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/401 consolidates and updates the list of Natura 2000 Sites of Community Importance (SCIs) across all 9 biogeographical regions: Alpine, Atlantic, Black Sea, Boreal, Continental, Macaronesian, Mediterranean, Pannonian, and Steppic.

Natura 2000 is the EU's network of protected natural areas, covering approximately 18% of EU land area and 8% of marine territory. It is the largest coordinated network of protected areas in the world, with over 27,000 sites across all Member States.

## Why It Matters

- SCIs are legally binding: Member States must designate them as Special Areas of Conservation (SACs) and implement conservation measures
- The list update adds new sites proposed by Member States and adjusts boundaries of existing sites
- This decision repeals 9 separate previous decisions, creating a single consolidated list (administrative simplification)
- Environmental impact assessments (EIA/SEA) must consider Natura 2000 sites, affecting infrastructure, energy, and construction projects

## Political Context

- Biodiversity and nature restoration are key pillars of the European Green Deal
- The Nature Restoration Regulation (EU) 2024/xxx set binding targets for ecosystem restoration
- Natura 2000 implementation is monitored via the State of Nature reporting (Article 17 Habitats Directive reports)
- DG ENV oversees Natura 2000 policy, with CINEA managing LIFE programme funding for site management
