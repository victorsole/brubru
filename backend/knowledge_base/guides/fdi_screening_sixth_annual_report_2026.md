# FDI screening: the Commission's sixth annual report (14 October 2026, COM(2026) 529): notifications up 40% in 2025

## QUICK FACTS
- **The document:** the Commission's **Sixth Annual Report on the screening of foreign direct investments into the Union**, **COM(2026) 529**, with Staff Working Document **SWD(2026) 290**, published by DG Trade and Economic Security on **14 October 2026**. It covers **calendar year 2025** under the current FDI Screening Regulation (EU) 2019/452.
- **Headline figures for 2025 (DG TRADE news item, 14 October 2026):**
  - Member States notified **667 investments** to the EU cooperation mechanism, up from **477 in 2024**: **+40%**, the largest annual rise since the mechanism started.
  - **88%** of the 667 cases were **closed within 15 days**.
  - Sectors with most notifications: **manufacturing 27%**, information and communication technologies **19%**, financial activities **17%**, wholesale and retail **13%**, professional activities **8%**.
  - **12%** of notified investments needed an **in-depth security risk assessment**, which the Commission describes as a 4% rise on the previous year. Of those in-depth cases, **manufacturing was 53%** (50% in 2024) and ICT almost a fifth.
  - The Commission issued **opinions in less than 2% of cases** (as in 2023 and 2024).
  - **Since late 2025 all 27 Member States have an FDI screening framework.**
- **The new regulation:** the **Foreign Investment Screening Regulation (FISR)** was **adopted on 17 June 2026** and **will apply from 17 January 2028**, replacing Regulation (EU) 2019/452 (in force since October 2020). These dates are DG TRADE's, stated in the 14 October item.
- **Label each figure with its year:** 667 and 40% are 2025 notifications; 477 is 2024. Do not present them as 2026 figures.
- **What was NOT read:** the report COM(2026) 529 and SWD(2026) 290 themselves (only the DG TRADE summary); no country breakdown, investor-origin figures or case names are given here, so give none.

## How to answer
"How many foreign investments were screened in the EU?": the sixth annual report (14 October 2026, COM(2026) 529) says Member States notified 667 investments to the Commission in 2025, 40% more than in 2024; 88% closed within 15 days, 12% needed an in-depth assessment, Commission opinions in under 2%. The new screening regulation applies from 17 January 2028.
