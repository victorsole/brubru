## CSDP architecture

The EU's **Common Security and Defence Policy** (CSDP) is an integral part of the **Common Foreign and Security Policy** (CFSP), established by **Articles 42-46 TEU** as amended by the Lisbon Treaty. CSDP enables the EU to:
- conduct civilian and military operations outside the Union under a UN mandate or with host-state consent
- develop the EU's defence-industrial base (handled in parallel by the European Defence Industry Programme + SAFE)
- progressively frame a common Union defence policy (Article 42(2) TEU) — the political ceiling above the operational mission portfolio

CSDP missions are deployed under Council Decisions adopted by **unanimity** in the Foreign Affairs Council (FAC). The HR/VP proposes; the Council decides; the EEAS (Crisis Management and Planning Directorate + Civilian Planning and Conduct Capability + EU Military Staff) operates.

## Current CSDP mission portfolio (12 May 2026 snapshot)

### Civilian missions (operating under EEAS Civilian Planning and Conduct Capability — CPCC)
- **EUAM Ukraine** — established Decision 2014/486/CFSP, Kyiv-based civilian advisory mission for security-sector reform; amended 11 May 2026 by 32026D1083
- **EUBAM Libya** — border assistance mission to Libya
- **EUBAM Rafah** — border assistance mission Rafah (Egypt-Gaza crossing), reactivated in late 2025
- **EUMM Georgia** — monitoring mission Georgia (post-2008 conflict)
- **EUCAP Sahel Mali** + **EUCAP Sahel Niger** — capacity-building missions (status under review post 2022-2024 Sahel coups)
- **EUCAP Somalia** + **EUTM Somalia** — Horn of Africa cluster
- **EUAM Iraq** + **EUAM CAR** + **EUAM RCA** — civilian advisory missions
- **EUPM Moldova** — Partnership Mission in Moldova (newer mission, 2023+)
- **EUAM Armenia** — Armenia mission (newer mission, post-Nagorno-Karabakh)

### Military missions (operating under EEAS EU Military Staff — EUMS)
- **EUFOR Althea** — Bosnia and Herzegovina (UN Chapter VII, since 2004) — successor to NATO SFOR
- **EUMAM Ukraine** — Military Assistance Mission Ukraine, established 2022 after Russia's full-scale invasion, training Ukrainian Armed Forces personnel on EU territory
- **EUMAM Mozambique** — Decision (CFSP) 2021/1143 + 32026D1084 amendments; training FADM in counter-insurgency
- **EUTM RCA** + **EUTM Mozambique** — training missions
- **EUNAVFOR Atalanta** — Indian Ocean / Gulf of Aden counter-piracy
- **EUNAVFOR ASPIDES** — Red Sea / Gulf of Aden maritime security (since February 2024)
- **EUNAVFOR MED Irini**: Mediterranean Sea, monitoring UNSC arms embargo on Libya. Since 2026 it also boards suspected Russian shadow-fleet vessels: on 28 September 2026 HR/VP Kallas said IRINI had conducted **six boardings in recent months** (the same six counted on 31 August, not six more), making it "the lead actor in boarding operations", with national boardings continuing in parallel and the existing UNCLOS framework used "to inspect and seize vessels" (EEAS transcript, Foreign Affairs Council (Defence), 28 Sep 2026).

## European Peace Facility (EPF)

The **European Peace Facility** is the EU's **off-budget** financial instrument established by **Council Decision (CFSP) 2021/509** of 22 March 2021 (CELEX **32021D0509**). It funds:
- common costs of military operations and missions under CSDP
- **assistance measures** to partner countries' armed forces (training, equipment including lethal weapons — a Lisbon-Treaty-stretching novelty)
- support to peace-support operations led by partners (African Union, UN)

EPF financial envelope: progressively topped up since 2021; current ceiling EUR 17 billion (2021-2027). The 12 May 2026 BiH assistance measure (32026D1082) is one of dozens of EPF-funded assistance measures since 2022 (Ukraine being the largest beneficiary, followed by Moldova + several African Union partners).

## Treaty basis

- **Article 42(1) TEU** — CSDP as integral part of CFSP, ensures Union's operational capacity drawing on civilian + military assets
- **Article 42(2) TEU** — progressive framing of a common Union defence policy (political ceiling)
- **Article 42(7) TEU** — mutual assistance clause (activated by France post-Bataclan 2015)
- **Article 43 TEU** — Petersberg tasks (humanitarian, rescue, peace-keeping, peace-making, conflict prevention, military advice and assistance, post-conflict stabilisation)
- **Article 44 TEU** — Council may entrust a mission to a group of Member States
- **Article 46 TEU** — Permanent Structured Cooperation (PESCO)
- **Article 28 TEU** — Operational decisions in CFSP (the legal home of EPF assistance measures)

## How this flows through Brubru answers

When a user asks about EUMAM, EUAM, EUTM, EUFOR, EUNAVFOR, EUBAM, or EPF:
- Cite the **specific Council Decision** + CELEX as legal anchor (e.g. Decision 2014/486/CFSP for EUAM Ukraine; Decision 2021/1143 for EUMAM Mozambique; Decision 2021/509 for EPF)
- Distinguish **civilian** (CPCC) vs **military** (EUMS) chain of command
- Distinguish **operational missions** (CSDP) vs **financial assistance measures** (EPF assistance measures)
- Cross-link to the **European Defence Union** institutional file (AFCO 2025/2212(INI)) if the user is asking about the broader treaty-architecture question
- Avoid conflating CSDP missions (foreign-policy operational instrument) with **NATO** missions (Atlantic Alliance treaty obligation) — these are distinct, even where Member-State force contributions overlap
