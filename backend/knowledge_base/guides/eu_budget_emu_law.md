## Multiannual Financial Framework (MFF) 2021-2027

### Structure

The MFF (Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2020/2093) sets maximum annual amounts (ceilings) for EU spending across seven headings:

| Heading | 2021-2027 Ceiling (EUR billion, 2018 prices) | Key Programmes |
|---------|----------------------------------------------|----------------|
| 1. Single Market, Innovation and Digital | 149.5 | Horizon Europe, Digital Europe, InvestEU, CEF |
| 2a. Cohesion | 330.2 | ERDF, ESF+, Cohesion Fund |
| 2b. Resilience and Values | 107.4 | RRF (NextGenerationEU), Erasmus+, Creative Europe, EU4Health |
| 3. Natural Resources and Environment | 378.9 | CAP, LIFE, Just Transition Fund |
| 4. Migration and Border Management | 25.7 | AMIF, BMVI, EBCGA |
| 5. Security and Defence | 14.9 | ISF, EDF |
| 6. Neighbourhood and the World | 110.6 | NDICI, IPA III, HUMA |
| 7. European Public Administration | 82.5 | EU institutions operating costs |
| **Total** | **1,211.0** | |

### NextGenerationEU (NGEU)

Exceptional recovery instrument of EUR 806.9 billion (current prices):
- **Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF)**: EUR 723.8 billion (EUR 338 billion grants + EUR 385.8 billion loans)
- **ReactEU**: EUR 50.6 billion (top-up for cohesion funds)
- **Rural development, Just Transition Fund, InvestEU, RescEU, Horizon Europe**: remaining amounts

### MFF Revision

The MFF can be revised by Council (unanimity, EP consent). Mid-term revision occurred in 2024 to address unforeseen needs (Ukraine, migration, competitiveness).

## Own Resources Decision (2020/2053)

### Revenue Sources

| Own Resource | Description | Annual Yield Estimate |
|--------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Traditional own resources** | Customs duties (25% retained by Member States for collection costs) | ~EUR 22 billion |
| **VAT-based** | 0.30% of harmonised VAT base (capped at 50% of GNI) | ~EUR 18 billion |
| **GNI-based** | Residual resource, balances the budget | ~EUR 105 billion |
| **Plastics-based** | EUR 0.80 per kg of non-recycled plastic packaging waste | ~EUR 6 billion |

### New Own Resources (Proposed/Adopted)

The Own Resources Decision authorises the introduction of new own resources linked to:
- EU Emissions Trading System (ETS) revenues
- Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) revenues
- Reallocated multinational corporate profits (OECD Pillar I)
- Financial transaction tax (under enhanced cooperation)

These require a separate Council decision (ongoing negotiations).

## Annual Budgetary Procedure (Art. 314 TFEU)

### Timeline

| Phase | Timing | Actor |
|-------|--------|-------|
| **Commission Draft Budget** | June | Commission |
| **Council position** | September-October | Council |
| **EP amendments** | October | European Parliament |
| **Conciliation** | November (21 days) | Joint EP-Council committee |
| **Adoption** | December | EP and Council |

### Key Principles

- **Annuality**: Budget covers one calendar year (carry-overs allowed for differentiated appropriations)
- **Universality**: All revenue covers all expenditure (no earmarking, with exceptions)
- **Equilibrium**: Revenue must equal expenditure (no deficit)
- **Unity**: All revenue and expenditure in a single document
- **Specification**: Appropriations specified by purpose (budget lines)

## Discharge Procedure (Art. 319 TFEU)

### Process

1. **European Court of Auditors (ECA)** publishes annual report and Statement of Assurance (DAS)
2. **Council** issues recommendation to EP (February-March)
3. **EP CONT Committee** examines accounts, conducts hearings with Commissioners
4. **EP plenary** votes to grant, postpone, or refuse discharge (April-May)

### Consequences of Refusal

- Political pressure on Commission (no legal obligation to resign)
- Only happened once in history (1998 Santer Commission -- resigned before formal refusal)
- Commission must report on corrective actions taken

## Anti-Fraud Mechanisms

### OLAF -- European Anti-Fraud Office (Regulation 883/2013)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Mandate** | Administrative investigations of fraud, corruption, other irregularities affecting EU financial interests |
| **Independence** | Operationally independent; reports to Supervisory Committee |
| **Investigative powers** | On-the-spot checks, access to documents, interviews |
| **Internal investigations** | Within EU institutions (staff misconduct, procurement fraud) |
| **External investigations** | In Member States (customs fraud, structural fund irregularities) |
| **Coordination cases** | Coordinates national investigations without direct powers |
| **Outcome** | Recommendations to national authorities for judicial action; recommendations to EU institutions for financial recovery |

### EPPO -- European Public Prosecutor's Office (Regulation 2017/1939)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Mandate** | Criminal investigation and prosecution of crimes against EU financial interests (PIF crimes) |
| **Jurisdiction** | 22 participating Member States (enhanced cooperation) |
| **PIF crimes** | Fraud, corruption, money laundering, VAT fraud (> EUR 10 million cross-border) |
| **Structure** | Central office (Luxembourg) + European Delegated Prosecutors (EDPs) in each Member State |
| **Investigative powers** | Full criminal investigation powers under national law (searches, wiretaps, asset freezing) |
| **Complementarity with OLAF** | EPPO handles criminal prosecution; OLAF handles administrative investigations and non-participating MS |

### Complementarity Matrix

| Situation | OLAF | EPPO | National Authorities |
|-----------|------|------|---------------------|
| VAT fraud < EUR 10M (participating MS) | Administrative investigation | May take over if cross-border | Primary investigator |
| VAT fraud > EUR 10M (participating MS) | Complementary investigation | Primary prosecutor | Assist EPPO (EDPs) |
| Structural fund fraud (participating MS) | Administrative investigation | Criminal prosecution | Implement EPPO decisions |
| Fraud in non-participating MS | Administrative investigation | No jurisdiction | Criminal prosecution |
| EU staff misconduct | Internal investigation | If PIF crime | N/A |

## European Stability Mechanism (ESM)

### Legal Basis

Intergovernmental treaty (outside EU legal order but linked to TFEU Art. 136(3) amendment).

### Instruments

| Instrument | Purpose | Conditionality |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| **Stability support loans** | General budget financing | Macroeconomic adjustment programme |
| **Precautionary credit line (PCCL)** | Preventive for countries with sound fundamentals | Enhanced surveillance |
| **Enhanced conditions credit line (ECCL)** | Countries with weaknesses not threatening stability | Corrective measures |
| **Bank recapitalisation** | Direct or indirect bank recapitalisation | Banking sector reform |
| **Primary market purchases** | Purchase sovereign bonds on primary market | Agreed adjustment programme |
| **Secondary market purchases** | Purchase sovereign bonds on secondary market | Macroeconomic adjustment |

### Backstop to SRF

The ESM provides a common backstop to the Single Resolution Fund (SRF) for banking crises, available from January 2024 (ratification completed).

## Stability and Growth Pact (Reformed)

### New Economic Governance Framework (2024)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Reference values** | 3% GDP deficit, 60% GDP debt (unchanged) |
| **National fiscal-structural plans** | 4-5 year plans (7 years with reforms) setting expenditure paths |
| **Net expenditure benchmark** | Single operational indicator replacing multiple rules |
| **Debt sustainability analysis** | Country-specific, using stochastic projections |
| **Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)** | Activated by 3% deficit breach; Council recommendation, corrective action |
| **Country-specific adjustment** | Minimum 0.5% structural adjustment for high-debt countries |

### European Semester Budgetary Surveillance

The Semester provides the annual cycle for fiscal coordination:
1. **Autumn Package** (November): Annual Sustainable Growth Survey, Alert Mechanism Report
2. **Country Reports** (February-March): In-depth economic analysis per Member State
3. **National Reform Programmes** (April): Member State plans for structural reforms
4. **Country-Specific Recommendations** (May-June): Council recommendations on fiscal and structural policies
5. **Implementation assessment** (ongoing): Commission monitors compliance
