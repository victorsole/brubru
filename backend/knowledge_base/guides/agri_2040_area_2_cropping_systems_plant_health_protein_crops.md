# AgRI 2040 area 2 of 8: cropping systems (plant health, breeding, protein crops)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** second of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). The Commission web page words it "cropping systems, plant health and protein crops". Preliminary, no obligation.
- **Purpose:** **climate-smart, sustainable, resilient and competitive cropping systems**.
- **Stress adaptation:** drought, salinity and extreme temperatures, including through agroecological approaches.
- **Plant health:** preparedness for emerging threats; rapid tools for **prevention, early detection, treatment and biological control of plant pests**.
- **Low-risk crop protection:** integrated approaches combining agroecological and agronomic practices with biological solutions, low-risk alternatives, **breeding, biotechnology, precision technologies and digital tools**.
- **Breeding and genetic resources:** plant breeding, seeds, plant varieties, biotechnology; development or reintroduction of climate-smart, resource-efficient, resilient, resistant and diverse varieties, including **neglected and underutilised species**, "with due consideration of the legal and policy context". The Communication's footnote for that context is **Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 of 17 June 2026 on plants obtained by certain new genomic techniques and their products** (CELEX 32026R1388, title checked at the Publications Office).
- **Protein crops:** a strategic component of a more resilient and sustainable **EU protein system**: agronomic performance, processing, nutritional value, food, feed and circular value chains.
