# 2028-2034 EU Programmes: Parliament's Committee Positions of 10 November 2026 (Competitiveness Fund, Horizon Europe, Connecting Europe Facility, Social Fund)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status: COMMITTEE positions, NOT Parliament's position and NOT law.** On Tuesday 10 November 2026 Parliament's committees voted their negotiating positions on four spending programmes of the 2028-2034 long-term budget. Each still goes to plenary, then to talks with the Council. Every figure below is the COMMITTEE's, in current prices, with the Commission's proposed figure beside it. Source: Parliament's four press releases of 10 November 2026.
- **European Competitiveness Fund, 2025/0555(COD), Industry, Research and Energy Committee (ITRE): adopted 61 votes to 13 with 12 abstentions.** Committee: **EUR 264.35 billion** (Commission: EUR 234.3 billion). New **EUR 10.11 billion flexibility reserve**. InvestEU guarantee ceiling **EUR 150 billion** (Commission: EUR 70 billion). Own envelope and governance for critical raw materials; a section for energy infrastructure; a defence research programme in three instruments; binding minimum allocations per policy window. The Fund pools 14 existing instruments into four policy windows. Mandate for talks approved 56 to 26 with 4 abstentions; to be announced at the 23-26 November 2026 plenary.
- **Horizon Europe 2028-2034, 2025/0543(COD), ITRE:** Committee: **EUR 200 billion** (Commission: EUR 175 billion); in 2025 prices EUR 177 billion. Budget guarantees for the European Research Council and the European Innovation Council; transfers within pillars capped at 10%; a high-level council of at least 30 independent experts; "fast track to excellence" and "fast track to innovation" schemes. The vote tally is NOT in the press release.
- **Connecting Europe Facility 2028-2034, 2025/0221(COD), joint ITRE and Transport (TRAN) committees: adopted 101 votes to 1 with 17 abstentions.** Committee: **EUR 91.28 billion** (Commission: EUR 81.42 billion), in three pillars: **transport EUR 37.96 billion, military mobility EUR 19.78 billion, energy EUR 33.53 billion**. Higher co-funding for projects serving several objectives.
- **European Social Fund 2028-2034, 2025/0239(COD), Employment and Social Affairs Committee (EMPL): adopted 40 votes to 6 with 10 abstentions.** Committee: keep the Fund as a **stand-alone programme with EUR 124.19 billion**, run through dedicated chapters in the National and Regional Partnership Plans; **at least 5% for the Child Guarantee**. 5% of EUR 124.19 billion is about EUR 6.2 billion; a different amount quoted by an MEP on social media (EUR 20 billion) is NOT in the press release.
- **Why these numbers are higher than the Commission's:** Parliament wants a larger 2028-2034 budget overall; the Council has not agreed any of these figures.
- **What was NOT read:** the adopted committee texts and the voting lists; the Horizon Europe specific programme (2025/0544(CNS)) and Euratom votes of the same day; rapporteurs are not listed here because they were not checked on the Legislative Observatory.
