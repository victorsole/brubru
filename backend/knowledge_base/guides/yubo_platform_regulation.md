## CORE REGULATORY STACK — teen-focused platform = DSA Art 28 defines the entire posture
- **Digital Services Act Art 28 (minor protection)** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — "appropriate and proportionate measures" for privacy/safety of minors, NO profiling-based ads to minors. Yubo's defining regulation.
- **CSAM Temporary Derogation** (Reg 2021/1232, CELEX 32021R1232) — **EXPIRED 3 Apr 2026**. Live-video + DM + teen user base = extreme exposure.
- **CSAM Regulation** (COM(2022) 209) — Yubo's biggest risk AND biggest necessity. Detection orders on live video and DMs would be onerous, but status quo (no derogation) is worse.
- **Combating Sexual Abuse Directive** (Dir 2011/92, CELEX 32011L0092; recast COM(2024) 60 in trilogue).
- **GDPR Art 8** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679) — minors' consent threshold varies 13-16 by MS (France = 15; DE, IT, PL, CZ = 16; ES, BE, DK, FI, PT, LV, SE, LT, BG, RO, UK-legacy = 13; NL = 12 for parental then 16). Yubo must honour every threshold.
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — Yubo's age-estimation (Yoti-based) triggers the Annex III 1(a) biometric-categorisation debate. DG CNECT's informal Nov 2025 view: age is not within narrow sensitive-attribute list. **Unresolved.**
- **AVMSD** (Dir 2018/1808, CELEX 32018L1808) — Stage-style live video is VSP under Art 28a-28b; age-appropriate design is a MS-level obligation.
- **ePrivacy Directive** (Dir 2002/58, CELEX 32002L0058) — confidentiality of DMs + cookie consent.
- **NIS2** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555) — Annex I Sector 8 covers Yubo; French ANSSI.
- **Cyber Resilience Act** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847) — Yubo iOS/Android apps.
- **Digital Fairness Act** — addictive design for teens is an explicit EC concern.
- **Digital Content Directive** (Dir 2019/770, CELEX 32019L0770) — premium features.
- **P2B Regulation** (Reg 2019/1150, CELEX 32019R1150) — creator/streamer economy.
- **Representative Actions** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828) — child-safety claims pathway.
- **TERREG** (Reg 2021/784, CELEX 32021R0784).
- **eIDAS 2** (Reg 2024/1183, CELEX 32024R1183) — EUDI Wallet age-attribute for French loi Studer compliance.

## FRANCE-SPECIFIC NATIONAL LAYER
- **Loi SREN** (French DSA transposition): ARCOM competent; overlaps with Irish DSC where Yubo is also established.
- **Loi Studer** (social networks + under-15): parental authorisation requirement for minors under 15.
- **CNIL** (French DPA): lead supervisory authority for French-headquartered Yubo.
- **ARCOM**: VSP + audiovisual competent authority.
- **ANSSI**: NIS2 competent authority.
- Yubo operates under the **highest national regulatory density** of any platform in this campaign — no Irish-style buffer.

## URGENT COMPLIANCE
- CSAM derogation expired 3 Apr 2026.
- DSA Art 28 in force 17 Feb 2024; Arcom + French DSC may enforce.
- NIS2 transposition passed 17 Oct 2024.
- Loi SREN + Loi Studer already applied in France.
