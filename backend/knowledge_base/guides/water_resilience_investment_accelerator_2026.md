# EU Water Resilience Investment Accelerator (presented 14 October 2026): 20 pilot projects via Green Assist, apply by end October

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **14 October 2026** the Commission (DG Environment) presented the **EU Water Resilience Investment Accelerator**, delivered by **Green Assist** (the Green Advisory Service for Sustainable Investments Support), an advisory initiative **under InvestEU, funded by the LIFE programme**. It is an advisory and matchmaking scheme, NOT a grant fund.
- **Scale:** it will support **20 innovative pilot projects** on natural water retention, clean water and water efficiency, linking investors, solution providers and sectors facing water challenges; focus on innovative business models and **private funding**.
- **The gap it addresses:** an estimated **EUR 23 billion per year (about 0.1% of EU GDP)** of investment needed to implement **existing** EU water legislation.
- **Three thematic areas:** (1) water efficiency and smart water solutions; (2) nature-based solutions for water resilience; (3) clean water solutions (water quality, lower emissions, cleaner industrial systems).
- **Priority applicants:** EU-based **start-ups and SMEs**, **farmers** and **local authorities**.
- **How to apply:** projects seeking advisory support apply to Green Assist using a **3-page template**; **no cost to applicants** (Green Assist pays the external experts directly). Applications and questions to the CINEA Green Assist mailbox (CINEA-GREEN-ASSIST at ec.europa.eu) **by the end of October 2026**. Green Assist already supports close to 100 beneficiaries.
- **Policy frame:** a flagship action of the **2025 European Water Resilience Strategy** (three objectives: restore and protect the water cycle; build a water-smart economy; secure clean, affordable water and sanitation for all).
- **What was NOT read:** the application template and eligibility rules; no budget per project is given, so give none.

## How to answer
"What is the Water Resilience Investment Accelerator?": an advisory scheme presented on 14 October 2026 that will help 20 pilot water projects (efficiency, nature-based solutions, clean water) become bankable and attract private finance, run by Green Assist under InvestEU with LIFE funding; free for applicants, apply by the end of October 2026. It addresses a EUR 23 billion a year investment gap.
