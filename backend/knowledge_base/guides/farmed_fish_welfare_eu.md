# Farmed Fish Welfare in the EU (DG SANTE Report 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Animal welfare for **farmed fish** in EU aquaculture -- regulatory framework, species-specific practices, slaughter standards, and the Commission's ongoing assessment of gaps vs. other farmed animals
- **LATEST (23 April 2026)**: DG SANTE published "New Report Highlights Gaps and Opportunities in Farmed Fish Welfare across Europe" (DG SANTE Audits and Analysis portal, ec.europa.eu/food/audits-analysis/news/details/164) -- systematic mapping of Member State practice across salmon, trout, sea bream, sea bass, carp, and tuna farming
- Commissioner: **Oliver Várhelyi** (Health and Animal Welfare) since December 2024 -- the "Animal Welfare" addition to the portfolio name is deliberate: Várhelyi's brief explicitly covers welfare legislation revision
- Lead DG: **DG SANTE** (Health and Food Safety, Directorate G -- Crisis preparedness in food, animals and plants, Unit G5 -- Animal welfare)
- Lead EP committees: **AGRI** (Agriculture and Rural Development) + **ENVI** (Environment, Public Health and Food Safety) + **PECH** (Fisheries) for aquaculture angle
- Council: AGRIFISH (Agriculture and Fisheries Council)
- Current legal framework: **Council Regulation (EC) No 1099/2009** on the protection of animals at the time of killing (slaughter; CELEX 32009R1099) -- applies to fish only in a limited way (Article 3(1): general obligation to spare unnecessary pain); **no species-specific fish slaughter rules** in Union law
- EU Aquaculture guidelines: Commission Communication COM(2021) 236 "Strategic Guidelines for a more sustainable and competitive EU aquaculture for the period 2021 to 2030" -- includes welfare dimension but no binding rules
- Missing framework: **no EU-wide legislation on farmed fish welfare during rearing**; fish do not have a dedicated welfare directive like calves (Directive 2008/119/EC), pigs (Directive 2008/120/EC), or laying hens (Directive 1999/74/EC)
- Scale: EU aquaculture produced **1 million tonnes in 2024** (Eurostat, 23 April 2026 release) -- production concentrated in Spain (mussels + sea bream), France (oysters), Greece (sea bream + sea bass), Italy (trout + sea bass), Ireland (salmon), Denmark (rainbow trout)

## Why this matters

Farmed fish welfare is one of the **most underregulated animal welfare areas** in the EU. The 23 April 2026 DG SANTE report confirms:

1. **No species-specific stunning rules**: unlike warm-blooded animals, where CO2, electrical, or mechanical stunning is prescribed, fish slaughter methods vary (percussive, electrical, ice-slurry, CO2 -- with welfare implications varying by species)
2. **High stocking density** in some systems (notably salmon cages) linked to aggression, injuries, and disease vulnerability
3. **Transport and handling stress** during harvest, live transport to markets, and restocking
4. **Sea lice infestation** (salmon) and treatment welfare trade-offs
5. **Sentience recognition**: peer-reviewed science has established fish sentience (pain perception, stress responses) -- EU recognised via TFEU Article 13 (animals as sentient beings) but implementation gap persists

The **EU Citizens' Initiative "End the Cage Age"** (successfully registered 2020) did not cover fish, leaving a political blind spot. EFSA Scientific Opinions on Atlantic salmon, rainbow trout, European sea bass, gilthead sea bream, and common carp welfare (2008-2009, updated 2023) provide the evidence base but have not been translated into binding rules.

## Commission's animal welfare revision (in progress)

The Commission's **comprehensive revision of the animal welfare acquis** (announced under the Farm to Fork Strategy, COM(2020) 381) was split into packages:

- **Package 1** (7 December 2023): Regulation on welfare of dogs and cats, Directive on protection of animals during transport, Regulation on welfare during slaughter (partial scope)
- **Package 2** (delayed, expected 2026-2027): **Species-specific farm welfare rules** -- this is where farmed fish welfare would sit

The 23 April 2026 DG SANTE report is positioned as **preparatory work for Package 2** -- mapping gaps and options before a legislative proposal.
