# EU Waste Shipment Regulation (WSR) and DIWASS Digital System

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Thursday 21 May 2026 — DIWASS GO-LIVE + NEW WSR APPLYING)**: DG ENV announced today that the new Waste Shipment Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 is now **fully applying across the EU** and that the **DIWASS Digital Waste Shipment System** is **operational** as the single mandatory channel for waste shipment notifications and Annex VII forms. Source: DG ENV news item "New Waste Shipment Regulation and DIWASS platform go live" (21 May 2026, ec.europa.eu/environment). Operators shipping waste cross-border within the EU OR for export/import must use DIWASS from this date; the legacy paper / national-portal channels are no longer accepted (subject to limited Annex VII transitional rules). Annex VII forms must reach the system **at least two working days before** the start of the shipment. The 8-association industry letter of 28 January 2026 requesting a 1-year transition was not accommodated; SME-targeted guidance has been published in parallel.

## What's new in the 2024 WSR

The 2024 WSR is a major rewrite of EU waste-shipment law, structured around three policy goals: (1) keep more waste in the EU for recycling, (2) align with the European Green Deal + Circular Economy Action Plan, (3) digitalise notifications and traceability.

### Tighter rules on exports outside the EU
- **Hazardous waste**: still prohibited to non-OECD countries (Basel Convention rule)
- **Non-hazardous waste for recovery to non-OECD countries**: from 21 May 2027, only allowed to countries that have **demonstrated capacity to handle waste in environmentally sound manner** and that have explicitly notified the Commission
- **Plastic waste exports to non-OECD countries**: prohibited from 21 May 2026 (with limited derogations)
- **Plastic waste exports to OECD countries**: stricter notification + monitoring; review clause for full prohibition

### Intra-EU shipments digitalised via DIWASS
- All notifications (Article 4) and Annex VII forms (Article 18) handled electronically through DIWASS from 21 May 2026
- Member State competent authorities + notifiers + carriers + recipients all connect to DIWASS
- Annex VII (information for shipments under Article 18 — green-listed waste, no notification required) must be **lodged ≥2 working days before shipment starts**
- DIWASS will be open **voluntarily** to third-country authorities and operators (UK exporters, for example)

### Stronger enforcement
- New rules on **inspections** and **risk-based checks** by Member State authorities
- Cross-border enforcement cooperation strengthened via DG ENV + the European Union Network for the Implementation and Enforcement of Environmental Law (IMPEL)
- Penalties for illegal shipments harmonised at higher floor

## LATEST (29 April 2026) — Switzerland exception proposal

The Commission proposed an **adjustment to the WSR to continue exports of municipal waste to Switzerland** for efficient waste treatment beyond 21 May 2026 (DG ENV midday express, 29 April 2026). The amendment recognises Switzerland's high environmental standards and operational interconnection with EU border-region waste-treatment plants. Procedural follow-up in Council + EP expected through Q3 2026.

## Industry concerns surfaced before the 21 May 2026 deadline

Per the joint letter to Commissioner Roswall (28 January 2026, Recycling Europe + 7 associations):

1. **Request: 1-year DIWASS transition period** — operators able to submit by post/email during transition without penalties; required during DIWASS rollout teething phase
2. **Request: repeal of the 2-working-day Annex VII pre-submission rule** — operationally disruptive for cross-border recyclers handling rapid material flows
3. **Request: keep current Annex VII form for paper procedures** — the new Annex VII (signature-heavy, designed for digital) is impractical on paper

These requests are NOT (yet) reflected in the Commission's published implementing acts — companies should plan for full enforcement from 21 May 2026 unless the Commission publishes a transition note in May 2026.

## Compliance checklist for waste-shipment operators

By 21 May 2026, operators handling shipments under WSR scope must:

- [ ] Register on DIWASS (notifiers + recipients + carriers)
- [ ] Adapt internal IT systems to feed shipment data into DIWASS
- [ ] Update legal templates: Annex VII, contracts of consignment, financial guarantees
- [ ] Train operational staff on the 2-working-day Annex VII pre-submission rule
- [ ] Audit cross-border supply chains for non-OECD plastic-waste flows (prohibited from 21 May 2026)
- [ ] If exporting to OECD non-EU: verify destination capacity status under Article 39
- [ ] Update due-diligence procedures under the Critical Raw Materials Act + Battery Regulation cross-references (battery waste streams, etc.)
