# EU Critical Communication System (EUCCS): proposal of 30 September 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** a Commission legislative proposal of **30 September 2026** to build a **European Union Critical Communication System (EUCCS)**: a secure, EU-wide communication service that lets **police, firefighters, emergency medical services and civil protection teams** talk to each other in real time across borders, including when land networks fail. Presented by Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen and Commissioner Magnus Brunner.
- **STATUS (1 October 2026): PROPOSAL only.** The Commission says "it is now for the European Parliament and the Council to adopt the **Regulations**" (plural: the package has more than one legal act). The COM numbers and OEIL procedure references were **not yet published** in Cellar or OEIL on 1 October 2026; do not quote any until they are. Nothing is adopted; nothing applies.
- **Timing:** gradual launch, with a transition period for Member States to connect **starting in 2030** (Commission).
- **Coverage:** all **27 Member States and the Schengen associated countries** ("all 31 countries", Commission).
- **What changes for responders:** a gradual move from **narrowband radio to broadband (5G)**; **EU satellites as back-up** when land networks are down (for example in a blackout); voice, video, messages, data and **geolocation in real time on one secure device**.
- **Infrastructure:** a **European Union Network Monitoring Centre** (a secure optical-fibre network so each country connects through one central point instead of many bilateral links) and a **European Union Security Monitoring Centre** (cyber-risk tracking, threat assessment, support to Member States). Encryption and a strong protocol against cyber and hybrid attacks.
- **Sovereignty:** the critical components (satellites, core mobile network, mobile broadband technology) are **developed by European companies**; the Commission says the system could also serve other services, **such as the armed forces**.
- **Governance:** Member States keep **full control of their national networks**; the system is governed through a **Member States' expert group**.
- **Where it comes from:** a key action of **ProtectEU**, the European Internal Security Strategy (April 2025), and of the **Preparedness Union Strategy** (March 2025), following the **Niinistö Report** on preparedness, which urged completing the system as soon as possible. Announced in President von der Leyen's political guidelines.

## Why it matters
The Commission's diagnosis: national critical-communication systems are neither technically compatible nor interlinked, so responders' systems "stop working when they cross a border" and emergency services often cannot communicate with each other during a rescue. Fires, floods, storms, drone threats to critical infrastructure and terrorist attacks are the cases the Commission names.
