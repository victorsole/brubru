# The five co-funded European partnerships for agri-food research (SWD(2026) 271)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Five co-funded partnerships** serve agri-food R&I under Horizon Europe: **Agroecology**, **Animal Health and Welfare (EUPAHW)**, **Agriculture of Data (AgData)**, **Forests (FOREST)** and **FutureFoodS**. Together: **421 participants (242 unique)** from **25 Member States and 11 associated countries**, budget **almost EUR 700 million, almost EUR 250 million from the EU**; after top-ups to Agroecology, EUPAHW and FutureFoodS, **over EUR 1,300 million, over EUR 500 million EU**. Strategic agendas were mostly co-created with the Standing Committee on Agricultural Research (SCAR).
- **Agroecology partnership** ("accelerating farming systems transition: agroecology living labs and research infrastructure"): **1 January 2024 to 31 December 2033**; coordinated by **Germany (Forschungszentrum Julich)**; tentative budget **EUR 300 million, half EU**; **111 partners, 31 countries**; **3 calls** published, **38 projects** running; a network of agroecology living labs and research infrastructures with **55 members**; a key action of the **farm to fork strategy**; builds a monitoring and data framework and a science-policy dialogue.
- **Animal Health and Welfare (EUPAHW):** launched **2024**; **EUR 360 million EU co-funding over ten years**; **55 projects** in 2026; "one health and one welfare"; the European Food Safety Authority and the European Medicines Agency take part in risk assessment. **Five priority areas:** surveillance and risk assessment; methods and tools; on-farm, transport and slaughter guidelines including aquaculture; **vaccines and treatments**; integrated approach with socio-economic aspects.
- **Agriculture of Data (AgData):** started **1 October 2025**; at least **7 years**; tentative budget **EUR 300 million, 30% EU co-funding**; national funded use cases plus transnational open-call projects; a strategic research and innovation agenda.
- **FOREST** (forests, forestry and the forest-based sector): ten years **2026-2036**; **100 organisations from 28 countries** at the start; **seven co-funded joint calls**, an estimated **200-250 transnational projects** and about 400 stakeholders; living labs; based on the strategic agenda of the EUFORE project.
- **FutureFoodS** (food systems): launched **June 2024**; **83 partners, 29 countries**; EU about **EUR 95 million (30%)**, Member States about EUR 200 million; Knowledge Hub of Living Labs and a food systems observatory; prepared by the coordination action FoodPathS.
- **Not a law and not a call:** partnerships are existing Horizon Europe instruments; COM(2026) 453 says the Commission will explore how to leverage partnerships further under Horizon Europe 2028-2034.
