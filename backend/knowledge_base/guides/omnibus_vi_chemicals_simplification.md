# Omnibus VI -- Chemicals Simplification

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Commission omnibus proposal simplifying EU chemicals legislation -- part of the 2026 simplification agenda. Sits alongside other omnibus packages (Omnibus I-V on sustainability reporting, omnibus on food/feed)
- Common names: "Omnibus VI", "Chemicals Omnibus", "Chemicals Simplification Omnibus"
- Commission proposal: expected Q2-Q3 2026 (announced as part of the 2026 simplification agenda)
- Legal basis: Articles 114 + 192 TFEU (internal market + environment)
- Lead DG: DG GROW (Internal Market) + DG ENV (Environment)
- Key agency: ECHA (European Chemicals Agency, Helsinki)
- Responsible Commissioners: Stéphane Séjourné (EVP Prosperity and Industrial Strategy), Jessika Roswall (Environment)
- EP lead committee: ENVI (Environment, Climate and Food Safety); ITRE opinion
- Commission simplification target: 25% reduction in reporting obligations (35% for SMEs) -- part of the broader Competitiveness Compass target
- EPRS At a Glance (20 April 2026): "Omnibus VI -- Simplification of requirements and procedures for chemicals" -- summarises Commission's planned simplification of REACH, CLP, biocides, POPs. Ref: EPRS_UNK_2987933f8f20
- Core legislation targeted: REACH (Regulation (EC) 1907/2006, CELEX 32006R1907), CLP (Regulation (EC) 1272/2008, CELEX 32008R1272), BPR (Regulation (EU) 528/2012, CELEX 32012R0528), POPs (Regulation (EU) 2019/1021, CELEX 32019R1021), Detergents (Regulation (EC) 648/2004), Fertilising Products (Regulation (EU) 2019/1009, CELEX 32019R1009)
- Related ongoing revision: REACH targeted revision -- COM(2023) 245, procedure 2023/0234(COD)
- Expected simplification levers: digital-first submissions, single ECHA submission portal, reduced duplicative reporting, clearer requirements for SMEs, streamlined authorisation/restriction procedures, alignment with CLP hazard classes (2023 update)

## Context -- Why Omnibus VI

The 2026 simplification agenda is the Commission's flagship response to the Draghi report and the Competitiveness Compass (COM(2025) 30). The EU chemicals rulebook is one of the densest in the world and has been flagged by industry and Member States as a major compliance burden for SMEs. Omnibus VI is expected to focus on:

- **Reporting simplification**: consolidating multiple reporting streams (REACH dossier updates, CLP notifications, biocides annual reports, POPs notifications) into a single harmonised data platform at ECHA
- **Authorisation process**: faster tracking for substances already authorised under equivalent non-EU regimes; clearer sunset date procedures
- **Biocides**: reducing overlap with REACH; simplified authorisation for low-risk active substances
- **SMEs**: proportionate fees, simplified data-sharing rules under REACH Article 27, improved ECHA guidance
- **Substance information**: clearer public access to safety data sheets (SDS) in digital format

## Positioning vs REACH Targeted Revision (2023/0234(COD))

Omnibus VI is procedurally distinct from the REACH Targeted Revision. Where REACH Targeted Revision modifies substantive obligations (restrictions, essential uses concept, generic risk management for most hazardous substances), Omnibus VI focuses on procedural/administrative simplification. Expect cross-references but not merger.
