# EU Climate Action Progress Report 2026 and Carbon Market Report (9 November 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Published Monday 9 November 2026 by the Commission:** the 2026 EU Climate Action Progress Report, **COM(2026) 601**, and the 2026 Carbon Market Report, **COM(2026) 596** (press release IP/26/2343). These are REPORTS: they create no new obligation by themselves.
- **Emissions (provisional data, the Commission's):** EU net greenhouse gas emissions fell **1.6% in 2025** compared with 2024 and are now **39% below 1990**, while the economy grew 74% over the same period. Emissions under the Emissions Trading System fell about 2%; emissions under the Effort Sharing Regulation stayed broadly stable.
- **2030 is at risk in the effort-sharing sectors** (domestic transport, buildings, agriculture, waste): they are expected to fall about **38.5% by 2030 compared with 2005, below the EU-wide target of 40%**. The overall 2030 target stays at least 55% net below 1990.
- **Nine Member States are NOT making sufficient progress towards their 2030 effort-sharing targets. The Commission will ask each for a corrective action plan: Austria, Croatia, Cyprus, Germany, Italy, Ireland, Lithuania, Malta and Sweden.** Exactly nine. The plans are requested, NOT yet submitted, as of 9 November 2026.
- **Land sector:** net removals up about 11% in 2025, yet a projected gap of 40 to 55 million tonnes of CO2 equivalent to the 2030 land-use target.
- **Carbon market:** EU ETS revenues **EUR 43.2 billion in 2025, up 11%** on 2024; EUR 23.9 billion went directly to Member States. Industrial installations -3.3%, maritime transport -4%, aviation +0.2%. The ETS target is a 62% cut by 2030 compared with 2005.
- **Clean technologies in 2025:** 1.9 million battery-electric cars registered (about one in six new cars); wind and solar capacity 611 GW (about 49% of electricity generation capacity); 41 GW of heat pumps installed (26 GW in 2024).
- **Climate damage:** EUR 822 billion in economic losses from climate-related extremes in the EU since 1980; about EUR 70 billion a year of adaptation investment needed up to 2050.
- **Context:** the amended European Climate Law, in force since April 2026, sets a binding 90% net cut by 2040; in July 2026 the Commission proposed the ETS reform for 2030-2040.
- **What was NOT read:** the two reports and their staff working documents themselves; every figure above is from the press release. The corrective action plan procedure is in the Effort Sharing Regulation (EU) 2018/842.
