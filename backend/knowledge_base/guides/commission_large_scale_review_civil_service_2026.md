# Commission Large-Scale Review: the High-Level Group report "The Future of the Commission's Civil Service" (Catherine Day, 26 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Transparency (report pp. 21-22, read 28 October 2026): under Recommendation 5 the Group proposes that the Commission 'propose a revision of Regulation 1049/2001' to move from access to documents on request to a proactive, information-centric model, where information is classified at creation and published by default, with exceptions for personal data, security and other legitimate interests, and is built for machine and human readers.** A recommendation to the Commission, NOT a proposal: Regulation (EC) No 1049/2001 is unchanged as of 28 October 2026 (see `public_access_to_documents_1049_2001`).
- **What happened (Monday 26 October 2026, IP/26/2253):** the **High-Level Reflection Group on the Future of the Commission's Civil Service, chaired by Catherine Day** (former Secretary-General), handed its report to **President von der Leyen and Commissioner Piotr Serafin** (Budget, Anti-Fraud and Public Administration). Statements: STATEMENT/26/2258 (von der Leyen with Serafin and Day) and STATEMENT/26/2259 (Serafin). **It is an external input, not a Commission decision**: no Staff Regulations change, no organisation chart change, nothing adopted by the College.
- **The document:** "The Future of the Commission's Civil Service", 36 pages, PDF https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/588d0070-1aa8-4bab-ae7b-e1c028a70e34_en, linked from the Large-Scale Review page https://commission.europa.eu/about/service-standards-and-principles/human-resources-strategy/large-scale-review-european-commission_en (read on 27 October 2026).
- **Mandate:** von der Leyen's mission letter to Serafin asked for a **large-scale review of the Commission's organisation and operations**. Three inputs: (1) an internal reflection of **15 cross-Commission workstreams** under three pillars, People and Culture, Structures, Processes, with more than 150 staff, from November 2025 for about nine months; (2) **external benchmarking by Gartner**, with OECD briefings; (3) the HLG, nine external experts appointed in a personal capacity as a sounding board.
- **Exactly nine recommendations (the report's own count, "nine inter-related proposals"):** (1) build the reform around a statement of purpose and a shared culture of collaboration as the norm, flexibility by design and a focus on outcomes, under the banner "One Commission"; (2) deliver all important future policy proposals through **cross-DG portfolio teams** with a designated lead, expected outcomes, timeline and resources, instead of single DGs; (3) redefine the **Secretariat-General as the strategic centre**, with a Priority Delivery Service; (4) a human-centred, responsible **AI transformation**, with an AI risk and rights impact assessment before any AI system is rolled out; (5) a **mandatory framework for regular de-prioritisation**, objectives set at political level, steered by the SG; (6) separate **strict-compliance areas** (budget spending, law, finance) from areas where **experimentation** is encouraged (process, innovation, technology, policy design); (7) agree the key capabilities of senior managers and **pre-qualify a time-bound list of candidates** for vacancies, balancing talent, geography and gender; (8) regular **country-specific strategic discussions at political level** and a more collaborative culture with Member States; (9) a thorough **review of the tasks of decentralised agencies** (still needed, or already done by the Commission or Member States), applying the AI work-redesign.
- **Next step:** Serafin presents the **final large-scale review report** (an "agenda for action") to the President and the College "by the end of 2026 at the latest" (review page; the press release says "in the months to come").
- **Members (nine):** chair Catherine Day; Marco Daglio, Darja Isaksson, Luukas Ilves, Maria Schack Vindum, Mirosława Boryczka, Giorgos Pitsilis, Theresa Twachtmann, Luis Romero Requena (affiliations on the review page).
- **Not read:** recommendation bodies beyond their headline paragraph, the workstream reports, the two statements. "Commission shake-up" is press wording, not the report's.
