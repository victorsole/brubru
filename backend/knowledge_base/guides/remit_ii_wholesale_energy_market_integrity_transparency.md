# REMIT II: Wholesale Energy Market Integrity and Transparency

## QUICK FACTS
- **REMIT** = Regulation (EU) No 1227/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2011 on wholesale energy market integrity and transparency. CELEX 32011R1227. OJ L 326, 8.12.2011, pp. 1-16. Legal basis: Article 194(2) TFEU (energy policy).
- **REMIT II** = the 2024 overhaul, adopted as **Regulation (EU) 2024/1106** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024 amending Regulations (EU) No 1227/2011 and (EU) 2019/942 to improve the Union's protection against market manipulation in the wholesale energy market. CELEX 32024R1106. Entered into force **7 May 2024** (20 days after OJ publication of 17 April 2024). Legislative procedure: **2023/0076(COD)**, based on Commission proposal COM(2023) 147.
- REMIT II amends **two** regulations at once: REMIT itself (1227/2011) AND the **ACER Regulation (EU) 2019/942**, which is how ACER gained new direct powers.
- Prohibits two forms of market abuse in wholesale energy: **insider trading** and **market manipulation** (including attempted manipulation), in electricity, natural gas, and (new since REMIT II) **hydrogen** and energy storage (including battery energy storage systems, BESS).
- **ACER gained direct cross-border investigation powers for the first time** under REMIT II: where a suspected breach affects the wholesale energy market in more than one Member State, ACER can lead or coordinate the investigation, working with National Regulatory Authorities (NRAs), rather than NRAs acting alone.
- **Consolidated REMIT text**: EUR-Lex CELEX 02011R1227-20250205 (most recent consolidated version incorporating REMIT II and follow-on delegated/implementing acts).
- **Registration**: Market participants register with their National Regulatory Authority (NRA); the data feeds ACER's centralised register, **CEREMP** (Centralised European Register for Energy Market Participants).
- **Reporting infrastructure**: transaction and fundamental data reported via authorised **Registered Reporting Mechanisms (RRMs)**; inside information disclosed via **Inside Information Platforms (IIPs)**. Over 100 RRMs are currently authorised EU-wide.
- **Third-country market participants** (established outside the EU/EEA) must designate an **EU representative** if they trade EU wholesale energy products; this obligation entered into force 8 November 2024.
- **New three-tier reporting architecture** under REMIT II: (1) **Continuous reporting**, bilateral OTC contract reporting window shortened from one month to **10 working days**; organised-marketplace transactions to D+2; (2) **Periodic reporting**, new category covering balancing services (monthly aggregated), long-duration gas storage, and hydrogen (annual, from 1 July 2028); (3) **Exposure reporting**, from Q1 2027, participants with annual volumes of 600 GWh or more must submit monthly forward-looking net-position data with 24-month forecasts.
- **Hydrogen phase-in**: exemptions for small producers (<=50 MW), geographically confined producers, and small energy-intensive users (<600 GWh/year); annual hydrogen reporting starts 1 July 2028.
- **LNG price-assessment and benchmark mechanism**: REMIT II inserted Articles 7a-7e requiring ACER to publish a daily LNG price assessment and, from 2025, an LNG benchmark, linked to the EU's 2022 gas-price response package (Regulation (EU) 2022/2576).
- **Algorithmic trading notification**: market participants using algorithmic trading techniques must notify their NRA.
- **REMIT II Implementing Regulation** (Commission implementing act detailing the new reporting formats and data fields) entered into force **29 April 2026**.
- **Enforcement and sanctions**: set and applied at national level by NRAs (REMIT does not centralise fines); NRAs must ensure penalties are effective, proportionate and dissuasive.
- **Interaction with financial-markets law**: REMIT concepts of insider trading and market manipulation are modelled on, but legally separate from, the **Market Abuse Regulation (MAR)** and **MiFID II**; wholesale energy derivatives traded as financial instruments can fall under both REMIT and MiFID II/MAR depending on the product and venue.
- **ACER seat**: Ljubljana, Slovenia.

## What REMIT Prohibits

REMIT applies EU-wide rules against market abuse to wholesale electricity, gas, and (since REMIT II) hydrogen markets, modelled on the financial-markets abuse regime but adapted to energy market characteristics:

- **Insider trading**: using inside information about wholesale energy products to trade, or disclosing it to others, before it is made public.
- **Market manipulation**: entering into transactions or issuing orders that give false or misleading signals about supply, demand or price; or securing prices at an artificial level; or using fictitious devices or other deception.
- **Attempted market manipulation**: also prohibited, even where the manipulation does not succeed.

Market participants must also publish inside information relevant to their own operations "effectively and in a timely manner" via an Inside Information Platform (IIP), unless a narrow delay exemption applies.

## REMIT II: The 2024 Overhaul

The Commission proposed the revision (COM(2023) 147) in March 2023 as part of the EU's response to the 2022 energy crisis, where price volatility exposed gaps in market surveillance. The European Parliament and Council reached political agreement in late 2023/early 2024; the final act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1106, was adopted 11 April 2024 and entered into force 7 May 2024.

### Scope extension
- **Hydrogen** is now explicitly a wholesale energy product (WEP), covering supply, transport and storage contracts, with a phased reporting timeline running to 2028.
- **Energy storage**, including battery energy storage systems (BESS), is treated using existing electricity supply/offtake reporting formats.
- **LNG** gains a dedicated price-assessment and benchmark mechanism (new Articles 7a-7e), building on the emergency gas-price response Regulation (EU) 2022/2576 adopted during the 2022 energy crisis.

### New reporting architecture
REMIT II replaces the previous largely binary reporting model with three tiers:
1. **Continuous reporting** (transaction-by-transaction): OTC bilateral contracts now reported within 10 working days (down from one month); organised-marketplace transactions within D+2.
2. **Periodic reporting** (aggregated, on a schedule): balancing services (monthly), long-duration gas storage, hydrogen (annually from 1 July 2028).
3. **Exposure reporting** (forward-looking): from Q1 2027, market participants with 600+ GWh of annual volume must submit monthly net-position data with 24-month forecasts, giving regulators earlier visibility of concentration risk.

### Third-country market participants
Market participants established outside the EU/EEA that trade EU wholesale energy products must designate an **EU representative** based in a Member State where they are active. This obligation took effect 8 November 2024.

### Algorithmic trading
Market participants using algorithmic trading techniques in wholesale energy markets must notify their National Regulatory Authority.

## Registration and Reporting Infrastructure

| Element | Role |
|---------|------|
| National Regulatory Authority (NRA) | First point of registration and day-to-day supervision for market participants in its Member State |
| CEREMP | Centralised European Register for Energy Market Participants, populated from national registers, searchable via the ACER-REMIT portal |
| Registered Reporting Mechanism (RRM) | Authorised intermediary through which market participants (or system operators on their behalf) submit transaction and fundamental data to ACER; over 100 RRMs currently authorised |
| Inside Information Platform (IIP) | Platform on which market participants publish inside information relevant to their own operations, as REMIT requires |
| Organised Marketplace | Exchanges, brokers and trading platforms; subject to their own order-book and transaction reporting duties, redefined under REMIT II |

## ACER's Expanded Role

REMIT II amends the ACER Regulation (EU) 2019/942 alongside REMIT itself, giving ACER:
- **Direct cross-border investigation powers** for the first time, where a suspected breach affects wholesale energy markets in more than one Member State, working alongside (rather than only supporting) NRAs.
- Responsibility for the new **LNG price assessment** (daily) and **LNG benchmark** (from 2025).
- Continued responsibility for market monitoring, guidance to NRAs and reporting parties, and maintaining CEREMP and the RRM/IIP registers via the ACER-REMIT portal.
- ACER updated its guidance to NRAs and reporting parties by the end of 2024 to reflect the REMIT II legal framework; the Commission adopted related implementing and delegated acts through 2025, with the REMIT II Implementing Regulation (setting out the new reporting formats and data fields) entering into force 29 April 2026.

## Enforcement and Sanctions

REMIT does not create EU-level fines. Each Member State designates its NRA (in most cases the national energy regulator) as the REMIT enforcement authority, with a duty to ensure penalties for insider trading, market manipulation and reporting breaches are effective, proportionate and dissuasive. ACER's new cross-border investigation power under REMIT II lets it coordinate fact-finding across NRAs, but the power to impose sanctions remains national.

## Related EU Instruments

| Instrument | Reference | Relationship to REMIT |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| ACER Regulation | Regulation (EU) 2019/942 | Amended alongside REMIT by REMIT II; source of ACER's new cross-border investigation powers |
| Market Abuse Regulation (MAR) | Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 | Parallel insider-dealing/market-manipulation regime for financial instruments; REMIT concepts are modelled on it but apply to physical wholesale energy products |
| MiFID II | Directive 2014/65/EU | Governs energy derivatives traded as financial instruments; overlaps with REMIT where a wholesale energy product is also a financial instrument |
| Gas price response package | Regulation (EU) 2022/2576 | 2022 emergency measures (including LNG price benchmark groundwork) that REMIT II's Articles 7a-7e build on |
| Electricity/Gas Market Design | Various | Sets the market structure REMIT polices for abuse |
