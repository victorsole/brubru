# EU Policy Monitoring: Pro Tips

How to efficiently track EU policy without drowning in PDFs.

## Core Principles

1. **Systemise, don't scroll** - Random checking leads to missed developments
2. **Relevance rule** - If no one pays you to read it, unsubscribe
3. **Go deep on one tool** - Too many platforms creates chaos
4. **Signal over noise** - Curate ruthlessly

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Routine (30 minutes)
1. Check your monitoring folder (one dedicated place for all alerts)
2. Scan overnight developments from your priority sources
3. Flag items needing action
4. Brief your team if needed

### Rules for Email/Alerts
- Create one dedicated folder for monitoring
- Set up rules so everything lands there automatically
- Check once daily, not continuously
- Notifications OFF during deep work

## Monitoring Tools

### Free Tools

**Google Alerts**
- Use exact phrases in quotes: "Deforestation Regulation", "AFIR review"
- Set up alerts for your key dossiers
- Catches early signals from news and official sources

**RSS Feeds** (use Feedly or Inoreader)
- Most DGs, think tanks, and NGOs publish RSS
- Create a single dashboard for all sources
- Categorise by policy area

**Official Calendars**
- Council calendar: upcoming ministerial meetings
- EP agenda: committee and plenary sessions
- Bookmark and check weekly

### Paid Platforms (pick ONE)

- **EU Issue Tracker** - Comprehensive monitoring
- **VoteWatch** - Voting analysis
- **Politico Pro** - News and analysis
- **Euractiv Pro** - Sector newsletters
- **Dixit Platform** - Social media monitoring, AI summaries

Master one platform deeply rather than superficially using many.

## Key Sources by Institution

### European Parliament
- Committee newsletters (subscribe directly per committee)
- OEIL alerts for procedures you follow
- EPRS publications RSS
- Plenary voting lists

### Council
- Press releases (no media spin)
- Presidency programme and priorities
- European Council conclusions
- Voting results database

### Commission
- Have Your Say (upcoming consultations)
- Press Corner daily news
- DG-specific event pages
- Work programme updates

## Building Your Intelligence Network

### LinkedIn Strategy
Build a curated list of 50 key people:
- MEPs on your committees
- Key APAs
- Member state attachés
- Specialist journalists
- Commission officials
- Think tank researchers

Turn on notifications ONLY for these people. Quality over quantity.

### Informal Sources
- Place Luxembourg conversations
- Committee coffee breaks
- Think tank events
- Permanent representation receptions

## Tracking a Specific File

### Essential Checkpoints
1. **Legislative Train** - Overall status and progress
2. **OEIL** - Procedure details, documents, timeline
3. **EUR-Lex** - Legal text and amendments
4. **Committee page** - Upcoming votes, draft reports
5. **Council register** - Member state positions (when available)

### Key Dates to Track
- Commission proposal publication
- Committee draft report
- Amendment deadlines
- Committee vote
- Plenary vote
- Council general approach
- Trilogue rounds
- Final adoption
- Entry into force
- Transposition deadline (for directives)

## Early Warning Signals

### Commission Phase
- Roadmaps and inception impact assessments
- Have Your Say consultations
- Expert group meetings
- Commission work programme mentions

### Parliament Phase
- Rapporteur appointment
- Committee exchanges of views
- Amendment trends
- Shadow rapporteur positions

### Council Phase
- Presidency priorities
- Working party discussions
- COREPER deliberations
- General approach adoption

## Common Pitfalls

- Monitoring too many files superficially
- Relying only on news (missing primary sources)
- Ignoring Council (where much happens behind closed doors)
- Missing consultation deadlines
- Not tracking implementation phase
- Over-relying on one source

## Weekly Review Checklist

- [ ] Any new procedures started in your area?
- [ ] Committee votes scheduled?
- [ ] Plenary votes upcoming?
- [ ] Council meetings on your files?
- [ ] New consultations opened?
- [ ] Key documents published?
- [ ] Personnel changes (rapporteurs, coordinators)?
