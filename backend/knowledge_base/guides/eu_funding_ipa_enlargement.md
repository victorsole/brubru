Guide covering EU pre-accession and enlargement funding instruments, with practical information for businesses and organisations in Western Balkans candidate countries seeking EU funding for agriculture, agrifood, rural development, and cross-border cooperation.

## IPA III: Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance

### Legal Framework

IPA III was established by Regulation (EU) 2021/1529 (CELEX: 32021R1529), published in OJ L 330 on 20 September 2021. It is the EU's main financial instrument supporting candidate and potential candidate countries on their path to EU membership. IPA III continues from IPA II (2014-2020) and runs for the 2021-2027 programming period.

Two key secondary acts supplement the regulation:

- **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2128**: sets out the thematic priorities and specific objectives for IPA III assistance
- **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/2236**: establishes the detailed rules for implementation

### Budget

The IPA III financial envelope is EUR 14.162 billion for 2021-2027, with total financial programming reaching EUR 14.99 billion including contributions. Climate mainstreaming accounts for 26.2% of the budget and biodiversity for 3.7%.

Cross-border cooperation programmes may use up to 3% of the total budget, with a maximum EU co-financing rate of 85%.

### Beneficiary Countries

IPA III covers seven primary beneficiaries:

| Country | EU Status (2026) | Key Sectors |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Albania | Candidate (negotiations opened June 2024) | Agriculture, tourism, energy, digital |
| Bosnia and Herzegovina | Candidate (negotiations opened March 2024) | Energy, transport, rule of law |
| Kosovo | Potential candidate (applied December 2022) | Rule of law, public administration |
| Montenegro | Candidate (most advanced in negotiations; PM cites EU security motive, April 2026) | Environment, energy, transport |
| North Macedonia | Candidate (negotiations ongoing) | Agriculture, connectivity, digital |
| Serbia | Candidate (negotiations ongoing; EUR 1.5B funding freeze threatened, April 2026) | Economy, rule of law, environment |
| Turkey | Candidate (negotiations suspended since 2018) | Limited IPA III access |

Moldova, Ukraine, and Georgia are associated beneficiaries, primarily accessing IPA III through regional and cross-border programmes.

### Eligible Participants

IPA III funding is open to entities from: EU Member States, IPA III beneficiary countries, EEA countries, and Neighbourhood Area countries (as listed in Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2021/947).

### Six Thematic Clusters

IPA III assistance is structured around six thematic clusters:

1. **Rule of Law and Fundamental Rights**: judiciary, anti-corruption, organised crime, human rights
2. **Good Governance and Alignment**: public administration reform, public financial management, statistical systems
3. **Green Agenda and Sustainable Connectivity**: environment, climate, energy, transport
4. **Competitiveness and Inclusive Growth**: education, employment, social policies, private sector development
5. **Resources, Agriculture and Cohesion**: agriculture, rural development, fisheries, food safety
6. **External Relations and Regional Cooperation**: cross-border and regional cooperation, people-to-people contacts

## Reform and Growth Facility for the Western Balkans

### Overview

The Reform and Growth Facility (RGF) is a new EUR 6 billion instrument for 2024-2027, adopted with the regulation entering into force on 25 May 2024. It reinforces IPA III and supports the Western Balkans' convergence with the EU single market.

The Growth Plan was adopted by the Commission on 8 November 2023. Western Balkans economies currently stand at approximately 35% of the EU average level.

### Four Pillars

1. **Single market integration**: alignment with EU acquis in key areas (free movement of goods, services, capital)
2. **Economic integration**: macro-economic stability, public financial management, investment climate
3. **Green and digital transition**: climate, energy efficiency, digitalisation
4. **Fundamentals**: rule of law, judicial reform, anti-corruption, public administration

## Western Balkans Investment Framework (WBIF)

The WBIF is a blending facility combining IPA III grants, bilateral donor contributions, and RGF resources to mobilise large-scale infrastructure and private sector investments across the Western Balkans.

### Recent Activity

- **January 2026**: EUR 171 million support package for infrastructure and private sector development across Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, and North Macedonia
- **2024 results**: EUR 463.7 million committed in blending and guarantee operations, mobilising EUR 1.27 billion in total investments
  - 74% transport
  - 16% water and sanitation
  - 10% other sectors (energy, social infrastructure)

## IPARD III: Pre-Accession Rural Development

IPARD (Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance for Rural Development) is the agricultural component of IPA III. IPARD III covers the 2021-2027 period and supports candidate countries in aligning with the EU Common Agricultural Policy.

### Supported Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Physical assets of agricultural holdings | Farm modernisation, equipment, irrigation |
| Processing and marketing | Agrifood processing facilities, storage, quality systems |
| Agri-environmental and organic farming | Organic conversion, sustainable practices |
| LEADER | Local development strategies, community-led development |
| Rural infrastructure | Roads, water supply, broadband in rural areas |
| Diversification of farm activities | Rural tourism, on-farm processing, renewable energy |
| Technical advisory services | Farm advisory, training, knowledge transfer |

### Albania-Specific IPARD Implementation

Albania is making progress on building the administrative capacity required to manage IPARD III funds. Key developments:

- Law on wine adopted, creating the legal framework for the wine sector
- FADN/FSDN (Farm Sustainability Data Network) implementation plan adopted
- Organic farming is currently the sole agri-environmental measure supported under IPARD

**Challenges for Albania:**
- Underinvestment in AKIS (Agricultural Knowledge and Innovation Systems) advisory services
- Limited digital solutions for farm management and traceability
- Institutional capacity gaps in food safety authorities
- Need for climate adaptation measures: water management, irrigation, drainage systems

### Agriculture and Wine in Albania

Albania's agriculture sector faces specific challenges that EU funding instruments are designed to address:

- **Climate adaptation**: water management, irrigation modernisation, and drainage systems are priority investment areas
- **Solar energy integration**: rural livelihood diversification includes renewable energy on agricultural holdings
- **Wine sector**: organic wine production and enoturism (wine tourism) are growing areas with export potential
- **Cross-border cooperation**: Interreg IPA South Adriatic programme (Italy-Albania-Montenegro) for 2021-2027 supports joint agrifood and tourism initiatives

### EU Wine Import Requirements

Companies exporting wine to the EU market must comply with:

| Regulation | CELEX | Subject |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Regulation (EU) 2019/934 | 32019R0934 | Oenological practices and restrictions |
| Regulation (EU) 2018/273 | 32018R0273 | Wine sector management measures (planting authorisations, registers, documents, certifications) |
| Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33 | 32019R0033 | Labelling, presentation, and designation of origin for wine products |

### Step-by-Step for Western Balkans Applicants

1. **Identify the right instrument**: IPA III for institutional reform and infrastructure; IPARD for agriculture and rural development; WBIF for large infrastructure; RGF for reform-linked disbursements (government level)
2. **Check eligibility**: IPA III is open to public institutions, NGOs, and businesses in beneficiary countries; IPARD targets agricultural holdings, agrifood processors, and rural communities
3. **Monitor calls**: subscribe to alerts on the Funding and Tenders Portal and the relevant national IPARD agency
4. **Prepare documentation**: business plan, proof of co-financing capacity, environmental compliance, alignment with national rural development strategy
5. **Apply through the correct channel**: centralised calls via Brussels portals; decentralised calls via national agencies (e.g., AZHBR in Albania for IPARD)

### Cross-Border Cooperation

The Interreg IPA programmes offer opportunities for joint projects between EU Member States and IPA III beneficiaries. For Albania, the key programme is:

- **Interreg IPA South Adriatic** (Italy-Albania-Montenegro): supports cooperation on innovation, environment, maritime economy, cultural heritage, and tourism for 2021-2027

Cross-border programmes are co-financed at up to 85% by the EU.
