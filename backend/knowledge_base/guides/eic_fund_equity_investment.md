# EIC Fund — Equity Investment Vehicle

## QUICK FACTS
- The EIC Fund is the **equity investment vehicle** of the EIC, deployed alongside Accelerator + STEP Scale Up.
- Total capitalisation: **over EUR 4 billion**.
- Investment status (June 2026): **300+ companies** invested across **25 countries**, **350 investment decisions**, **EUR 1.4 billion** committed, **12 successful exits**, **EUR 5 billion** mobilised from **1,000+ co-investors**.
- Leverage: **EUR 3.5 of private capital crowded in per EUR 1 of EIC Fund investment**.
- Legal structure: Luxembourg-domiciled SICAV-RAIF (financial vehicle); investment advisor of record: **European Investment Bank (EIB) Group**.
- Two compartments:
  - **Standard** — EIC Accelerator blended-finance equity, EUR 0.5-10M tickets.
  - **STEP** — EIC STEP Scale Up equity, EUR 10-30M tickets.
- Governance: EIC Fund Board (broadest decision-making power) + EIC Fund Advisory Committee (independent advice to EIB + Fund Manager).
- Investment advisor: EIB Group — conducts due diligence, proposes investments, supports portfolio management, acts as investor of record.
- Distinct from the **Scaleup Europe Fund** (separate EUR 5B fund-of-funds managed by EQT under EIF; see `eic_scaleup_europe_fund_2026.md`).

## Investment scope

- **All verticals** (no sector exclusion): deeptech, AI, semiconductors, biotech, climate tech, advanced manufacturing, photonics, quantum, robotics, etc.
- Geography: EU Member States + Horizon Europe Associated Countries.
- TRL range: 5/6 → 9 (post-Accelerator selection) and TRL 9+ (STEP scale-up).
- Stage: late-seed to late-Series C+.

## Two routes into the EIC Fund

### Route 1 — EIC Accelerator blended finance (Standard compartment)

Triggered automatically when an Accelerator applicant chooses the **blended finance** track (grant + equity) at submission.

Process:
1. Applicant submits Step 1 (Short Proposal) opting in for blended finance.
2. If selected at Step 3 (Jury Interview), relevant project information transmitted to the EIC Fund.
3. EIC Fund + EIB conduct investment due diligence.
4. EIC Fund Investment Decision: **2-6 months** from selection.
5. If due diligence finds insufficient maturity for equity, applicant enters **grant-only mode** with re-assessment at defined GA milestones.
6. Equity deployment: EUR 0.5-10M in tranches, typically co-invested alongside private VCs.

BAS (Business Acceleration Services) supports co-investor search in parallel.

### Route 2 — EIC STEP Scale Up (STEP compartment)

Triggered by direct application to STEP Scale Up (separate from Accelerator).

Process:
1. Applicant submits full business plan + pitch deck + qualified investor pre-commitment (≥20% of target round).
2. Remote evaluation → STEP Seal awarded to all qualifiers.
3. Selected applicants invited to interview with EIC Fund + EIB team.
4. Full investment due diligence (legal, tax, technical, commercial, ESG).
5. EIC Fund Investment Committee decision.
6. Equity deployment: EUR 10-30M, anchoring a round of EUR 50-150M+.

## Due diligence components

Typical EIC Fund DD pack required from applicant:
- **Corporate documents**: incorporation, articles of association, shareholders' agreement, board resolutions
- **Capitalisation**: full cap table, dilution model, option pool, convertible notes outstanding
- **Financials**: audited last 3 years + current management accounts + 5-year financial plan
- **Legal**: litigation history, material contracts, IP register, FTO opinion
- **Technical**: TRL assessment, IP portfolio, regulatory pathway
- **Commercial**: customer pipeline, sales metrics, competitive positioning
- **ESG**: alignment with EU sustainable finance framework (SFDR Article 8 minimum)
- **Dual-use screening** if defence-adjacent

## Investment terms (standard institutional VC structure)

- **Preferred shares** typically (1x non-participating preference standard)
- Information rights (monthly KPI report, board observer)
- ROFR (Right of First Refusal) on share transfers
- Anti-dilution (broad-based weighted average)
- Tag-along / drag-along clauses
- ESG covenants per EU sustainable finance framework
- No EIC Fund board seat by default — **board observer** typical

## Co-investment requirement

The EIC Fund **does NOT lead rounds**. It anchors alongside private lead investors:
- Standard compartment (Accelerator blended): typically takes 10-25% of the round
- STEP compartment: typically takes 20-40% of the round (anchoring EUR 50M+ catalytic rounds)

This co-investment model is the source of the **3.5x leverage ratio** (every EUR 1 EIC = EUR 3.5 private capital crowded in).

## Governance bodies

### EIC Fund Board
- Highest decision-making body under the Fund's Luxembourg corporate statutes.
- Oversees investment strategy.
- Approves the annual investment plan.
- Reports to the Commission via DG RTD.

### EIC Fund Advisory Committee
- Independent advice on individual investment cases.
- Provides comments to the EIB and the Fund Manager.
- Composition: industry experts + senior investors.

### EIB Group
- Investment advisor of record.
- Conducts due diligence.
- Proposes investments to the Fund Board.
- Supports portfolio management (active monitoring + value-add).
- Acts as investor of record (signs subscription agreements on the Fund's behalf).

### European Commission (DG RTD + DG ECFIN)
- Sets the strategic priorities + budget envelope.
- Reviews the annual report.
- Does NOT make individual investment decisions.

## Distinct from the Scaleup Europe Fund

The EIC Fund (EUR 4B+) is **NOT** the same as the Scaleup Europe Fund (EUR 5B, May 2026, fund-of-funds, managed by EQT, anchored by EIF). The two coexist:

- **EIC Fund** — direct equity into individual companies, EUR 0.5-30M tickets, deployed alongside Accelerator + STEP
- **Scaleup Europe Fund** — fund-of-funds investing in PRIVATE growth-stage VC + PE funds that themselves invest in EU scale-ups; later-stage anchor (Series C+, pre-IPO)

See `eic_scaleup_europe_fund_2026.md` for full Scaleup Europe Fund detail.
