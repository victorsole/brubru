## Overview

MiFID II (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive) and MiFIR (Markets in Financial Instruments Regulation) form the cornerstone of EU financial markets regulation. Together they establish the Single Rulebook for securities markets, governing investment services, trading venues, market transparency, and investor protection across the EU and EEA.

MiFID II replaced the original MiFID (2004/39/EC) to address gaps exposed by the 2008 financial crisis and the rise of high-frequency trading. The directive-regulation split was deliberate: MiFID II (a directive) allows Member State flexibility on organisational requirements for investment firms, while MiFIR (a regulation) imposes directly applicable, uniform transparency and trading rules.

The framework is supervised at EU level by the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), which produces binding technical standards (RTS/ITS) and maintains the ESMA Interactive Single Rulebook for MiFID II.

## Scope and Key Requirements

### Who is covered
- Investment firms (brokers, dealers, portfolio managers, investment advisers)
- Trading venues: regulated markets, multilateral trading facilities (MTFs), organised trading facilities (OTFs)
- Systematic internalisers (SIs)
- Data reporting services providers
- Third-country firms accessing EU markets

### Market Structure
MiFID II introduced the OTF category to capture broker crossing networks and bring OTC derivatives trading onto regulated platforms (complementing EMIR). It strengthened the pre- and post-trade transparency regime for equities and extended it to non-equity instruments (bonds, structured finance products, emission allowances, derivatives).

### Investor Protection
- Product governance: manufacturers must define target markets; distributors must assess suitability
- Inducements: restrictions on payments from third parties (commissions, research)
- Best execution: firms must take sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result for clients
- Suitability and appropriateness tests for retail investors
- Cost and charges disclosure requirements

### Transparency
- Pre-trade transparency: quotes must be published for instruments traded on venues
- Post-trade transparency: transactions must be reported in near real-time
- Consolidated Tape Provider (CTP): the 2024 review creates a mandatory EU consolidated tape for bonds and equities
- Transaction reporting to competent authorities (RTS 22, extended scope in 2024 review)

### Commodity Derivatives
- Position limits on commodity derivatives to prevent excessive speculation
- Position management powers for trading venues
- Ancillary activity exemption for non-financial entities hedging commercial risk

## 2024 Review: Key Changes

The MiFID II/MiFIR review texts entered into force on 28 March 2024. Key amendments:

| Change | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Payment for Order Flow (PFOF) | Prohibition with temporary exemptions for Member States (until 30 June 2026) |
| Consolidated Tape | Mandatory EU consolidated tape for bonds (first) and equities; ESMA to select CTP providers |
| Designated Publishing Entity (DPE) | New regime for post-trade transparency of OTC transactions |
| Systematic Internalisers | Revised SI definition and obligations |
| Research unbundling | Partial re-bundling allowed for SME research |
| Circuit breakers | Harmonised provisions across trading venues |
| Ban on receiving PFOF | Applies from 30 June 2026 for all Member States |

ESMA is implementing Level 2 measures through phased consultations:
- May 2024: Transparency rules, RTS 2, RTS 23, commodity derivatives
- July 2024: RTS 1, CTP input/output data, SI provisions
- October 2024: RTS 22 on transaction reporting
- April 2025: Derivatives transparency, package orders

## Relevance for Advocacy

MiFID II/MiFIR is central to any financial services lobbying strategy in the EU. The 2024 review is now in implementation phase (Level 2 delegated acts), meaning advocacy shifts from co-legislators to ESMA consultations and DG FISMA technical discussions. Key advocacy windows:
- ESMA public consultations on RTS/ITS (ongoing through 2025)
- Member State transposition of MiFID II amendments (deadline September 2025)
- Consolidated tape provider selection process
- Retail Investment Strategy package (separate but closely linked to MiFID II investor protection rules)
