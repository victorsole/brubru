# Strategy measures: closing the gap from lab to fab (investment, valorisation, scale-up)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** COM(2026) 453 final, section 2.1. A Communication: the Commission "will explore", "promote" or "encourage"; nothing here is a legal obligation, all "without prejudging" the next MFF. Starting quarters are in the Annex 1 guide.
- **Diagnosis:** Europe makes excellent agri-food science but private R&I investment is insufficient and results reach the market slowly; SMEs and startups take part in Horizon Europe only to a limited extent; fragmented funding, regulatory complexity and weak support at higher technology readiness levels (TRL) are the barriers.
- **Make EU-funded projects more business-oriented:** elements in calls for proposals, **more private-sector and market experts in proposal evaluation** (action 2, 2027), incentives for valorisation of results; a **targeted call** on valorisation and AI (action 1, 2027).
- **Lower entry barriers for SMEs and startups:** more visible and accessible **cascade funding** (financial support to third parties), simpler procedures, support to **build consortia**.
- **Portfolio and scale-up:** manage the portfolio **by TRL** with support tailored to each stage; explore **scale-up pathways** (a document-based framework mapping funding instruments by TRL); stronger links between **Horizon Europe and the future European Competitiveness Fund**, using the **ECF InvestEU Instrument** as a delivery tool.
- **Partners:** **EIT Food**, the **European Innovation Council**, the Enterprise Europe Network and its successor the **EU4Business Network**, **ETP Food for Life**; a **renewed fast-track-to-innovation** scheme (smaller consortia, shorter time-to-grant, higher TRL, more startups, SMEs, farmers, foresters and industry).
- **Promising results:** a mechanism to identify them early, with **light-touch, rapid and flexible top-up funding** to bridge to early market validation.
- **Innovation vouchers:** small flexible grants for external business-creation and acceleration services (training, business models, customer research, intellectual property, access to testers and corporate partners), working with incubators, accelerators, Living Labs and pilot plants.
- **Intellectual property:** tailored support building on the European IP Helpdesk, Horizon IP Scan and the Horizon Results Booster.
