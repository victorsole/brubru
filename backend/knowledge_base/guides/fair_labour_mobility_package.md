# Fair Labour Mobility Package (adopted 15 September 2026)

## QUICK FACTS

- **What it is**: a Commission package on working and moving across borders in the EU, made of **one Communication and five legislative proposals**. Adopted by the College on **Tuesday 15 September 2026** at its 2578th meeting in Strasbourg.
- **Responsible**: Executive Vice-President for Social Rights and Skills, Quality Jobs and Preparedness, **Roxana Mînzatu**.
- **Status: PROPOSALS, NOT LAW.** All five instruments must still be adopted by the European Parliament and the Council under the ordinary legislative procedure. None has a CELEX of its own yet. Never describe any part of this package as being in force, and never invent a CELEX or an entry-into-force date for it.
- **Primary sources**: Commission press release **IP/26/1851**, Q&A **QANDA/26/1852**, factsheets **FS/26/1853** (ELA), **FS/26/1854** (ESSPASS), **FS/26/1855** (skills portability), speech **SPEECH/26/1881** (Mînzatu), and the College order of the day **OJ(2026) 2578 final**, items 11 to 16. DG EMPL news page, 15 September 2026.
- **Headline figure**: an estimated **EUR 5 billion in benefits by 2040**, of which about **EUR 1 billion is simplification gains**, from faster recognition of professional qualifications, savings for business, workers and authorities, and fraud avoidance.

## European Social Security Pass (ESSPASS)

- Lets people **request and receive social security documents fully online**, including the **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)** and the **portable document A1** for posted workers.
- Documents are stored and presented through the **EU Digital Identity Wallet**; administrations verify them quickly and securely.
- **Phasing, as proposed**: the portable document **A1 one year after the ESSPASS Regulation enters into force**; the **EHIC and other documents within three years**.
- Estimated savings over 12 years: up to **EUR 64 million for businesses**, **EUR 69 million for healthcare providers**, **EUR 72 million for labour inspectors** through faster cross-border verification. Digital signatures could avoid up to **EUR 277 million in fraud** over the same period.

## Skills portability and professional qualifications

- Qualifications issued in a **standardised digital format**, available in the EU Digital Identity Wallet, so authenticity can be verified.
- A new **EU qualifications comparison tool** drawing on national qualifications databases, free for employers and workers.
- **Regulated professions**: a mandatory digital procedure cuts cross-border recognition for doctors, nurses, engineers and teachers from **three months to five weeks**.
- **Common training frameworks** extend automatic recognition to more regulated professions, based on a preliminary list of **10 additional professions** registering shortages. The **first such framework was adopted on 15 September 2026, for physiotherapists**, one of the most mobile professions in the EU.
- **Qualifications obtained outside the EU** (for EU citizens and for third-country professionals): common standards cut procedures from **14 months on average to four months**, roughly halving employers' administrative hiring costs. For **seven professions** where EU-wide training standards already exist, qualifications from accredited institutions outside the EU reviewed by European quality assurance bodies would be **recognised automatically**.
- **Member States keep full control** over who is admitted to their territory and over the standards required to practise a profession.

## Strengthening the European Labour Authority

See the `ela_agency_overview` guide for ELA as it stands in law today under Regulation (EU) 2019/1149. The proposal would give ELA:

- a **risk-assessment capability for inspections**, so national inspectorates can target controls;
- **dedicated national inspection liaison officers** for cross-border cooperation and expertise;
- **grants** to organisations supporting people working in another Member State and employers recruiting or providing services across borders;
- an **expanded mandate** covering abuses against third-country nationals working in the EU and combatting illegal employment;
- a **simplified mediation procedure** to resolve cross-border disputes faster and avoid litigation.

## What the package is for

Per the Commission, it should: let workers prove and claim social security and health insurance rights and have qualifications understood everywhere in the EU and formally recognised where the profession is regulated; protect mobile workers from abuse while removing slow administrative procedures that hinder cross-border recruitment and service provision; and give national administrations more efficient cooperation to enforce workers' rights and fight fraud.

## Next steps

People see the first practical benefits only once the European Parliament and the Council adopt the initiatives and the main measures enter into force. **No adoption date can be given.** Watch the ordinary legislative procedure for each of the five proposals separately, since they will not move at the same speed.
