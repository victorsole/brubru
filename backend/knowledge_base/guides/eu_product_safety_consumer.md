# EU Product Safety and Consumer Protection

## QUICK FACTS
- General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR): Regulation (EU) 2023/988, CELEX 32023R0988, applicable from 13 December 2024
- Replaces: General Product Safety Directive 2001/95/EC
- Digital Services Act (DSA): Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065 (online marketplace obligations)
- Lead DG: DG JUST (Justice and Consumers), DG GROW (Internal Market)
- Commissioner: Michael McGrath (Democracy, Justice, Consumer Protection)
- EP lead committee: IMCO (Internal Market and Consumer Protection)
- IMCO Chair: Anna Cavazzini (Germany, Greens/EFA)
- Safety Gate (RAPEX): EU rapid alert system for dangerous non-food products
- Market surveillance: Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, CELEX 32019R1020
- IMCO Temu hearing: 17 April 2026 (Thursday), following SHEIN (2025) and AliExpress hearings
- Key issue: non-EU online marketplaces selling unsafe/non-compliant products to EU consumers

## Overview

The EU product safety and consumer protection framework ensures that products placed on the internal market are safe. The General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR), applicable since December 2024, modernises the safety framework for the digital age, extending obligations to online marketplaces and addressing risks from products sold via e-commerce platforms based outside the EU.

## General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988

### Key Innovations vs Old Directive
| Area | Old (GPSD 2001/95) | New (GPSR 2023/988) |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Online marketplaces | Not covered | Specific obligations (Art. 22) |
| Product recalls | Voluntary/national | Harmonised recall procedures, direct consumer notification |
| Risk assessment | General safety requirement | Structured risk assessment, including psychological risks |
| Traceability | Limited | Economic operator identification on product/packaging |
| Accident reporting | Member State systems | Harmonised Safety Gate/RAPEX integration |
| Consumer rights | Limited redress | Right to repair, replacement, or adequate refund for recalled products |

### Online Marketplace Obligations (Article 22)
Online marketplaces must:
- Designate a single point of contact for market surveillance authorities
- Register on Safety Gate portal
- Remove unsafe products within specified timeframes upon authority notification
- Inform consumers who purchased recalled products
- Cooperate with market surveillance authorities
- Random checks on product listings for safety compliance

## IMCO Scrutiny of Non-EU Platforms (2025-2026)

The European Parliament's IMCO committee has conducted a series of hearings with non-EU online marketplaces:
- **SHEIN**: Questioned on product safety compliance, supply chain transparency, REACH chemical compliance
- **AliExpress**: Questioned on customs duty evasion, product safety, IP infringement
- **Temu** (17 April 2026): Hearing on adherence to EU rules on product safety, consumer protection, and digital platforms (DSA compliance)

Key concerns:
- Products below EUR 150 customs threshold flooding EU market
- Non-compliance with REACH, toy safety, electrical safety standards
- Lack of EU-based responsible economic operator
- Difficulty in enforcement against non-EU sellers
- Consumer data protection (GDPR compliance)

## Related Legislation
- **DSA (Digital Services Act)**: Platform obligations for illegal content, including unsafe products
- **DMA (Digital Markets Act)**: Gatekeeper obligations (separate from product safety)
- **REACH**: Chemical safety requirements for products
- **Toy Safety Regulation**: Specific rules for toys (revision ongoing)
- **Customs reform**: Proposed abolition of EUR 150 de minimis threshold
