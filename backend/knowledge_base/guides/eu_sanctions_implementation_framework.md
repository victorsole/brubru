# EU Sanctions Implementation Framework

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU restrictive measures (sanctions) framework — adoption, implementation, coordination, and enforcement
- Legal basis: Article 215 TFEU (financial and economic sanctions); Article 75 TFEU (terrorism-finance sanctions); Common Foreign and Security Policy (Articles 28-29 TEU) for political decisions
- Procedure: Council adopts sanctions on a HR/VP proposal under Article 215 TFEU after a CFSP Council Decision under Article 29 TEU; Member States enforce nationally
- EU Sanctions Envoy: David O'Sullivan (since 2023, role established to coordinate enforcement and counter circumvention)
- Coordinators Forum: 7th Forum held 30 April 2026 — convened by O'Sullivan
- High-level meetings on sanctions implementation: 9th held 30 April 2026, hosted by Commissioner Maria Luís Albuquerque (Financial Services)
- Responsible DG: DG FISMA (Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union) for financial sanctions; EEAS for CFSP coordination
- Russia sanctions anchor regulation: Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014 (CELEX 32014R0833) — restrictive measures concerning Russia's actions destabilising Ukraine
- Iran sanctions anchor: multiple regulations under Council Decision 2010/413/CFSP framework
- Belarus sanctions: Council Regulation (EC) No 765/2006 (CELEX 32006R0765)

## How EU sanctions are adopted

1. **Political decision** — Council adopts a CFSP Decision under Article 29 TEU (unanimity)
2. **Implementation** — Council adopts an Article 215 TFEU Regulation on a joint proposal from the HR/VP and the Commission
3. **National enforcement** — Member States designate competent authorities; FIUs investigate suspicious transactions; courts apply criminal penalties under Directive (EU) 2024/1226 (sanctions-violation criminalisation)
4. **Sanctions-violation criminalisation directive**: Directive (EU) 2024/1226 (CELEX 32024L1226), transposition deadline 20 May 2025

## Sanctions architecture (institutional map)

| Layer | Body | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Political | Council (CFSP) | Adopts Decision under Art 29 TEU (unanimity) |
| Legal | Council | Adopts Regulation under Art 215 TFEU |
| Coordination | EU Sanctions Envoy (O'Sullivan) | Convenes Coordinators Forum, leads circumvention work |
| Coordination | Commissioner Financial Services (Albuquerque) | Hosts high-level implementation meetings |
| Operational | Member State competent authorities | Designate, freeze assets, license exemptions |
| Operational | National FIUs | Suspicious-transaction reports |
| Enforcement | National courts | Criminal penalties under Dir 2024/1226 |
| Audit | DG FISMA + EEAS | Assess effectiveness, propose new packages |

## Russia sanctions package count

As of 30 April 2026, the EU has adopted **19+ packages** of sanctions against Russia since 2014 (with the heaviest concentration after 24 February 2022). The framework regulation (Reg 833/2014) has been amended dozens of times. Track package number when communicating; never invent a package number.

A corrigendum to Reg 2024/1745 (further amending Reg 833/2014) was published in the OJ on 30 April 2026 — see daily_briefs row 32024R1745R(10).
