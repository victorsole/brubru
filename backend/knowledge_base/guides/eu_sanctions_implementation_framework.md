## How EU sanctions are adopted

1. **Political decision** — Council adopts a CFSP Decision under Article 29 TEU (unanimity)
2. **Implementation** — Council adopts an Article 215 TFEU Regulation on a joint proposal from the HR/VP and the Commission
3. **National enforcement** — Member States designate competent authorities; FIUs investigate suspicious transactions; courts apply criminal penalties under Directive (EU) 2024/1226 (sanctions-violation criminalisation)
4. **Sanctions-violation criminalisation directive**: Directive (EU) 2024/1226 (CELEX 32024L1226), transposition deadline 20 May 2025

## Sanctions architecture (institutional map)

| Layer | Body | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Political | Council (CFSP) | Adopts Decision under Art 29 TEU (unanimity) |
| Legal | Council | Adopts Regulation under Art 215 TFEU |
| Coordination | EU Sanctions Envoy (O'Sullivan) | Convenes Coordinators Forum, leads circumvention work |
| Coordination | Commissioner Financial Services (Albuquerque) | Hosts high-level implementation meetings |
| Operational | Member State competent authorities | Designate, freeze assets, license exemptions |
| Operational | National FIUs | Suspicious-transaction reports |
| Enforcement | National courts | Criminal penalties under Dir 2024/1226 |
| Audit | DG FISMA + EEAS | Assess effectiveness, propose new packages |
