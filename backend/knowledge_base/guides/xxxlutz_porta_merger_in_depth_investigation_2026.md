# XXXLutz / Porta: the Commission opens an in-depth merger investigation (12 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** Commission press release IP/26/2126, 12 October 2026: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2126
- **What happened:** on **12 October 2026** the European Commission opened an **in-depth (Phase II) investigation** under the **EU Merger Regulation** into **XXXLutz's proposed acquisition of Porta** (press release IP/26/2126). Case number **M.11895**.
- **Deadline:** the transaction was notified on **7 September 2026**; the Commission now has **90 working days, until 25 February 2027**, to decide. Opening an in-depth investigation **does not prejudge the outcome**. (A Phase I review normally takes 25 working days.)
- **The Commission's preliminary concerns, two markets:** (1) **retail supply of furniture in Germany, Czechia and Slovakia** (in Germany particularly in discount stores): the two groups are among the strongest furniture retailers there, many of their stores compete in the same local areas, and the concern is higher prices and less choice; (2) **procurement of furniture from manufacturers and suppliers in Germany, Czechia, Slovakia and Poland**: XXXLutz buys through its purchasing association **GIGA International**, and if Porta's brands joined it one of the very few large alternative customers would disappear, which could increase supplier dependence.
- **The parties:** **XXXLutz KG** (Austria; brands include XXXLutz, Moemax, Roller, Braun, Home24, Moebelix; its shareholders also hold controlling stakes in POCO, But and the Conforama companies of France, Iberia and Switzerland). **Porta** (Germany; brands porta Moebel, SB Moebel Boss, Moebel LETZ in Germany and ASKO in Czechia and Slovakia). The deal would combine more than 140 Porta-brand stores with more than 1,800 XXXLutz-group stores.
- **Separate gun-jumping investigation (case M.11895.AP):** the Commission is also examining whether the parties took actions contributing to a change of control before notification. It is separate from the competition assessment opened on 12 October.
- **Other Phase II merger cases open at the same time (per the release):** MMG / Anglo American's nickel business; the UPM / Sappi joint venture; the Saipem / Subsea7 merger.
