# EU-UK Security and Defence Partnership

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (published week to 27 August 2026 - UK PARTICIPATION IN UNION PROGRAMMES)**: **Decision No 1/2026 of the Specialised Committee on Participation in Union Programmes**, established by Article 8(1)(s) of the **EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement** - CELEX `22026D1962`. The Specialised Committee is the TCA's own governance machinery for programme participation; a decision of this committee is a binding act of the Agreement's institutional structure, not an EU legislative act, and carries no CELEX in the 3xxxx legislation series.
- **EPRS Briefing (10 May 2026, EP Think Tank portal)**: "**The British defence industry and EU-UK defence cooperation**" — briefing on the UK defence industry's role as a partner in European-defence supply chains, the legal architecture of UK participation in EU defence programmes post-Brexit (SAFE, EDIP, PESCO third-state participation under Article 9 of Council Decision (CFSP) 2020/1639), and the May 2025 EU-UK Summit Joint Declaration commitments on a Security and Defence Partnership.
- **Foundational instrument**: **EU-UK Security and Defence Partnership (SDP)** — Joint Declaration signed at the EU-UK Summit on 19 May 2025 (London). The SDP is not yet a binding international agreement; it is a political framework for negotiated cooperation agreements + Council Decisions on UK participation in specific EU instruments.
- **Wider legal context**: **EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement** (TCA), signed 30 December 2020, in force since 1 May 2021 (CELEX **22021A0430(01)**). The TCA does not cover foreign + security + defence policy — leaving the SDP as the bridging architecture.
- **Lead service**: **EEAS** (European External Action Service); DG DEFIS (Defence Industry and Space) for industrial-cooperation files
- **Commissioner-side counterparts**: HR/VP Kaja Kallas (EEAS, FAC); Andrius Kubilius (Defence and Space, Lithuania); Maroš Šefčovič (Trade — TCA Joint Council co-chair on EU side)
- **EP committees**: AFET + SEDE (defence dimension); INTA (TCA + economic dimension)
- **Legal basis**: Articles 21-22 TEU (CFSP general framework); Articles 207, 217, 218 TFEU (international agreements); UK participation case-by-case under each EU defence instrument's third-state participation provisions

## Why this matters in May 2026

The **British defence industry** is one of the largest in Europe (BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce Defence, Babcock, Thales UK, Leonardo UK, Lockheed Martin UK, MBDA UK). UK industrial participation in EU defence programmes was politically constrained post-Brexit despite the operational logic of integrated European supply chains. The 2024-2025 European defence-financing surge (SAFE EUR 150 billion + EDIP + Strategic Compass implementation) created a sharp policy question: **can UK industry participate in EU-funded defence procurement and joint development**?

The 10 May 2026 EPRS briefing surveys this landscape and analyses:
1. **SAFE participation**: Reg (EU) 2025/... establishing the Security Action for Europe loan facility allows third-state participation under specific conditions (security-of-supply guarantees + EU value-added share); UK accession to SAFE programmes is being negotiated case-by-case
2. **EDIP** (European Defence Industry Programme): the COM(2024) 150 proposal as adopted by Council includes a third-state participation track via association agreements
3. **PESCO** projects: UK currently participates in **Military Mobility** PESCO project (one of 70+ active PESCO projects); other PESCO projects with UK industrial input being explored
4. **EDA**: European Defence Agency Administrative Arrangement 2025 with UK — covers research + capability development cooperation
5. **EU-UK Joint Operations**: UK participation in EU CSDP missions on a case-by-case basis (UK contributed to **EUMAM Mozambique** training during 2023-2024; status under SDP framework)

## Cooperation pillars

The May 2025 Joint Declaration committed both sides to negotiate cooperation arrangements on:

1. **Defence procurement + industrial cooperation** (SAFE, EDIP)
2. **Crisis management + CSDP missions**
3. **Military mobility** (PESCO + EU funding instruments)
4. **Maritime security** (Indo-Pacific + Black Sea + Atlantic)
5. **Cyber + space defence**
6. **Hybrid threats + foreign information manipulation**
7. **Sanctions coordination** (Article 215 TFEU + UK sanctions regime alignment)
8. **Counter-terrorism + serious organised crime**

## State of play (May 2026)

- Negotiation rounds on the Security and Defence Partnership cooperation arrangements continued through H2 2025 + H1 2026 under successive presidencies
- The **TCA review** under Article 776 TCA (every 5 years from 2026) is now active — provides a political moment for parallel SDP institutionalisation
- **Council Decision 32026D1069** of 12 May 2026 — "Council Decision on the position to be taken on behalf of the EU vis-à-vis the UK regarding the determination under Article 540(2) of the Trade and Cooperation Agreement" — illustrates the granular Council-decision traffic on UK-relations files
- **Defence-industrial cooperation pilots** (under EDA Administrative Arrangement) include joint munitions production efforts in 2025-2026 (artillery shells + air-defence missiles)

## How this flows through Brubru answers

When users ask about EU-UK defence cooperation, UK participation in SAFE/EDIP/PESCO, or the SDP:
- Reference the **19 May 2025 EU-UK Summit Joint Declaration** as the political anchor (not yet a binding agreement)
