# Food 2040 areas 3 and 4: resource efficiency and zero-pollution; food environments and consumer empowerment

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** areas 3 and 4 of the **six** Food 2040 areas of action (COM(2026) 453, section 3.4).
- **Area 3, resource efficiency and zero-pollution in food systems:** new knowledge and solutions to raise resource efficiency, **reduce food waste from processing to consumption** and **decouple food systems from fossil fuels**, including petrochemical products for packaging and transport. It helps food and drink manufacturers, transport companies and other operators cut pollution while preserving biodiversity, supports the **energy transition of food systems**, and covers **food packaging**: less material, more bio-based packaging, better recyclability and shelf life. It also covers **microbiome-based solutions** in post-harvest environments (storage, processing, packaging) to reduce pollution and improve preservation.
- **Area 4, food environments and consumer empowerment:** understanding what shapes what people eat: **availability, affordability, accessibility, labelling, packaging, transport, distribution and marketing** of food, and the role of **social media**. It develops solutions that improve availability, affordability and transparency of healthy and sustainable choices, is closely linked to the diets area, and draws on behavioural, social and cultural sciences to strengthen **consumer trust and education** and support demand-side transformation.
- **Related Food 2030 facts (SWD(2026) 271):** food waste is **58 million tonnes a year** in the EU, market value about **EUR 132 billion**; projects such as ZeroW (nine living labs), REFRESH (fed the EU Platform on Food Losses and Food Waste), PRECIOUS and WASTEWISE (2024-2028), MAGNO (packaging).
