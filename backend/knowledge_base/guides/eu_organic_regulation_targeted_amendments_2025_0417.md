# Organic Regulation targeted amendments, procedure 2025/0417(COD): provisional agreement of 20 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **STATUS (Tuesday 20 October 2026): the Council (Irish presidency) and the European Parliament reached a provisional agreement on the targeted amendments to Regulation (EU) 2018/848 on organic production and labelling** (Council press release of 20 October 2026, 12:20; Commission press release IP/26/2210). **NOT yet law**: the agreement must be endorsed by the Council and by Parliament, then legal-linguistic revision and formal adoption, aimed "before the end of 2026"; the regulation enters into force the day after its publication in the Official Journal.
- **Procedure: 2025/0417(COD). Commission proposal COM(2025) 780 of 16 December 2025 (CELEX 52025PC0780, title verified at Cellar): "Proposal for a Regulation amending Regulation (EU) 2018/848 as regards certain production, labelling and certification rules and certain rules on trade with third countries".** Rapporteur: Camilla Laureti (S&D, Italy), AGRI; AGRI position and mandate to negotiate adopted 14 July 2026; Council negotiating mandate 11 May 2026 (EP Legislative Train entry). Note: the earlier organic reform (Regulation 2018/848 itself) was procedure 2014/0100(COD); do not mix the two.
- **Why:** the Court of Justice judgment of 4 October 2024 in C-240/23 (Herbaria Kraeuterparadies II) held that products imported under the equivalence regime may not use terms referring to organic production or the EU organic logo unless additional conditions are met.
- **What was agreed (Council release, read in full):** imports from non-EU countries whose rules are recognised as equivalent may still be marketed as "organic" and may use their own country's organic logo; the EU organic logo only if they also meet additional EU production and control requirements (living soil, animal welfare, processing); the recognition of 11 non-EU countries (including Argentina, Canada, New Zealand and the United States), due to expire on 31 December 2026, is extended to 31 December 2036; the Commission's proposal to allow the EU logo on products with less than 5% of ingredients from non-compliant non-EU sources was REMOVED; the certification exemption for small operators selling unpackaged products directly to consumers rises from EUR 20 000 to EUR 40 000 annual turnover (five-year average) and from 5 000 kg to 10 000 kg, and is extended under conditions to small online retailers of pre-packed organic products; temporary, phased-out flexibility for non-organic protein feed for poultry and pigs and for aquaculture juveniles; products labelled under current rules may be sold until stocks run out; transitional arrangements for imports.
- **Figures:** organic land is 11% of EU agricultural land (Commission); organic farmland grew 79%, from 9.5 to 16.9 million hectares (Commission figure quoted by the Council). The current framework applies since January 2022.
