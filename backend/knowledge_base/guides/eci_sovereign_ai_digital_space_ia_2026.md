# European Citizens' Initiative "Creation of a European Digital Space (.IA) for Sovereign AI Governance" (registered 6 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **6 October 2026** the European Commission **registered** the European Citizens' Initiative (ECI) entitled **"Creation of a European Digital Space (.IA) for Sovereign AI Governance"** (press release IP/26/2064, headline "sovereign European AI domains"). Registration means the initiative is **legally admissible**. It does NOT mean the Commission supports it.
- **What the organisers ask:** the Commission "to propose a framework for high-trust digital spaces and to take the initiative at European level to negotiate with ISO and ICANN with a view to safeguarding the .IA domain". **Three objectives:** (1) an EU framework promoting **"High-Trust Digital Spaces"** and testing environments (**sandboxes**) for ethical AI; (2) negotiate with **ISO** and **ICANN/IANA** on the **reservation, protection and allocation of the namespace .IA** as an asset of European digital sovereignty; (3) ensure that the registration and resolution of .IA incorporate **eIDAS 2.0** (Sovereign Digital Identity), **algorithmic auditing under the AI Act** and **GDPR** data protection.
- **Signatures NOT yet open:** after registration "the organisers have **six months to open the 12-month period of signature collection**" (press release; Regulation (EU) 2019/788, Article 8(1)). Nothing has been collected yet. A successful initiative needs **one million valid statements of support from at least seven Member States** (one quarter of the Member States), with minimum numbers per country.
- **What registration does NOT do:** "The Commission has not analysed the substance of the proposals at this stage. The registration does not influence the Commission's final decision on its merits." The Commission decides on the initiative only if it reaches the one-million threshold, and then must react and justify its decision. The initiative expresses only the organisers' views.
- **Admissibility test (three conditions):** the action does not manifestly fall outside the Commission's powers to propose a legal act; it is not manifestly abusive, frivolous or vexatious; it is not manifestly contrary to the Union's values (Article 2 TEU) or the Charter.
- **Scale:** the Commission says it has registered **136 initiatives** since the ECI began (April 2012).
- **Do not state:** that .IA exists as a domain, that ISO or ICANN have agreed to anything, or that the Commission plans a proposal.

## Why it matters
It is a citizens' agenda-setting tool: if it gathers a million signatures, the Commission must respond publicly. The topic links to the EU's digital sovereignty debate, the AI Act, eIDAS 2.0 and internet governance (ICANN is the global body for domain names; ISO maintains the two-letter country and territory codes that top-level domains build on).

## FAQ
**Is .IA an official EU domain?** No. The initiative asks the Commission to negotiate for it.
**Can I sign it now?** Not yet: the signature collection period has not been opened by the organisers.
**Will the Commission propose a law?** Only if the initiative gathers the required signatures and the Commission decides to act; registration commits it to nothing.
