# European Committee of the Regions (CoR)

## Overview

The **European Committee of the Regions (CoR)** is the EU advisory body representing local and regional authorities. Established by the Maastricht Treaty (1992), it gives regions, cities, and municipalities a direct voice in EU decision-making. The CoR must be consulted on legislation affecting regional and local government, and may also issue own-initiative opinions.

- **Treaty basis:** Articles 300, 305-307 TFEU
- **Members:** 329 members + 329 alternates from all 27 Member States
- **Mandate:** 5 years (current: 2025-2030)
- **President:** Kata Tutto (Hungary, PES) -- until summer 2027
- **Seat:** Rue Belliard 101, 1040 Brussels (shared building with EESC)
- **Website:** https://cor.europa.eu

## Role and Powers

The CoR is **consulted** by the Commission, Council, and EP on proposals affecting:
- Economic, social, and territorial cohesion
- Trans-European networks (transport, energy, telecoms)
- Health, education, culture
- Employment and social policy
- Environment, energy, climate
- Transport

The CoR may also issue **own-initiative opinions** on any matter with regional/local impact and **refer matters to the CJEU** if the subsidiarity principle is violated (Article 8 of Protocol No 2).

### Types of Opinions

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Mandatory** | Treaties require CoR consultation (e.g. cohesion policy, transport, environment) |
| **Optional (facultative)** | Commission, Council, or EP requests CoR opinion on other matters |
| **Own-initiative** | CoR decides to issue opinion on its own |
| **Simplified procedure** | Short deliberation, no plenary debate, vote only |

## Commissions (Thematic)

The CoR has 6 thematic commissions that prepare draft opinions:

| Code | Name | Policy Focus |
|------|------|-------------|
| CIVEX | Citizenship, Governance, Institutional and External Affairs | Enlargement, migration, governance, external relations |
| COTER | Territorial Cohesion Policy and EU Budget | Cohesion policy, MFF, structural funds, urban/rural policy |
| ECON | Economic Policy | Single market, industry, competitiveness, SMEs, customs, public procurement |
| ENVE | Environment, Climate Change and Energy | Green Deal, climate, energy, water, biodiversity |
| NAT | Natural Resources | Agriculture, rural development, fisheries, forestry, food |
| SEDEC | Social Policy, Education, Employment, Research and Culture | Social rights, education, youth, research, digital, culture, health |

There is also a **CFAA** (Commission for Financial and Administrative Affairs) handling internal governance.

## Relationship with Other Institutions

| Institution | Interaction |
|-------------|-------------|
| European Commission | Sends legislative proposals to CoR for consultation; attends plenary debates |
| European Parliament | May request CoR opinions; EP President addresses CoR plenaries |
| Council of the EU | May request CoR opinions; Council Presidency addresses CoR |
| EESC | Shares building and some administrative services; complementary advisory roles (EESC = civil society, CoR = regional/local authorities) |
| National Parliaments | CoR monitors subsidiarity via REGPEX network |
