# Houthi attacks: the European Parliament's resolution of 22 October 2026 (2026/2939(RSP))

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **Thursday 22 October 2026** the European Parliament adopted a resolution on the escalation of Houthi attacks and the implications for regional security, procedure **2026/2939(RSP)**, adopted text **P10_TA(2026)0366** (AFET). The vote tally is not in the press release: do not give one.
- **What Parliament asks:** that the **Council put the Houthi movement, including its military and political wings, leadership and military and security apparatus, on the EU list of terrorist organisations**, following a proposal by High Representative Kaja Kallas; that the Houthis' funding channels be dismantled and their assets frozen; an immediate end to hostilities inside and outside Yemen; stronger EU-Gulf security cooperation.
- **What Parliament condemns:** the missile and drone attacks by the Houthis against **Saudi Arabia** (the press release pictures the attack on Abha International Airport of 7 October 2026) and their military offensive in Yemen, called flagrant violations of international law and international humanitarian law; earlier Houthi missile and drone attacks against Israel and the United Arab Emirates; the Iranian regime's role (weapons, missile and drone technology, training, financing) and the coordination between the Houthis, Hezbollah and other Iran-backed groups; threats to Red Sea shipping.
- **What Parliament supports:** the security, sovereignty and territorial integrity of Saudi Arabia and of Yemen's legitimate government.
- **What this is NOT:** a resolution is Parliament's position, not EU law. A listing on the EU terrorist list is a **Council** decision (Common Position 2001/931/CFSP regime); none had been adopted when this guide was written. Do not say the Houthis "are" EU-listed.
- **Related EU action already in place:** EUNAVFOR Aspides (Red Sea maritime security operation, since February 2024) and the EU's Yemen sanctions regime: see `eu_nato_southern_neighbourhood` and `eu_sanctions_implementation_framework`.
- **Not read:** the resolution text itself (not yet available in English when written); only the EP press release of 22 October 2026 (20261018IPR48266).
