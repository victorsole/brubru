## Two-Track Architecture (Politico EU reveal, 16 April 2026)

The Commission designed AccelerateEU as a **two-track package** to navigate the tension between short-term price cushioning and the medium-term electrification push:

### Track A -- Short-term price cushioning (weeks)
- State aid flexibility for electricity price relief to energy-intensive industries (legal basis: Article 107(3)(c) TFEU + Temporary Crisis and Transition Framework)
- Grid tariff reform to reduce cost pass-through to consumers
- Gas demand-reduction measures (voluntary) with coordinated early-winter preparation
- Oil reserves release coordination (already used during March 2026 crisis peak)
- Targeted support for transport sector (fisheries EMFAF mobilisation already live since 16 April 2026)

### Track B -- Medium-term electrification (months-years)
- Electricity tax cuts proposal (Energy Taxation Directive revision momentum)
- REDIII Delegated Act review scheduled for **June 2026** -- renewable hydrogen definition + additionality rules
- Power Purchase Agreements facilitation (SWD(2026) 118 synopsis feeds into Commission Recommendation)
- European energy grids package acceleration (EPRS_BRI(2026)774752) -- TEN-E Regulation revision + permit-granting directive
- 235 cross-border energy projects (PCI/PMI 5th list, 9 April 2026) -- interconnection boost
- Electricity supplier switching in 24 hours by end 2026 (Commission implementing rules adopted 14 April 2026)

## Contextual trade-off (Contexte EU, 20 April 2026)

Contexte EU framed the internal Commission debate as a "vicious cycle of the EU's energy price dilemma": short-term subsidies that cushion the Iran-war price impact risk increasing fossil fuel demand and undermining electrification. AccelerateEU is Ribera's attempt to square this circle -- price relief conditioned on, and paired with, acceleration of clean-energy deployment.

## Stakeholder signals

- **Industry (Eurofer, CEFIC, BusinessEurope):** welcomed short-term price relief, cautious on permanent state aid loosening
- **Utilities (Eurelectric):** supportive of grid investment track, pressed for accelerated permitting
- **NGOs (CAN Europe, WWF EU):** welcomed electrification push, criticised fossil subsidy extension risk
- **Trade unions (ETUC):** pressed for job transition safeguards tied to state aid grants
- **Member States:** France + Germany push state aid flexibility; Netherlands + Nordics push single-market discipline; Southern MS (ES, IT, EL, PT) push electricity tax cuts
