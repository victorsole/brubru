- Committee chair: Adam Jarubas (EPP, Poland)
- Replaces: Directive 2001/83/EC (Community code for human medicines) + Regulation (EC) 726/2004 (centralised procedure)
- Key innovation: new data exclusivity framework (8+1 years, max 11), AMR transferable voucher, mandatory shortage prevention plans
- Data exclusivity: 8 years regulatory data protection + 1 year market protection (standard). Up to 3 additional years for unmet needs, new active substances, new therapeutic indications. Maximum 11 years total.
- Orphan medicines: 11 years market exclusivity for breakthrough orphan products addressing diseases with no available treatment
- AMR: transferable 12-month data exclusivity voucher for priority antimicrobials, mandatory prescriptions, stewardship plans, environmental risk assessments
- Generic/biosimilar access: enhanced Bolar exemption clarifies patent rights not infringed for studies needed to obtain marketing authorisation or HTA
- Supply chain: mandatory shortage prevention plans, dual monitoring (national + EU), EMA critical shortages list
- Regulatory improvements: electronic submissions, unlimited marketing authorisation validity, regulatory sandboxes

## Overview

The EU Pharmaceutical Legislation Reform is the most significant overhaul of EU medicines law since 2004. It replaces Directive 2001/83/EC and Regulation (EC) 726/2004 with a modernised framework addressing three core objectives: boosting innovation and competitiveness, ensuring equitable access to affordable medicines, and strengthening supply chain resilience.

The provisional agreement reached on 11 December 2025 after four trilogues (June, October, November, December 2025) represents a compromise between the Commission's original proposal (shorter data exclusivity to accelerate generic entry) and Parliament/Council demands for stronger innovation incentives.

## Data Exclusivity Framework (as agreed)

| Protection type | Duration | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory data protection (standard) | 8 years | All new marketing authorisations |
| Market protection (standard) | +1 year | Automatic |
| Unmet medical needs bonus | +12 months | Addresses an unmet medical need |
| New active substance bonus | +12 months | Meets specific clinical trial conditions |
| New therapeutic indication bonus | +12 months | Significant clinical benefit |
| **Maximum total** | **11 years** | All bonuses combined |
| Orphan breakthrough | 11 years | Diseases with no available treatment |
