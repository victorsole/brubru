## Categories of countries under Horizon Europe rules

Horizon Europe divides participating countries into three categories, each with distinct eligibility and funding rules:

### EU Member States
All 27 EU Member States are eligible to participate in Horizon Europe calls and can receive funding.

### Associated Countries
These countries have signed association agreements with the EU and participate under equivalent conditions to EU Member States. Association is governed by Regulation (EU) 2021/695. Specific limitations or conditions may apply based on the work programme and call requirements, including:
- Restrictions on participation to EU legal entities only
- Conditions attached to entities controlled by ineligible countries
- Requirements based on the place of establishment to meet policy objectives

### Other Third Countries
Non-associated countries are divided into subcategories:
- Low-to-middle-income countries automatically eligible for funding
- Countries with specific bilateral funding mechanisms (complementary funding)
- All other countries, typically participating at their own cost unless participation is deemed essential

## Special cases (UK, Switzerland, Faroe Islands, Türkiye)

### United Kingdom

- Status: Associated to Horizon Europe from 2024 onwards
- Coverage: Entire Programme except EIC Fund (which is part of the EIC Accelerator and provides investment through equity or other repayable forms)
- Participation: UK entities can participate and receive funding equivalent to EU Member States
- Grant signature: No Horizon Europe grants signed with UK entities for budgetary commitments before 2024 (save for exceptional circumstances defined in work programmes)

### Switzerland

- Status: Associated to both Horizon Europe and Euratom Research and Training Programme from 2025 onwards
- Coverage: Entire Horizon Europe Programme
- Participation: Swiss legal entities can participate under equivalent conditions to EU Member States
- Euratom: Also associated to entire Euratom Research and Training Programme

### Faroe Islands

- Status: Associated Country with full participation rights
- Coverage: Eligible for all Horizon Europe calls
- Participation: Treated as an Associated Country despite their external relationship with the EU

### Türkiye

- Status: Associated Country
- Coverage: Eligible for Horizon Europe participation
- Participation: Equivalent conditions to EU Member States

## How third-country participants integrate in consortia

### Consortium roles for Associated Countries

Associated Countries and their legal entities can participate in consortia in any role, including as coordinators and beneficiaries. They receive funding equivalent to EU Member States unless specific work programme or call restrictions apply.

### Consortium roles for other third countries with automatic funding eligibility

Low-to-middle-income countries' participants are automatically funded for beneficiary roles. They may participate at their own cost in other roles unless otherwise specified.

### Exceptional funding for other third countries

Legal entities from countries not automatically eligible can receive funding if:

- The country is explicitly identified in the work programme and call for proposals as eligible for funding, OR
- The granting authority determines that participation as a beneficiary is essential for implementing the project, based on:
  - Outstanding competence or expertise
  - Access to particular research infrastructures
  - Access to particular geographical environments
  - Access to particular data

### Cost-sharing arrangements

Non-associated countries without automatic funding eligibility typically participate at their own cost. Some countries have established complementary funding mechanisms with national financing arrangements to support their participants.

### Restrictive measures compliance

All participants, regardless of origin, must comply with EU restrictive measures under Article 29 TEU and Article 215 TFEU. Entities subject to these measures cannot participate in any capacity, including as beneficiaries, affiliated entities, associated partners, subcontractors, or recipients of financial support.

## Euratom Research and Training Programme

### Associated Countries under Euratom

The Euratom Research and Training Programme (Council Regulation (Euratom) 2021/765) has two associated countries:

- Switzerland (associated from 2025 onwards for award procedures implementing budgetary commitments for the year 2025 onwards)
- Ukraine (associated)

Note that the United Kingdom did not associate to the Euratom Programme.
