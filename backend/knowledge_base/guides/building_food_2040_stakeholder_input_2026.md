# Building Food 2040: stakeholder input (631 survey responses, 63 submissions, July 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** the report **"Building FOOD 2040: Stakeholder Input for the new EU R&I Strategic Agenda for Food Systems"**, **DG Research and Innovation** (Directorate B Healthy Planet, Unit B.2 Bioeconomy and Food Systems), edited by **Alessandro Gallina and Pierre-Mathieu Pelissier**, manuscript **May 2026**, announced **13 July 2026** (ISBN 978-92-68-40288-7, doi 10.2777/2306913). It analyses an **EU-wide survey run in March 2026**, evidence for **Food 2040**. It does not necessarily reflect the Commission's position.
- **Size:** **631 valid survey responses** and **63 written submissions** (position papers, policy briefs, roadmaps); **59 of 63** propose areas to complement or specify the **11 Food 2030 pathways**. (SWD(2026) 271 says "more than 600" and 52% research and academia; the report says 631 and 51.5%.)
- **Top five priorities for Food 2040:** (1) **reduced environmental and climate impacts beyond the farm gate, 46.4%**; (2) **improved health through healthier dietary habits, 44.7%**; (3) **resilience and preparedness, including strategic autonomy and crises, 44.1%**; (4) **nature-based solutions and agroecological practices, 32.2%**; (5) **behavioural change and citizen empowerment for accessible, affordable, healthy, sustainable diets, 29.6%**.
- **Improving the 11 pathways:** stronger alignment with sector realities **59.4%**, clearer objectives and impacts **38.7%**, consolidation (merge or simplify) **30.9%**, plus better **monitoring**.
- **Who answered:** research and academia **51.5%**; citizens **9.4%**; agriculture and farming **8.4%**; government and policy **7.8%**; NGOs **7.6%**; food manufacturing and processing **6.7%**; consumer groups **1.7%**; retailers, distributors and logistics **0.3%**. Most answers from **Spain and Germany**; per capita, **Belgium, Ireland, Finland and Denmark**. **More than a third** did not know Food 2030 well enough to judge it.
- **Findings:** the Food 2030 **systems approach** is valued; bridge the **"valley of death"** up to technology readiness level (TRL) 6; integrate **primary producers in post-farm-gate valorisation**; **capital investment** in infrastructure; **faster EU regulatory procedures**; **EU strategic autonomy**; themes: **protein diversification**, **circularity**, **short supply chains**, **local resilience**; pilot plants, demonstration facilities, bio-manufacturing capacity, shared public-private facilities; domestic **aquaculture** and **plant-based protein processing**; echoes of the **Draghi report**.
- **Three conclusions:** deepen engagement with **post-farm-gate actors**; a renewed, more **operational** set of pathways with impacts and monitoring; link research to **pilot infrastructure, finance, investment and predictable regulation**.
- **Not to be confused with** the AgRI 2040 open consultation (701 replies).
