# EMA Scientific Committees — 2026 Meeting Calendar

QUICK FACTS:
- The European Medicines Agency (EMA), based in Amsterdam, runs monthly plenary meetings of its scientific committees. The three most relevant to the research-based pharmaceutical industry are CHMP, PRAC and COMP.
- **CHMP** (Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use) adopts opinions on marketing-authorisation applications, line extensions and safety referrals for human medicines, which the European Commission turns into EU-wide authorisations. **2026 plenary dates:** 26-29 January, 23-26 February, 23-26 March, 20-23 April, 18-21 May, 22-25 June, 20-23 July, 17-20 August (run by written procedure), 14-17 September, 12-15 October, 9-12 November, 7-10 December.
- **PRAC** (Pharmacovigilance Risk Assessment Committee) assesses safety signals, periodic safety update reports, post-authorisation safety studies and referrals, and recommends EU-wide risk-minimisation measures. **2026 plenary dates:** 12-15 January, 9-12 February, 9-12 March, 7-10 April, 4-7 May, 8-11 June, 6-9 July, 3-6 August (written procedure), 31 August-3 September, 28 September-1 October, 26-29 October, 23-26 November.
- **COMP** (Committee for Orphan Medicinal Products) reviews orphan-designation applications and advises on the EU orphan-medicines framework. **2026 plenary dates:** 20-22 January, 17-19 February, 17-19 March, 14-16 April, 11-13 May, 16-18 June, 14-16 July, 8-10 September, 6-8 October, 3-5 November, 1-3 December (no meeting in August).
- **All meetings are held at EMA headquarters, Domenico Scarlattilaan 6, 1083 HS Amsterdam, Netherlands.** The full schedule is in Brubru's My EU Calendar (filter institution = EMA).

## What these committees do and why they matter

The EMA is the EU's scientific gatekeeper for medicines. Its committees are where the technical assessments that decide whether a medicine reaches European patients actually happen. For pharmaceutical companies and trade associations, the committee calendar sets the rhythm of the regulatory year: submission deadlines, opinion dates and the windows for scientific advice all key off these plenaries.

- **CHMP** is the central committee for human medicines. A positive CHMP opinion is the step before a European Commission marketing-authorisation decision under the centralised procedure. CHMP also handles post-authorisation matters, variations of major interest and safety referrals.
- **PRAC** is the safety committee. Its monthly assessment of signals and periodic safety reports drives label changes, risk-management plans and, where needed, suspensions or referrals. PRAC normally meets the week before CHMP so its recommendations can feed into the CHMP plenary.
- **COMP** decides orphan designations, which unlock the orphan incentives (protocol assistance, fee reductions and market exclusivity). It is directly relevant to the orphan-incentive debate in the pharmaceutical legislation revision.

## Working out the next meeting

To find the next meeting from today's date, take the committee's 2026 list above and pick the first plenary whose start date is on or after today. For example, if today is in late May 2026, the next CHMP plenary is 22-25 June 2026, the next PRAC is 8-11 June 2026, and the next COMP is 16-18 June 2026.

## Other EMA committees

EMA also runs the CAT (Committee for Advanced Therapies), PDCO (Paediatric Committee), CVMP (veterinary medicines) and HMPC (herbal medicinal products). Brubru currently tracks CHMP, PRAC and COMP in the calendar; ask if you need the others added.
