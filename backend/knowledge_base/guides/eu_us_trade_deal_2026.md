# EU-US Trade Deal 2026

## QUICK FACTS
Status: EU-US trade deal negotiated by von der Leyen; EP vote expected within approximately 2 weeks (late March/early April 2026)
Context: Iran war and Trump tariff threats creating pressure on both sides
EP vote outcome: Uncertain and "bitterly contentious" (Politico EU, 16 March 2026)
Trump tariffs: Ongoing threat of tariffs on EU goods; "US patience with EU wearing thin"
Political divide: Some MEPs want to use trade deal as leverage on international law issues; others fear antagonising Trump
Borrell position: Former HR/VP called for suspending implementation of "unfair" US trade deal in wake of Iran war
Key committee: INTA (International Trade)
Lead Commissioner: Maros Sefcovic (Trade) / Valdis Dombrovskis (Economy)
Treaty basis: Article 207 TFEU (common commercial policy)

## Overview

The EU and the United States have negotiated a trade agreement that is now before the European Parliament for ratification. The deal was negotiated by Commission President von der Leyen and aims to normalise transatlantic trade relations amid escalating global tensions.

## Political Context

### In Favour of the Deal
- Avoiding a transatlantic trade war at a time of global instability
- Securing market access for EU exporters
- Maintaining the transatlantic alliance amid the Iran crisis and Ukraine war
- Germany and other export-dependent Member States want stable US trade relations

### Against the Deal / Seeking Conditions
- Former HR/VP Josep Borrell: Called the deal "unfair" and urged EU to suspend its implementation as leverage against US positions on Iran/international law
- Progressive MEPs: Want Council conclusions calling for international law to be upheld (indirect rebuke to Trump)
- Concerns about US unilateral tariffs and extraterritorial sanctions
- Trump's "very bad future" threats to NATO allies create political resistance
- Iran war has hardened anti-US sentiment in parts of the EP

### Timing Pressure
- Politico EU reports "time runs out to avert new trade war as US patience with EU wears thin"
- EP vote expected in approximately 2 weeks (late March/early April 2026)
- European Council (19-20 March) may discuss trade as part of competitiveness agenda
- Iran crisis is consuming political bandwidth that would otherwise go to trade ratification

## Related EU Trade Issues

- **Steel and aluminium tariffs**: Longstanding US tariffs on EU metals
- **Digital services taxes**: US opposition to EU/Member State digital levies
- **Agricultural standards**: Persistent differences on food safety, GMOs, hormones
- **Subsidy competition**: Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) concerns about green subsidy race
- **Technology export controls**: Coordination on China-related tech restrictions
