# Court of Justice, 5 November 2026: COVID airline aid to KLM and Condor (Ryanair loses two General Court wins)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Two Court of Justice judgments of Thursday 5 November 2026** on appeal from the General Court, both against Ryanair, both on COVID-era State aid to airlines. Final judgments of the Court of Justice; no further appeal.
- **KLM, Joined Cases C-266/24 P (Commission v Ryanair), C-269/24 P (KLM v Ryanair) and C-289/24 P (Air France and Air France-KLM v Ryanair), press release 157/26.** The aid: **EUR 3.4 billion** from the Netherlands (a State guarantee on a bank loan and a State loan) for KLM's liquidity. History: the General Court annulled the 2020 approval (2021); the Commission re-approved it on 16 July 2021, naming KLM and its subsidiaries the sole beneficiaries; the General Court annulled that too, holding that Air France and the Air France-KLM holding should have counted as beneficiaries. **The Court of Justice sets that judgment aside, dismisses Ryanair's action IN PART and sends the case BACK to the General Court** for the pleas it had not ruled on. Why: the General Court rightly used the "economic unit" concept but erred in law by substituting its own assessment for the Commission's without showing the Commission's conclusion was implausible. **So the KLM approval stands for now, but the case is NOT closed.**
- **Condor, Case C-505/24 P (Condor Flugdienst v Ryanair), press release 158/26.** The aid: **EUR 321 million** restructuring aid from Germany, approved on 26 July 2021 without a formal investigation (Condor's difficulties came from Thomas Cook's insolvency and the pandemic). The General Court had annulled the approval, saying the Commission should have had doubts (burden sharing). **The Court of Justice sets that judgment aside and, ruling itself, DISMISSES Ryanair's action: the Condor approval stands. This case IS closed.**
- **Different outcomes, keep them apart:** KLM = referred back to the General Court; Condor = Ryanair's action dismissed for good. EUR 3.4 billion is KLM's, EUR 321 million is Condor's.
