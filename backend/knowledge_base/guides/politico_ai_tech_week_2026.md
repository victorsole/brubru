## What the summit is

Politico Europe's annual flagship convening of EU digital-policy stakeholders. Three days of panels and on-the-record interviews with Commission officials, MEPs, Council representatives, sponsor executives, civil society, academics. The 2026 edition lands two months before the AI Act's Annex III high-risk obligations apply (2 August 2026), six months into the Digital Omnibus legislative procedure (COM(2025) 836-837), and during open Commission proceedings against Apple, Google, Meta under DMA enforcement powers.

## Commission speakers — DG CNECT and adjacent

- **Renate Nikolay** — Deputy Director-General, **DG CNECT** (Communications Networks, Content and Technology). Reports to Director-General Roberto Viola. German national, joined the Commission via DG TRADE and DG JUST. Lead Commission interlocutor on DSA and DMA cross-border enforcement coordination, Code of Conduct on illegal hate speech (revised, first results published 10 April 2026), Digital Services Coordinators network. Key voice on "regulatory predictability" framing — pushes back against the Digital Omnibus narrative that the EU rulebook is too complex.

- **Ekaterina Zaharieva** — **Commissioner for Startups, Research and Innovation** (Bulgaria, EPP). Portfolio combines Horizon Europe / FP10 (EUR 175 billion proposed for 2028-2034), the European Research Council, and the EU Inc. (28th Regime) startup file COM(2026) 321 adopted 18 March 2026 jointly with Commissioners McGrath (Justice) and Virkkunen (Tech). Key voice for the "competitive AI" angle: ECF (European Competitiveness Fund) policy windows on AI/digital and the AI Continent Action Plan.

- **Thibaut Kleiner** — **Director, Future Networks**, DG CNECT. Owns the Digital Networks Act file (COM(2026) 0016, procedure 2026/0013(COD), published 21 January 2026) which replaces the European Electronic Communications Code, BEREC Regulation, and the Open Internet Regulation. Investment-gap focus: EUR 148 billion for full fibre, EUR 33.5 billion for full 5G. French national, long Commission career across DG CNECT regulatory and policy roles.

- **Kilian Gross** — **Director, Enabling and Emerging Technologies**, DG CNECT. AI Office reporting line. Owns AI Act implementation files: harmonised standards (Mandate M/613), GPAI Code of Practice working sessions, the AI Act Service Desk. Austrian national. Voice on "how the AI Act is actually being implemented", as opposed to the more political Code-of-Practice debate.

- **Björn Juretzki** — **Head of Unit, Data Policy and Innovation**, DG CNECT. Owns Data Act implementation, European Data Spaces, and the data-governance pillar of the Digital Omnibus (COM(2025) 837 — repeals Data Governance Act, Open Data, Free Flow of Data, Platform-to-Business). German national.

## What to watch — analytical anchors

1. **DMA narrative under pressure.** Sponsor framings (Accenture, Google, Visa) emphasise "regulatory complexity"; expect counter-framings from Caffarra, Karlitschek, Domínguez, Teixeira on "EU sovereignty erosion" if Digital Omnibus weakens DMA / DSA. Cavazzini-Schwab DMA enforcement debate (28 April 2026 plenary) is the immediate institutional context.
2. **GPAI Code of Practice — voluntary or de facto mandatory?** Code signatories are positioning for harmonised standards (which will be binding under Article 40 AI Act). Expect Kilian Gross / Sean Perryman to argue for Code-derived standards; Privitera / Schaake for stricter Article 51 systemic-risk classification.
3. **Cookie / ePrivacy simplification.** The "60% of cookies no longer require consent" headline of Digital Omnibus COM(2025) 837 is the most consumer-facing element. BEUC's Teixeira will hold the line; Visa / Google will push for adoption-acceleration framing.
4. **Cybersecurity Act 2 timeline.** Gregorová is at preparatory phase; ENISA is positioning to expand certification scope. NIS2 implementation gaps (Member State transposition slipping) are a sub-theme that Groothuis will likely raise.
5. **EU Inc. cross-link.** Zaharieva will tie the startup file (COM(2026) 321) to the AI / digital agenda — competitive AI requires EU-incorporated capital and EU-stack infrastructure. Repasi (EP rapporteur on EU Inc.) is not on the speakers list as of 30 April 2026; check Politico's updated agenda.
