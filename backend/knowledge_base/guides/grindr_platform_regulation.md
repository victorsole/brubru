# Grindr Inc. — EU Regulatory Exposure

## QUICK FACTS
- **EU Transparency Register:** **NOT REGISTERED** — no direct entry, no intermediary declared, zero Brussels presence
- **Listed:** NYSE: GRND; 2025 revenue ~$400M; 28% FY 2025 revenue growth
- **Core audience:** LGBTQ+ adults; LBGTIQ-targeted dating/social networking
- **HQ:** West Hollywood, CA (Grindr Inc.); Grindr LLC subsidiary operates in EU
- **Brussels presence:** zero (the strongest cold-outreach signal of the 8 platforms in this campaign)

## CORE REGULATORY STACK
- **Digital Services Act** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — near-VLOP threshold debate (EU MAU estimates >40M); NI-ICS classification under discussion; Art 28 minor protection applies even though service is 18+; Ireland-headquartered → Irish DSC competent.
- **CSAM Temporary Derogation** (Reg 2021/1232, CELEX 32021R1232) — **EXPIRED 3 April 2026**. Direct-message-heavy platform without derogation has no legal basis for proactive DM scanning.
- **Combating Sexual Abuse Directive** (Dir 2011/92, CELEX 32011L0092) — grooming criminalisation, livestreaming, CSAM. 2024 recast (COM(2024) 60) in trilogue.
- **CSAM Regulation** (COM(2022) 209) — pending; Grindr's DM model = maximum exposure to detection orders.
- **GDPR Art 9 special-category data** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679) — sexual orientation (explicit) + HIV status (health data) are Art 9 categories; Grindr has 2021 Norwegian DPA fine of NOK 65M (≈ €6.3M) for ad-tech data sharing without Art 9 valid consent; 2023 Turkish KVKK fine.
- **ePrivacy Directive** (Dir 2002/58, CELEX 32002L0058) — location tracking, advertising cookies; profile proximity data = location data regime.
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — photo moderation, spam filtering, deepfake profile detection; Art 5 prohibitions on emotion inference; high-risk if biometric categorisation.
- **NIS2 Directive** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555) — Annex I Sector 8 online social networking services platform; Grindr in scope.
- **Cyber Resilience Act** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847) — Grindr app = product with digital elements; 11 Dec 2027 core obligations.
- **Representative Actions Directive** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828) — NOYB and other QCEs active; data-misuse pathway.
- **European Accessibility Act** (Dir 2019/882, CELEX 32019L0882) — Grindr Unlimited (paid) checkout in scope.
- **P2B Regulation** (Reg 2019/1150, CELEX 32019R1150) — advertiser business user relationships.
- **PSD2 / PSD3** — Grindr Unlimited payments; Apple/Google IAP dependencies.
- **AMLR** (Reg 2024/1624) — only if Grindr re-enters crypto/tipping with convertible assets.
- **Digital Fairness Act** — upcoming proposal H2 2026; dating-app "hook" patterns, subscription cancellation friction in scope.

## URGENT COMPLIANCE
- CSAM derogation expiry (3 Apr 2026) — proactive DM scanning needs new legal basis or consent model.
- NIS2 transposition passed 17 Oct 2024.
- DSA Art 28 in force; adult 18+ platforms have stricter age-verification expectations; EAA applies 28 Jun 2025.

## ENFORCEMENT HISTORY

- Turkish KVKK, **2023**: fine for similar data processing.
- No EU-level (Irish DPC / DSC) enforcement as of April 2026.
