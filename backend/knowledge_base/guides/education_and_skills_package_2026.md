# Education and Skills Package (5 November 2026): basic skills, EU Teachers Agenda, VET strategy, EDU-EDIC

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted Thursday 5 November 2026 by the Commission** (IP/26/2338, Q&A QANDA/26/2339; Executive Vice-President Roxana Mînzatu). **Proposals and communications, NOT binding law:** the two Council Recommendations are not binding and create no EU obligation (the Commission's own words).
- **The documents, by number (Commission document register, 5 November 2026; NOT yet in Cellar or EUR-Lex on 6 November):** COM(2026) 785, proposal for a **Council Recommendation on reversing the decline in basic skills**; COM(2026) 795, proposal for a **Council Recommendation on an attractive teaching profession ("EU Teachers Agenda")**; COM(2026) 770, the package communication; COM(2026) 805 (DG EMPL), the communication with the **VET strategy**. The Recommendations need the Council to adopt them.
- **Why (the Commission's PISA 2025 reading):** about **one in three 15-year-olds** underachieves in literacy and mathematics, **one in four in science**, and underachievement in digital skills **exceeds 40%**.
- **Targets already agreed (June 2026, by the Member States, NOT new in this package):** by 2030, below 15% low achievers among 15-year-olds in reading, mathematics and science, and below 15% among eighth graders in computer and information literacy.
- **New tools:** **European School Alliances** (schools from several Member States, each with a measurable improvement plan for at least one basic skill; Erasmus+ support from 2027, ambition of up to **100 new alliances a year from 2028**); an **EU Network of Advisory and Support Services** from 2027 plus **30 regional services** funded by Erasmus+; a **European Healthy Screens Pledge**.
- **EDU-EDIC:** the European Digital Infrastructure Consortium on Education, set up by **Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2413 of 3 November 2026** (OJ L, 4 November 2026; in force on publication) at the request of eight founding Member States.
- **VET:** around **60% of EU jobs** are in VET-related fields (the Commission's figure).
- **What was NOT read:** the four COM documents themselves (not yet published in Cellar); the facts above are from the Commission's press release and Q&A.
