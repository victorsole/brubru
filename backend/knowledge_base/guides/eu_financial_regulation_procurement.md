# EU Financial Regulation and Public Procurement

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU institutional procurement under the Financial Regulation
- Legal basis: Financial Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046, Title V (Public Contracts)
- Key principles: Transparency, proportionality, equal treatment, non-discrimination, sound financial management
- Thresholds: <EUR 15,000 single tender; EUR 15,000-144,000 negotiated (min 3 candidates); >EUR 144,000 open/restricted procedure
- Procedure types: Open, restricted, negotiated, competitive dialogue
- Evaluation: Minimum 3 committee members from 2+ organisational entities
- Award methods: Best price-quality ratio (typical 60/40 or 70/30), lowest price, lowest cost
- Framework contracts: Max 4 years (single operator, cascade, reopening, or mixed)
- Payment deadline: 30 days from receipt of valid invoice

- Publication: TED (ted.europa.eu), eForms mandatory from October 2023
- Related directive: Directive 2014/24/EU (Member State general procurement)

Guide for EU institutional staff managing procurement procedures under the Financial Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046. Covers contracting authority responsibilities from planning through contract management.

## Legal Framework

### Financial Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046

Title V (Public Contracts) and Title VII (External Actions) govern procurement by EU institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies. The Rules of Application (Annex I) provide detailed procedural guidance.

### Key Principles (Art. 160)

- **Transparency**: Publication of contract notices, evaluation criteria disclosed in advance
- **Proportionality**: Requirements proportionate to contract value and complexity
- **Equal treatment**: No discriminatory specifications or criteria
- **Non-discrimination**: Open to all economic operators from EU and third countries with international agreements
- **Sound financial management**: Economy, efficiency, effectiveness

## Procurement Thresholds

### Current Thresholds (2024-2025)

| Value Range | Procedure | Publication |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| < EUR 15,000 | Single tender (negotiated without publication) | No publication required |
| EUR 15,000 - EUR 144,000 | Negotiated with minimum 3 candidates | Contract award notice only |
| > EUR 144,000 | Open or restricted procedure | OJEU (TED) + Portal |
| > EUR 750,000 (services) or EUR 5,382,000 (works) | Full procedure with enhanced publicity | OJEU + Portal + additional publicity |

### Threshold Calculation Rules

- Aggregate the total estimated value including options and renewals
- Do not split contracts artificially to avoid thresholds
- Framework contracts: estimate total value of all specific contracts over the framework duration
- Lots: aggregate all lot values; below-threshold exemption available for lots under EUR 80,000 (services/supplies) if their total does not exceed 20% of the aggregate

## Procedure Types

### Open Procedure (Art. 164)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| When to use | Default for above-threshold procurement |
| Participation | Open to all economic operators |
| Deadline | Minimum 35 days from OJEU publication |
| Stages | Single stage: submission, evaluation, award |
| Advantage | Simplest, fastest above-threshold procedure |

### Restricted Procedure (Art. 164)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| When to use | Large number of expected candidates, need to limit evaluation burden |
| Participation | Two-stage: expression of interest, then invitation to tender |
| Deadline | Stage 1: 30 days; Stage 2: 25 days |
| Selection | Minimum 5 candidates invited (minimum 3 if fewer qualified) |
| Advantage | Limits evaluation workload while maintaining competition |

### Negotiated Procedure (Art. 164)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| When to use | Below-threshold, or specific Art. 164 grounds (technical exclusivity, extreme urgency, failed open/restricted) |
| Participation | Minimum 3 candidates for middle-value; may be single candidate below EUR 15,000 |
| Negotiation | Price and terms may be negotiated |
| Documentation | Negotiation record mandatory |

### Competitive Dialogue (Art. 164)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| When to use | Complex contracts where the contracting authority cannot define technical means |
| Participation | Pre-qualification, then dialogue phase, then final tenders |
| Duration | Dialogue continues until solutions identified; can be lengthy |
| IP | Dialogue proposals remain confidential |

## Evaluation Committee

### Composition (Art. 150)

- **Minimum 3 members** from at least 2 organisational entities
- One member acts as chairperson
- Members must have no conflict of interest (signed declaration of absence of conflict)
- Secretary (non-voting) manages logistics and documentation

### Evaluation Process

1. **Opening committee**: Verifies completeness of tenders (administrative compliance)
2. **Exclusion criteria check**: Declarations on honour, EDES database verification
3. **Selection criteria assessment**: Technical and professional capacity, financial capacity
4. **Award criteria scoring**: Technical quality and price (best price-quality ratio or lowest price)
5. **Evaluation report**: Detailed reasoning for scores, ranking, recommendation

### Award Criteria

| Method | Description | Typical Split |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Best price-quality ratio** | Weighted combination of quality and price | 60/40 or 70/30 (quality/price) |
| **Lowest price** | Price only (quality threshold met at selection stage) | Used for simple commodity purchases |
| **Lowest cost** | Lifecycle cost approach | Includes operating, maintenance, disposal costs |

## Technical Specifications

### Drafting Rules

- Must not favour a specific product, brand, or economic operator
- Use performance-based specifications where possible
- Reference European or international standards (with "or equivalent")
- Environmental and social criteria permitted if linked to subject matter
- Accessibility requirements mandatory (EU Web Accessibility Directive for digital services)

### Common Pitfalls

- Copying specifications from a preferred supplier's catalogue
- Overly restrictive qualification requirements that limit competition
- Mandatory certifications not available in all Member States
- Language requirements disproportionate to the contract

## Framework Contracts

### Types

| Type | Award of Specific Contracts | When to Use |
|------|----------------------------|-------------|
| **Single operator** | Order forms (no reopening of competition) | Standardised services, clear pricing |
| **Multiple operators** (cascade) | Awarded in cascade order to first-ranked | Moderately standardised, ranked providers |
| **Multiple operators** (reopening) | Mini-competition among framework contractors | Customised services, variable scope |
| **Multiple operators** (mixed) | Some direct award, some mini-competition | Large frameworks with varied needs |

### Duration

- Maximum **4 years** (including renewals)
- Specific contracts may extend beyond framework duration if awarded before expiry

### Estimated Value

The contract notice must state the estimated and maximum total value of all specific contracts. There is no obligation to reach the estimated value.

## Contract Management

### Contract Modification (Art. 172)

| Modification Type | Threshold | Process |
|-------------------|-----------|---------|
| Minor (non-substantial) | Does not change nature; value increase < 10% (services/supplies) or < 15% (works) | Administrative order |
| New services/works unforeseen | Up to 50% of original value | Negotiated modification |
| Change of contractor | Specific grounds (succession, insolvency) | Formal amendment |
| Substantial | Changes nature of contract | New procurement procedure required |

### Payment Terms

- Standard payment deadline: **30 days** from receipt of valid invoice
- Pre-financing: Covered by financial guarantee if > EUR 500,000
- Interim payments: Against deliverable acceptance
- Final payment: After final acceptance and submission of all deliverables

### Liquidated Damages

- May be applied for late delivery or non-performance
- Must be specified in the contract
- Typically 0.5-1% of contract value per week of delay, capped at 10%

## E-Submission and TED

### TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)

- All above-threshold notices published on TED (ted.europa.eu)
- eForms: New XML-based notice format (mandatory from October 2023)
- Contract award notices published within 30 days of award

### E-Submission

- Electronic submission via eSubmission platform (for EU institutions)
- Mandatory for above-threshold procurement
- Double envelope system: technical offer and financial offer submitted separately

## Conflict of Interest (Art. 61)

### Definition

A conflict of interest exists where the impartial and objective exercise of the functions of a financial actor or other person involved in budget implementation is compromised for reasons involving family, emotional life, political or national affinity, economic interest, or any other direct or indirect personal interest.

### Prevention Measures

- Declaration of absence of conflict of interest by all evaluation committee members
- EDES (Early Detection and Exclusion System) database check
- Rotation of evaluation committee members where feasible
- Separation of duties between authorising officer and financial verifier

## Ex-Ante Publicity Waivers

### Grounds for Waiver (Art. 164.1)

- **Extreme urgency**: Unforeseeable events requiring immediate action (not due to the contracting authority's own delay)
- **Technical exclusivity**: Only one operator can perform the contract (with supporting evidence)
- **Repeat services**: Under specific conditions for similar services within 3 years
- **After failed procedure**: No suitable tender received in open/restricted procedure

### Documentation Required

- Authorising officer's reasoned decision
- Market research evidence (for technical exclusivity claims)
- Declaration of urgency (for urgency claims)
- All waivers subject to ex-post audit scrutiny
