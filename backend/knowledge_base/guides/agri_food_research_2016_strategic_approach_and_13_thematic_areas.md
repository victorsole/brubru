# The 2016 strategic approach to EU agricultural R&I and its 13 thematic areas

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it was:** the Commission's 2016 strategic approach to EU agricultural research and innovation, which guided Horizon 2020 (Societal Challenge 2) and Horizon Europe 2021-2027 (Cluster 6). AgRI 2040 **updates and replaces** it (COM(2026) 453, September 2026). SWD(2026) 271 evaluates it: **more than EUR 3 billion in 565 projects (2014-2024)**.
- **Four objectives:** long-term **food security**; **environmental sustainability and resilience** of competitive land-based primary production; **sustainable growth of rural territories**; better **delivery of research results for policy impact**.
- **Five priority areas:** three on **creating value from land** (resource management, in particular soil, water and biodiversity; healthier plants and animals; integrated ecological approaches from farm to landscape) and two on **enhancing rural innovation** (new openings for rural growth; human and social capital in rural areas).
- **Co-creation:** with Member States and associated countries through the Standing Committee on Agricultural Research (SCAR) and **three AgriResearch Conferences (2016, 2018, 2023)**; the 2023 one (31 May to 1 June 2023, Brussels, "Knowledge, innovation and skills for sustainable horizons") set **13 thematic areas**: (1) climate-smart agriculture; (2) biodiversity-friendly agriculture; (3) sustainable management of land, soil, water and nutrients; (4) sustainable management of forests; (5) healthy, sustainable and resilient cropping systems; (6) healthy, sustainable and resilient livestock systems; (7) new farming, food and bio-based systems; (8) rural opportunities; (9) agroecology and organic farming at farm and landscape levels; (10) socio-economic performance and modernising policies; (11) digital and data technologies; (12) enhancing knowledge flows and skills; (13) international cooperation.
- **The fourth conference in the series** was the **EU AgRI 2040 Conference** (24-25 September 2026, Brussels), held after the Communication to start work on priorities for Horizon Europe 2028-2034 and the European Competitiveness Fund; no conclusions had been read on 13 October 2026. (SWD(2026) 271 section 5.1.2 writes "2028-2033" once; the Communication and Commission news say 2028-2034.)
- **AgRI 2040 vs the 2016 list:** AgRI 2040 has **eight preliminary areas**, not 13 thematic areas and not five priority areas.
- **Policy links listed:** CAP reforms, Sustainable Development Goals, European Climate Law, Nature Restoration Regulation, farm to fork, forest, biodiversity and soil strategies, organic action plan, long-term vision for rural areas, Vision for Agriculture and Food, generational renewal strategy, bioeconomy strategy, life sciences strategy.
