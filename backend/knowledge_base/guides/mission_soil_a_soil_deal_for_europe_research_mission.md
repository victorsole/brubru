# EU Mission "A Soil Deal for Europe" (Mission Soil): aims, numbers, projects

## QUICK FACTS
- **Aim:** **healthy soils by 2050**; one of the five EU Missions under Horizon Europe (the others: adaptation to climate change, cancer, climate-neutral and smart cities, restore our ocean and waters). Goal of **100 living labs and lighthouses by 2030**.
- **Numbers (SWD(2026) 271):** since 2021 **45 living labs** set up; **63 R&I projects funded 2021-2024, EUR 469 million, nearly 1,400 organisations**; the **Mission Soil Manifesto** has **over 3,500 signatures** (nearly 700 legal entities); **37 Mission Soil Ambassadors**; **European Mission Soil Week** since 2023 (about 300-350 participants a year); an annual **Investment Forum**. Mission Soil also spent **over EUR 30 million** on circular soil improvers from bio-waste.
- **Living labs:** user-centred, place-based, transdisciplinary R&I ecosystems where land managers, scientists, citizens, businesses and local authorities co-design and test solutions; **lighthouses** demonstrate exemplary practice. Example: LILAS4SOILS, five living labs in six countries on carbon farming (manure incorporation, reduced tillage, grassland, cover crops), with farmers funded by cascade calls.
- **Project clusters:** harmonised **EU soil health monitoring framework** (BENCHMARKS, AI4SoilHealth, WHEATWATCHER); threats (**erosion** EUROSION; **desertification** TERRASAFE, MONALISA; **nitrogen** NitroScope); open knowledge and data (SoilWise, SOLO); innovation hotspots on **biodiversity, carbon farming, soil pollution and remediation, circular solutions**; business models (NOVASOIL, SoilValues, InBestSoil); the **soil-water nexus** (SpongeWorks, Path4Med, SEACURE); **peat alternatives** (Hort2thefuture, SPIN-FERT); soil literacy (LOESS, CURIOSOIL, SoilTribes).
- **Policy fit:** complements the CAP (conditionality, eco-schemes, rural development) by developing and testing solutions; **supports implementation of the Soil Monitoring Law**, **Directive (EU) 2025/2360 of 12 November 2025** (CELEX 32025L2360, title checked at the Publications Office).
- **In the strategy:** named as an example of long-term interconnected ecosystems (COM(2026) 453, section 2.1) and linked to AgRI 2040 area 1 (soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital).
