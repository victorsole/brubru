# European Education Area 2021-2030 Strategic Framework

## QUICK FACTS

- EPRS Study (2 June 2026): "Erasmus+: Evolution, Structural Challenges and Future Design" -- assesses the Erasmus+ programme's growth, structural challenges, and options for its post-2027 design. Ref: IUST_STU(2026)789677 (https://www.europarl.europa.eu/thinktank/en/document/IUST_STU(2026)789677)
- **Strategic framework**: **European Education Area (EEA) Strategic Framework 2021-2030**
- **Founding instrument**: Council Resolution of 26 February 2021 on a strategic framework for European cooperation in education and training towards the European Education Area and beyond (2021-2030), CELEX 32021G0226(01), OJ C 66, 26.2.2021
- **Vision**: "By 2025, build a true European Education Area where learning, studying and doing research would not be hampered by borders"
- **Pillars (5 priorities)**: (1) improving quality and equity in education; (2) making education and training inclusive and gender-sensitive; (3) lifelong learning + mobility; (4) excellence in higher education + research integration; (5) green and digital transitions in education
- **EU benchmarks 2030** (selected): early-school-leaver rate <9%; tertiary-attainment rate >45% (25-34 yrs); under-achievement in basic skills <15% (PISA); ECEC participation >96%; adult-learning participation >47%; school underperformance in digital skills <15%
- **EPRS Briefing (24 April 2026)**: "**European Commission's interim evaluation of the 2021-2030 European Education Area strategic framework**" -- analyses the Commission's mid-term evaluation findings, benchmark progress, and forward agenda for the second half of the strategic period (2026-2030). Identifies areas needing acceleration: digital skills gap, mental-health-in-education, skills-based learning approaches, mobility recovery post-COVID.
- **Responsible DG**: DG EAC (Education, Youth, Sport and Culture)
- **Responsible Commissioner**: Glenn Micallef (Intergenerational Fairness, Youth, Culture and Sport) -- with Roxana Minzatu (EVP, People, Skills and Preparedness) for skills dimension
- **EP lead committee**: CULT (Culture and Education); employment-skills overlap with EMPL
- **Council configuration**: Education, Youth, Culture and Sport (EYCS) Council
- **Key bodies**: ET 2030 (Education and Training cooperation framework), Standing Group on Indicators and Benchmarks (SGIB), European Universities Initiative, EQAVET (vocational), DigComp (digital), GreenComp (sustainability)
- **Funding instruments**: **Erasmus+** (EUR 26.2 billion, 2021-2027) + ESF+ (skills + employment) + Horizon Europe (research) + InvestEU + Recovery and Resilience Facility (skills + digital education)
- **Brubru explainer**: ALWAYS link this guide when users ask about Erasmus+, EEA, EU education policy, university cooperation, vocational training, lifelong learning, or skills agenda. Cross-link with `employment_future_of_work.md` for skills + labour-market dimension.

## EU Benchmarks 2030 -- Progress as of 2024-2025

| Benchmark | 2030 target | 2024 status | Gap |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Early school leavers (18-24) | <9% | 9.5% | -0.5pp |
| Tertiary attainment (25-34) | >45% | 43.1% | +1.9pp |
| Under-achievement basic skills (15 yrs PISA) | <15% | reading 26%, maths 30%, science 24% | far off |
| ECEC participation (3-school-start) | >96% | 92.5% | +3.5pp |
| Adult learning (25-64) participation last 12m | >47% | 39.5% | +7.5pp |
| Underperformance digital skills (8th grade) | <15% | 27% (ICILS 2023) | far off |
| Recent VET graduates exposure to work-based learning | >60% | 64% | met |

Source: EPRS Briefing (24 April 2026) interim evaluation summary; Eurostat Education and Training Monitor 2024.

## Areas Needing Acceleration (Commission interim evaluation)

1. **Digital skills crisis** -- ICILS 2023 confirmed major underperformance in digital competence among 8th-graders. Action: accelerate Digital Education Action Plan + Digital Skills and Jobs Coalition
2. **Mental health in education** -- post-COVID generation showing rising mental health issues affecting learning outcomes. New Commission proposals: school mental health framework, peer support
3. **Skills-based learning approaches** -- Commission accelerating shift from credential-based to competence-based education + training (Skills First initiative)
4. **Mobility recovery** -- Erasmus+ outbound mobility numbers recovering but below pre-COVID baselines for some MS
5. **Equity gaps** -- socioeconomic background remains strongest predictor of educational outcomes (PISA 2022 confirmed)

## Major Sub-Policies and Initiatives

### European Universities Initiative
- 65 alliances of universities across the EU + associated countries
- Joint degrees, virtual + physical mobility, joint research
- Erasmus+ funded; 2025-2027 second wave
- EUR 1.1 billion budget envelope 2021-2027

### European Higher Education Area (EHEA / Bologna Process)
- 49 countries (intergovernmental, beyond EU)
- ECTS, Diploma Supplement, Quality Assurance (ENQA)
- Brexit complication: UK still in EHEA but outside EU programmes

### Vocational Education and Training (VET)
- Council Recommendation on VET (2020) + Osnabrück Declaration (2020)
- EQAVET quality framework
- VET excellence centres + apprenticeship support

### Digital Education Action Plan 2021-2027
- 13 actions across 2 priorities (high-performing digital education ecosystem + digital skills + competences)
- DigComp 2.2 (citizen digital competence framework)
- Connectivity4Schools
- AI in education ethical guidelines (2024)

### Skills + Lifelong Learning
- European Year of Skills (2023-2024) outcomes embedded
- Microcredentials Council Recommendation (2022)
- Individual Learning Accounts Council Recommendation (2022)
- Transitional pathway for proximity and social economy ecosystems

### Inclusion + Equity
- Council Recommendation on Pathways to School Success (2022)
- Council Recommendation on Roma equality, inclusion and participation in education (2021)
- Common values + civic learning + history teaching guidelines

### Green Education
- GreenComp (sustainability competence framework)
- Council Recommendation on Learning for the Green Transition (2022)
- Education for Climate Coalition

## Funding 2021-2027

| Instrument | Envelope | Education share |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Erasmus+ | EUR 26.2 billion | 100% |
| ESF+ | EUR 99 billion (CPR) | approximately 25% to skills + education |
| Horizon Europe | EUR 95.5 billion | research + Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions |
| Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) | EUR 723.8 billion (loans + grants) | approximately EUR 70 billion to education + skills (MS plans) |
| InvestEU | EUR 26.2 billion | Social Investment + Skills window |

## Funding 2028-2034 (Commission proposal MFF)

- Erasmus+ + European Solidarity Corps merged into single mobility programme
- Combined envelope: EUR 36.2 billion (+30% combined increase vs current)
- Located in Heading 2 (Competitiveness Fund) of MFF 2028-2034
- See `mff_2028_2034.md`

## EP Position

- CULT committee championing higher Erasmus+ ambition + protection from cuts
- Recurring resolutions on EEA strategic framework (2021, 2023, 2025)
- 2026 EP plenary debate on interim evaluation expected H2 2026

## Member State Variation

- **Top performers**: Finland, Estonia, Ireland, Netherlands, Denmark, Sweden -- meet most benchmarks
- **Mid performers**: Germany, France, Belgium, Austria, Czechia, Slovenia -- mixed
- **Catch-up MS**: Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary, Italy, Spain (regional variation), Greece, Portugal -- benchmark gaps largest
