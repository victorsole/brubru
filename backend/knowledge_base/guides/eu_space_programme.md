# EU Space Programme and Defence

## QUICK FACTS
- Full name: EU Space Programme
- Type: Regulation
- Legal reference: Regulation (EU) 2021/696
- Total budget: EUR 14.88 billion (MFF 2021-2027)
- Responsible DG: DG DEFIS
- Operational agency: EUSPA (Prague)
- Components: Galileo (GNSS, EUR 9.0B), Copernicus (Earth observation, EUR 5.4B), IRIS2 (secure connectivity, EUR 6.0B incl. private), SSA/SST (EUR 0.5B), GOVSATCOM (EUR 0.5B)
- Galileo constellation: 30 satellites in MEO (23,222 km), 5 services (Open, HAS, PRS, SAR, SoL)
- Copernicus satellites: Sentinel-1 through Sentinel-6, free/full/open data policy
- IRIS2 timeline: Initial services 2027, full operational capability 2029
- EU Space Act: Commission proposal expected 2025, EP lead committee ITRE
- European Defence Fund: Regulation (EU) 2021/697, EUR 7.95 billion (2021-2027)
- European launchers: Ariane 6 (operational July 2024), Vega-C (return to flight 2025)

Guide for DG DEFIS policy officers and space industry professionals covering the EU Space Programme components, EU Space Act, defence synergies, and EUSPA governance.

## EU Space Programme (Regulation (EU) 2021/696)

### Programme Components

| Component | Purpose | Operator | Budget 2021-2027 |
|-----------|---------|----------|------------------|
| **Galileo** | Global satellite navigation (GNSS) | EUSPA | EUR 9.0 billion |
| **EGNOS** | European Geostationary Navigation Overlay | EUSPA | Included in Galileo budget |
| **Copernicus** | Earth observation | ESA (space), EUMETSAT (marine/atmosphere) | EUR 5.4 billion |
| **IRIS2** | Secure connectivity (governmental satcom) | PPP consortium | EUR 6.0 billion (incl. private) |
| **SSA/SST** | Space Situational Awareness / Space Surveillance and Tracking | EU SST Consortium (national entities) | EUR 0.5 billion |
| **GOVSATCOM** | Governmental satellite communications | EUSPA | EUR 0.5 billion |

### Total Budget

EUR 14.88 billion from the MFF (2021-2027), making it one of the largest EU programmes.

## Galileo

### System Architecture

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Constellation** | 30 satellites (24 operational + 6 active spares) in MEO (23,222 km altitude) |
| **Ground segment** | 2 Galileo Control Centres (Oberpfaffenhofen, Fucino), global network of ground stations |
| **Services** | Open Service, Commercial Service, Public Regulated Service (PRS), Search and Rescue (SAR), Safety of Life |

### Services

| Service | Accuracy | Access | Users |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| **Open Service (OS)** | < 1 metre (with dual frequency) | Free, global | Smartphones, vehicle navigation, IoT |
| **High Accuracy Service (HAS)** | < 20 cm | Free, global (since 2023) | Precision agriculture, surveying, autonomous vehicles |
| **Public Regulated Service (PRS)** | Encrypted, robust | Authorised government users only | Military, police, critical infrastructure, customs |
| **Search and Rescue (SAR)** | Localisation to < 2 km | Global | Cospas-Sarsat maritime/aviation distress |
| **Safety of Life (SoL)** | Integrity monitoring | Certified | Civil aviation, maritime navigation |

### PRS Access

- National PRS Authorities designated by each Member State
- Competent PRS Authority (CPA) manages national PRS users
- Manufacture and licensing of PRS receivers strictly controlled
- Third-country access possible through international agreements

## Copernicus

### Sentinel Satellites

| Satellite | Mission | Data |
|-----------|---------|------|
| **Sentinel-1** | Radar imaging (SAR) | All-weather, day-night surface monitoring |
| **Sentinel-2** | Multi-spectral optical imaging | Land monitoring, agriculture, forestry, water quality |
| **Sentinel-3** | Ocean and land monitoring | Sea surface temperature, ocean colour, land topography |
| **Sentinel-4** | Atmospheric monitoring (geostationary) | Air quality over Europe (from Meteosat) |
| **Sentinel-5/5P** | Atmospheric composition | Ozone, NO2, aerosols, methane, CO |
| **Sentinel-6** | Radar altimetry (ocean) | Sea level, ocean surface wind and wave |

### Copernicus Services

| Service | Operator | Coverage |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Land Monitoring (CLMS)** | EEA | Land use/cover, vegetation, water bodies |
| **Marine Environment (CMEMS)** | Mercator Ocean | Ocean physics, biogeochemistry, sea ice |
| **Atmosphere Monitoring (CAMS)** | ECMWF | Air quality forecasts, greenhouse gases |
| **Emergency Management (CEMS)** | JRC | Rapid mapping for disasters, early warning |
| **Climate Change (C3S)** | ECMWF | Climate data store, reanalysis, indicators |
| **Security (CSS)** | Multiple | Border surveillance, maritime surveillance |

### Data Policy

- **Free, full, and open data policy** (Copernicus Regulation)
- Data distributed through Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE)
- API access for automated retrieval
- Commercial use permitted without restriction
- Sentinel data drives a downstream industry worth EUR 1.3 billion/year (estimated)

## IRIS2 (Infrastructure for Resilience, Interconnection and Security by Satellite)

### Overview

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Secure, resilient satellite connectivity for governments and underserved areas |
| **Constellation** | Multi-orbit: LEO + MEO satellites |
| **Services** | Governmental (encrypted, crisis communication) + commercial (broadband for underserved areas) |
| **Delivery model** | Public-private partnership (PPP) concession |
| **Consortium** | SpaceRISE (SES + Eutelsat + Airbus + Thales + others) |
| **Timeline** | Initial services by 2027; full operational capability by 2029 |
| **Budget** | EUR 6 billion (EUR 2.4 billion EU + EUR 3.6 billion private investment) |

### Strategic Rationale

- Digital sovereignty: reduce dependence on non-EU satellite constellations (Starlink, OneWeb)
- Crisis resilience: maintain connectivity when terrestrial infrastructure fails
- Coverage: rural and remote areas, maritime, Arctic
- Defence synergies: dual-use capacity for military and civilian needs

## SSA/SST (Space Situational Awareness)

### Space Surveillance and Tracking (SST)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Operators** | EU SST Consortium: France (CNES), Germany (DLR/BAAINBw), Spain (CDTI/INTA), Italy (ASI), Portugal (PT-MoD), Romania (ROSA), Poland (POLSA) |
| **Capabilities** | Track objects in orbit, predict collisions, monitor re-entries, detect fragmentation |
| **Database** | EU SST catalogue of objects in Earth orbit |
| **Services** | Collision avoidance alerts, re-entry predictions, fragmentation analysis |

### Space Weather (SWE)

- Monitoring solar activity and its effects on space and ground infrastructure
- Early warning for geomagnetic storms
- Services for satellite operators, aviation, power grid operators

### Near-Earth Objects (NEO)

- Monitoring potentially hazardous asteroids and comets
- International cooperation (ESA, NASA, IAWN)
- Planetary defence coordination

## EU Space Act (Proposed)

### Key Provisions

| Area | Proposed Rules |
|------|---------------|
| **Space traffic management** | Rules for active debris removal, in-orbit servicing, de-orbiting timelines |
| **Spectrum management** | Coordination of radio frequency usage for EU space assets |
| **Space debris mitigation** | Mandatory end-of-life disposal (25-year rule moving to 5-year recommendation) |
| **In-orbit services** | Regulatory framework for satellite servicing, refuelling, assembly |
| **Launch services** | European autonomous access to space strategy; preference for European launchers |
| **Space sustainability** | Environmental impact assessment for large constellations |
| **Security screening** | FDI screening for critical space technologies |

### Legislative Process Status

- Commission proposal expected 2025
- EP lead committee: ITRE
- Council: Competitiveness Council configuration
- Trilogue negotiations expected 2026

## Autonomous Access to Space

### European Launchers

| Launcher | Operator | Payload Capacity | Status |
|----------|----------|-------------------|--------|
| **Ariane 6** | ArianeGroup (ESA launcher programme) | 11.5t to LEO (A62); 21.6t to LEO (A64) | Operational (inaugural launch July 2024) |
| **Vega-C** | Avio | 2.3t to LEO | Return to flight 2025 (after Vega-C failure December 2022) |
| **Future (micro/mini)** | Multiple EU companies (Isar Aerospace, PLD Space, Rocket Factory Augsburg) | 0.3-1.5t to LEO | Development phase |

### Strategic Autonomy Rationale

- Guaranteed access to space for Galileo, Copernicus, IRIS2 satellite deployment
- Avoid dependence on non-EU launch providers for strategic assets
- Support European space industry competitiveness
- Preserve technological capabilities in launcher technology

## Quantum Space Gravimetry R&I

### Context

Quantum sensors for space gravimetry represent a next-generation technology for:
- Higher precision gravity field measurement
- Improved geodesy and sea-level monitoring
- Detection of subsurface resources and structures
- Climate science (ice mass balance, water cycle monitoring)

### EU R&I Activities

- Horizon Europe Cluster 4 calls on quantum technologies for space
- ESA NAVISP (Navigation Innovation and Support Programme) quantum navigation elements
- EIC Pathfinder projects on cold atom interferometry for space applications

## EU-NATO Space Cooperation

### Framework

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Basis** | EU-NATO Joint Declarations (2016, 2018, 2023) |
| **Space domains** | SSA data sharing, space weather, resilient PNT (Positioning, Navigation, Timing) |
| **Challenges** | Non-EU NATO Allies' access to EU space assets; EU strategic autonomy vs Allied interoperability |
| **Practical cooperation** | Staff-to-staff contacts, parallel and coordinated exercises, information sharing protocols |

## European Defence Fund (Regulation (EU) 2021/697)

### Space-Defence Synergies

| Area | EDF Contribution | Space Link |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| **Space-based ISR** | R&D on military Earth observation | Copernicus security service synergies |
| **Resilient PNT** | Military-grade PNT solutions | Galileo PRS (Public Regulated Service) |
| **Satellite communications** | Military satcom terminals and services | GOVSATCOM, IRIS2 governmental services |
| **Space domain awareness** | Military space surveillance capabilities | EU SST partnership |
| **Counter-space** | Protective measures for space assets | Not offensive; focus on resilience and hardening |

### EDF Budget

EUR 7.95 billion (2021-2027): EUR 2.65 billion for research, EUR 5.3 billion for development.

## EUSPA (EU Agency for the Space Programme)

### Role and Governance

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Mandate** | Operational management of Galileo, EGNOS, GOVSATCOM; Copernicus market uptake; IRIS2 procurement; security accreditation |
| **Location** | Prague (Czech Republic) |
| **Governance** | Administrative Board (Member States + Commission + EP observers); Executive Director |
| **Budget** | Directly managed by EUSPA under EU budget |
| **Staff** | ~350 (growing with IRIS2 and Copernicus responsibilities) |

### Key Functions

| Function | Activities |
|----------|-----------|
| **Galileo operations** | Satellite constellation management, ground segment operations |
| **Security accreditation** | Security Accreditation Board (SAB) for all EU space programme components |
| **Market development** | Downstream applications, GNSS/EO market reports, Horizon Europe synergies |
| **Innovation** | EU Space Technology and Innovation contests, start-up support, Cassini Matchmaking |

|-----------|-------------|

- ~1,000 space start-ups in Europe (2024)
- Key hubs: Paris, Munich, London (pre-Brexit hub, still relevant), Amsterdam, Madrid, Rome
- Focus areas: Earth observation analytics, IoT connectivity, in-orbit services, space debris monitoring, quantum communication
