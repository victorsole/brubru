## PESCO Military Mobility project

PESCO = Permanent Structured Cooperation, established by Council Decision (CFSP) 2017/2315 under Articles 42(6) + 46 TEU + Protocol No 10.

## CEF Military Mobility envelope (2021-2027)

Under **CEF Regulation 2021/1153** (CELEX 32021R1153), a ring-fenced envelope of **€1.69 billion** funds dual-use TEN-T infrastructure (rail upgrades, bridge load-bearing improvements, road carrying capacity, port + airport hardening for military use).

Calls administered by **CINEA**:
- **CEF Military Mobility call 2022** — €330 million awarded
- **CEF Military Mobility call 2024** — €807 million awarded
- **CEF Military Mobility call 2025** — under preparation per Action Plan 3.0

Eligible projects need to be on the **EU military requirements list** (Member State military authorities + EUMS) AND on the TEN-T network (or compatible). Spanish + Italian + French Mediterranean Corridor projects are explicitly named in MM 3.0 priorities.

## Action Plan 3.0 — what's new (Sept 2024)

The 2024 Action Plan introduces 4 priority workstreams:

1. **Infrastructure dual-use**: identification + funding of MOVE/DEFIS jointly-vetted projects (railways, ports, airports, roads, bridges)
2. **Regulatory framework**: harmonising customs + cross-border movement procedures + dangerous goods handling
3. **Capabilities + interoperability**: pre-positioning + Mutual Assistance Mechanism (mutual support between Member States during crisis-driven movements)
4. **Resilience + supply security**: protecting critical logistics infrastructure (ports, hubs) under hybrid threat scenarios

## Why this matters for rail freight stakeholders

- **Dual-use rail upgrades** are eligible for CEF Military Mobility (€1.69B ring-fenced) AND CEF Transport General Envelope (€25.81B) — projects that qualify for both can stack co-financing
- **Loading gauge + axle load harmonisation** drives the same standards needed for civilian rail freight
- **Cross-border interoperability** (ERTMS, customs, dangerous goods) benefits civil + military
- **Critical infrastructure protection** rules under the Critical Entities Resilience Directive 2022/2557 also apply to rail nodes designated as essential
