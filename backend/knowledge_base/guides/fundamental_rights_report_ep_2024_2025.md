# EP Fundamental Rights Report 2024-2025 (Strolenberg INI)

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EP annual **own-initiative report on the situation of fundamental rights in the EU in 2024-2025**, drafted under Rule 54 of the EP Rules of Procedure, LIBE lead committee
- **Rapporteur: Anna Strolenberg** (Greens/EFA, Netherlands)
- EP plenary debate: Item 26 on OJQ-10-2026-04-28_EN agenda, Tuesday 28 April 2026, 15:00-22:00 debate block, Strasbourg
- Type: EP internal mechanism (separate from the Commission's annual Rule of Law Report -- see `commission_rule_of_law_report_2025`) -- the EP's own flagship self-assessment on where fundamental rights stand inside the Union
- Scope: 2024-2025 biennial assessment (the EP shifted from annual to biennial cadence from 2021 onwards to allow deeper thematic focus)
- Legal basis: Article 2 TEU (Union values), Article 6 TEU (Charter of Fundamental Rights), Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union (2000/C 364/01, binding since Lisbon December 2009)
- Key external inputs: EU FRA (Fundamental Rights Agency, Vienna) Annual Fundamental Rights Report; Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights reports; ECtHR case law; UN Special Rapporteur findings
- Previous editions: 2022-2023 (rapporteur Katarina Barley, S&D, adopted March 2024); 2020-2021 (rapporteur Juan Fernando López Aguilar, S&D); 2018-2019 (rapporteur Clare Daly, The Left)

## Likely themes (pre-adoption, based on Strolenberg's draft and LIBE discussions)

As a Greens/EFA rapporteur, Strolenberg's 2024-2025 report is expected to emphasise:

1. **Rule of law backsliding** in Hungary (pre-Magyar era), Slovakia, Italy, partially Poland pre-2023
2. **Media freedom**: Spyware (Predator, Pegasus), SLAPP implementation gaps (Directive (EU) 2024/1069), European Media Freedom Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1083) enforcement
3. **Racism, xenophobia, Roma discrimination**: rise of Alternative für Deutschland (AfD) and Patriots for Europe group dynamics; implementation of EU Roma strategic framework
4. **Migration and asylum**: implementation of the Pact on Migration and Asylum (adopted May 2024, applicable June 2026), pushback allegations at external borders (Frontex accountability)
5. **LGBTIQ rights**: Hungary's 2021 anti-LGBTIQ law, Italy's surrogacy restrictions, Slovakia's partnerships debate
6. **Women's rights**: Istanbul Convention full EU accession (completed 28 June 2023), consent-based rape definition debate (see `consent_based_rape_definition`)
7. **Children's rights**: CSAM Regulation (ongoing trilogue -- see `csam_regulation_online`), migrant and refugee children, Ukrainian forcibly deported children (see `ukraine_children_deportation`)
8. **Disability rights**: European Disability Card and Parking Card Directive (Directive (EU) 2024/2841), Disability Strategy 2021-2030 mid-term review
9. **AI and fundamental rights**: AI Act implementation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), prohibited practices entry into force February 2025, high-risk rules delay debate (see `ai_act_amendments_2026`)
10. **Civic space and freedom of assembly**: civil society organisations' ability to operate, funding restrictions, anti-protest laws
