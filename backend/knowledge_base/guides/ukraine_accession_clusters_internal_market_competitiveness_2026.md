# Ukraine opens the internal market and competitiveness clusters of its EU accession talks (13 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** at the accession conference of **13 October 2026** (Luxembourg, Irish presidency), the EU and **Ukraine opened negotiations on two clusters: "internal market" (cluster 2) and "competitiveness and inclusive growth" (cluster 3)**. Read in the release republished by the EU Delegation to Ukraine (EEAS, dated 14 October 2026); the Council's own page was behind a bot check.
- **Cluster 2, internal market: 9 chapters:** free movement of goods (1); freedom of movement for workers (2); right of establishment and freedom to provide services (3); free movement of capital (4); company law (6); intellectual property law (7); competition policy (8); financial services (9); consumer and health protection (28).
- **Cluster 3, competitiveness and inclusive growth: 8 chapters:** digital transformation and media (10); taxation (16); economic and monetary policy (17); social policy and employment (19); enterprise and industrial policy (20); science and research (25); education and culture (26); customs union (29).
- **Benchmarks:** the EU "has also set benchmarks for the future provisional closure of those chapters". Opening is NOT closing: no chapter of Ukraine's was provisionally closed on 13 October.
- **Sequence for Ukraine (this release):** the **external relations** cluster was opened in **July 2026** (third accession conference, 14 July 2026); accession negotiations were opened on **25 June 2024**. The release does not state which other clusters are open; give no total.
- **Quote:** Thomas Byrne, Ireland's Minister of State for European affairs and defence: "We have today reached another milestone on Ukraine's EU membership journey."
- **The method:** since the 2020 revised methodology, the **33 chapters** are grouped in **6 clusters**: Fundamentals; Internal market; Competitiveness and inclusive growth; Green agenda and sustainable connectivity; Resources, agriculture and cohesion; External relations. Accession is merit-based and needs unanimity.
- **Moldova** opened the same two clusters the same day (Council headline); the Moldova release itself was NOT read, so give no Moldova detail beyond that.

## How to answer
"Which accession clusters did Ukraine open in October 2026?": on 13 October 2026 Ukraine opened cluster 2 (internal market, 9 chapters) and cluster 3 (competitiveness and inclusive growth, 8 chapters), after opening external relations in July 2026. Benchmarks for closing them were set; nothing was closed.
