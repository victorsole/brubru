# Beneficial ownership information: the EU data formats (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2354)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2354 of 16 October 2026 on formats for submitting beneficial ownership information. CELEX 32026R2354, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/2354/oj. Published in the Official Journal L on 22 October 2026.
- **Legal basis:** the sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directive, Directive (EU) 2024/1640 (AMLD6, CELEX 32024L1640), which makes Member States run central beneficial ownership registers; the data items it formats are those of the Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (AMLR), Articles 57 (legal entities) and 58 (legal arrangements).
- **Dates (Article 6):** enters into force on the twentieth day after publication (11 November 2026); **applies from 10 July 2027**, the same day as the AMLR and the AMLD6 transposition deadline. NOT in application today.
- **Who it binds:** the entities in charge of the national central registers (what format they must accept and display) and, through them, every legal entity, trust or similar arrangement filing its beneficial owners. It does NOT change who counts as a beneficial owner or the 25% threshold.
- **Formats for the person (Article 1(1)):** first names and surnames in full; place and date of birth (Annex II date format); residential address (Annex III); country of residence, nationalities (multiple, with a "stateless" option) and country issuing the identity document as ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 codes; type and number of identity document. A deceased beneficial owner may be marked as such instead (Article 1(2)).
- **Formats for the interest (Article 1(3)):** type of ownership (direct, indirect); role for legal arrangements (settlor, trustee, beneficiary, object of a power, default taker, protector, other ultimate controller) and for legal entities (founder, management body in management or supervisory function, beneficiary, other controller); percentage of ownership with two decimals; nature of control (nine options, from voting rights and appointment rights to nominee arrangements and acting in concert); date from which the interest is held.
- **Collective investment undertakings (Article 1(4)):** percentage of units held, ability to influence the investment policy and other control, as text or a file upload.
- **Reuse (Article 1(5)):** where the data already sits in another register, the central register must reuse it or request it in the established format.
- **Not read:** Articles 2 to 5 and Annexes I to III in detail (date and address conventions, the case where no beneficial owner can be identified, the senior managing officials fallback). Read them before quoting a field not listed here.
- **Why it matters:** it is the first technical act under the 2024 AML package to reach the OJ; registers, company secretaries and compliance software must align their data models by 10 July 2027.
- **Related guides:** `eu_anti_money_laundering` (the package), `amla_regulatory_instruments_pipeline` (AMLA's RTS and ITS).
