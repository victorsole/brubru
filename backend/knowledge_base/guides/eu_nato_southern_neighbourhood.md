## Key Cooperation Instruments

1. **EU-NATO Joint Declarations (2016, 2018, 2023)**: strategic framework; 74 concrete actions including 3 new priority areas added in 2023 (climate change, emerging tech, hybrid)
2. **Staff-to-staff cooperation**: regular coordination EEAS-NATO International Staff; PSC chair + North Atlantic Council chair informal meetings
3. **Hybrid CoE + EU Hybrid Fusion Cell**: threat analysis; memoranda of cooperation
4. **MARSUR + NATO Maritime Command**: Mediterranean situational awareness
5. **Strategic Compass implementation**: Rapid Deployment Capacity (5,000 personnel), cyber defence, space
6. **EDIP / SAFE**: potential interoperability gains for NATO standards

## Hybrid Threats in the South

Per EPRS Briefing, specific southern-flank hybrid threats include:

- **Russia in Sahel**: Africa Corps (successor to Wagner) in Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger; destabilisation of EU/French footprint
- **Russia in Libya**: LNA/Haftar alignment, Tartus alternative port
- **China's infrastructure diplomacy**: Suez, Algiers, Tangier Med ports; Huawei networks
- **Iran proxies**: Hezbollah, Houthis (Red Sea/Suez shipping disruption post-Hamas war)
- **Migration instrumentalisation**: Belarus precedent; Sahel routes; Niger/Libya departure points
- **Disinformation**: anti-French/anti-EU narratives across Francophone Africa
- **Energy**: Turkish EEZ disputes, Egyptian offshore gas, pipeline routes

## Red Sea / Suez Shipping Impact

The Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping (since November 2023) + Iran-Israel crisis (April-June 2025) have:
- Rerouted 50-60% of Asia-Europe shipping around Cape of Good Hope (+10-14 days, +30% fuel cost)
- Exposed dependence on Suez; Commission emergency plan (April 2026)
- Triggered EUNAVFOR Aspides (Red Sea operation, February 2024)
- NATO Operation Sea Guardian enhanced patrols
