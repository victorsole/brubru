# European Radioisotope Valley Initiative (ERVI) — medical radioisotope supply

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** a Commission **Communication**, not a Regulation. **C/2026/6132**, adopted
  **10 September 2026**, led by **DG Energy**. A strategic framework, so it creates **no binding
  obligation on any company** — that distinction matters when advising a client.
- **Name:** European Radioisotope Valley Initiative, **ERVI**.
- **Structure: five strategic priorities and 10 actions.**
- **The problem it addresses:** more than **10 million medical procedures a year** in the EU use
  radioisotopes, mainly in **oncology and cardiology**. The EU is the **world's leading producer** and
  exports about **half** of its output, yet critical parts of the chain depend on foreign suppliers,
  the production facilities are ageing, and alternative suppliers are competing investment away.
- **The geopolitical point:** it targets dependency on **Russia**, and is a direct contribution to the
  **REPowerEU Roadmap** on ending Russian energy imports.

### The five priorities, in the Communication's own order
1. **Research and development, and bringing innovation to market.** A **Strategic Research and
   Innovation Agenda (SRIA)** to connect research to market and patient needs, drawing on the emerging
   **IPCEI for innovative nuclear technologies**, the **European Innovation Council** and new European
   partnerships.
2. **Investment in production facilities.** Build **EU-based metallic HALEU** (High Assay Low Enriched
   Uranium) capacity and **produce stable isotopes currently imported from Russia**; upgrade existing
   reactors and build new facilities. Member States are pointed at the **EU Startup and Scaleup
   Strategy**, the **SGEI State aid framework** and the **Critical Medicines Act** (political
   agreement reached in 2026).
3. **Market monitoring and forecasting**, building on the **European Observatory on the Supply of
   Medical Radioisotopes** and the EU mechanisms for tackling shortages of essential medicines.
4. **Strategic international partnerships**, with the **UK and US** named specifically as crucial for
   a resilient metallic **HALEU** chain.
5. **An evolving regulatory framework**: cooperation with the **IAEA** on reliable international
   transport, measures to ease transport between Member States, and streamlining EU and national
   procedures for **radiation protection and waste management** in nuclear medicine.

### How it got here
- **June 2024:** the **Council** called on the Commission to maintain Europe's global leadership in
  medical radioisotope supply and make swift progress on concrete building blocks.
- **2021: SAMIRA**, the Strategic Agenda for Medical Ionising Radiation Applications, which ERVI
  builds on.
- **May 2025:** the **REPowerEU roadmap** identified establishing ERVI as one of its key actions.
- **March 2026:** the Commission published an **ERVI feasibility study** after consulting Member
  States and stakeholders.

### What to tell a user
ERVI is an investment-and-coordination framework. For a company in the nuclear-medicine chain the
actionable parts are the **funding routes** (IPCEI, EIC, European partnerships, SGEI State aid) and
the **HALEU and stable-isotope localisation push**, not a compliance deadline. For a hospital or
health system the relevant thread is **security of supply** and the shortages mechanisms.
