## Overview

The Citizens Energy Package addresses energy affordability, consumer protection, and energy poverty. While the Clean Energy Investment Strategy tackles the supply side (grids, generation, finance) and the SMR Strategy focuses on nuclear technology, this package is consumer-facing -- it aims to lower household energy bills, empower prosumers, protect vulnerable consumers, and ensure proper implementation of existing EU energy laws.

Treaty basis: Article 194 TFEU (energy policy); Article 169 TFEU (consumer protection).

## 4 Pillars and 9 Actions

### PILLAR I: Lowering Energy Bills for Households

**Action 1: Lower electricity taxes and levies**
- Reduce to EU minimum levels
- Result: household bills lowered by ~14%, saving average EUR 200/year per household
- Requires Member State action on national taxation

**Action 2: Lower network costs for local consumers**
- Network tariffs represent 25-33% of electricity prices
- Commission guidance on network tariffs: C(2025) 4010 final (published 2025)
- Targeted incentives for energy communities and energy sharing schemes
- Reward local generation that reduces grid stress

**Action 3: Boost clean and energy-efficient technologies**
- European Energy Efficiency Financing Coalition
- Commission Recommendation COM(2026) 1526 on private investment in energy efficiency
- Focus on heating (62.5% of household energy) and water heating (15.1%)
- One-stop shops for energy efficiency services (Recommendation 2026/536)

### PILLAR II: Protecting and Empowering Consumers

**Action 4: Faster supplier switching**
- Technical rules for 24-hour switching (Q1 2026)
- Standardised contract summaries recommendation (Q1 2026)
- Standardised bill templates (2027)
- Citizens lose EUR 152/year by not switching -- market transparency is key

**Action 5: Energy self-production and sharing**
- 8,000+ energy communities already active in Europe
- By 2030 projections: 16 million households + 630,000 SMEs could produce own renewable energy
- Tenfold increase in community renewable capacity expected
- Savings: EUR 260-550/year per household; EUR 440-930/year per energy community member
- Community energy could reach 90 GW = 8% of total renewable generation by 2030
- Energy communities action plan forthcoming
- Data interoperability regulation (2027)

**Action 6: Flexible retail contracts**
- Demand flexibility could lower electricity bills by up to 40%
- Potential annual savings: EUR 2.7 billion by 2030
- Guidance on remuneration of flexibility (Q1 2026)
- Implementing Regulation on interoperability (2027)
- Partnerships for Better Homes programme (2026)

**Action 7: Consumer trust**
- New code of service for energy suppliers (Q4 2026)
- Guidance on risk management for suppliers (Q1 2026)
- Post-2029 EU consumer energy rights legal proposal (2028) -- this signals future legislation

### PILLAR III: Tackling Energy Poverty and Vulnerability

**Action 8: Reduce energy poverty**
- 42 million Europeans (9.2%) could not adequately heat homes in 2024
- Social Climate Fund: up to EUR 86.7 billion (2026-2032)
- National building renovation plans due by end 2026
- New LIFE programme call for energy poverty projects
- Updated Recommendation on energy poverty (Q4 2026)
- Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EU) 2024/1275 (CELEX 32024L1275)

**Action 9: Protect from disconnections**
- 31 million Europeans late on utility bills in 2024
- Early warning systems for consumers at risk
- Disconnection safeguards guidelines (Q2-Q3 2026)

### PILLAR IV: Implementing Existing EU Laws

This pillar focuses on proper enforcement and guidance, not new legislation:

- Guidance on energy communities, self-consumption, vulnerable consumer protection
- Disconnection prevention guidelines
- Contract summaries, supplier risk management
- Market-based retail prices, flexibility remuneration
- Supplier switching technical rules
- Citizens Energy Forum upgraded as focal point
- Citizens Energy Advisory Hub, Energy Poverty Advisory Hub
- Covenant of Mayors, EU Islands Initiative, Coal Regions in Transition Initiative

## OJ Recommendations (10 March 2026)

### Recommendation (EU) 2026/536 (CELEX 32026H0536)
**One-stop shops for energy efficiency and buildings**
- Practical guidance for Member States on establishing one-stop shops
- Implements EED recast (Directive (EU) 2023/1791) Article 30
- Helps citizens access energy efficiency services and building renovation support

### Recommendation (EU) 2026/537 (CELEX 32026H0537)
**Unlocking private investment in energy efficiency**
- Investment need: EUR 370 billion/year for 2021-2030
- Investment gap: EUR 170 billion/year
- EU budget covers only 14.4% of the gap
- Key role for ESCOs, energy communities, utilities, DSOs, district heating/cooling, real estate, net-zero tech providers
- EU budgetary resources for energy efficiency increased 491% in 2021-2027 MFF (from EUR 26.5 to EUR 156.6 billion, mostly RRF)

## Connection to Other Energy Package Guides

- **Clean Energy Investment Strategy** (COM(2026) 116): The supply-side counterpart. Action 3d of that strategy funds energy efficiency for households/SMEs with EUR 500 million pilot. This package focuses on the demand side: lower bills, consumer rights, energy communities. See `clean_energy_investment_strategy.md`
- **SMR Strategy** (COM(2026) 117): SMRs contribute to energy affordability by providing stable baseload generation and reducing fossil fuel dependence, which in turn moderates wholesale electricity prices. See `smr_strategy_nuclear.md`
- **EU Energy Policy** (umbrella): This package builds on the Electricity Market Design reform (2024), REPowerEU emergency measures, and Fit for 55 consumer protection provisions. See `eu_energy_policy.md`
