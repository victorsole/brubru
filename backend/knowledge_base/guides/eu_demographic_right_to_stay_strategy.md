## What is the Right to Stay Strategy

A new EU policy frame asserting that EU citizens should not have to leave their region, town or village due to lack of services, jobs, housing or opportunities. It crystallises a long-running strand in EU regional policy -- "places matter" -- into an explicit cross-DG action programme. The strategy is non-legislative; it sets a direction that downstream Country-Specific Recommendations, Cohesion Policy partnership agreements, and the post-2027 MFF reflect.

## Why now (May 2026)

- **Demographic urgency**: many EU regions face shrinking working-age populations, ageing, brain-drain to capital cities, declining service availability (schools, GPs, post offices)
- **Cohesion Policy reform**: The MFF 2028-2034 negotiations are live (EP voted negotiating mandate 29 April 2026, see `mff_2028_2034`); the Right to Stay Strategy positions Cohesion Policy as a core lever for territorial inclusion in the new programming period
- **Political signal**: Fitto's portfolio -- Cohesion + Reforms + Implementation -- combines the territorial lens with the Recovery and Resilience Facility track. The strategy fits both
- **Companion to housing + anti-poverty agenda**: the same week (College adopted Social Package on 6 May 2026), the Right to Stay launch ties services + jobs + housing into one "place-based" programmatic answer

## What's in the strategy

The DG REGIO press release frames the strategy around five pillars (text framed by DG REGIO; legislative + funding instruments TBD):

1. **Local services** -- maintain and modernise schools, healthcare, transport, post offices, broadband connectivity in disadvantaged regions
2. **Jobs and skills** -- Talent Booster Mechanism scale-up; reskilling for green/digital transitions in regions with industrial decline
3. **Housing** -- direct cross-link to the European Affordable Housing Plan (Pillar 4 -- protecting the most affected, see `european_affordable_housing_plan`)
4. **Innovation and entrepreneurship** -- support for SMEs, smart-village programmes, EU Mission for Climate-Neutral and Smart Cities adapted for smaller centres
5. **Civic and cultural participation** -- New European Bauhaus, EU Mission Adaptation to Climate Change, citizen-led action

## Bilbao Cities Forum 2027

In a paired DG REGIO announcement (6-7 May 2026), **Bilbao** (Basque Country, Spain) was selected to host the **Cities Forum 2027** -- the EU's flagship gathering of urban-policy stakeholders. Cities Forum is the urban-policy delivery vehicle that complements the Right to Stay Strategy's place-based logic.
