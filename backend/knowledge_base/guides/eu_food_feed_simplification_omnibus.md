# EU Food and Feed Simplification Omnibus ("Omnibus X")

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Commission omnibus proposal simplifying EU food and feed legislation -- part of the wider 2026 simplification agenda
- Common names: "Omnibus X", "Omnibus IX" (numbering fluctuates in early 2026 discourse), "Food and Feed Simplification Omnibus", "Agri-food Omnibus"
- Commission proposal: COM(2025) series, announced late 2025; expected formal proposal early-to-mid 2026
- Commission simplification target: Cut reporting burdens for food and feed operators by at least 25% (35% for SMEs) by end of 2027, in line with Competitiveness Compass COM(2025) 30
- Lead DGs: DG SANTE (Health and Food Safety) + DG AGRI (Agriculture and Rural Development)
- Commissioners: Oliver Varhelyi (Health and Animal Welfare) + Christophe Hansen (Agriculture and Food)
- Likely EP lead committee: ENVI (Environment, Climate, Food Safety) with opinions from AGRI and IMCO -- **not yet assigned as of mid-April 2026**
- Council configuration: AGRIFISH (Agriculture and Fisheries)
- Procedure status (as of 17 April 2026): Commission preparing proposal; no OEIL reference yet; EP committee assignment and rapporteur not yet designated
- Related guides: `eu_food_safety_pesticides`, `common_agricultural_policy`, `bioeconomy_food_systems`, `novel_food_insects`

## Scope of Simplification

The food and feed omnibus targets the **acquis on food and feed law**, estimated at 200+ acts. The simplification exercise reviews:

### Horizontal food law
- **General Food Law Regulation** (Reg (EC) 178/2002, CELEX 32002R0178) -- principles, EFSA, RASFF
- **Official Controls Regulation** (Reg (EU) 2017/625, CELEX 32017R0625) -- official controls across the food chain
- **Food Information to Consumers Regulation** (Reg (EU) 1169/2011, CELEX 32011R1169) -- labelling

### Feed law
- **Feed Hygiene Regulation** (Reg (EC) 183/2005, CELEX 32005R0183)
- **Feed Additives Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1831/2003, CELEX 32003R1831)
- **Animal By-Products Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1069/2009, CELEX 32009R1069)
- **Undesirable Substances in Feed Directive** (Dir 2002/32/EC, CELEX 32002L0032)

### Product-specific
- **Pesticides MRL Regulation** (Reg (EC) 396/2005, CELEX 32005R0396)
- **Plant Protection Products Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1107/2009, CELEX 32009R1107)
- **Genetically Modified Food and Feed Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1829/2003, CELEX 32003R1829)
- **Novel Food Regulation** (Reg (EU) 2015/2283, CELEX 32015R2283)
- **Food Additives Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1333/2008, CELEX 32008R1333)
- **Food Enzymes Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1332/2008, CELEX 32008R1332)
- **Food Flavourings Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1334/2008, CELEX 32008R1334)
- **Food Contact Materials Regulation** (Reg (EC) 1935/2004, CELEX 32004R1935)

### Quality schemes
- **Geographical Indications** (Reg (EU) 2024/1143, CELEX 32024R1143)
- **Quality Schemes** (Reg (EU) 1151/2012)

## Policy Drivers

### Competitiveness Compass (29 January 2025, COM(2025) 30)
- Flagship simplification agenda
- 25% burden reduction target; 35% for SMEs
- Food and feed omnibus is one of ~7 sectoral omnibus packages planned (automotive, sustainability/Omnibus I, AI-data, fisheries, chemicals, etc.)

### Letta Report (April 2024) + Draghi Report (September 2024)
- Both identified food chain regulatory complexity as competitiveness drag
- Small food producers particularly burdened by compliance costs

### Farm-to-Fork Strategy rollback concerns
- Christophe Hansen portfolio (Agriculture and Food) signals reduced ambition on environmental requirements
- Industry welcomes simplification; NGOs (BirdLife, BEUC, European Environmental Bureau) warn against deregulation under simplification banner

### RASFF modernisation
- Rapid Alert System for Food and Feed (RASFF) digital modernisation built into omnibus
- Reduced double-reporting; integration with Digital Operational Single Market

## Likely Elements

Based on Commission signalling and the Competitiveness Compass:

1. **Consolidated digital reporting portal** for food and feed operators
2. **Proportionality for SMEs**: simplified documentation, risk-based controls
3. **Mutual recognition of national approvals** within the EU single market
4. **Sunset / review clauses** for secondary legislation
5. **Delegated-act consolidation**: reduce proliferation of implementing acts
6. **Digital labelling**: e-labels for foodstuffs (QR-code based supplementary labelling)
7. **Fast-track procedures**: for novel foods, feed additives, GMO authorisations
8. **Streamlined MRL procedures**: align with EFSA and Codex Alimentarius timelines
9. **GMO authorisation reform**: simplified New Genomic Techniques (NGT) pathway (separate proposal 2023/0411(COD) under trilogue -- may be folded in)

## Political Risk

- **Left-coded critique**: simplification seen as cover for deregulation; environmental NGOs + Socialist/Green MEPs opposed
- **Right-coded critique**: EP People's Party wants more aggressive burden reduction; FPE/ECR + some EPP critique "EU over-regulation"
- **Centre-ground**: Commission tries to balance both; rapporteur designation in ENVI will reveal political complexion
