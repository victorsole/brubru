# EU sanctions listings of 16 November 2026 (Foreign Affairs Council): five regimes, who was added

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **Monday 16 November 2026** the Foreign Affairs Council added names to **five different EU sanctions regimes**. All ten acts were published in the Official Journal the same day and **entered into force on 16 November 2026** (date of publication). These are LISTINGS under existing regimes. This is NOT a new numbered "sanctions package" against Russia: the Commission President said on 13 November that a 22nd package would be proposed "soon"; NOT proposed as of 16 November 2026.
- **1. Ukraine territorial integrity regime: 12 individuals and 101 entities (113 in all).** Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2574 (CELEX 32026R2574, http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/2574/oj) and Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2575 (CELEX 32026D2575). The Council's press release: nearly 50 Russian banks and financial institutions; the sole shareholder of the retailer Wildberries and the person controlling the majority of Ozon (e-commerce); oil and energy companies (refineries, the St Petersburg oil terminal); shadow fleet: companies from the United Arab Emirates, China, Seychelles and Oman buying Russian crude oil products, and owners or managers of sanctioned vessels.
- **2. Russia's destabilising activities regime (hybrid threats, information manipulation): 30 natural persons and 1 entity.** Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2558 (CELEX 32026R2558) and Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2556 (CELEX 32026D2556). The entity is Social Design Agency LLC, a Moscow public relations company carrying out information manipulation. The 30 + 1 belongs to THIS regime, never to the Ukraine regime's 12 + 101.
- **3. Cyber-attacks regime: exactly one entity, NoName057(16)**, a pro-Russian hacker group active since March 2022 that runs denial-of-service attacks with the DDoSia toolkit. Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2550 (CELEX 32026R2550) and Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2546 (CELEX 32026D2546). The Council's headline "30 individuals and two entities" adds regimes 2 and 3 together.
- **4. Hamas and Palestinian Islamic Jihad regime: 1 person and 1 entity.** Mustafa Moin Ayash and the social media outlet he founded, Gaza Now, listed for soliciting donations for Hamas. Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2568 (CELEX 32026R2568) and Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2570 (CELEX 32026D2570). The Decision also lets Member States exempt a listed person from the travel ban for a judicial process or to serve a prison sentence.
- **5. EU Global Human Rights Sanctions Regime: exactly four entities, all Haitian armed criminal groups:** 5 Segond, 400 Mawozo, Gran Ravine and Krazé Baryè, for killings, sexual violence and grave violations against children. Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2569 (CELEX 32026R2569) and Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2571 (CELEX 32026D2571).
- **Who must act:** every person and company in the EU. Funds of listed persons are frozen at once; banks and traders should screen the new names against the consolidated list.
- **What was NOT read:** the annexes were read for counts and for the names given above, NOT name by name; of the Council's five press releases only the one on the 113 listings was read in full (the others: headline and lead). The 16 November Council also approved a payment of just under EUR 8 billion to Ukraine under the Ukraine Facility: see the Ukraine Support Loan guide.
