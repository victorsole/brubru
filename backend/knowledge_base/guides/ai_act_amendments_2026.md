# AI Act Amendments 2026 (Nudification Ban + High-Risk Delay)

## QUICK FACTS
- Base regulation: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), CELEX 32024R1689
- Amendment proposal: COM(2026) series, early 2026
- Procedure: ordinary legislative procedure
- EP lead committees: IMCO + LIBE
- Key changes: (1) Ban on AI nudification systems, (2) Delay of high-risk AI provisions, (3) SME/SMC exemptions extended
- Council position: agreed 13 March 2026 -- added nudification ban, fixed timelines for high-risk rules
- EP plenary vote: 26 March 2026 (Brussels mini-plenary) -- 1st reading position adopted
- High-risk standalone AI systems: new application date 2 December 2027
- High-risk AI in products: new application date 2 August 2028
- AI regulatory sandboxes: deadline extended to 2 December 2027
- Commissioner: Henna Virkkunen (EVP, Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy)
- AI Office: reinforced supervisory powers for AI systems based on general-purpose AI models
- JRC report (16 April 2026): "Fractured reality: how algorithms fuel polarisation and affect democracy" -- JRC Publications Office study on algorithmic amplification, filter bubbles, echo chambers, platform design and democratic impact. Relevant to AI Act Article 10 (risk management), DSA Article 34 (systemic risk assessment), DSA Article 40 (researcher data access). See also `dsa_enforcement` and `digital_markets_act` guides.

## Overview

In early 2026, the Commission proposed targeted amendments to the AI Act to address implementation challenges and new AI risks. The two main pillars are:

1. **Nudification ban**: Adding the generation of non-consensual sexual and intimate content (AI nudification/deepfake pornography) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the list of prohibited AI practices under Article 5.

2. **High-risk timeline adjustment**: Delaying the application of rules on high-risk AI systems by up to 16 months to allow standards and conformity assessment tools to be ready.

## Council Position (13 March 2026)

The Council broadly maintained the Commission proposal but added:
- Explicit prohibition of AI nudification and CSAM generation (new Article 5 entry)
- Fixed timeline: 2 December 2027 for standalone high-risk AI systems, 2 August 2028 for product-embedded high-risk AI
- Extension of AI regulatory sandbox deadline to 2 December 2027
- Clarification of AI Office competences vs national authorities (law enforcement, border management, financial institutions remain under national supervision)

## EP Plenary Vote (26 March 2026)

MEPs adopted their 1st reading position, including the nudification ban and high-risk delay. Trilogue negotiations with Council expected to be swift given broad alignment.

## DSA Enforcement Context (26 March 2026)

On the same day, the Commission announced:
- Preliminary findings that Pornhub, Stripchat, XNXX, XVideos breach the DSA by allowing minors access
- Investigation into Snapchat's compliance with child protection rules under the DSA

These DSA enforcement actions complement the AI Act nudification ban in the broader EU child safety framework.
