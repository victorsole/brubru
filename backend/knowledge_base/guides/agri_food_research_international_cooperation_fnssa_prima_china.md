# International cooperation in agri-food research: EU-AU FNSSA, PRIMA, EU-China (SWD(2026) 271)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Reach:** partners from third countries in **34%** of Horizon 2020 and **19%** of Horizon Europe projects on agriculture, forestry and rural areas (2014-2025).
- **EU-African Union FNSSA** (Food and Nutrition Security and Sustainable Agriculture partnership): launched **2017**, part of the **AU-EU High-Level Policy Dialogue on Science, Technology and Innovation**; sustainable intensification, nutrition-sensitive food systems, agricultural markets and trade; **over EUR 125.5 million for 20 collaborative projects**. The **International Research Consortium (IRC-FNSSA)**, governed by statutes of **2025**, makes it a long-term joint research governance mechanism; a **FNSSA Roadmap 2027-2036** is forthcoming. Linked: AU Research Grant programme, **DeSIRA**, **Global Gateway**; projects CEA-FIRST (2023-2027, 21 partners), CANALLS (2023-2026, eight agroecology living labs, more than 20,000 farmers), PrAEctiCe (2022-2026), AfriFOODlinks, INCiTiS Food, HealthyFoodAfrica (2020-2025, ten African cities).
- **PRIMA** (Partnership for Research and Innovation in the Mediterranean Area): **2018-2028**, public-public; funded by Horizon 2020 (**EUR 220 million**), Horizon Europe (**EUR 105 million**) and **20 participating countries** (about **EUR 274 million**); water, sustainable agriculture and food production, with the water-energy-food-ecosystems nexus; 2018-2025: **305 projects**, **2,900 beneficiaries** (69 water, 129 farming systems, 95 agri-food value chains, 12 nexus).
- **EU-China Flagship on Food, Agriculture and Biosolutions:** a bilateral cooperation line named in the SWD.
- **African swine fever:** projects ASFaVIP (live attenuated vaccines in wild boar and domestic pigs) and VAX4ASF (next-generation vaccine platform with a DIVA strategy to distinguish infected from vaccinated animals).
- **Eastern Europe:** a 2023 analysis of urban food-system projects found 25 Member States represented and **lacking Eastern European participation**; the Communication names **BIOEAST** cooperation with Ukraine, Moldova and the Western Balkans.
- **In the strategy:** the shared priority **global cooperation** (COM(2026) 453, section 3.1) names the AU-EU roadmap, India, the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States, BIOEAST, PRIMA, FAO, WHO, WOAH, CGIAR and the OECD.
