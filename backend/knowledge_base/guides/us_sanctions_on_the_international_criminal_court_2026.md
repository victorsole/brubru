# US sanctions on the International Criminal Court and the EU response (9-10 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **22 October 2026: Parliament awarded the 2026 Sakharov Prize for Freedom of Thought to the ICC and its officials** (announced by President Metsola in plenary). Detail in `sakharov_prize_2026_icc`.
- **What was announced:** on **9 October 2026** the United States announced its intention to impose sanctions on the **International Criminal Court (ICC) as an institution**. The Commission says there is a **six-month delay before the measures are applicable**, which leaves "room for dialogue"; the High Representative "takes note" of the US decision to delay the entry into force and gives no period.
- **EU responses (the Commission and High Representative statements were read in full; Parliament's is cited by headline):**
  - **Commission statement, 9 October 2026 (STATEMENT/26/2127):** the EU "has been a constant supporter" of the ICC; the US announcement is "deeply regrettable"; the EU "will work closely with the ICC to ensure it continues to operate effectively" and with all **125 States Parties** to the Rome Statute; it "stands ready to take any necessary measures to enable the Court to carry out its work both in investigating and prosecuting war crimes and crimes against humanity, and to protect the interests of EU operators within the Single Market".
  - **Statement by the High Representative on behalf of the EU, 10 October 2026 (Council, 11:35):** the EU "deeply regrets" the announcement; it urges the US to reverse the measures; it welcomes the US readiness to discuss its concerns with the States Parties and "takes note" of the delay in entry into force, which "creates space for diplomacy"; it says attacks or threats against the Court, its officials, personnel and those cooperating with it, including civil society and service providers, "are not acceptable"; and that the EU "stands ready to take all the necessary measures to ensure that the Court continues to operate effectively".
  - **European Parliament, 9 October 2026:** a joint statement by the chairs of the AFET, DROI, INTA and JURI committees and of the delegation for relations with the United States calling for EU solidarity with the ICC (press release 20261009IPR48086).
- **No EU countermeasure has been adopted.** The statements say the EU "stands ready" to act; they name no instrument, so do not state that the EU will activate a specific legal tool.
- The HR statement recalls the ICC's complementarity principle: the Court acts only where a state is unwilling or unable genuinely to investigate or prosecute.

## How to answer
Describe the situation as an announced US intention with a six-month delay, and the EU position as regret, a call to reverse and readiness to act. If asked what the EU will do, say that no measure has been adopted and quote the "stands ready" wording.
