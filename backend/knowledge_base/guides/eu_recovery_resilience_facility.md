# EU Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) and National Recovery Plans (NRRPs)

## QUICK FACTS
- Regulation: Regulation (EU) 2021/241 (CELEX: 32021R0241), OJ L 57, 18.2.2021, amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/795 (STEP, CELEX: 32024R0795)
- Full name: Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF)
- Parent framework: NextGenerationEU (NGEU), Regulation (EU) 2020/2094 (CELEX: 32020R2094)
- Total envelope: EUR 723.8 billion (2018 prices), approximately EUR 806.9 billion current prices
- Breakdown: EUR 338 billion grants + EUR 385.8 billion loans (original); RePowerEU chapter added EUR 20B grants + EUR 225B loan capacity
- RePowerEU chapter added: Regulation (EU) 2023/435 (CELEX: 32023R0435, 1 March 2023)
- Lead DG: DG ECFIN (Economic and Financial Affairs), supported by SG RECOVER Task Force
- Responsible Commissioner: Valdis Dombrovskis (Economy and Productivity, Latvia)
- EP committees: BUDG and ECON (joint procedure)
- Council composition: ECOFIN Council approves Council Implementing Decisions on each NRRP
- Climate earmark: minimum 37% of each NRRP
- Digital earmark: minimum 20% of each NRRP
- Social expenditure earmark: minimum 6 of 4 Pillar of Social Rights principles
- Milestones and targets: payment-linked, independently verified (Member State self-assessment + Commission assessment + Council opinion)
- Payment schedule: biannual, up to 2026 (grants) / 2026 (loans)
- Sunset clause: commitments by 31 December 2023; payments by 31 December 2026
- Post-2026: MFF 2028-2034 successor under discussion
- EPRS Briefing (13 April 2026): "Latvia's National Recovery and Resilience Plan: Latest state of play" Ref: EPRS_UNK_4a2fdee08c24
- EPRS Briefing (9 April 2026): "Poland's National Recovery and Resilience Plan: Latest state of play" Ref: EPRS_UNK_b99524223f53

## Key Case Studies

### Hungary: Frozen Funds (Article 7 TEU + Conditionality Regulation)
- RRP approved December 2022 with 27 super milestones on judicial independence
- As of April 2026: zero payments made
- With Magyar government (election 12 April 2026), milestone fulfilment roadmap being prepared
- See `hungary_election_2026_magyar.md`

### Poland: Unlock and Implementation (EPRS 9 April 2026)
- Tusk government (2023-) restored rule of law, unlocked funds 29 February 2024
- First payment December 2024 (EUR 6.3B)
- Second payment request submitted end 2025; in Commission assessment
- RePowerEU chapter (EUR 25B additional) in implementation phase
- Challenges: slow project tendering, municipal capacity constraints
- Key reform areas: judicial independence, media pluralism, anti-corruption framework

### Latvia: Ongoing Implementation (EPRS 13 April 2026)
- Plan total EUR 1.96B grants + EUR 320M loans (limited loan uptake due to low sovereign debt)
- Climate earmark 38%, digital 21%
- Focus: energy efficiency in buildings, digital skills, healthcare IT, transport (Rail Baltica)
- Achievements: energy efficiency renovations exceeded targets, digital public services advanced
- Challenges: labour market bottlenecks, procurement delays in rail infrastructure
- RePowerEU chapter focuses on renewable electricity and green hydrogen

## Milestones and Targets Framework

- Each NRRP contains typically 100-400 M&Ts
- Milestone: qualitative (e.g., adoption of a law)
- Target: quantitative (e.g., 50,000 households renovated)
- Payment requests: biannual; Commission has 2 months for preliminary assessment, then Economic and Financial Committee (EFC) opinion
- Partial suspension possible if specific M&Ts unfulfilled
- Amendment procedure: MS can request modification under Article 21

## RePowerEU Chapter (since 2023)

- Purpose: accelerate green transition and cut Russian fossil fuel dependency
- New funding: EUR 20B grants (from ETS innovation fund + Brexit Adjustment Reserve) + EUR 225B loans (unused RRF loans + reserves)
- Priorities: energy efficiency, renewables, heat pumps, hydrogen, energy infrastructure, skills
- Accelerated permitting for renewables
- Member States must add RePowerEU chapter by Article 6 procedure

## Governance and Accountability

- European Semester link: RRP implementation integrated into CSRs
- European Parliament scrutiny: Recovery and Resilience Dialogue with Commission every 4 months
- National parliaments: hearings with Commission representatives
- European Court of Auditors: special reports (ECA Report 26/2023 on absorption)
- EPPO: competent for fraud protecting EU financial interests
- OLAF: administrative investigations into fraud/corruption

## MFF 2028-2034 and RRF Successor

Commission proposal expected summer 2026 (COM(2026)XXX). Options discussed:
- Single national plan per MS (integrated RRF + Cohesion)
- Performance-based financing extended
- Climate earmark raised to 40%+
- Defence integration (new pillar)
- Enlargement readiness allocation
