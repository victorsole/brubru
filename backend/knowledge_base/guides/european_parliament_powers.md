# The Powers of the European Parliament

## QUICK FACTS
- **Four powers**: **legislative**, **budgetary**, **supervisory** (democratic oversight), and **external/consent**. The EP is the world's only directly-elected transnational assembly (720 MEPs, ~450 million citizens, term 10: 2024–2029).
- **Legislative**: co-legislator with the Council under the **ordinary legislative procedure** (Art 294 TFEU) — Parliament and Council adopt EU law jointly on a Commission proposal. For certain areas the EP only **consents** or is **consulted** (special procedures).
- **Budgetary**: the EP and Council jointly **adopt the annual EU budget** and the multiannual financial framework (MFF); the EP also grants the **discharge** (signing off on how the budget was spent).
- **Supervisory**: the EP **elects the Commission President**, approves the College via hearings, can **censure the Commission** (motion of censure), puts questions to the institutions, and runs committees of inquiry.
- **External**: the EP must **consent** to international agreements (trade, association, accession) and conducts interparliamentary diplomacy via delegations.
- **Why Brubru cares**: a file's procedure + the relevant power determine how decisive the EP is — central to Predictions, Position Analysis and explaining "can the Parliament actually stop this?". Source: `europarl.europa.eu/about-parliament/en` powers pages.

## 1. Legislative power
- Under the **ordinary legislative procedure (codecision, Art 294 TFEU)**, the EP and Council are equal co-legislators: the Commission proposes, committees amend, plenary votes a position, and the two institutions agree a joint text (often at first reading via trilogues). See `eu_legislative_procedures_explained.md`.
- Under **special procedures**, the Council legislates and the EP either **consents** (accept/reject, no amendment) or is **consulted** (non-binding opinion).
- The EP also has a (limited) **right to request legislation**: it can ask the Commission to bring forward a proposal (Art 225 TFEU).

## 2. Budgetary power
- The EP and Council are the **two arms of the budgetary authority**: they jointly adopt the **annual budget**.
- The EP votes on the **multiannual financial framework (MFF)** (consent) — see `mff_2028_2034.md`.
- The EP grants the **discharge** to the Commission (and other bodies) for budget implementation — a key accountability lever, handled by the **CONT** committee.

## 3. Supervisory / democratic-oversight power
- **Elects the Commission President** (proposed by the European Council, taking account of the election result — the *Spitzenkandidat*/lead-candidate logic).
- **Approves the College of Commissioners** after **public hearings** of each commissioner-designate before the relevant committee.
- Can adopt a **motion of censure** forcing the whole Commission to resign (two-thirds majority).
- **Scrutiny tools**: oral and written questions, committees of inquiry, own-initiative reports, debates on topical issues, and scrutiny of delegated/implementing acts.
- Elects the **European Ombudsman**; involved in appointments (e.g. Court of Auditors, ECB Board hearings).

## 4. External / consent power
- The EP must give its **consent** to most international agreements the EU concludes — trade agreements (Art 207/218 TFEU), association agreements, and the **accession** of new Member States. Without EP consent, the agreement cannot enter into force.
- Conducts **parliamentary diplomacy** through interparliamentary delegations and joint assemblies (see `ep_intergroups_and_delegations.md`).
- Adopts **resolutions** on foreign-policy and human-rights situations (non-binding but politically weighty).
