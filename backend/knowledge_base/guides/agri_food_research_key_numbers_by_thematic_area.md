# Key numbers by theme in SWD(2026) 271: Mission Soil, bioeconomy, rural, organic, digital, knowledge, international

## QUICK FACTS
- **Soil (Mission Soil, "A Soil Deal for Europe"):** since 2021 **45 living labs** set up (target **100 by 2030**); **63 projects funded 2021-2024, EUR 469 million, nearly 1,400 organisations**; Manifesto **over 3,500 signatures**; **37 ambassadors**; Mission Soil Week since 2023. **EJP SOIL** (2020-2025): 26 institutes, 24 countries, over EUR 40 million plus EUR 40 million EU, 45 projects.
- **Bioeconomy:** **25 projects, EUR 122 million** in Horizon Europe Cluster 6 since 2021; **71 projects, EUR 395 million** in Horizon 2020; Mission Soil adds **over EUR 30 million** on circular soil improvers. **CBE-JU** gave about **EUR 185 million** in 2022-2025 to primary-producer projects (**76 primary-sector entities, farmers about 73%**).
- **Rural opportunities:** **EUR 183 million (33 projects)** since 2016; **EUR 250 million to over 60 projects** under the Horizon Europe flagship action "Research and innovation for rural communities" (EU rural action plan).
- **Agroecology and organic:** the organic action plan intends **at least 30%** of the agriculture, forestry and rural R&I budget for organic-relevant topics; **EUR 490 million in 59 topics in 2021-2024**; OrganicAdviceNet targets about **1,000 organic advisers**. The Agroecology partnership: EUR 300 million tentative, 111 partners, 31 countries.
- **Digital and data:** the partnership **Agriculture of Data** started **1 October 2025**, at least seven years, EUR 300 million tentative, 30% EU.
- **Knowledge flows:** **more than 60 thematic networks**; **over 5,200 EIP Operational Groups** since 2014, **more than 6,600** planned for 2023-2027; **over 14,000 practice abstracts**; **EU-FarmBook** (2022-2029, all 24 EU languages).
- **International:** third-country partners in **34%** of Horizon 2020 and **19%** of Horizon Europe projects (2014-2025); EU-African Union **FNSSA**: over **EUR 125.5 million**, 20 projects; **PRIMA**: 305 projects, 2,900 beneficiaries.
- **Co-funded partnerships (five):** 421 participants (242 unique), 25 Member States and 11 associated countries, almost EUR 700 million (almost EUR 250 million EU); after top-ups over EUR 1,300 million (over EUR 500 million EU).
- **Climate Farm Demo:** 1,460 pilot demo farms in 28 countries. **Food 2030:** about EUR 770 million.
