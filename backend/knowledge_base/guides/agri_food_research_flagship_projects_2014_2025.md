# EU agri-food research flagships (1): climate, soil, biodiversity, forests, plant health, crops

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** the "flagship stories" of SWD(2026) 271 (8 September 2026). Examples chosen by the Commission, not a ranking. RIA = research and innovation action, IA = innovation action, CSA = coordination and support action.
- **Climate:** **Climate Farm Demo** (2022-2029, CSA): **1,460 pilot demo farms in 28 countries**, **over 230 climate advisers**, target **4,500 demonstration events**, 10 living labs, reach **250,000 stakeholders including 150,000 farmers**, a farm-level monitoring, reporting and verification process. **ClimateSmartAdvisors** (2023-2030, CSA): 27 countries, **1,500 advisers**, **260 communities of practice**, 12 thematic areas. **ClieNFarms** (2022-2025, IA): Catalogue of Climate Solutions (for example herbs in feed to cut methane). **CLIMED-FRUIT** (2022-2025): nine Operational Groups, seven countries, Mediterranean perennial crops. Peatlands: Paludi4All, PALUS DEMOS, PaluWise.
- **Biodiversity and soil:** **SHOWCASE** (2020-2025, RIA): 11 sites in 10 countries, handbook "Farming with Biodiversity". **SoildiverAgro** (2019-2025, RIA): 15 case studies in six zones, over 10,000 taxonomic units. **SEA2LAND** (2021-2025, IA): nine bio-based fertiliser technologies from fish waste. **EJP SOIL** (2020-2025): 26 institutes, 24 countries. Pollinators: RestPoll, Safeguard, ProPollSoil, AGRI4POL.
- **Forests:** the FOREST partnership (2026-2036); projects SMURF, Small4Good, HOMED, FORSAID, INFORMA, CLIMB-FOREST, OptFor-EU, FORWARDS (ForestWard Observatory), OptFORESTS (genetic resources), SINTETIC (traceability of trees, logs, boards), TIMBERHAUS, WoodStock, EUFORE.
- **Plant health:** **XF-ACTORS** (2016-2021, RIA): the first European project devoted to *Xylella fastidiosa*, **29 partners, 14 countries**, genomic sequencing of its European origins, risk maps. Integrated pest management: IPMWorks, IPM Decisions, AdoptIPM, SuperPests, WeLaser.
- **Crops and genetic resources:** **ADAPT** (2020-2024, RIA): heat- and drought-tolerant potato, up to **54 varieties** tested in four countries, survey of **550 growers**. **GEN4OLIVE** (2020-2025, IA): 16 partners, 7 countries, **over 500 olive cultivars**, the **Cordoba World Olive Germplasm Bank** placed under the **International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources**, 25 SMEs engaged. Breeding: Neurice, SolACE, UNTWIST, LIVESEED, ECOBREED, BRESOV, InnObreed. Protein crops: EUCLEG, LEGENDARY, VALPRO Path, IntercropValuES.
