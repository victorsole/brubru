# Methane Regulation: Commission proposes a one-year delay of the importer rules (COM(2026) 569, 14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **14 October 2026** the Commission proposed a targeted amendment to **Regulation (EU) 2024/1787** on the reduction of methane emissions in the energy sector: **COM(2026) 569 final**, a proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council (press release IP/26/2119, Q&A QANDA/26/2120). It is a PROPOSAL: Parliament and Council must adopt it.
- **Why (Commission's words):** the prolonged closure of the **Strait of Hormuz (since 27 February 2026)**, which carries about 20% of world LNG and 25% of seaborne oil, a "potentially very difficult winter" of high energy prices, and the risk of extra pressure on the security of supply of imported oil, gas and coal.
- **What changes, read in the proposal's Article 1 (it amends Articles 28 and 29 only):**
  - **Article 28(1), importers' equivalence demonstration:** today it applies **from 1 January 2027**; the proposal makes it an annual report **by 31 May 2029 and every year after**, covering the previous year's supply under contracts concluded or renewed on or after 4 August 2024. Article 28(2) reporting on efforts moves to the same 31 May 2029 date.
  - **Article 29(1), methane intensity reporting** by Union producers and importers: **5 August 2028 becomes 5 August 2029**, then by 31 May every year.
  - **Article 29(2), demonstrating intensity below the maximum values:** **5 August 2030 becomes 5 August 2031** (for contracts concluded or renewed after 5 August 2030).
  - Entry into force: twentieth day after OJ publication (Article 2).
- **What does NOT change:** the domestic monitoring, reporting, verification and mitigation duties on EU operators, and the Regulation's scope. The Commission says the ambition "remains steadfast".
- **Related Commission steps:** two Recommendations of July 2026 (compliance solutions, Recommendation (EU) 2026/1834, and penalties); a call for feedback launched **12 October 2026** on a draft Implementing Act with reporting templates for oil, gas and coal methane emissions; work on delegated acts on a methane-intensity methodology.
- **First reaction read (social signal, not a position of Parliament):** Greens/EFA MEP **Jutta Paulus** called it, on 14 October, a departure from fact-based policy ("Vorschlag, Methan-Verordnung um 1 Jahr zu verschieben").
- **Procedure number:** COM(2026) 569 was on the Commission's register on 15 October and not yet on Cellar; its Parliament procedure reference was not checked on 15 October. Do not invent one.

## How to answer
"Is the EU delaying the Methane Regulation?": the Commission PROPOSED on 14 October 2026 (COM(2026) 569) to push the importer obligations back by about a year: equivalence reporting by 31 May 2029 instead of applying from 1 January 2027, intensity reporting from 5 August 2029 instead of 2028, intensity limits from 5 August 2031 instead of 2030. Not adopted yet; the domestic rules stay as they are.
