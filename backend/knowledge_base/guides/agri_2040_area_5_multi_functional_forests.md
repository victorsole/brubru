# AgRI 2040 area 5 of 8: multi-functional forests

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** fifth of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). Preliminary, no obligation.
- **Purpose:** sustainable management of forests for **multiple benefits**, following the principles of **active sustainable forest management as defined by Forest Europe**; increase the resilience of forest ecosystems and forest-based sectors; **reward foresters for the ecosystem services** they provide, in rural and urban contexts.
- **Priority actions:** restoration and protection of **forest habitats**, climate mitigation and adaptation, **agroforestry**, the **forest-based bioeconomy**.
- **Evidence:** adaptive and resilient forest management practices and the impact of management on sustainability; knowledge on **forest carbon sinks including forest soil**.
- **Threats:** resilience against **new and emerging pests and diseases**; restoring damaged forests; reducing **wildfire** risk.
- **Species and genetics:** underutilised tree species (processing technology, digital innovation, business models); tree species diversity; **conservation of forest genetic resources** and tree propagation.
- **Wood products:** better understanding of benefits including **long-term carbon storage** and value from by-products; a stronger role for foresters in the value chain; new business models; **financial incentives for non-provisioning services**; societal expectations including wellbeing.
- **Related research:** the co-funded **FOREST** partnership (2026-2036, 100 organisations from 28 countries, seven joint calls).
- **Search words (other languages):** forêts, sylviculture, forestiers (FR); bossen, bosbouw, boseigenaren (NL); bosques, silvicultura, selvicultores (ES); boscos, silvicultura, silvicultors (CA); foreste, selvicoltura, silvicoltori (IT).
