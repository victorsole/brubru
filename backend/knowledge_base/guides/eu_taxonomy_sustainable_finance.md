## EU Taxonomy Framework

### What It Is
The EU Taxonomy is a classification system that defines which economic activities are environmentally sustainable. It directs capital flows toward transition-supporting investments and reduces greenwashing.

### Six Environmental Objectives
1. Climate change mitigation (CCM)
2. Climate change adaptation (CCA)
3. Sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources (WTR)
4. Transition to a circular economy (CE)
5. Pollution prevention and control (PPC)
6. Protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems (BIO)

### Four Conditions for Taxonomy Alignment
1. **Substantial contribution** to at least one of the six objectives
2. **Do No Significant Harm (DNSH)** to the other five objectives
3. Compliance with **minimum safeguards** (human rights, labour, anti-corruption)
4. Meets **technical screening criteria** set out in delegated acts

### Activity Classification
- **Eligible**: Activity is listed in the delegated acts (potential to contribute)
- **Aligned**: Activity meets all four conditions (substantial contribution + DNSH + safeguards + TSC)

### Three Disclosure KPIs
Companies must report taxonomy-aligned proportions for:
- **Turnover** (revenue from aligned activities)
- **CapEx** (capital expenditure on aligned activities, split into types a, b, c)
- **OpEx** (operating expenditure on aligned activities)

## Platform on Sustainable Finance

- Advisory expert group established under Article 20 of Regulation (EU) 2020/852
- Current chair: Helena Vines Fiestas (Spanish CNMV Commissioner)
- Third mandate: February 2026 -- end 2027, 35 members (28 appointed via July 2025 call)
- Tasks: Technical screening criteria advice, stakeholder request analysis, capital flow monitoring, transition finance guidance
- Stakeholder requests: 646 received at first cut-off (Dec 2023), 161 at second (Sep 2025)

### Third Mandate Priorities
1. Revising technical screening criteria (delegated acts planned for 2026)
2. Developing new criteria across all six objectives
3. Simplifying the Taxonomy and sustainable finance framework
4. Monitoring capital flows to sustainable investments

## March 2026 Revision

The Commission launched a call for feedback on revising criteria for sustainable economic activities. Key issues:
- **Simplification**: Omnibus Regulation (February 2025) risks 80% reduction in companies subject to reporting (threshold raised to 1,000 employees, EUR 450M turnover)
- **Agriculture**: Major sector still not covered by Taxonomy
- **DNSH complexity**: Criteria seen as too complex, ambiguous, heterogeneous across sectors
- **OpEx KPI**: Problematic to calculate, proposals to make it optional
- **Mining**: Concerns about WFD revision linked to mining expansion

### Stakeholder Positions
- 163 investors (EUR 6,600 billion AUM) signed collective statement defending CSRD + Taxonomy disclosures
- 19 networks representing 100,000 European companies (Business for Better Tomorrow) support maintaining Taxonomy
- Environmental organisations concerned about weakening under simplification agenda
