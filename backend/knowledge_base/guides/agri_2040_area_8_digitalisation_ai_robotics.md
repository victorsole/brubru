# AgRI 2040 area 8 of 8: digitalisation, AI and robotics

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** eighth of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). Preliminary, no obligation.
- **Purpose:** **data valorisation** across agri-food supply chains, forestry and rural services so that actors can use data, AI and digital solutions effectively; better access to high-quality data for informed decisions and evidence-based policies; **advanced AI including generative AI**.
- **Priority:** development, accessibility and uptake of digital solutions for a broad range of users, including **small and medium-sized farms and rural businesses**.
- **Key technologies:** **software and IT tools, robotics, drones, augmented and virtual reality, remote and proximal sensing**, fitted to users of all skill levels.
- **AI adoption:** accelerate it to **reduce administrative burdens**, improve performance and access to knowledge and support faster, better-informed decisions through predictive insights; **AI-powered robotic applications** across farming, forestry and rural services.
- **Trust and skills:** demonstrate the benefits and costs of digital solutions to build trust; develop skills, know-how and advisory capacity.
- **Linked instruments:** the **EDIC for Agri-Food** (Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1978), the agrifoodTEF testing and experimentation facilities under the Digital Europe Programme, the Partnership on **Agriculture of Data** (EUR 300 million tentative, started 1 October 2025), Common European Data Spaces.
- **Search words (other languages):** numérisation, intelligence artificielle, robotique, données agricoles (FR); digitalisering, kunstmatige intelligentie, robotica, landbouwdata (NL); digitalización, inteligencia artificial, robótica, datos agrarios (ES); digitalització, intel·ligència artificial, robòtica, dades agràries (CA); digitalizzazione, intelligenza artificiale, robotica, dati agricoli (IT).
