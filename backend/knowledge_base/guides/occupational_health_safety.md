## Overview

EU occupational health and safety law protects over 170 million workers across the EU. The Framework Directive 89/391/EEC establishes general principles: employer duty to ensure worker safety, risk assessment obligation, preventive measures, worker information and training, health surveillance. Specific "daughter directives" cover particular risks: chemical agents, carcinogens, biological agents, display screen equipment, manual handling, noise, vibration, asbestos, and more.

## Carcinogens and Mutagens Directive (CMD)

The CMD is the main instrument protecting workers from occupational cancer, which causes an estimated 100,000 deaths per year in the EU. It has been revised six times since 2004:

| Revision | Year | Key Changes |
|----------|------|-------------|
| CMD1 | 2017/2398 | Added 11 substances (chromium VI, hardwood dust, etc.) |
| CMD2 | 2019/130 | Added 5 substances (formaldehyde, cadmium, etc.) |
| CMD3 | 2019/983 | Added 5 substances (nickel, acrylonitrile, benzene) |
| CMD4 | 2022/431 | Extended scope to reprotoxic substances; added lead, diisocyanates |
| CMD5-6 | Ongoing | Further substance additions, lowering existing OELs |

### Current EMPL Vote (April 2026)
The EMPL committee is voting on an update to EU rules protecting workers from hazardous chemical exposure. Key elements:
- New or revised binding occupational exposure limits (OELs) for specific substances
- Enhanced provisions for lead exposure (construction, battery manufacturing, recycling)
- Diisocyanates: widely used in polyurethane production, construction foams, automotive coatings
- Strengthened risk assessment and substitution obligations

## EU Strategic Framework on OSH 2021-2027

Three objectives:
1. **Anticipating change**: addressing risks from digitalisation, green transition, demographic change
2. **Improving prevention**: "Vision Zero" approach to work-related deaths
3. **Increasing preparedness**: lessons from COVID-19 pandemic response

Key actions:
- Review of workplace directive (89/654/EEC) and display screen equipment directive (90/270/EEC)
- EU-OSHA campaigns on digitalisation and psychosocial risks
- Asbestos exposure: Directive (EU) 2023/2668 lowering OEL for asbestos (CELEX 32023L2668)

## Key Statistics
- 2.9 million non-fatal work accidents per year in EU
- ~3,400 fatal work accidents per year
- ~100,000 occupational cancer deaths per year
- Economic cost: 3.3% of EU GDP (EUR 476 billion annually)
- Sectors most affected: construction, manufacturing, agriculture, mining, healthcare
