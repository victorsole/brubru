# European Parliament Plenary, Strasbourg 5-8 October 2026: What Was Adopted on Tuesday 6 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **AGILE defence innovation programme (adopted 6 Oct 2026, 536 votes to 101, 16 abstentions):** procedure **2026/0078(COD)** (joint SEDE and ITRE committees; the ITRE chair is Borys Budka). A new **EUR 115 million instrument** for fast, targeted funding of **SMEs, start-ups and scale-ups** developing emerging and disruptive defence technologies, with a **four-month time-to-grant** and matchmaking with defence "primes". Parliament and Council negotiators had provisionally agreed it. **Next:** Council must formally endorse it before publication in the Official Journal; "the programme will enter into operation in the beginning of 2027" (Parliament press release).
- **Carcinogens, Mutagens and Reprotoxic substances at work: sixth revision, "CMRD" (adopted 6 Oct, 499 for, 49 against, 104 abstentions)**, procedure **2025/0232(COD)**, committee EMPL, rapporteur **Liesbet Sommen (EPP)**. It amends Directive 2004/37/EC with **new limit values for cobalt and inorganic cobalt compounds (battery production), polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (steel, iron, aluminium) and 1,4-dioxane**, plus a **long-term occupational exposure limit value for isoprene**; the Commission must assess further limit values for **welding fumes**; clearer rules on personal protective equipment (including the right to regular breaks); extra protection for **firefighters** and other emergency personnel; training on **hazardous medicinal products**. Parliament says the measures should prevent about **1,700 lung cancer cases and 19,000 other illnesses over 40 years**. The vote endorsed a provisional agreement with the Council; Council adoption and Official Journal publication were not stated in Parliament's release.
- **Psychosocial risks, stress and mental health at work (adopted 6 Oct, 413 for, 210 against, 29 abstentions):** a **legislative initiative report** (procedure **2026/2023(INL)**, EMPL, rapporteur **Estelle Ceulemans (S&D)**). It asks the Commission for a **new EU proposal** on work-related psychosocial risks, **preceded by an impact assessment** including an assessment of existing legislation. It is a **request, not a law**: the Commission decides what to propose. Asks include employer obligations on psychosocial risk assessment and action plans, policies against violence, harassment and bullying, **human oversight of algorithmic management**, the **right to disconnect**, individual **return-to-work plans**, and explicit recognition that psychosocial risks can give rise to occupational diseases (ILO figure cited: more than 840,000 deaths a year).
- **INSPIRE simplification (Omnibus VIII), procedure 2025/0393(COD), ENVI, rapporteur Emma Wiesner (Renew), report A10-0233/2026: RESOLVED by the plenary minutes ("Results of votes", 6 October, item 9.7).** Parliament adopted the committee's amendments 1-23 collectively, rejected amendment 24 (The Left, 284-350-24), approved the Commission proposal as amended by **515 votes to 108, with 35 abstentions** (roll call), and then **adopted the request for referral back to the committee**. So Parliament has set its position but **the first reading is NOT closed**: the file went back to ENVI. Both earlier readings were partly right (ENVI's "adopts its position" and the minutes' "amendments adopted ... referral back"). It is not law.
- **Also adopted 6 Oct:** alignment with the EU economic governance framework and further simplification (**2025/0311(COD)**, 450-135-75); euro-area financial-stability surveillance (**2025/0312(COD)**, 458-138-61); the diversified funding strategy (**2025/0313(APP)**, 462-121-75); a resolution on the EU's approach to corporate tax policy (**2025/2210(INI)**, 364-69-162).
- **Wednesday 7 October (see `ep_plenary_october_2026_wednesday_adoptions`):** Parliament ADOPTED a recommendation on EU-China relations (2025/2117(INI), 454-86-110) and approved three EGF files (EUR 6.91 million for workers in Belgium, Spain and Finland); the **2028-2034 MFF** was debated in plenary: a debate only, **no vote**. **Thursday 8 October:** votes on three Rule 115 objections and resolutions (including on Iran, Georgia, Azerbaijan, the Russian Duma elections, EU-Africa trade).
- **No KIDS Act vote:** on 6 October the plenary heard a Commission statement by EVP Virkkunen on protecting children in the digital world; there was no resolution.

## FAQ
**Is the CMRD revision in force?** No: Parliament adopted it on 6 October 2026; Council adoption and publication in the Official Journal must follow, then Member States have two years to transpose, with longer transitional periods for certain limit values (Parliament's release).
**Will the EU adopt a psychosocial-risks directive?** Parliament has only asked for a proposal, after an impact assessment. The Commission has three months to reply to a legislative initiative request.
