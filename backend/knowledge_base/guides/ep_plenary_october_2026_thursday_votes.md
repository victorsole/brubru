## FAQ
**Did Parliament recognise the new Russian State Duma?** No: 482-41-39 for the resolution refusing to recognise it as legitimate.
**Did Parliament object to the lead-in-gunshot measure?** No. Both motions fell (166-346-21 and 193-333-22).
**Why did the hydroxyanthracene and red yeast rice objections fail with more votes for than against?** An objection under Rule 115(4)(c) needs a majority of all Members (361 of 720); they got 282 and 291.
**Did Parliament adopt a resolution on Islamist entryism?** Yes, B10-0424/2026, 381-167-15 on a roll call; its content has not been read here.
**What was the vote on the anti-corruption strategy?** By show of hands: the resolution was adopted, no count is recorded.
**What was the vote on the EU-Africa trade and investment resolution?** By show of hands: the resolution was adopted, no count is recorded.
**Are these resolutions binding?** No. They set Parliament's position and ask the Council, the Commission and the High Representative to act; sanctions are decided by the Council.
