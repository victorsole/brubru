# European Parliament Plenary, Strasbourg 5-8 October 2026: Votes on Thursday 8 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status of this guide:** written on 8 October 2026 at about 12:30 from the Parliament's own press releases, which gave the vote counts. The official "Results of votes" minutes for 8 October were **not yet published**, and **other Thursday items on the agenda (the earlier reading of the agenda, in the Tuesday guide, recorded three Rule 115 objections and an EU-Africa trade item for Thursday) have no published result in the sources read**: do not state a result for them. Vote counts are in the form for-against-abstentions.
- **Russia: Parliament refused to recognise the State Duma as legitimate (adopted 482 for, 41 against, 39 abstentions).** Committee AFET. The resolution rejects the Duma elections of **18-20 September 2026** as "neither free nor fair" and says they were held with no genuine alternative candidates, no free media and no credible observers; it condemns the "elections" held in occupied parts of **Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia**, Moldova's **Transnistria** and Georgia's **Abkhazia and Tskhinvali/South Ossetia**. It calls on all EU countries to adopt the same non-recognition policy, to refrain from official contact with the newly constituted Duma and its members, and to oppose their participation in interparliamentary assemblies; it speaks against normalising Russia's participation in international sporting and cultural events.
- **Sanctions and no normalisation (same resolution):** MEPs ask EU countries for "further extensive restrictive measures" against people and entities involved in political persecution, naming **judges, prosecutors, officials of Rosfinmonitoring, members of Russia's Central Election Commission** and officials who organised the staged votes in occupied territories. EU relations with Russia "cannot be normalised" without a just and lasting peace for Ukraine, full territorial integrity, withdrawal of Russian troops, release of prisoners of war, return of deported Ukrainian children and full reparations.
- **Three human-rights resolutions adopted on Thursday (Subcommittee on Human Rights):** **Iran 541-11-25**: abolish the death penalty, annul the death sentences of **Taraneh Rahimi (20) and Leila Abolhasani (43)**, broaden sanctions, recognise "gender apartheid" as a crime against humanity, refer the regime to the International Criminal Court. **Georgia 445-37-97**: condemns attempts by the Georgian Dream party to ban opposition parties, asks for the release of political prisoners including **2025 Sakharov Prize laureate Mzia Amaglobeli**, and for targeted sanctions on **Bidzina Ivanishvili, Irakli Kobakhidze, Shalva Papuashvili and Kakha Kaladze**. **Azerbaijan 492-23-60**: any future EU-Azerbaijan partnership agreement must depend on tangible human-rights improvements and release of political prisoners; asks for the release of **Ali Karimli, Anar Mammadli and journalist Anar Abdullayev**, and for the High Representative to prepare targeted measures under the **EU Global Sanctions Regime**.
- **European school alliances (adopted 443 for, 98 against, 47 abstentions):** a resolution of the Culture and Education committee, rapporteur **Nela Riehl (Greens/EFA, Germany)**, welcoming the decision to put a **European school alliances pilot scheme** in the education package the Commission is to present shortly. Parliament asks that it cover citizenship education, democratic competences, media literacy, critical thinking and knowledge of the EU, include schools from disadvantaged contexts, vocational and sport or arts schools, keep a geographical balance, and respect that education is a Member State responsibility.

## FAQ
**Did Parliament recognise the new Russian State Duma?** No. It adopted a resolution refusing to recognise the Duma as the legitimate representative and legislative body of the Russian Federation (482-41-39).
**Are these resolutions binding?** No. They are non-legislative resolutions that set Parliament's position and ask the Council, the Commission and the High Representative to act; sanctions are decided by the Council.
**Where are the full results?** In the "Results of votes" minutes of the sitting, published by the Parliament after the vote; they were not yet available when this guide was written.
