# EU-Mauritius Sustainable Fisheries Partnership Agreement and Protocol 2026-2030: Commission proposals of 13 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **Three Commission proposals dated 13 October 2026** (all proposals, none adopted): **COM(2026) 544 final** (Council Decision on the **signing and provisional application** of the new Sustainable Fisheries Partnership Agreement (SFPA) and its Implementing Protocol 2026-2030, procedure 2026/0309(NLE)); **COM(2026) 545 final** (Council Decision on the **conclusion**, 2026/0310(NLE)); **COM(2026) 547 final** (Council Regulation **allocating the fishing opportunities** among Member States, 2026/0311(NLE)).
- **Why now:** the 2014 Fisheries Partnership Agreement entered into force on 28 January 2014 and renews tacitly; its current Protocol (in force since 7 September 2023, four years) **expires on 20 December 2026**. The Council authorised negotiations by a Decision of **31 March 2026**; the negotiators **initialled** the new texts on **4 September 2026**.
- **Duration:** the new Agreement applies for **four years** from provisional application (Article 20), tacitly renewable for successive four-year periods; the Protocol applies four years from its signature (Article 11). The new Agreement replaces the 2014 one.
- **Fishing opportunities:** **25 tuna purse seine vessels and 25 surface longliners**, in line with scientific advice and the Indian Ocean Tuna Commission (IOTC). **Proposed allocation (COM(2026) 547):** purse seiners **Spain 14, France 10, Italy 1**; longliners **Spain 7, France 16, Portugal 2**.
- **Money:** **EUR 700,000 a year**: **EUR 200,000 for access** (a reference tonnage of **4,000 tonnes a year**) plus **EUR 500,000 a year of sectoral support** for Mauritius's fisheries sector and blue economy.
- **Legal basis:** Article 43(2) TFEU for the Decisions (the agreement serves the common fisheries policy) and **Article 43(3) TFEU** for the allocation Regulation; exclusive Union competence. The Agreement contains a clause on the consequences of violating human rights and democratic principles. The EDPS was consulted.
- **Status:** the Council has still to act on all three. Do not say the Agreement is signed or in force.
