# EU Regulatory Landscape for Major Spanish Companies (IBEX 35 and Beyond)

## QUICK FACTS
- Scope: EU legislation applicable to large Spanish companies across 5 key sectors
- Sectors: Banking (Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank), Energy (Repsol, Iberdrola, Endesa, Naturgy), Telecom (Telefonica, Cellnex), Tech/Defence (Indra, Amadeus, Navantia), Retail (Inditex, Mercadona)
- Total regulatory acts: 60+ primary EU legislative acts across all sectors
- Deep dive: https://brubru.beresol.eu/pharma-laws/ (pharma sector)
- Source: Brubru eu_laws database scan, April 2026
- AESIA (Spanish AI Supervisory Agency): 147 investigations opened in 18 months
- 23+ Spanish companies confirmed in EU Transparency Register (see table below)
- Highest lobbying spend: Repsol EUR 800K, Santander EUR 800K, BBVA EUR 800K, Indra EUR 700K
- Most lobbyists: Indra (10 FTE, 7 EP passes), Mercadona (4.5 FTE, 3 EP passes), Iberdrola (3.3 FTE)

## Banking Sector (Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank, Sabadell, Bankinter, Ibercaja)

Spanish banks face 18+ overlapping EU regulations:
- **Prudential:** CRD IV (Dir 2013/36, CELEX 32013L0036) + CRR (Reg 575/2013, CELEX 32013R0575)
- **Digital resilience:** DORA (Reg 2022/2554, CELEX 32022R2554) -- in force January 2025
- **Crypto:** MiCA (Reg 2023/1114, CELEX 32023R1114) -- in force June/December 2024
- **AML:** New AML Regulation (Reg 2024/1624, CELEX 32024R1624) + AMLA (Reg 2024/1620, CELEX 32024R1620)
- **Payments:** PSD2 (Dir 2015/2366, CELEX 32015L2366)
- **Markets:** MiFIR (Reg 600/2014, CELEX 32014R0600), MAR (Reg 596/2014, CELEX 32014R0596)
- **AI:** Credit scoring is high-risk under AI Act (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689)
- **Sustainability:** CSRD (Dir 2022/2464, CELEX 32022L2464)
- **Data:** GDPR (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679)

## Energy Sector (Repsol, Iberdrola, Endesa, Naturgy, Moeve, Acciona, Enagas, Redeia)

Spanish energy companies face 20+ regulations:
- **Emissions:** EU ETS revised (Dir 2023/959, CELEX 32023L0959), CBAM (Reg 2023/956, CELEX 32023R0956)
- **Renewables:** RED III (Dir 2023/2413, CELEX 32023L2413), 42.5% target by 2030
- **Efficiency:** EED recast (Dir 2023/1791, CELEX 32023L1791)
- **Gas/Hydrogen:** Gas/H2 Market Package (Reg 2024/1789), Methane Regulation (Reg 2024/1787, CELEX 32024R1787)
- **Electricity:** Market Design Reform (Reg 2024/1747, CELEX 32024R1747)
- **Infrastructure:** TEN-E (Reg 2022/869, CELEX 32022R0869), 235 PCI/PMI projects (April 2026)
- **Market integrity:** REMIT II (Reg 2024/1106, CELEX 32024R1106), new implementing rules April 2026
- **Climate:** European Climate Law (Reg 2021/1119, CELEX 32021R1119)
- **Transport fuels:** FuelEU Maritime (Reg 2023/1805), AFIR (Reg 2023/1804, CELEX 32023R1804)

## Telecom Sector (Telefonica, Cellnex, MasOrange)

Spanish telecom companies face 14+ regulations:
- **Platform:** DSA (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065), DMA (Reg 2022/1925, CELEX 32022R1925)
- **AI:** AI Act (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689)
- **Communications:** EECC (Dir 2018/1972, CELEX 32018L1972)
- **Cybersecurity:** NIS2 (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555), CRA (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847)
- **Data:** Data Act (Reg 2023/2854, CELEX 32023R2854), DGA (Reg 2022/868, CELEX 32022R0868), GDPR
- **Privacy:** ePrivacy Directive (Dir 2002/58, CELEX 32002L0058)
- **Pending:** Digital Networks Act (proposed 2025), ePrivacy Regulation

## Tech and Defence Sector (Indra, Amadeus, Navantia, Airbus Spain)

- **AI:** AI Act -- military exemption (Art. 2(3)) but dual-use systems still covered
- **Cybersecurity:** CRA (Reg 2024/2847), NIS2 (Dir 2022/2555), Cybersecurity Act (Reg 2019/881, CELEX 32019R0881)
- **Defence:** EDF (Reg 2021/697, CELEX 32021R0697, EUR 7.95B), EDIP (Reg 2023/1525)
- **Procurement:** Public Procurement (Dir 2014/24), Defence Procurement (Dir 2009/81, CELEX 32009L0081), IPI (Reg 2022/1031)
- **Pending:** SAFE (EUR 150B), ReArm Europe, Cloud/AI Development Act (May 2026)

## Retail and Consumer Sector (Inditex, Mercadona, El Corte Ingles, Puig)

- **Products:** ESPR (Reg 2024/1781, CELEX 32024R1781), digital product passport
- **Packaging:** PPWR (Reg 2024/3250, CELEX 32024R3250), recyclability and reuse targets
- **Sustainability:** CSRD (Dir 2022/2464), EU Taxonomy (Reg 2020/852)
- **Consumer:** Consumer Rights Directive (Dir 2011/83, CELEX 32011L0083), UCPD (Dir 2005/29, CELEX 32005L0029)
- **Safety:** GPSR (Reg 2023/988, CELEX 32023R0988), marketplace obligations
- **Supply chain:** CRM Act (Reg 2024/1252, CELEX 32024R1252), Deforestation Regulation (Reg 2023/1115)
- **Pending:** Green Claims Directive, Digital Fairness Act
