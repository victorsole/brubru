# Air Passenger Rights Regulation -- 2026 Revision (Reg 261/2004)

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Monday 15 June 2026 — PROVISIONAL AGREEMENT, DG MOVE welcomes the deal)**: Negotiators reached a political agreement on the long-stalled **revision of the EU air passenger rights rules**, ending a legislative deadlock that had blocked the file since 2013. Member States' ambassadors (Coreper) confirmed the negotiating outcome on **13 June 2026**, and the Commission (DG MOVE) **welcomed the "landmark agreement" on 15 June 2026**. The deal still requires **formal adoption by the European Parliament and the Council** before it becomes law; entry into force is **expected in 2027**. Status: provisional political agreement — NOT yet formally adopted; the final consolidated texts and their CELEX numbers will follow on adoption. Source: DG MOVE press release (15 June 2026); Council/Coreper (13 June 2026).
- **Core instrument under revision: Regulation (EC) No 261/2004** (CELEX **32004R0261**) — common rules on compensation and assistance to air passengers in the event of denied boarding, cancellation, or long delay. Often referred to as "EU261".
- The package also revises **Regulation (EC) No 2027/97** (CELEX **31997R2027**) on **air carrier liability** in respect of the carriage of passengers and their baggage by air.
- **Original proposal:** COM(2013)0130 — the Commission tabled the revision in March 2013; it remained blocked in Council for over a decade until the 2026 agreement.
- **Three-hour delay compensation trigger RETAINED** — Parliament resisted the Council's push to raise the threshold for compensation entitlement to five hours.
- **Cabin baggage in the base fare:** airlines must include both a **personal item and a standard cabin bag** in the advertised base fare for flights to and from EU airports.
- **Family seating:** **guaranteed adjacent seating for children under 14** travelling with an accompanying adult, at no extra cost.
- **Automatic claims:** airlines must **send pre-filled compensation claim forms automatically** following a disruption, rather than requiring passengers to initiate claims.
- **Scope:** flights departing from an EU airport (any carrier) and flights into the EU operated by EU carriers, consistent with the existing 261/2004 scope.

## Overview
Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 is the cornerstone of EU air passenger protection, setting fixed compensation (EUR 250 / 400 / 600 depending on flight distance) plus rights to care, re-routing and reimbursement when flights are cancelled, delayed at length, or passengers are denied boarding. The Commission proposed a modernising revision in 2013 (COM(2013)0130) to clarify rights after major CJEU rulings (notably the *Sturgeon* line of case law establishing compensation for long delays), but the file stalled in the Council for years over the delay-compensation threshold and the treatment of extraordinary circumstances.

The **June 2026 provisional agreement** unblocks the file. It preserves the substance of existing entitlements (including the three-hour compensation trigger), adds consumer-friendly transparency and service obligations (cabin baggage in the base fare, family seating, automatic pre-filled claims), and revises the companion air-carrier-liability regime (Reg 2027/97). Implementation of the Montreal Convention liability limits for baggage and passengers sits alongside this revision.

## What changes for passengers and carriers
- **Compensation entitlement** keeps the **three-hour** long-delay trigger (no move to five hours).
- **Base-fare baggage**: a personal item plus a standard cabin bag must be included in the headline price (curbs unbundled cabin-bag fees on EU flights).
- **Family/accessibility**: free adjacent seating for under-14s with an adult; assistance obligations for passengers with reduced mobility remain.
- **Claims handling**: carriers must proactively issue pre-filled claim forms after disruptions, shifting the administrative burden away from passengers.
- **Transparency**: clearer information duties on rights, re-routing options and total price.

## Institutional landscape
- **Lead Commission DG:** DG MOVE (Mobility and Transport).
- **Lead EP committee:** TRAN (Transport and Tourism). For the current rapporteur and shadow rapporteurs, consult the OEIL procedure file directly — do not assume names from older sources.

## Status and timeline
- **2013:** Commission proposal COM(2013)0130.
- **2013-2025:** blocked in Council (delay-threshold and extraordinary-circumstances disputes).
- **May-June 2026:** intensive trilogues; **Coreper confirmation 13 June 2026**; DG MOVE welcomes the deal 15 June 2026.
- **Next:** formal adoption by the European Parliament and the Council; **entry into force expected 2027** (with transition periods for some obligations).
- Caveat: until both institutions formally adopt the final texts, treat the content as a **provisional agreement**, not enacted law.
