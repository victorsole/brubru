# EU and the Global Coalition on Telecommunications (GCOT)

## QUICK FACTS

- **Topic**: EU strategic partnership with the **Global Coalition on Telecommunications (GCOT)** — informal multilateral grouping working to ensure global telecommunications infrastructure is built on a foundation of security, resilience and cooperation
- **EU status**: **First strategic partner** of GCOT, announced **Monday 4 May 2026** at the inaugural GCOT meeting in **Ottawa, Canada**
- **Source**: DG CNECT news (4 May 2026): https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/european-union-becomes-global-coalition-telecommunications-first-strategic-partner
- **GCOT founding members (7 governments)**: Australia, Canada, Finland, Japan, Sweden, United Kingdom, United States
- **Founded**: 2024 (informal multilateral grouping; not a treaty body)
- **Responsible Commissioner (EU side)**: **Henna Virkkunen** (EVP for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy)
- **Responsible DG (EU side)**: **DG CNECT** (Communications Networks, Content and Technology)
- **EU participation modes (per the 4 May announcement)**:
  - Participate in GCOT discussions at different levels
  - Contribute to specific workstreams led by GCOT member governments
  - Endorse publications and initiatives on a voluntary basis (not bound)
- **Workstream priorities**: supply chain diversification, security, resilience, sustainability, **next-generation technologies (6G)**
- **Geopolitical framing**: GCOT is a like-minded-democracies forum on telecoms supply-chain security, distinct from ITU. Implicit positioning: counterweight to non-democratic supplier dependencies in critical telecoms infrastructure (e.g. legacy 5G RAN supply concerns).

## Why this matters for the EU digital rulebook

The GCOT partnership lands the same week as:

1. **Digital Networks Act (DNA)** — Regulation proposal COM(2026) 0016, procedure 2026/0013(COD), published 21 January 2026; ITRE rapporteur Michał Kobosko (Renew, Poland) tabled the draft report on Tuesday 5 May 2026. The DNA includes chapters on network resilience and cybersecurity that align with GCOT's supply-chain security workstream. Cross-link: `digital_networks_act`.
2. **Cybersecurity Act 2 / ENISA revision** — COM(2026) 11, procedure 2026/0011(COD); rapporteur Markéta Gregorová (Greens/EFA, Czech Republic). Telecoms infrastructure resilience falls under the broader cybersecurity certification framework. Cross-link: `cybersecurity_act`.
3. **Digital Markets Act Review** — COM(2026) 178 final (28 April 2026): forward-focus on cloud + AI as next enforcement frontiers. While DMA itself does not regulate telecoms, the cloud-services investigations (Microsoft Azure / AWS designation decisions due Nov 2026) intersect with telecoms-cloud convergence. Cross-link: `digital_markets_act`.
