# 2026 Annual Overview Report on Simplification, Implementation and Enforcement (COM(2026) 715)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted Wednesday 4 November 2026 with the 2027 work programme: COM(2026) 715 final** (CELEX 52026DC0715), the Commission's second annual overview report. It covers **1 August 2025 to 31 July 2026**. A report, NOT a legislative proposal.
- **Omnibus packages IN FORCE (the Commission's list):** Omnibus I (sustainability reporting and due diligence), Omnibus II (EU investment instruments), Omnibus III (common agricultural policy), parts of Omnibus VII (its digital AI component) and Omnibus X (biocidal products).
- **Political agreement reached, NOT yet in force as of the report:** Omnibus IV (small mid-caps, digitalisation, common specifications), Omnibus V (defence) and Omnibus VI (chemicals). Omnibus V has since been published as Regulation (EU) 2026/2382 (in force 23 November 2026).
- **Six omnibus packages proposed in the period:** digital, environmental legislation, automotive, food and feed safety, taxation, energy products.
- **The Commission's savings estimates (its numbers, not measured savings):** about **EUR 13 billion a year** from the proposals of the period; once all **12 omnibus packages** are adopted, about **EUR 22.3 billion a year** in administrative costs plus **EUR 5.6 billion** in one-off costs, "provided their level of ambition is maintained".
- **Evidence gathering:** 49 implementation dialogues, over 60 reality checks, more than 1,000 stakeholders over the year.
- **Enforcement (the reporting period):** 874 infringement procedures opened and 499 closed; close to 2,000 cases pending in all; 332 pre-infringement dialogues opened, with a 74% resolution rate. The Commission calls this the highest number of infringement procedures in a decade.
- **Gold-plating:** a Pact against gold-plating (announced in the 2026 State of the Union) and a "review and remove" mechanism in the European Semester; a transposition-roadmap IT tool already used for 37 directives.
- **What was NOT read:** the report's annexes and its per-act burden tables; figures above are from the Commission's Q&A (QANDA/26/2331), checked against the report's opening pages.
