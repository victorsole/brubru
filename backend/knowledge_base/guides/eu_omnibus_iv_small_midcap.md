# Omnibus IV — Small Mid-Cap (SMC) simplification package

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Monday 7 September 2026 -- JURI VOTES THE LEGAL BASIS OF THE THIRD INSTRUMENT)**: Brubru's EP eMeeting store holds a **final voting list** on the **JURI** agenda of 7 September 2026, Item 19, titled *"Voting list_Legal basis_Omnibus IV_IMCO"*, for **2025/0133(COD)**. A legal-basis vote in JURI is a **Rule 40 opinion on the correct Treaty basis**, not a vote on the substance; it can change which committee leads and which procedure applies. **IMCO** is the lead committee on that file and reached a **provisional (trilogue) agreement on 14 July 2026** (Coreper letter confirming the provisional agreement, in our store). Source: `ep_emeeting_documents`, JURI 2026-09-07 and IMCO 2026-07-14.
- **LATEST (Thursday 2 July 2026 — provisional agreement reached)**: The Omnibus IV Small Mid-Cap package reached **provisional (trilogue) agreement**. In the European Parliament it was handled by two joint committees on 2 July 2026: **CJ58 (ECON + ENVI + LIBE)** confirmed the provisional agreement on the **Regulation 2025/0130(COD)** (voting list "VL-Provisional Agreement", agreed compromise text, EP offer letter), and **CJ12 (ECON + JURI)** held a **voting-list reconsultation after trilogues** on the **Directive 2025/0131(COD)**. The package now heads to formal committee/plenary confirmation and Council adoption. Source: EP eMeeting CJ58(2026)0702_1 + CJ12(2026)0702_1, 2 July 2026.
- **What it is**: the **fourth "omnibus" simplification package** of the von der Leyen II Commission's competitiveness/burden-reduction agenda (Draghi Report follow-through). Its signature move is creating a new **"Small Mid-Cap" (SMC)** enterprise category — companies **larger than SMEs but below the large-cap threshold** — so that SME-style regulatory reliefs and proportionality can be extended to them across multiple sectoral instruments.
- **Three instruments** (all three adopted by the Commission on **21 May 2025**):
  - **Regulation 2025/0130(COD)** — COM(2025) 501; C10-0092/2025. Amends, among others, **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)**, **Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 (Prospectus Regulation)**, and the trade-defence Regulations **(EU) 2016/1036** (anti-dumping) and **(EU) 2016/1037** (anti-subsidy), plus further horizontal simplifying measures.
  - **Directive 2025/0131(COD)** — COM(2025) 502; C10-0093/2025. Amends **Directive 2014/65/EU (MiFID II)** and **Directive (EU) 2022/2557 (Critical Entities Resilience, CER)** as regards the extension of certain mitigating/proportionality measures to small mid-caps.
  - **Directive 2025/0133(COD)** -- COM(2025) 503, 21 May 2025; SWD(2025) 130. Amends **thirteen product directives** -- 2000/14/EC (outdoor noise), 2011/65/EU (RoHS), 2013/53/EU (recreational craft), 2014/29/EU (simple pressure vessels), 2014/30/EU (EMC), 2014/31/EU (non-automatic weighing), 2014/32/EU (measuring instruments), 2014/33/EU (lifts), 2014/34/EU (ATEX), 2014/35/EU (low voltage), 2014/53/EU (radio equipment), 2014/68/EU (pressure equipment) and 2014/90/EU (marine equipment) -- **as regards digitalisation and common specifications**. Stated aim: the Commission's 25% reporting-burden reduction target from the *Long-term competitiveness of the EU: looking beyond 2030* communication, applied through a "digital by default" approach (digital instructions and digital declarations of conformity in place of paper). **Lead committee: IMCO** (not ECON). Compromise amendments 26 January 2026; **provisional agreement 14 July 2026**; **JURI legal-basis vote 7 September 2026**.
- **EP handling**: split across committees. On the two ECON-anchored files it is a joint-committee procedure -- **ENVI + LIBE** joined on the Regulation (code CJ58), **JURI** joined on the Directive 2025/0131 (code CJ12). The **third file, 2025/0133(COD), is led by IMCO** with **JURI giving a legal-basis opinion** (7 September 2026). Anyone answering "which committee handles Omnibus IV" must name IMCO as well as ECON.
- **Council configuration**: COMPET / ECOFIN depending on the file.
- **Objective**: reduce compliance costs for mid-sized companies (data protection record-keeping, prospectus disclosure, market-conduct rules) by giving the SMC category a defined legal status and proportionate obligations — a core plank of the Commission's simplification and competitiveness drive.
- **Distinct from**: the **Savings and Investment Union (SIU)** legislative package (2025/0381-0383, see `savings_and_investment_union`) — different files, different objective (capital-markets integration vs. corporate simplification), even though both were in ECON on 2 July 2026. Also distinct from the **Digital Omnibus** (`digital_omnibus_package`) and the chemicals/automotive/food-feed omnibuses.
- **Cross-links**: `savings_and_investment_union`, `digital_omnibus_package`, `gdpr_data_protection`, `eu_financial_markets_mifid`, `cbam_carbon_border_adjustment_mechanism`.

## Why it matters for advocacy
- Creating a defined **Small Mid-Cap** category is a structural change: any firm above the SME ceiling but below large-cap could gain access to lighter-touch obligations across GDPR record-keeping, prospectus disclosure, and MiFID II conduct rules.
- The GDPR amendment inside a competitiveness omnibus is politically sensitive — data-protection advocates watch for any weakening of the record-of-processing and DPO thresholds; industry watches for the cost relief.
- Provisional agreement on 2 July 2026 means the substantive negotiation is settled; the window to influence is now Council formal adoption and any delegated/implementing acts.
