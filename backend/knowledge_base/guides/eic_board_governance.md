# EIC Board, Programme Managers + Governance

## QUICK FACTS
- The **EIC Board** is the strategic advisory body established by the Horizon Europe legislation (Regulation EU 2021/695) to advise on the EIC strategy and work programme.
- Composition: **20 members** — independent experts (innovators, investors, academics, scaleup-stage founders, R&I policy experts).
- Members are appointed by the European Commission via **open call for expressions of interest**.
- Last appointment round: **December 2025** — 15 new members + 5 returning members.
- Term length: typically 2-year mandates, renewable once.
- Chair: **Michiel Scheffer** since **1 June 2023**.
- Frequency: **5-6 plenary meetings per year**, plus working groups.
- Transparency Register: published as expert group **ID 3823**, with minutes + agendas available.
- The EIC Board is DISTINCT from the **EIC Fund Board** (the Luxembourg-SICAV governance body for the equity vehicle — see `eic_fund_equity_investment.md`).
- Historic predecessors:
  - 2016-2018: **High-Level Group of Innovators** (advised on designing the EIC concept)
  - 2018-2021 (until 31 May 2021): **EIC Pilot Advisory Board** (advised on the Horizon 2020 EIC pilot)
  - 2021- : the current EIC Board under Horizon Europe

## What the EIC Board does

Per Horizon Europe legislation, the Board advises on:
- **Overall strategy** for the EIC
- The **EIC Work Programme** (annual implementation document)
- The setting of **Pathfinder Challenges** and **Advanced Innovation Challenges**
- The selection methodology + jury composition
- Synergies with other EU funding instruments
- ESG + gender + widening priorities
- Position papers / statements on the future of the EIC

## EIC Programme Managers — the operational deeptech experts

A distinguishing feature of the EIC: **8 Programme Managers** appointed full-time for periods of up to **4 years** (renewable once), recruited from industry, academia, national labs, and federally-funded research centres.

### What Programme Managers do

Two primary responsibilities:

1. **Identify potential challenges** for emerging technologies and disruptive innovations:
   - Stakeholder engagement + strategic analysis
   - Design of **Pathfinder Challenges** + **Advanced Innovation Challenges (AIC)**
   - Awareness creation among applicants
   - Co-selection of best projects with external evaluators

2. **Pro-active management** of selected portfolios + projects:
   - Develop shared technology roadmaps
   - Broker opportunities to reach market (introductions, customer access)
   - Connect projects with Business Acceleration Services, investors, partners

### How they differ from project officers

Standard EISMEA project officers handle **administrative grant management** (reporting, payments, amendments). Programme Managers are **technology champions** who actively shape portfolios from a strategic + commercial perspective. They participate in jury interviews and recommend portfolio interventions.

## EISMEA — the executing agency

- **EISMEA** = European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency
- Brussels-based; established 2021.
- Responsible for **operational implementation**: publishing calls, running evaluations, managing grants, payments, audits, communications, the EIC Forum + Community.
- Reports to the European Commission (lead DGs: DG RTD + DG GROW).
- Director: **Jean-David Malo** (since 2021).

## How decisions cascade — who decides what

```
European Commission (DG RTD + DG GROW)
   ├── Adopts the annual EIC Work Programme (Commission Implementing Decision)
   ├── Allocates the annual budget
   ├── Appoints the EIC Board, EIC Fund Board members
   └── Approves the strategic plan
       │
       ▼
EIC Board (20 members, advisory)
   ├── Advises on the WP, calls, challenges, synergies
   └── Publishes statements + position papers
       │
       ▼
EISMEA (executing agency)
   ├── Publishes calls on the F&T Portal
   ├── Manages submission + evaluation logistics
   ├── Manages grant agreements + amendments
   └── Pays beneficiaries
       │
       ▼
EIC Programme Managers (8)
   ├── Design Pathfinder Challenges + AIC challenges
   ├── Select expert evaluators
   ├── Participate in jury interviews
   └── Pro-actively manage portfolios

EIC Fund Board (Luxembourg SICAV governance)
   ├── Approves investment strategy
   ├── Oversees the Fund Manager
   └── Reports to the Commission via DG RTD

EIB Group (investment advisor)
   ├── Conducts DD on each investment
   ├── Recommends investments to the Fund Board
   └── Acts as investor of record
```
