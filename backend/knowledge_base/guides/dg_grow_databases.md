# DG GROW Databases for Single Market Compliance

QUICK FACTS:
- Full name: DG Internal Market, Industry, Entrepreneurship and SMEs (DG GROW)
- Portal: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/tools-databases_en
- Scope: Product safety, conformity assessment, technical regulations, trade barriers, industrial ecosystems
- Key databases: NANDO (notified bodies), TRIS (technical regulations), TBT (trade barriers), EMI (industrial ecosystems)
- Relevance: Public procurement, market access, CE marking, product compliance, SME support

## NANDO - Notified Bodies Database

NANDO (New Approach Notified and Designated Organisations) lists all conformity assessment bodies designated by EU/EEA countries. Now hosted on the Single Market Compliance Space.

**What it covers:**
- Notified bodies that assess product conformity before EU market placement
- Covers 20+ EU harmonisation directives (Construction Products, Machinery, Medical Devices, PPE, Low Voltage, EMC, ATEX, Pressure Equipment, Radio Equipment, Toys Safety, etc.)
- Each body has a 4-digit identification number
- Bodies can be searched by directive, country, product area, or body number

**Why it matters for procurement:**
- Tenders for products in regulated sectors may require CE marking via a notified body
- Bidders need to know which conformity assessment bodies are available in the buyer's country
- Relevant directives include: Medical Devices Regulation (2017/745), Construction Products (305/2011), Machinery (2006/42/EC), PPE (2016/425), Lifts (2014/33/EU)

**URL:** https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/single-market-compliance-space/

## TRIS - Technical Regulation Information System

TRIS manages notifications under Directive (EU) 2015/1535 (the transparency procedure). When an EU member state drafts a new technical regulation, it must notify the Commission. Other member states and the Commission can comment or issue detailed opinions.

**What it covers:**
- Draft national technical regulations from all EU/EEA countries
- 3-month standstill period during which adoption is suspended
- Comments from other member states and detailed opinions from the Commission
- Product sectors: construction, food, transport, electrical, chemical, energy, safety, environmental

**Why it matters for procurement:**
- New national regulations can change product requirements in specific sectors
- Regulations in standstill may affect tender specifications
- Cross-border bidders need to be aware of upcoming regulatory changes in the buyer's country

**URL:** https://technical-regulation-information-system.ec.europa.eu

## TBT - Technical Barriers to Trade

The TBT database tracks WTO Technical Barriers to Trade notifications and EU comments. TBT measures are technical regulations, standards, or conformity assessment procedures that may create obstacles to trade.

**What it covers:**
- EU TBT notifications to the WTO
- Third-country TBT notifications with EU comments
- Product areas: chemicals, pharmaceuticals, medical devices, automotive, energy, food, construction, textiles, electronics

**Why it matters for procurement:**
- TBT notifications signal potential trade barriers for cross-border procurement
- EU notifications may change procurement requirements
- Third-country barriers affect EU exporters and international tenders

**URL:** https://technical-barriers-trade.ec.europa.eu

## EMI - European Monitor of Industrial Ecosystems

EMI tracks green and digital transition across 14 EU industrial ecosystems, providing data on technology adoption, investments, skills, environmental performance, trade, and production.

**14 Industrial Ecosystems:**
1. Aerospace & Defence
2. Agri-food
3. Construction
4. Creative & Cultural Industries
5. Digital
6. Electronics
7. Energy Intensive Industries
8. Energy - Renewables
9. Health
10. Mobility, Transport & Automotive
11. Proximity, Social Economy & Civil Security
12. Retail
13. Textiles
14. Tourism

**Key indicators tracked:**
- Environmental: GHG emissions, water use, materials extraction, biodiversity
- Digital: Digital intensity index, technology adoption rates
- Investment: Venture capital, capital investment, EU R&D funding
- Skills: Skilled professionals availability, job advertisement trends
- Innovation: Patent applications, startup/scale-up activity

**Why it matters for procurement:**
- Provides sector health data for tender analysis
- Growing sectors may have more procurement opportunities
- Sectors in transition may have different compliance requirements

**URL:** https://monitor-industrial-ecosystems.ec.europa.eu

## Other DG GROW Tools

- **CosIng**: Cosmetic ingredients database under Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009
- **ICSMS**: Market surveillance communication system
- **Standardisation Requests (ENORM)**: Standardisation requests to ESOs
- **Regulated Professions Database**: Professional access requirements across EU
- **SME Self-Assessment Tool**: Determine SME qualification under Recommendation 2003/361/EC
- **SDG Library**: Single Digital Gateway interoperability requirements
