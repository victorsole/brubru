# EU-Mercosur Interim Trade Agreement: Provisional Application from 1 May 2026

## QUICK FACTS

- **STATUS (24 April 2026)**: **Provisional application begins 1 May 2026** -- DG TAXUD published guidance and confirmed the activation of the **Interim Trade Agreement (ITA)** between the EU and Mercosur (Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay). Brubru users should distinguish carefully between (1) the political agreement reached December 2024, (2) the bilateral safeguard regulation Reg. (EU) 2026/687 (CELEX 32026R0687, published 19 March 2026), and (3) the **provisional application of the Interim Trade Agreement from 1 May 2026** -- which is what changes operational reality for traders. Full ratification of the EU-Mercosur Partnership Agreement (EMPA) by all Member State parliaments remains pending and may take several years.
- **CELEX reference**: 22026A00184 (Council Decision authorising signature and provisional application of the Interim Trade Agreement, EUR-Lex link below)
- **Trading bloc covered**: 27 EU Member States + Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay -- combined market of approximately 700 million people
- **Responsible DG**: DG TAXUD (Taxation and Customs Union) for customs implementation; DG TRADE for policy coordination; DG AGRI for agricultural-import safeguards
- **Responsible Commissioners**: Maros Sefcovic (Trade and Economic Security); Wopke Hoekstra (Customs)
- **EP lead committee**: INTA (International Trade); opinion: AGRI, DEVE, ENVI, AFET
- **Council configuration**: FAC (Trade format)
- **Legal basis**: Article 207 TFEU (Common Commercial Policy), Article 218(5) TFEU (signing + provisional application), Article 218(6) TFEU (ratification consent)
- **Architecture**: Two-tier deal: (1) **EMPA** -- full Partnership Agreement (political + cooperation + trade pillars) requiring all 27 MS parliaments to ratify; (2) **Interim Trade Agreement (ITA)** -- trade-only chapters extracted to allow provisional application before EMPA ratification
- **DG TAXUD guidance page**: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/provisional-application-eu-mercosur-interim-trade-agreement-begins-1-may-2026-2026-04-24_en
- **Origin rules**: Chapter 3 of the ITA covers Rules of Origin and Origin Procedures + annexes + appendices. Goods must comply with Chapter 3 to claim preferential treatment. Goods "in transit" or held in "temporary storage" on 1 May 2026 are eligible if importers file **origin statements within six months**.
- **Practical tools for traders**: **Access2Markets** platform + **ROSA** (Rules of Origin Self-Assessment tool) -- both at trade.ec.europa.eu
- **Bilateral safeguard regulation**: Regulation (EU) 2026/687, CELEX 32026R0687, OJ 19 March 2026 -- enables EU to suspend tariff preferences or reintroduce duties on Mercosur agricultural imports causing serious injury to EU producers. Applies to BOTH EMPA and ITA.
- **Sensitive sectors**: Agricultural quotas (beef, sugar, ethanol, poultry, rice, eggs, honey, garlic), automotive tariffs, ROO for textiles + leather, sustainability + Paris Agreement clauses, dispute settlement
- **Why provisional application now?**: The Commission and Council pushed the ITA carve-out to provide European exporters early access to Mercosur markets ahead of full EMPA ratification (which faces resistance from FR + AT + IE + PL on agriculture and deforestation grounds)
- **Negotiation timeline**: Started 1999, reached political agreement June 2019, blocked, reactivated 2023, **closed politically December 2024**, signed 2025-2026, provisional application of ITA from **1 May 2026**

## Overview

The EU-Mercosur trade relationship has been negotiated for over **25 years** -- the longest-running EU trade negotiation in history. After multiple rounds, blocking moments and reactivations, the political agreement was closed in December 2024 between Commission President von der Leyen and the four Mercosur heads of state (Lula, Milei, Lacalle Pou, Pena).

The two-tier architecture splits the deal into:

1. **EMPA (EU-Mercosur Partnership Agreement)** -- full deal covering political dialogue, cooperation, trade. Requires unanimous Council + EP consent + ratification by all 27 EU MS parliaments. Long horizon (likely 2027-2030 ratification).
2. **ITA (Interim Trade Agreement)** -- trade-only chapters peeled off to allow earlier provisional application. EU side: Council Decision authorising signature + provisional application + EP consent. Mercosur side: parallel domestic procedures.

**Provisional application from 1 May 2026** means EU and Mercosur tariffs on covered goods start to drop. Importers and exporters can claim preferential origin under Chapter 3 from that date forward.

## What Changes on 1 May 2026

| Element | Change |
|---------|--------|
| Tariffs | Phased reductions begin on most goods; some immediate to zero, others on staircase 4/7/10/15 years |
| Quotas | Agricultural quotas open: beef (99,000 t), sugar (180,000 t), ethanol (650,000 t), poultry (180,000 t) etc. |
| Origin rules | Chapter 3 ITA applies; importers file origin statements within 6 months for in-transit goods |
| Safeguards | Reg. 2026/687 active in parallel -- EU can suspend preferences for ag products on serious-injury grounds |
| Customs | Access2Markets + ROSA tools available; Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) status crucial |
| Sustainability | Trade and Sustainable Development chapter applies; Paris Agreement compliance clause active |
| Dispute settlement | Government-to-government panel system available for ITA breaches |

## Sensitive Sectors

| Sector | EU sensitivity | Mercosur sensitivity |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Beef | EU producers worried about hormone-free + deforestation-linked imports; Reg. 2026/687 safeguard | Brazil + Argentina want full quota access |
| Sugar / ethanol | Spain + France pressure on quota cap; bioethanol blending mandates | Brazil large producer |
| Automotive | Mercosur protects local assemblers; phased EU import liberalisation | Argentina + Brazil tariffs drop slowly |
| Textiles + leather | EU footwear + leather under origin-rules pressure | Mercosur leather (Brazil) seeking access |
| Pharma | EU exporters benefit; data-protection chapter | Mercosur public-health flexibilities |
| Geographical Indications | EU protects 350+ GIs (Champagne, Parmigiano, Rioja, etc.) | Mercosur cheese + wine exporters concerned |
| Public procurement | EU access to Mercosur tenders | Limited; politically sensitive |

## Political Context

- **France**: Long opposition driven by farming lobby (FNSEA) + deforestation concerns. Macron sought Council renegotiation; eventually accepted with Reg. 2026/687 safeguard + Mercosur deforestation declaration
- **Austria, Ireland, Poland**: Sceptical, focus on agriculture
- **Spain, Portugal, Germany, Netherlands, Italy**: Strong supporters (manufacturing exports, Latin America links)
- **Brazil**: Lula presidency provides green credibility (Amazon protection commitments); domestic agri-business pressure
- **Argentina**: Milei backed deal; Mercosur constraint with Brazil
- **Civil society**: NGOs (FoE Europe, Greenpeace, Mercosur peasant movements) maintain opposition
