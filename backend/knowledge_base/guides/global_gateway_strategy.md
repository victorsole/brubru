- **NDICI / External Action Guarantee efficiency reform (29 April 2026, OJ L 4 May 2026)**: **Regulation (EU) 2026/995** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2026 amending Regulation (EU) 2021/947 (NDICI - Global Europe) "as regards increased efficiency of the External Action Guarantee". CELEX: 32026R0995. URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/AUTO/?uri=OJ:L_202600995. The External Action Guarantee (EAG) is the EUR 53.4 billion risk-coverage layer of NDICI that backs European Fund for Sustainable Development Plus (EFSD+) blended-finance and guarantee operations -- the principal financial machinery behind Global Gateway's EUR 300 billion mobilisation target. The 2026/995 amendment streamlines EAG governance: (a) faster decision-making on guarantee allocations to implementing partners (EIB, EBRD, national DFIs); (b) simplified reporting / monitoring obligations; (c) clearer accountability split between Commission, EEAS and implementing partners; (d) improved coordination with the Global Gateway pipeline. Most substantive NDICI-Global Europe change since the instrument's 2021 launch. Implementation acts expected Q3 2026. Important for partners pursuing Global Gateway-aligned investment proposals: faster guarantee decisions = shorter project pipeline timelines.

## Overview

Global Gateway is the EU's strategy for boosting smart, clean, and secure infrastructure investments in developing and emerging countries. It aims to mobilise EUR 300 billion between 2021 and 2027 through a "Team Europe" approach combining EU institutions, Member States, development finance institutions, and the private sector.

The strategy is widely seen as the EU's geopolitical answer to China's Belt and Road Initiative, offering partner countries a values-based alternative with high standards on transparency, sustainability, and governance.

## Investment Pillars

| Pillar | Focus Areas |
|--------|-------------|
| Digital | Fibre optic cables, data centres, 5G, digital skills |
| Climate and energy | Renewable energy, green hydrogen, power grids |
| Transport | Railways, ports, roads with sustainability standards |
| Health | Vaccine manufacturing, pharmaceutical supply chains |
| Education and research | Higher education partnerships, Erasmus+ international |

## Key Projects and Partnerships

- **Africa-Europe Investment Package**: EUR 150 billion target
- **EU-Latin America Global Gateway Investment Agenda** (July 2023)
- **EU-ASEAN connectivity**: digital and green corridors
- **Trans-Caspian corridor**: connecting Central Asia to Europe
- **Lobito Corridor** (Angola-Zambia-DRC): strategic minerals transport

## EP Assessment (March 2026)

The EP report on "Global Gateway -- past impacts and future orientation" (2025/2073(INI)) examines:
- Effectiveness of investments delivered so far
- Coordination with Member State bilateral programmes
- Visibility and branding challenges vs BRI
- Alignment with EU development policy objectives (SDGs)
- Future orientation beyond 2027 (next MFF)

Rapporteurs: Chloe Ridel (S&D) and Hildegard Bentele (EPP). Debate scheduled for 26 March 2026 in Brussels plenary.
