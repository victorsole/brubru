# European Business Wallets Regulation, 2025/0358(COD): trilogue negotiations opened on 14 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **The proposal:** Commission proposal **COM(2025) 838** of **19 November 2025** (CELEX 52025PC0838), establishing **European Business Wallets**, which the Commissioner describes as letting European businesses communicate with public administrations and handle administrative paperwork "in a fully digital manner". Commissioner responsible: **Henna Virkkunen** (DG CONNECT).
- **Procedure:** **2025/0358(COD)**, ordinary legislative procedure, first reading.
- **Parliament:** lead committee **ITRE**, rapporteur **Eero Heinäluoma (S&D)**, appointed 18 December 2025. Shadows: Marion Walsmann (EPP), Mélanie Disdier (PfE), Rihards Kols (ECR), Morten Løkkegaard (Renew), Damian Boeselager (Greens/EFA). Opinions: **IMCO** (rapporteur Veronika Cifrová Ostrihoňová, Renew) and **JURI** (Axel Voss, EPP); LIBE and BUDG gave no opinion.
- **Parliament's steps (OEIL):** committee vote and decision to open negotiations **10 September 2026**; report **A10-0240/2026** tabled **23 September 2026**; the mandate was announced in plenary on **5 October** and confirmed on **7 October 2026** (Rule 72).
- **Trilogues opened on 14 October 2026:** Commissioner Virkkunen wrote on 15 October that negotiations with Parliament and Member States had started "yesterday". Source: her post on the Commission's Mastodon, not an institutional press release.
- **Council:** the date and content of the Council's negotiating mandate were **not found** in the sources read (OEIL lists no Council step). Do not invent one.
- **Status:** NOT adopted; first-reading negotiations under way. Other bodies: EESC opinion (18 March 2026), Committee of the Regions opinion (1 July 2026).
