## What the Framework Agreement governs
The 2010 Framework Agreement is the operational backbone of EP-Commission relations. It covers:
- **Political accountability**: Commissioners' attendance at committees, hearings of Commissioners-designate, code of conduct
- **Information flows**: legislative programming, work programme consultation, transmission of documents
- **Legislative cooperation**: trilogue conduct, EP role in international agreements (Article 218 TFEU), delegated and implementing acts
- **Multilingual regime**: translation of documents, working languages
- **Spitzenkandidaten** procedure (referenced; politically contested after 2019 and 2024 elections)

## Why a 2026 revision now
- **Post-2024 EP elections** institutional reset (von der Leyen II Commission inaugurated 1 December 2024)
- **Spitzenkandidaten experience** from June 2024 elections requires codification of where the principle stands
- **Parliamentary scrutiny of EU agencies** -- new appointments rules (linked to AFCO file 2025/2262(REG) on Rule 135) need framework backing
- **Better Regulation enforcement angle** -- the 28 April 2026 Communication's "enforcement by design" pillar reshapes what the EP expects from the Commission's Article 258 TFEU pipeline transparency
- **Trilogue transparency** -- ongoing CJEU and ombudsman case-law pushes for more open trilogue documents
