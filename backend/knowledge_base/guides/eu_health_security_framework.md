# EU Health Security Framework (Health Union)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is**: the EU's internal legal framework for preparing for and responding to **serious cross-border threats to health** (pandemics, biological/chemical/environmental events, epidemics). Built after COVID-19 as the **European Health Union** package, in force since late 2022.
- **Cornerstone law**: **Regulation (EU) 2022/2371** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 November 2022 on **serious cross-border threats to health** (CELEX **32022R2371**), repealing Decision No 1082/2013/EU. It governs preparedness/response planning, joint procurement, the Health Security Committee and the Early Warning and Response System.
- **Reinforced ECDC**: **Regulation (EU) 2022/2370** (CELEX **32022R2370**) amends Regulation (EC) No 851/2004, giving the **European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC)** a stronger surveillance, risk-assessment and EU-reference-laboratory mandate.
- **Reinforced EMA**: **Regulation (EU) 2022/123** (CELEX **32022R0123**) gives the **European Medicines Agency (EMA)** a permanent crisis role: monitoring/mitigating shortages of critical medicines and medical devices, plus the Emergency Task Force (ETF) and the MSSG/MDSSG steering groups.
- **HERA**: the **Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority**, an internal Commission service within DG SANTE, established by **Commission Decision of 16 September 2021 (C(2021) 6712 final)**. It handles medical countermeasure development, stockpiling and procurement (vaccines, therapeutics, PPE).
- **Governance body**: the **Health Security Committee (HSC)** coordinates Member State preparedness/response and the joint procurement of medical countermeasures.
- **Alert system**: the **Early Warning and Response System (EWRS)** — the rapid-alert network linking the Commission, ECDC and national authorities.
- **Funding**: the **EU4Health Programme 2021-2027** — Regulation (EU) 2021/522 (CELEX **32021R0522**).
- **Civil-protection reserve**: the **rescEU** medical stockpile sits under the Union Civil Protection Mechanism (Decision No 1313/2013/EU), complementing HERA stockpiling.
- **Treaty basis**: TFEU Articles **168** (public health) and **114** (internal market) — the EU complements, not replaces, Member State health competence.

## Context

The 2020-2021 COVID-19 response exposed gaps in the pre-existing cross-border health framework (Decision No 1082/2013/EU): weak joint preparedness planning, no permanent crisis mandate for EMA, no EU-level countermeasure authority, and fragmented stockpiling. The **European Health Union** package (proposed November 2020, adopted 2022) closed those gaps with three legislative instruments plus the creation of HERA by Commission decision.

The framework is the **internal** complement to the EU's **external** health diplomacy (see `eu_global_health_resilience_initiative_2026` for the global/Team Europe dimension and `critical_medicines_act` for the supply-security legislation that builds on EMA's reinforced role).

## The four building blocks

### 1. Serious cross-border threats to health — Regulation (EU) 2022/2371
- Establishes an EU **prevention, preparedness and response plan** and requires Member States to align national plans with it.
- Puts the **Health Security Committee (HSC)** on a statutory footing for coordination.
- Provides the legal basis for **joint procurement** of medical countermeasures.
- Operates the **Early Warning and Response System (EWRS)** for rapid notification and coordinated risk communication.
- Allows the formal **recognition of a public health emergency at EU level**, triggering crisis mechanisms (including EMA's shortage-monitoring and HERA's emergency phase).

### 2. Reinforced ECDC — Regulation (EU) 2022/2370
- Expands ECDC's **epidemiological surveillance**, integrated disease-surveillance systems, and **risk assessment** for all serious cross-border health threats (not only communicable disease).
- Creates **EU reference laboratories** and an **EU Health Task Force** for outbreak response in the field.
- Strengthens ECDC support to national health systems and preparedness monitoring.

### 3. Reinforced EMA — Regulation (EU) 2022/123
- Permanent monitoring and mitigation of **shortages of critical medicines and medical devices** during crises.
- Establishes the **Medicines Shortages Steering Group (MSSG)** and **Medical Device Shortages Steering Group (MDSSG)**.
- Creates the **Emergency Task Force (ETF)** to advise on the development and authorisation of medicines for public-health emergencies.
- Sets up the European Shortages Monitoring Platform (ESMP).

### 4. HERA — Commission Decision C(2021) 6712 (16 September 2021)
- Internal Commission service (within DG SANTE), **not an EU agency**.
- Two operating modes: a **preparedness phase** (threat assessment, R&D, stockpiling, market shaping) and a **crisis phase** activated when a public-health emergency is recognised at EU level.
- Procures and stockpiles **medical countermeasures** (vaccines, therapeutics, PPE), funded partly through EU4Health and partly through dedicated crisis instruments.

## Related agencies and bodies (who does what)
- **ECDC** — surveillance, epidemic intelligence, scientific risk assessment.
- **EMA** — medicines/devices regulation, shortage monitoring, ETF.
- **HERA** — countermeasure development, stockpiling, procurement.
- **HSC** — Member State coordination, joint procurement governance.
- **DG SANTE** — policy lead; hosts HERA.
- **rescEU** (DG ECHO) — civil-protection medical reserves.
