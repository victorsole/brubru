# Innovative Health Initiative Joint Undertaking (IHI), successor to IMI/IMI2

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** Innovative Health Initiative Joint Undertaking (IHI JU), an EU public-private partnership co-funding cross-sector, precompetitive health research and innovation under Horizon Europe.
- **Legal basis:** Council Regulation (EU) 2021/2085 of 19 November 2021, the "Single Basic Act" establishing nine Joint Undertakings under Horizon Europe (CELEX 32021R2085). IHI is one of the nine.
- **Lineage:** Innovative Medicines Initiative (IMI), 2008-2013 (FP7) -> IMI 2, 2014-2020 (Horizon 2020) -> IHI, 2021-2031 (Horizon Europe). The IHI Programme Office continues to manage and promote legacy IMI/IMI2 projects.
- **Legal life:** Established as an EU body until 31 December 2031 (per Regulation 2021/2085), to allow ongoing projects launched during the 2021-2027 Horizon Europe funding period to run to completion and be wound down properly.
- **Budget:** EUR 2.4 billion total for 2021-2027. Roughly half (EUR 1.2 billion) is EU cash from Horizon Europe. The other half is industry: EUR 1 billion committed by IHI's founding industry partners (mostly in-kind contributions) plus up to EUR 200 million from other life-science organisations that join as Contributing Partners.
- **Members (industry side):** Five industry associations across four "constituent members": EFPIA (European Federation of Pharmaceutical Industries and Associations, including Vaccines Europe), MedTech Europe (medical technology industry), EuropaBio (biotechnology industry), and COCIR (medical imaging, radiotherapy, health ICT and electromedical industries). Sources sometimes count these as "five sectors" because Vaccines Europe is listed separately from EFPIA despite sitting under it.
- **Public partner:** The European Union, represented by the European Commission.
- **Executive Director:** Dr Niklas Blomberg. Staff: around 55. Location: Brussels.
- **Mission:** Translate health research and innovation into real benefits for patients and society; move the paradigm from "disease care" to "health care" (prevention, diagnosis, treatment, disease management) by integrating pharmaceutical, medical device, diagnostics, digital health and biotech sectors that traditionally work in silos.
- **Distinguish from:**
  - **Critical Medicines Alliance / Critical Medicines Act** -- a separate EU instrument on securing supply and reducing API import dependency (China/India), not a research funder. See `pharma_sector_regulatory_landscape.md`.
  - **EU4Health** -- a separate EU funding programme for health system resilience, crisis preparedness and public health, not primarily an R&I partnership. See `eu4health_2027_priorities.md`.
  - **Horizon Europe** itself -- IHI is one delivery mechanism (a Joint Undertaking / institutionalised European Partnership) inside Horizon Europe, not the whole programme.
- **Client relevance:** IHI is the only EU instrument that co-funds cross-sector (pharma + medtech + diagnostics + digital health + vaccines) precompetitive health R&I. EFPIA (a Brubru client) is a founding constituent member and the largest single industry contributor by association membership.
- **Key URLs:** `https://www.ihi.europa.eu/` (homepage); `https://www.ihi.europa.eu/about-ihi/mission-and-objectives` (mission and legal objectives); EUR-Lex CELEX 32021R2085 (founding regulation).

## What IHI does

IHI funds collaborative research projects that bring together health-related industries, universities, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), patient organisations and regulatory bodies. It runs competitive calls for proposals (Horizon Europe-style, two-stage or single-stage depending on the call) where the topics are shaped jointly by the Commission and the industry associations through IHI's governance structure, then refined via a public "Ideas Incubator" where anyone can suggest topics.

As of mid-2026, IHI has run thirteen calls for proposals. Call 13 (announced 2 July 2026) is worth EUR 84.8 million and covers immune diseases in the elderly, a network for health innovators, and the "3Rs" agenda (Replace, Reduce, Refine the use of animals in drug safety studies). The annual IHI Forum brings together the partnership's stakeholder community; IHI Forum 2026 is scheduled for 20-21 October in Brussels.

## Legal objectives (from Regulation (EU) 2021/2085)

IHI's objectives are set out directly in the founding legislation, not merely in a strategy document. This matters for advocacy: the legal objectives are binding constraints on what IHI can fund and how it reports impact.

**General objectives** (shared framing with all nine Joint Undertakings, applied to health):
1. Turn health research and innovation into real benefits for patients and society.
2. Deliver safe, effective health innovations covering the full spectrum of care, from prevention through diagnosis to treatment, particularly where there is unmet public health need.
3. Make Europe's health industries globally competitive.

**Specific objectives** (detailed further in the Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda, SRIA):
1. Improve understanding of the factors affecting health and the development and treatment of diseases.
2. Integrate fragmented health research and innovation efforts by bringing together health industry sectors and other stakeholders, enabling tools, data, platforms, technologies and processes that facilitate prevention, diagnosis, treatment and management of disease.
3. Demonstrate the feasibility of integrated healthcare solutions drawing on technologies from different sectors, designed around the needs of patients and healthcare professionals.
4. Make better use of opportunities to gather and use health data in research and care, while respecting privacy legislation (GDPR, and increasingly the European Health Data Space framework).
5. Develop methods for assessing the value of innovative, integrated health care solutions to patients, carers, healthcare professionals, organisations and other stakeholders (this is the objective most relevant to regulatory science and Health Technology Assessment work).

**Operational objectives** include increasing patient and citizen involvement in health innovation, and improving skills for cross-sectoral health innovation.

**Cross-cutting Joint Undertaking objectives** (shared with the other eight JUs established by the same regulation): boosting EU competitiveness, contributing to the EU's response to global challenges, accelerating the green and digital transitions, strengthening scientific excellence, and stimulating research and innovation in SMEs.

## Research priorities

IHI's Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda (SRIA) translates the legal objectives into thematic and cross-cutting priority areas. Historically and in recent calls these cluster around:
- Cardiometabolic disease
- Neurodegenerative disease (including dementia, a major legacy theme from IMI)
- Oncology
- Infectious disease and antimicrobial resistance
- Mental health
- Rare diseases
- Diseases of ageing and immune-system decline (Call 13, 2026)
- Cross-cutting digital health, AI and data platform themes that apply across disease areas

Choice of future thematic areas depends on disease burden (severity and number of people affected), economic impact, and the potential for cross-sectoral transformation of the innovation process itself (e.g. platform trials, animal-free testing methods).

## Policy contribution

IHI is designed to feed several other EU policy tracks, not to operate in isolation:
- **Horizon Europe** (the umbrella programme IHI sits inside)
- **Europe's Beating Cancer Plan**
- The Commission's **Industrial Strategy for Europe**
- The **Pharmaceutical Strategy for Europe** (the political framework behind the 2026 Pharma Package reform, see `eu_pharmaceutical_legislation_reform.md`)
- The **European Health Data Space** (data platforms and interoperability work funded by IHI projects feed EHDS implementation, see `european_health_data_space.md`)
- **UN Sustainable Development Goal 3** (healthy lives and well-being for all ages)

IHI's specific objective on assessing the value of integrated health solutions (objective 5 above) is the natural link to regulatory science: projects generating real-world evidence, biomarkers, and outcome-measurement frameworks are intended to be usable in EMA scientific advice procedures and in national/EU Health Technology Assessment processes under the HTA Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2021/2282, joint clinical assessments). See `ema_scientific_committees.md` and `medical_devices_regulation.md` for the regulatory bodies these outputs are meant to reach; Brubru does not have confirmation of a formal standing IHI-EMA Innovation Task Force liaison mechanism beyond this general policy-contribution framing, so this should be treated as directional, not an institutionalised channel.

## IMI legacy and continuity

IHI did not start from zero. It inherited the governance, secretariat (the "IHI Programme Office"), and an active portfolio from the Innovative Medicines Initiative. Notable IMI-era legacy projects still referenced in IHI communications include:
- **EHDEN** (European Health Data & Evidence Network) -- federated real-world data network using the OMOP common data model.
- **EU-PEARL** (EU Patient Centric Clinical Trial Platforms) -- generic framework and tools for patient-centric platform trials, active since 2019, 36 partner organisations.
- **RADAR-AD** -- remote monitoring technologies for functional decline in early Alzheimer's disease.

The IHI Programme Office continues to manage and promote projects launched under IMI even though new calls run under the IHI/Horizon Europe framework.

## Funding model detail

| Contribution source | Amount | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Horizon Europe (EU cash) | EUR 1.2 billion | Cash, matches project grants |
| IHI founding industry partners (EFPIA/Vaccines Europe, MedTech Europe, EuropaBio, COCIR) | EUR 1 billion | Mostly in-kind (R&D resources, personnel, data, infrastructure) |
| Contributing Partners (other life-science organisations that opt in) | Up to EUR 200 million | Cash or in-kind, additional to founding partners |
| **Total (2021-2027)** | **EUR 2.4 billion** | |

Most organisations established in the EU or in a country associated to Horizon Europe are eligible to receive IHI funding. Industry partner companies participating in IHI projects typically do not receive EU funding themselves; their contribution is the in-kind side of the match.

## Governance

- **Administrative Board:** joint EU/industry governance body, standard across all nine Horizon Europe Joint Undertakings established by Regulation (EU) 2021/2085.
- **Executive Director:** Dr Niklas Blomberg, responsible for day-to-day management and legal representation of the Joint Undertaking.
- **States Representatives Group:** advisory body of EU/associated-country representatives.
- **Science and Innovation Panel / Stakeholder Forum:** advisory input from the wider scientific and stakeholder community (patients, regulators, SMEs).

## For EFPIA and pharma industry stakeholders

IHI is the primary EU-level R&I co-funding channel through which EFPIA members can shape precompetitive research topics jointly with the Commission, ahead of formal calls, via the "Become a Contributing Partner" and "Ideas Incubator" mechanisms. Because IHI project results (data platforms, biomarkers, trial methodologies) are explicitly meant to feed the European Health Data Space and Health Technology Assessment / regulatory-science ecosystems, engagement with IHI call topic-setting is a lower-visibility but high-leverage advocacy channel compared with reacting to legislative proposals after publication. For the broader legislative and regulatory environment IHI-funded innovations will eventually have to navigate, see `eu_pharmaceutical_framework.md`, `eu_pharmaceutical_legislation_reform.md` and `pharma_sector_regulatory_landscape.md`.
