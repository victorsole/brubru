# EU AgRI 2040 Conference, Brussels, 24-25 September 2026: what took place, programme, speakers

## QUICK FACTS
- **It took place.** The DG AGRI event page (read 13 October 2026) says "This event has ended": **Thursday 24 September 2026, 08:30, to Friday 25 September 2026, 15:00 (CEST)**, at the **Charlemagne building, Rue de la Loi 170, 1000 Brussels**; language **English**; organiser **DG AGRI**; live streaming was available. Title: *Future-proofing EU Agri-Food through research and innovation*.
- **Purpose:** it introduced the recently adopted Communication COM(2026) 453 (AgRI 2040 and Food 2040). Per the event page, stakeholders "helped shape the priorities of the next Horizon Europe EU Research and Innovation Framework Programme and the future European Competitiveness Fund", co-identifying R&I priorities for agriculture, forestry and rural areas in **nine thematic breakout sessions** (see the breakout-sessions guide).
- **Day 1, 24 September, "Shaping up a strong EU research and innovation ecosystem":** master of ceremony journalist Sasha Twining; **09:30 high-level opening with keynote speeches by Commissioner Christophe Hansen (Agriculture and Food) and Commissioner Ekaterina Zaharieva (Startups, Research and Innovation)**; 10:00 panel on enabling the Vision for Agriculture and Food through R&I with **Elisabeth Werner** (Director-General DG AGRI), **James Morrison** (Director Healthy Planet, DG RTD) and Bill Callanan (Chief Agricultural Scientist, Ireland); 11:00 session on deployment, scaling-up and uptake: **Diego Canga Fano** (acting Deputy Director-General DG AGRI) presented the new strategic approach and AgRI 2040, then a panel with Richard Zaltzman (CEO, EIT Food), Adrián Ferrero (EU Mission Soil Board), Jan van Esch (Netherlands ministry) and Iulia Blagu (Romanian National Association of Women in Agriculture); 14:00-17:30 two rounds of parallel sessions (1-6); 17:40 reports; reception to 20:00.
- **Day 2, 25 September, "Partnering for new R&I opportunities in the EU and globally":** parallel sessions 7-9 from 09:00, reports 11:15; **11:30 panel on partnerships** moderated by Diego Canga Fano with FAO's Chief Agricultural Scientist (Charles Martin Spillane), CGIAR's Director (Anna Okello), the European Investment Bank (Jean-François De Saedeleer), the BIC Governance Board chair (Katariina Kemppainen), the coordinator of the Horizon Europe Agroecology partnership (Ulrike Ziegler), the Novo Nordisk Foundation (Mette Damgaard Nielsen) and Tereos (Valérie Corre); **closing remarks by James Morrison and Elisabeth Werner** at 12:45; end 15:00 after lunch.
- **Exhibition and pitch platform:** dozens of projects and initiatives (Climate Farm Demo, ClimateSmartAdvisors, EU-FarmBook, the Agroecology, FutureFoodS, AgData, EUPAHW and FOREST partnerships, EIT Food, the EIC, Mission Soil, the JRC) and 20 pitches during the breaks.
