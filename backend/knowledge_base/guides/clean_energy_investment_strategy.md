# Clean Energy Investment Strategy

QUICK FACTS:
- Reference: COM(2026) 116 final
- Adopted: 10 March 2026 (College of Commissioners)
- Commissioner: Teresa Ribera (EVP, Clean, Just and Competitive Transition) + Stephane Sejourne (EVP, Industrial Strategy)
- Part of: 2026 Energy Package (with SMR Strategy COM(2026) 117 and Citizens Energy Package COM(2026) 115)
- Investment gap: EUR 660 billion/year needed through 2030, EUR 695 billion/year for 2031-2040 (vs EUR 240 billion/year baseline 2011-2021)
- CEF-Energy budget: fivefold increase from EUR 5.84 billion to EUR 29.91 billion (proposed under 2028-2034 MFF)
- EIB commitment: over EUR 75 billion financing over next 3 years
- InvestEU mobilised: EUR 57 billion in energy investments to date
- Status: Communication (non-legislative), but triggers regulatory and budgetary follow-up
- Related guides: SMR Strategy (smr_strategy_nuclear.md), Citizens Energy Package (citizens_energy_package.md), EU Energy Policy (eu_energy_policy.md)

## Overview

The Clean Energy Investment Strategy sets out the Commission's framework for closing the massive investment gap in clean energy infrastructure. Europe needs to nearly triple annual clean energy investment from EUR 240 billion to EUR 660 billion by 2030 to meet its climate and energy targets. The strategy focuses on mobilising the EUR 33.7 trillion in privately managed assets in Europe, including EUR 12 trillion controlled by institutional investors (insurers and pension funds).

Treaty basis: Article 194 TFEU (energy policy, shared competence).

## 4 Actions

### Action 1: Strengthening Grid Operator Balance Sheets

| Instrument | Description | Scale |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Strategic Infrastructure Investment Fund (SII Fund) | EIB equity co-investment platform alongside infrastructure funds | Up to EUR 500 million anchor capital |
| Operator Securitisation Facility (OSF) | Converts regulated revenue streams into immediate liquidity via off-balance sheet structures | Commission + EIB |
| Hybrid Bonds | EIB purchases hybrid bonds from regulated utilities (treated as equity) | Balance sheet relief |

### Action 2: Loan Securitisation for Small Operators

- Free up bank lending capacity via securitisation of existing loan portfolios
- "Growth for Energy" operation by EIB to support local banking networks lending to small grid operators and renewable developers
- Target: smaller Distribution System Operators (DSOs) and community energy projects

### Action 3: De-risking Innovative Technologies

| Sub-action | Technologies | Instrument |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 3a: Next-gen commercialisation | Long-duration storage (LDES), floating wind, floating solar, ocean energy, airborne wind, agrivoltaics, bio-based renewables, CCS/CCUS, geothermal | Venture debt + equity via InvestEU |
| 3b: Nuclear/SMRs | Light-water SMRs, Advanced Modular Reactors (AMRs), Microreactors, fusion | EUR 200 million InvestEU top-up from Innovation Fund until 2028. See SMR Strategy guide |
| 3c: Deep-tech | Disruptive energy companies | Scale Up Europe Fund |
| 3d: Energy efficiency | Residential and SME retrofits | EUR 500 million pilot for "energy efficiency as a service" in 2026; InvestEU Sustainability Guarantee Product (EIF); Energy efficiency Accelerator under European Competitiveness Fund. See Citizens Energy Package guide |
| 3e: Coordination | Member State public financing synergies | Energy Union Task Force + Energy Transition Investment Council |

### Action 4: Energy Transition Investment Council

- New high-level body with investment community, institutional investors, Member States, Commission officials
- Builds on existing Investors Dialogue on Energy (established 2022)
- Sub-group with EIB, International Financial Institutions, National Promotional Banks
- First meeting: Q2 2026

## Connection to Other Energy Package Guides

- **SMR Strategy** (COM(2026) 117): Action 3b of this strategy directly funds SMR/AMR de-risking via EUR 200 million InvestEU top-up. See `smr_strategy_nuclear.md`
- **Citizens Energy Package** (COM(2026) 115): Action 3d funds energy efficiency for households/SMEs. The Citizens Package focuses on the demand side (lower bills, energy communities), while this strategy focuses on the supply side (investment, grids, generation). See `citizens_energy_package.md`
- **EU Energy Policy** (umbrella): This strategy sits within the broader Fit for 55, REPowerEU, and Clean Industrial Deal frameworks. See `eu_energy_policy.md`
