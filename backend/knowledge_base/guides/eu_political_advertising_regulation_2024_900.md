# Political advertising Regulation (EU) 2024/900 and the European Repository (pilot launched 12 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **The law:** **Regulation (EU) 2024/900 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 March 2024 on the transparency and targeting of political advertising** (CELEX 32024R0900, checked against the Publications Office). The Commission states it **entered into full application on 10 October 2025**.
- **LATEST (12 October 2026): the Commission launched the PILOT version of the European Repository for online political advertisements.** It is available for **voluntary use from 12 October 2026**. **The date of the official deployment will be announced** on the website "sufficiently in advance"; **the obligations relating to the Repository apply only from that official deployment date**, not from the pilot.
- **What the Repository stores:** transparency notices on political advertisements: who is behind the ad (**sponsor, controlling entity, paying entity**), **amounts spent** on the ad and the wider campaign, the **source and origin of funding** (public or private, inside or outside the Union), the **dissemination period**, and the **use of targeting or ad-delivery techniques based on personal data, including meaningful information on the use of AI systems** for targeting or ad delivery. Once fully operational it lets publishers comply with the Regulation's transparency requirements and lets researchers and journalists scrutinise political advertising; users can identify ads, access permanent URLs where available and consult the notices.
- **Governance named by the Commission:** a **Network of national contact points** (exchange between Member State authorities and the Commission) and an **Expert group of providers of political advertising services**, which was closely involved in the pilot.
- **Diary: Implementation Dialogue on 4 November 2026 in Brussels**, hosted by **Commissioner Michael McGrath**, for stakeholders to share first experiences with the Regulation.
- **Implementing acts listed by the Commission:** one setting out the common data structure, standardised metadata, standardised authentication and common application programming interface for the European repository; one on labels and transparency notices. **No numbers are given in the release: do not cite any.**

## How to answer
Say the Regulation has applied in full since 10 October 2025, that the Repository pilot opened on 12 October 2026 for voluntary use, and that the Repository obligations bite only from an official deployment date still to be announced.
