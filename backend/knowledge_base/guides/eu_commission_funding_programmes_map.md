# EU Commission Funding Programmes — Programme → Managing Body → Policy

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Monday 20 July 2026 — ERC WORK PROGRAMME 2027 ADOPTED, STARTING GRANT CALL OPENS 22 JULY):** the **ERC Work Programme 2027** was established by the ERC Scientific Council and **formally adopted by the Commission**, with an **estimated budget of EUR 2,810.75 million** (subject to adoption of the 2027 EU budget). Calls for every main frontier grant: **Starting, Consolidator, Advanced, Synergy and ERC Plus**, plus Proof of Concept with two deadlines. **The first competition, the ERC Starting Grant 2027 call, OPENS on 22 July 2026.** Key rule changes announced in advance: **broader eligibility windows for Starting and Consolidator Grants** and **limits on the number of proposals a researcher may submit under a single work programme** (readjusted reapplication rules). Source: ERCEA news, 20 July 2026 + the Work Programme 2027 PDF (erc.europa.eu). **Tenderator-relevant.**
- **What this is**: a map of the main EU funding programmes, **who manages each** (DG or executive agency) and **which policy** it serves. This is the universe behind Brubru's **Tenderator**.
- **Two delivery modes**: **direct management** (the Commission/agency runs the calls — e.g. Horizon Europe, CEF, LIFE, Erasmus+) and **shared management** (Member States run the money under Commission rules — e.g. cohesion funds, CAP). The Recovery & Resilience Facility is **performance-based** (paid on reform/investment milestones).
- **Where calls live**: direct-management calls are on the **EU Funding & Tenders portal (SEDIA)** — the source Tenderator ingests.
- **Source**: programme pages across the DG subdomains + agency `programmes_en` pages (read 31 May 2026). Budget figures are the published **2021–2027** envelopes; a new MFF (post-2027) is under negotiation.

## Recovery & cross-cutting
- **Recovery & Resilience Facility (RRF)** — performance-based, milestone-paid; coordinated by SG REFORM / ECFIN; the RRF Scoreboard tracks disbursement.
- **InvestEU** — budgetary guarantee mobilising private investment.
- **STEP** (Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform) — a label/booster across programmes for critical tech.

## Truthfulness note
Programme-to-agency assignments and budgets reflect the **2021–2027 MFF** as published; assignments can change with the next MFF. Where a call's exact managing entity matters operationally, confirm on the **Funding & Tenders portal** rather than relying on this map alone.
