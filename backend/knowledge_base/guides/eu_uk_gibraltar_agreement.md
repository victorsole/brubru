# The EU-UK Agreement in respect of Gibraltar

## QUICK FACTS

- **Full title**: *Agreement in respect of Gibraltar between the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, of the one part, and the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, of the other part.* Note the parties: **the EU AND Euratom** on one side. That is why the package needs three separate instruments rather than one.
- **Procedure**: **`2026/0058(NLE)`** — a non-legislative (NLE) file, **not** ordinary legislative procedure.
- **Legal basis**: **Article 218(6) TFEU** (confirmed on the OEIL procedure file). That means the **consent procedure**: Parliament votes yes or no on the whole text and **cannot amend it**. This is the single most important procedural fact about this file.
- **The three Commission documents, all tabled 17 February 2026**:
  | Reference | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | **COM(2026)0091** | Proposal for a Council Decision on the **signing**, on behalf of the Union, and on **provisional application** |
  | **COM(2026)0092** | Proposal for a Council Decision on the **conclusion**, on behalf of the Union — this is the one carrying `2026/0058(NLE)` |
  | **COM(2026)0093** | Recommendation for a Council Decision approving conclusion **by the European Commission** (the Euratom side) |
- **Council document**: 06435/2026. **EP document**: `C10-0186/2026`.
- **NEXT STEP, and it is imminent**: the file is item 2 on the agenda of the **joint AFET + INTA committee meeting on Tuesday 1 September 2026** (`CJ38(2026)0901_1`), the first day of the committee week after the summer recess.
- **Stage reached (OEIL, 26 August 2026)**: *Preparatory phase in Parliament*. **No rapporteur has been appointed yet** — the OEIL rapporteur table is empty. Do not name one.

## Why a separate Gibraltar treaty exists at all

Gibraltar left the EU with the United Kingdom on 31 January 2020, but it was **carved out of the EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement**. The TCA governs the EU's relationship with the UK generally and explicitly does not apply to Gibraltar, which left a gap no other instrument filled: a land border with Spain, at La Línea de la Concepción, across which several thousand people commute daily, with no treaty basis for how it should work.

That gap is what this agreement closes. It is a **bilateral EU-UK treaty**, negotiated by the Commission on a Council mandate, even though its practical substance concerns the Gibraltar-Spain frontier.

## What the consent procedure means in practice

Under Article 218(6) TFEU the sequence is:

1. The Commission negotiates on a Council mandate and tables the signing and conclusion proposals (done: February 2026).
2. The Council decides on **signing** and, where provided, **provisional application** — which is why COM(2026)0091 exists separately. Provisional application lets parts of a treaty operate before every ratification is complete.
3. The **European Parliament gives or withholds consent**. It votes on the text as it stands.
4. The Council adopts the **conclusion** decision, and the agreement enters into force.

**Parliament cannot amend the text.** Its leverage is the threat of refusal and the conditions it attaches in an accompanying resolution. Anyone asking "what amendments will the EP table on Gibraltar?" is asking a question the procedure does not allow.

## What to watch

- **Rapporteur appointment.** A joint AFET/INTA file needs a rapporteur in each committee, or a single rapporteur under Rule 58/59 arrangements. None is appointed. The choice signals how contested the file is.
- **Whether the 1 September meeting is a first exchange of views or a consideration of a draft recommendation.** The former means months of work ahead; the latter means the timetable is short.
- **Provisional application.** If the Council has already applied parts provisionally, the practical border arrangements may be operating before Parliament votes, which changes the politics of the consent vote.
- **The Spain dimension.** The frontier arrangements are the substance, and Spanish domestic politics bear on them, but they sit outside the EU procedure itself.

## What NOT to say

- **Do not call this part of the TCA.** It is a distinct treaty precisely because the TCA excludes Gibraltar.
- **Do not describe it as adopted or in force.** As of 26 August 2026 it is in the preparatory phase in Parliament.
- **Do not name a rapporteur, a committee vote date or a plenary date.** None is on file.
- **Do not describe amendments to the agreement.** Consent procedure: none are possible.
