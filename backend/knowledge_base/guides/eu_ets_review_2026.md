## What the EU ETS does

The **EU Emissions Trading System** is the world's first and largest cap-and-trade carbon market. It covers approximately **40% of EU greenhouse gas emissions** through a fixed cap of allowances (EUAs) that decreases over time. Operators (power generators, industrial installations above thresholds, intra-EEA airlines, and from 2024 maritime operators above 5,000 GT) must surrender one EUA for every tonne of CO2-equivalent emitted.

**Current ETS coverage (Phase 4, 2021-2030)**:
- Power sector + heat production
- Energy-intensive industries: steel, cement, aluminium, chemicals, refineries, glass, ceramics, pulp, paper
- Intra-EEA aviation (since 2012); extra-EEA aviation under CORSIA from 2027
- **Maritime** (added by Dir 2023/959): covers vessels >5,000 GT; phase-in 40% (2024) → 70% (2025) → 100% (2026); extends to general cargo + offshore vessels >5,000 GT from 2027
- **ETS2** (separate cap-and-trade system from 2027): buildings + road transport + small industry not in ETS1

## The ETS Review track (7 July 2026)

The Commission ETS Review for 7 July 2026 will examine:

1. **Linear Reduction Factor (LRF)** trajectory toward the **2040 climate target** (90% net GHG reduction binding ceiling, Reg (EU) 2026/667 — European Climate Law as amended)
2. **Free allocation phase-out** for industrial sectors covered by **CBAM** (Reg (EU) 2023/956) — the schedule under Article 10a(1a) requires free allocation to decrease as CBAM-covered imports face equivalent carbon costs
3. **Market Stability Reserve (MSR)** parameters — the 24% intake rate is currently scheduled to revert to 12% after 2026; review will set the post-2026 path
4. **ETS revenue use** — Article 10(3) revenue earmarking and the Climate Social Fund (Reg (EU) 2023/955) interaction
5. **ETS2 implementation readiness** — Member State preparation for the buildings + road transport launch from 2027 (allowance auctioning starts in 2026); price-stabilisation mechanism design under Article 30h
6. **Maritime coverage expansion** to <5,000 GT vessels (assessment by 31 December 2026)
7. **Innovation Fund + Modernisation Fund** governance + scope expansion
