# Georgia Visa-Free Travel Suspension

## QUICK FACTS
Regulation: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/496
CELEX: 32026R0496
Published: OJ L, 9 March 2026
Adopted: 6 March 2026
Legal basis: Article 8 of Regulation (EU) 2018/1806 (visa list regulation)
Subject: Temporary suspension of visa exemption for Georgian nationals with diplomatic, service, and official passports
Context: Response to deteriorating rule of law in Georgia, Predator spyware allegations, and crackdown on opposition

## What Happened

On 6 March 2026, the Commission suspended visa-free travel for Georgian passport holders carrying diplomatic, service, and official passports. This follows the broader decision announced on the same day (IP/26/564) to suspend the general visa exemption for Georgian nationals.

This is an unprecedented step: the EU has never before suspended an existing visa liberalisation agreement in this manner. It uses the suspension mechanism in Article 8 of the visa list regulation, which allows temporary suspension when a third country no longer meets the conditions for visa-free travel.

## Political Context

- Georgia's ruling party Georgian Dream has been accused of democratic backsliding since 2023
- The European Parliament has repeatedly called for action, including in resolutions on the rule of law in Georgia
- The Predator spyware case (discussed at the EP plenary this week, 11 March) involves surveillance of opposition politicians
- Political prisoners include Elene Khoshtaria, subject of a Rule 150 human rights debate this week
- The EP plenary debates the Georgian situation on Wednesday 11 March 2026
