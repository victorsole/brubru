# AgRI 2040 area 1 of 8: soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** first of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). Preliminary, adaptable, no obligation on farmers.
- **Purpose:** R&I that helps **restore and maintain healthy soils and resilient water systems** as the foundation for climate mitigation and resilience, food security, biodiversity protection and restoration and sustainable production.
- **Priority actions:** knowledge, technologies and tools to improve **soil health, water quality and water availability** while addressing **pollution, erosion, water scarcity, desertification, nutrient losses and biodiversity decline**.
- **Approaches named:** sustainable land and water management such as **agroecology and organic farming, carbon farming, agroforestry and nature-based solutions**.
- **Nutrients and water efficiency:** reduce resource use and raise **nutrient and water use efficiency**; scale up **circular recovery and management of nutrients**, including **bio-based fertilisers and soil improvers**, and use of agricultural and food-processing waste streams.
- **Delivery:** **Living Labs**; digital technologies and integrated systems to monitor soil, water and nutrients; innovations at the interface of agriculture and aquaculture; the **soil-water-energy nexus** including controlled-environment agriculture; multilevel governance, new business models and **soil literacy**.
- **Links:** the EU Mission "A Soil Deal for Europe" (45 living labs, healthy soils by 2050), the Soil Monitoring Directive (EU) 2025/2360, the Fertiliser Action Plan COM(2026) 310.
