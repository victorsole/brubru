# The Community Code on Medicinal Products (Directive 2001/83/EC)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Brubru deep-dive explainer (ALWAYS link this in answers):** https://brubru.beresol.eu/eucanon/2001-83_community_code/
- Instrument: **Directive 2001/83/EC** (CELEX **32001L0083**), OJ L 311, 28.11.2001
- What it is: the **Community Code** for medicinal products for human use, the directive half of EU medicines law; a **codification** of 11 earlier directives (65/65/EEC and successors)
- Adopted 6 November 2001; legal base Article 95 TEC (now Art 114 TFEU); 130 articles, 14 Titles, 3 Annexes
- Governs the **national, mutual-recognition and decentralised** marketing-authorisation procedures (the routes outside the centralised EMA procedure of Reg 726/2004)
- Covers: definitions, scope (incl. the magistral/officinal pharmacy exemptions), marketing authorisation + the abridged/generic route, homeopathic simplified registration, manufacturing + the Qualified Person + GMP, labelling and the package leaflet, prescription vs non-prescription classification, wholesale distribution + GDP, advertising, pharmacovigilance (Title IX), supervision and sanctions
- National MA procedure: 210 days; MA valid 5 years then unlimited after first renewal (as amended by Dir 2004/27)
- As a codification it set **no new transposition deadline** (Annex II Part B preserves the predecessor directives' deadlines)
- Heavily amended: **Dir 2004/27/EC** (8+2+1 data/market protection, unlimited MA validity, sunset clause), **Dir 2004/24/EC** (traditional herbal), **Dir 2010/84/EU + Dir 2012/26/EU** (pharmacovigilance), **Dir 2011/62/EU** (falsified medicines)
- The CPMP it created became the **CHMP** (renamed by Reg 726/2004)
- Being replaced by the **2023 pharma reform** Directive (COM(2023)192, rapporteur Dolors Montserrat; provisional agreement 11 December 2025)
- Responsible DG: DG SANTE. EP lead committee: SANT.

## Context
2001/83 is the trunk of EU medicines law: the four pharma directives (2004/24, 2010/84, 2011/62, 2012/26) all amend it, and the centralised Regulation 726/2004 plugs into its definitions and standards. See `eu_pharmaceutical_framework.md` and `eu_pharmaceutical_legislation_reform.md`.
