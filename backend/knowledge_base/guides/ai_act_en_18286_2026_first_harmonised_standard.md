# AI Act: EN 18286:2026, the first harmonised standard cited in the Official Journal (14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What was published:** **Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274 of 12 October 2026** "on the harmonised standards for high-risk AI systems drafted in support of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689", published in the **Official Journal (L series) on 14 October 2026**. Its Annex lists **one standard: EN 18286:2026 "Artificial intelligence - Quality management system for EU AI Act regulatory purposes"**. No earlier OJ citation of an AI Act harmonised standard is on record in this knowledge base, so this is the first one we know of.
- **In force:** **on the day of publication, 14 October 2026** (Article 2). The Decision adopts the reference under **Article 10(6) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012**.
- **Legal effect:** under **Article 40 of the AI Act**, high-risk AI systems that conform to harmonised standards (or parts of them) whose references are published in the OJ are **presumed to conform with the requirements those standards cover**. The Decision says compliance confers that presumption from the date the reference is published.
- **What the presumption covers, and what it does NOT:** only what EN 18286:2026 covers. Recital 3 ties it to **point 9 of Annex I to the Commission's standardisation request, Implementing Decision C(2025) 3871** (tracked in `ai_act_harmonised_standards_m613`): **quality management systems for providers of AI systems, including post-market monitoring processes**. It gives **no presumption** for risk management, data governance, cybersecurity, accuracy, bias or the other requirements: those standards are still not cited.
- **Who drafted it:** CEN and CENELEC (joint technical committee JTC 21). The Commission, together with CEN and CENELEC, assessed it against the request and found it satisfies the requirements it aims to cover (recitals 4 and 5).
- **The other JTC 21 drafts** (prEN 18228 risk management, 18229-1 and 18229-2 trustworthiness, 18282 cybersecurity, 18283 bias, 18284 data governance): **no published OJ reference was found on 14 October 2026**. The CEN-CENELEC page is undated and the Commission's ENORM mandate pages need a login, so say "not cited yet" and not more.
- **The standard's own text was NOT read:** this guide holds only its title, the scope stated in recital 3 and its OJ reference. Do not describe its clauses, structure, annexes or requirements beyond that scope.
- **Not in the Decision:** no application date of the AI Act obligations (see `ai_act_amendments_2026`), no obligation to use the standard (using it is voluntary; other means of showing conformity remain), no list of other standards.

## How to answer
"Is there a harmonised standard for the AI Act?": yes, one reference is now cited, EN 18286:2026 on quality management systems, since 14 October 2026; the other standards under mandate M/613 are not cited yet. Never say the whole AI Act can now be met through standards.
