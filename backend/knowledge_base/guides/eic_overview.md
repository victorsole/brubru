# European Innovation Council (EIC) — Programme Overview

## QUICK FACTS
- The EIC is Horizon Europe's deeptech/breakthrough-innovation arm — Pillar III "Innovative Europe" of Regulation (EU) 2021/695.
- Total Horizon Europe 2021-2027 envelope for the EIC: ~EUR 10.1 billion (commitments to date EUR 6.5 billion).
- 2026 EIC Work Programme total budget: over **EUR 1.4 billion** across 5 instruments + Prizes + BAS.
- 5 instruments in 2026: **Accelerator** (EUR 634M) · **STEP Scale Up** (EUR 300M) · **Pathfinder** (EUR 262M) · **Transition** (EUR 100M) · **Advanced Innovation Challenges / AIC** (EUR 6M).
- Pre-Accelerator (Widening Hop-On Facility) returns with its 2027 call (no 2026 call). The 6th "instrument" (Prizes) runs separately.
- Executing agency: **EISMEA** — European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency (Brussels).
- Strategic body: **EIC Board** — 20 members, chair Michiel Scheffer since 1 June 2023, 5-6 plenary meetings per year. Transparency Register expert group ID 3823.
- 8 EIC Programme Managers (deeptech industry experts on full-time 4-year secondments) design Pathfinder Challenges + AIC + manage portfolios pro-actively.
- EIC Fund: dedicated equity vehicle, capitalisation > EUR 4 billion, 300+ companies invested across 25 countries, EUR 1.4B invested, EUR 3.5 leveraged per public EUR 1.
- 3 unicorns from the EIC portfolio (as of June 2026), 350 investments by the Fund, 12 successful exits, EUR 5B mobilised from private investors, 1,000+ co-investors.
- Lead Commissioner 2024-: Ekaterina Zaharieva (Startups, Research and Innovation, Bulgaria).
- Lead DG: **DG RTD** (Research and Innovation). Operational implementation under EISMEA + EIC Fund Manager (advised by the European Investment Bank).

## Where the EIC sits inside Horizon Europe

```
Horizon Europe (Reg. EU 2021/695)
├── Pillar I — Excellent Science (ERC, MSCA, Research Infrastructures)
├── Pillar II — Global Challenges (6 thematic Clusters + Joint Undertakings + Missions)
└── Pillar III — Innovative Europe
    ├── European Innovation Council (EIC) ← THIS
    ├── European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) + 9 KICs
    └── European Innovation Ecosystems (EIE)
```

The EIC is the *single* one-stop shop for breakthrough innovation in the EU — distinct from EIT (which builds innovation communities/KICs) and from the EIE (which strengthens national innovation ecosystems). All 3 share Pillar III of Horizon Europe.

## The 5 instruments — what they cover

| Instrument | TRL covered | Beneficiary | Max grant | Equity? | 2026 budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pathfinder** (Open + Challenges) | 1-4 | Consortia (3+ from 3 MS/AC) — Challenges allow single applicants & 2-partner consortia | EUR 4M (booster +EUR 50k) | No | EUR 262M (Open 166 + Challenges 96) |
| **Transition** | 4 → 5/6 | Single SME / spin-off / RTO + small consortia (2-5 entities) — MUST build on prior EU project results | EUR 2.5M (booster +EUR 50k) | No | EUR 100M |
| **Accelerator** | 5/6 → 9 | Single SME / start-up / mid-cap ≤ 499 employees (mid-caps = equity-only) | EUR 2.5M grant | Yes — EUR 0.5-10M (blended) | EUR 634M |
| **STEP Scale Up** | 9+ | Deeptech scale-ups + small mid-caps in STEP priority sectors | n/a — equity only | Yes — EUR 10-30M (catalysing EUR 50M+ rounds) | EUR 300M |
| **Advanced Innovation Challenges (AIC pilot)** | demand-driven | Start-up / SME / RPO | EUR 300k Stage 1 + EUR 2.5M Stage 2 (lump sum) | No | EUR 6M (2026 pilot) |

The **Pre-Accelerator** ("EIC Hop-On Facility" under WIDERA) is paused in 2026 — next call opens 5 May 2027 (deadline 18 November 2027), EUR 500K-1M per SME at 70% funding rate, 2-year max duration, mono-beneficiary from Widening countries (BG, HR, CY, CZ, EE, EL, HU, LV, LT, MT, PL, PT, RO, SK, SI + AC + outermost regions).

## Cross-instrument tools

- **Seal of Excellence**: awarded to proposals that pass the 13/15 evaluation threshold in Accelerator (and similar thresholds in Pathfinder/Transition) but are not funded due to budget. Used by Member States to allocate national/regional funding without re-evaluating.
- **STEP Seal**: separate badge layered on top, awarded to proposals aligned with the STEP technology priorities (Digital + Deep Tech, Clean Tech, Biotech), unlocking aligned funding (Cohesion Funds, RRF redirected funding, Innovation Fund, etc.).
- **Business Acceleration Services (BAS)**: post-grant coaching, mentoring, investor matchmaking, peer learning, Women TechEU, Talents programme — open to all EIC beneficiaries.

## Governance — who decides what

1. **European Commission (DG RTD)** — adopts the annual Work Programme (Commission Implementing Decision); allocates the budget.
2. **EISMEA** — implements operationally: publishes calls, runs evaluations, manages grants, communications.
3. **EIC Board** — strategic advice on the work programme + EIC strategy. 20 members, currently chaired by Michiel Scheffer. Hold 5-6 plenary meetings per year. Members appointed by the Commission via open call for expressions of interest (last round: December 2025, 15 new + 5 returning).
4. **EIC Programme Managers** — 8 deeptech industry experts on full-time 4-year secondments (current portfolios: advanced materials for energy, cleantech, renewable energy & alternative resources, AI, sustainable semiconductors, biotech, health & biotech, MedTech & AI in healthcare). Design Pathfinder Challenges + AIC challenges, select expert evaluators, pro-actively manage portfolios.
5. **EIC Fund Board** — oversees the Fund's investment strategy (under the Fund's Luxembourg corporate statutes, advised by the EIB).
6. **EIB Group** — investment advisor of record for the EIC Fund: conducts due diligence, proposes investments, supports portfolio management.

History note: From 2018-2021 the EIC was run as a *pilot* under Horizon 2020 with an **EIC Pilot Advisory Board** (until end of May 2021), preceded by a High-Level Group of Innovators (2016-2018). The full EIC and current EIC Board were established under Horizon Europe in 2021.

## When to use which instrument — quick decision tree for advisors

- TRL 1-4, want to validate a *radically new science-towards-technology breakthrough* → **Pathfinder**
- TRL 4 → 5/6, want to *mature a prior EU-funded result* into an investable proposition → **Transition**
- TRL 5/6 → 9, want grant + equity to scale a deeptech → **Accelerator (blended)**
- TRL 9, scale-up needing EUR 50M+ round equity-only → **STEP Scale Up**
- Demand-driven thematic moonshot in 2026 priorities (Physical AI Robotics, NAMs) → **AIC**
- Widening-country SME wanting to join an ongoing Horizon Europe consortium → **Pre-Accelerator (Hop-On)** — wait for 5 May 2027 call
