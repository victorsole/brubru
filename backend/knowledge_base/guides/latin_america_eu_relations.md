# EU-Latin America and Caribbean Relations

## QUICK FACTS

- **Strategic frame**: EU-LAC Strategic Agenda + EU-CELAC summit cycle (last summit: 17-18 July 2023, Brussels; next: late 2026 expected)
- **Geographic scope**: Latin America (Mexico, Central America, Caribbean) + South America (Mercosur + Andean Community + Chile)
- **Responsible Commissioners**: Maros Sefcovic (Trade); Jozef Sikela (International Partnerships); Kaja Kallas (HR/VP, External Action)
- **Responsible DGs**: DG TRADE, DG INTPA (International Partnerships), EEAS, DG NEAR (rare for LAC), DG ENER (energy partnerships)
- **EP committees**: AFET (Foreign Affairs), DEVE (Development), INTA (International Trade), Latin American Parliament (Parlatino) delegation
- **Population covered**: approximately 660 million (LAC region)
- **Total EU-LAC trade (2024)**: approximately EUR 280 billion (goods)
- **EU-LAC FDI (2024)**: EU stock in LAC approximately EUR 745 billion (more than China + Russia + Japan combined)
- **Key strategic interests**: critical raw materials (lithium triangle Argentina/Bolivia/Chile, copper Chile/Peru, REE Brazil), green hydrogen (Chile), renewable energy partnerships, Latin America rebalancing vs. China BRI, democratic resilience (Venezuela / Cuba / Nicaragua)
- **EPRS Briefing (24 April 2026)**: "**Colombia ahead of the 2026 presidential elections**" -- ahead of presidential elections (first round expected May 2026, second round June 2026), EPRS analyses Colombia's political landscape post-Petro era, security situation, narcotrafficking, EU-Colombia Trade Agreement implementation, and migration cooperation. Cross-references EU-Andean Community Agreement.
- **Commissioner Sikela mission Addis Ababa (24 April 2026)**: announced new Global Gateway investments. While Addis Ababa is geographically African, the announcement followed a regional pattern of Sikela's deployment that includes parallel Latin America missions (CELAC focus 2026-2027). See `global_gateway_strategy.md`.

## Mexico: Modernised Global Agreement (April 2026 update)

- **AFET + INTA joint amendments PE787.714v01-00** (April 2026) tabled. Rapporteur Borja Giménez Larraz (EPP, ES) for AFET; co-amendments from INTA.
- **INTA amendments on the Interim Trade Agreement PE787.663v02-00** -- rapporteur Javi López (S&D, ES).
- Modernisation triggered by 2025 Trump tariffs on Mexico (USMCA tensions); EU positioned as alternative trade partner.
- Key updates: digital trade chapter, energy cooperation, anti-corruption, sustainable development chapter (Paris Agreement clause), small businesses.

See `eu_trade_defence.md` for the amendment dynamics.

## Brazil

- **Lula presidency (since January 2023)**: green credibility (Amazon protection); domestic agri-business pressure on Mercosur deforestation safeguards
- **2024-2025 Brazil G20 + COP30 Belém November 2025**: Brazil hosted COP30; outcomes defined adaptation finance landscape
- **Bilateral cooperation**: digital partnership, climate finance, green hydrogen, raw materials
- **Critical concern**: deforestation -- EUDR (Reg. 2023/1115) impact on Brazilian beef + soy + leather; Mercosur safeguard regulation 2026/687 partly addresses

## Argentina

- **Milei presidency (since December 2023)**: pro-market reforms; backed Mercosur deal
- **Critical raw materials**: lithium triangle anchor (Argentina + Bolivia + Chile)
- **EU concerns**: institutional rule-of-law trajectory; alignment with USA vs. EU on multilateralism

## Chile

- **Modernised Framework Agreement** (signed 13 December 2023): trade pillar provisionally applied; political + cooperation pillars require all 27 MS ratification
- **Critical raw materials**: world's largest copper producer + lithium reserves
- **Green hydrogen**: Chile pioneering Atacama Desert green H2 production for export to EU
- **Critical Raw Materials Act partner**: yes (signed Strategic Partnership)

## Colombia

- **EU-Andean Community Trade Agreement** in force since 2013
- **2026 presidential elections** (first round May, second round June): post-Petro political landscape; security + narcotrafficking + migration on agenda
- **EPRS Briefing 24 April 2026**: detailed political + security + economic analysis ahead of elections
- **Drug policy**: Colombia core narcotrafficking source; EU-CELAC dialogue on drug supply chain interception

## Venezuela

- **Sanctions regime**: EU sanctions since 2017 (targeted measures, periodic renewals)
- **Maduro government**: contested 2024 election outcome; EU + USA + most LAC dispute results
- **Migration**: Venezuelan migrants in Colombia (~3 million), Peru (~1.5 million), drive South America regional pressure
- **EU position**: support democratic transition; ECHO humanitarian assistance ongoing

## Cuba

- **Political Dialogue and Cooperation Agreement** in force since 2017
- **EU-US tensions**: USA Helms-Burton extraterritorial sanctions regime (Title III) -- EU Blocking Statute Reg. (EC) 2271/96 protects EU operators
- **Reform trajectory**: limited; EU emphasises human rights + economic reform

## EU-CELAC Summit Cycle

- **Last summit**: 17-18 July 2023, Brussels (after 8-year gap)
- **Outcomes**: 41-paragraph declaration; Russia-Ukraine paragraph contested (Nicaragua + Cuba opposed)
- **Next summit**: late 2026 expected (CELAC presidency: TBC; EU rotating presidency context: Cyprus H1 2026, Ireland H2 2026, Lithuania H1 2027)
