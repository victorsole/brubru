# Quantum Act (proposed 25 November 2026) and the Sovereign Tech Equity Fund

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status: PROPOSED by the Commission on Wednesday 25 November 2026** (adopted by the College in Strasbourg on 24 November, published 25 November). Title: Proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on a framework of measures for strengthening the Union's quantum ecosystem (Quantum Act), **COM(2026) 965** (Commission document register, 25 Nov). **NOT adopted; no OEIL procedure number as of 25 November 2026** (Parliament and Council have not started). Lead: Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen.
- **What it is:** an "enabling framework" to turn Europe's quantum research into industrial capacity, secure supply chains and prepare digital systems for **post-quantum cryptography**. It supports the 2025 Quantum Europe Strategy (goal: EU a global leader in quantum by 2030).
- **Three pillars, exactly:** (1) research excellence: a **Quantum Excellence Network** of research institutes, **Grand Challenges** (examples: quantum computers that keep working when errors occur; navigation without satellite signals), more post-quantum cryptography research; (2) bringing quantum into use: pan-European quantum infrastructures (computers, simulators, quantum-enhanced medical imaging), **Quantum Technology and Innovation Hubs** for start-ups and SMEs, competence and acceleration centres in each Member State, **strategic projects**, public procurement; (3) security of systems and supplies: monitoring of critical components and supply-chain dependencies, a coordinated transition to post-quantum cryptography with "targeted procurement safeguards".
- **Sovereign Tech Equity Fund (STEF):** the Act "allows for the establishment" of a new investment vehicle giving equity and similar finance to technologies essential for tech sovereignty and economic security (quantum and other critical technologies, the Tech Sovereignty Package). To be set up with the EIB Group and Member States. **No fund size is given in the press release: do not state one.**
- **Governance:** a **Quantum Europe Framework Initiative** aligns EU and national programmes; a **Quantum Strategy Board** (Commission and Member States); a **European Quantum Alliance** (industry, universities, research organisations); the **EuroHPC Joint Undertaking** (or its successor) carries out EU-level quantum research and infrastructure work.
- **Money:** future EU funding "would depend on budget decisions" (the next MFF). Public quantum investment by the EU and Member States: more than EUR 11 billion (2025 Strategy figure). Builds on EuroQCI, EuroHPC quantum computers, the Quantum Flagship (EUR 1 billion), quantum pilot lines, the Quantum Skills Academy.
- **Sources:** IP/26/2567, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2567 ; factsheet FS/26/2569.
