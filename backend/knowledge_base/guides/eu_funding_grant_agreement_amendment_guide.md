# Grant Agreement Amendment Guide

## QUICK FACTS

- Covers all 2021-2027 MFF programmes: Horizon Europe, European Innovation Council (EIC), Connecting Europe Facility (CEF), DIGITAL Europe, LIFE, Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values (CERV), Erasmus+, European Social Fund Plus (ESF+), Creative Europe (CREA)
- Amendment Types: 40+ AT-clauses covering participant changes, project scope, schedule, budget, and termination
- Minor amendments: participant info changes, short name updates, small budget reallocations (Automatic or Self-Select on Portal)
- Major amendments: consortium composition, project duration, cost eligibility, funding rate changes (often require EU approval)
- Financial thresholds: Prefinancing guarantees apply above EUR 430,000; non-profit rule kicks in above EUR 60,000; CFS (Certificate on Financial Statements) required for high-value grants
- Amendment Tool: Available on Funding & Tenders Portal (ec.europa.eu) for self-service submission
- Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks for standard amendment approval if documentation is complete
- Initiator: Consortium coordinator (usually) or EU Project Officer
- Approval: EU Granting Authority (DG/Joint Undertaking) final decision
- Key rule: Amendment must be submitted BEFORE the change takes effect (except retroactive retroactive exit dates and end-of-work dates are sometimes allowed; termination dates cannot be retroactive)
- Mandatory supporting documents: Updated Annex 1 (Description of Action) and Annex 2 (Budget), Declaration of Honour, legal proof of entity status where relevant
- No withdrawal: Once an amendment is signed, the terms become legally binding; termination cannot be undone except by adding the beneficiary back via AT1

## When to Amend (and When NOT to)

An amendment is necessary when a significant change affects the grant agreement's core content under Article 39 of the Annotated Grant Agreement. Minor administrative changes (participant email, office address, phone) can often be managed via Continuous Reporting in the portal without a formal amendment.

**Reasons to amend:**
- A beneficiary joins, leaves, or changes role (consortium changes)
- The project scope, deliverables, or workplan changes materially
- Budget must be reallocated significantly between cost categories or beneficiaries
- Project timeline shifts (start date, duration, end date, reporting periods)
- Cost eligibility rules need adjustment (equipment depreciation, unit costs, flat rates, lump sums)
- Payment terms require change (prefinancing amount, guarantee, MIM contribution)
- Funded rate or maximum grant amount needs adjustment
- Project must be suspended or terminated early
- Beneficiary changes name, legal status, or location (triggering Participant Register revalidation)

**When NOT to amend:**
- Administrative contact updates (email, phone, office address) handled via Continuous Reporting
- Very minor budget moves within a beneficiary's existing cost category (often <5% tolerance)
- Subcontractors swap (if the contract value stays the same and was pre-approved)
- Associate partner information updates (if no legal change)
- Informal progress reports and quarterly updates (use Periodic Reporting module)

## Amendment Procedure (Step by Step)

### Before You Submit

1. **Review the need**: Determine whether the change requires a formal amendment or can be handled via Continuous Reporting or a simple letter to the Project Officer.
2. **Identify which AT-clauses apply**: Use the Amendment Guide table above and the Portal's AT-clause selector to map the change to 1 or more amendment types.
3. **Check mandatory combinations**: Many AT-clauses must be paired (e.g. AT4 termination requires AT21 if tasks are reallocated, and AT11 if the coordinator leaves). See the table for dependencies.
4. **Prepare supporting documents**:
   - Updated Annex 1 (Description of Action) if scope, tasks, or resources change
   - Updated Annex 2 (Estimated Budget) if budget allocation or maximum grant amount changes
   - Declaration of Honour (template on Portal, signed by LEAR)
   - Accession Form (new beneficiary only, template on Portal)
   - Final financial statement (if beneficiary is terminating, within 60 days)
   - Legal proof (affiliation agreements, court extracts, governance documents, if status changes)
   - Beneficiary opinion (if terminating a partner, their written consent or proof you requested it)
   - PI motivation letter (Horizon Europe ERC grants only, if AT1/AT2/AT6/AT11/AT21/AT25/AT26/AT35/AT36/AT37)
5. **Contact the Project Officer if unsure**: Before submitting, ask via the Portal's Process Communications button. EU staff will advise which clauses to use and flag missing documents early.

### Submission via Funding & Tenders Portal

6. **Log in to the Portal**: Use your LEAR or designated consortium coordinator credentials.
7. **Navigate to Amendment Information screen**: My Project(s) > Actions > Manage Project > Amend Grant Agreement.
8. **Select amendment type(s)**: Tick the AT-clause(s) from the dropdown. The Portal will alert you to mandatory combinations.
9. **Upload documents**: Attach the updated Annex 1, Annex 2, Declaration of Honour, and any additional supporting documents. Documents must be in PDF or MS Office formats; file names should be descriptive (e.g. "Annex1_v2_20260617.pdf").
10. **Provide justification text**: Briefly describe why the amendment is needed and any key dates (e.g. "Beneficiary XYZ exiting 30 June 2026 due to funding constraints"; "Project scope increased to include workstream on AI governance").
11. **Review and submit**: Double-check all required fields are complete. Submit once ready. The system will lock the amendment (no changes allowed after submission).

### Post-Submission (EU Review)

12. **EU Project Officer assessment**: The granting authority's Project Officer (typically at DG RTD, DG ENV, etc.) reviews the amendment for completeness and legal compliance.
    - If documents are incomplete or AT-clause combinations are wrong, the Project Officer will reject the amendment and notify you via Portal message. You must resubmit.
    - If the amendment is approved in principle, the Officer may request clarifications or minor revisions (e.g. "clarify the new beneficiary's research background").
13. **Final approval and signature**: Once approved, the amendment is routed for electronic signature by the Project Officer and the Coordinator (or nominated PCoCo if coordinator is changing). Signatures are collected via the Portal.
14. **Entry into force**: The amendment takes effect 30 days after both parties sign it (unless the amendment specifies an earlier effective date).

### Timeline and Contacts

- **Submission to approval**: Typically 2 to 4 weeks if all documents are provided and the amendment is straightforward.
- Amendments with missing or erroneous documents may require 1 to 2 resubmissions (cost: 2 to 4 additional weeks).
- Contact your Project Officer immediately if the amendment is time-critical (e.g. termination imminent).

## Special Cases

### Beneficiary Termination and Withdrawal

**Scenario:** A partner wants to leave or must be removed mid-project.

- **AT-clause**: AT4 (Beneficiary termination)
- **Exit date**: The date the beneficiary stops contributing to the project. This can be retroactive (e.g. "exited 15 May 2026" submitted 17 June). Reflects when their cost eligibility ended.
- **Termination date**: The legal effective date of removal from the GA. This CANNOT be retroactive; it must be on or after the amendment submission date. Takes effect 30 days after signature.
- **Key requirement**: Within 60 days of the termination date, the beneficiary (or the new coordinator if the terminated beneficiary was the coordinator) must submit a final financial statement itemising all costs claimed to date, plus a repayment calculation if the grant overpaid them.
- **Coordinator change**: If the departing beneficiary is the coordinator, AT11 is mandatory. A replacement must be nominated.
- **Supporting docs**: Updated Annex 1 + Annex 2, final statement, CFS (if applicable), and beneficiary's signed opinion (or proof that you requested it). If termination is due to breach, include supporting evidence (bankruptcy notice, liquidation order, court decision, etc).

### Consortium Changes (Adding or Removing Partners)

**Adding a new beneficiary:**
- **AT-clause**: AT1 (new beneficiary), AT2 (affiliated entity if applicable), AT3 (associated partner if no budget)
- **Process**: The new entity must first be registered and validated in the Participant Register. A Participant Identification Code (PIC) is assigned; the LEAR (Legal Entity Appointed Representative) role is designated. This can take 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Start date options**: A fixed date (can be retroactive to project start), the accession form signature date, or the amendment's entry-into-force date. Consult the Project Officer if retroactivity is needed.
- **Supporting docs**: Accession Form (signed by new beneficiary's LEAR), updated Annex 1 + Annex 2, Declaration of Honour (new beneficiary), and legal proof of entity status (articles of association, registration extract, recent financial statements).
- **Special case (ERC)**: If the Horizon Europe ERC grant's PI (Principal Investigator) is changing, a new supplementary agreement is required.

**FPA and SGA context:** For Framework Partnership Agreements (FPA) paired with Specific Grant Agreements (SGA), the new beneficiary must be added to the FPA first; they can then participate in specific SGAs (or just one SGA) as the consortium chooses.

### No-Cost Extensions

**Scenario:** Project cannot be completed on time, but no additional budget is available.

- **AT-clause**: AT25 (extend project duration)
- **Mandatory pairing**: AT26 (adjust reporting periods) and AT21 (update Annex 1 if the work plan timeline shifts).
- **Rules**:
  - The extended period must have no additional EU funding.
  - Prefinancing, if any, cannot be increased.
  - The new end date must be feasible given available team capacity and access to resources.
  - Reporting deadlines shift to accommodate the new timeline.
- **Supporting docs**: Updated Annex 1 + Annex 2, brief justification (e.g. "experimental results delayed; need 6 additional months to complete assays and publication").

### Consortium Composition Under FPA / SGA

If your grant is structured as an FPA with multiple SGAs:
- A participant added at FPA level automatically becomes eligible for all new SGAs signed after the amendment.
- Participants removed at FPA level do NOT automatically exit existing SGAs; you can choose to keep them on an ongoing SGA to finish their workload (if the SGA's signed text allows). This is a key difference from simple GA amendments.
- SGA-specific terminations are handled separately (a separate amendment to that SGA only, not the FPA).

## Documents and Templates Required

All templates and documents are available on the Funding & Tenders Portal under Reference Documents. Keep local copies for version control.

| Document | When Required | Source / Template | Notes |
|----------|---|----------|-------|
| Updated Annex 1 (Description of Action) | Any scope, task, or resource change (AT1, AT2, AT4, AT6, AT11, AT21, AT25, AT26, etc) | Portal > Reference Documents > Annex 1 template (programme-specific) | Must reflect current project timeline and task allocation; version control with date/author |
| Updated Annex 2 (Estimated Budget) | Any budget reallocation, new beneficiary, or grant amount change (AT1, AT2, AT4, AT41, AT43a, etc) | Portal > Reference Documents > Annex 2 template (programme-specific) | Include cost breakdowns by beneficiary and cost category; ensure sum matches maximum grant amount after amendment |
| Declaration of Honour (DoH) | New beneficiary (AT1), new affiliated entity (AT2), or as requested by EU | Portal > Reference Documents > DoH template | Signed by LEAR of the beneficiary being added; confirms no conflict of interest and compliance with EU eligibility rules |
| Accession Form (Annex 3) | Addition of new beneficiary only (AT1) | Portal > Reference Documents > Annex 3 template | Signed by new beneficiary's LEAR and Coordinator; formally accedes the new partner to the GA |
| Final Financial Statement (FS) | Beneficiary termination (AT4); final report at project end | Beneficiary prepares internally; templates in SyGMa (continuous reporting module) | Must itemise all eligible costs claimed to date; submitted within 60 days of termination date |
| Certificate on Financial Statements (CFS) | If grant contribution >EUR 430,000 at any point during project | Auditor (external or internal) prepares; templates on Portal | Certifies that the financial statement is a fair and complete record and complies with the GA cost eligibility rules |
| Legal Proof of Affiliation (AT2) | Affiliated entity being added | Entity provides (shareholder registry, articles, inter-company agreement, etc) | Demonstrates the legal control or ownership link between beneficiary and affiliated entity |
| Beneficiary Opinion (AT4) | Termination of partner (coordinator not initiating) | Departing beneficiary's LEAR signs (or written proof that you requested it) | Confirms the beneficiary agrees to exit, or documents that you formally requested consent |
| PI Motivation Letter (Horizon Europe ERC) | Any change affecting scope, team, or timeline in ERC grants (AT1, AT2, AT11, AT21, AT25, etc) | PI prepares | Justifies the change and confirms it does not compromise research excellence |
| New Supplementary Agreement (Horizon Europe ERC PI change) | Change of ERC Principal Investigator (AT11 variant) | EU / Horizon Europe office provides | Formalises the PI change and any conditions (e.g. transfer of ownership of equipment) |
| Bank Account Validation (AT12) | Change of payment coordinator or beneficiary bank details | Beneficiary updates details via Participant Register; EU validates | Must pass the IBAN check and match the registered entity |
| Proof of Nomination (AT11 without coordinator consent) | Coordinator change if former coordinator does not agree (AT11) | Other beneficiaries provide (meeting minutes, email chain, etc) | Demonstrates that the consortium voted and nominated a replacement; nominated beneficiary signs proof of acceptance |
| Justification for Dispute Resolution Change (AT17) | Change of legal framework for international organisation beneficiary | Beneficiary / Project Officer provides | Explains why jurisdiction shift is needed (e.g. relocation of HQ) |
