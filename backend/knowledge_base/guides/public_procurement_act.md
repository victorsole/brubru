# Public Procurement Act (COM(2026) 590)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Proposed 9 September 2026.** COM(2026) 590 final, procedure **2026/0265 (COD)**. Accompanying documents SEC(2026) 590, SWD(2026) 590 (subsidiarity grid), SWD(2026) 591 (impact assessment), SWD(2026) 592 (executive summary). Press release IP/26/1817.
- **It is a Regulation replacing three Directives.** Full short title: Regulation **on public contracts and concessions**, **repealing Directives 2014/23/EU (concessions), 2014/24/EU (public sector) and 2014/25/EU (utilities)**. Moving from directive to regulation removes national transposition — that is the central design choice.
- **Legal basis: Article 114 TFEU.** Ordinary legislative procedure. It is a **proposal**: not adopted, not in force, no application date. It "will be negotiated by the European Parliament and the Council before its adoption and entry into force".
- **Scale.** Public procurement is around **15% of EU GDP**. Executive Vice-President Séjourné presented the existing rulebook as roughly **900 pages**.
- **R&D SERVICES ARE NOT IN THIS ACT.** R&D service contracts, **including pre-commercial procurement**, are **excluded from its scope** and regulated in the **European Innovation Act** (COM(2026) 567, 2026/0264(COD)), expressly "to avoid overlaps". The exclusion does **not** extend to the R&D elements that form part of this Act's own new **innovation procedure**, and unlike the Innovation Act regime, this Act **does** cover the purchase of the commercial end-product. See `european_innovation_act.md`.
- **Why now.** It responds to the European Court of Auditors' findings — **declining competition, limited SME and cross-border participation, and the rise of single-bid procedures** — to calls from the Council and Parliament, to the **Draghi and Letta reports**, and to von der Leyen's Political Guidelines.
- **Trade instruments.** Chapter 5 explicitly incorporates, including by cross-reference, the principles of **Regulation (EU) 2022/1031 (International Procurement Instrument)** on the origin of economic operators and goods. It sits alongside **Regulation (EU) 2023/2675 (Anti-Coercion Instrument)** and **Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014** on Russia.
- **EP reaction, 8 September:** the Internal Market committee chair welcomed the move **beyond price-only criteria**.

## It amends fourteen acts, and Brubru already covers several of them

This is the part most coverage misses. Beyond repealing the three 2014 directives, the proposal
**amends nine Regulations and five Directives**. Several are instruments Brubru holds guides or
compliance material for, so their procurement-facing provisions change if this passes:

| Amended act | Commonly known as |
|---|---|
| (EU) 2024/1781 | **Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)** |
| (EU) 2024/2847 | **Cyber Resilience Act** |
| (EU) 2025/40 | **Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)** |
| (EU) 2023/1542 | **Batteries Regulation** |
| (EU) 2024/1760 | **Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD)** |
| (EU) 2024/1252 | **Critical Raw Materials Act** |
| (EU) 2024/1735 | **Net-Zero Industry Act** |
| (EU) 2024/3110 | Construction Products Regulation |
| (EU) 2024/1157 | Waste Shipments Regulation |
| (EC) 1370/2007 | Public passenger transport services |
| 2008/98/EC | Waste Framework Directive |
| (EU) 2019/882 | **European Accessibility Act** |
| (EU) 2022/2381 | Gender balance on corporate boards |
| (EU) 2023/1791 | **Energy Efficiency Directive** |

When answering about any of these, say that a **pending proposal would amend it** — never that the
amendment is in force.

## Status and what to watch

- Proposal stage, **2026/0265(COD)**. Lead committee in the Parliament is expected to be **IMCO**,
  whose chair commented on 8 September; confirm against the OEIL procedure file rather than assuming.
- The repeal-and-replace structure means the transitional provisions and the application date are
  where the real negotiation will land. Read the final articles rather than inferring a date.
