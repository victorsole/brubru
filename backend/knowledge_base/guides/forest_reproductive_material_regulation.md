# Forest Reproductive Material Regulation (FRM)

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU framework on the marketing of forest reproductive material (seeds, plant parts, planting stock used for forestry purposes) -- recast / update of Council Directive 1999/105/EC
- Current legal basis: **Council Directive 1999/105/EC** of 22 December 1999 on the marketing of forest reproductive material, CELEX 31999L0105, OJ L 11, 15.1.2000
- EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:31999L0105
- Type: Currently a directive; recast proposal under EU Plant Reproductive Material (PRM) reform package may shift FRM into a regulation (directly applicable)
- Lead DG: DG SANTE (Health and Food Safety, Plant Health Unit) + DG ENV (Forestry / LULUCF dimension)
- Lead EP committee: AGRI (Agriculture and Rural Development) + ENVI opinion (forest health, biodiversity)
- Council configuration: AGRIFISH
- Legal basis: Article 43(2) TFEU (Common Agricultural Policy) and Article 192(1) TFEU (environment), depending on scope of recast
- Categories of FRM under Directive 1999/105/EC: "source-identified", "selected", "qualified", "tested" -- progressive levels of genetic quality
- Key species in scope: 47 native and naturalised European tree species (oak, beech, pine, spruce, fir, larch, etc.)
- Marketing principles: only material from approved basic material sources may be marketed; certification by Member State authorities; master certificate of provenance
- **LATEST (22 April 2026)**: **AGRI Draft Recommendation PE787.674v01** "Forest Reproductive Material Regulation" tabled by **Herbert Dorfmann (EPP, Italy)**. Source: europarl.europa.eu/committees/en/agri. Recommendation likely covers consent procedure for an international agreement or interinstitutional negotiating mandate -- exact subject of the recommendation to be confirmed against OEIL once the procedure number resolves.
- **AGRI rapporteur**: Herbert Dorfmann -- South Tyrolean farmer, AGRI veteran, lead voice on mountain forestry + agroforestry. Frequent rapporteur on forest reproductive material, plant health, and biodiversity files

## Why this matters

Forest reproductive material is **the seed corn of European forestry**. The 1999 Directive set certification rules but is now widely seen as outdated: it pre-dates the EU Forestry Strategy 2030 (COM(2021) 572), the LULUCF Regulation update, the Nature Restoration Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1991), the Deforestation Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1115), and the climate-resilience challenge of accelerated tree mortality (drought, bark beetle, fire).

Brubru users tracking forest policy face three converging fronts:

1. **Plant Reproductive Material (PRM) reform package**: Commission proposal COM(2023) 414 on PRM (excluding forestry) is in interinstitutional negotiations; some Member States and EP groups argue FRM Directive 1999/105/EC needs parallel modernisation.
2. **Forestry Strategy 2030 implementation**: includes a target on forest genetic diversity, climate-resilient tree species selection, and assisted migration for climate adaptation. Modernised FRM rules are the regulatory plumbing.
3. **Nature Restoration Regulation Article 12 (forest ecosystems)**: requires Member States to use site-adapted, climate-resilient FRM in restoration projects -- explicit cross-reference to Directive 1999/105/EC and a candidate hook for revising it.

## What the Dorfmann Recommendation Likely Covers

The AGRI draft recommendation PE787.674 is part of an EP "consent" or "interinstitutional negotiating mandate" track. Three plausible subjects:

- **Consent to a Council Decision** authorising EU accession to or amendment of an international agreement on forest genetic resources (the OECD Scheme for the Certification of Forest Reproductive Material is the obvious candidate -- the EU is a participant; the OECD scheme was last revised October 2024)
- **Negotiating mandate for trilogues** on a forthcoming Commission proposal recasting Directive 1999/105/EC into a regulation
- **Implementing-act mandate** specifying technical updates to FRM categories under existing Directive 1999/105/EC framework

Once the procedure number is confirmed against OEIL, this guide should be updated with the precise scope.
