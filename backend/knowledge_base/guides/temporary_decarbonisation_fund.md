# Temporary Decarbonisation Fund (TDF)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Full name:** Temporary Decarbonisation Fund -- proposed regulation establishing time-limited EU instrument to de-risk industrial decarbonisation capex
- **Type:** Regulation proposal (Commission-initiated)
- **Lead DG:** DG GROW (Internal Market, Industry) with DG CLIMA + DG ENER input
- **Responsible Commissioners:** Stephane Sejourne (EVP Prosperity and Industrial Strategy), Teresa Ribera (EVP Clean, Just and Competitive Transition)
- **Political parent:** Clean Industrial Deal (COM(2025) 85) + Competitiveness Compass (COM(2025) 30)
- **EP lead committee:** ITRE (Industry, Research and Energy); opinions expected from ENVI + ECON + BUDG
- **Council configuration:** COMPET (Competitiveness) with TTE (Energy) input
- **ITRE Draft Opinion (21 April 2026):** **PE786.735v02** -- "Draft Opinion on the proposal for a regulation establishing the Temporary Decarbonisation Fund." Rapporteur: **Massimiliano Salini** (EPP, Italy). Amendment deadline: **8 May 2026**. Source: EP committees portal, ITRE committee page
- **Temporary Crisis and Transition Framework (TCTF):** State aid complement currently in force until December 2026 (Commission Communication C/2023/1711) -- TDF would partially bridge post-TCTF deployment
- **Budget envelope:** TBC in Commission proposal (likely Competitiveness Fund / MFF 2028-2034 Heading 1 component)
- **Target beneficiaries:** energy-intensive industries (EIIs) -- steel, aluminium, chemicals, cement, glass, pulp/paper, refineries; automotive supply chain; hydrogen-ready industrial sites
- **Target technologies:** electrification of industrial heat, green hydrogen uptake, CCUS pilots, grid-connection capex, heat pump and heat network integration
- **Interplay with CBAM:** TDF designed to cushion EII transition alongside CBAM scope expansion (see `cbam_downstream_goods_extension.md`)
- **Interplay with ETS:** Innovation Fund and Modernisation Fund continue; TDF is a new instrument, not a merger
- **Interplay with STEP / Competitiveness Fund:** STEP Regulation (EU) 2024/795 and proposed European Competitiveness Fund (ECF) under MFF 2028-2034 provide longer-term architecture; TDF provides bridging finance

## Why a Temporary Fund?

The Clean Industrial Deal identified a **capex cliff** for European heavy industry between 2025 and 2030:
1. US Inflation Reduction Act subsidies drawing EII investment across the Atlantic
2. Chinese overcapacity in clean-tech (steel, chemicals, solar, batteries) depressing EU prices
3. TCTF (state aid flexibility) expiring end-2026 without successor deployed
4. MFF 2028-2034 Competitiveness Fund only applicable from 1 January 2028

The TDF is designed as a **time-limited instrument** (likely 2026-2030 or 2027-2031) to bridge this cliff with project-based grants and blended finance for specific decarbonisation capex.

## Expected Architecture (based on Salini draft opinion + Commission signals)

### Eligible projects
- Industrial electrification of process heat (electric boilers, industrial heat pumps)
- Green/low-carbon hydrogen uptake in hard-to-abate sectors
- CCUS at EII sites (capture, transport, permanent storage)
- Grid-connection capex for renewable-intensive industrial clusters
- Heat network integration connecting industrial waste heat to district heating
- Battery gigafactory supply chain (critical raw materials processing)

### Selection criteria (Salini emphasis)
- Union-wide benefit and cross-border spillovers
- GHG abatement per euro of EU support
- Protection against single-Member-State concentration (level playing field)
- Coherence with National Energy and Climate Plans (NECPs) under Governance Regulation 2018/1999
- Coherence with Grid Investment Plans (under European Grids Package)

### Governance
- Commission-managed direct management (likely CINEA or HaDEA executive agency)
- Annual call mechanism; multi-stage evaluation (admissibility, operational, financial)
- Joint committee with Member State input (Article 103 TFEU / Article 194 TFEU mixed legal base)
- Reporting alignment with Clean Industrial Deal KPIs

## EP Political Map (early signal from Salini draft)

- **EPP (Salini, rapporteur):** supports strong TDF envelope + level-playing-field safeguards + simplified application for SMEs
- **S&D (likely shadow):** supports TDF + conditionality on quality jobs, Just Transition, collective-bargaining compliance
- **Renew (likely shadow):** supports market-based selection criteria, wary of national-preference carve-outs
- **Greens (likely shadow):** scrutinise CCUS funding split (prefer renewables/electrification priority); conditional on 1.5°C alignment
- **ECR (likely shadow):** supports industrial competitiveness; wary of state aid amplification across MS
- **ID / PfE / ESN:** positions likely TBC at ITRE shadow meeting

## Lobbying Stakes

- **Eurofer (European Steel Association):** strong backer, pushing for capex grants + operational support for early electrification
- **Cefic (Chemicals):** supports TDF, pressing for hydrogen + CCUS dual-track eligibility
- **Cembureau (Cement):** CCUS-focused, argues for inclusion of lime and ceramics
- **Eurelectric:** supports grid capex eligibility; cautious on generation-mix favouritism
- **BusinessEurope:** supportive, pushing for light-touch state aid compatibility
- **ETUC / IndustriAll Europe:** supportive with Just Transition conditionality + social dialogue obligations
- **CAN Europe / WWF EU:** scrutinising CCUS allocation + fossil lock-in risks
- **ACEA (automotive):** pushing for supply-chain inclusion (batteries, critical raw materials processing)
