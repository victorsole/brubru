# EHDS Stakeholder Forum: Commission call for members (published 13 October 2026, applications by 10 December 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **13 October 2026** the Commission (DG for Health and Food Safety) announced that it **invites organisations and public entities other than Member State authorities to apply to become members of the European Health Data Space (EHDS) Stakeholder Forum**, and invites organisations and individuals working with health data to join the **EHDS Stakeholder Community** on the EU Health Policy Platform. Both support the **implementation of the EHDS**.
- **Deadline:** applications may be submitted **by 10 December 2026**.
- **Size and membership:** about **80 members**: organisations ("Type C" members) and public entities other than Member States' authorities ("Type E" members). Applicants need proven, relevant expertise in the **primary and secondary use of electronic health data** within the EHDS framework. The Commission aims for balance across **five categories**: citizens, patients and consumers; healthcare providers and health ecosystems; industry and standards development organisations; research and academia; public entities, including EU and international organisations.
- **Role:** the Forum provides stakeholder input on the **governance and implementation of the EHDS**, covering primary and secondary use: **data access, data quality, interoperability, usability and artificial intelligence**; it exchanges good practice, identifies emerging issues and links the EHDS governance bodies with the wider community. It is advisory input, not decision-making.
- **Working method:** at least one hybrid **General Assembly a year** plus regular online meetings; documents and meetings generally in English; participation is **not remunerated**, travel and subsistence may be reimbursed under Commission rules; members nominate representatives with good English.
- **How to apply:** a cover letter, a completed classification form (Annex I) and a completed selection-criteria form (Annex II), **signed and sent by email to SANTE-EHDS-STAKEHOLDER-FORUM@ec.europa.eu**; any official EU language is accepted and **English is encouraged** to ease the evaluation.
- **Not covered here:** the EHDS Regulation's own obligations and dates (see `ehds_european_health_data_space` and `european_health_data_space`); the Commission page gives no selection date.

## How to answer
"How do I join the EHDS Stakeholder Forum?": apply by 10 December 2026 with a cover letter and the two forms; about 80 seats in five stakeholder categories; the Community on the EU Health Policy Platform is separate and open to individuals.
