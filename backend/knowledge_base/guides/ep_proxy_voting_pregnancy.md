## Why this matters
The European Electoral Act (Council Decision 76/787/ECSC, EEC, Euratom, last consolidated version) currently does not provide for proxy voting in EP plenary. MEPs who cannot be physically present (or remotely connected during the limited remote-voting period) cannot exercise their vote -- including during pregnancy, around childbirth, or in the immediate post-natal period. Several national parliaments (UK House of Commons, Scottish Parliament, Spanish Congress, French Assemblée) have introduced proxy or remote-voting arrangements covering parental leave; the EP has not. The 2025/2195(INL) initiative seeks to amend the European Electoral Act to allow a designated MEP to cast a proxy vote during pregnancy and a defined post-birth period.

## Political context
- **Cross-group support**: institutional procedural reform with broad support from S&D, Greens/EFA, Renew, EPP modernisers, and The Left
- **Opposition**: from groups concerned about precedent for further remote/proxy mechanisms beyond the parental scope
- **Scope debate**: how long the post-birth proxy applies (often debated at 6-12 weeks); whether adoptive parents are covered
