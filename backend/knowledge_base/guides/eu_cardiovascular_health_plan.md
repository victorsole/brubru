# EU Cardiovascular Health Plan

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU-level strategy to reduce the burden of cardiovascular disease (CVD) -- leading cause of death in the EU
- Status: **Call for Evidence open** (DG SANTE, announced 21 April 2026)
- Lead DG: DG SANTE (Health and Food Safety)
- Responsible Commissioner: Oliver Varhelyi (Health and Animal Welfare)
- Supporting units: SANTE.C (Public Health, Cancer and Health Security), SANTE.B (Medical Products and Innovation)
- Legal basis: Article 168 TFEU (public health complementary competence)
- Sister strategy: Europe's Beating Cancer Plan (BECP, COM(2021) 44), with EU4Health funding support
- Delivery vehicle: expected Commission Communication + Council Recommendation, implementation via EU4Health programme
- EU4Health Regulation: Regulation (EU) 2021/522 (CELEX 32021R0522), budget EUR 5.3 billion (2021-2027), MFF 2028-34 successor pending
- EU CVD burden: CVD causes approximately 1.7 million deaths/year in the EU (36% of all deaths); approximately EUR 282 billion/year economic cost (healthcare + productivity loss) per OECD/EUROSTAT
- EU CVD incidence: 60+ million people living with CVD in the EU
- NCD framework (WHO): Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) strategy addressing heart disease, cancer, diabetes, respiratory disease, mental health
- UN SDG 3.4: reduce premature mortality from NCDs by one third by 2030 (EU commitment)
- Related legislation: Tobacco Products Directive (2014/40), Tobacco Tax Directive revision (pending), Regulation on food nutrition claims (1924/2006), Regulation on health claims, Nutri-Score voluntary labelling (France, Spain, Belgium, Germany, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Switzerland)
- Committee: EP ENVI + sub-committee SANT (Subcommittee on Public Health)
- Council configuration: EPSCO (Employment, Social Policy, Health and Consumer Affairs)
- Key stakeholders: European Society of Cardiology (ESC), European Heart Network (EHN), European Federation of National Associations of Heart Patients (EHPA), WHO Europe

## Call for Evidence (21 April 2026)

**DG SANTE launched a Call for Evidence** on the upcoming EU Cardiovascular Health Plan. Stakeholders -- patient groups, medical societies, industry, civil society, Member States -- invited to submit contributions via HaveYourSay.

Scope of the consultation:
- Prevention: tobacco, alcohol, salt/sugar/fat intake, physical inactivity, air pollution
- Early detection and screening: hypertension, cholesterol, diabetes
- Equity of access to cardiac care across Member States
- Digital health tools: telemedicine, AI diagnostics, wearables
- Research and innovation: Horizon Europe Cluster 1 (Health) alignment
- Workforce: cardiologists, nurses, emergency responders
- Data: European Health Data Space (Regulation (EU) 2025/327) enablers

Closing date: TBC -- typically 6 to 12 weeks for DG SANTE Calls for Evidence. Check HaveYourSay portal.

## Why Now

Political drivers for the CVD Plan:
1. **CVD = leading killer**: despite major progress on cancer (BECP), CVD has received less coordinated EU attention
2. **Inequality**: east-west and north-south gaps in CVD mortality persist (Bulgaria and Romania have ~2x the mortality rate of France/Spain)
3. **Air pollution link**: air pollution contributes to approximately 300k premature CVD deaths/year in the EU; AAQD revision (Directive (EU) 2024/2881) creates political opening
4. **Women's CV health gap**: under-diagnosis and under-treatment of CVD in women documented by ESC; equality angle
5. **Industry opportunity**: medical devices, pharmaceuticals, digital health tools -- Competitiveness Compass + Apply AI Strategy complementarity
6. **EU4Health 2028-34**: CVD plan becomes flagship for health envelope in next MFF

## Likely Policy Instruments

Based on BECP precedent, the CVD Plan is expected to combine:
- **Legislative**: revision of tobacco/alcohol/food labelling rules, possible salt reduction Regulation
- **Financial**: EU4Health actions, Horizon Europe missions, EIB health investment facility
- **Soft law**: Council Recommendation on CVD screening/prevention, best-practice exchange
- **Data/monitoring**: integration into European Health Data Space, EuroStat SILC health module
- **Awareness**: European CVD Awareness Week, school curricula guidance
