## The 21 April 2026 High-Level Meeting

Commission press release IP/26/856 (21 April 2026) announced that the EU, Ukraine and Canada will co-host a high-level meeting on returning Ukrainian children. This reinforces the existing International Coalition for the Return of Ukrainian Children (co-chaired Ukraine-Canada since 2024) and brings the EU institutional weight to the coalition.

Expected objectives:
1. Pressure on Russia to release children before peace negotiations conclude
2. Legal pathway: coordination with ICC, Ukrainian prosecutor, Council of Europe Register of Damage
3. Humanitarian pathway: reunification via Qatar, Vatican, Saudi Arabia, UAE intermediaries (Qatar mediated 100+ returns since 2022)
4. Identification and tracking: centralised database of deported children (Yale HRL + Ukrainian Ministry)
5. Support for returned children: psychosocial care, reintegration, Ukrainian citizenship recovery for those issued Russian passports

Likely deliverables: political declaration, funding pledges, commitment to expand the International Coalition membership.

## Legal Framework

### International Law
- **Geneva Convention IV (1949)**, Article 49 -- prohibition of forcible transfers and deportations of protected persons
- **Additional Protocol I (1977)**, Article 78 -- evacuation of children only in narrowly defined circumstances
- **Rome Statute**, Article 8(2)(a)(vii), 8(2)(b)(viii) -- war crimes of unlawful deportation
- **Genocide Convention (1948)**, Article II(e) -- forcibly transferring children of the group to another group
- **UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989)**, Articles 7, 8, 9, 10 -- right to preserve identity, family relations, nationality

### ICC Action
- ICC warrants (17 March 2023): Putin + Lvova-Belova for war crime of unlawful deportation and transfer of children
- ICC Trust Fund for Victims active in Ukraine
- Russia not a State Party to the Rome Statute -- surrender conditional on Putin/Lvova-Belova travelling to a State Party or the matter being referred to UN Security Council (vetoed by Russia)

### EU Legal Response
- **Council Decision (CFSP) 2023/432** -- individual sanctions on Lvova-Belova and 9 associated persons (17 children's rights officials, judges, directors of children's camps)
- **Directive (EU) 2011/95** (Qualification Directive) -- applies to Ukrainian children who cross EU borders
- **Temporary Protection Directive (2001/55/EC)** -- activated 4 March 2022 by Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/382; extended to March 2027 by Decision (EU) 2025/XXX
- **Eurojust Regulation (EU) 2018/1727** -- Joint Investigation Team on international crimes in Ukraine, includes child deportation cases

## Bring Kids Back UA Initiative

Ukrainian government-led initiative, 10-point plan:
1. Identification and search for deported children
2. Return of children to Ukraine or re-unification with families
3. Psychological and social support for returned children
4. Recovery of personal documents and Ukrainian citizenship
5. Health rehabilitation (some children issued Russian vaccines/medical records)
6. Education support (catch-up for missed schooling + language)
7. Legal support (prosecution of perpetrators)
8. International coalition building
9. Information campaign
10. Monitoring and accountability

Partners: Saving Ukraine (NGO), Save Ukraine (NGO), SOS Children's Villages, UNICEF, Council of Europe, Qatar (mediation), Vatican (mediation).
