# EIC Advanced Innovation Challenges (AIC) — Pilot Process (2026-2027)

## QUICK FACTS
- New 2026 **pilot**, inspired by the ARPA approach but adapted to Horizon Europe rules: stage-gated, demand-driven grants steered by EIC Programme Managers, with users (industry, public sector, regulators) involved from the start.
- **Stage 1 (2026):** lump sum of **EUR 300,000** for **at most 9 months** to validate and benchmark a solution. Budget **EUR 6 million**. Deadline was **26 February 2026** (closed). Single applicants only: a start-up, SME or research performing organisation (larger companies cannot apply alone). Part B (cover + sections 1-3) **maximum 11 pages**. Projects had to start by **1 October 2026**.
- **Stage 2 (2027, restricted to Stage 1 projects):** lump sum **up to EUR 2.5 million** for **up to 2.5 years**, to reach TRL 6-7 with real-world testing. Indicative budget **EUR 25 million** and indicative deadline **18 June 2027**, both subject to the 2027 EIC Work Programme. Single entity or consortium of 2-3. Part B maximum **22 pages**. Evaluated through a **live pitch (virtual or physical) before an independent expert jury chaired by the EIC Programme Manager**; result about 6-8 weeks after the deadline.
- **Funding rate 100%** for both stages.
- **Thresholds (both stages):** Excellence 4/5, Impact 4/5, Implementation 3/5; Stage 1 adds a **portfolio-based selection** for a balanced set of solutions.
- **2026 Challenges:** `HORIZON-EIC-2026-AIC-01` **Accelerating Physical AI: Embodied Intelligence for the Next Frontier of AI-Powered Robotics** (Stage 1 entry: a physical AI prototype tested in the lab, at least TRL 4, test infrastructure, and a use case backed by a letter of intent from an end user or robotics integrator); `HORIZON-EIC-2026-AIC-02` **Translating Disruptive New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) into Practice**.

EIC Work Programme 2026, Commission Decision C(2026) 4080 (17 June 2026), Section IV and Annex 1; EIC Programme Managers page (read 8 October 2026); Funding & Tenders Portal topic records (search API, 8 October 2026).

## Stage 1 in detail
- At least three independent expert evaluators score each proposal (0-5 per criterion); proposals above all thresholds go to a portfolio selection by a committee chaired by the Programme Manager responsible for the Challenge.
- Criteria: Excellence (clear problem and compelling solution; novelty and disruptive potential; credible performance gains), Impact (new or transformed markets; adoption and scale-up; attractiveness to investors, industry or procurers; credible commercial impact), Implementation (realistic work plan; resources; applicant capacity).
- Grant agreement signed quickly, at the latest about 5 months after the deadline; applicants were urged to prepare PIC validation and bank details with their National Contact Point in advance.
- Each project allocates at least 1 person-month to pro-active portfolio management and takes part in a user workshop organised by the Agency.

## Stage 2 in detail
- Only projects funded in Stage 1 may apply; the Stage 1 entity must be the applicant or a consortium member, or the applicant must hold the rights to use the Stage 1 results.
- Progress to Stage 2 depends on a full proposal assessed together with the Stage 1 milestones (for Physical AI: benchmarked performance data in at least one pilot case and a clear commitment from end users).
- Criteria: Excellence (advances over competitors; credible and robust foundation; ambition), Impact (market creation; users or customers attracted; investor attractiveness), Implementation (credible work plan; capacity to execute fast under uncertainty; risks).
- The Stage 2 grant may start retroactively on the day the result is communicated.

## How AIC differs from Pathfinder Challenges
| Feature | AIC | Pathfinder Challenges |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | prototype tested in the lab (TRL 4 for Physical AI) | early research (around TRL 2) |
| Funding | two stages: EUR 300,000 then up to EUR 2.5 million | one grant, up to about EUR 4 million |
| Lump sum | yes | yes (2026 Pathfinder grants are lump sums too) |
| Applicants | Stage 1 single entities only; Stage 2 single or 2-3 partners | single entities or consortia |
| 2026 budget | EUR 6 million (Stage 1) | EUR 96 million |
