# Connecting Europe Facility (CEF) — Transport envelope

## QUICK FACTS

- **Full name**: Regulation (EU) 2021/1153 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 July 2021 establishing the Connecting Europe Facility and repealing Regulations (EU) No 1316/2013 and (EU) No 283/2014
- **CELEX**: 32021R1153
- **EUR-Lex**: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32021R1153
- **OJ reference**: OJ L 249, 14.7.2021
- **Period**: 2021-2027 (next CEF II envelope under MFF 2028-2034 in design)
- **Total budget (2021-2027)**: **€33.7 billion**
- **Lead DG**: DG MOVE (Mobility and Transport) — call administration delegated to **CINEA** (Climate, Infrastructure and Environment Executive Agency)
- **Commissioner**: Apostolos Tzitzikostas (Sustainable Transport and Tourism)
- **CINEA Director**: cross-check current organigramme
- **CEF Energy €600M call (29 April 2026)**: CINEA launched a €600 million CEF Energy call for energy infrastructure projects. NOTE — CEF Energy is the second envelope of the same regulation (Reg (EU) 2021/1153) but distinct from CEF Transport. CEF Energy is led by **DG ENER** (not DG MOVE) and supports cross-border electricity, hydrogen, smart grids, and offshore renewable infrastructure. Brubru tracking should treat CEF Transport and CEF Energy as separate guides — split planned. Source: cinea.ec.europa.eu 29 April 2026.

## Three envelopes

| Envelope | Budget (€B) | Eligibility |
|---|---:|---|
| **General Envelope (Transport)** | **25.81** | All Member States; pre-identified projects on TEN-T core/extended core/comprehensive layers |
| **Cohesion Envelope** | **11.29** | Member States eligible for the Cohesion Fund (lower-GDP MS) |
| **Military Mobility** | **1.69** | Dual-use infrastructure on TEN-T identified by the Member States + Commission for joint civil-defence use |

The **Military Mobility envelope** was new under CEF II (2021-2027) and reflects the post-2017 Council push for dual-use logistics. See `military_mobility_dual_use_logistics.md`.

## What CEF Transport funds

| Action type | Co-financing rate | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| **Studies** | up to 50% | Feasibility, design, environmental impact assessments |
| **Works on the Core Network** (cross-border) | up to 50% | Rail tunnels, bridges, freight corridor capacity upgrades |
| **Works on the Comprehensive Network** | up to 30% | Less strategic projects |
| **ERTMS deployment** | up to 50% | Signalling rollout on Core Network corridors |
| **Innovation and digitalisation** | up to 50% | Telematics applications, traffic management |
| **Synergy with energy + digital** | up to 50% | e.g. rail electrification + alternative fuels infrastructure |

Cohesion Envelope projects can receive co-financing rates up to **85%** (works on the Cohesion territory).

## Annual Work Programme + calls

The **CEF Transport Multi-Annual Work Programme (MAWP)** is published every 2-3 years. Annual calls are typically:
- **Q3 each year**: indicative call topics published
- **Q4 each year**: calls open
- **Q1-Q2 next year**: applications due (~3-4 months window)
- **Q3-Q4 next year**: evaluation + grant agreement signature

Recent calls (illustrative):
- **CEF Transport call 2024** — €5 billion across 9 priority areas
- **CEF Transport call 2023** — €7 billion (largest single call)
- **Military Mobility call 2024** — €807 million

## How CEF interacts with the TEN-T Regulation

CEF is the **funding instrument**; TEN-T Regulation 2024/1679 is the **policy framework**. The TEN-T Regulation defines the network + standards + deadlines; CEF funds the projects that close the gap. Reg 2024/1679 explicitly amends Reg 2021/1153 (CEF) to align eligibility with the new three-layer network architecture.

## Funding rules + audit

- **Pre-identified projects**: Annex I of CEF Regulation lists pre-identified projects + corridors. Projects on this list have priority but still compete in calls.
- **CINEA evaluation**: independent expert evaluation, ranked, then committee vote
- **Grant agreement**: typically 4-7 years duration, milestone-based payments
- **Audit + clawback**: Commission can claw back funds if milestones missed; OLAF + EPPO have jurisdiction over fraud cases

## MFF 2028-2034 — successor instrument

The current CEF expires end-2027. The Commission's MFF 2028-2034 proposal (COM(2025) 570, 16 July 2025) reorganises EU funding into a smaller number of consolidated envelopes. The **European Competitiveness Fund** consolidates many existing instruments. CEF Transport is expected to continue in some form — confirm via OEIL once the MFF 2028-2034 sectoral regulations are tabled (expected Q3-Q4 2026).

EP plenary adopted its negotiating mandate on 29 April 2026 at 1.27% of EU GNI ceiling.
