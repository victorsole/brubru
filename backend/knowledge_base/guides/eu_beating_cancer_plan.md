Guide covering Europe's Beating Cancer Plan, EU cancer policy framework, screening programmes, pharmaceutical measures, and EP implementation review.

## Overview

Cancer is the second leading cause of death in the EU, with 2.7 million new cases and 1.3 million deaths annually. Europe's Beating Cancer Plan (COM(2021) 44) is the Commission's comprehensive strategy to address the full cancer pathway: prevention, early detection, diagnosis and treatment, and quality of life for patients and survivors.

The plan allocates EUR 4 billion across multiple EU programmes and proposes 10 flagship initiatives.

## Four Pillars

### Pillar 1: Prevention

- Tobacco control: Revision of Tobacco Products Directive, tobacco excise harmonisation (TEDOR)
- Alcohol: EU approach to harmful alcohol consumption, cancer warning labels
- HPV vaccination: Target 90% of target population by 2030
- Environmental carcinogens: REACH restrictions on workplace carcinogens (6th revision of Carcinogens and Mutagens Directive, Directive (EU) 2022/431)
- Physical activity, nutrition, obesity prevention

### Pillar 2: Early Detection

- **Cancer Screening Recommendation** (Council Recommendation, 2 December 2022): Expanded screening beyond breast, cervical, and colorectal to include lung, prostate, and gastric cancers
- Target: 90% of eligible EU population offered screening by 2025 (breast, cervical, colorectal)
- EU-supported screening schemes and quality assurance guidelines
- Digital health tools for early detection

### Pillar 3: Diagnosis and Treatment

- EU Comprehensive Cancer Centres: Network of excellence, target 90% of patients with access by 2030
- European Reference Networks (ERNs): Cross-border specialist care for rare cancers
- Cancer Diagnostic and Treatment for All (CancerDiag) initiative
- Digital tools: European Cancer Imaging Initiative, AI-assisted diagnostics
- Pharmaceutical strategy: Access to cancer medicines, shortages prevention

### Pillar 4: Quality of Life

- Survivorship: Estimated 12 million cancer survivors in EU by 2030
- Right to be Forgotten: Commission recommendation on financial services access for cancer survivors
- Return to work: Guidelines for employers
- Palliative care: Quality standards across Member States
- Childhood cancer: European initiative on understanding cancers in children

## EP Implementation Review (April 2026)

### SANT Draft Report: Implementation of Europe's Beating Cancer Plan

- **Document:** PE786.738v01-00
- **Rapporteur:** Vlad Vasile-Voiculescu (Renew, Romania)
- **Committee:** SANT (Subcommittee on Public Health)
- **Status:** Draft report stage (April 2026)
- **Scope:** Assessment of progress on all 10 flagship initiatives, screening uptake, cancer inequalities, pharmaceutical access, and remaining gaps

This is the EP's first comprehensive implementation review of the Beating Cancer Plan since its launch in 2021. Expected to identify areas where implementation has lagged and recommend corrective actions.

## Horizon Europe Mission on Cancer

- Budget: EUR 378.2 million (2021-2027)
- Goal: By 2030, more than 3 million additional lives saved, living longer and better
- Focus: Understanding cancer, prevention and early detection, diagnosis and treatment, quality of life
- Mission Board: Independent expert advisory body
