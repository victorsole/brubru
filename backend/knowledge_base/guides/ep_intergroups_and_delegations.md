# European Parliament Intergroups and Delegations

## QUICK FACTS
- **Two cross-cutting structures** beyond committees: **intergroups** (informal cross-party thematic groupings of MEPs) and **delegations** (official bodies for relations with non-EU parliaments).
- **Intergroups** (~28 in term 10): NOT official EP bodies. They let MEPs from different groups and committees meet on a theme (SMEs, animal welfare, LGBTIQ+, anti-corruption, cancer, etc.). Governed by EP rules on their establishment; they may not engage in activities that could be confused with Parliament's official work.
- **Delegations** (~48): **official** standing bodies maintaining the EP's relations with the parliaments of non-EU countries and with regional assemblies — bilateral (e.g. D-US, D-CN, D-UA) and multilateral (joint parliamentary assemblies, parliamentary cooperation committees).
- **Why Brubru cares**: intergroup membership signals MEP thematic priorities (useful for Position Analysis / outreach); delegations frame the EP's external/foreign-policy engagement (relevant to AFET/INTA files and trade/association agreements).
- **Source**: `europarl.europa.eu/meps/en/about/groupings` (intergroups) + `europarl.europa.eu/delegations/en/` (delegations).

## Intergroups
- **What they are**: voluntary groupings of MEPs across political groups and committees, formed to exchange views on a particular theme and with civil society. They have **no legislative power** and are not part of Parliament's official structure.
- **How they form**: established at the start of a term under EP rules ("Rules governing the establishment of intergroups"); must be supported by at least three political groups; members declare any support received (transparency).
- **Examples (term 10, ~28)**: Anti-Corruption; Anti-Racism and Diversity; Biodiversity, Hunting and Countryside; Cancer and Rare Diseases; Children's Rights; Disability; Freedom of Religion or Belief; LGBTIQ+; Mental Health; SMEs; Seas, Rivers, Islands and Coastal Areas (SEARICA); Sky and Space; Social Economy; Trade Unions; Welfare and Conservation of Animals; Wine, Quality Foodstuffs and Spirits; among others.
- **Unofficial groupings**: some thematic MEP groupings exist outside the formal intergroup framework; they are listed separately and carry no official status.
- **Data**: each intergroup has a page at `meps/en/intergroup/details/{id}/{name}`.

## Delegations
- **What they are**: official EP bodies (each with a chair, vice-chairs and members) that maintain and develop the Parliament's international contacts at parliamentary level. They mirror the EU's external relations and feed Parliament's input on enlargement, association and trade.
- **Types**:
  - **Bilateral interparliamentary delegations** — relations with a single country or group (e.g. D-US United States, D-CN China, D-UA Ukraine, D-IL Israel, D-JP Japan).
  - **Joint Parliamentary Committees (JPCs)** — with candidate/associated countries.
  - **Parliamentary Cooperation Committees (PCCs)** and **multilateral assemblies** — e.g. ACP-EU, Euro-Latin American (EuroLat), Euronest, Union for the Mediterranean, Euromed.
- **Activities** (per delegation): ordinary meetings, interparliamentary meetings, a calendar, and documents (meeting documents, EP resolutions, EU texts, communiqués).
- **Data**: each delegation has pages at `delegations/en/{code}/…` (home, members, about/history, activities, documents).

## How Brubru uses this
- **Chat**: answer "is there an EP intergroup on X?" or "which delegation handles relations with country Y?" from this guide.
- **Position Analysis / outreach**: an MEP's intergroup memberships indicate thematic priorities and potential allies on a file.
- **External-policy files**: delegation activity (resolutions, communiqués) contextualises AFET/INTA/DEVE dossiers and trade/association agreements.
