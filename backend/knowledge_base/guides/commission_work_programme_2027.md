# Commission work programme 2027 (COM(2026) 710): 23 new initiatives, 18 withdrawals, 14 repeals

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted Wednesday 4 November 2026: COM(2026) 710 final, "Commission work programme 2027. European Union - standing up for one another"** (CELEX 52026DC0710, with four annexes). A plan, NOT law: every date in the annexes is indicative ("subject to change").
- **Annex I: exactly 23 new initiatives.** Single market and competitiveness: preventing market segmentation by suppliers (Q3), permitting acceleration (Q3), single market barriers prevention (Q3), bank competitiveness and simplification package (Q1), European Corporation on Critical Raw Materials, digital transformation package with simplification and copyright (Q2), transport simplification package (Q1), RefuelEU Aviation review (Q2), energy post-2030 framework and simplification package (Q1). Security: update of visa rules (Q1), Prum international police data exchange (Q2). Social: housing simplification package (Q2), European care deal (Q2), European Universities alliances 2.0 incl. a legal status (Q4), skills guarantee (Q3), right to stay (Q1). Environment: review of heavy-duty vehicle CO2 standards (Q3), European water initiative (Q2), European heatwave plan (Q2), review of the Birds and Habitats directives and Natura 2000 (Q3). Democracy: Recommendation on banning conversion practices (Q2), Recommendation on safety in politics (Q1), review of the political advertising Regulation. All quarters are 2027.
- **Annex III: exactly 18 proposals the Commission intends to WITHDRAW within six months.** Status: listed for withdrawal, NOT withdrawn as of 6 November 2026 (Parliament and Council may first give their views). They are: airport slots 2011/0391(COD); European Deposit Insurance Scheme 2015/0270(COD); significant digital presence tax 2018/0072(CNS); **Green Claims Directive 2023/0085(COD)**; **Financial Data Access (FIDA) 2023/0205(COD)**; Head Office Tax 2023/0320(CNS); **BEFIT 2023/0321(CNS)**; **Late Payment Regulation 2023/0323(COD)**; transport operators facilitating smuggling 2021/0387(COD); visa exemption Kuwait and Qatar 2022/0135(COD); long-term residents recast 2022/0134(COD); European cross-border associations 2023/0314(COD) and 2023/0315(COD); the communication on adding hate speech to EU crimes COM(2021) 777; **2023/0463(COD), the DIRECTIVE on interest representation on behalf of third countries**, and SEPARATELY 2023/0462(COD), its companion REGULATION amending the IMI and Single Digital Gateway Regulations (both "withdrawn in absence of further progress"); two Egypt PRIMA Council decisions COM(2025) 494 and 495.
- **The Energy Taxation Directive is NOT on the withdrawal list** (a press leak of 3 November said it might be).
- **Annex IV: exactly 14 envisaged repeals** of spent acts. Annex II: priority pending proposals.
- **The Commission's own figures (press release IP/26/2321, Q&A QANDA/26/2322):** simplification proposals so far could save over EUR 22 billion a year; new "Pact against gold-plating" and a "review and remove" mechanism in the European Semester.
- Adopted with the **2026 Annual Overview Report on Simplification** (COM(2026) 715, see `annual_overview_report_simplification_2026`).
- **What was NOT read:** Annex II in full (only its title and count).
