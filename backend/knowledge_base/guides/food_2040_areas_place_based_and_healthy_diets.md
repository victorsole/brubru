# Food 2040 areas 1 and 2: place-based food systems; healthy and sustainable diets

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** the first two of the **six** Food 2040 areas of action (COM(2026) 453, section 3.4). Food 2040 has six areas; AgRI 2040 has eight.
- **Area 1, place-based food systems:** connects with the enabler **territories and communities**. Local (including urban) and regional food economies are resilient, inclusive incubators of innovation and social cohesion. It combines technological and social innovation with community engagement, education and skills, across **rural-urban and city-region interfaces**, entrepreneurship initiatives, co-creation spaces, networks of cities and **Living Labs**. **Regional Innovation Valleys for Bioeconomy and Food Systems** are the named example of interregional collaboration.
- **Area 2, healthy and sustainable diets:** R&I for healthier, more sustainable and nutritious foods, in line with the priority on food and feed safety, nutrition and health. It supports **diversification of protein sources**, citizens' awareness of **biotech-based ingredients** and **AI to predict food safety**.
- **Area 2 topics:** food safety, contaminants, macro- and micronutrients, enzymes, reformulation, food-health interactions; **microbiome science** (food and gut microbiome functionality, links to human disorders, microbial biomarkers); cultural, behavioural and social drivers of diets; the needs of an **ageing population**; accessibility and affordability; dietary diversification **within planetary boundaries**. The Communication notes that over half of the world's population struggles to access healthy diets.
- **Related Food 2030 work:** the pathways on urban food systems, nutrition and sustainable healthy diets, the microbiome and alternative proteins (see `food_2030_funding_results_and_evaluation`).
