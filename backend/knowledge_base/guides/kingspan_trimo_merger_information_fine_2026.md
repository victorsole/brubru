# Commission fines Kingspan EUR 40 million for misleading information in a merger review (October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **8 October 2026** the European Commission fined **Kingspan EUR 40 million** for providing incorrect and misleading information during the Commission's 2021 review of Kingspan's planned acquisition of **Trimo**, under the EU Merger Regulation (EUMR, Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004, CELEX 32004R0139). Press release IP/26/2084.
- **The fine is for the information, NOT for the merger.** Kingspan abandoned the acquisition in April 2022; the fine was imposed later, in October 2026.
- **Exactly four infringements, EUR 10 million each (total EUR 40 million):** (1) misleading information about how Kingspan tracks penetration rates for mineral fibre sandwich panels; (2) incorrect or misleading information about the availability of "bidding data" (records of bids won and lost); (3) incorrect information about Kingspan board members' involvement in the planned acquisition, other acquisitions and industry trends; (4) incorrect or misleading information about Kingspan's research and development for mineral fibre sandwich panels. The Commission treated each as serious and at least negligent.
- **How it was given:** in the merger notification form or in replies to requests for information under Articles 11(2) and 11(3) EUMR. The legal basis for the fine is **Article 14(1) EUMR**, which allows fines of up to **1% of total turnover** for intentionally or negligently supplying incorrect or misleading information.
- **Timeline:** notification March 2021; in-depth investigation opened April 2021; Statement of Objections on the deal March 2022; deal abandoned April 2022; the Commission opened the information-fine investigation in **November 2022**; Statement of Objections of **19 March 2024** (six instances alleged); a Supplementary Statement of Objections dropped two of the six; decision 8 October 2026.
- **Market:** mineral fibre sandwich panels, used to build, renovate and insulate industrial and commercial buildings.
- **Appeal:** the press release does not say whether Kingspan will appeal. Do not state it.
- Source: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2084

## Why it matters

Merging parties must give the Commission correct, non-misleading information both in the notification and in every reply to a request for information. The Commission says notifications and information requests are its primary sources for reviewing a merger, so accuracy and completeness are essential. The Commission found that Kingspan asserted the non-existence of key documents, misrepresented facts or claimed it could not provide key information that only the company could supply, and it fined the company even though the deal had been abandoned.
