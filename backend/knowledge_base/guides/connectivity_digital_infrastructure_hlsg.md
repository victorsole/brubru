# High-Level Study Group on Connectivity and Digital Infrastructure

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Commission High-Level Study Group on Connectivity and Digital Infrastructure -- expert group convened to inform EU connectivity and digital infrastructure policy
- Announced: 20 April 2026 (Commission welcomes study group)
- Convened by: DG CNECT (Communications Networks, Content and Technology)
- Responsible Commissioner: Henna Virkkunen (EVP for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy)
- Responsible DG Director-General: Renate Nikolay (Acting DG CNECT from March 2026; confirmation process ongoing)
- Related Commission files: Digital Networks Act COM(2026) 0016 (procedure 2026/0013(COD)); White Paper on digital infrastructure needs (COM(2024) 81)
- Mandate: advise on regulatory and investment priorities for Europe's digital infrastructure -- fibre, 5G/6G networks, cloud, edge compute, submarine cables, satellite connectivity
- Duration: 18 months (initial), renewable; deliverables in tranches (interim report Q3 2026, final report Q1 2027)
- Composition: expected ~15-20 members -- senior industry executives, academics, infrastructure operators, Member State representatives, civil society
- Expected outputs: policy recommendations for next Commission mandate; input into Digital Networks Act trilogues; coordination with Member State digital ministries
- Budget: supported under Digital Europe Programme (Horizon Europe where applicable)

## Why Now

The EU faces three connected challenges:

1. **Infrastructure investment gap**: Draghi report estimates EUR 200 billion annual digital investment gap. European telcos fragmented vs US hyperscalers.
2. **Strategic autonomy**: submarine cables + satellite connectivity increasingly contested (CableGATE, IRIS2 constellation scale-up)
3. **6G standardisation**: global race with US + China; EU SNS JU (Smart Networks and Services Joint Undertaking) scaling R&D but industrial deployment lags

The HLSG follows the Commission's White Paper "How to master Europe's digital infrastructure needs?" (COM(2024) 81) and feeds into:
- Digital Networks Act (COM(2026) 0016, proposed 21 January 2026)
- Digital Decade 2030 targets (75% cloud adoption, 100% 5G coverage in populated areas, 10,000 climate-neutral edge nodes)
- Tech Sovereignty Package (scheduled 27 May 2026, delayed from 23 April)
- Common EU Telecoms Market proposal (part of Tech Sovereignty Package)

## Expected Focus Areas

1. **Telecom market consolidation**: single-market obstacles to pan-European operators; spectrum harmonisation
2. **Fibre deployment**: gigabit connectivity targets (Gigabit Infrastructure Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1309)
3. **6G roadmap**: coordination with 3GPP, ETSI, ITU-R; funding continuity post-Horizon Europe
4. **Cloud + edge**: interaction with Cloud and AI Development Act (Tech Sovereignty Package); sovereign cloud; GAIA-X follow-up
5. **Submarine cables**: EU Cable Security Action Plan (2025); 7 critical cable projects identified
6. **Satellite connectivity**: IRIS2 (Infrastructure for Resilience, Interconnectivity and Security by Satellite) scale-up; LEO constellations
7. **Data centres**: environmental footprint (Energy Efficiency Directive (EU) 2023/1791, data centre reporting obligations from May 2024)
8. **Submarine cables + security**: interplay with DORA, NIS2, CER (Critical Entities Resilience Directive)
