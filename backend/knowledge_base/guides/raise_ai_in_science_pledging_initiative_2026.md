# RAISE pledging initiative for AI in science (launched 15 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **15 October 2026**, at the **European AI in Science Summit in Dublin** (organised by the Commission and the Irish Council Presidency), the Commission launched the **RAISE (Resource for AI Science in Europe) pledging initiative for "AI in Science Enablers"** (press release IP/26/2175).
- **Purpose:** mobilise **private-sector and philanthropic** support for European researchers working with AI, beyond what public funding provides, and create matchmaking between private actors and the AI-in-science community.
- **What can be pledged (in kind or financial):** access to frontier AI models, computing capacity, research software licences, data, cloud services, scholarships, fellowships and training. A pledge must show sustained support **beyond direct commercial interest**.
- **Initial commitments (the Commission's list, seven):** Novo Nordisk Foundation, Nvidia, Elsevier, Sanofi, Frontiers, Mistral and Google DeepMind. The initiative stays open.
- **Mistral AI and CERN** announced, within RAISE, a partnership to develop what is "set to become the largest foundation model in physics" (Commission wording; no size or date given).
- **Also launched or presented:** the **RAISE Hub, RAISE Forum and RAISE Observatory** (online platforms), and **updated Living Guidelines on the Responsible Use of Generative AI in Research**, now covering agentic AI.
- **Review:** annually, with pledges presented at future editions of the summit.
- **Background:** part of the **European Strategy for AI in Science** (October 2025), alongside the Apply AI Strategy. RAISE is not a funding programme with its own EU budget line; no euro amount is given in the press release.
- **Not read:** the pledge texts of each company and the RAISE Hub pages.
