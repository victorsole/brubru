# European Semester 2027 Autumn Package (24 November 2026): draft budget verdicts, deficit procedures, Alert Mechanism Report

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted by the Commission on Tuesday 24 November 2026** and presented the same day to Parliament (Commissioner Dombrovskis, EVP Mînzatu). It opens the 2027 Semester cycle. Recommendations in it are proposals until the Council adopts them.
- **Documents (Commission register, 24 Nov):** COM(2026) 955 (the Autumn Package communication), COM(2026) 956 (Alert Mechanism Report 2027), COM(2026) 957 (Council Recommendation on the economic policy of the euro area), COM(2026) 958 (proposal for a Joint Employment Report 2027), COM(2026) 959 (Council Recommendation on human capital), COM(2026) 1050 (assessment of fiscal policies for 2026-2027), COM(2026) 1051 (Article 126(3) report on Germany and Lithuania), COM(2026) 1052 (no effective action: Bulgaria), COM(2026) 1053 (no effective action: Hungary).
- **Draft budgetary plans of 17 euro-area countries, exactly three groups:** compliant (8): Cyprus, Estonia, France, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Portugal, Slovakia. At risk of non-compliance (2): Finland, Germany. At risk of MATERIAL non-compliance (6): Croatia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Slovenia. Bulgaria: awaiting a revised corrective path; its announced measures "would contribute" to correcting the deficit.
- **Excessive deficit procedures: 10 Member States.** Held in abeyance for 8 (Austria, Belgium, Finland, France, Italy, Poland, Romania, Slovakia): no new step, procedure still open. Italy is projected to correct its deficit in 2026; the Commission "may consider" closing its procedure in spring 2027 if outturn data confirm it. **Bulgaria and Hungary: the Commission recommends Council decisions under Article 126(8) TFEU establishing NO effective action.** Germany and Lithuania: Article 126(3) report (the first step towards a possible procedure), NOT a procedure opened.
- **Alert Mechanism Report 2027: in-depth reviews for five countries:** Hungary, Italy, Slovakia (imbalances last cycle), Romania (excessive imbalances), and **Bulgaria, new** (current-account deficit, cost competitiveness, house prices and household borrowing). Reviews in the first half of 2027; decisions in the Spring Package.
- **Euro area:** fiscal stance broadly neutral in 2026, mildly contractionary in 2027; Autumn 2026 Economic Forecast (17 Nov): aggregate deficit up markedly in 2026, broadly stable in 2027, debt ratio still rising.
- **People:** Joint Employment Report: labour market resilient, job growth slowed, notably in manufacturing. Human capital Recommendation: skills shortages in strategic sectors, especially clean energy and defence including space. A European Skills Observatory was launched the same day.
- **Tax gaps, same day ("Mind the Gap 2026", COM(2026) 630, DG TAXUD):** over EUR 200 billion lost every year to tax compliance gaps; VAT gap about EUR 125 billion in 2023 (9.5% of liabilities); corporate tax gap almost EUR 50 billion (9.2%, 25 Member States); tax expenditures cost about EUR 750 billion a year.
- **Sources:** IP/26/2553, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_2553 ; Q&A QANDA/26/2554 ; COM numbers read on the Commission's document register on 25 Nov.
- **What was NOT read:** the country opinions and the AMR text themselves (only the press release and Q&A). Country figures (deficits, debt) are not in this guide: do not give any.
