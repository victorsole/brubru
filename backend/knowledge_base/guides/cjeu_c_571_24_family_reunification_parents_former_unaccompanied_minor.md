# Court of Justice, Case C-571/24: reasonable time for parents' family reunification after a refugee minor turns 18 (8 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Ruling:** judgment of the Court of Justice (**Fourth Chamber**) of **8 October 2026** in **Case C-571/24** (CELEX 62024CJ0571), a preliminary reference from the **Oberverwaltungsgericht Berlin-Brandenburg (Germany)** of 14 May 2024. Language of the case: German. Hearing 18 September 2025; the Advocate General's opinion was delivered on 30 October 2025.
- **Question:** under **Article 10(3)(a) of Council Directive 2003/86/EC on the right to family reunification**, a refugee who is an **unaccompanied minor** may be joined by parents. The Court's earlier judgment **A and S (C-550/16, 12 April 2018)** dealt with a refugee who turns 18 during the asylum procedure (age assessed at the time of the asylum application). The new case asks within what time the parents must apply.
- **Holding:** where a third-country national **entered a Member State as an unaccompanied minor, reached the age of majority during the asylum procedure and was granted refugee status before the delivery of A and S (12 April 2018)**, the parents' application for family reunification **must be lodged within a reasonable period from the date on which the minor was recognised as a refugee**. **Whether the period is reasonable is assessed on all the circumstances of each case.**
- **Basis of this guide:** the operative part of the judgment as published on EUR-Lex was read; the Court's reasoning (grounds) was not, so state no reasoning, no legal principle and no earlier case beyond what is written here.
- **What it does not say:** it fixes **no number of days or months**; it does not cover refugees recognised after 12 April 2018 (the operative part is limited to those recognised before A and S); it does not cover subsidiary protection.
- **Context:** the reference also engages the Charter rights to respect for private and family life and the best interests of the child (Articles 7 and 24).

## How to answer
"How long do parents have to apply for family reunification with a refugee who was an unaccompanied minor?": for refugees recognised before 12 April 2018, within a reasonable period from recognition, judged case by case (C-571/24, 8 October 2026). No fixed deadline.
