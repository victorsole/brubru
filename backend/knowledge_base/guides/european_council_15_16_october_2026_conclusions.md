# European Council of 15-16 October 2026: conclusions adopted on 15 October (competitiveness and energy prices, long-term budget, enlargement)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status (16 October 2026): the European Council MET on 15 October and ADOPTED conclusions on three items:** I. Competitiveness (paragraphs 1-12), IV. Next Multiannual Financial Framework (paragraphs 34-35) and VIII. Enlargement and reforms (paragraphs 74-78). Source: Council press release published 16 October 2026, 00:40.
- **NOT read, NOT in that release:** sections II-III and V-VII (paragraphs 13-33 and 36-73). The invitation letter's other agenda items were Ukraine, Russia's hybrid attacks and defence readiness, migration and the Middle East; which section covers which is not known. The meeting continued on 16 October. Say these texts were not available in Brubru's sources; never describe their content.
- **Competitiveness (paragraphs 2-5):** leaders ask the co-legislators to conclude by the **end of 2026** the files of the "One Europe, One Market" roadmap, naming the **28th regime for company law ("EU Inc.", a proposed Regulation, NOT a directive), the Industrial Accelerator Act, the European grids package, the digital, environmental and automotive simplification packages, and the automotive package**. They "take note" of the Council positions on the food and feed safety simplification package and on the market integration and supervision package. They ask the Commission for a proposal to **cut reporting obligations, in particular for SMEs**, and call for "legislative and regulatory self-restraint".
- **Energy prices (paragraphs 6-11):** a coordinated response of **"targeted and temporary measures"**. The European Council **welcomes** the Commission proposal postponing certain obligations of the **Methane Regulation**, and welcomes the Commission's **intention** (not yet a decision) to: (1) **prolong the temporary State aid framework** for the most exposed industrial sectors; (2) develop a **short-term framework for EU refineries**; (3) **bundle energy demand on a voluntary basis**. It asks the co-legislators to **accelerate the Market Stability Reserve file** and the Commission to **update its assessment of all impacts of ETS2** (design and safeguards).
- **Multiannual Financial Framework 2028-2034 (paragraphs 34-35):** leaders discussed the Irish Presidency's **updated draft negotiating box** and invited **President Costa** to take negotiations forward. An agreement **before the end of 2026** would allow the legal acts to be adopted in 2027 so that EU funding continues without interruption in January 2028. **No figures were agreed.**
- **Enlargement (paragraphs 74-78):** accession stays merit-based; the Council is asked to analyse the Commission communication **"Preparing for a wider Union"**; leaders welcome the drafting of the **accession treaty with Montenegro** and note the Irish Presidency's intention to provisionally close Montenegro's remaining chapters, subject to reforms.
- **Press, not the conclusions:** POLITICO reported on 16 October that the budget showdown "slips to November". That is a report, not a decision of the European Council.
