## What this report is about
The report examines how the EU can deepen integration **using existing Treaty provisions** -- without triggering a Convention or full Treaty revision under Article 48 TEU. Key levers:

### 1. Passerelle clauses (general bridges)
- **Article 48(7) TEU general passerelle**: European Council can decide unanimously to switch a TFEU area from unanimity to QMV, or from special legislative procedure to ordinary legislative procedure (subject to no national parliament objection within 6 months)
- **Article 48(7) TEU procedural passerelle**: switch a special legislative procedure to OLP

### 2. Specific passerelle clauses (sectoral bridges)
- **CFSP (Article 31(3) TEU)**: European Council can extend QMV to other CFSP areas
- **Family law cross-border (Article 81(3) TFEU)**: Council can decide unanimously to switch certain family-law measures to OLP
- **Multiannual Financial Framework (Article 312(2) TFEU)**: European Council can authorise the Council to act by QMV on the MFF Regulation
- **Environment fiscal measures (Article 192(2) TFEU)**: passerelle to OLP for measures of fiscal nature
- **Social policy (Article 153(2) TFEU)**: passerelle for some social-policy measures

### 3. Enhanced cooperation (Article 20 TEU + Articles 326-334 TFEU)
- Group of Member States proceeds with deeper integration; minimum 9 MS; non-discrimination toward non-participants

### 4. Article 218 TFEU framework for international agreements
- Mixed agreements vs EU-only agreements
- Provisional application as a tool for early effects ahead of full ratification (e.g. Mercosur ITA)

## Why this matters in 2026
- **Russia-Ukraine war + 2024 enlargement push** revived debate about CFSP QMV under Article 31(3) TEU
- **MFF 2028-2034 negotiations**: failure to reach Council unanimity by end-2026 could push for Article 312(2) TFEU passerelle
- **Defence integration**: 2025/2212(INI) Common European Defence Union INI explicitly explores Article 31(3) TEU passerelle as an option short of full Treaty change
- **Spitzenkandidaten and EU lead candidates**: institutional cooperation under existing Articles 14, 17 TEU rather than Treaty change
