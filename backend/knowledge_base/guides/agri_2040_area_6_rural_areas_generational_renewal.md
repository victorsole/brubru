# AgRI 2040 area 6 of 8: rural areas (services and generational renewal)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** sixth of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). The Commission web page words it "rural areas, services and generational renewal". Preliminary, no obligation.
- **Purpose:** R&I for the **competitiveness and attractiveness of rural areas**, supporting **generational renewal** and improving **services and infrastructure** for living and working conditions; stronger links between farms, forests, territories and food systems.
- **Particular attention:** **innovation and entrepreneurship by women and young people** in rural areas; **place-based approaches** at local scale, including **regulatory sandboxes**.
- **Public services:** innovation in **education, healthcare, transport and mobility and digital connectivity**; market access; green businesses.
- **Jobs and skills:** new job opportunities in diverse sectors to retain talent and attract new actors; **education, vocational training and lifelong learning**; robust **digital infrastructures**.
- **Related strategies and funding:** the strategy for generational renewal in agriculture (COM(2025) 872); Horizon Europe flagship action "Research and innovation for rural communities" (EUR 250 million to over 60 projects, SWD(2026) 271); EUR 183 million (33 projects) for rural areas since 2016.
- **Rural facts:** 25% of the EU population live in rural areas, which cover more than 75% of the territory.
