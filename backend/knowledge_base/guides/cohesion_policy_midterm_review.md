# Cohesion Policy 2021-2027: Mid-Term Review

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: Mid-term review (MTR) of Cohesion Policy 2021-2027 -- reallocation of cohesion funds in response to new priorities
- **LATEST (22 April 2026)**: DG REGIO formally republished **"Results of the Mid-Term Review of cohesion policy"** news item on whats-new/newsroom. Confirms aggregate MS reallocation patterns: Eastern/Baltic defence-heavy, Southern water+housing, Western skills+grids, Nordic civil protection+Arctic. Establishes baseline for 2028-2034 cohesion negotiations under MFF successor.
- **LATEST (14 April 2026)**: DG REGIO published "Advancing the Just Transition in Europe: Results of the first PSLF call for proposals" -- Public Sector Loan Facility under Just Transition Mechanism Pillar 3 awards first call projects. Pairs with MTR reallocation results as twin cohesion/JTF signals.
- **LATEST (21 April 2026)**: DG REGIO news item "Results of the Mid-Term Review of cohesion policy" first published. Sets out aggregate MS reallocation choices, sectoral shifts, and baseline for 2028-2034 cohesion planning.
- Legal basis: Common Provisions Regulation (CPR), Regulation (EU) 2021/1060 (CELEX 32021R1060), Articles 18 and 86
- Responsible DG: DG REGIO (Regional and Urban Policy)
- Responsible Commissioner: Raffaele Fitto (EVP, Cohesion and Reforms, Italy) -- also Cohesion portfolio
- Commission "Mid-Term Review and Cohesion Policy Renewed Focus" Communication: COM(2025) 58 final, adopted 18 February 2025
- CPR amendment for MTR: Regulation (EU) 2025/XXX (adopted October 2025) -- enables flexibility: up to **20% of allocations can be reprogrammed** without major approval process
- Mid-term review deadline for MS: 31 March 2026 (submission of revised programmes)
- Funds covered: ERDF (Regulation 2021/1058), ESF+ (Regulation 2021/1057), Cohesion Fund (Regulation 2021/1056), Just Transition Fund (Regulation 2021/1056), EMFAF (Regulation 2021/1139)
- Total envelope 2021-2027: approximately EUR 392 billion (ERDF 226 + ESF+ 99 + CF 42 + JTF 19 + EMFAF 6)
- PSLF (Public Sector Loan Facility): Just Transition Mechanism Pillar 3; first call results published 14 April 2026
- Priorities for reallocation (MTR focus areas): defence, housing, water resilience, clean energy transition, skills for industrial transformation, migration management (border regions), civil protection
- Flexibility instrument: up to 20% of each programme can be reallocated between specific objectives without major Commission amendment procedure (full CPR amendment procedure required beyond 20%)

## Policy Drivers

### 2024-2025 Context
- Russia-Ukraine war reshaped EU security priorities
- Draghi and Letta Reports identified competitiveness gap
- Housing crisis political salience (Housing Crisis Communication 2026)
- Water stress documented in JRC 2025 State of the Environment
- AI adoption lag (Apply AI Strategy COM(2025) 723)
- Iran war 2026 exacerbated energy security

### MTR Rationale
The Commission's February 2025 Communication proposed targeted flexibility:
1. **Defence-related projects** eligible (barracks, dual-use infrastructure, SMEs in defence supply chains)
2. **Competitiveness Fund bridge** -- MTR allocations can fund competitiveness projects pending 2028-34 MFF
3. **Water infrastructure** -- repair, reuse, desalination, drought response
4. **Housing affordability** -- deep renovation, social housing, energy-poverty alleviation
5. **Skills** -- reskilling for AI/net-zero transitions
6. **Energy grids** -- local interconnections, electrification, heat networks

### Contested Issues
- **Defence in cohesion**: France, Germany, Poland, Baltics pushed; Portugal, Ireland, Austria initially resisted; compromise included safeguards
- **Housing**: subsidiarity concerns from some MS (ES, IT, GR) on Commission scope
- **Net-zero conditionality**: risk of loosening green conditionality noted by civil society

## Results (21 April 2026 DG REGIO publication)

Key patterns from MTR submissions (based on initial MS reporting):
- **Eastern/Baltic MS** prioritised defence and border security reallocations (approximately 10-15% shifted)
- **Southern MS (ES, IT, PT, GR)** prioritised water, housing, and climate adaptation
- **Western MS (FR, DE, BE, NL, LU)** prioritised skills and energy grids
- **Nordic MS** (FI, SE, DK) prioritised civil protection + Arctic/defence
- **Cohesion countries** (PL, HU, RO, BG, HR, EL, PT, SK, LV, LT, EE, CY, MT) retained cohesion fund focus with defence/water add-ons

Aggregate reallocation estimated at approximately **10-12% of total envelope** shifted through MTR.

## PSLF First Call Results (14 April 2026)

The Public Sector Loan Facility (PSLF) under the Just Transition Mechanism Pillar 3 published its first call results. The DG REGIO news item "Advancing the Just Transition in Europe: Results of the first PSLF call for proposals" details:
- EIB-operated loan facility for public-sector projects in JTF-eligible territories
- Combines EU grant component with EIB loan
- Priority: coal/lignite regions transition, energy infrastructure, social investment, reskilling
- Selected projects feed into MTR allocations

## Link to MFF 2028-2034

The MTR is the **last major reallocation** under the current CPR. For MFF 2028-2034, the Commission proposal (COM(2025) 570 final, 16 July 2025) merges cohesion into **National and Regional Partnership Plans (Heading 1: EUR 865 billion)**. The MTR outcomes provide baseline for:
- Measuring actual shifted preferences (where did MS send money under flexibility?)
- Informing 2028-34 partnership plan content
- Cohesion advocacy positioning (CEMR, CPMR, EUROCITIES, AER) for the autumn 2026 EP mandate vote
- BUDG co-rapporteur Monika Hohlmeier (EPP, Germany) drafting budgetary assessment on Justice 2028-34 (PE787.672) -- parallel process
