# EU Anti-Money Laundering Framework: AMLA, 6th Directive, and International Comparison

## QUICK FACTS
- AML Package adopted 30 May 2024 (official adoption), entered into force 9 July 2024
- Three instruments: AMLR (Regulation), AMLD6 (Directive), AMLAR (AMLA Regulation)
- Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR): Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (CELEX: 32024R1624), OJ L, 19.6.2024
- 6th Anti-Money Laundering Directive (AMLD6): Directive (EU) 2024/1640 (CELEX: 32024L1640), transposition deadline 10 July 2027
- AMLA Regulation: Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 (CELEX: 32024R1620) establishing the Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism
- AMLA seat: Frankfurt am Main, Germany (selected 22 February 2024 by Council-EP joint vote)
- AMLA Chair: Bruna Szego (Italy), appointed December 2024; took office 1 January 2025
- AMLA operational start: 1 July 2025; direct supervision of ~40 EU cross-border financial institutions from 2028
- Lead DG: DG FISMA (Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union)
- Responsible Commissioner: Maria Luis Albuquerque (Financial Services, Portugal)
- EP committees: ECON (lead) and LIBE (joint procedure)
- Obliged entities expanded: crypto-asset service providers (CASPs), football agents, major football clubs, traders of luxury goods (EUR 10,000+ cash), precious metals dealers
- Cash payment limit: EUR 10,000 EU-wide (national lower limits allowed)
- Beneficial ownership threshold: 25% ownership or control
- EPRS Briefing (13 April 2026): "International comparison of Anti-Money Laundering frameworks" -- compares EU AML Package 2024 with US (BSA, FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Reporting), UK (MLR 2017 + NECC), Singapore, Switzerland, Canada. Key finding: EU now has most comprehensive single rulebook globally; US BOI reporting stalled in court March 2025. Ref: EPRS_UNK_cde5179e962c

## Structure of the EU AML Package

### 1. AML Regulation (AMLR) -- Regulation (EU) 2024/1624
- Directly applicable -- single rulebook for all Member States
- Harmonised definitions, CDD (customer due diligence), beneficial ownership rules
- Risk-based approach, enhanced due diligence triggers
- Central register of beneficial ownership (BORs) accessible to competent authorities, FIUs, obliged entities (for CDD), media, civil society (with legitimate interest)
- Sanctions regime: up to 10% of annual turnover or EUR 10 million (max) for serious breaches

### 2. 6th AML Directive (AMLD6) -- Directive (EU) 2024/1640
- Framework for national AML/CFT supervision
- Strengthens FIUs (Financial Intelligence Units)
- Cross-border FIU cooperation via FIU.net
- Access to information: law enforcement access to beneficial ownership data
- Transposition deadline: 10 July 2027

### 3. AMLA Regulation -- Regulation (EU) 2024/1620
- Establishes EU-level supervisor (AMLA)
- Direct supervision of selected financial institutions (~40, criteria: high-risk, cross-border)
- Indirect supervision through national supervisors
- AMLA Executive Board: Chair + 5 members; General Board: national supervisors
- Sanctioning powers: fines, restrictions, intervention tools
- AMLA Support Forum with FIUs
- Reporting obligations to EP and Council

## Obliged Entities

Financial sector:
- Credit institutions, investment firms, insurance companies, payment institutions
- Crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) under MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114)
- Collective investment undertakings

Non-financial sector:
- Lawyers, notaries, accountants (with threshold carve-outs)
- Real estate agents including intermediaries in letting
- Dealers in precious metals/stones, luxury goods (cars, yachts, private jets), cultural goods
- Gambling services
- Trust and company service providers
- Mortgage and consumer credit intermediaries
- Crowdfunding service providers (MiCA-linked)
- Investment migration operators ("golden visas" intermediaries)
- Professional football clubs (top division), football agents (phased-in 2027-2029)
- Non-fungible token (NFT) service providers

## International Comparison (from EPRS 13 April 2026)

### United States
- Bank Secrecy Act (BSA, 1970) + USA PATRIOT Act (2001)
- FinCEN (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network)
- Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting under Corporate Transparency Act 2021: challenged in federal court (NSBA v Yellen, ruling March 2024); Treasury paused enforcement March 2025 for US domestic entities
- Sanctions regime: OFAC
- Gap vs EU: no EU-style CASP sector coverage, no luxury goods/football scope

### United Kingdom (post-Brexit)
- Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (MLR 2017, as amended)
- Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023
- National Economic Crime Centre (NECC) -- NCA unit
- No EU AMLA equivalent; supervisory gap addressed via FCA supervisory activity

### Singapore
- Corruption, Drug Trafficking and Other Serious Crimes Act (CDSA)
- Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) as integrated supervisor
- Mutual Evaluation Report (MER) FATF 2024: compliant on most recommendations

### Switzerland
- Federal Act on Combating Money Laundering (AMLA Switzerland)
- FINMA supervision
- Bearer share reform 2019 (abolished most bearer shares)
- Gap: non-financial sector coverage less comprehensive than EU

### Canada
- Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA)
- FINTRAC as FIU and supervisor
- Beneficial ownership register: federal register live 2024, provincial patchwork

## Risks and Challenges (EPRS findings)

- Fragmented national transposition remains a risk even with single rulebook (AMLD6 still a directive)
- Beneficial ownership register access: CJEU Case C-37/20 and C-601/20 (Nov 2022) struck down "general public" access; AMLR/AMLD6 recalibrated to "legitimate interest"
- Cross-border enforcement: FIU.net technical harmonisation ongoing
- Sanctions coherence with OFAC/UK OFSI
- Sanctions evasion via shell companies, trusts, crypto
- Cost for obliged entities (especially SMEs, NFT providers)

## Key Legislation

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 AMLR
- Directive (EU) 2024/1640 AMLD6
- Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 AMLAR
- Previous: Directive (EU) 2015/849 AMLD4, Directive (EU) 2018/843 AMLD5
- Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 (Transfer of funds, applies to crypto-assets, "Travel Rule", CELEX: 32023R1113)
- Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)

## Brubru User Implications

- **Banks and financial institutions:** prepare for AMLA direct supervision 2028; review CDD policies against single rulebook
- **Crypto firms (CASPs):** Travel Rule since 30 December 2024; MiCA + AMLR alignment
- **Real estate and luxury goods:** new CDD obligations; cash limit EUR 10,000
- **Football clubs and agents:** phased onboarding 2027-2029; review corporate structure
- **Lawyers, notaries, accountants:** confirm AMLD6 national transposition timelines in your Member State
- **Compliance consultancies:** AMLA recruitment ongoing (Frankfurt)
- **Policy professionals:** monitor AMLA Executive Board appointments, RTS/ITS roadmap
