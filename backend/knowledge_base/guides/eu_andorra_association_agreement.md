# EU-Andorra Association Agreement

## QUICK FACTS
- **CURRENT STATUS (verified via OEIL, 27 April 2026)**: STILL **"Preparatory phase in Parliament"** — the Council has NOT adopted the decision and the EP has NOT given consent. The documents remain **"Proposal for a Council Decision"**. Do NOT say the agreement has been signed, concluded by the Council, ratified, or is in force — none of those have happened.
- **TWO parallel NLE procedures (standard pattern for EU international agreements)**:

  - **2024/0102(NLE)** = **COM(2024) 191 final** (CELEX `52024PC0191`) = Proposal for a Council Decision on the **signing, on behalf of the European Union, and on provisional application** of the Agreement
  - Both adopted by the Commission and submitted on **26 April 2024**, Brussels
- **Document the user is most likely to cite**: COM(2024) 191 = signing + provisional application proposal (CELEX `52024PC0191`).
- **Legal basis (technical, on the proposals)**: **Article 217 TFEU** (association agreements) + **Article 218 TFEU** paragraphs 6, 7, and 8 second subparagraph. The proposals also reference Article 21 TFEU, Article 45 TFEU, Article 263 TFEU, Article 290 TFEU, and Article 346 TFEU in specific provisions. The political basis is **Article 8 TEU** (good neighbourly relations) + **Declaration No 3 on Article 8** (small-sized neighbouring countries with specific relations of proximity).
- **EP lead committee**: **AFET** (Foreign Affairs). Procedure type: NLE — **Consent by Parliament**.
- **AFET rapporteur**: **Željana ZOVKO** (HR, EPP). **Shadow rapporteur**: **Laura BALLARÍN CEREZA** (ES, S&D). Both have engaged Andorran/Sammarinese governments and the Commission.
- **AFET committee draft report**: **PE770.052**, dated **23 April 2025**. Most recent documented procedural activity.
- **Responsible Commission service**: **Secretariat-General**. Politically responsible: **Ursula von der Leyen** (President; SG reports directly). Negotiating Commissioner during the talks: **Maroš Šefčovič** (VP for Interinstitutional Relations and Foresight) — called it "the most comprehensive agreement the EU will have with any third country", going "even beyond" the EEA.
- **Council working party**: **EFTA Working Party**.
- **Provisional application**: foreseen in **Article 112** of the Agreement itself — once the Council authorises signing, the Agreement applies provisionally pending completion of the conclusion procedure (i.e. before full EU + AD + SM ratification).
- **Negotiations concluded** at chief-negotiator level: **7 December 2023** (Andorra + San Marino). Monaco withdrew on 14 September 2023 after a year-long legal/economic assessment.
- **Negotiations formally launched**: 18 March 2015 in Brussels by HR/VP Federica Mogherini (Council mandate: December 2014).
- **Lead on EU side**: transferred from EEAS to European Commission Secretariat-General on 1 January 2022.
- **Sequence still ahead**: Council adopts signing decision (2024/0102) → signature + provisional application starts → EP consent on conclusion (AFET → plenary, on 2024/0101) → Council adopts conclusion decision → AD + SM ratification → full entry into force.
- **Andorra (the state)**: 468 km², ~80,000 population, Catalan official language, parliamentary democracy since 1993. Historical *co-principality* (French head of state + Spanish Bishop of Urgell as titular co-princes, retained as ceremonial heads of state).
- **Existing EU-Andorra framework** (will be replaced/extended by the AA): Customs Union Agreement (1991, free movement of agricultural goods + Joint Committee + Customs Cooperation Committee); Cooperation Agreement (2005, transport/education/environment); Savings Tax Agreement (2005); Monetary Agreement (2011, euro as official currency + minting + transposition of EU financial/banking *acquis*).
- **Schengen**: Andorra is NOT in Schengen but coordinates visa requirements with the Schengen area and accepts Schengen visas.
- **Agreement architecture**: 1 Framework Agreement (FA) + 7 Framework Protocols (FPs) + 2 State Protocols (one per country) + **25 technical annexes per State Protocol**. Joint to AD + SM where shared; bilateral matters in the State Protocols.
- **Sectoral coverage**: participation in the EU internal market under equal conditions of competition, plus framework for cooperation in R&D, education, social policy, environment, consumer protection, culture, regional cooperation. Non-discrimination on grounds of nationality applies.
- **Financial services**: NOT immediate access. **15-year progressive access**, conditional on a successful audit of each state's regulatory and supervisory frameworks. Compliance with the **anti-money-laundering acquis** is a pre-condition. **ESAs** (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA) play a central role in the audit. Politico (August 2023): the three ESA chairs warned for a "cautious approach" given historically less rigorous financial regulation in the three micro-states.
- **Tobacco**: Andorra committed (declaration annexed to the agreement) "not to increase the price differentials for tobacco products (including taxes) that exist … in relation to the neighbouring Member State with the lowest prices", and to a **30-year progressive integration of tobacco products into the customs union with the EU**.
- **Road transport**: protocol on mutual recognition of vehicle standards and truck driver qualifications; Andorra and San Marino have exemptions from a number of EU transport-policy provisions and a **2-year grace period** for several others.
- **Institutional framework**: dynamic regulatory alignment (Andorra/SM must adopt EU updates to listed regulations/directives automatically); ECJ as ultimate arbiter of disputes on interpretation/application; Andorran and Sammarinese courts may submit preliminary-reference cases to the ECJ; consistent interpretation in line with ECJ case law.
- **Comparison with EEA** (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway): the AA provides for participation in the internal market on equivalent conditions to the EEA, but is described by Šefčovič as going "even beyond" the EEA model.
- **Why Monaco withdrew (14 September 2023)**: legal and economic assessment by the Prince's Government concluded against pursuing the AA. Monaco continues to rely on bilateral agreements with France (customs, financial supervision) and a separate EU agreement on the application of certain *acquis* (medicines, cosmetics, medical devices).
- **Andorran government portal (official, Catalan)**: `andorraue.ad`. Key documents: Acord marc i protocols marc; Protocol Andorra; Declaracions; Annexos; *Guia ràpida de l'Acord d'associació*; *Dossier explicatiu amb llistat de canvis*; impact analysis by **Andorra Recerca i Innovació (AR+I)**.
- **Cap de Govern d'Andorra**: Xavier Espot (DA — Demòcrates per Andorra). Government statement to General Council: 21 March 2024.
- **EP recommendation** (pre-conclusion): 13 March 2019 — Parliament called for transparent communication with citizens, creation of an institutional framework in each of the three states to implement the AA, and adjustment of *acquis* adoption requirements to the dimensions and resources of the three small states.
- **EPRS source**: PE 766.263 — Angelos Delivorias, EPRS Members' Research Service, November 2024 (first edition, "International Agreements in Progress" series).

## Overview

The EU has long-standing relations with five Western European micro-states that are not Member States: the Holy See, Andorra, Liechtenstein, Monaco and San Marino. Liechtenstein is in the EEA via EFTA; the other four operate under bilateral agreements covering selective parts of the EU *acquis*. After the Council asked for an assessment in 2010 and the Commission set out five options in November 2012 (status quo, sectoral agreements, association, EEA membership, EU membership), the Council in December 2014 mandated negotiations on an association agreement with Andorra, Monaco and San Marino. Negotiations launched on 18 March 2015 and concluded on 7 December 2023 with Andorra and San Marino. Monaco withdrew in September 2023.

The Association Agreement is the deepest non-membership relationship the EU has ever offered: full participation in the internal market under equal conditions of competition, dynamic regulatory alignment with the EU *acquis*, and ECJ jurisdiction over disputes — going beyond the EEA template in scope and institutional integration.

## Andorra-specific obligations and adaptations

- **Tobacco**: 30-year progressive integration into the customs union; binding declaration on price-differential cap vs neighbouring MS with lowest tobacco prices.
- **Financial services**: 15-year ramp; AML *acquis* precondition; ESA-led audit.
- **Road transport**: mutual recognition + 2-year grace for several EU transport rules; specific exemptions retained.
- **Customs**: existing 1991 Customs Union Agreement absorbed/replaced by the FA + State Protocol regime.
- **Currency**: monetary agreement of 2011 remains the legal base for euro use; transposition of financial/banking *acquis* continues.
- **Dynamic alignment**: Andorra commits to automatically adopt EU updates to listed regulations/directives — a structural sovereignty trade-off.
- **Dispute settlement**: ECJ as ultimate arbiter; Andorran courts may refer preliminary questions.
- **Institutional capacity**: AA establishes a Parliamentary Association Committee (FP IV); creates an arbitration mechanism (FP V) and codifies cooperation on competition rules (FP II) and statistics (FP III).

## Institutional architecture (per COM(2024) 191, Articles 76 and 108 of the Framework Agreement)

The Agreement creates a multi-tier institutional framework:

- **Association Committee** — top political body, joint to all three parties (EU, Andorra, San Marino).
- **Joint Committees** — one EU-Andorra, one EU-San Marino. Article 76 of the Framework Agreement is the institutional anchor.
- **Subcommittees** under each Joint Committee:
  - **Customs Cooperation Subcommittee**
  - **Subcommittee on Food Safety and Veterinary and Phytosanitary Matters**
  - **Subcommittee on Financial Services**
  - **Statistical Subcommittee**
  - Additional subcommittees and working groups may be set up under Article 76(8) of the FA.
- **EU representation in these bodies**: the **European Commission** represents the Union and expresses Union positions, per Article 17(1) TEU.
- **Routine Joint Committee decisions** (those that simply extend EU acts to AD/SM with technical adjustments) — Commission may take the position on behalf of the Union.
- **Non-routine Joint Committee decisions** — Union position established under **Article 218(9) TFEU** (Council decision on Commission proposal).
- **Modifications to State Protocols** (excluding Annexes, per Article 108 of the FA) — Commission may approve on behalf of the Union under **Article 218(7) TFEU**, after informing the Council. Council may **block by qualified blocking minority** (Article 16(4) TEU) — in which case the Commission must reject the proposed modification.
- **State aid in Andorra and San Marino** — the **European Commission** is the responsible authority for applying EU State aid law in the territory of the two associated states. Major institutional novelty.
- **Dispute settlement** — the **Court of Justice of the European Union** is the ultimate arbiter for disputes on interpretation and application. Andorran and Sammarinese courts may submit preliminary references to the ECJ.

## Specific adaptations and transitional periods (from COM(2024) 191 explanatory memorandum)

- **Free movement of persons**: quantitative limits on certain types of residence, **inspired by those agreed with Liechtenstein in the EEA Agreement**. This is a structural concession to AD's and SM's small populations.
- **Tobacco**: 30-year progressive integration into the customs union; binding Andorran declaration on price-differential cap vs neighbouring MS with lowest tobacco prices.
- **Financial services**: maximum 15-year staggered access; AML *acquis* precondition; **emergency powers** of the European Supervisory Authorities (EBA/EIOPA/ESMA) explicitly preserved beyond the standard internal-market regime; requirements on local provision of services apply.
- **Telecommunications, transport, energy**: transitional periods for take-over, implementation, and application of relevant EU legal acts.
- **Specific industrial-sector adaptations**: where AD/SM lack a particular industrial sector (e.g. parts of transport), targeted carve-outs apply.
- **Dynamic regulatory alignment**: EU acts adopted after entry into force are added to the Annexes via Joint Committee decisions, with technical adaptations only.

## Stakeholder positions

- **European Commission (Šefčovič)**: AA goes "even beyond" the EEA — most comprehensive third-country agreement.
- **European Parliament (AFET)**: 2019 recommendation supportive; 2023 CFSP implementation report stressed importance of advancing AA negotiations.
- **European Supervisory Authorities (EBA/EIOPA/ESMA)**: cautious on financial-services integration; warned (August 2023, Politico) about historically less rigorous regulation and money-laundering risk in the three micro-states.
- **Andorran Government (Espot, DA)**: presents AA as "positive integration" preserving Andorran specificities; large public-information effort via `andorraue.ad`, AR+I impact analysis, *Guia ràpida*, *Dossier explicatiu*, "Estirant del fil" podcast (42 episodes), 7 explanatory video summaries, 8 public-meeting recordings.
- **San Marino**: parallel AA on identical FA + own State Protocol; Italian as official language; concluded same day as Andorra.
- **Monaco (withdrew)**: continues with France-mediated bilateral arrangements; not associated with the Schengen *acquis* but participates in Schengen via pre-existing arrangements with France.

## What this means for Brubru users

- **Lobbying and stakeholder relations**: the AA opens an entirely new EU-related arena for Andorran and Sammarinese stakeholders (banks, tobacco industry, transport operators, professional services). EU-side stakeholders should expect new market participants on internal-market terms with multi-year ramps in financial services and tobacco.
- **Catalan-language angle**: this is the first major EU agreement whose primary partner is a sovereign Catalan-speaking state. Communications, public consultations and follow-up implementation will require Catalan-language outputs from EU institutions and from third-party advisors.
- **Compliance**: dynamic alignment means Andorran and Sammarinese entities will track the same EU pipeline as EU operators. Brubru's chat, My EU Calendar, Legislative Tracker, and EU Law Comply features apply directly to them once the AA enters into force.
- **Predictions**: AFET consent vote will be the next political bottleneck — watch for shadow-rapporteur dynamics across EPP, S&D, Renew, Greens, ECR, ID, the Left.
