# AgRI 2040 and Food 2040: the six shared priorities and three common enablers

## QUICK FACTS
- **Six shared priorities** (COM(2026) 453, section 3.1) guide both frameworks: they are NOT areas of action. **Three common enablers** (section 3.2) support them. AgRI 2040 has eight areas and Food 2040 six; these lists are separate.
- **1. Competitiveness, startups and scaleups:** an R&I ecosystem linking researchers and innovators with farmers, foresters, rural actors, industry, startups and SMEs.
- **2. Food security, resilience and preparedness:** aligned with the European Preparedness Union Strategy (early warning, all-hazards approach); stress-testing and foresight; EU supply of **proteins, vitamins and essential nutrients**; the Action Plan on the EU protein system.
- **3. Natural resources and circularity:** climate-smart solutions that also protect biodiversity; linear to circular systems; preventing food loss and waste; aligned with the bioeconomy strategy, the **Nature Restoration Regulation** and the upcoming **Circular Economy Act**.
- **4. Food and feed safety, nutrition and health:** safety, **food chain integrity** (authenticity, traceability, transparency, fraud prevention) and nutrition together; microbiome-based innovation; **One Health**.
- **5. Fairness:** fair value creation and sharing of risks, costs and benefits; attractive careers for newcomers; quality jobs; benefits shared across regions and generations.
- **6. Global cooperation:** Global Gateway; the **African Union-EU roadmap on food and nutrition security and sustainable agriculture** and its International Research Consortium; India and the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States; **BIOEAST** with Ukraine, Moldova and the Western Balkans; **PRIMA**; FAO, WHO, WOAH, CGIAR, OECD.
- **Enabler: governance:** coordination across sectors and levels; inclusive engagement, youth, gender equality, skills; foresight with Member States; One Health (for example antimicrobial resistance); examples: the Mission "A Soil Deal for Europe", the Bioeconomy Mutual Learning Exercise.
- **Enabler: technologies:** digital including **AI**, **data** as a strategic asset, **biotechnology** (a first European Biotech Act is proposed, a second to follow); in complementarity with the proposed Observatory on Emerging Technologies, Common European Data Spaces, European Digital Infrastructure Consortia and Digital Innovation Hubs.
- **Enabler: territories and communities:** place-based innovation, Living Labs, LEADER, smart villages, the Interregional Innovation Investments instrument, the Thematic Smart Specialisation Platform on Agri-Food.
