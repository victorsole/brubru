# EU-UK summit of 20 November 2026: ETS linking, SPS (agri-food) and Youth Experience Scheme negotiations concluded

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** at the **EU-UK summit on Friday 20 November 2026** the two sides announced the **conclusion of NEGOTIATIONS** on three agreements: (1) **linking the EU and UK Emissions Trading Systems (ETS agreement)**; (2) a **Common Sanitary and Phytosanitary Area (SPS Agreement)**; (3) a **Youth Experience Scheme (YES)**. **Concluded negotiations are NOT signed, NOT ratified and NOT in force** (status 25 November 2026). Both sides "look forward to the conclusion of these agreements... and their joint and timely entry into force".
- **ETS linking:** the EU and UK committed to applying it **as of 2028**, subject to ratification and entry into force in both. Once in force, the UK can be added to **Annex III of the CBAM Regulation (EU) 2023/956** by a **Commission delegated act (Article 2(11))**, so goods originating in the UK would be **exempt from the EU CBAM**; a corresponding UK approach is expected. Until then CBAM applies to UK goods as to any import. Rationale: linked carbon markets mean comparable carbon pricing, so a border adjustment is no longer needed.
- **SPS Agreement:** the vast majority of movements of animals, animal products, plants and plant products between Great Britain, the Crown Dependencies and the EU would take place **without the certificates or controls now required**; the same benefits extend to Great Britain-Northern Ireland movements through the SPS Agreement and the Windsor Framework.
- **Youth Experience Scheme:** negotiations concluded; builds on the 2025 Common Understanding. Separately, the **UK joins Erasmus+ in 2027** (call opened 18 November 2026).
- **Also in the joint statement:** progress towards UK participation in the EU internal electricity market (exploratory talks concluded December 2025); implementing the EU-UK Security and Defence Partnership (guide `eu_uk_security_defence_partnership`); support for Ukraine; cooperation on shared fish stocks.
- **Sources:** Joint Statement of the EU-UK Summit, STATEMENT/26/2512, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/statement_26_2512 ; DG TAXUD news "EU and UK agree to link Emission Trading Systems (ETS)", 20 November 2026.
- **What was NOT read:** the agreement texts (not published); the Q&A on summit outcomes. Dates of signature are not known: give none.
