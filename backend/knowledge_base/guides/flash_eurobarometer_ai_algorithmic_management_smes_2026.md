# Flash Eurobarometer: AI and algorithmic management in European SMEs and mid-caps (published 14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** a **Flash Eurobarometer survey on AI and algorithmic management (AM) in European SMEs and mid-caps**, published by the Commission on **14 October 2026** (press release IP/26/2160), at the start of the **European AI Innovation Month**.
- **Algorithmic management** = digital systems that help managers organise the workforce or support decisions on tasks, schedules, performance or staffing; they can be AI-powered and can automate decisions or only recommend them.
- **Headline results (surveyed SMEs and mid-caps):**
  - **42%** already use AI and AM, mostly in a limited, experimental way; **21%** more plan to.
  - Uses: customer service and client relations **47%**; monitoring performance and productivity **36%**; logistics, planning and staff scheduling **30%**.
  - **61%** say AI helps them take decisions; **75%** believe AI and AM will improve job quality.
  - **90%** say people take the decisions; only **2%** of decisions are fully automated.
  - Barriers: high investment costs **50%**, legal uncertainty **49%**, lack of expertise **48%**.
  - **49%** do not trust AI or automated systems for workforce or staff decisions.
- **Policy link:** the Commission points to the GDPR and the **AI Act**, and to the upcoming **Quality Jobs Act**, which "will increase clarity for companies and better protect workers' rights in relation to algorithmic management" (second-stage social partner consultation held).
- **Do not mix with the February 2025 survey** on AI at work (citizens, not companies): there 62% of Europeans viewed robots and AI positively at work and 84% said AI needs careful management for privacy and transparency.
- **Digital Decade target:** at least 75% of EU businesses taking up AI, cloud or big data by 2030.
- **What was NOT read:** the survey report itself (sample size, fieldwork dates, country results, survey number); only the press release. Give no country figures.

## How to answer
"How many European SMEs use AI?": the Flash Eurobarometer published on 14 October 2026 found 42% of surveyed SMEs and mid-caps already use AI or algorithmic management (21% more plan to); 90% say humans still decide and 2% of decisions are fully automated; cost (50%), legal uncertainty (49%) and lack of expertise (48%) are the main barriers.
