# Court of Justice, Cases C-872/24 and C-909/24, Digi România and Investcapital: unfair terms in telecom contracts (8 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Ruling:** judgment of the Court of Justice (**Eighth Chamber**) of **8 October 2026** in joined **Cases C-872/24 (Digi România SA v VK) and C-909/24 (Investcapital Ltd v PN)** (CELEX 62024CJ0872), on references from the **Tribunalul Arad (Romania)**. Decided **without an Advocate General's opinion**. It interprets **Council Directive 93/13/EEC on unfair terms in consumer contracts**.
- **1. Late-payment penalties:** a term in an **electronic communications services contract** that imposes penalties for late payment **falls within Directive 93/13 and may be declared unfair, even if the sector's national law, unlike other sectors, sets no cap** on those penalties (Article 3(3) and point 1(e) of the annex).
- **2. Terms equivalent to one national law calls unfair:** where national law expressly classes as unfair a term requiring a defaulting consumer to pay a disproportionately high sum, **materially equivalent terms**, judged by their effects in an adversarial hearing, **may be treated as unfair without a further Article 3(1) examination**.
- **3. The court must disapply, not reduce:** the national court must **exclude the application** of such terms and **cannot reduce the penalty amount**.
- **4. Early-termination payments can be assessed:** Article 4(2) does **not preclude** an unfairness assessment of a term requiring a consumer who ends a **minimum-commitment contract** early, for a reason attributable to them, to pay **compensation equal to the remaining monthly instalments**, even though the Court has treated such compensation as **remuneration for a supply of services for VAT purposes**.
- **5. 24-month contracts:** a term in a contract of at least **24 months** can be declared unfair where, on early termination attributable to the consumer, the consumer must pay the remaining commitment period **calculated by including the discounts** granted for the services and goods supplied.
- **Basis of this guide:** the operative part of the judgment as published on EUR-Lex was read; the Court's reasoning (grounds) was not, so state no reasoning, no legal principle and no earlier case beyond what is written here.
- **Limit:** the Court gives the interpretation; whether a given term is unfair is for the national court. It does not set a euro cap.
- **Who benefits:** consumers of mobile and broadband contracts with minimum terms; operators' penalty and early-exit clauses are open to challenge.

## How to answer
"Can a telecom operator charge the whole remaining contract on early termination?": the Court held on 8 October 2026 that such a term can be assessed for unfairness and, in a 24-month contract that counts granted discounts into the sum, can be declared unfair; the national court decides.
