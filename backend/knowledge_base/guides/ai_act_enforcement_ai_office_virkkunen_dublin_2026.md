# AI Act enforcement begins: the AI Office's first requests for information to model providers (Virkkunen, Dublin, 14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Confirmed by the Commission itself (Monday 26 October 2026, reply from the @EUCommission Mastodon account):** "since our enforcement powers under the AI Act came into effect on 2 August, we've already sent our first requests for information to providers of the most advanced models around the world addressing issues such as security, external evaluation and model use", with a link to the Commission's AI Act enforcement page on digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu (post: https://ec.social-network.europa.eu/@EUCommission/117507270444143376). Still **no names and no number of recipients**: a social post corroborates the Dublin statement, it does not add facts.
Source: Executive Vice-President **Henna Virkkunen**'s speech at the **International AI Summit in Dublin**, 14 October 2026 (SPEECH/26/2150). These are her statements; the Commission has published no list of the requests.
- **Enforcement has started:** the Commission's enforcement powers over general-purpose AI models apply **since 2 August 2026**, and "the Commission has already taken our first enforcement actions under the AI Act". The **AI Office** "has started enforcing these rules" and **sent Requests for Information** on issues "such as security, external evaluation and monitoring how models are used". **She named no company and gave no number of recipients:** never name providers as recipients; any count of companies comes from another source and must be attributed to it.
- **Incidents:** she cited summer 2026 cases of AI agents "escaping their environment", inserting malicious code and using social deception, and said that **"last Friday" (9 October 2026)** she called a meeting with the **AI Act Scientific Panel** to seek advice on how to investigate recent incidents.
- **Her reading of the law:** the AI Act applies to all advanced models placed on the EU market wherever the provider is based; it covers risks such as loss of control, biological weapons, mass manipulation affecting elections and cyberattacks; the **General-Purpose AI Code of Practice** (signed by most providers) includes measures such as external evaluators.
- **The day before (13 October):** European ministers adopted a **joint declaration** on AI safety and sovereignty, trust in applied AI (including in the public sector) and AI infrastructure and skills (as she reported it; the declaration was not read).
- **Capacity figures she gave:** **19 AI Factories** and **13 national Antennas**; more than **EUR 30 billion** mobilised for **up to seven AI Gigafactories**; the **Cloud and AI Development Act** would triple EU computing capacity in 5-7 years; the **EUR 5 billion Scaleup Europe Fund**; nearly **EUR 2 billion** mobilised under the Apply AI Strategy; a new public equity capacity with the EIB Group to be ready by the start of the next MFF at the latest. She noted **Mistral's public preview of Mistral Large 4** "last week".
- **Dates:** the **European AI Innovation Month** was launched on 14 October with the Irish presidency (nearly 100 events in over 15 countries), closing on **17 November 2026 at the Apply AI Summit** in Brussels; the **Special Envoy for Industrial AI** report is due in November, followed by industrial AI alliances.

## How to answer
"Has the EU started enforcing the AI Act on AI models?": yes, according to Virkkunen on 14 October 2026: since 2 August the Commission can enforce the rules for general-purpose AI models, and the AI Office has sent its first requests for information (security, external evaluation, monitoring of use). No companies were named and no fines announced.
