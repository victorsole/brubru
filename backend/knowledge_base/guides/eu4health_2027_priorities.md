# EU4Health 2027: priorities consultation

## QUICK FACTS

- **Topic**: EU4Health 2027 annual work programme — Commission consultation on priorities for next year
- **Status**: Consultation open as of **Monday 4 May 2026** (DG SANTE newsroom)
- **Source**: ec.europa.eu/newsroom/sante/newsletter-archives/74926
- **Responsible DG**: **DG SANTE** (Health and Food Safety)
- **Responsible Commissioner**: **Olivér Várhelyi** (Health and Animal Welfare, Hungary)
- **Programme parent**: **EU4Health programme 2021-2027**, established by Regulation (EU) 2021/522 (CELEX 32021R0522), total envelope EUR 5.3 billion over 7 years
- **Cycle**: Annual work programme (AWP) is adopted yearly; the 2027 AWP is the **last under the current EU4Health programme** before transition to the post-2027 successor instrument under the MFF 2028-2034 negotiations
- **Funding mechanism**: Grants, procurement, joint actions with Member States, prizes, indirect management with international organisations (WHO et al.)
- **Stakeholder consultation purpose**: collect inputs from Member State health authorities, NGOs, healthcare professionals, patient organisations, academia, industry on which thematic priorities should drive 2027 calls

## Related signals (week of 5 May 2026)

- **HTACG flash report (4 May 2026)**: DG SANTE published flash report from the **Member State Coordination Group on Health Technology Assessment (HTACG)** meeting of **30 April 2026**. HTACG operates under the **HTA Regulation** (Regulation (EU) 2021/2282, CELEX 32021R2282) and is responsible for joint clinical assessments + joint scientific consultations. Source: health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/flash-report-member-state-coordination-group-hta-htacg-30-april-2026-2026-05-04_en
- **SCCS preliminary opinions** (4 May 2026): Acetophenone safety in cosmetics (deadline 6 July 2026); Glyoxylic Acid; Hydroxycitronellal — all three are Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety opinions open for comments under the Cosmetic Products Regulation (Reg (EC) 1223/2009).
- **Mental health x screentime communication** (4 May 2026): DG SANTE published "Addressing the mental health impacts of excessive and passive screentime" — feeds into the broader EU Comprehensive Approach to Mental Health (Communication 2023) and intersects with DSA Article 28 (minors' protection). Source: ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner (DG SANTE 4 May 2026).
- **Webinar for high-risk medical devices developers**: DG SANTE registration open for 19 June 2026 webinar (Medical Devices Regulation 2017/745 + In Vitro Diagnostic MDR 2017/746 implementation).
