# How agri-food innovation reaches farmers (1): multi-actor approach, Living Labs, cascade funding

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** SWD(2026) 271 final (sections 2.3, 3.1) and COM(2026) 453 final (section 2.2). Intentions and evidence, no obligation on farmers.
- **Multi-actor approach:** an interactive, transdisciplinary mode of R&I in which relevant actors (farmers, foresters, rural actors, advisers, businesses, civil society) take part **throughout** a project so outcomes are co-owned and demand-driven. Under Horizon Europe it implies **seven mandatory requirements**; **mandatory in more than 70% of relevant calls**; applied by Horizon Europe Cluster 6, the Soil Mission and the Circular Bio-based Europe JU. Introduced in Horizon 2020, reinforced in Horizon Europe; it drew on EIP-AGRI Operational Groups.
- **Reach:** used by **3,681 unique organisations** (over 1,000 SMEs, 28%) in 2014-2024; agriculture, forestry and rural projects: **195 (EUR 1,040.7 million) in Horizon 2020, 219 (EUR 1,241.8 million) in Horizon Europe**; Food 2030 projects: 19 in Horizon 2020, 69 in Horizon Europe (60% overall).
- **Weak points:** inconsistent implementation, late involvement of actors, uneven adoption of results; the Food 2030 evaluation asked for more structural support. Support projects: **PREMIERE** (2023-2027, helps consortia, national contact points and evaluators prepare multi-actor proposals, with a seed-funding pilot) and LIAISON.
- **Living Labs and lighthouses:** place-based, user-centred R&I ecosystems where land managers, scientists, citizens, businesses and authorities co-design and test solutions in real life; lighthouses demonstrate exemplary solutions. Central to the **agroecology**, **agriculture of data** and **forest** partnerships and **Mission Soil** (100 living labs and lighthouses targeted by 2030).
- **Cascade funding (financial support to third parties):** funds passed by consortia to small actors including SMEs, **farmers and foresters**, for testing and validating results; legal basis **Article 207 of Financial Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2024/2509** (CELEX 32024R2509, title checked). The Communication wants it **more visible and accessible**.
- **Social sciences and humanities:** share of agri-forestry-rural projects flagged fell from **52.6% (2014-2020) to 29.3% (2021-2024)**; in Cluster 6, **21%** of participants and **38%** of 2026-2027 topics expected to have such a dimension; Food 2030 projects rose from 43 to 62.
