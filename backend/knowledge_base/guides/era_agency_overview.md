# ERA: European Union Agency for Railways

## QUICK FACTS
- Full name: European Union Agency for Railways (ERA)
- Seat: Valenciennes and Lille, France
- Origins: created in 2004 (Second Railway Package) as the "European Railway Agency", operational from 2006; renamed the "European Union Agency for Railways" in June 2016 under the Fourth Railway Package, keeping the ERA acronym
- Legal basis: Regulation (EU) 2016/796 (CELEX 32016R0796), part of the Fourth Railway Package technical pillar alongside Directive (EU) 2016/797 on interoperability (CELEX 32016L0797) and Directive (EU) 2016/798 on safety (CELEX 32016L0798)
- Status: an independent, decentralised EU regulatory agency, separate from the Commission, Parliament and Member States
- Executive Director: Oana Gherghinescu (Romanian), in post since 1 June 2025, succeeding Josef Doppelbauer
- Governance: Management Board (one voting representative per Member State plus two voting Commission representatives, and six non-voting stakeholder representatives covering railway undertakings, infrastructure managers, the railway industry, trade unions, passengers and freight customers), an Executive Board, and a Board of Appeal that hears appeals against ERA decisions
- Responsible DG: DG MOVE (Mobility and Transport); Responsible Commissioner: Apostolos Tzitzikostas (Sustainable Transport and Tourism)
- EP lead committee: TRAN (Transport and Tourism)
- One-Stop Shop (OSS): ERA's online portal (oss.era.europa.eu), live since 2019, the single electronic entry point for single safety certificates, vehicle (type) authorisations and ERTMS trackside approvals; the first single safety certificate was issued in September 2019
- ERTMS system authority: ERA specifies, approves and safeguards the interoperability of ERTMS trackside and onboard equipment across the EU rail network
- Adopts and maintains the Technical Specifications for Interoperability (TSIs) covering infrastructure, rolling stock, signalling (TSI CCS) and operations
- Runs EU rail registers and databases including ERADIS (safety certificates/licences), RINF (register of infrastructure), ERATV (register of authorised vehicle types) and ERAIL (accident/incident data), plus coordinates with National Safety Authorities (NSAs) and National Investigation Bodies (NIBs)
- Supports the Single European Railway Area and the TEN-T rail network objectives, and has a growing role in rail cybersecurity coordination

ERA is the EU's railway agency: it does not write rail legislation (the Commission proposes, Parliament and Council co-legislate) but since 2019 it directly issues certificates and authorisations valid across the EU, replacing what used to be up to 27 separate national procedures.

## What ERA does

Before the Fourth Railway Package, a railway undertaking wanting to operate in several Member States needed a separate safety certificate and vehicle authorisation from each national authority. ERA's central innovation, delivered through the One-Stop Shop from 2019, is to act as a single point of contact so applicants can obtain EU-wide validity in one process:

- **Single Safety Certificates (SSCs)**: issued to railway undertakings, valid for operations across the areas of use specified, without needing a separate certificate per Member State
- **Vehicle (type) authorisations**: issued for placing vehicles on the market, valid across the specified area of use, working alongside National Safety Authorities where relevant
- **ERTMS trackside approvals**: ERA checks trackside ERTMS projects against the TSI CCS baseline before Member States deploy them, acting as the ERTMS "system authority"
- **TSIs**: ERA drafts and maintains the Technical Specifications for Interoperability that harmonise rolling stock, infrastructure, energy, signalling and operational rules across the network
- **Registers and telematics**: maintains ERADIS, RINF, ERATV and ERAIL, and works on telematics applications for freight and passenger services
- **Safety oversight and investigation cooperation**: coordinates with NSAs on common safety methods and indicators, and supports the network of NIBs that investigate serious rail accidents
- **Railway cybersecurity**: increasingly coordinates Member State and sector work on cybersecurity for rail control-command and signalling systems
- **Single European Railway Area**: supports implementation of Directive 2012/34/EU (recast) on the internal market for rail services

|-------------|-------|-------|
| **ERA Regulation** -- Reg (EU) 2016/796 | 32016R0796 | Establishes the Agency, its tasks, governance and the One-Stop Shop mandate |
| **Railway Interoperability Directive** -- Dir (EU) 2016/797 | 32016L0797 | Interoperability of the EU rail system; legal basis for the TSIs |

## Governance and bodies

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| **Management Board** | Sets strategy, adopts the budget and work programme, appoints the Executive Director. One voting representative per Member State, two voting Commission representatives, and six non-voting stakeholder representatives (railway undertakings, infrastructure managers, railway industry, trade unions, passengers, freight customers) |
| **Executive Director** | Oana Gherghinescu (since 1 June 2025). Runs day-to-day operations, represents the Agency, and leads on ERTMS deployment, high-speed rail infrastructure and safety cooperation with Member States |
| **Executive Board** | Supports the Management Board on preparatory and administrative matters |
| **Board of Appeal** | Hears appeals against ERA decisions, for example on certification, authorisation or ERTMS approval outcomes |

## Engaging with ERA

- **One-Stop Shop (OSS)**: railway undertakings, keepers and manufacturers apply for single safety certificates, vehicle authorisations and ERTMS trackside approvals through oss.era.europa.eu, using an email-based account with HTTPS access
- **National Safety Authorities and National Investigation Bodies**: ERA coordinates rather than replaces NSAs and NIBs, which retain roles in national-specific rules, supervision and accident investigation
- **Public consultations on TSIs**: draft TSI revisions and recommendations go through stakeholder consultation before adoption by the Commission
- **Registers**: ERADIS, RINF and ERATV are publicly queryable for safety certificate, infrastructure and vehicle-type data
