# European Court of Auditors, annual reports on the 2025 financial year (published 8 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Agencies (OJ C of 28 October 2026, C/2026/5415, CELEX 52026TA05415): the ECA publishes its annual report on EU agencies for the 2025 financial year, with the agencies' replies, on 29 October 2026** at https://www.eca.europa.eu/en/publications/SAR-AGENCIES-2025 . The OJ notice only announces the publication: the report's findings were NOT read; give no agency-level opinion until it is.
- **What:** the ECA's annual reports on the **2025 financial year**, published **8 October 2026** (President **Tony Murphy**). Opinions: **clean** on the 2025 accounts (with a warning that the Commission's new financial IT system caused accounting errors needing manual fixes) and on revenue; **adverse on EU budget spending** (the key-facts sheet says for the 7th consecutive year); **qualified, separately, on spending under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF)**.
- **Money in 2025:** EU payments **EUR 216.4 billion** = **EUR 176.3 billion from the EU budget** + **EUR 40.1 billion from the RRF**. The audit population for budget expenditure was EUR 130.8 billion. (RRF expenditure in the accounts is EUR 45.4 billion: a different measure.)
- **Estimated error rate, EU budget spending: 3.8% in 2025** (range 2.7% to 4.9%, above the 2% materiality threshold); 3.6% in 2024; 5.6% in 2023; 4.2% in 2022; 3.0% in 2021.
- **By area (2025, with 2024):** **Natural resources and environment 3.9% (2.6%)**; **Cohesion, resilience and values 6.6% (5.7%)**; together almost two thirds of the audit population. Most common errors: eligibility issues (ineligible costs, projects, activities or beneficiaries), breaches of public procurement and grant award rules, missing supporting documents. 42.0% of the population was free from material error, mainly entitlement-based payments such as direct payments to farmers (though ineligible land was found in some).
- **RRF:** payments to **nine Member States** had milestones or targets not satisfactorily fulfilled, plus double funding and eligibility-period problems; weaknesses in the Commission's assessments and in national control systems (key-facts sheet: 9 of 37 grant payments did not comply). By end-2025 the Commission had made **102 grant payments totalling EUR 223.9 billion**, leaving 38% of grants for the final year.
- **Debt:** EU debt **EUR 738.9 billion** at end-2025 (2021: 235.6; 2022: 348.1; 2023: 458.5; 2024: 601.3); outstanding commitments EUR 501.1 billion. **EU borrowing could reach EUR 1 trillion by 2027**, largely NextGenerationEU; **interest on NGEU non-repayable support over 2028-2034 could be around EUR 93 billion**; the Commission's 2028-2034 proposal sets a fixed EUR 24 billion a year (current prices) for interest and principal. ECA message: caution; without the proposed new revenue streams the budget faces a significant shortfall.
- **Link to the next budget:** POLITICO reported on 14 October that Murphy warned the Commission's plan to put about half of the 2028-2034 budget into one flexible pot would make it "more difficult for us to follow the money" (POLITICO's report; the ECA's written views are its opinions of May 2026).
- **Basis:** the ECA publication page, key-facts sheet and "2025 EU audit in brief"; the full annual report was not read, so give no per-country rates.
