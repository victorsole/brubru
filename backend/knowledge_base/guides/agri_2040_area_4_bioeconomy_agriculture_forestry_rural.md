# AgRI 2040 area 4 of 8: bioeconomy in agriculture, forestry and rural areas

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** fourth of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). Preliminary, no obligation.
- **Purpose:** efficient and sustainable use of **agricultural and forestry land and biomass** and **biomanufacturing**, with evidence for business models and governance tools that create **additional income for primary producers and rural communities**.
- **Bio-based inputs:** improve European production of **bio-based alternatives to synthetic inputs**, including **bio-based (organic) fertilisers and low-carbon fertilisers**; the Communication's footnote names the **Fertiliser Action Plan, COM(2026) 310 final**.
- **Biomass:** follow the **cascading principle** for biomass; enhance **co-production of renewable energy**; opportunities for **wood-based products and materials**.
- **Business models:** new models to cut production costs and raise the resilience of biomass producers; **local valorisation** of sustainable biomass, by-products and residues; circular processes, recycling and reuse of bio-based products.
- **Related:** the bioeconomy strategy COM(2025) 960; the Circular Bio-based Europe Joint Undertaking (EUR 2 billion, 2021-2031); the Mission Soil circular soil improvers.
