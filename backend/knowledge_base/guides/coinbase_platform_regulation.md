## CORE REGULATORY STACK — financial services primary, platform governance secondary
- **MiCA** (Reg 2023/1114) — THE defining regulation. Title II (ARTs), III (EMTs), V (CASPs: authorisation, custody, exchange, brokerage), VI (market abuse). Coinbase holds Ireland MiCA authorisation; transitional period ended 30 Dec 2025.
- **DORA** (Reg 2022/2554) — Digital Operational Resilience Act; applied 17 Jan 2025. Third-party ICT risk, incident reporting, resilience testing. CASPs are financial entities in scope.
- **AML Regulation (AMLR)** (Reg 2024/1624, CELEX 32024R1624) — applies 10 July 2027. AMLA in Frankfurt already operational (supervision starts 2027).
- **6AMLD** (Dir 2024/1640, CELEX 32024L1640) — MS mechanisms; transposition by 10 July 2027.
- **Transfer of Funds Regulation (TFR recast)** (Reg 2023/1113) — "Travel Rule" for crypto transfers €1,000+. Applies 30 Dec 2024.
- **PSD2** (Dir 2015/2366) + **PSD3 / PSR** (pending) — euro on/off-ramps, Coinbase Card.
- **GDPR** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679) — KYC data subjects; SARs with law enforcement; cross-border transfer rules (US).
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — transaction risk scoring, fraud detection (Annex III credit-related potentially high-risk). Applied 2 Feb 2025 (prohibitions); 2 Aug 2026 (high-risk).
- **NIS2** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555) — financial entities + CASPs = highly critical operators.
- **CRA** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847) — Coinbase Wallet app; hardware wallets if marketed in EU.
- **EMIR + MiFID II** — if crypto-linked derivatives offered.
- **DSA** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — marginal: wallet app + website have minimal hosting/UGC features.
- **Representative Actions** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828) — crypto consumer claims pathway.
- **eIDAS 2** (Reg 2024/1183, CELEX 32024R1183) — EUDI Wallet integration for KYC flows.
