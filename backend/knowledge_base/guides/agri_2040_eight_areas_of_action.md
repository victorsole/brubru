# AgRI 2040: the eight preliminary areas of action (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3)

## QUICK FACTS
- **AgRI 2040 has EIGHT preliminary areas of action.** Three lists, three numbers: AgRI 2040 has eight areas, Food 2040 (the food-systems framework) has six areas, and the strategy has six shared priorities. Source: Communication **COM(2026) 453 final**, 8 September 2026, section 3.3, confirmed by the Commission's web page, the factsheet and the news item.
- **What AgRI 2040 is:** the framework for R&I in **agriculture, forestry and rural areas**. It identifies thematic and cross-cutting R&I needs through evidence and co-creation, is **regularly updated**, mobilises funding and partners around segments of the value chain and gives a coherent governance structure. It creates **no obligation** for farmers or foresters.
- **The eight areas (one line each):**
  1. **Soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital:** healthy soils, resilient water, nutrient efficiency, agroecology, carbon farming, agroforestry.
  2. **Cropping systems:** drought, salinity and heat adaptation, plant health, low-risk crop protection, breeding, **protein crops**.
  3. **Livestock systems:** animal health and welfare, biosecurity, vaccines, prudent antimicrobial use, sustainable feed, pasture; contributes to the livestock strategy.
  4. **Bioeconomy in agriculture, forestry and rural areas:** biomass, biomanufacturing, bio-based and low-carbon fertilisers, renewable energy, wood products.
  5. **Multi-functional forests:** active sustainable forest management, resilience to pests and fire, carbon sinks, rewarding foresters.
  6. **Rural areas:** competitiveness, attractiveness, **generational renewal**, services, women and young people.
  7. **Stronger agri-food value chain:** farmers' position, short supply chains, quality schemes (organic, geographical indications).
  8. **Digitalisation, AI and robotics:** data valorisation, AI including generative AI, robots, drones, sensing.
- **Status:** **preliminary**: "a basis to start co-creating" that "can be adapted"; they reflect the Vision, the consultation (701 replies), SCAR input and SWD(2026) 271. The **EU AgRI 2040 Conference (24-25 September 2026, Brussels) took place** and held nine breakout sessions (the eight areas plus climate change and biodiversity) to co-identify priorities; **no conclusions or conference report had been read on 13 October 2026**. Areas of action to be developed with stakeholders from **Q4 2027**. Funding vehicles: Horizon Europe 2028-2034 and the European Competitiveness Fund; no budget figure.
- The Commission web page words some areas longer ("cropping systems, plant health and protein crops"; "livestock systems, animal health and welfare"; "rural areas, services and generational renewal"): the same eight areas.
