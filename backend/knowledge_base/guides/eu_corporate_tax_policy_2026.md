## Context: What "Changing Environment" Means

The Kollár report lands at a turning point in EU corporate tax policy. Five forces converge:

1. **Pillar Two Global Minimum Tax (15%)**: Council Directive (EU) 2022/2523 transposing OECD Pillar Two adopted December 2022, effective from January 2024 (income inclusion rule) and January 2025 (undertaxed profits rule). Implementation in 27 MS uneven; first compliance year results landing 2026. Estimated EUR 14 billion additional revenue across EU (varies by source).
2. **BEFIT (Business in Europe: Framework for Income Taxation)**: Commission proposal COM(2023) 532 of 12 September 2023 -- common tax base for groups with EUR 750 million+ turnover, formula-based allocation. Stalled in Council since 2024 due to unanimity blockers (Estonia, Hungary, Ireland concerns).
3. **US tax-policy divergence**: Second Trump administration cut corporate tax to 15% (federal) plus state-level cuts; capital flow incentives to US escalating. EU competitiveness gap widens.
4. **Capital and residence mobility**: Tech, pharma, finance HQ relocation accelerating (Ireland, Netherlands, Luxembourg defending attractive regimes; France + Germany pushing for harmonisation floors).
5. **Post-2027 EU own resources reform**: New Own Resources package (COM(2023) 261 + 262) includes corporate-tax-based contribution; politically tied to MFF 2028-2034 ratification. ETS share + CBAM transfers + corporate residual already on the table.

## What the Kollár Report Likely Argues

Hungarian EPP rapporteur Kollár has historically pushed back against tax harmonisation that erodes Member State competitiveness levers. Expected report angles:

- **Pillar Two implementation**: pragmatic stocktaking of Member State experiences, technical fixes, defence of national flexibility within OECD framework
- **BEFIT scepticism**: questioning timing given US divergence; argument that BEFIT plus Pillar Two is double burden
- **Tax sovereignty preservation**: emphasising Article 113/115 TFEU unanimity, opposing creeping QMV via Article 116 TFEU
- **SME carve-outs**: Mittelstand concerns, family-owned business taxation
- **Innovation incentives**: R&D tax credits, IP boxes, defence of the IP-box regime against OECD scrutiny
- **Capital markets union linkage**: tax harmonisation conditional on CMU progress

S&D + Greens shadow rapporteurs likely push opposite direction (faster BEFIT adoption, minimum effective rate above 15%, public country-by-country reporting expansion). The trilogues happen at committee + plenary stage; INI reports do not trigger interinstitutional negotiations but do shape Commission's next legislative proposal.

## Key Tax Files in EP/Council Pipeline

- **Pillar Two Directive review**: scheduled 2026 under Directive (EU) 2022/2523 Article 56
- **BEFIT proposal COM(2023) 532**: still Council unanimity-blocked
- **Unshell Directive (ATAD III)**: Commission proposal COM(2021) 565 on shell entities -- progressing slowly
- **DAC9 (Directive on Administrative Cooperation, 9th amendment)**: Pillar Two information exchange
- **VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA)**: Council adoption 11 March 2025, applicable phased 2030/2035
- **FASTER Directive**: Council adoption September 2024 on faster withholding tax procedures
- **Public country-by-country reporting**: Directive (EU) 2021/2101 -- effective from June 2024
