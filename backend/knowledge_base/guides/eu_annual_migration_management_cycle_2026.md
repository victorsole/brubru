# Second Annual Migration Management Cycle (6 November 2026): who is under migratory pressure for 2027

## QUICK FACTS
- **Launched Friday 6 November 2026 by the Commission under the Pact on Migration and Asylum** (press release IP/26/2365, Q&A QANDA/26/2366). Three parts: the **second European Annual Asylum and Migration Report**, a **Commission decision determining the migratory situation of each Member State**, and a **proposal for the 2027 Annual Solidarity Pool** to be adopted by the Council. Documents on the Commission's register the same day: **COM(2026) 835** (communication) and **COM(2026) 836** (proposal for a Council Implementing Decision); **NOT yet in Cellar on 6 November**.
- **UNDER migratory pressure (four Member States, eligible to use the Solidarity Pool, subject to applying the responsibility rules): Greece and Cyprus** (disproportionate arrivals), **Italy** (disproportionate arrivals after search and rescue at sea; "more progress remains necessary" on responsibility rules), **Spain** (overall situation, in particular the incident in Ceuta).
- **AT RISK of migratory pressure (15): Belgium, Czechia, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, France, Croatia, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovenia, Finland:** priority access to the Permanent EU Migration Support Toolbox, closer monitoring.
- **Significant migratory situation (six), may ask the Council for a full or partial deduction from their Pool contributions: Bulgaria, Czechia, Estonia, Croatia, Austria, Poland.** (Czechia, Estonia, Croatia and Poland are on both lists: that is the Commission's text.)
- **The 2027 Solidarity Pool proposal is NOT public** (rule of the Pact); the Council decides its size and each Member State's share; once adopted, contributions become public and legally binding. Contributions: relocation or responsibility offsets, financial support, alternative measures, or a mix.
- **The Commission's figures (its report):** illegal border crossings **more than halved over two years**, down **by more than a third in the first half of 2026**; unauthorised movements **-22%** and asylum applications **-19%** in 2025; EU-wide return rate **over 30%**; one third of registered applications processed under accelerated or border procedures since the Pact applied; Eurodac operational in all Member States; the 2026 Pool's implementation "on track".
- **The Pact has applied in full since June 2026.** This is the second cycle (the first set the 2026 Pool).
- **What was NOT read:** COM(2026) 835 and 836 themselves (not yet published in Cellar); everything above is from the press release and Q&A.
