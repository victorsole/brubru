# Sanofi commitments on disparaging a rival flu vaccine: Commission makes them binding (14 October 2026, case AT.40998)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Decision:** on **14 October 2026** the Commission made legally binding, under **Article 9 of Regulation 1/2003**, commitments offered by **Sanofi** (France) to address preliminary concerns under **Article 102 TFEU** (abuse of a dominant position). Case **AT.40998**; press release IP/26/2154.
- **The products:** enhanced flu vaccines for people over 60. Sanofi's **Efluelda** competes with **Fluad** (CSL Seqirus), the only other enhanced flu vaccine marketed in the EU.
- **The concern:** Sanofi may be dominant in enhanced flu vaccines in **Germany and France**. From **2024 until at least autumn 2025** it may have run a misleading campaign aimed at healthcare professionals (mainly Germany, less intensely France): suggesting Fluad's evidence base is weaker, contrary to the ECDC and national immunisation advisory groups; misrepresenting national vaccination recommendations; and suggesting the German recommendation for Fluad faced unresolved scientific objections.
- **The commitments:**
  - **Until March 2030**, no promotional statements to healthcare professionals in Germany and France that criticise or cast doubt on national vaccination recommendations, present Fluad negatively, unduly suggest Efluelda is safer or more effective, or suggest Fluad's evidence base is weaker; the same in input to Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs).
  - In Germany, most Efluelda promotional material must refer clearly to the latest national vaccination recommendations.
  - **Corrective step:** for **24 months**, a statement on Sanofi's German and French websites that the national recommendations treat **Fluad and Efluelda as equivalent**, both with a robust evidence base; sent to healthcare professionals and KOLs in Germany previously targeted.
  - A **monitoring trustee** reports to the Commission.
- **Timeline:** formal investigation and Preliminary Assessment **26 June 2026**; market test **8 July to 21 August 2026**, after which Sanofi extended the outreach to KOLs in Germany.
- **Legal effect:** a commitments decision **does not find an infringement**. Breach of the commitments can bring a fine of up to **10% of worldwide annual turnover** or periodic penalties of up to 5% of daily turnover.
- **What was NOT read:** the decision and commitments text (to appear in the case register under AT.40998).

## How to answer
"Was Sanofi fined over Fluad?": no fine and no infringement finding. On 14 October 2026 the Commission accepted binding commitments (AT.40998): Sanofi stops disparaging CSL Seqirus's Fluad in Germany and France until March 2030 and publishes for 24 months a statement that both vaccines are equivalent under national recommendations.
