# Food 2030: funding, results and evaluation (SWD(2026) 271)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Food 2030:** the R&I framework for **food systems transformation** under Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe 2021-2027, dating from **2016** (first calls 2017); **11 pathways** and **four cross-cutting priorities**. Its successor is **Food 2040** (six areas of action).
- **Funding:** about **EUR 770 million** under Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe, until the 2023/2024 work programme and excluding the **FutureFoodS** co-funded partnership.
- **FutureFoodS:** launched **June 2024**; **83 partners from 29 countries**; EU contribution about **EUR 95 million (30%)**, about **EUR 200 million** more from Member States; prepared by the coordination action FoodPathS (2022-2025) with the Standing Committee on Agricultural Research.
- **Outputs:** **18 intellectual property applications and 1,160 peer-reviewed publications under Horizon 2020**; **2 and 134 so far under Horizon Europe** (many projects still run). About **82%** of Horizon 2020 Food 2030 projects published and about **13%** registered intellectual property; about 39% and 2.5% under Horizon Europe so far; about 8.8 publications per project by 2024.
- **SMEs:** **21.5% of participants (372 SMEs) in Horizon 2020** and **22.6% (362 SMEs) in Horizon Europe**, receiving **EUR 84.2 million (17.8%)** and **EUR 100.6 million (20.5%)** of net EU contribution.
- **Trackers (Horizon Europe 2021-2024):** about **66.9%** of Food 2030 funding at least indirectly for climate, **20.7%** biodiversity, **21.9%** digital, **12.3%** AI.
- **Evaluation:** independent experts (2025) called its intervention logic "pioneering" (urban food policy, food safety, alternative proteins, microbiome); a 2026 EU survey confirmed this; the evaluation flagged **fragmentation of networks and stakeholder fatigue** among Living Labs, and that more structural support is needed for the multi-actor approach.
- **Food sector figures (2023):** food and drink industry **EUR 1,523 billion turnover**, **EUR 296 billion value added**, 4.8 million people, 309,000 enterprises (14% of manufacturing enterprises); **80%** micro-enterprises; SMEs **99%** of firms, **39%** of turnover, **57%** of employment; 2.7 million food retail and service enterprises (17.1 million people); average spending **EUR 4,290** per person. Food systems cause almost **30%** of global greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Needs in Europe:** over **42 million** people in the EU cannot afford a quality meal every second day (Eurostat 2023); **58 million tonnes** of food waste a year, about EUR 132 billion.
