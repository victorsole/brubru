# AgRI 2040 and Food 2040: the 19 actions of Annex 1 of COM(2026) 453 and when each starts

## QUICK FACTS
- Annex 1 of the Communication lists **19 key actions** in five groups, each with a **starting quarter** (Commission work-planning dates, NOT legal deadlines; all "without prejudice" to the next MFF).
- **Closing the gap from lab to fab:** (1) targeted call in current Horizon Europe on valorising results and more AI in agriculture and food systems, **2027**; (2) more private-sector and market experts evaluating proposals, **2027**; (3) harvest R&I needs from EU pre-market assessment bodies, **Q4 2027**; (4) closer links between Horizon Europe agri-food research, **EIT Food** and the **European Innovation Council**, **Q4 2026**; (5) launch a **JRC-managed Science for Policy Centre on Agriculture, Forestry, Rural Development and Food System Innovation and Transformation**, **Q4 2027**; (6) toolbox for startups and scaleups with SCAR, **Q4 2027**; (7) Member States to use national and regional partnership plans for the innovation ecosystem, **Q4 2026**.
- **Uptake by farmers, foresters and rural actors:** (8) harmonise multi-actor approach terminology, evaluation and monitoring, **Q1 2028**; (9) collaborative matchmaking platforms, **Q1 2028**; (10) link Horizon Europe national contact points to national CAP networks through EIP-AGRI events, **2027**; (11) revise the practice abstract format, **Q4 2027**; (12) map and promote testing and demonstration sites, **Q4 2027**; (13) valorise the results of the EU study on Horizon Europe tools for sustainable innovation, **Q4 2026**.
- **Food technology:** (14) encourage private investment in open-access pilot and demo-scale bioprocessing infrastructure, **Q1 2028**; (15) reinforce the health-food link (cardiometabolic and neurological disease, personalised nutrition), **Q1 2027**.
- **AgRI 2040:** (16) host the first AgRI 2040 conference, **Q3 2026**; (17) develop the areas of action with stakeholders, **Q4 2027**.
- **Food 2040:** (18) develop R&I activities on the six areas, **Q3 2028**; (19) first Food 2040 high-level conference, **Q4 2028**.
- The Communication says the Commission will "explore", "promote" or "encourage": these are intentions, not obligations on anyone.
