# SFDR Review ("SFDR 2.0"), procedure 2025/0361(COD): state of play

## QUICK FACTS
- **STATE OF PLAY (read on OEIL and the Council site, 22 October 2026): the review of the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation is in the trilogue phase.** Parliament's negotiating mandate was confirmed by plenary on **7 October 2026** (Rule 72); the Council adopted its position on **24 June 2026**. NOT yet law; no provisional agreement as of 22 October 2026. There is NO plenary vote on the text before a trilogue deal.
- **Proposal:** **COM(2025) 841 of 20 November 2025**, amending **Regulation (EU) 2019/2088** (SFDR, CELEX 32019R2088, applied since March 2021) and the PRIIPs key information document rules. Procedure **2025/0361(COD)**, ordinary legislative procedure. The proposal text was NOT read for this guide.
- **Parliament:** lead committee **ECON** only (the ENVI committee decided NOT to give an opinion); rapporteur **Gerben-Jan Gerbrandy (Renew, Netherlands)**, appointed 29 January 2026; committee vote and decision to open negotiations **10 September 2026**; report **A10-0234/2026** tabled 15 September 2026; announced in plenary 5 October, confirmed 7 October 2026. OEIL status: "Awaiting Parliament's position in 1st reading". The report's content was NOT read.
- **Council position (Coreper II, 24 June 2026, Cyprus presidency, press release "Council agrees position on simpler transparency rules for sustainable financial products"):** three product categories replace the Article 8/9 practice: **"sustainable"**, **"transition"** and **"ESG basics"**; products in the first two must disclose principal adverse impacts using **at least three indicators** from a Commission list (a fourth for fossil-fuel investments); companies in the fossil-fuel sector may enter the transition category if **20% of capital expenditure** is EU-taxonomy-aligned and they hold a time-bound emissions-reduction strategy; general-purpose issuances by EU public bodies may enter the transition category; **alternative investment funds offered only to professional investors** may skip the categorisation rules.

## Context
SFDR (Regulation (EU) 2019/2088) obliges financial market participants and advisers to disclose how they integrate sustainability risks and adverse impacts. The Commission's 2024-2025 review found the disclosures too long and complex and the Article 8/9 distinction used as a de facto label, which fed greenwashing confusion.
