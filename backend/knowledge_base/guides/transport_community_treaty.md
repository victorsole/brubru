## What It Is

Council Decision (EU) 2026/523 authorises the EU position on staff salary scales within the Transport Community, an international organisation established by the Transport Community Treaty (signed 2017, in force since 2019).

The Transport Community connects the EU with Western Balkans partners (Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia) for the purpose of creating an integrated transport market and extending the EU transport acquis to the region.

## Structure

This decision has a nested structure typical of Article 218(9) TFEU acts:
1. The outer Council Decision (3 articles) authorises the EU position
2. The inner draft decision of the Regional Steering Committee (4 articles + salary annex) contains the actual staff remuneration framework

The salary table covers 5 grade levels with monthly min/max ranges in euros.
