# EU-Mexico High-Level Dialogue on Health: first meeting (Mexico City, 14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** the **first meeting of the EU-Mexico High-Level Dialogue on Health**, held in **Mexico City on 14 October 2026**, co-chaired by **Mexico's Secretary of Health Dr David Kershenobich** and **EU Commissioner for Health and Animal Welfare Olivér Várhelyi**. Also present: **COFEPRIS** Federal Commissioner Dr Víctor Hugo Borja and representatives of Mexico's foreign affairs and science ministries.
- **Origin:** launched by leaders at the **8th EU-Mexico Summit on 22 May 2026**, where the **Modernised Global Agreement** and the **Interim Trade Agreement** were signed.
- **Topics of the kick-off:** non-communicable diseases (**cardiovascular diseases, diabetes and mental health** as shared priorities), pandemic prevention, preparedness and response, regulatory cooperation, **medicines and medical devices**, research and innovation, and **digital health** (health data governance).
- **Aims (Commission's words):** turn shared priorities into results for patients, stronger health systems and **more resilient, diversified supply chains**.
- **What was NOT read:** the second half of the Commission's page (further sections after digital health); no agreements, figures or next-meeting date are given here, so give none.
