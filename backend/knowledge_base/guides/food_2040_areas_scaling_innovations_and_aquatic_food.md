# Food 2040 areas 5 and 6: scaling innovations and fair solutions; aquatic food

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** areas 5 and 6 of the **six** Food 2040 areas of action (COM(2026) 453, section 3.4).
- **Area 5, scaling innovations, business models and fair food systems solutions:** a food systems transition that is innovative, competitive and **closer to market**, as well as socially fair and respectful of ecosystems and planetary boundaries. R&I should lead more often to commercial applications (products, ventures, technologies), which need (i) collaboration between academia, industry and institutions or infrastructures, (ii) investment and (iii) the right partnerships. It is defined in cross-reference to the measures on closing the gap from lab to fab and on food technology. Structural barriers, inequities and vulnerabilities are to be addressed; **AI, digital platforms and biotechnologies** for scale-up are a priority.
- **Area 6, aquatic food:** in line with the **forthcoming EU ocean and water research and innovation strategy**: sustainable **fisheries and aquaculture** in marine and inland waters, food security and a **regenerative blue bioeconomy**.
- **Area 6 topics:** climate-related and other natural risks to aquatic food value chains; models of interactions between fisheries, aquaculture and aquatic ecosystems; **selective, low-impact fishing gear** to cut by-catch and habitat disturbance; animal welfare and health of farmed and fished aquatic species; breeding and feeding and **less reliance on feed imports**; co-design of the management of aquatic living resources with fishers and aquaculture farmers; sustainable aquaculture (**organic, offshore, integrated multi-trophic**); advanced fermentation; market uptake of **underutilised aquatic biomass** (fisheries and aquaculture by-products, algae, invasive species); short aquatic food value chains.
- **Related Food 2030 projects:** AWARE (fish farming in recycled water, Puglia), IMPRESS (zero-waste aquatic value chains), EcoScope, SEAwise, FutureEUAqua, NewTechAqua.
