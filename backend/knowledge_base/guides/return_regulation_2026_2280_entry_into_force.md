# Return Regulation (EU) 2026/2280: published 15 October 2026, in force 16 October 2026, most rules apply from 17 October 2027

## QUICK FACTS
- **The act:** Regulation (EU) 2026/2280 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 October 2026 establishing a common system for the return of third-country nationals staying illegally in the Union. **Published in the Official Journal (OJ L) on Thursday 15 October 2026.** Signed in Strasbourg on 7 October 2026 by Roberta Metsola (Parliament) and Thomas Byrne (Council). ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2280/oj
- **Procedure:** 2025/0059(COD), Commission proposal COM(2025) 101 of 11 March 2025; Parliament position 17 June 2026; Council decision 1 October 2026.
- **Entry into force: 16 October 2026** (the day after publication, Article 50(1)). It is a REGULATION: directly applicable, no transposition.
- **Split application (Article 50(2) and (3)), read in the OJ text:**
  - **From 16 October 2026:** Article 1 (subject matter), Article 2(1)-(3), Article 3 point (3), Article 4 (fundamental rights), Article 5(13) and (15), **Article 14 (return to a third country with which there is an agreement or arrangement: the legal basis for what the press calls "return hubs")**, Article 15 (best interests of the child), Article 16 (age assessment of minors), Article 34 (readmission), Article 35 (external dimension and cooperation with third countries), Article 41 (competent authorities), Article 43 (Frontex support), Articles 47 to 49 (committee procedure, reporting, repeal).
  - **From 17 October 2027: every other provision** (the return procedure itself, detention, entry bans and the rest). Do NOT say the whole Regulation applies now.
- **Article 14 in one line:** returning a person to such a third country requires an agreement or arrangement with it, which may only be concluded where international human rights standards, including non-refoulement, are respected; the agreement must set out procedures, conditions of stay, the third country's obligations and what happens on a violation.
- **Repeal (Article 49):** Directive 2008/115/EC (the Return Directive), Directive 2001/40/EC (mutual recognition of expulsion decisions) and Decision 2004/191/EC are **repealed for the Member States bound by this Regulation with effect from 17 October 2027**; references to them read as references to the Regulation (correlation table in the Annex). "Bound by" matters: not every Member State takes part in this area.
- **What was NOT read:** the recitals and Articles 5 to 13 and 17 to 46 in detail; their content is not summarised here. For the negotiation history (trilogue deal of 1 June 2026, rapporteur Malik Azmani, LIBE vote) see `returns_policy_reform`.

## How to answer
"Is the Return Regulation in force?": yes, from 16 October 2026 (OJ L of 15 October 2026, Regulation (EU) 2026/2280). Only a first set of articles applies at once, including Article 14 on agreements with third countries ("return hubs"); the main return rules apply from 17 October 2027, when the 2008 Return Directive is repealed.
