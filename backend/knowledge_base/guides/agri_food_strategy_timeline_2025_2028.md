# AgRI 2040 and Food 2040: timeline from the Vision (2025) to the Food 2040 conference (2028)

## QUICK FACTS
- **February 2025:** the Vision for Agriculture and Food (COM(2025) 75) announces a new strategic approach to R&I.
- **15 December 2025:** the open consultation (online survey) for AgRI 2040 is launched at the **EU Agri-Food Days 2025**, session "The future of EU agri-food: data and innovation for food security". It closes **25 January 2026** with **701 replies** (report April 2026).
- **January 2026:** the Standing Committee on Agricultural Research (SCAR) sets up a task force on startups and scaleups (15 countries). **27 February 2026:** SCAR consultation workshop.
- **March 2026:** Food 2040 survey and the **Food 2040 stakeholder workshop (25 March 2026, Brussels, 80 participants)**; cluster workshops of Food 2030 projects on 10 March and 16 March 2026 (and 10 September and 24 October 2025).
- **13 July 2026:** the report **Building Food 2040** (631 survey responses, 63 written submissions) is announced.
- **8 September 2026:** the Commission adopts **Communication COM(2026) 453 final** and **SWD(2026) 271 final**; the same day the Implementing Decision setting up the **EDIC for Agri-Food** (Decision (EU) 2026/1978).
- **24-25 September 2026:** the **EU AgRI 2040 Conference**, Brussels (Charlemagne building), "Future-proofing EU Agri-Food through research and innovation": it **took place** (08:30 on 24 September to 15:00 on 25 September; the event page says "This event has ended"), opened with keynotes by Commissioners Hansen and Zaharieva, with nine breakout sessions to co-identify R&I priorities for the eight AgRI 2040 areas plus climate change and biodiversity. **No conclusions or report had been read on 13 October 2026**; give none.
- **Annex 1 starting quarters:** **Q3 2026** first AgRI 2040 conference (action 16); **Q4 2026** actions 4, 7 and 13; **2027** actions 1, 2 and 10; **Q1 2027** action 15; **Q4 2027** actions 3, 5, 6, 11, 12 and 17 (AgRI 2040 areas of action developed with stakeholders); **Q1 2028** actions 8, 9 and 14; **Q3 2028** action 18 (R&I activities on the six Food 2040 areas); **Q4 2028** action 19 (first Food 2040 high-level conference).
- These are Commission work-planning dates, not legal deadlines. Funding it points to: **Horizon Europe 2028-2034** and the **European Competitiveness Fund**, without prejudging the next MFF.
