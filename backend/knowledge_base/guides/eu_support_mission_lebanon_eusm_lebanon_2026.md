# EUSM Lebanon: the new EU CSDP Support Mission in Lebanon (established 12 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **12 October 2026** the Council adopted a decision **establishing the European Union Support Mission in Lebanon (EUSM Lebanon)** under the Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP), at the Foreign Affairs Council in Luxembourg. The Council's press release is dated **12 October 2026, 12:40**. High Representative **Kaja Kallas** had announced on 8 October, in Beirut, that Foreign Ministers would give the "final green light" on the following Monday.
- **Legal act (read in the Official Journal on 13 October 2026):** **Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2296 of 12 October 2026 on a European Union Support mission in Lebanon (EUSM Lebanon)**, OJ L of 13 October 2026, 21 Articles (ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/2296/oj). It **enters into force on the date of adoption (12 October)** and **applies for three years from the LAUNCH of the mission** (Article 21), so the three years start only with the launch decision.
- **Mandate:** the mission is to contribute to peace and security in Lebanon, support the strengthening of state institutions and promote lasting stability "in accordance with United Nations Security Council Resolution 1701"; it will strengthen the capacities of the **Lebanese Armed Forces (LAF)** and the **Internal Security Forces (ISF)** and support **maritime security governance**.
- **Two pillars under one political mandate:**
  - **Military pillar:** advisory support and training for the LAF on **border security and maritime security**, including land border surveillance and control, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, counter-smuggling and operational coordination.
  - **Civilian pillar:** advisory support and specialised training for the ISF to strengthen operational capabilities and build sustainable training structures, including **cybercrime and anti-smuggling**, plus support for **port security governance, including the Port of Beirut**.
- **How the High Representative put it:** the mission "will advise and assist the Lebanese Armed Forces and Internal Security Forces to strengthen territorial control, border security, and internal security".
- **Links to other EU tools:** the mission will facilitate and complement EU assistance provided through the **European Peace Facility** and coordinate with EU institutions, Member States, the UN and other partners supporting Lebanon's security sector.
- **NOT yet launched (Article 20):** the **Council decision to launch EUSM Lebanon is adopted after the Operation Plan (civilian pillar) and the Mission Plan with rules of engagement (military pillar) are approved**, on the date the Military Commander and Civilian Operation Commander recommend; any deployment is preceded by an in-depth security assessment. Say the mission is **established, not launched**, and that **no start date, no size and no head of either pillar** is in the Decision.
- **Where it works (Article 1(2)):** initially **within Lebanon north of the Litani River**; **south of the Litani only when the necessary political and security conditions are met and the Lebanese government agrees**, with the Political and Security Committee (PSC) authorised to decide that.
- **Structure (Articles 3 to 5, 11):** the **PSC** exercises political control and strategic direction under the Council and the High Representative; **unified chain of command**; **headquarters in Lebanon**; a **civilian pillar** under the **Civilian Operation Commander** (the Managing Director of the Civilian Operations Headquarters) and a **military pillar** under a **Military Commander**; a Joint Support Coordination Cell links the two.
- **Money (Articles 10 and 14):** civilian pillar, charged to the Union budget: financial reference amount **EUR 4,303,135.85 for the six months following entry into force**. Military pillar, common costs under the **European Peace Facility** (Decision (CFSP) 2021/509): **EUR 2,880,000 for the first six months**, at 70% for commitments and 35% for payments (Article 51(2) of that Decision); the Council decides later periods.
- **How it started (recitals):** the Council approved a **Crisis Management Concept on 24 July 2026**; the **President of the Council of Ministers of Lebanon invited the Union by letter of 5 October 2026**. The status of the mission and its personnel is to be set by an **agreement between the Union and Lebanon** (Article 17, Article 37 TEU).
- **Background the Council cites:** the European Council conclusions of 18-19 June 2026 (support for the LAF "through a possible CSDP mission and reinforced assistance under the European Peace Facility"); the Council approved a **Crisis Management Concept on 24 July 2026**; the Lebanese authorities invited the EU to set up the mission.

## How to answer
State that EUSM Lebanon was established on 12 October 2026 with a three-year initial mandate and two pillars, and that a separate launch decision is still to come. Give the Decision number 2026/2296, the three years from launch, the Litani condition, the headquarters in Lebanon and the two financial reference amounts if asked; do not state a size, a start date or the names of the pillar heads (none is in the Decision).
