# EUSM Lebanon: the new EU CSDP Support Mission in Lebanon (established 12 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What happened:** on **12 October 2026** the Council adopted a decision **establishing the European Union Support Mission in Lebanon (EUSM Lebanon)** under the Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP), at the Foreign Affairs Council in Luxembourg. The Council's press release is dated **12 October 2026, 12:40**. High Representative **Kaja Kallas** had announced on 8 October, in Beirut, that Foreign Ministers would give the "final green light" on the following Monday.
- **Mandate and duration:** an **initial mandate of three years**. The mission is to contribute to peace and security in Lebanon, support the strengthening of state institutions and promote lasting stability "in accordance with United Nations Security Council Resolution 1701"; it will strengthen the capacities of the **Lebanese Armed Forces (LAF)** and the **Internal Security Forces (ISF)** and support **maritime security governance**.
- **Two pillars under one political mandate:**
  - **Military pillar:** advisory support and training for the LAF on **border security and maritime security**, including land border surveillance and control, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, counter-smuggling and operational coordination.
  - **Civilian pillar:** advisory support and specialised training for the ISF to strengthen operational capabilities and build sustainable training structures, including **cybercrime and anti-smuggling**, plus support for **port security governance, including the Port of Beirut**.
- **How the High Representative put it:** the mission "will advise and assist the Lebanese Armed Forces and Internal Security Forces to strengthen territorial control, border security, and internal security".
- **Links to other EU tools:** the mission will facilitate and complement EU assistance provided through the **European Peace Facility** and coordinate with EU institutions, Member States, the UN and other partners supporting Lebanon's security sector.
- **NOT yet deployed:** after this decision "operational planning will continue with a view to the future deployment of the mission". **The Council will adopt a separate decision launching EUSM Lebanon once planning is complete**: say that the mission is established, not launched, and that no deployment date, size, budget or Mission Commander has been published.
- **Background the Council cites:** the European Council conclusions of 18-19 June 2026 (support for the LAF "through a possible CSDP mission and reinforced assistance under the European Peace Facility"); the Council approved a **Crisis Management Concept on 24 July 2026**; the Lebanese authorities invited the EU to set up the mission.
- As of the afternoon of 12 October 2026 the Council Decision's number and legal text had not yet appeared in the Official Journal index; the substance above comes from the Council press release. If a live Official Journal lookup returns the Decision, cite it.

## How to answer
State that EUSM Lebanon was established on 12 October 2026 with a three-year initial mandate and two pillars, and that a separate launch decision is still to come. Do not state a size, a budget, a headquarters or a start date.
