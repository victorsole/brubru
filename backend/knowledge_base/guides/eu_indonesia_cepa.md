# EU-Indonesia Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) and Investment Protection Agreement (IPA)

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Monday 29 June 2026 — COMMISSION SENDS THE AGREEMENTS TO COUNCIL)**: The European Commission presented the Council with its proposals for the **signature and conclusion** of both the **EU-Indonesia Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)** and the **Investment Protection Agreement (IPA)**. Four proposals for Council Decisions were tabled: CEPA signing (**COM(2026) 341**, CELEX **52026PC0341**) + CEPA conclusion (**COM(2026) 340**, CELEX **52026PC0340**); IPA signing (**COM(2026) 342**, CELEX **52026PC0342**) + IPA conclusion (**COM(2026) 343**, CELEX **52026PC0343**). Source: DG TRADE, `policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-presents-council-ambitious-indonesia-agreements-2026-06-29_en`.
- **Negotiations concluded**: **23 September 2025 in Bali** — Commissioner **Maroš Šefčovič** (Trade and Economic Security) and Indonesia's Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs **Airlangga Hartarto** signed the substantive conclusion after nearly a decade of talks (launched 2016).
- **Headline market access**: the CEPA removes import duties on **98.5% of tariff lines** and simplifies export procedures for EU goods to Indonesia, including agri-food. Establishes rules of origin, sustainability and traceability disciplines.
- **Procedure now**: Council approves **signature** → European Parliament gives its **consent** → Council adopts the **conclusion** → **entry into force**. Targeted entry into force: **2027**. The CEPA is a Common Commercial Policy act (Article 207 TFEU); the IPA, covering investment protection (incl. ISDS-type provisions), is a **mixed agreement** requiring national ratification by all Member States before it fully enters into force.
- **Why it matters**: part of the EU's strategy to **diversify trade partnerships** (away from over-reliance on any single partner), secure critical-raw-materials and energy supply chains, and open Southeast Asia's largest economy (~280 million people). Steered through the Council under the **Irish Presidency** (H2 2026) — see `council_presidency_trio_programme`.
- **Lead**: DG TRADE; Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič. EP committee: **INTA** (International Trade).

## What the CEPA and IPA are

The **CEPA** is a modern free-trade agreement: near-total tariff elimination on goods, services liberalisation, public procurement access, rules on digital trade, geographical indications, SMEs, and a Trade and Sustainable Development (TSD) chapter (labour + environment commitments). The **IPA** is a separate investment-protection treaty guaranteeing fair and equitable treatment, protection against unlawful expropriation, and a reformed investment-court-system mechanism for dispute settlement.

The two are negotiated together but travel on different legal tracks: the CEPA is **EU-only** (exclusive competence, so Council + EP consent suffice), while the IPA is **mixed** (shared competence on investment protection), so it also needs ratification in every Member State — which is why an IPA typically enters into force years after the trade agreement.
