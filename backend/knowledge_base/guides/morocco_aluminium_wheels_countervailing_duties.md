# Aluminium Road Wheels from Morocco (Countervailing Duties, Reg (EU) 2025/500)

## QUICK FACTS

- **Short name:** Morocco ARW countervailing duties / Reg 2025/500
- **Full title:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/500 of 13 March 2025 imposing definitive countervailing duties on imports of certain aluminium road wheels originating in Morocco
- **CELEX:** 32025R0500
- **OJ reference:** OJ L, 2025/500

## QUICK ANSWERS

**What did the EU decide?** It imposed definitive countervailing (anti-subsidy) duties on aluminium road wheels imported from Morocco, after finding that two Moroccan exporting producers received countervailable subsidies that caused material injury to the EU wheel industry.

**What are the duty rates?** Dika Morocco Africa S.A. (DMA): **31.4%** (TARIC additional code C897); Hands 8 S.A.: **5.6%** (C873); all other imports from Morocco: **5.6%** (C999). These are on top of the existing anti-dumping duties of 9.0% to 17.5% (Reg (EU) 2023/99).

**Why is DMA's rate so much higher?** DMA is a subsidiary of the Chinese group CITIC Dicastal. Its overall subsidy rate was 31.45%, dominated by a preferential price of inputs (15.65%, cheap aluminium from related Chinese suppliers) and de facto loans linked to capital goods (5.44%) and inputs (2.03%), plus Moroccan tax and import-duty exemptions and grants.

**What is the cross-border subsidy doctrine here?** The Commission found that preferential financing provided by the Government of China under the Belt and Road Initiative, channelled through CITIC Dicastal into its Moroccan subsidiary, is attributed to the Government of Morocco (the country of export). Morocco set up the cooperation framework, granted special-economic-zone access and authorisations, and retained powers to monitor the investment. CITIC Dicastal's stated aim was to avoid the EU's anti-dumping duties on Chinese wheels.

**What is the legal authority for that?** The Court of Justice judgment of 28 November 2024 in the joined Egyptian fibreglass cases (Hengshi Egypt and Jushi Egypt, C-269/23 P and C-272/23 P, ECLI:EU:C:2024:984), which confirmed that a foreign government's investment in a firm in another country can be a countervailable subsidy attributable to the host (export) government.

**Why was the duty not capped at the injury margin?** The lesser-duty rule does not apply in anti-subsidy investigations under the current rules, unless setting the duty at the full subsidy amount is clearly not in the Union interest. The Commission found no such reason, so the duty equals the full subsidy amount.

**What was the injury?** Moroccan imports rose from 878,000 items (2020) to 5,930,000 in the investigation period (index 675); their market share rose from 2% to 9%. The EU industry's profitability fell from 1% (2020) to minus 3%, its market share fell from 71% to 65%, and a price squeeze took hold (sales price 49 to 65 EUR per item against unit cost 49 to 82 EUR per item).

**Did Morocco object on the Association Agreement?** Yes. Morocco argued countervailing duties are barred by Articles 8 and 9 of the EU-Morocco Euro-Mediterranean Association Agreement. The Commission rejected this, relying on Article 36, which makes the GATT Article VI rules (and thus the WTO ASCM and the basic Regulation) the applicable disciplines for trade-distorting subsidies.

## OVERVIEW

This is the EU's first anti-subsidy action against Morocco and the fourth post-judgment application of the cross-border subsidy attribution doctrine. The case follows the template of EU countervailing-duty investigations (procedure, product, subsidisation, injury, causation, Union interest, definitive measures) but its defining feature is the attribution of Chinese Belt-and-Road financing to Morocco as the country of export. It runs in parallel with anti-dumping duties already in force on the same product since January 2023.

## KEY NUMBERS

- Definitive countervailing duties: DMA 31.4%; Hands 8 5.6%; all other Morocco 5.6%.
- DMA overall subsidy 31.45%; Hands 8 overall 5.60%.
- Largest single subsidy: preferential price of inputs for DMA, 15.65%.
- Parallel anti-dumping duties: 9.0% to 17.5% (Reg (EU) 2023/99).
- Moroccan import volume index: 100 (2020) to 675 (investigation period); market share 2% to 9%.
- Union industry profitability: 1% (2020) to minus 3% (investigation period); market share 71% to 65%.
- Aluminium road wheels are about 0.7% of car manufacturers' costs.

## RELATED BRUBRU GUIDES AND CANON ENTRIES

- Fourth canon entry of the `anti_subsidy_trade_remedies` family, alongside the China BEV countervailing duties (Reg (EU) 2024/2754), the China and Egypt glass-fibre fabrics countervailing duties (Reg (EU) 2020/776) and the India and Indonesia stainless-steel countervailing duties (Reg (EU) 2022/433). It is the clearest application of the cross-border subsidy doctrine after the Court of Justice affirmed it in November 2024.
