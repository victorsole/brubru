## Overview

The Banking Union reform package revises the EU's crisis management and deposit insurance framework. It consists of three interconnected legislative files that update the Single Resolution Mechanism (SRM), the Bank Recovery and Resolution Directive (BRRD), and the Deposit Guarantee Schemes Directive (DGSD).

The ECON committee adopted recommendations for second reading on 19 March 2026, signalling the final stage of legislative negotiations. These are:

- **A10-0067/2026**: SRM Regulation -- early intervention measures and resolution tools (Tinagli)
- **A10-0066/2026**: BRRD -- resolution measures and valuation services (Niedermayer)
- **A10-0065/2026**: DGSD -- deposit guarantee scope expansion (Peter-Hansen)

## Key Reforms

### SRM Regulation (806/2014) Amendments
- Expanded resolution toolkit for medium-sized banks
- Improved early intervention powers for the Single Resolution Board
- Faster resolution procedures to reduce market disruption
- Enhanced cooperation between national resolution authorities and SRB

### BRRD (2014/59/EU) Amendments
- Updated resolution measures including improved valuation services
- Streamlined bail-in procedures
- Enhanced creditor hierarchy rules
- Better alignment with international standards (FSB TLAC)

### DGSD (2014/49/EU) Amendments
- Expanded deposit guarantee scope
- Harmonised coverage across Member States
- Improved payout procedures and timelines
- Enhanced role of deposit guarantee schemes in resolution funding

## Political Context

The Banking Union remains incomplete without a European Deposit Insurance Scheme (EDIS), which is not part of this package. The CMDI reforms represent incremental progress on the crisis management pillar while the deposit insurance pillar (third pillar) remains politically blocked.

Key stakeholders:
- ECB/SSM: Support broader resolution scope
- SRB: Advocates for stronger resolution tools
- National DGSs: Cautious about harmonisation
- Banking industry: Mixed -- supports clarity, concerned about costs
