## Why now

The Fertilisers Action Plan is the Commission's response to a multi-cause cost shock on EU farmers' fertiliser bills:

1. **Iran war (March 2026 onwards)**: gas-price spike → ammonia production cost spike → urea + ammonium-nitrate prices spike (Europe imports significant volumes of finished fertilisers + dependent gas inputs)
2. **EU ETS exposure on the fertiliser industry**: Yara, ICL, KAFCO, Fertiberia, OCI all carbon-leakage-risk producers
3. **CBAM Phase 1 (Reg (EU) 2023/956)**: imported nitrogen fertilisers fall in CBAM scope, raising the landed cost of cheaper third-country product
4. **Russian + Belarusian fertiliser sanctions** (since 2022): EU continues to import Russian fertiliser at significant volumes (legal but politically contested)
5. **Critical Raw Materials Act overlap**: phosphate rock (an EU strategic raw material) — EU 90%+ import-dependent; Russia, Morocco, China dominant
6. **High-Level Dialogue on Fertilisers** held 13 April 2026 (Brussels) — industry + Member States + Commission stakeholder consultation that fed into the Action Plan drafting

## Expected pillars (based on industry position papers + High-Level Dialogue + Reuters coverage)

1. **Decarbonisation of EU fertiliser production**: green ammonia, electrified Haber-Bosch, hydrogen-ready facilities. ETS revenue redirection to finance the transition is on the table (industry is asking, Commission is considering).
2. **Affordability for EU farmers**: short-term financial relief (cited by Hansen at the High-Level Dialogue), regulatory burden reduction, possible CAP Strategic Plans flexibility allowing fertiliser-cost subsidies.
3. **Reduced import dependency**: CBAM strengthening + screening of Russian/Belarusian flows + investment incentives for EU production capacity. **Fertilisers Europe (industry body) is asking for a regulatory framework for circular mineral fertilisers (recovered nutrients from waste streams)** — reduces dependency on virgin phosphate rock.
4. **Nutrient-use efficiency**: decision-support tools for farmers, precision agriculture, integration with the CAP Strategic Plans and the GAEC standards (especially GAEC 4 + GAEC 9 on nutrient management).
5. **Strategic autonomy + food security framing**: positions the Plan as part of the wider EU agri-food sovereignty agenda alongside the **CAP 2028-2034 sustainable farming guidance** (DG AGRI, 28 April 2026) and **CBAM Phase 1**.

## Existing legal anchors the Action Plan will build on

- **Fertilising Products Regulation**: Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 (CELEX 32019R1009) — sets quality + labelling requirements for CE-marked fertilising products in the single market
- **Nitrates Directive**: Council Directive 91/676/EEC — water-protection limits on nitrate
- **CBAM Regulation**: Regulation (EU) 2023/956 + 2025/0419(COD) downstream extension proposal
- **EU ETS Directive**: Directive 2003/87/EC (consolidated)
- **Common Agricultural Policy Strategic Plans Regulation**: Regulation (EU) 2021/2115 — Articles 13 (GAEC) + 31 (eco-schemes)
- **Critical Raw Materials Act**: Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 — phosphate rock is on the strategic + critical list
- **Industrial Emissions Directive**: Directive 2010/75/EU recast — covers ammonia/urea production facilities
