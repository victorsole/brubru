## Active and Upcoming Legislative Files

### ReFuelEU Aviation -- Sustainable Aviation Fuels (SAF)
- Regulation (EU) 2023/2405, CELEX 32023R2405
- Procedure: 2021/0205(COD)
- Status: ADOPTED (entered into force 1 January 2024, obligations phased in from 2025)
- SAF blending mandates: 2% (2025), 6% (2030), 20% (2035), 34% (2040), 42% (2045), 70% (2050)
- Synthetic fuel sub-mandate: 1.2% (2030), rising to 35% (2050)
- Applies to: all EU airports with >800,000 passenger movements or >100,000 tonnes of freight
- Airline concern: high cost of SAF vs. conventional jet fuel, supply availability, competitive disadvantage vs. non-EU carriers

### EU ETS for Aviation
- Directive 2003/87/EC as amended by Directive (EU) 2023/958 (CELEX 32023L0958)
- Scope: intra-EEA flights (since 2012); international flights covered by CORSIA
- Free allowances phased out: 100% auctioning from 2026
- ETS review for aviation upcoming: update to include maritime-aviation interactions
- Procedure announced: Update of EU ETS for maritime, aviation and stationary installations
- EPRS Briefing (8 April 2026): "CountEmissionsEU: Measuring emissions from transport services" -- greening of freight transport proposals (July 2023). Ref: EPRS_BRI(2023)757562

### CORSIA (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation)
- ICAO-led global scheme for international aviation emissions
- EU notification and alignment procedure announced
- EU position: CORSIA complements but does not replace EU ETS for intra-EEA flights

### EU261 -- Passenger Rights Revision
- Current: Regulation (EC) 261/2004, CELEX 32004R0261
- Revision proposed: COM(2013) 130, procedure 2013/0072(COD)
- Status: STALLED in trilogue since 2014; Commission opinion on EP amendments tabled
- Key issues: compensation thresholds, extraordinary circumstances definition, connecting flights, tarmac delays
- Industry position: A4E, ERA, IATA urge "realistic stance"; current EU261 costs airlines and passengers EUR 8.1 billion annually
- Interplay with Regulation (EC) 2027/97 on air carrier liability
- CONCILIATION stage (April 2026): COREPER I (15 April) preparing for conciliation on air passenger rights revision
- Transport Council (21 April 2026): Informal videoconference of Transport Ministers; air passenger rights conciliation preparation; Middle East crisis transport sector impact

### Single European Sky 2+ (SES2+)
- Procedure: 2013/0186(COD)
- Status: long-running negotiations, still in trilogue
- Objective: reform EU airspace management, reduce fragmentation (currently 27 national systems)
- Key body: Eurocontrol (intergovernmental, not EU)
- Airline impact: more efficient routes = lower fuel burn, shorter flights, fewer delays
- EP rapporteur: historically TRAN committee lead

### EU/USA/Iceland/Norway Air Transport Agreement
- Procedure: 2011/0102(NLE)
- Status: Draft report in TRAN (rapporteur Johan Danielsson, S&D, Sweden), tabled February 2026
- Document: Committee draft report PE784.150
- Covers: Open Skies provisions, market access, competition rules, environmental standards

### Aviation Security Screening Equipment

## Key Policy Areas for Airlines

### Emissions and Climate
- EU ETS auctioning (100% from 2026): direct cost impact
- SAF mandates (ReFuelEU): blending costs, supply chain challenges
- CORSIA alignment: avoiding double regulation for international flights
- Fit for 55 package implications for aviation fuel taxation

### Passenger Rights
- EU261 revision: compensation levels, delay thresholds, extraordinary circumstances
- Regulation 2027/97: carrier liability for baggage and accidents
- Accessibility requirements (PRM regulation)

### Market Access and Competition
- EU-US Open Skies / Air Transport Agreement
- Safeguarding competition in air transport (Regulation (EU) 2019/712, CELEX 32019R0712)
- Slot allocation regulation
- State aid rules for airlines and airports

### Safety and Security
- EASA (European Union Aviation Safety Agency): type certification, airworthiness
- Aviation security screening equipment certification
- Drone regulation (U-space, Regulation (EU) 2019/947)

### Infrastructure
- Single European Sky reform (SES2+): airspace efficiency
- Airport capacity and slot allocation
- TEN-T airports (Core and Comprehensive Network)

## Surface Transport: Rail, Inland Waterways, and Multimodal

### State Aid GBER for Rail/Inland Waterways/Multimodal (March 2026)

**Regulation (EU) 2026/562** (CELEX: 32026R0562) -- Commission Regulation of 16 March 2026 declaring certain categories of aid in the rail, inland waterways and multimodal transport sector compatible with the internal market in application of Articles 107 and 108 TFEU. This is a new General Block Exemption Regulation (GBER) for surface transport state aid, allowing Member States to grant aid for:
- Rail infrastructure and rolling stock investments
- Inland waterway infrastructure modernisation
- Multimodal transport hubs and intermodal terminals
- Shift from road to rail/waterway (modal shift incentives)

This removes the need for prior Commission notification for qualifying aid measures, significantly reducing administrative burden for Member States investing in sustainable transport.
