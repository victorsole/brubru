# Omnibus VIII: Environmental Simplification Package (December 2025)

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (6-8 Oct 2026): INSPIRE and the EPR suspension.** **INSPIRE (2025/0393(COD), Emma Wiesner, A10-0233/2026): settled by the plenary minutes of 6 October:** the committee's amendments 1-23 were adopted, the Commission proposal as amended was approved by 515 votes to 108 (35 abstentions) and the request for referral back to committee was adopted, so Parliament has its position but the first reading is NOT closed; the file is back in ENVI. **EPR authorised-representative suspension (2025/0395 and 2025/0396(COD), rapporteur Ingeborg ter Laak, EPP):** ENVI voted on 5 October, **74 for, 0 against, 4 abstentions on batteries and packaging** and **72 for, 0 against, 5 abstentions on textiles, footwear, electronic appliances and single-use plastics**; it narrows the Commission's suspension to EU-based micro and small enterprises (fewer than 50 persons and turnover or balance sheet under EUR 10 million), keeps the duty for non-EU companies and adds a sunset clause; plenary expected 19-22 October (Parliament press release 5 October).
- **What it is:** the Commission's eighth simplification omnibus, **10 December 2025**: six proposals cutting burden in EU environmental law. All are ordinary legislative procedure, ENVI-led; references verified on OEIL and Cellar on 1 October 2026.
- **STATUS (1 Oct 2026): none is law.** Council: Coreper mandates on 24 June 2026 (burden-reduction regulation and directive, INSPIRE); **Council mandate on the speeding-up regulation on 30 September 2026**; Presidency target: agreement with Parliament by the end of 2026. Parliament: INSPIRE voted in ENVI 10 Sep (A10-0233/2026, plenary forecast 5 Oct); EPR suspensions on ENVI's 5 Oct vote (plenary forecast 19 Oct); the other three await committee (forecast 23 Nov).
- **The six files:**
  - Speeding-up environmental assessments regulation: COM(2025) 984, **2025/0391(COD)**, rapporteur Emma Wiesner (Renew).
  - Batteries Regulation 2023/1542 + Industrial Emissions Portal Regulation 2024/1244: COM(2025) 981, **2025/0397(COD)**, rapporteur Susana Solís Pérez (EPP). Wider "producer" definition (any seller established elsewhere, any selling technique), a definition of the substances of very high concern for the hazard label, light-means-of-transport packs replaceable at module level, not cell level. **It does NOT touch the battery passport or its 18 February 2027 date** ("passport" does not appear in the proposal).
  - Waste Framework, Industrial Emissions, Medium Combustion Plants and 2024/1785 directives: COM(2025) 986, **2025/0394(COD)**, rapporteur Susana Solís Pérez (EPP).
  - INSPIRE (Directive 2007/2/EC, spatial data): COM(2025) 985, **2025/0393(COD)**, rapporteur Emma Wiesner (Renew).
  - EPR authorised-representative suspension, batteries and packaging (regulation): COM(2025) 982, **2025/0395(COD)**, rapporteur Ingeborg ter Laak (EPP).
  - EPR authorised-representative suspension, waste, WEEE and other directives: COM(2025) 983, **2025/0396(COD)**, rapporteur Ingeborg ter Laak (EPP).
- **Council's changes to the speeding-up regulation (30 Sep 2026):** an exhaustive list of strategic projects for the new "toolbox"; permitting provisions from the Net-Zero Industry Act, Chips Act and Critical Raw Materials Act streamlined; longer time limits; substantial preclusion rewritten to comply with the Aarhus Convention; Habitats and Birds provision extended to recurring activities (forestry, watercourse maintenance) with safeguards; flexible environmental single point of contact.
- **Packaging:** the Council also asked for a "targeted solution" on PPWR (Regulation (EU) 2025/40) EPR obligations for small packaging producers.
- **Frame:** 'One Europe, One Market' roadmap (24 April 2026) aims to finish all simplification packages by the end of 2027.

## Who to ask about what
- **Permitting and environmental assessment** (wind, grids, mines, factories): the speeding-up regulation. Companies care about the strategic-project list and the time limits; NGOs and lawyers about the Aarhus-compliant preclusion clause and the Habitats Directive changes.
- **Battery makers and importers:** the batteries part changes who counts as a producer and how LMT batteries must be repairable. The battery passport and its 18 February 2027 date are untouched by this package.
- **Any company selling packaged or electrical goods across borders:** the EPR suspension files remove, for now, the obligation to appoint an authorised representative in each Member State.
