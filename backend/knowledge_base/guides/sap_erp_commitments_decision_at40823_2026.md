# SAP ERP maintenance and support: Commission commitments decision of 9 July 2026, Case AT.40823

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is: a commitments decision under Article 9 of Regulation (EC) No 1/2003, adopted 9 July 2026** (C(2026) 4685 final); summary published in the OJ C on 3 November 2026 (C/2026/5731). **NOT a fine, and NOT a finding of infringement:** the Commission makes SAP's commitments binding and considers there are "no longer grounds for action".
- **Who and what:** SAP SE; maintenance and support services for on-premises Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software in the European Economic Area. Possible abuse of dominance under Article 102 TFEU and Article 54 of the EEA Agreement. The case started on the Commission's own initiative; proceedings opened 25 September 2025.
- **Concerns, exactly four linked practices:** (1) the All-or-Nothing policy: same provider and support level for all on-premises ERP licences; (2) no termination of support for unused licences ("shelfware"); (3) extended initial licence terms during which support cannot be ended; (4) the reinstatement policy for customers returning after a gap.
- **Final commitments (improved after a market test with 43 submissions):**
  - customers can split their landscape into separate commercial installations, each with its own provider or support level; the split takes effect **four times a year**, not once;
  - in single-metric contracts, fees can fall by **up to 20% a year** for underuse (first offer: 10%);
  - partial termination without recalculating the maintenance base in three more cases (customer-specific maintenance only; failed projects attributable to SAP; customer insolvency);
  - customers may end **up to 10% of licences every two years** if their workforce fell by at least 10% in those two years, retroactive to **1 January 2025**;
  - licences can be reduced after divesting a business;
  - extended initial terms are clarified and not restarted at each new licence purchase; reinstatement fees abolished; back-maintenance fees cut to 50%, capped at 6 months, and waived for a list of unsupported products;
  - a single information point, an internal clearing structure for disputes, and an independent monitoring trustee.
- **Duration and scope:** the commitments apply to all customers worldwide for **10 years**; policies and contracts updated within three months of notification of the Decision.
- **Timeline:** preliminary assessment 25 September 2025; initial commitments 14 November 2025; market test notice 17 November 2025; Advisory Committee opinion and Hearing Officer report 6 July 2026; decision 9 July 2026.
- **If SAP breaks them (Regulation (EC) No 1/2003, not the summary):** a fine under Article 23(2)(c), and periodic penalty payments under Article 24. Neither is imposed here.
