# EU sanctions on circumvention networks: 3 individuals and 9 entities listed on 15 October 2026

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **15 October 2026** the Council listed **three individuals and nine entities** for importing sanctioned goods into Russia and for circumventing EU sanctions on **oil** and **financial transactions** (Council press release "Closing loopholes").
- **Legal acts (OJ L, 15 October 2026):** **Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2332** amending Decision 2014/145/CFSP, and **Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2333** implementing Regulation (EU) No 269/2014. This is the regime on actions **undermining Ukraine's territorial integrity**, not the economic-sanctions packages and not the hybrid-threats regime.
- **Who is listed (the Council's own examples):**
  - **MOVERS GROUP LLC** (Russian cargo company, parallel imports) and its General Director **Dmitry Chudovsky**;
  - **LLC DT INIER** (licensed Russian customs broker) and majority shareholder **Ivanna Savchuk**;
  - **BEGLER TEKSTIL INSAAT ELEKTRONIK VE DIS TICARET LTD** and **MEDSE GROUP LLC** (trading companies based in **Türkiye**);
  - **United Stream LLC** (Russian payment agent) and founder **Svetlana Gumenyuk**;
  - oil: **DIS Logistics DMCC**, **Vigor Marine Services LLP**, **SHIVA SHIPPING LTD** (shadow fleet ecosystem) and **HURUS TRADE FZE** (Russian crude to India); entities based in the **United Arab Emirates, India and the Seychelles**.
- **Effects:** asset freeze; ban on making funds or economic resources available; **travel ban** for the individuals.
- **Kaja Kallas** (High Representative): circumvention is "akin to aiding and abetting" Russia's war; sanctions "have already denied Moscow over one trillion euro" (her figure).
- **Not read:** the annexes of the two acts (statements of reasons, identifiers, country of each entity one by one). Do not assign a country to an entity beyond what is stated above.
