## Context

The Global Health Resilience Initiative responds to a convergent set of post-COVID + post-2024 geopolitical pressures on the global health architecture:
- **WHO Pandemic Treaty** finalised in May 2025 at the 78th World Health Assembly — the Treaty introduces a Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing System (PABS), strengthens supply-chain transparency, and establishes a Global Surge & Response framework. EU is a strong supporter but bilateral implementation depends on partner-country uptake.
- **International Health Regulations (IHR 2005) amendments** entered into force 19 September 2025 — strengthened reporting, pandemic-emergency declaration powers for the WHO Director-General, and equity provisions.
- **Geopolitical fragmentation** of global health funding: US disengagement from WHO under the second Trump administration; reduced contributions to GAVI + Global Fund; pressure on the EU to fill the leadership vacuum.
- **Outbreak environment 2025-2026**: Hantavirus outbreaks (May 2026 — Andes Hantavirus; HSC Opinion issued 12 May 2026 — see DG SANTE 12 May 2026 news), Mpox transitions, avian-influenza H5N1 zoonotic concerns.
- **EU Global Health Strategy (2022)** — JOIN(2022) 23 final, 30 November 2022 — the prior strategic anchor; the 2026 Initiative refreshes its priorities for the second von der Leyen Commission term.

## Expected pillars (pending text publication)

Based on College tentative-agenda framing, EEAS public messaging, and the wider geopolitical-health context:

1. **Pandemic preparedness + WHO Pandemic Treaty support**: financial + technical support to partner countries for IHR + Pandemic Treaty implementation; PABS-mechanism EU-side operationalisation
2. **EU as global health actor**: strengthened EEAS health diplomacy; coordinated Member State + Commission positioning at WHO, Global Fund, GAVI Boards; alignment with the **Team Europe** approach
3. **Cross-border health threats**: scale-up of HERA stockpiles + procurement + RescEU medical reserves; reinforcement of ECDC capacity
4. **Health-system strengthening in partner countries**: NDICI–Global Europe + IPA + future MFF 2028-2034 alignment of health-cooperation envelopes
5. **One Health + zoonotic-risk surveillance**: ties to EU Veterinary Medicines + Animal Health Regulation + zoonotic-disease surveillance
6. **Climate-health nexus**: integration with the European Climate Adaptation Plan + EU4Health Programme 2027 review

## Existing legal anchors

- **TFEU Article 168** (Public Health) — EU competence to complement Member State action on public health
- **EU Health Union package** — Regulation (EU) 2022/2371 on cross-border threats to health (CELEX 32022R2371) + Regulation (EU) 2022/123 reinforcing EMA + Regulation (EU) 2022/2370 reinforcing ECDC
- **HERA**: Commission Decision C(2021) 6712 (16 September 2021) — Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority within DG SANTE
