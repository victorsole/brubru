# Maternity and Paternity Leave in the EU

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU framework for maternity, paternity, parental and carers' leave -- mandatory minimum standards + Member State divergence
- EPRS At a Glance (20 April 2026): "Maternity and paternity leave in the EU" -- summary of minimum EU standards, MS divergence, gender-equality dimension. Ref: EPRS_UNK_613d0da18fbd
- Core legislation: **Work-Life Balance Directive (EU) 2019/1158** of 20 June 2019 on work-life balance for parents and carers, CELEX 32019L1158, OJ L 188, 12.7.2019
- EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32019L1158
- Transposition deadline: 2 August 2022 (most MS transposed; infringement procedures ongoing for laggards)
- **Pregnant Workers Directive (92/85/EEC)**: CELEX 31992L0085 -- minimum 14 weeks' maternity leave (2 weeks compulsory post-birth); paid at least sick-pay level; job protection
- **Parental Leave Directive (2010/18/EU)**: repealed and replaced by Work-Life Balance Directive
- Work-Life Balance Directive key provisions:
  - **Paternity leave**: 10 working days paid at sick-pay level, around birth (MS defined)
  - **Parental leave**: minimum 4 months per parent; 2 months non-transferable; paid at an "adequate" level (MS defined)
  - **Carers' leave**: 5 working days per year for employees caring for relatives with serious medical reason
  - **Flexible working arrangements**: right to request for parents of children under 8 and carers
  - **Work-related rights**: protection against dismissal for taking leave; return to same or equivalent post
- **Equal Pay Directive (EU) 2023/970**: pay transparency -- parental leave factored into pay audits
- Responsible Commissioner: Roxana Minzatu (EVP People, Skills and Preparedness)
- Responsible DG: DG EMPL (Employment, Social Affairs and Inclusion), Unit D.2 (Gender Equality and Equal Pay)
- EP lead committee: EMPL jointly with FEMM
- Council configuration: EPSCO
- Current debate: Commission Social Package (6 May 2026 College meeting) -- anti-poverty strategy, child guarantee, disability 2030, housing exclusion -- will include references to parental support

## Member State Minimum Levels (April 2026)

**Maternity leave (weeks, fully paid)** (selected):
- Bulgaria: 58 weeks (410 days at 90%), longest in EU
- Greece: 43 weeks
- Slovakia: 34 weeks
- Croatia: 30 weeks
- Italy: 22 weeks (5 mandatory pre-birth, 3 months post-birth at 80%)
- Germany: 14 weeks (6 before, 8 after)
- France: 16 weeks
- Spain: 16 weeks (extended to 26 weeks combined parental)
- Netherlands: 16 weeks
- Portugal: 17 weeks + 3 weeks extended leave

**Paternity leave (weeks, fully paid)**:
- Spain: 16 weeks (equalised with maternity since 2021)
- Finland: 9 weeks
- Portugal: 5 weeks
- Slovenia: 4 weeks
- Germany: 2 weeks (new from 2024)
- France: 4 weeks (extended in 2021 from 11 to 28 days)
- Italy: 2 weeks
- Ireland: 2 weeks

**Parental leave uptake by fathers** (EU average 2024): ~25% (Sweden 88%, Portugal 81%, Spain 79%, Germany 43%, Poland 1%, Greece 1%)

## Persistent Gaps

Per EPRS, key issues:

1. **Payment levels**: Work-Life Balance Directive requires "adequate" payment but MS set level. Some MS pay parental leave at minimum wage or flat rate, reducing father uptake
2. **SME impact**: flexible working arrangements creating compliance cost; Commission considering guidance + de minimis simplification
3. **Self-employed parents**: not fully covered; Commission Council Recommendation (2019/C 387/01) provides soft-law framework
4. **Fathers' leave uptake**: gender gap in uptake (EU average men 25% vs women ~75%) -- economic incentives + social norms
5. **Carers' leave**: 5 days minimum but many MS offer only unpaid carers' leave -- EU average uptake <5%
6. **Fertility / IVF leave**: not harmonised; some MS (France, Spain, Portugal) now recognise
7. **Miscarriage leave**: only 4 MS recognise (Belgium, Portugal, Ireland recent reform); UK provides 2 weeks

## Brubru User Questions Addressed

- "What is the minimum paternity leave in the EU?" -- 10 working days paid, per Work-Life Balance Directive
- "Does Spain have longer paternity leave?" -- Yes, 16 weeks (equalised with maternity) since 2021
- "What is parental leave?" -- 4 months per parent, 2 non-transferable, paid at "adequate" level (MS defined)
- "Is miscarriage leave EU harmonised?" -- No; only 4 MS recognise it (BE, PT, IE)
- "Why is father uptake of parental leave low?" -- Varies by MS; key drivers are payment levels and non-transferability rules
