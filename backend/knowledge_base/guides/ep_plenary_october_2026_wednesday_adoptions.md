# European Parliament Plenary, Strasbourg 5-8 October 2026: What Happened on Wednesday 7 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **EU-China relations: Parliament adopted a RECOMMENDATION on Wednesday 7 October 2026 (454 for, 86 against, 110 abstentions).** Report **A10-0237/2026**, procedure **2025/2117(INI)**, committee **AFET**, rapporteur **Hilde Vautmans (Renew, Belgium)**; addressed to the other EU institutions, **not a law**. A "stronger and principled" EU-China policy: dialogue, but relations "have reached a critical point" over China's unfair economic practices and its position on Russia's war; concern over "verified reports that the Chinese military has trained Russian military personnel to fight in Ukraine".
- **Economic content:** a "rigorous de-risking strategy" for **critical raw materials, medicines, batteries, electric vehicles, ICT, semiconductors**; stronger **investment screening**; **overcapacity and subsidies** named as challenges; **reciprocity** and "proportionate EU action where China fails to open its internal markets"; **Global Gateway** as the alternative to the Belt and Road. One China policy reaffirmed; no change to the Taiwan status quo "by force or coercion". The rapporteur linked the vote to "this week's Franco-German initiative" (`franco_german_trade_instrument_letter_oct_2026`) and hoped Commissioner **Šefčovič** would take the "united economic message to Beijing tomorrow" (8 October).
- **EGF: three applications approved, EUR 6.91 million in total.** **EGF/2026/001 Belgium, Cora: EUR 3.8 million**, 1,010 workers (605-56-7, report A10-0241/2026); **EGF/2026/002 Spain, Galicia automotive: EUR 2.7 million**, 400 workers (608-55-7, A10-0242/2026); **EGF/2026/003 Finland, Valmet Automotive: EUR 410,310**, 233 workers (601-57-9, A10-0243/2026). Procedures 2026/0195, 0197 and 0220(BUD) (which is which not checked). Committee BUDG.
- **MFF 2028-2034: a DEBATE, NO vote.** Co-rapporteur **Siegfried Mureşan (EPP)**: "any cuts to the budget ... will not be a basis for the consent of the Parliament"; the Commission proposed **1.15% of GNI** against **1.14%** for 2021-2027. Co-rapporteur **Carla Tavares (S&D)**: "structural challenges". See `mff_2028_2034`.
- **Also adopted on 7 October (plenary minutes):** the **Eurovignette amendment** on CO2 classes for trucks and buses (475-96-70; see `eurovignette_road_charging_co2_classes_2026`); France's accession to the **Inter-American Convention on Sea Turtles** (658-2-4); the Parliament's consultation vote on **consular protection for unrepresented EU citizens** (Düpont, A10-0254/2026, 470-180-18; see `eu_consular_protection_unrepresented_citizens_2023_0441`).

## FAQ
**Did Parliament vote on the 2028-2034 EU budget on 7 October?** No. It was a debate with the Commission and the Council. Parliament's formal consent comes at the end of the negotiation.
**Is the EU-China text binding?** No. It is a recommendation addressed to the other institutions; it sets Parliament's position and asks for action, but only the Commission and Council can act on it.
**Where is the Commission's China policy decided?** In the Commission (trade, investment screening, trade defence) and the Council; Parliament's role is its resolutions, its consent to agreements and its legislative files. See `eu_china_relations`.
