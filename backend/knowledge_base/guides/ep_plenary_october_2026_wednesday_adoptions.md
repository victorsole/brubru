# European Parliament Plenary, Strasbourg 5-8 October 2026: What Happened on Wednesday 7 October

## QUICK FACTS
- **EU-China relations: Parliament adopted a RECOMMENDATION on Wednesday 7 October 2026 (454 for, 86 against, 110 abstentions).** Procedure **2025/2117(INI)**, committee **AFET**, rapporteur **Hilde Vautmans (Renew, Belgium)**; addressed to the other EU institutions, **not a law**. It asks for a "stronger and principled" EU-China policy: dialogue on global challenges, but relations "have reached a critical point" over China's unfair economic practices and its position on Russia's war; concern over "verified reports that the Chinese military has trained Russian military personnel to fight in Ukraine".
- **Economic content:** a "rigorous de-risking strategy" for **critical raw materials, medicines, batteries, electric vehicles, ICT, semiconductors**; stronger **investment screening**; China's **overcapacity and subsidies** named as challenges; **reciprocity** and "proportionate EU action where China fails to open its internal markets"; **Global Gateway** as the alternative to the Belt and Road Initiative. Also: One China policy reaffirmed while opposing any change to the Taiwan status quo "by force or coercion", resilience against cyberattacks and interference, Tibet, Xinjiang, political prisoners. The rapporteur linked the vote to "this week's Franco-German initiative" (see `franco_german_trade_instrument_letter_oct_2026`) and hoped Commissioner **Šefčovič** would take the "united economic message to Beijing tomorrow" (8 October).
- **EGF: three applications approved on 7 October, EUR 6.91 million in total.** **Belgium EUR 3.8 million** for 1,010 workers of the **Cora hypermarkets, Wallonia** (605-56-7); **Spain EUR 2.7 million** for 400 automotive workers in **Galicia** (608-55-7); **Finland EUR 410,310** for 233 **Valmet Automotive** workers (601-57-9). Procedures **2026/0195(BUD), 2026/0197(BUD), 2026/0220(BUD)** (which belongs to which country was not checked). Committee BUDG.
- **MFF 2028-2034: a DEBATE on Wednesday morning, NO vote.** Co-rapporteur **Siegfried Mureşan (EPP)**: "any cuts to the budget ... will not be a basis for the consent of the Parliament"; the Commission proposed **1.15% of GNI** against **1.14%** for 2021-2027; national budgets grew **68%** in seven years. Co-rapporteur **Carla Tavares (S&D)**: "we are no longer dealing with temporary crises, but with structural challenges". Parliament's consent is required for the MFF regulation. See `mff_2028_2034`.

## FAQ
**Did Parliament vote on the 2028-2034 EU budget on 7 October?** No. It was a debate with the Commission and the Council. Parliament's formal consent comes at the end of the negotiation.
**Is the EU-China text binding?** No. It is a recommendation addressed to the other institutions; it sets Parliament's position and asks for action, but only the Commission and Council can act on it.
**Where is the Commission's China policy decided?** In the Commission (trade, investment screening, trade defence) and the Council; Parliament's role is its resolutions, its consent to agreements and its legislative files. See `eu_china_relations`.
