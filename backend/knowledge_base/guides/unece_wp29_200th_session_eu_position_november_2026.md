# UNECE WP.29 200th session, 17-20 November 2026: the Commission's proposed EU position (COM(2026) 548)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Proposal:** **COM(2026) 548 final of 13 October 2026**, a Council Decision under **Article 218(9) TFEU** setting the position the EU takes in the **World Forum for Harmonization of Vehicle Regulations (WP.29)** of the UN Economic Commission for Europe on the proposals to be voted at its **200th session, 17 to 20 November 2026** (procedure 2026/0312(NLE)). It is a proposal: the Council has still to adopt it.
- **How WP.29 works:** it meets three times a year (March, June, November). Under the **Revised 1958 Agreement** (EU party since 24 March 1998) proposals pass by **qualified majority of the contracting parties present and voting**; under the **Parallel Agreement** (global technical regulations, EU party since 15 February 2000) by **consensus**. Unless a blocking minority objects within six months, an adopted UN regulation enters into force; in the EU, **publication in the Official Journal completes the transposition**. The EU has exclusive competence (Article 3(2) TFEU).
- **What may be voted:** amendments to **UN Regulations Nos 0, 46, 49, 79, 90, 95, 96, 127, 129, 148, 149, 152, 153, 157, 168, 169, 171, 174, 176 and 178**; **UN GTR Nos 20, 21, 22 and 25**; and UN resolutions **R.E.5** (light source categories) and **M.R.3** (vehicle interior air quality).
- **Topics named in the proposal (selection):** international whole vehicle type approval (IWVTA) requirements; indirect vision (R46); engine emissions measurement clarifications (R49); steering effort tests (R79); **dual-fuel hydrogen engines in tractors and non-road mobile machinery** (R96); pedestrian protection test procedures; child restraints; road illumination, including **adaptive driving beam for L3 vehicles**; advanced emergency braking for M1 and N1 vehicles; **automated lane keeping systems** (reporting and a new Annex 7 on data exchange through DETA, the database for the exchange of type approval documentation); real driving emissions for automated vehicles; event data recorders for heavy-duty vehicles of 8 to 12 tonnes; driver control assistance systems; safety-belt reminders; emergency lane keeping. **GTR 20** adds thermal propagation requirements for batteries, **GTR 21** a system bench method for power determination, **GTR 22** conditions for the virtual distance test, **GTR 25** better test descriptions.
- **Why it matters in EU law:** EU type-approval law (Regulation (EU) 2018/858 and the General Safety Regulation (EU) 2019/2144) incorporates UN regulations; the Commission notes the system let Regulation (EC) No 661/2009 repeal more than 50 EU directives.
