# United States' 2026 National Defence Strategy -- EU-US Transatlantic Implications

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: US 2026 National Defence Strategy (NDS) -- Trump administration defence doctrine, implications for NATO, EU defence policy, transatlantic burden-sharing
- EPRS Briefing (20 April 2026): "The United States' 2026 National Defence Strategy" -- EPRS analysis of NDS priorities, budget implications, pivot to Indo-Pacific, impact on European security. Ref: EPRS_UNK_db744ca4564c
- US document status: 2026 NDS released by US DoD, classified summary; unclassified version published April 2026
- Key themes (per EPRS): prioritisation of Indo-Pacific theatre over Europe; reduced US force posture in Europe (~80,000 troops down from ~100,000); conditional burden-sharing with NATO allies; China as pacing threat; Russia as acute threat but reduced US lead
- Implications for EU: accelerated Strategic Compass implementation; SAFE loans (EUR 150B) and EDIP scale-up; renewed push for European Defence Union (AFCO institutional report 2025/2212(INI))
- Predecessor: 2022 Biden administration NDS -- emphasised "integrated deterrence" with allies
- Context: Trump-Rutte confrontation (9 April 2026) over NATO burden-sharing; UK public sentiment toward Trump's US plunging amid Iran war

## Key NDS 2026 Priorities (per EPRS)

1. **Indo-Pacific primacy**: China designated as "pacing threat"; US force redeployment from Europe/ME to Pacific
2. **Conditional alliance architecture**: NATO Article 5 reaffirmed but with explicit burden-sharing demands (3% GDP target for European NATO members by 2030)
3. **Nuclear modernisation**: accelerated Sentinel ICBM programme, B-21 Raider deployment, possible tactical nuclear re-posturing
4. **AI + autonomous systems**: Replicator Initiative 2.0, focus on "attritable" autonomous systems
5. **Space + cyber**: expanded Space Force, CYBERCOM elevated to unified combatant command
6. **Reduced European footprint**: conditional drawdown of US forces in Germany, Poland, Romania unless European allies accept burden-sharing terms
7. **Middle East**: continued support for Israel post-Iran ceasefire; reduced ground presence; reliance on carrier strike groups + forward basing (Bahrain, UAE)

## Implications for EU Defence Policy

- **Strategic autonomy push**: likely acceleration of:
  - European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP, Regulation (EU) 2025/2643)
  - SAFE (EUR 150B loans for defence production)
  - Common European Defence Union (AFCO 2025/2212(INI) institutional report)
  - Joint procurement (EDF next cycle)
- **Council**: expected special Defence Council configuration discussions; Kubilius (Defence) more prominent
- **EP**: SEDE subcommittee focus; draft reports on CSDP + PESCO strengthening
- **Member states**: divergence between Atlanticists (Poland, Baltics) and autonomists (France, Germany) accentuated
- **Nuclear**: French/UK nuclear umbrella debate revived; German nuclear-sharing review possible
- **Industrial**: ASAP (Act in Support of Ammunition Production) successor proposals; Ukraine support continuation
