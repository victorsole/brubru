## What the Talent Pool does

The EU Talent Pool is an **EU-level IT platform** that matches **third-country jobseekers** with EU employers' **shortage occupation** vacancies. Key features:

1. **Voluntary participation** — Member States opt in to use the Pool; employers post vacancies; third-country jobseekers register profiles. No Member State is obliged to recruit from the Pool.
2. **EU shortage occupations list** — the Pool covers occupations that have been flagged as EU-wide shortage occupations (informed by Eurostat + EU Labour Market Observatory data). The shortage list is reviewed periodically.
3. **Talent Partnerships integration** — the Pool acts as the operational layer for the Commission's Talent Partnerships with third countries (Egypt, Tunisia, Morocco, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Kenya, ...).
4. **Single Permit Directive alignment** — the Pool does NOT create a new visa category; successful matches still go through national single-permit procedures (Directive (EU) 2024/1233 recast).
5. **Cost layer** — the Pool is operated by the European Labour Authority (ELA) on behalf of the Commission; recruitment costs sit with the employer; jobseeker registration is free.

## Why it matters

- **Demographic-decline counter-measure**: Eurostat EUROPOP 2026 projects EU-27 population to drop 11.7% by 2100 (from ~450m to ~397m). Legal migration corridors are part of the policy mix to maintain labour-force capacity.
- **Pact on Migration and Asylum complement**: the Talent Pool sits inside the legal-migration arm of the broader Pact framework — managed third-country recruitment reduces pressure on irregular-migration + asylum-procedure channels.
- **Single Market / Competitiveness**: the Draghi report (September 2024) explicitly called for accelerated legal-migration corridors for shortage occupations as a competitiveness tool; the Talent Pool delivers on that.

## Compliance implications (Brubru EU Law Comply candidate)

For HR / recruitment / migration-law functions:

1. **Employer onboarding** — companies wanting to access the Pool must register with their national contact point; data-protection regime (GDPR + Talent Pool implementing acts) applies.
2. **Job-vacancy criteria** — vacancies posted on the Pool must match the EU shortage occupations list; vacancies in non-shortage occupations are not eligible.
3. **National single-permit alignment** — successful matches still require national work permits; the Pool does not bypass national immigration procedures.
4. **Fair-recruitment safeguards** — the Regulation imposes obligations on employers to comply with national labour standards + collective agreements + minimum-wage rules; non-compliance can lead to exclusion from the Pool.

## Brubru user questions addressed

- "Has the EU Talent Pool been adopted?" → Yes, on 29 April 2026; published in OJ as Regulation (EU) 2026/1047 on 11 May 2026 (CELEX 32026R1047).
- "When can I start using it?" → The IT platform is run by ELA; operational launch is expected 2027-2028 after secondary legislation + technical implementation. Member State opt-in is the gating step.
- "Is it the same as a Blue Card?" → No. The Talent Pool is a matching platform, not a visa category. Successful matches still require a national work permit, typically routed through the Single Permit Directive (Directive (EU) 2024/1233 recast) or the Blue Card Directive (Directive (EU) 2021/1883) for highly-skilled workers.
- "Which occupations qualify?" → Only EU shortage occupations on the periodically-reviewed list (informed by Eurostat + EU Labour Market Observatory data).
- "Do employers pay?" → Yes, recruitment costs sit with the employer; jobseeker registration is free.
