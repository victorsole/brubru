# AgRI 2040 area 7 of 8: stronger agri-food value chain

## QUICK FACTS
- **Place in the strategy:** seventh of the **eight** preliminary areas of action of AgRI 2040 (COM(2026) 453, section 3.3). Preliminary, no obligation. It is framed as part of the shared **fairness** priority of AgRI 2040 and Food 2040.
- **Purpose:** new knowledge and tools to **strengthen farmers' position in the value chain** and to address **cost pressures** and sustainable transitions by collaborating across the chain.
- **Local and short chains:** promote **on-farm and local food processing** and commercialisation; improve the governance of rural food systems; reinforce **urban-rural linkages**; support innovations for farmers in **short, local and regional supply chains**.
- **Business models:** new sustainable business models; **digitalisation and decarbonisation** of the chain.
- **Quality schemes:** R&I should support **EU quality schemes, including organic production and geographical indications**, and the processing and marketing of organic products.
- **Related research:** projects on geographical indications and organic value chains (GI SMART, THEROS, ALLIANCE, Strength2Food) and short supply chains (COACH, FOODRUS, FAIRCHAIN, SMARTCHAIN) in SWD(2026) 271.
