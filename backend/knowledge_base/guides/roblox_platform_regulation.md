## CORE REGULATORY STACK — VLOP watch + child safety
- **Digital Services Act** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — **VLOP designation imminent** per industry analysis. Art 28 minor protection critical (Roblox's core audience is under-13).
- **CSAM Regulation** (COM(2022) 209) — extreme exposure: voice chat, DMs, real-time UGC with minors.
- **CSAM Temporary Derogation** (Reg 2021/1232, CELEX 32021R1232) — **EXPIRED 3 Apr 2026**.
- **Combating Sexual Abuse Directive** (Dir 2011/92, CELEX 32011L0092; recast COM(2024) 60 in trilogue).
- **GDPR Art 8** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679) — under-13 consent thresholds; parental controls; Roblox's age-gating pre-dates EU requirements in US context.
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — moderation AI, Roblox Assistant (generative AI for creators), Muting AI; GPAI deployment disclosures.
- **Product Liability Directive recast** (Dir 2024/2853) — software product liability; Roblox Studio + platform.
- **Digital Content Directive** (Dir 2019/770, CELEX 32019L0770) — Robux + Premium subscription.
- **Copyright in DSM Art 17** (Dir 2019/790, CELEX 32019L0790) — OCSSP: user-created games + assets.
- **AVMSD** (Dir 2018/1808, CELEX 32018L1808) — Roblox Video, livestreams = VSP elements.
- **P2B Regulation** (Reg 2019/1150, CELEX 32019R1150) — creator economy (Devex payout model).
- **Digital Fairness Act** — loot boxes / Robux monetisation / whale-farming of minors.
- **NIS2** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555).
- **Cyber Resilience Act** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847).
- **TERREG** (Reg 2021/784, CELEX 32021R0784).
- **Representative Actions** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828).
- **European Accessibility Act** (Dir 2019/882, CELEX 32019L0882).
- **eIDAS 2** (Reg 2024/1183, CELEX 32024R1183) — EUDI Wallet age-attribute for under-13 + 13-17 tiers.
