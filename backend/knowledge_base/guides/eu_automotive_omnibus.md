# EU Automotive Omnibus (Automotive Industrial Action Plan)

## QUICK FACTS
- Initiative: EU Automotive Omnibus, part of the Automotive Industrial Action Plan (AIAP)
- Commission Communication: "An Industrial Action Plan for the European Automotive Sector" COM(2025)95, published 5 March 2025
- Omnibus proposal: published March 2026 (package simplifying existing automotive legislation)
- Lead DG: DG GROW; supporting DG CLIMA, DG MOVE, DG ENER
- Responsible Commissioner: Stephane Sejourne (EVP for Prosperity and Industrial Strategy), with Apostolos Tzitzikostas (Transport, Greece), Wopke Hoekstra (Climate, Netherlands)
- EP committees: ITRE (lead), TRAN and ENVI opinions
- Objective: restore competitiveness of EU automotive sector (13 million direct/indirect jobs, 7% EU GDP) against Chinese EV competition and high energy costs
- Legal basis: Article 114 TFEU (internal market); Article 192 TFEU (environment) for climate-related parts
- EPRS Briefing (14 April 2026): "EU automotive omnibus [EU Legislation in Progress]" -- Ref: EPRS_BLOG_eu-automotive-omnibus
- EPRS Briefing (13 April 2026): "EU automotive omnibus - 13-04-2026" -- Ref: EPRS_UNK_e49bf37302be
- **NEW IN OJ (7 May 2026)**: **Regulation (EU) 2026/1046** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2026 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 -- as regards the calculation of emission credits for heavy-duty vehicles. CELEX: 32026R1046. Published OJ:L_202601046, 7 May 2026. Companion file to the CO2 Standards Flexibility component for HDVs.

## Key Components of the Omnibus

### 1. CO2 Standards Flexibility (Regulation (EU) 2019/631)
- Amendment allowing manufacturers to pool compliance across 2025-2027 (3-year averaging)
- Preserves 2035 zero-emission target for new cars and vans
- Grants credit for plug-in hybrids meeting electric-range criteria
- Source regulation CELEX: 32019R0631

### 2. Battery Value Chain Support
- Aligns with Battery Booster Strategy
- Accelerated permitting for battery gigafactories under Industrial Accelerator Act (COM(2026)100)
- "Made in EU" criteria for battery cells (min 35% EU content by 2028)

### 3. EU Charging Infrastructure
- Reinforces AFIR (Regulation (EU) 2023/1804, CELEX: 32023R1804) deployment
- Mandatory heavy-duty charging corridors (TEN-T core network by 2030)
- Universal connector standards

### 4. Supply Chain and Critical Raw Materials
- Links to Critical Raw Materials Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1252)
- Preferential access to EU Critical Raw Materials Demand Aggregation Platform (launched 13 April 2026)
- Restrictions on sensitive battery IP transfer to third countries

### 5. Type Approval Simplification
- Amendments to Regulation (EU) 2018/858 (Type Approval Framework, CELEX: 32018R0858)
- Digital type approval certificates, EU Business Wallet integration
- Faster roadworthiness review cycles aligned with 2025/0096(COD) Roadworthiness package

### 6. Workforce and Just Transition
- European Social Fund Plus (ESF+) flexibility for automotive clusters
- Reskilling pact with SMEs in Tier 2/Tier 3 suppliers (Germany, Italy, France, Spain, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary)
- Temporary State Aid framework for restructuring (consultation launched 13 April 2026 by Commission)

## Political and Industry Context

- Industry calls (ACEA, CLEPA, ANFIA, VDA): dilution of 2035 ban, extended hybrid window
- Environmental NGOs (T&E, Climate Action Network): preserve 2035, reject rollback
- Member States: Germany, Italy, Czechia push flexibility; France, Netherlands, Nordic countries defend climate targets
- Political groups: EPP (pro-industry), S&D (split), Renew (conditional), Greens/Left (opposed to rollback)

## Brubru User Implications
