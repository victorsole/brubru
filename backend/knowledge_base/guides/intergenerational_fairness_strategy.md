## Overview

On 5 March 2026, the European Commission presented the first-ever **Strategy on Intergenerational Fairness**, aiming to ensure a fair balance of benefits and burdens across current and future generations. The strategy promotes long-term thinking in governance and connects to the Commission's broader agenda on demographic change, youth policy, and social rights.

The strategy was presented jointly by EVP Roxana Minzatu and Commissioner Glenn Micallef alongside the Gender Equality Strategy 2026-2030 at the same College of Commissioners meeting.

## Four Key Initiatives

### 1. Intergenerational Fairness Index
- A new measurement tool to assess how effectively nations protect younger and future generations' interests
- Helps identify policy gaps across Member States
- Covers economic, environmental, social, and fiscal dimensions
- Comparable across Member States and over time

### 2. Future Literacy Toolkit
- Policymaking resources for public administrations throughout EU Member States
- Foresight tools and methodologies for long-term policy design
- Training programmes for civil servants on intergenerational impact assessment
- Linked to the Commission's Better Regulation agenda

### 3. Demography Forum
- Platform for exchanging insights on demographic trends across Europe
- Brings together policymakers, researchers, and civil society
- Addresses ageing, fertility decline, migration, and regional disparities
- Annual event with thematic focus areas

### 4. Voices of the Future Initiative
- Programme inviting local and regional authorities to participate in shaping regional development strategies
- Youth participation mechanisms in policy design
- Links to the EU Youth Dialogue and European Youth Forum
- Connects to the Committee of the Regions' youth engagement work

## Policy Context

### Demographic Challenges in the EU
- EU population projected to decline from 2026 onwards (Eurostat)
- Old-age dependency ratio rising: from 34% (2024) to projected 57% by 2050
- Pension and healthcare systems under increasing pressure
- Youth unemployment persistently higher than adult rates in most Member States
- Housing affordability crisis affecting younger generations disproportionately
