# EU Staff Categories and Grades

## QUICK FACTS
Legal basis: Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Union, CELEX: 01962R0031-20240101
Last major reform: 2014 (Regulation No 1023/2013), CELEX: 32013R1023
Institutions covered: All EU institutions, bodies, offices and agencies
Recruitment body: EPSO (European Personnel Selection Office)

## Staff Categories

The EU institutions employ staff in three main categories, each with its own grade structure and career path.

### AD (Administrator) - Grades AD 5 to AD 16

Policy-making, legislative drafting, analysis, management. Requires a university degree (at minimum a bachelor's).

|-------|-------------|---------------------------|

| AD 6 | Administrator (2-3 years experience) | 5,565 |
| AD 7 | Senior administrator / team coordinator | 6,302 |
| AD 8 | Senior administrator / Deputy HoU | 7,136 |
| AD 9 | Head of Unit / Senior expert | 8,079 |
| AD 10 | Head of Unit | 9,147 |
| AD 11 | Head of Unit / Senior adviser | 10,357 |
| AD 12 | Adviser / Senior Head of Unit | 11,727 |
| AD 13 | Director | 13,278 |
| AD 14 | Director / Director-General | 15,033 |
| AD 15 | Director-General | 16,602 |
| AD 16 | Director-General (exceptional) | 17,697 |

Each grade has 5 steps with automatic step increases every 2 years.

### AST (Assistant) - Grades AST 1 to AST 11

Executive, technical and administrative support tasks. Requires post-secondary education or secondary education + 3 years experience.

| Grade | Typical Role | Monthly Base Salary (2024) |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|
| AST 1 | Entry-level assistant | 3,271 |
| AST 2 | Junior assistant | 3,607 |
| AST 3 | Assistant (standard EPSO entry level) | 3,977 |
| AST 4 | Experienced assistant | 4,384 |
| AST 5 | Senior assistant | 4,832 |
| AST 6 | Senior assistant | 5,326 |
| AST 7 | Principal assistant | 5,871 |
| AST 8 | Principal assistant | 6,472 |
| AST 9 | Chief assistant | 7,134 |
| AST 10 | Chief assistant | 7,864 |
| AST 11 | Chief assistant (highest) | 8,670 |

**Key differences between AST 1, AST 2 and AST 3:**
- AST 1: Entry-level, typically internal competitions or exceptional recruitment. Basic executive/clerical tasks. Monthly base: 3,271 EUR.
- AST 2: Junior assistant level, slightly more autonomous work. Monthly base: 3,607 EUR.
- AST 3: Standard EPSO competition entry level for assistants. Most external AST competitions recruit at AST 3. Requires post-secondary diploma or secondary education + 3 years relevant experience. Monthly base: 3,977 EUR.

## Other Staff Types

| Type | Description | Legal Basis |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Contract Agents (CA) | Fixed-term, grades FG I to FG IV | CEOS Title IV |
| Temporary Agents (TA) | Fixed-term, same AD/AST grades | CEOS Title II |
| Seconded National Experts (SNE) | From Member State administrations, 2-4 years | Council Decision |
| Trainees (Blue Book) | 5-month paid traineeships, twice yearly | Institution rules |
| Interim staff | Via agencies, short-term | Framework contracts |

## EPSO Competitions

EPSO organises competitions to recruit permanent officials (AD and AST):
- **AD 5 competitions**: University graduates, entry-level administrators
- **AST 3 competitions**: Post-secondary diploma holders, entry-level assistants
- **AD 7 competitions**: Specialists with professional experience (lawyers, economists, IT)
- **CAST (Contract Agents)**: Permanent open calls for FG I-IV, no competition needed

## Allowances and Benefits

On top of base salary, EU officials receive:
- Expatriation allowance: 16% of base salary (if working outside home country)
- Household allowance: 2% of base salary
- Dependent child allowance: 418 EUR/month per child
- Education allowance: Up to 282 EUR/month per child
- EU tax (internal): 8-45% progressive (no national income tax on EU salary)
- Pension: 2% per year of service, max 70% of final basic salary after 35 years
