# Horizon Europe: extra EUR 500 million to the end of 2027 (Nature and Bridging calls, autumn 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What:** on **29 September 2026** the Commission allocated an **additional EUR 500 million to Horizon Europe until the end of 2027**, for **two new cross-cutting calls**: protecting biodiversity ("Transformative change towards a nature-positive economy and society", the **Nature** actions) and strengthening EU competitiveness and bringing research results to market (the **Bridging** actions). Source: European Research Executive Agency (REA) news, 9 October 2026.
- **Who manages it:** REA manages **around half** of the EUR 500 million. The other half is managed elsewhere in the Commission; its topics are **not covered here**.
- **REA's topics and budgets (REA's figures):**
  - Nature action 1: **EUR 41.45 million**, three topics (incl. effects of PFAS on biodiversity), opens **13 October 2026**.
  - Nature action 2: **EUR 108 million**, five topics (invertebrate decline, nature-restoration labs, wildlife trafficking, invasive species, biodiversity monitoring), opens **13 October 2026**.
  - Bridging, advancing Pillar II results: **EUR 32.5 million**, opens **20 October 2026**; proposals must build on at least one completed multi-beneficiary Horizon Europe Pillar II project (Clusters 1-6).
  - Bridging, valorising results via innovation ecosystems: **EUR 20 million**, opens **20 October 2026**.
  - Research-infrastructure Bridging topics: **EUR 30.5 million**, three topics, opening **9 March** (2027).
- **Events:** info day **16 October 2026**; brokerage event **19 October 2026**.
- **Why "Bridging":** these topics prepare the ground for the next programme, **Horizon Europe 2028-2034**, and its links with the future **European Competitiveness Fund**.
- **Not read:** the call documents and deadlines on the Funding and Tenders Portal; the topics managed outside REA.
