# EU Customs Reform

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Thursday 2 July 2026 — UCC Implementing Act amended to streamline non-preferential origin)**: The Commission **adopted amendments to the Union Customs Code Implementing Act** (UCC Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2447) to **streamline the application of non-preferential origin rules**. Non-preferential origin determines the "economic nationality" of goods for purposes such as trade-defence measures (anti-dumping/countervailing duties), quantitative restrictions, origin marking, and trade statistics — so the simplification reduces administrative friction for traders determining origin under the residual rules. This is a technical maintenance amendment to the existing UCC framework (Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 + Implementing Regulation 2015/2447), distinct from both the EUR 3 small-parcels duty and the broader Customs Reform proposal below. Source: EC News (taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu / ec.europa.eu), 2 July 2026.
- **LATEST (Wednesday 1 July 2026 — the EUR 3 duty ENTERS INTO APPLICATION)**: From **1 July 2026** the EU **abolishes the outdated EUR 150 customs-duty exemption** for e-commerce packages and applies a **temporary flat EUR 3 customs duty per item** on low-value consignments (intrinsic value up to EUR 150) imported from outside the EU. The measure applies **until 1 July 2028**, bridging to the full Customs Reform's permanent abolition of the de-minimis relief once the reform is adopted. DG TAXUD published implementation guidance + the legal text on entry into application. Framing (per DG TAXUD): "increase fairness for EU businesses and safety for consumers" (the Shein/Temu low-value-parcel context). This is the entry-into-application of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1200 (below), not a new instrument. Source: taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu newsroom, 1 July 2026.
- **PREVIOUS (Friday 5 June 2026)**: **Temporary EUR 3 customs duty on low-value imported parcels** — Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1200 (CELEX: 32026R1200), published 5 June 2026, amends UCC Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2447 to set the rules implementing a temporary EUR 3 customs duty on distance sales of imported goods in a consignment with an intrinsic value not exceeding EUR 150. This is the interim e-commerce measure (the Shein/Temu low-value-parcels context) that bridges to the full Customs Reform's planned abolition of the EUR 150 de minimis duty relief. ## Overview

The EU Customs Reform proposes the most comprehensive overhaul of EU customs since the creation of the customs union in 1968. It replaces the Union Customs Code (UCC) framework with a modernised system centred on data-driven risk management, a new EU Customs Authority, and a single digital interface (EU Customs Data Hub).

## Key Elements

### EU Customs Authority
- New EU agency replacing the current coordination model
- Centralised risk management for all goods entering the EU
- Direct operational role in customs supervision

### EU Customs Data Hub
- Single digital window for all customs declarations
- Real-time data sharing between customs authorities, traders, and carriers
- Integration with existing systems (TARIC, NCTS, ICS2)
- Mandatory use by 2032

### E-commerce and Low-Value Consignments
- Abolition of EUR 150 customs duty exemption for e-commerce
- Online platforms (deemed importers) responsible for customs duties on goods sold to EU consumers
- Addresses unfair advantage of non-EU e-commerce platforms (Temu, Shein, AliExpress)

### Simplified Procedures
- Trust and Check traders: most trusted operators process goods with minimal intervention
- Streamlined procedures for SMEs
- Risk-based approach replacing blanket controls
