# Court of Justice, 5 November 2026: Nord Stream 2 loses its appeal against the 2019 gas directive amendment (C-118/25 P)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Judgment of Thursday 5 November 2026, Case C-118/25 P, Nord Stream 2 AG v Parliament and Council (CURIA press release 160/26). The Court of Justice DISMISSES the appeal and upholds the General Court's judgment of 27 November 2024. The 2019 rules therefore REMAIN in force.** Final; no further appeal.
- **What was challenged:** **Directive (EU) 2019/692**, which in 2019 extended the EU gas market rules (unbundling of supply from transmission, third-party access, transparency) to pipelines connecting a Member State with a third country, including sections in a Member State's territorial sea. It lets Member States grant derogations only for pipelines **completed before 23 May 2019**; Nord Stream 2 (Russia to Germany under the Baltic Sea) was not.
- **Procedural history:** the General Court first declared the action inadmissible; the Court of Justice set that aside on **12 July 2022** and found it admissible only as regards the derogation provision; the General Court then dismissed it on the merits (27 November 2024); this judgment ends the case.
- **Why (the Court's reasons, from the press release):** its 2022 judgment did not oblige the General Court to examine a difference in treatment with other pipeline operators; and the EU institutions never gave Nord Stream 2 "precise, unconditional and consistent" assurances that the gas rules would not apply to it, so its **legitimate expectations** argument fails (there had been indications for years that the rules would apply).
- **What this does NOT decide:** sanctions, gas imports from Russia or the pipeline's operation; only the validity of the 2019 derogation rule as challenged.
