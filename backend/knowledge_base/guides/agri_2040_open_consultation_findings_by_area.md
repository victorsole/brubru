# AgRI 2040 open consultation: findings by area (what 701 respondents said)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** DG AGRI summary of the open consultation (15 December 2025 to 25 January 2026, 701 replies), sections 3.1 to 3.5. Not an official Commission position.
- **1. R&I as enabler of the Vision for agriculture and food:** the four objectives of the **2016 strategic approach** (long-term food security; environmental sustainability and resilience of land-based primary production; sustainable growth of rural territories; better delivery of research results for policymaking) still highly or somewhat relevant for **up to 92%**. **Up to 94%** back extending the scope to: attracting new generations, competitiveness of small and medium farms, sustainability, agriculture in harmony with nature, **biotechnology and AI**, uptake, new business models, bioeconomy, circularity and nature-based solutions, local experimentation sites, advisory services, consumer needs. Open answers (**89 respondents, 12.7%**): regional disparities, citizen science, community-led initiatives, food sovereignty, the value chain beyond the farm.
- **2. Startups, scaleups and SMEs:** only **31%** say support for a seamless investment journey is adequate. Proposed actions (proof-of-concept support; better coordinated EU support; synergies between **Horizon Europe, the future European Competitiveness Fund and National and Regional Partnership Plans**; more use of EU instruments for startups; results feeding them; more company participation): **up to 82%** agree. Open answers (**42, 6%**): valorisation as a design feature, **EU funding directly to farmers** as co-owners, long-term pilots and multi-year on-farm experiments.
- **3. Uptake:** only **up to 36%** agree dissemination and use of results work today. Options (multi-actor approach, **cascading grants**, **Living Labs**, links to **EIP-AGRI Operational Groups**, digital dissemination, **on-farm demonstrations**, peer-to-peer, links to **AKIS** and Horizon thematic networks, **AI-accessible inventories** of practices): **up to 88%** agree, roughly 70-85% per measure, highest for on-farm demonstrations. Open answers (**248, 35.4%**): incentives for **early adopters** (CAP instruments, risk-sharing, outcome-based payments), education, real-life engagement, alignment with territorial needs.
- **4. Cooperation:** public-public alignment (EU, Member States, associated countries), public-private (business and philanthropy), international organisations and third countries, cross-disciplinary work (One Health, bioeconomy, biotech, blue economy): **up to 88%** agree. Open answers (**181, 25.8%**): researcher mobility, shared infrastructures, **data interoperability**, smaller countries, mission-oriented partnerships.
- **5. Thematic priorities:** the **13 priority areas** of the 2023 AgriResearch Conference were confirmed as relevant by nearly all. Extra topics (**248, 35.4%**): **carbon farming, circular agri-food, agroforestry, plant breeding**, labour, generational change, training.
- **Use:** the Communication and SWD(2026) 271 cite the 94%, 82% and 88% figures; the eight preliminary AgRI 2040 areas list the consultation among their inputs.
