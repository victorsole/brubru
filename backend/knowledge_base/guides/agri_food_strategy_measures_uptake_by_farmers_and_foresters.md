# Strategy measures: accelerating uptake of knowledge and innovation by farmers, foresters and rural actors

## QUICK FACTS
- **Source:** COM(2026) 453 final, section 2.2. Intentions, not obligations. CAP instruments can stimulate demand for innovation (investment support for new technologies, sustainable practices, digital tools).
- **Three dimensions of scaling:** **scaling out** (proven solutions to more users and places), **scaling up** (policies and regulations that support uptake), **scaling deep** (new practices, values and beliefs).
- **Multi-actor approach and Living Labs:** the multi-actor approach is **mandatory in more than 70% of relevant Horizon calls**; problems remain: inconsistent implementation, late involvement of actors, uneven adoption of results. The Commission runs a **study on the impact of existing instruments**, will **harmonise terminology and methods** and strengthen project-level evaluation and monitoring (action 8, **Q1 2028**), explore **pre-proposal support for consortium-building** and **simplified financial support for fair remuneration of farmers' participation** under Horizon Europe 2028-2034, and explore **collaborative platforms** connecting practitioners to projects (action 9, **Q1 2028**).
- **Bottom-up innovation:** validate **traditional knowledge** and practitioner-led solutions; engage farmers in testing across regions; assess **costs and benefits** of innovations; **map testing and demonstration sites** (EU and national Living Labs, demonstration farms, Lighthouses) (action 12, **Q4 2027**).
- **Horizon Europe and the CAP:** systematic links between national contact points and CAP networks through **EIP-AGRI** events (action 10, **2027**); Operational Groups to join R&I projects and spread results through **EU Thematic Knowledge Hubs** (Horizon Europe Cluster 6 work programme 2026-2027); stronger Living Lab and Operational Group synergies; collaboration with **agrifoodTEF** testing facilities.
- **Practical knowledge:** knowledge repositories and interoperable infrastructures; **revised EIP-AGRI practice abstracts** (action 11, **Q4 2027**); peer-reviewed practitioner handbooks; digital tools and **AI** for dissemination.
- **Skills:** training material from thematic and advisory networks into national AKIS programmes; links between advisers and universities; vocational and higher education; European Skills Intelligence Observatory, Large-Scale Partnership for the agri-food ecosystem, Erasmus+ Centres of Vocational Excellence, EIT Knowledge and Innovation Communities; entrepreneurs, regional authorities, civil society and **citizen science**.
