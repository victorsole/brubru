# EU Demographic Change and Population Projections

## QUICK FACTS
- Topic: EU demographic trends -- population decline, ageing, migration, fertility, policy response
- Latest Eurostat projection (April 2026): **EU population projected to drop by 11.7% by 2100** (from ~450 million in 2026 to ~397 million by 2100)
- Eurostat publication (16 April 2026): EUROPOP 2026 projection release; European Statistical Monitor -- April edition
- Current EU-27 population (2026): ~450 million
- Peak year: EU population already peaked around 2022-2023
- Main drivers: low fertility (EU average 1.45 children/woman vs 2.1 replacement), ageing, rising mortality, migration balance
- Working-age population (20-64): projected to fall from ~266 million (2026) to ~207 million by 2070 (-22%)
- Old-age dependency ratio: from 34% (2026) to 58% (2070) -- from 3 workers per retiree to 1.7
- Lead Commissioner: Glenn Micallef (Intergenerational Fairness, Youth, Culture and Sport) + Roxana Minzatu (Social Rights, Skills, Quality Jobs, Preparedness) + Dubravka Suica (Mediterranean)
- Lead DG: DG EMPL (social/employment), Eurostat (statistics), DG JUST (family and youth), JRC (research)
- Key policy documents: Demography Toolbox (COM(2023) 577), Intergenerational Fairness Strategy (expected 2026), New Demographic Deal (Council June 2023)

## Eurostat April 2026 Projections (EUROPOP 2026)

Published 16 April 2026 as part of the European Statistical Monitor April edition:

| Metric | 2026 | 2050 | 2075 | 2100 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| **Total EU-27 population** | 450 M | 445 M | 425 M | 397 M |
| **Change vs 2026** | 0% | -1.1% | -5.6% | -11.7% |
| **Working-age (20-64)** | 266 M | 244 M | 225 M | 210 M |
| **Old-age (65+)** | 100 M | 130 M | 140 M | 135 M |
| **Old-age dependency ratio** | 34% | 56% | 63% | 68% |
| **Total fertility rate** | 1.45 | 1.50 | 1.55 | 1.60 |

**Country-level variation**:
- **Largest projected declines**: Bulgaria (-22%), Lithuania (-19%), Romania (-18%), Croatia (-17%), Greece (-16%)
- **Population growth or stability**: Luxembourg (+15%), Malta (+8%), Ireland (+5%), Cyprus (+4%), Sweden (+2%)
- **Germany**: -7%; France: -3%; Italy: -13%; Spain: -6%; Poland: -15%

## Drivers of Decline

### Low Fertility
- EU average Total Fertility Rate (TFR): 1.45 (2026), lowest in Italy (1.23), Spain (1.19), Poland (1.29)
- Above-EU average: France (1.80), Ireland (1.75), Sweden (1.67)
- Fertility stagnation since 2008 global financial crisis; accelerated decline post-COVID

### Rising Mortality
- Life expectancy has plateaued since 2019 (COVID impact still measurable)
- EU life expectancy at birth: 81.5 years (2026), down from peak of 81.7 in 2019
- Gender gap: women 84.2, men 78.8

### Migration
- Net migration to EU: ~+1.8 million/year (2021-2025 average)
- Only offsetting ~50% of natural decrease
- Geographic distribution uneven: concentrated in Germany, France, Spain; East-to-West brain drain within EU

### Depopulation of Rural Areas
- 40% of EU regions classified as "shrinking" (losing population faster than national average)
- Rural depopulation: Bulgaria, Romania, Southern Italy, rural France, inner Spain ("Vaciada España")
- Urban concentration accelerating

## Policy Response Framework

### 1. Demography Toolbox (COM(2023) 577)
Published 11 October 2023. Four pillars:

1. **Supporting parents** -- work-life balance, childcare (Barcelona 2030 targets: 50% under-3s, 96% 3+ in formal care)
2. **Empowering younger generations** -- youth employment, education, ALMA programme
3. **Empowering older generations** -- longer working lives, digital inclusion
4. **Managing migration responsibly** -- legal pathways, integration

### 2. Intergenerational Fairness Strategy (expected 2026)
Commissioner Micallef's flagship. Elements:
- Youth Guarantee review
- Pension system stress-testing
- Inter-generational equity indicators
- See `intergenerational_fairness_strategy` guide

### 3. New Demographic Deal (June 2023 Council Conclusions)
Council committed to:
- Factor demographic change into all EU policies
- Enhance regional and local responses
- Strengthen Eurostat monitoring (EUROPOP updates)

### 4. Specific Instruments
- **Cohesion Policy**: EUR 387 billion (2021-2027); depopulation indicators in Common Provisions Regulation
- **ESF+**: EUR 99 billion, 30% targeted at social inclusion including youth / older workers
- **Erasmus+**: EUR 26.2 billion, mobility and youth
- **EU Care Strategy** (COM(2022) 440): Barcelona childcare targets; long-term care guidance
- **Skills Pact**: 10 million trained (April 2026 milestone)
- **Single Market for Elderly**: cross-border pension portability, silver economy

## Economic Impact

### Ageing Report 2024 (Commission + EPC)
- **Public pension expenditure**: from 11.5% of GDP (2022) to 12.8% by 2070 (if current policies maintained)
- **Health care**: from 6.8% to 7.6% of GDP
- **Long-term care**: from 1.7% to 2.9% of GDP
- **Education**: declining from 4.6% to 4.3% (fewer children)
- **Total age-related expenditure**: from 24.4% to 26.9% of GDP by 2070

### Productivity Challenge
- With workforce shrinking 22% by 2070, GDP growth depends critically on:
  - Productivity gains (AI, automation, capital deepening)
  - Labour market participation (women, older workers, migrants)
  - Total hours worked
- **Draghi Report** (September 2024): EUR 750-800 billion/year additional investment needed; demography a key driver

## Pension Systems at Risk

With old-age dependency ratio doubling:
- **Pay-as-you-go schemes** (Germany, France, Italy, Spain): severe pressure; retirement age reforms ongoing (France 64, Germany 67, Italy 67+)
- **Fully funded pillars**: better positioned (Netherlands, Denmark, Sweden)
- **Savings and Investment Union** (2025 initiative): mobilise EU savings for long-term investment

## Migration as Partial Offset

Current Commission approach (under Brunner and von der Leyen):
- **Legal pathways**: EU Talent Pool, Blue Card revision (Dir 2021/1883)
- **Skills matching**: EURES reinforcement, EURES Talent
- **Integration**: Action Plan on Integration and Inclusion 2021-2027
- **Temporary protection**: Ukrainian refugees -- ~4 million extension 2026
- **Return enforcement**: Returns Regulation reform ongoing (COM(2025) 101)

Political contradiction: Commission demography framing says we need more migrants, but political momentum (EUCO, Council) favours tighter migration controls.
