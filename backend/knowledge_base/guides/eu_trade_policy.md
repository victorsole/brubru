# EU Trade Policy

## QUICK FACTS
- Responsible DG: DG TRADE
- Responsible Commissioner: Maros Sefcovic (Commissioner for Trade and Economic Security)
- EP lead committee: INTA (International Trade)
- Council configuration: FAC (Trade) -- Foreign Affairs Council in trade format
- Legal basis: Articles 206-207 TFEU (Common Commercial Policy)
- EU has exclusive competence on trade policy (since Lisbon Treaty)
- Active FTAs: 40+ trade agreements covering 70+ countries
- Key current negotiations: EU-Australia, EU-India, EU-Indonesia, EU-Mercosur, EU-Canada DTA

## Key Trade Agreements

### CETA (EU-Canada)
- **Status:** Provisionally applied since 21 September 2017; full ratification pending all Member State parliaments
- **CELEX:** Council Decision (EU) 2025/703
- **Coverage:** 99% of tariff lines abolished
- **Trade volume (2024):** EUR 75.6bn goods, EUR 48.9bn services
- **EU surplus:** EUR 20bn (goods), EUR 8bn (services)
- **Canada rank:** 12th-largest EU goods trading partner

### EU-Canada Digital Trade Agreement (March 2026)
On 6 March 2026, Commissioner Sefcovic and Canada's Minister for International Trade Maninder Sidhu **launched negotiations** for an EU-Canada Digital Trade Agreement (DTA). This builds on CETA to expand cooperation into the digital economy.

### EU-Mercosur
- Political agreement reached December 2024
- Covers: Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay
- Awaiting legal scrubbing and ratification
- Significant agricultural import implications
