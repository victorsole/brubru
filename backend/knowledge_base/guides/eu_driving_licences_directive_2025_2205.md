# Driving Licences Directive (EU) 2025/2205: mobile licence, accompanied driving at 17, two-year probation

## QUICK FACTS
- **Act:** **Directive (EU) 2025/2205 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2025 on driving licences** (CELEX 32025L2205), published in the OJ on **5 November 2025**, in force on the twentieth day after publication. It amends Regulation (EU) 2018/1724 and Directive (EU) 2022/2561 and **repeals Directive 2006/126/EC and Commission Regulation (EU) No 383/2012 from 26 November 2029**.
- **Dates:** Member States must transpose by **26 November 2028** and apply the measures from **26 November 2029**. Earlier dates: Article 9(2) points (j) and (k) by and from **26 November 2027**; **Article 17 (accompanied driving) by and from 26 November 2028**. The Commission must adopt implementing acts on mobile driving licences by **26 November 2026** (Article 5(7)).
- **Mobile driving licence (Article 5):** Member States **shall issue** mobile (digital) driving licences to the Union specification in Annex I Part C; holders must be able to retrieve them electronically **free of charge**; they are issued **for use with European Digital Identity Wallets as electronic attestations of attributes** (Regulation (EU) No 910/2014); no data beyond Annex I Part D; a verifier must not keep personal data unless law allows. A "driving licence" may be physical, digital or both (Article 2). Detail: `mobile_driving_licence_implementing_regulation_2026_2266`.
- **Accompanied driving (Article 17):** Member States shall issue category B licences marked **Union code 98.02 to applicants aged 17**; holders under 18 drive only with an accompanying person in the front passenger seat, who must be at least **24** and have held the relevant licence for more than five years.
- **Probationary period (Article 18):** every new driver is a "novice driver" for a period set by the Member State of **not less than two years**; stricter alcohol rules for novice drivers.
- **Minimum ages (Article 7):** **16** for AM, A1 and B1; **18** for A2, B, BE, C1 and C1E (plus 17 under accompanied driving); category A motorcycles 20 (two years' experience on A2, waivable from 24); C and CE 18 with a CPC; Member States may adjust some ages.
- **EU driving licence network (Article 22):** Member States use it for mutual assistance, exchange and replacement of licences and verification.
- **Not in the Directive:** no single EU licence issued by Brussels, no EU-wide licence-ban rule in these articles, and no date by which every Member State's mobile licence must be live other than the transposition and application dates above.

## How to answer
For "when does it apply": transposition 26 November 2028, application 26 November 2029; accompanied driving from 17 applies from 26 November 2028. Do not say Member States must already offer mobile licences.
