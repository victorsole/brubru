# European Innovation Act (COM(2026) 567)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Proposed 9 September 2026.** COM(2026) 567 final, procedure **2026/0264 (COD)**. Accompanying documents SEC(2026) 567, SWD(2026) 567, SWD(2026) 568. Press release IP/26/1810.
- **Full title:** Regulation establishing a framework of measures for strengthening the Union innovation ecosystem and **amending Regulation (EU) 2017/1001** (the EU trade mark Regulation, EUIPO's founding act).
- **Legal basis: Article 114 TFEU.** Ordinary legislative procedure. It is a **proposal** — not adopted, not in force, no CELEX for an adopted act, and no application date.
- **REGULATORY SANDBOXES ARE NOT IN THIS ACT.** They are a **separate proposal for a Council Recommendation** presented the same day. The Commission's own wording: "In parallel, the Commission will help innovators … through a specific proposal on regulatory sandboxes." Never describe sandboxes as a pillar, chapter or element of the Innovation Act.
- **Two problems it addresses**, in the Commission's framing: access to finance for innovators, and **R&D procurement**.
- **Three Chapters:**
  - **Chapter I** — subject matter, scope, definitions. Applies to R&D services procurement that falls **outside** Directives 2014/23/EU, 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU.
  - **Chapter II** — common rules for running R&D procurement procedures, **including joint R&D procurement** across Member States.
  - **Chapter III** — tasks given to the **European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO)**: a common framework for **IP valuation**, an **EU-wide marketplace for commercialising IP**, and a **competence centre** helping innovators access **IP-backed finance**.
- **The Commission's figures.** Finance strand: **EUR 10.2 billion per year** in additional financing and about **EUR 35 million** in administrative cost savings. R&D-procurement strand: **EUR 25.92 billion** in additional annual profits for companies and **EUR 1 billion per year** saved by public buyers.
- **The JRC figure.** A separate Joint Research Centre analysis (RHOMOLO ex-ante macroeconomic assessment, JRC Working Papers on Territorial Modelling and Analysis No. 05/2026) puts three of the reforms at up to **EUR 452 billion in cumulative GDP** and **half a million jobs** across the EU **over ten years**. This fed the impact assessment. Cite it as ten-year cumulative, never as an annual figure.
- **Who:** Commissioner **Ekaterina Zaharieva** (Startups, Research and Innovation), presented with Executive Vice-President **Séjourné**. Part of the **EU Startup and Scaleup Strategy** and the **Competitiveness Compass**.

## The interlock with the Public Procurement Act — get this right

The Innovation Act and the **Public Procurement Act** (COM(2026) 590, 2026/0265(COD)) were proposed on
the same day and **deliberately divide the ground between them**:

- **R&D service contracts, including pre-commercial procurement, are excluded from the Public
  Procurement Act** and regulated **here instead**, expressly "to avoid overlaps".
- The distinction is what is being bought. **Pre-commercial / R&D procurement buys research and
  development services**, not the finished thing. The Public Procurement Act, unlike this regime,
  **also covers the purchase of the commercial end-product**.
- The exclusion does **not** extend to the R&D elements inside the Public Procurement Act's own new
  **innovation procedure**.

An answer that treats the two as one reform, or that puts R&D procurement in the Public Procurement
Act, has the division backwards. See `public_procurement_act.md`.

## Why the Commission says it is needed

Most R&D procurement falls **outside** the 2014 procurement directives — typically because the
benefits do not accrue exclusively to the public buyer for its own use, as in pre-commercial
procurement. Those procedures are therefore governed by fragmented national rules, or exempt
altogether and subject only to the Treaty principles as interpreted by the Court of Justice. The
proposal cites the **European Court of Auditors' Special Report 28/2023** on the low level of
competition in EU public procurement.

## Status and what to watch

- Proposal stage. It now goes to the **European Parliament and the Council** under the ordinary
  legislative procedure; committee allocation and rapporteurs follow.
- Because Chapter III hands new tasks to **EUIPO**, the amendment to Regulation (EU) 2017/1001 is the
  operative hook — watch that article in any compromise text.
