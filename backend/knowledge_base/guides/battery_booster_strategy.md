# EU Battery Booster Strategy

## QUICK FACTS
- Full name: Battery Booster Strategy
- Type: Commission Communication + Commission Decision (Battery Booster Facility)
- Communication: C(2025) 8950, adopted 16 December 2025
- Draft Decision: establishing the Battery Booster Facility (stakeholder feedback until 15 March 2026)
- Total package: EUR 1.8 billion
- Battery Booster Facility: EUR 1.5 billion in interest-free loans from the Innovation Fund (EU ETS revenues)
- Additional policy measures: EUR 300 million
- Instrument: Interest-free, performance-based loans for battery cell producers during ramp-up phase
- Legal basis: Innovation Fund under EU ETS, Article 15 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/856
- Related legislation: Net Zero Industry Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1735), Battery Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1542)
- Responsible DG: DG CLIMA (Innovation Fund management)
- Commissioner: Wopke Hoekstra (Climate, Net Zero and Clean Growth)
- Part of: EU Automotive Package (December 2025)
- Status: Draft Decision under consultation (March 2026); no funds disbursed yet (inter-service negotiations ongoing)

## Overview

The Battery Booster Strategy was adopted by the Commission on 16 December 2025 as part of the broader EU Automotive Package. It aims to accelerate the development of a fully EU-made battery value chain by providing targeted financial support to European battery cell producers during the critical ramp-up phase of manufacturing.

The centrepiece is the Battery Booster Facility: EUR 1.5 billion in interest-free loans from the Innovation Fund (financed by EU ETS allowance auctioning revenues). The remaining EUR 300 million covers targeted policy measures to strengthen upstream supply chains, foster innovation, and coordinate Member State efforts.

## Battery Booster Facility -- Design

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Funding source | Innovation Fund (EU ETS revenues) |
| Amount | EUR 1.5 billion |
| Form | Interest-free, performance-based loans |
| Eligible projects | European battery cell production sites in ramp-up phase |
| Ramp-up phase | Transitional period from construction/installation to high-volume manufacturing |
| Objective | Catalyse private investment, accelerate industrial deployment, strengthen EU global battery market position |
| Legal basis | Article 15 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/856 |
| Call for Proposals | To be launched after final Commission Decision |
| Stakeholder feedback | Open until 15 March 2026 via EU Survey |
| Contact | clima-innovation-fund@ec.europa.eu |

## EU Automotive Package (December 2025)

The Battery Booster is one component of the wider Automotive Package:

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Battery Booster Strategy | EUR 1.8B for EU battery value chain |
| CO2 targets flexibility | Adjusted compliance mechanisms for 2035 |
| "Made in EU" incentives | Member States must link financial incentives for corporate vehicles to EU-manufactured low/zero-emission vehicles |
| Car labelling | New consumer information rules on fuel efficiency, CO2 emissions, and EU manufacturing origin |
