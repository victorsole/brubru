# European Commission Priorities 2024–2029 (von der Leyen II)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What this is**: the **seven priorities** of the von der Leyen II Commission (2024–2029) and the flagship initiatives + lead DGs under each — so Brubru frames any file against the right political objective.
- **Foundational text**: the **Political Guidelines 2024–2029** (published 18 July 2024), available in all 24 languages. Translated into action via the annual **Work Programme**.
- **Headline shift from 2019–2024**: the previous Commission had six priorities led by the **European Green Deal**; the new mandate reframes around **competitiveness** (the Competitiveness Compass / Clean Industrial Deal) and **security & defence**.
- **Why Brubru cares**: every Commission proposal cites a priority; knowing the priority → lead DG mapping makes Chat, Predictions and the brief sound institutionally fluent.
- **Source**: `commission.europa.eu/priorities-2024-2029_en` (read 31 May 2026).

## The seven priorities

1. **A new plan for Europe's sustainable prosperity and competitiveness** — growth, enterprise, innovation, fairness. Flagships: the **Competitiveness Compass**, the **Clean Industrial Deal**, an industrial-decarbonisation + affordable-energy agenda. *Lead DGs*: GROW, COMP, ECFIN, CLIMA, ENER, CNECT, RTD.
2. **A new era for European defence and security** — defence readiness, preparedness, crisis management. Flagships: the **White Paper on European Defence Readiness 2030**, EDIP, SAFE. *Lead DGs*: DEFIS, HOME; with the EEAS.
3. **Supporting people, strengthening our societies and our social model** — social fairness, solidarity, equal opportunities, quality jobs, skills. *Lead DGs*: EMPL, EAC, JUST.
4. **Sustaining our quality of life: food security, water and nature** — a competitive, resilient agriculture & food system, biodiversity, climate adaptation. Flagships: the **Vision for Agriculture and Food**, a Water Resilience Strategy. *Lead DGs*: AGRI, ENV, CLIMA, SANTE, MARE.
5. **Protecting our democracy, upholding our values** — citizens at the heart of democracy, rule of law, countering information manipulation. Flagships: a **European Democracy Shield**. *Lead DGs*: JUST, COMM, HOME; SG.
6. **A global Europe: leveraging our power and partnerships** — the wider neighbourhood, partnerships, peace and economic stability. Flagships: **Global Gateway**, enlargement. *Lead DGs*: INTPA, ENEST, MENA, TRADE; with the EEAS.
7. **Delivering together and preparing our Union for the future** — a modern, reinforced EU budget and a reform agenda. Flagships: the **next MFF (post-2027)**, budget reform. *Lead DGs*: BUDG, SG, ECFIN.

## How the priorities become law
Priorities → annual **Work Programme** → **call for evidence / public consultation** → **impact assessment** (reviewed by the Regulatory Scrutiny Board) → **inter-service consultation** → **College adoption** → negotiation with Parliament + Council → **Official Journal**. See `eu_commission_decision_making.md` and `better_regulation_enforcement_communication.md`.

## Truthfulness note
Priority **titles are paraphrased** for readability; the authoritative wording is in the Political Guidelines and the priority pages. Commissioner portfolio names can differ from priority titles — describe by **function and lead DG**, and avoid asserting an individual Commissioner owns a whole priority unless verified.
