# How the European Commission Decides

## QUICK FACTS
- **What this is**: how the College of Commissioners actually takes decisions, and the strategic-planning cycle that frames them — so Brubru can explain "how does the Commission adopt a proposal?" accurately.
- **Collegiality**: the College decides **collectively** — every decision is the College's, whichever procedure is used. It meets **weekly (Wednesdays)**.
- **Four procedures**: **oral**, **written**, **empowerment**, **delegation** (below).
- **Right of initiative**: the Commission **proposes**; Parliament + Council negotiate and adopt. The final act can differ substantially from the proposal, and is then published in the **Official Journal**.
- **Source**: `commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/decision-making-process-commission_en` + strategy-documents pages (read 31 May 2026).

## The four decision procedures
1. **Oral procedure** — used for **politically sensitive** or important items, debated at the weekly College meeting (prepared by Commissioners' Cabinets in advance).
2. **Written procedure** — Commissioners agree **collectively in writing** when no debate is needed; if no member objects within the deadline, it is adopted.
3. **Empowerment procedure** — the College **empowers a Commissioner** to take a (management/administrative) decision on its behalf within a defined mandate.
4. **Delegation procedure** — a **Director-General** is delegated to act on the College's behalf for routine matters.
Empowerment and delegation handle routine business efficiently **without breaking collegiality** — the decision remains the College's.

## The strategic-planning cycle
The Commission plans in a yearly loop:
- **Political Guidelines** (start of mandate) → the President's missions for the College.
- **Annual Work Programme** → the year's planned initiatives (after the State of the Union).
- **Strategic Plans** (per DG, multi-year) → long-term objectives + impact indicators.
- **Management Plans** (per DG, annual) → that year's activities.
- **Annual Activity Reports** (per DG) → what was achieved.
- **Management & Performance Report** → the College takes **political responsibility for the EU budget**.

## From idea to law (the pipeline)
1. **Planning** — Work Programme entry.
2. **Call for evidence** (≈4 weeks) and **public consultation** (≥12 weeks) on the **Have Your Say** portal.
3. **Impact assessment**, scrutinised by the independent **Regulatory Scrutiny Board**.
4. **Inter-service consultation** — all relevant DGs clear the draft.
5. **College adoption** (one of the four procedures).
6. **Co-decision** — negotiation with Parliament + Council (ordinary legislative procedure).
7. **Publication** in the Official Journal; entry into force (see `official_journal_explained.md`).
