# Clean corporate vehicles: the proposed Regulation on zero- and low-emission corporate fleets (2025/0421(COD))

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status (6 November 2026): a Commission proposal, NOT law.** Parliament's joint ENVI-TRAN committee adopted its position on **Wednesday 4 November 2026 by 89 votes to 29, with 4 abstentions** (EP press release 20261103IPR48612). Plenary is next: OEIL's indicative plenary date is **14 December 2026** (first reading); that date is a forecast, NOT a fixed vote. Council position: not found in the sources read.
- **Proposal:** COM(2025) 994, procedure **2025/0421(COD)**, part of the Commission's automotive package of 16 December 2025 (with the CO2 standards revision 2025/0420(COD) and the vehicle rules omnibus 2025/0422(COD), see `eu_automotive_omnibus`). Referral announced in Parliament on 9 February 2026.
- **Rapporteurs (OEIL):** **Tiemo Wölken (S&D) for ENVI** and **François Kalfon (S&D) for TRAN**, both appointed 5 March 2026 (joint committee under Rule 59). Shadows include Dariusz Joński and Raúl de la Hoz Quintano (EPP), Roman Haider and Anne-Sophie Frigout (PfE), Carlo Fidanza and Jacek Ozdoba (ECR), Jan-Christoph Oetjen and Yvan Verougstraete (Renew), Kai Tegethoff (Greens/EFA), Dario Tamburrano (The Left).
- **Parliament's committee position (its press release, not the adopted text):** covers corporate cars and vans registered by **large businesses: more than 250 employees and turnover over EUR 50 million**; excludes vehicles for civil protection, public order, essential services and military mobility; **national aspirational targets** for the share of zero- and low-emission vehicles in new corporate registrations by **2030 and 2035, ranging from 67% to 95%** depending on the Member State; national plans from **2028**; public financial support only for zero- and low-emission corporate vehicles **"made in the EU"**. Parliament's background figure: **58% of new cars and 90% of vans are corporate vehicles**.
- **The same day:** ENVI adopted the CO2 standards text 48-30-2 and the joint ENVI-IMCO-TRAN committee the vehicle rules omnibus 105-39-21 (see `eu_automotive_omnibus`).
- **What was NOT read:** the proposal COM(2025) 994 itself and the committee's adopted amendments; the targets above are Parliament's committee position, which can change in plenary and in trilogue.
