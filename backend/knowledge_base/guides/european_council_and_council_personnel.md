## General Secretariat of the Council (GSC)

The GSC supports both the European Council and the Council of the EU. It is headed by Secretary-General Therese Blanchet, who is also Secretary-General of the European Council.

## COREPER -- Permanent Representatives Committee

COREPER prepares the work of the Council of the EU. It has two configurations:
- COREPER II: Ambassadors (Permanent Representatives). Handles political files: foreign affairs, ECOFIN, JHA, general affairs.
- COREPER I: Deputy Permanent Representatives. Handles technical files: environment, transport, social, competitiveness.

### Permanent Representations (27 Member States)

Each Member State maintains a Permanent Representation to the EU in Brussels, headed by a Permanent Representative (ambassador) who sits in COREPER II and a Deputy Permanent Representative who sits in COREPER I.

All 27 Permanent Representations: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden.
