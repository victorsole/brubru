# AGILE programme, Regulation (EU) 2026/2387: EU defence innovation funding for small and medium-sized firms, EUR 115 million for 2027

## QUICK FACTS
- **In force? NO as of 3 November 2026, YES from 4 November 2026** (Article 25: the day after publication, NOT the twentieth day). OJ L of 3 November 2026; signed 21 October 2026. CELEX 32026R2387, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/2387/oj , procedure 2026/0078(COD), COM(2026) 135.
- **Open for applications? NO as of 3 November 2026.** The Programme runs from **1 January to 31 December 2027** (Article 1) and works through a work programme adopted by Commission implementing act (Article 16). The Commission says "fully operational as of early 2027" (IP/26/2132). No call date or work programme has been read: give none.
- **Money:** indicative **EUR 115 000 000** in current prices for 2027 (Article 4), which also covers technical and administrative assistance. It may pay **up to 100%** of eligible costs (Article 14).
- **Who can receive it:** recipients must be **SMEs**, including start-ups and scale-ups, except for the supporting actions in Article 10(1)(d) (Article 9(3)). They must be established in the EU or an associated third country, with executive management there, and not controlled from a non-associated country (Article 9(4)-(5)). **Associated countries are possible, not automatic:** EFTA members of the EEA, and Ukraine (Article 7).
- **What it funds, exactly four forms (Article 10(1)):** (a) rapid development of emerging and disruptive defence products, including civilian technology with dual-use potential; (b) market uptake: demand aggregation, field testing with end users, matchmaking; (c) **relocation** of a firm or its executive management from a non-associated third country to the EU or an associated country, tied to (a) or (b); (d) support such as qualification, certification, infrastructure, manufacturing access, investment readiness, skills, studies.
- **NOT fundable (Article 9(9)):** actions already fully financed elsewhere; products prohibited by international law; lethal autonomous weapons without meaningful human control over selection and engagement decisions when striking humans (early warning and countermeasures remain possible); cluster munitions.
- **Speed, and whose numbers they are:** in the Regulation: accelerated award on a preliminary evaluation and self-declaration (Article 12(2)); final evaluation within four months of the application deadline (recital 31); costs may be covered from up to three months before the call closes (Article 8(3)). **The Commission's own figures, NOT in the articles:** four-month time-to-grant, technology reaching forces in 1 to 3 years, 20 to 30 projects (IP/26/2132, 12 October 2026).
- **Inducement intervention (Article 13):** the work programme may temporarily suspend the establishment and management conditions; its budget is a limited share of the Programme, there is no pre-financing, and funding is recovered if the conditions are not then met.
- **Also amends** Regulation (EU) 2024/795 and Regulation (EU) 2026/467 (the Ukraine loan). Commission evaluation report by 1 July 2028 (Article 24).
- **Not read in detail:** the articles on committee procedure, security and audit. **Votes:** Parliament 6 October 2026, Council 12 October 2026. Overview: `eu_defence_readiness_omnibus_v_and_agile_2026`.
