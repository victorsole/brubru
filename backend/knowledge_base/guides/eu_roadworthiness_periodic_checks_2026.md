# EU Periodic Roadworthiness Checks Package (2026 revision)

## What the revision does

The 2026 revision modernises the 2014 Roadworthiness Package along five axes:

### 1. Bring electric and connected vehicles into scope explicitly
The 2014 framework was designed before mass-market EV adoption. The revision adds:
- High-voltage battery health checks (state-of-health diagnostics, thermal-management integrity)
- ADAS calibration verification (LKAS, AEB, ACC, lane departure)
- Cybersecurity-relevant electronic control unit (ECU) checks aligned with **UNECE WP.29 R155 / R156** (already binding under Regulation (EU) 2018/858)
- Over-the-air (OTA) software update logging — last-update version and source

### 2. Tighten frequency for older and high-mileage vehicles
- Cars older than **10 years**: **annual** roadworthiness check (currently the default in many MS is every 2 years for cars older than 4 years)
- High-mileage vehicles (taxis, rentals, vans used commercially): mandatory annual check regardless of age — closes a gap in current Article 5(2) of Directive 2014/45/EU

### 3. Mileage fraud
- Standardised mandatory **odometer reading at every inspection**, transmitted to a central national database
- Cross-Member-State odometer registry interoperability — leverages the eIDAS 2.0 and Single Digital Gateway infrastructure
- Strict reporting obligation on inspection centres; record-keeping minimum 10 years (currently varies 3-7 years)

### 4. Light vehicles (L-category)
- Mandatory **periodic checks for L3e motorcycles** (over 125 cc) in all Member States — currently optional in 6 MS
- Optional opt-in regime for e-bikes and e-scooters above 25 km/h

### 5. Emissions
- Updated test methods for **NOx + PM** measurement aligned with Euro 7 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1257)
- Mandatory **OBD-II** (On-Board Diagnostics) emissions data reading at every test — closes the gap exposed by dieselgate
- Random "remote sensing" roadside checks added to Directive 2014/47/EU scope

## Member State implementation map (2026 baseline before revision)

| Member State | Frequency (cars < 4y) | Frequency (4-10y) | Frequency (10+y) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany (HU/AU) | 3y | 2y | 2y |
| France | 4y | 2y | 2y |
| Italy | 4y | 2y | 2y |
| Spain (ITV) | 4y | 2y | 2y / annual >10y |
| Netherlands (APK) | 4y/3y/2y/annual | annual >8y | annual |
| Poland | 3y | 2y | annual |
| Sweden | varies | varies | varies |

(Source: cross-reference DG MOVE's 2024 implementation report on Directive 2014/45/EU.)

The revision will not fully harmonise frequencies but will set **minimum** intervals so that high-frequency MS retain their regime while low-frequency MS (FR, IT) align upward for vehicles over 10 years old.

## Stakeholder map

- **CITA** (International Motor Vehicle Inspection Committee) — industry body of inspection-centre operators; advocates wider scope on ADAS calibration
- **FIA** (Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile) — represents motoring associations; advocates user-friendly e-vehicle test procedures
- **ACEA** (European Automobile Manufacturers' Association) — manufacturers; concerned about over-the-air update logging burden and ECU access
- **ETSC** (European Transport Safety Council) — pushes for annual tests on older cars on safety grounds
- **ERA** (European Union Agency for Railways) — out of scope but cross-references multimodal transport safety

## Compliance roadmap (post-adoption)

- Trilogue: opens after Council adopts its general approach (expected H2 2026 under Polish Presidency, second half 2026)
- Adopted text → entry into force the 20th day after OJ publication
- Transposition period: typically 24 months from entry into force (standard for transport directives)
- Technical annexes (test procedures, ADAS calibration spec, OBD-II data list) likely shipped as **delegated/implementing acts** within 18 months of entry into force

## What changes for inspection centres

- Equipment investment: ADAS-calibration tooling, EV high-voltage diagnostic equipment, OBD-II data link readers (estimated EUR 80,000-150,000 per centre to upgrade)
- Workforce upskilling: certified EV-safety training, ADAS calibration certification
- IT integration with national odometer registry + cross-MS interoperability via SDG
- Likely Member State support via **Cohesion Policy / ERDF** for SME inspection-centre upgrades
