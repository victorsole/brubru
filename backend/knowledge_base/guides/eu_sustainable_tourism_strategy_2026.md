# EU Sustainable Tourism Strategy (COM(2026) 777, 4 November 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Adopted Wednesday 4 November 2026: "EU Sustainable Tourism Strategy", COM(2026) 777 final** (CELEX 52026DC0777), with the staff working document **SWD(2026) 777 final** ("Tourism - A Key Asset for Europe"). A communication, NOT a legislative proposal: it creates no obligation for businesses.
- **Commissioners:** presented by Executive Vice-President Raffaele Fitto and Commissioner Apostolos Tzitzikostas (press remarks, 4 November).
- **The Commission's figures for the sector (IP/26/2281):** about **10% of EU gross value added**, **more than 20 million jobs**, **5 million businesses, 99% of them SMEs**; 76% of Europeans plan a trip in the next six months.
- **Dated commitments in the strategy (Commission's own deadlines):**
  - **by the end of 2026:** a proposal to revise and modernise the **VAT rules for the travel and tourism sector**;
  - **by 2027:** an **EU Simplification Initiative for tourism** (evidence on the administrative burden on businesses and destinations) and the **EU Tourism Academy** (training, digital micro-credentials, a network of national tourism institutes, universities and VET providers);
  - **by 2028:** a study on working conditions in tourism.
- **Other flagships:** an **EU Tourism Observatory** (trends, a common performance framework for the sector), further work on the **Common European Tourism Data Space**, the first **Tourism Europe Days** (a biennial high-level event), and work towards a **seamless European travel area**.
- **Builds on:** the 2022 Tourism Transition Pathway and the Council's European Agenda for Tourism 2030.
- **Overtourism:** the Commission's social posts frame it as pressure on local communities ("packed beaches, long queues"); the strategy's own measures on destinations were not read in detail (see below).
- **What was NOT read:** the strategy's sections on destination management, short-term rentals and connectivity in full, and the SWD. Do not attribute specific overtourism or short-term rental measures to it without reading them.
