# My EU Bubble: what each of the 25 sub-tabs does

## QUICK FACTS
- My EU Bubble is Brubru's cockpit. It has exactly 25 sub-tabs in four groups.
- Start here: Overview, Policy Interests, My Documents, News.
- Legislative Monitoring: My Tracked Files, My OJ, Amendments, Comparator, Legislative Train: state of play, Votes.
- Institutional Monitoring: My EU Calendar, Transcripts, Council Watch, MEP Watch, Plenary Order of Business, Parliamentary Questions, EU Public Consultations, Lobby Meetings.
- Analysis & Strategy: Position Analysis, Predictions, Brubru Databases, Research & Evidence, Stakeholder Mapping, Strategy Docs, Tender Docs.
- Most tabs filter themselves through the user's Policy Interests, so setting those first is the highest-value action.
- That list of 25 is complete. A tab not named on it does not exist.

## Start here

**Overview** is the front door: a personalised greeting, the tiles that summarise what moved, and a digest of the user's files.

**Policy Interests** is where the user declares the topics they work on. It is the spine of the product, because the monitoring tabs read from it to decide what to show.

**My Documents** holds the user's own material: amendments, analyses, strategy notes and generated documents, all editable and searchable.

**News** is EU institutional news filtered to the user's interests, presented as a featured story over a card feed rather than a raw list.

## Legislative Monitoring

**My Tracked Files** is the personal watchlist of legislative files, with the committee work, Commission documents and legislative-train position attached to each.

**My OJ** shows the day's Official Journal, with a date selector and facets, grouped by act type and filtered to the user's interests. It answers "what was published today".

**Amendments** collects amendment work in sub-tabs, including amendments tabled by MEPs on files the user follows.

**Comparator** is a spreadsheet workspace for comparing files side by side: rows are files, columns are rapporteur, status, committee, deadlines and article counts, and every cell is cited. It is built so the user picks files rather than typing CELEX or procedure references from memory.

**Legislative Train: state of play** tracks the Commission's priority trains and where each file sits along them.

**Votes** carries European Parliament roll-call results in two sub-tabs, committee results and plenary results.

## Institutional Monitoring

**My EU Calendar** is the multi-source institutional calendar with month, week and day views. Signed-out and trial users see a preview rather than the full tab.

**Transcripts** is a catalogue of EP committee transcripts, AI-transcribed from EP Multimedia Centre recordings, plus recordings suggested from the user's interests. Minutes and final reports remain authoritative.

**Council Watch** shows what the Council of the EU is doing on the user's topics: configuration meetings plus outcomes and press, filtered by interests.

**MEP Watch** ranks the MEPs most active on the user's files by questions tabled, and opens into an MEP's questions and their EP profile.

**Plenary Order of Business** lists upcoming sittings and the dossiers plenary recently adopted on the user's interests.

**Parliamentary Questions** holds written and oral questions tabled by MEPs, pre-filtered to the user's Policy Interests.

**EU Public Consultations** tracks Commission consultations and Have Your Say initiatives with their deadlines. Signed-out and trial users see a preview.

**Lobby Meetings** presents Transparency Register meetings through four views, all through the interests lens: who met whom, on what, and when.

## Analysis & Strategy

**Position Analysis** sets out, for a given file, where the Commission, the Parliament by political group, and the Council by member state stand, with a confidence level on each reading.

**Predictions** forecasts legislative outcomes: timelines, probability, and group-by-group breakdown. It is metered by plan, with a monthly allowance on Starter and Advocate and unlimited use plus Council analysis on Professional.

**Brubru Databases** exposes Brubru's own datasets through several sub-tabs, for users who want to see what the platform actually holds.

**Research & Evidence** is a curated, independent filter over studies, EPRS publications and impact assessments. It is not part of Brubru Databases.

**Stakeholder Mapping** draws the actor graph around a topic or a file, either as an overview of the user's interests or centred on one file, with two rendering styles.

**Strategy Docs** is a strategy-scoped, file-anchored view over My Documents: position papers, briefings and talking points tied to the file they concern.

**Tender Docs** is the paperwork side of the Tenderator inside the cockpit, for drafting and storing tender submission documents.

## Pointing users at the right tab

Match the question to the tab: what moved today goes to News or My OJ; who decides goes to MEP Watch, Council Watch or Position Analysis; when goes to My EU Calendar or Plenary Order of Business; what was said goes to Transcripts; will it pass goes to Predictions; how do I change the text goes to the Amendator; comparing several files at once goes to the Comparator.

Always name one sub-tab alongside My EU Bubble, and never describe a click path that has not been verified: say what the tab does and where it lives, and stop there.
