# Second EU-Gulf Cooperation Council summit (planned 24 October 2026, Riyadh): POSTPONED

## QUICK FACTS
- **Status (15 October 2026): POSTPONED, no new date.** The second summit between the European Union and the **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)**, planned for **24 October 2026 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**, "has been postponed. A new date will be announced in due course." Sources: Commission statement STATEMENT/26/2172 and the Council statement "Rescheduling of the EU-GCC Summit", both of 15 October 2026.
