# EU agri-food research 2014-2024: headline evidence in SWD(2026) 271

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** Commission Staff Working Document **SWD(2026) 271 final** (8 September 2026, 91 pages, CELEX 52026SC0271), "Analytical overview of the achievements of the **2016 strategic approach to agricultural research and innovation** and **Food 2030** ... and insights from stakeholder consultation activities". It is the evidence base for COM(2026) 453. Data: **Horizon Dashboard, 2014-2024** (Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe 2021-2024); 2025-2027 not covered, so figures will change.
- **Headline:** **more than EUR 3 billion in 565 R&I projects** (agriculture, forestry and rural areas, 2014-2024).
- **By action type (Horizon 2020 / Horizon Europe):** research and innovation actions **158 projects, EUR 969.9 million / 174, EUR 1,118.5 million**; innovation actions **37, EUR 258.7 million / 55, EUR 318.6 million**; coordination and support actions **69, EUR 167.7 million / 65, EUR 254.9 million**; co-funded instruments **4, EUR 98 million / 3, EUR 147.2 million**. Projects grew about **15% a year**, EU funding about **20%**; innovation actions fastest (from 1 project in 2016 to 17 in 2020).
- **Multi-actor approach:** **195 projects (EUR 1,040.7 million) in Horizon 2020, 219 (EUR 1,241.8 million) in Horizon Europe**; **3,681 unique organisations**, over **1,000 SMEs (28%)**.
- **Weak spots the SWD names:** lower private co-investment (the "leverage factor") because **innovation actions are 17%** of agri-forestry-rural funding against 29% (Societal Challenge 2 or Cluster 6) and 33% (Pillar III or II); SMEs funded **below the average beneficiary**; **4.5%** of Horizon 2020 projects led to an **intellectual property application** (7.3% in Societal Challenge 2); only **32** applications in 2014-2024 (13 trademarks, 10 patents); **social sciences and humanities** projects fell from **52.6% to 29.3%**; technology readiness level (TRL) reporting is voluntary and thin.
- **Oversubscription:** on average **two excellent proposals went unfunded for each one funded** (1:2; RIA 1:2, IA 1:3, CSA 1:1); strongest demand in livestock, soil and plant protection topics.
- **Conclusion:** common needs: more operational frameworks, stronger links from research to deployment, alignment with users, monitoring, governance capacity, competitiveness, resilience and territorial realities.
- **Policy fit:** R&I fed the CAP reforms, the Climate Law, the Nature Restoration Regulation, the soil, biodiversity, forest and farm-to-fork strategies, the Soil Monitoring Directive (EU) 2025/2360 and the Fertiliser Action Plan.
