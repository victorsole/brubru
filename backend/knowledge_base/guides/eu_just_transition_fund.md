## What the JTF does

The JTF is **Pillar 1 of the Just Transition Mechanism (JTM)** — the EU's instrument to ensure that the climate transition is socially fair. It funds:

- **Productive investment in SMEs** in transition territories — including start-ups + diversification investments
- **Investment in creating new firms** (incubators, accelerators in coal-mining or carbon-intensive regions)
- **Research and innovation activities** + technology transfer
- **Affordable green-energy technology deployment** at local level
- **Investments in renewable energy (subject to State aid rules)** for transition territories
- **Investments in smart and sustainable local mobility** (clean transport infrastructure)
- **Rehabilitation and decontamination of brownfield sites** (mine reclamation, contaminated industrial sites)
- **Circular economy investments** (waste prevention, reuse, recycling)
- **Upskilling and reskilling of workers** (cross-link with EGF — JTF covers more upstream / prevention while EGF covers post-redundancy)
- **Job-search assistance + active inclusion of jobseekers**
- **Technical assistance** for managing authorities

## What the JTF does NOT do

- Investments in **nuclear power generation** (excluded)
- Investments in **fossil fuels** (excluded — including natural gas with limited exceptions in transition territories under strict conditions)
- Investments in **tobacco**
- Decommissioning of fossil-fuel plants where Member State's transition plan does not require it
- General regional-development spending (that's ERDF)

## The Just Transition Mechanism — three pillars

| Pillar | Instrument | Volume (2021-2027) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pillar 1 — **Just Transition Fund (JTF)** | Reg (EU) 2021/1056 | EUR 17.5bn | Grants to transition territories |
| Pillar 2 — **InvestEU Just Transition scheme** | InvestEU dedicated window | EUR 1.8bn EU guarantee unlocking ~EUR 10-15bn private investment | Equity + debt for transition projects |
| Pillar 3 — **Public Sector Loan Facility** | Reg (EU) 2021/1229 | EUR 1.5bn grant + EIB loans for ~EUR 10bn total | Public-sector investments |

## Eligibility — Just Transition Territories

JTF support is **geographically targeted**. Member States identified their **Territorial Just Transition Plans (TJTPs)** in 2021-2022 and these were approved by the Commission. Eligible territories typically include:

- **Coal-mining regions** (Poland Silesia, Czech Moravia-Silesia, Romania Jiu Valley, Bulgaria Stara Zagora, Greece Western Macedonia, Spain Asturias + Castilla y León, Germany Lusatia + Rhineland)
- **Carbon-intensive industrial regions** (steel: Sweden Norrbotten, Germany Saarland; chemicals: Belgium Antwerp + Hainaut, Italy Sicily Priolo + Sardinia)
- **Peat-extraction regions** (Ireland Midlands, Estonia Ida-Viru, Finland)
- **Outermost regions** with specific transition needs

## The Just Transition Platform

Created in 2020 as the operational hub for JTM implementation:
- **Knowledge sharing** + peer-learning across transition territories
- **Capacity-building** (technical assistance for managing authorities + project promoters)
- **Project pipeline development**
- **Industry partnerships**
- **Stakeholder dialogue** (Member States + regions + cities + employers + unions + civil society)

The Platform is hosted by DG REGIO with support from the EIB + JRC. It convenes annual Just Transition Conferences and produces sector-specific guidance notes.

The **2026 beneficiary cohort** announced 21 May 2026 is the latest selection round under the Platform's capacity-building support stream.

## Compliance implications for project promoters

- **State aid**: JTF investments must comply with the **General Block Exemption Regulation (GBER)** and the **Climate, Environmental Protection and Energy Aid Guidelines (CEEAG)** — both subject to the **Clean Industrial Deal State Aid Framework** updates (March 2026)
- **Do No Significant Harm (DNSH)** principle: mandatory for all JTF-funded investments under Art 6 of the Common Provisions Regulation (Reg (EU) 2021/1060)
- **Social safeguards**: project promoters must demonstrate engagement with affected workers + communities
- **Monitoring**: standard cohesion-policy KPI framework (jobs created, training delivered, MW renewable capacity, brownfield ha rehabilitated, etc.)

## Post-2027 — JTF in the MFF 2028-2034

The MFF 2028-2034 framework (proposed 16 July 2025) proposes:
- Integration of JTF into a **wider "Climate Adaptation and Transition" cluster** under the new MFF heading 5 (cohesion + transitions)
- Potential continuation as a stand-alone instrument under a different name
- Trilogue ongoing; outcome targeted mid-2027
