# Spain and Catalonia: waste law and extended producer responsibility (national and regional layer)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Spanish framework law**: **Ley 7/2022, de 8 de abril, de residuos y suelos contaminados para una economía circular** (BOE-A-2022-5809).
- **It repealed Ley 22/2011**, de 28 de julio, de residuos y suelos contaminados. Ley 22/2011 is **no longer in force**. Any answer citing Ley 22/2011 as the current Spanish waste framework is wrong.
- **Catalan framework**: **Decret Legislatiu 1/2009**, de 21 de juliol, pel qual s'aprova el Text refós de la Llei reguladora dels residus (DOGC 5430). Catalonia holds devolved competence over waste management; the Agència de Residus de Catalunya (ARC) is the competent authority.
- **EU parent instruments**: Directive 2008/98/EC (Waste Framework Directive), as amended by **Directive (EU) 2025/1892** (textile EPR and food waste) and Directive (EU) 2018/851.
- **Textile EPR in Spain**: Ley 7/2022 created the legal basis; the operative scheme requires a **Real Decreto** which was still in preparation as of August 2026. Until that RD is adopted and in force, textile producers in Spain have **no operative Spanish EPR obligation**, only the EU timetable to prepare for.
- **EU deadlines that bind Spain and Catalonia**: transposition of Directive (EU) 2025/1892 by **17 June 2027**; textile EPR schemes established by **17 April 2028**; micro-enterprises covered from **17 April 2029**.
- **Cross-link**: `textile_epr_waste_framework_directive_2025_1892`, `ecodesign_digital_product_passport`, `eu_packaging_packaging_waste_2025_40`

This guide covers the Spanish and Catalan layer that sits under the EU waste and circular-economy acquis. It exists because answers on this layer were the weakest area found in the 5 August 2026 query audit: one answer cited the repealed Ley 22/2011 as current, and another applied a healthcare-waste decree to municipal workwear.

## The instrument hierarchy, in order

1. **EU**: Directive 2008/98/EC as amended. A Directive, so it binds Spain as to result and needs national transposition.
2. **Spain (State)**: **Ley 7/2022** sets the framework, plus Reales Decretos per waste stream.
3. **Catalonia (Autonomous Community)**: **Decret Legislatiu 1/2009** and ARC implementing rules. Catalonia may be **more stringent** than the State minimum, which Article 193 TFEU also permits relative to EU law.
4. **Municipal**: local ordinances on collection, containers and civic amenity sites (*deixalleries*).

A compliance question about a Spanish operation is not answered by the EU layer alone. Always identify which of these four layers the obligation actually comes from.

## Ley 7/2022: what it actually does

**Note on article numbers:** cite them only when the article text is available in context. Where it is not, describe the obligation and point to the BOE consolidated text rather than guessing a number.

- **Framework for extended producer responsibility** across waste streams, with the detailed regime for each stream delegated to a Real Decreto. This is an empowerment, not a self-executing obligation.
- **Separate collection of textiles** by local authorities: Spain set a deadline of **31 December 2024**, ahead of the EU's 1 January 2025 date under Directive (EU) 2018/851.
- **Restriction on destroying unsold non-perishable goods**, including textiles. Spain legislated this before the EU-level ban in Article 25 of the ESPR (Regulation (EU) 2024/1781) and the two regimes now sit side by side. Check both.
- **Single-use plastics measures** and the associated **special taxes** on non-reusable plastic packaging, which is a genuinely Spanish addition with no direct EU equivalent.
- **Registro de Productores de Producto**: producers must register. Registration is per stream, and a stream only becomes live once its Real Decreto is in force.
- **Regime of infringements and penalties**, administered by the autonomous communities.

## The textile EPR gap, precisely stated

As of August 2026 the position is:

| Layer | Status |
|---|---|
| EU | Directive (EU) 2025/1892 in force since 16 October 2025. Transposition due 17 June 2027, schemes due 17 April 2028 |
| Spain (framework) | Ley 7/2022 provides the legal basis for textile EPR |
| Spain (operative) | **Real Decreto on textile EPR not yet adopted.** Draft consulted; adoption pending |
| Catalonia | ARC operates separate textile collection with municipalities and social economy entities; no autonomous textile EPR scheme substituting for the State RD |

**What this means for a producer:** there is a legal basis but not yet an operative Spanish scheme. The correct answer to "what must we do now in Spain" is: prepare, monitor the RD, and work to the EU dates, not "you must already contribute to a Spanish textile EPR scheme".

**Do not** state that Spanish textile EPR obligations are "fully in force" or give a past completion date. That was a defect in the 5 August audit.

## Catalonia specifics

- **Agència de Residus de Catalunya (ARC)** is the competent authority: registers, authorisations, inspections, and the Catalan waste registers.
- **Decret Legislatiu 1/2009** is the consolidated Catalan waste law.
- Catalonia has a strong **social economy** collection sector for textiles, which matters because Directive (EU) 2025/1892 gives social economy entities explicit protections: they may keep their own collection points, must get equal or preferential siting, cannot be refused participation in a producer responsibility organisation's system, and are not obliged to hand over what they collect.
- **Decret 27/1999, de 9 de febrer** concerns the **management of healthcare waste** (*gestió dels residus sanitaris*). It is **NOT** the rule for municipal workwear, uniforms or post-consumer textiles. Citing it for textile circularity is a category error and was a confirmed defect in the 5 August audit.

## Common errors to avoid

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "Ley 22/2011, de residuos y economía circular" | Ley 22/2011 is **repealed**; the current law is **Ley 7/2022**. The phrase "y economía circular" belongs to Ley 7/2022's title, not to Ley 22/2011 ("y suelos contaminados") |
| Decret 27/1999 governs textile waste classification | Decret 27/1999 governs **healthcare** waste. Use Decret Legislatiu 1/2009 for the general Catalan waste framework |
| "Reglament (UE) 2023/956" for ecodesign | 2023/956 is **CBAM**. The ESPR is Regulation (EU) **2024/1781** |
| Spanish textile EPR obligations already apply | Framework exists in Ley 7/2022; the **operative Real Decreto is pending**. EU dates: 17 June 2027 and 17 April 2028 |
| Spain's textile separate-collection deadline is 2025 | Spain's own deadline was **31 December 2024**, ahead of the EU's 1 January 2025 |

## Terminology, Catalan and Spanish

| English | Català | Español |
|---|---|---|
| Extended producer responsibility | responsabilitat ampliada del productor (**RAP**) | responsabilidad ampliada del productor (**RAP**) |
| Waste | residus | residuos |
| Separate collection | recollida separada | recogida separada |
| Preparing for re-use | preparació per a la reutilització | preparación para la reutilización |
| Producer responsibility organisation | organització de responsabilitat del productor | sistema colectivo de responsabilidad ampliada (**SCRAP**) |
| Food waste | malbaratament alimentari | desperdicio alimentario |
| Regulation (the instrument) | **Reglament** | Reglamento |
| Directive | Directiva | Directiva |
| Landfill | abocador | vertedero |
| Civic amenity site | deixalleria | punto limpio |

Note: **SCRAP** (sistema colectivo de responsabilidad ampliada del productor) is the standard Spanish term for a collective producer responsibility organisation and is what a Spanish practitioner will use.
