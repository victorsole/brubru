## What the 2021 Child Guarantee did

The 2021 Council Recommendation (32021H1004) requires Member States to **guarantee effective and free access** for children at risk of poverty or social exclusion to:

1. **Early childhood education and care (ECEC)**
2. **Education and school-based activities**
3. **At least one healthy meal each school day**
4. **Healthcare**
5. **Healthy nutrition** (free or affordable)
6. **Adequate housing**

Each Member State was required to submit a **National Action Plan for the Child Guarantee** to 2030, identifying target groups, instruments, monitoring framework, and ESF+ allocations. National Coordinators were designated.

The 2021 instrument tied delivery to the **ESF+ child poverty earmark**: at least 5% of ESF+ resources in Member States with above-EU-average AROPE-among-children rates (i.e. above ~24%); 3% in others — under Regulation (EU) 2021/1057 Article 7(5).

## What the 2026 strengthening adds

The 6 May 2026 strengthened Recommendation operationalises mid-term review findings from 2024-2025 and aligns with the parallel:

- **Anti-Poverty Strategy** (same College day) — see `eu_anti_poverty_strategy_2026`
- **Council Recommendation on fighting housing exclusion** (same College day) — see `eu_housing_exclusion_recommendation_2026`
- **Enhanced disability rights strategy to 2030** — see `eu_disability_rights_post2024`

Likely substantive lines (consistent with mid-term review findings):

1. **Free school meals** scope extension (currently variable across MS)
2. **Mental health** access for children and adolescents — ties to JRC mental-health-x-screentime work
3. **Online safety dimension** — cross-link with DSA Article 28 (protection of minors), CSAM regulation, Age Verification framework recommendation (28 April 2026, same College cycle)
4. **Housing-for-children** — direct overlap with the housing exclusion Recommendation; bedroom adequacy and homelessness-prevention for families with children
5. **Migrant and refugee children** access guarantees
6. **Roma children** — historically the largest poverty-risk subgroup; cross-link with EU Roma Strategic Framework 2020-2030
7. **Children in alternative care / unaccompanied minors**
8. **Strengthened monitoring** through expanded Child Guarantee Monitoring Framework (DG EMPL + Joint Research Centre)
9. **ESF+ acceleration** — top-up encouragement for high-AROPE Member States
10. **National Coordinators** mandate broadening + EU-level coordination (DG EMPL)

The 2030 target remains: **at least 5 million fewer children at risk** of poverty or social exclusion by 2030.

## Member State implementation status (entering 2026 strengthening)

Per the European Child Guarantee Monitoring Framework second update (2024 baseline):

- 27 Member States submitted National Action Plans (with delays in several cases)
- ESF+ child-poverty earmark fully programmed
- Regional disparities: AROPE-among-children below 18% in CZ, NL, SI; above 30% in BG, RO, ES, IT (regional pockets)
- Implementation gaps: free school meals (most MS partial), free ECEC (most MS means-tested), mental health access for adolescents (uneven), housing adequacy (no MS at full coverage)

The 2026 strengthening is the formal mechanism to align MS National Action Plans against the Pillar of Social Rights Action Plan 2.0 (due 22 July 2026).

## Key figures

- 19.6 million EU children (under 18) at risk of poverty or social exclusion (Eurostat SILC 2024)
- AROPE-among-children rate: 24.4% (vs 19.4% all-population)
- ESF+ minimum child-poverty earmark: 5% in high-AROPE MS, 3% elsewhere
- 2030 target: 5 million children fewer (i.e. ~14.6 million by 2030, AROPE rate ~18%)
- Free school meals coverage: ~58% of EU children received subsidised/free meals in 2023 (uneven)
- Childcare coverage: Barcelona target (>33% under-3 ECEC, >90% age 3 to compulsory schooling); 2022 achievement: 35.5% / 88% — close to target

## Why this matters

1. **First strengthening since 2021** — child poverty has not fallen as fast as the 2021 Recommendation envisaged
2. **Operational anchor** for ESF+ programming and Semester recommendations on child-poverty
3. **Bridge to MFF 2028-2034** — the success metrics anchor the case for ESF+ successor envelope and child-poverty earmark continuation
4. **Multi-portfolio crosswalk** — DG EMPL + DG EAC (education) + DG SANTE (health) + DG REGIO (housing) + DG HOME (migrant children)
5. **Companion to housing + anti-poverty + disability** — the 6 May Social Package is the most comprehensive social-policy bundle of the 2024-2029 mandate to date
