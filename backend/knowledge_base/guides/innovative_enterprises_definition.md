# EU Definition of Innovative Enterprises, Startups and Scaleups

## QUICK FACTS
- Commission Recommendation (EU) 2026/720 of 18 March 2026
- CELEX: 32026H0720
- Published: Official Journal L series, 24 March 2026
- Type: Non-binding Commission Recommendation
- Responsible DG: DG GROW (Internal Market, Industry, Entrepreneurship and SMEs)
- Responsible Commissioner: Stephane Sejourne (EVP, Prosperity and Industrial Strategy)
- Context: Part of the EU startup and scaleup policy framework
- Related: Startup Nations Standard, SME Relief Package, 28th Regime Innovation Act

## Overview

Commission Recommendation (EU) 2026/720 establishes harmonised EU-wide definitions for three categories of enterprises:

1. **Innovative enterprise** -- a company that introduces new or significantly improved products, processes, or business models
2. **Innovative startup** -- an early-stage innovative enterprise (typically under 5 years old)
3. **Innovative scaleup** -- an innovative enterprise in a rapid growth phase

## Purpose

The harmonised definitions aim to:
- Provide legal certainty for Member States designing support programmes
- Enable cross-border recognition of startup/scaleup status
- Facilitate access to EU funding instruments (Horizon Europe, InvestEU, EIC)
- Support data collection and benchmarking across the Single Market
- Align national startup visa and tax incentive programmes

## Policy Context

This recommendation builds on:
- **Startup Nations Standard** (2022): voluntary commitments by Member States
- **SME Relief Package** (September 2023): COM(2023)535
- **28th Regime Innovation Act**: proposed framework for EU-level company law for startups (see `28th_regime_innovation_act.md`)
- **Industrial Accelerator Act**: broader EU industrial competitiveness framework (see `industrial_accelerator_act.md`)
- **EIC Accelerator**: EU's flagship startup investment instrument under Horizon Europe

## Implications for EU Policy Professionals

- Member States should align national definitions with the Recommendation
- Affects eligibility criteria for State aid exemptions (GBER innovative enterprise category)
- Relevant for public procurement innovation partnerships
- Important for EU funding programme design and evaluation
