# EU sanctions regime on Russia's destabilising activities (hybrid threats): renewed to 9 October 2027

## QUICK FACTS
- **The regime:** restrictive measures "in view of Russia's destabilising activities", set up on **8 October 2024** by **Council Decision (CFSP) 2024/2643** (CELEX 32024D2643) and **Council Regulation (EU) 2024/2642** (CELEX 32024R2642). It targets those responsible for Russia's hybrid activities against the EU and its Member States and also against third countries and international organisations, including Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference (FIMI).
- **Renewal on 8 October 2026:** the Council prolonged the individual measures by one year, **until 9 October 2027**, by **Decision (CFSP) 2026/2258** (CELEX 32026D2258; Article 10 date changed from 9 October 2026 to 9 October 2027; published in the Official Journal on 8 October 2026 and in force on 9 October 2026, the day after publication).
- **Who is listed now:** **80 individuals and 20 entities** (Council press release of 8 October 2026). Listed persons face an asset freeze; EU citizens and companies may not make funds or economic resources available to them; natural persons also face a **travel ban** into or through EU territory.
- **The same day, the list was amended** by Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2257 (CELEX 32026R2257): entries concerning **eight natural persons** amended and **one deleted**. Its recitals cite the European Parliament report on hybrid threats approved on **16 September 2026**.
- **A person moved between regimes:** Olga Alekseevna Belyavtseva was **deleted** from the hybrid-threats list (Decision 2026/2258, Annex, entry 11) and **added** to the Ukraine territorial-integrity regime (Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2264 amending Decision 2014/145/CFSP, CELEX 32026D2264, entry 2121; Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2263 amending Regulation (EU) No 269/2014, CELEX 32026R2263). This is a transfer between two separate regimes, NOT a delisting from EU sanctions.
- **Why now:** the European Council conclusions of **18 and 19 June 2026** condemned recent hybrid attacks and called for more resilience, preparedness and protection of critical infrastructure; hybrid attacks are also an item on the agenda of the European Council of 15 and 16 October 2026.
- Sources: Council press release https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/08/russia-s-hybrid-activities-council-prolongs-restrictive-measures-until-october-2027/ ; acts on EUR-Lex under the CELEX numbers above.
