# EU strategic approach to agri-food research and innovation: AgRI 2040 and Food 2040 (COM(2026) 453)

## QUICK FACTS
- **What it is:** the **New Strategic Approach to Research and Innovation for competitive, resilient and sustainable agriculture, forestry, rural areas and food systems**, subtitled *Future-proofing EU agri-food through AgRI 2040 and Food 2040*. A **Commission Communication**, **COM(2026) 453 final**, adopted **8 September 2026** (CELEX 52026DC0453), with staff working document **SWD(2026) 271 final** (CELEX 52026SC0271). It is NOT a law: no obligation for farmers, foresters, food businesses or researchers, and **no budget of its own**. It steers future research **work programmes**.
- **The EU AgRI 2040 Conference took place on 24-25 September 2026 in Brussels** (Charlemagne building; the DG AGRI event page says "This event has ended"). It opened with keynotes by Commissioners Hansen and Zaharieva and ran **nine thematic breakout sessions** (the eight areas plus climate change and biodiversity). The event page lists no conclusions or report: none had been read on 13 October 2026, so give none. The strategy was **adopted on 8 September 2026**; its **19 actions** start between **Q3 2026 and Q4 2028**.
- **One approach, two interlinked frameworks:** **AgRI 2040** (agriculture, forestry and rural areas) has **exactly EIGHT preliminary areas of action**; **Food 2040** (food systems, including fisheries and aquaculture) has **exactly SIX areas of action**. Three lists, three numbers: AgRI 2040 has eight areas, Food 2040 has six areas, and the strategy has six shared priorities.
- **The eight AgRI 2040 areas:** (1) soil, agri-ecosystems, water and nutrient capital; (2) cropping systems; (3) livestock systems; (4) bioeconomy in agriculture, forestry and rural areas; (5) multi-functional forests; (6) rural areas; (7) stronger agri-food value chain; (8) digitalisation, AI and robotics.
- **The six Food 2040 areas:** place-based food systems; healthy and sustainable diets; resource efficiency and zero-pollution in food systems; food environments and consumer empowerment; scaling innovations, business models and fair food systems solutions; aquatic food.
- **Six shared priorities:** competitiveness, startups and scaleups; food security, resilience and preparedness; natural resources and circularity; food and feed safety, nutrition and health; fairness; global cooperation. **Three common enablers:** governance; technologies; territories and communities. Priorities and enablers are NOT areas of action.
- **Replaces:** the 2016 strategic approach to EU agricultural R&I (now AgRI 2040) and Food 2030 (now Food 2040); answers the Vision for Agriculture and Food (COM(2025) 75).
- **Money:** to be deployed under **Horizon Europe 2028-2034** and relevant parts of the **European Competitiveness Fund**, "without prejudging" the next MFF. Give no budget figure.
- **Who:** DG AGRI and DG RTD; Commissioners **Christophe Hansen** (Agriculture and Food) and **Ekaterina Zaharieva** (Startups, Research and Innovation).
- **Evidence:** more than **EUR 3 billion in 565 projects (2014-2024)**; open consultation **701 replies**; Food 2040 survey **631 responses** plus 63 submissions.
