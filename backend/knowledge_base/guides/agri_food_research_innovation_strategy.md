# Agri-Food Research and Innovation: AgRI 2040 and Food 2040

## QUICK FACTS
- **LATEST (Tuesday 8 September 2026 — the strategy is ADOPTED and is a COMMUNICATION, not a law)**: the Commission adopted the **New Strategic Approach to Research and Innovation for competitive, resilient and sustainable agriculture, forestry, rural areas and food systems**, subtitled *Future-proofing EU agri-food through AgRI 2040 and Food 2040*. Reference **COM(2026) 453 final** of 8.9.2026, CELEX **52026DC0453**, accompanied by staff working document **SWD(2026) 271 final** (CELEX 52026SC0271). It creates **no obligations** for any operator: it is a strategic framework that shapes future work programmes. Announced the same day by DG AGRI ("Commission launches agri-food innovation strategy") and DG RTD.
- **The two frameworks, and the difference between them**: **AgRI 2040** covers **agriculture, forestry and rural areas**; **Food 2040** covers **food systems**. They are interlinked and run in parallel, each with **six areas of action**.
- **The problem it is written against**: agri-food tech investment in Europe **fell by 67% between 2020 and 2024**, while levels in the United States and Canada were 50-70% higher. R&I output is not converting into productivity gains.
- **The sector, in the Commission's own figures (2023 unless stated)**: EU agriculture and food systems generated **more than EUR 1 trillion in added value** and employ **around 30 million people, 15% of all EU jobs**. Food and drink is the EU's **largest manufacturing sector**: 4.8 million people, 309,000 businesses, EUR 1.5 trillion turnover, EUR 296 billion value added. The EU is the **world's largest agri-food exporter**, with a **EUR 50 billion trade surplus in 2025**. Rural areas hold **25% of the EU population** across **more than 75% of the territory**; forests and wooded land cover **nearly 44%**.
- **First dated milestone**: the **first AgRI 2040 conference, Q3 2026** — this quarter. Then areas of action under AgRI 2040 **Q4 2027**; Food 2040 R&I activities **Q3 2028**; first Food 2040 high-level conference **Q4 2028**.
- **19 actions in Annex 1**, each with a starting quarter. Nothing in the Communication is a deadline for business.
- **Companion act, same OJ day**: **Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1978** of 8 September 2026 (CELEX **32026D1978**, OJ L 2026/1978 of 9.9.2026) **sets up the European Digital Infrastructure Consortium for Agri-Food (EDIC for Agri-Food)**.
- Lead DGs: **DG AGRI** and **DG RTD**. The Standing Committee on Agricultural Research (SCAR) and the JRC both carry named actions.
- Funding vehicles named: **Horizon Europe**, the **European Competitiveness Fund (ECF)**, **EIT Food**, the **European Innovation Council (EIC)** — all *without prejudice to the next MFF 2028-2034*.

## What the strategy actually is

A **Communication**, i.e. a policy document addressed to the Parliament, the Council, the EESC and the
Committee of the Regions. It is not a Regulation or a Directive, creates no obligation on any farmer,
food business or research organisation, and confers no rights. Its practical effect is that it steers
what future **work programmes** will fund and how.

Do not describe it as legislation, and do not attach compliance deadlines to it.

## Six cross-cutting priorities

These guide the implementation of both frameworks:

1. **Competitiveness, startups and scaleups** — an R&I ecosystem connecting researchers, farmers, foresters, rural actors, industry, startups and other SMEs.
2. **Food security, resilience and preparedness** — aligned with the European Preparedness Union Strategy: early warning, an all-hazards approach, stress-testing and foresight; EU production and supply of proteins, vitamins and essential nutrients; links to the Action Plan on the EU protein system.
3. **Natural resources and circularity** — climate-smart solutions, biodiversity and ecosystem function, moving from linear to circular systems, preventing food loss and waste. Aligned with the EU bioeconomy strategy, the Nature Restoration Regulation and the upcoming **Circular Economy Act**.
4. **Food and feed safety, nutrition and health** — guided where relevant by the **One Health** approach.
5. **Fairness** — including a fairer position for primary producers in the value chain.
6. **Global cooperation** for sustainable agriculture, forestry, rural areas and food systems — including the **African Union-EU roadmap on Food and Nutrition Security and Sustainable Agriculture** and possible work with the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States.

## Three common enablers

- **Governance** — coordination across sectors, disciplines, stakeholders and decision levels; inclusive engagement, youth participation, gender equality, skills.
- **Technologies**
- **Territories and communities**

## EDIC for Agri-Food (Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1978)

Adopted the same day and published in **OJ L 2026/1978 of 9 September 2026**, CELEX **32026D1978**.

- **Legal basis**: Article 14(3)(a) of **Decision (EU) 2022/2481** establishing the **Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030**, which empowers the Commission to set up **European Digital Infrastructure Consortia (EDICs)**.
- **Applicants (13 February 2026)**: Austria, Croatia, Finland, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Romania, Slovenia and the region of Flanders. **Poland joined on 8 June 2026.**
- **Observers**: Germany, Ireland, Latvia, Spain.
- **Host Member State: France**, which recognises the EDIC as an international body for VAT purposes under Article 143(1)(g) and Article 151(1)(b) of Directive 2006/112/EC, and as an international organisation under Article 11(1)(b) of Directive (EU) 2020/262.
- **Mission (per its Statutes)**: enhance the European agri-food digital ecosystem through EU-wide interoperable, use-case-based deployment actions.

An EDIC is an implementation vehicle for a **multi-country project** under the Digital Decade, not a
funding programme and not a regulator.

## What to say when asked

- If asked **what obligations it creates**: none. It is a Communication.
- If asked **when it starts**: the first dated item is the **first AgRI 2040 conference in Q3 2026**; everything else runs 2027-2028.
- If asked **how it will be funded**: Horizon Europe and the European Competitiveness Fund are named, but the Communication says repeatedly that it is *without prejudice to the next MFF* — do not state a budget figure for it, because it does not carry one.
