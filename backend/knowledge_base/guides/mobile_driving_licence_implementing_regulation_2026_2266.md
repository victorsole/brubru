# Mobile driving licences: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2266 (published 14 October 2026)

## QUICK FACTS
- **Act:** **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2266 of 13 October 2026** "laying down detailed provisions for the application of Directive (EU) 2025/2205 as regards mobile driving licences", published in the OJ (L series) on **14 October 2026**. It enters into force on the **twentieth day after publication, that is 3 November 2026**. It is the implementing act the Directive asked for by 26 November 2026 (Article 5(7)). Parent act: `eu_driving_licences_directive_2025_2205`.
- **Format and data (Annex I):** mobile driving licences are issued in **ISO/IEC mdoc format** (as specified in Annex II to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2979), with the data model of **ISO/IEC 18013-5:2021, clause 7, with listed adaptations** to match the Directive's data fields (for example family name and given names in Latin-1 characters, maximum 150 characters).
- **Issuance (Article 3):** as **qualified electronic attestations of attributes**, or as attestations issued by or for a public body responsible for an authentic source, under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, using the protocols and interfaces of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2982 to issue to **European Digital Identity Wallets**. The issuing authority must keep the data accurate.
- **Verification (Article 4):** with the technical means of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014; Member States may also use the **EU driving licence network**. Issuers must also tell the Commission about a security incident affecting issuance or validity if the Commission would not otherwise be told.
- **Validity and revocation (Article 6):** any technical validity period is allowed; if it is **longer than one hour** the issuer must revoke the licence when the wallet unit attestation is revoked, on exchange or replacement in another State, when the right to drive is cancelled, withdrawn, suspended or restricted, or on a confiscation notice.
- **Confiscation:** handled through the EU driving licence network; the issuing State revokes (if validity exceeds one hour), informs the holder and, on request, issues a new mobile licence carrying a note that states the date, the confiscating State and the period of effect.
- **Exchange (Articles 8 and 9):** the exchanging State checks in which formats the applicant holds the licence, notifies the issuing State, which revokes the mobile licence; for third-country licences the exchanging State asks the third country to revoke the mobile format and records it in its national register.
- **Appearance and notifications (Articles 5 and 7):** Member States tell the Commission their visual design rules, which the Commission publishes; Member States send the Commission the data on their issuing authorities **by 3 November 2027** (Annex II).
- **Not in the text:** no date by which each Member State must launch a mobile licence; the Directive's own dates govern (see parent guide).
