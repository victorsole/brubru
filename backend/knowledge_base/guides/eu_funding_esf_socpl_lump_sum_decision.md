# ESF+ Social Prerogatives and Presidency Lump Sum Decision

## QUICK FACTS

- Legal basis: Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 2018/1046 (Financial Rules); implemented for ESF+ and Erasmus+ programmes under DG EMPL (Directorate General for Employment, Social Affairs and Inclusion)
- Decision date: 31 May 2021 (Ref. Ares(2021)3568602)
- Scope: Lump sum financing for networking events organised by the Presidency of the Council of the EU for the period 2021-2027
- Applies to: Public authorities or entities mandated by them as beneficiaries under the Prerogatives and Specific Competences budget lines
- Event types covered: Networking events such as Senior Labour Inspectors Committee (SLIC), Public Employment Services (PES), Directors General for Vocational Education and Training (DG VET), and similar recurring events
- Fixed amount: EUR 16,000 per event for structural and preparation costs
- Variable amount: EUR 260 per participant (estimated at application stage)
- Maximum grant cap: EUR 50,000 per event
- Payment trigger: Receipt of signed participants' list and outcome report
- Reduction clause: Variable component reduced proportionally if actual participants fall below estimated by more than 10%; fixed component never reduced

## Programme context (ESF+ Social Prerogatives and Presidency)

The European Social Fund Plus (ESF+) is the primary instrument for supporting employment, social inclusion, and human capital development across the European Union. The Presidency of the Council of the EU organises regular networking events every six months as part of its rotating mandate.

These events bring together high-level national representatives to discuss joint policy priorities and implementation challenges. The key constituencies include Senior Labour Inspectors, Public Employment Services managers, vocational education and training directors, and social protection officers who work on transversal EU policy matters.

This lump sum decision sits within the broader simplification agenda of the 2021-2027 Multi-Annual Financial Framework (MFF), which prioritises reducing administrative burden whilst maintaining sound financial management.

## What the lump sum decision covers

The Decision authorises lump sum financing exclusively (no actual cost reimbursement) for the organisation and implementation of Presidency networking events under:

- ESF+ Regulation 2021/1057
- Erasmus+ (education and training strand)
- Prerogatives and Specific Competences budget lines of DG EMPL

The eligible cost categories are:

- Staff costs and costs of goods and services needed for the preparation, organisation, and follow-up of the event (printing, mailing, communication)
- Room rentals
- Interpretation costs
- Accommodation and catering costs during the event

No other cost categories are eligible under this lump sum arrangement.

## Action categories and lump sum amounts

The Decision establishes lump sum financing for two principal categories of Presidency networking events:

### Standard Networking Events (under lump sum terms)

Regular networking events bringing together senior officials from national administrations to discuss shared policy priorities. Historical examples include:

- Senior Labour Inspectors Committee (SLIC)
- Public Employment Services (PES)
- Directors General for Vocational Education and Training (DG VET)
- Mutual Information System on Social Protection (MISSOC)

These events are characterised by:

- Average duration: approximately 2 days per event
- Average attendance: approximately 84 participants per event per day
- Average preparation time: 59.5 organisation days per event (across preparation, delivery, and follow-up)

### High-Level Conferences (excluded from this decision)

Ministerial or Commissioner-level conferences (involving ministers, Commissioners, or Directors-General) are **not** covered by this Decision, as each is considered a unique, bespoke event requiring tailored costing.

### Lump sum formula

The Commission set the lump sum based on historical cost analysis from 16 events across nine EU Presidencies (2014-2020 period):

**Fixed component (structural costs):**

- Organisation costs: EUR 10,681
- Room rentals: EUR 3,763
- Interpretation: EUR 2,785
- Preparation and follow-up: EUR 1,874
- **Total fixed lump sum: EUR 16,000**

**Variable component (per participant):**

- Hotel (2 days): EUR 162
- Meals and coffee (2 days): EUR 151
- **Total unit cost per participant: EUR 260** (rounded from EUR 262)

## How the lump sum is paid (work package completion under lump sum management)

The lump sum grant agreement follows a simple output-based payment model:

### Grant Determination

The single lump sum is determined at award stage based on:

- The fixed amount of EUR 16,000 (applied to all qualifying events)
- The variable amount of EUR 260 multiplied by the estimated number of participants stated in the applicant's proposal

Total awarded amount: Fixed + (Variable unit cost x Estimated participants), capped at a maximum of EUR 50,000.

### Payment conditions

The lump sum is paid following receipt of:

1. A signed list of actual participants who attended the event
2. A report on the outcome of the event

No detailed cost documentation, financial statements, or cost reporting is required from the beneficiary.

### Adjustment for actual attendance

The variable component of the grant is adjusted based on actual participant numbers:

- **No reduction** if actual participants are between 90 per cent and 100 per cent of estimated (within 10 per cent tolerance margin)
- **Proportional reduction** if actual participants fall below 90 per cent of estimated; the lump sum is cut by the percentage difference beyond the 10 per cent threshold
  - Example: if estimated 100 participants but only 89 attend, no reduction applies (within tolerance). If only 88 attend, reduction is 2 per cent (100 minus 88 equals 12 per cent; 12 minus 10 per cent threshold equals 2 per cent reduction).
- **Fixed component (EUR 16,000) is never reduced**, regardless of actual attendance

### Future application

The Commission may apply the same lump sum approach to other types of Presidency networking events created in future, following an equivalent cost analysis methodology.

## Eligible beneficiaries

Eligible beneficiaries under this Decision are:

- **Public authorities** at national, regional, or local level
- **Entities mandated by public authorities** to carry out the networking event activities

This restriction to public or publicly-mandated entities is intentional, as it:

- Reduces administrative burden and fraud risk (these are low-risk, stable entities with robust accounting systems)
- Ensures beneficiaries are focused on the output of the event rather than detailed cost recovery
- Aligns with the nature and status of applicants as primary decision-makers in European employment and social policy networks

Private sector entities are not eligible.

**Key clarifications this guide provides:**

- Maximum grant is capped at EUR 50,000 per event
