## CORE REGULATORY STACK
- **Digital Services Act** (Reg 2022/2065, CELEX 32022R2065) — hosting provider, local neighbourhood UGC. Not a VLOP but DSA full stack applies (notice-action, T&Cs, minor protection — though user base skews adults).
- **GDPR + ePrivacy** (Reg 2016/679, CELEX 32016R0679; Dir 2002/58, CELEX 32002L0058) — **verified-address model** (home address is personal data); location advertising. Profile verification process creates Art 32 security obligations.
- **Political Advertising Regulation** (Reg 2024/900) — non-partisan voter resources collaboration (Vote.org, Democracy Works) in US may qualify in EU context. Ad targeting near polls → obligations. Applied 10 October 2025 for transparency; full 10 April 2026.
- **AI Act** (Reg 2024/1689, CELEX 32024R1689) — ML-based content moderation, "Kindness Reminder" prompts, algorithmic feed ranking. Prohibitions 2 Feb 2025; GPAI 2 Aug 2025.
- **Digital Fairness Act** — ad-based revenue model; dark patterns around ad preferences.
- **P2B Regulation** (Reg 2019/1150, CELEX 32019R1150) — local business listings, small-business advertising partners.
- **Consumer protection (CRD + Omnibus)** (Dir 2011/83 + Dir 2019/2161) — Nextdoor for Business paid services.
- **NIS2** (Dir 2022/2555, CELEX 32022L2555) — Annex I Sector 8 social networking services platform.
- **Cyber Resilience Act** (Reg 2024/2847, CELEX 32024R2847) — Nextdoor app.
- **TERREG** (Reg 2021/784, CELEX 32021R0784) — low volume but applies.
- **Representative Actions** (Dir 2020/1828, CELEX 32020L1828) — neighbourhood data / profiling claims pathway.
- **European Accessibility Act** (Dir 2019/882, CELEX 32019L0882) — paid features; app UI.
- **CSAM** — adult-focused but DSA Art 28 minor-protection still applies.
- **Law Enforcement Directive** (Dir 2016/680, CELEX 32016L0680) — Nextdoor's police partnership programme requires LED compliance for EU police data sharing.

## NATIONAL REGULATORY TOUCHPOINTS (decentralised model)
- Nextdoor operates in verticals **local to each MS** — neighbourhoods, municipal authorities, police community engagement. This creates fragmented regulatory exposure:
  - **Germany:** BfDI + BKA + state DPAs
  - **France:** CNIL + Arcom
  - **Netherlands:** AP + AT Digitale Infrastructuur
  - **Spain:** AEPD + CNMC
  - **Italy:** Garante + AGCOM
  - **Ireland:** DPC (as likely DSC)
- No single Irish-style buffer; each MS regulator engages Nextdoor independently.
