# European Committee of the Regions plenary, 14 October 2026: eleven opinions reported on 14 October (cities, transport, IAA, cars, bioeconomy and more)

## QUICK FACTS
The CoR (the EU's assembly of local and regional representatives; its opinions are advisory) reported these opinions as adopted at its plenary session on **14 October 2026** (eleven items; anything adopted later in the session was not read). Read from the CoR's own news items: the lead of each, one (Agenda for Cities) in full. Rapporteurs as the CoR names them (country/political group).
- **EU Agenda for Cities:** rapporteur **Kieran McCarthy (IE/EA)**, Cork City Council; **adopted unanimously**. Asks for real simplification, continuous dialogue between all levels of government and legal guarantees for cities' role in designing future EU spending programmes; territorial cohesion between urban and rural areas at its core.
- **Bioeconomy strategy:** **Karl Vanlouwe (BE/EA)**, unanimously: stronger regional role in the EU's revised bioeconomy framework; the bioeconomy already counts **17.1 million jobs and EUR 863 billion** value added (CoR's figures).
- **Transport gaps and the "right to stay":** **Marco Marsilio (IT/ECR)**, President of Abruzzo, unanimously: investment beyond big cities and main corridors, less transport poverty, technology-neutral alternative fuels.
- **EU Anti-Racism Strategy 2026-2030:** **Pär Löfstrand (SE/Renew Europe)**: backs the strategy; racism must be tackled together with poverty and social exclusion.
- **Research and Technology Infrastructures:** **Markku Markkula (FI/EPP)**: RTIs as strategic assets for sovereignty and resilience.
- **Environmental objectives:** **Stefania Proietti (IT/PES)**, President of Umbria: regions need more resources to deliver them.
- **Atlantic macro-regional strategy** ("An Atlantic Macro-region"): **Ander Caballero Barturen (ES/Renew Europe)**: unite sea and land.
- **Common Market Organisation and EU School Scheme:** **Alessio Mammi (IT/PES)**, unanimously: a stronger CMO for farmers.
- **New European Bauhaus, from vision to implementation:** **Tsvetelina Simeonova-Zarkin (BG/Renew Europe)**: local leadership, citizens and youth.
- **Industrial Accelerator Act:** the Act must not widen territorial gaps (rapporteur not named in the lead read).
- **Automotive Package:** **María Chivite (ES/PES)**: investment for the transition to electric vehicles; debate with the Automotive Regions Alliance chair.
- **Also announced:** **Duisburg, North-East Romania and Umbria** won the **2027 European Entrepreneurial Region** award.
- **What was NOT read:** the opinions' texts and their CoR document numbers; vote counts except "unanimously" where stated.
