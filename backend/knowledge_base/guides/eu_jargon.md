# EU Jargon Glossary

Essential terminology for EU policy professionals.

## Council & COREPER

**Antici Group** - Named after Italian diplomat Paolo Antici. Prepares COREPER II meetings (Permanent Representatives/Ambassadors) which handle foreign affairs, economic/financial affairs, and justice/home affairs. Senior advisors who preview agendas and identify political sensitivities.

**Mertens Group** - Named after Belgian diplomat Vincent Mertens. Prepares COREPER I meetings (Deputy Permanent Representatives) which handle technical/sectoral issues like environment, transport, employment, internal market. Equivalent to Antici but for COREPER I.

**COREPER** - Committee of Permanent Representatives. Two formations:
- COREPER II: Ambassadors (political/horizontal files)
- COREPER I: Deputies (technical/sectoral files)

**Council Configurations** - The Council meets in 10 different formations depending on policy area: General Affairs, Foreign Affairs, Economic/Financial (ECOFIN), Agriculture/Fisheries, Environment, Transport/Telecom/Energy, Employment/Social, Competitiveness, Education/Youth/Culture, Justice/Home Affairs.

**Council Working Parties** - 150+ technical groups of national experts who refine proposals before they reach COREPER or ministers. The real technical negotiation happens here.

**Trio Presidency** - Three consecutive Council presidencies cooperating on an 18-month programme. Current system ensures continuity across rotating 6-month presidencies.

**A-points / B-points** - A-points are items agreed at working party level, adopted without discussion. B-points require ministerial discussion.

## European Parliament

**APA (Accredited Parliamentary Assistant)** - Staff supporting MEPs under direct EP contract, based in Brussels, Strasbourg, or Luxembourg. Handle research, speechwriting, legislative drafting, scheduling.

**Group Coordinator** - MEP responsible for coordinating their political group's position within a committee. Key gatekeepers for amendments and voting strategies.

**Rapporteur** - MEP tasked with drafting reports on legislative proposals. Negotiates with shadow rapporteurs from other groups and represents EP in trilogue.

**Shadow Rapporteur** - MEPs from other political groups who follow a file and negotiate with the rapporteur on their group's behalf.

**Intergroups** - Informal cross-party groupings of MEPs interested in specific topics (e.g., Digital Agenda, Animal Welfare, LGBTI Rights). Not official bodies but influential.

**Part-Session** - Plenary sitting periods. Main plenaries in Strasbourg (typically 4 days/month), mini-plenaries in Brussels (typically 2 days).

## Legislative Process

**OLP (Ordinary Legislative Procedure)** - Standard co-decision procedure where EP and Council must agree. Multiple readings possible, usually resolved at first reading via trilogue.

**Trilogue** - Informal negotiations between EP, Council, and Commission to reach agreement before formal readings. Where most deals are made.

**INI Report (Own-Initiative Report)** - Non-legislative EP report on topics where Parliament wants to influence policy without a Commission proposal.

**Delegated Act** - Commission act amending/supplementing non-essential elements of legislation. EP and Council can object within set deadline (usually 2 months + possible 2-month extension).

**Implementing Act** - Commission act specifying how EU law should be applied. Subject to comitology (member state scrutiny) but less political control than delegated acts.

**Impact Assessment** - Commission analysis before proposing legislation. Examines economic, social, environmental effects. Published alongside proposals.

**Inter-service Consultation (ISC)** - Internal Commission process where all relevant DGs review draft proposals before adoption. Ensures policy coherence.

## Commission

**DG (Directorate-General)** - Commission departments organised by policy area (e.g., DG CLIMA for climate, DG COMP for competition).

**College of Commissioners** - The 27 Commissioners who collectively adopt Commission decisions. Meets weekly on Wednesdays.

**Cabinet** - Private office of a Commissioner. Head of Cabinet is key power broker.

**Chef de file** - Lead DG responsible for a file when multiple DGs are involved.

**Liste de Point Prévu** - Provisional agenda of upcoming Commission meetings.

## Budget & Finance

**MFF (Multiannual Financial Framework)** - EU's 7-year budget framework setting spending ceilings per policy area. Current MFF: 2021-2027.

**Own Resources** - EU's revenue sources: customs duties, VAT-based contributions, GNI-based contributions, plastic levy.

**Common Provisions Regulation (CPR)** - Governs spending of structural funds (ERDF, ESF+, Cohesion Fund, EMFAF, AMIF, ISF, BMVI).

## Other Key Terms

**Acquis (communautaire)** - The accumulated body of EU law that candidate countries must adopt to join the EU.

**Subsidiarity** - Principle that EU should only act when objectives cannot be sufficiently achieved by member states.

**Proportionality** - Principle that EU action should not exceed what is necessary to achieve objectives.

**CELEX Number** - Unique identifier for EU legal documents (e.g., 32016R0679 for GDPR).

**Comitology** - System of committees through which member states oversee Commission implementing powers.
