## 9. Risk Management and Contingencies

### 9.1 Potential Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Speaker cancellation | Medium | High | Have backup speakers confirmed |
| Low attendance | Medium | High | Multiple invitation waves, phone calls to key invitees |
| Technical failure | Low | Medium | Backup equipment, technical person on-site |
| Catering issues | Low | Low | Confirm 48h before, have contact on speed dial |
| MEP patronage withdrawn | Low | High | External venue as backup |
| Protest/disruption | Low | Medium | Liaise with security, have response plan |

### 9.2 Contingency Plans

**If Key Speaker Cancels:**
- Backup speaker: [Name, pre-confirmed]
- Adjust programme timing
- Notify attendees if major speaker

**If Attendance is Low:**
- Adjust room setup to smaller configuration
- Reduce catering orders (if possible)
- More intimate discussion format

**If Technical Problems:**
- Backup equipment ready
- Can proceed without AV if necessary
- Print key slides as handouts

**If Patron MEP Cancels:**
- Book external venue (keep quote ready)
- Find alternative MEP patron
- Adjust date if necessary
