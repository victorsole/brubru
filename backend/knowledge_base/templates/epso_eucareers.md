## Introduction to EPSO and EU Careers

### What is EPSO?

The **European Personnel Selection Office (EPSO)** was established in 2002 as the centralised HR agency for the European Union. EPSO serves as the recruitment gateway for most EU institutions and agencies, selecting qualified candidates through competitive examinations and creating reserve lists from which institutions can recruit staff.

**Key Facts:**
- EPSO receives approximately **60,000-70,000 applications per year**
- Around **1,500-2,000 candidates are recruited** by EU institutions annually
- Serves all major EU institutions: European Parliament, European Commission, European Council, Council of the EU, Court of Justice, Court of Auditors, EEAS, and more
- Based in Brussels, Belgium

### EU Institutions Employment Overview

The EU institutions employ over **60,000 people from the 27 EU Member States**, working in various locations:
- **Brussels** (Belgium) - Main location for Commission, Parliament, and Council
- **Luxembourg** - Court of Justice, Court of Auditors, and various agencies
- **Strasbourg** (France) - European Parliament sessions
- **Throughout the EU** - Decentralised agencies and representations
- **Worldwide** - EU delegations in over 140 countries

### Why Work for the EU?

**Career Benefits:**
- Work on major issues affecting 500+ million European citizens
- Climate change, migration, safety, security, trade, digital policy, and more
- Competitive salaries with comprehensive benefits package
- Multicultural, multilingual working environment
- Career mobility across different services and institutions
- Professional development and language training opportunities
- Job security (for permanent positions)
- Work-life balance with 24-30 days annual leave

**Impact:**
- Participate directly in EU legislative processes
- Shape policies that affect Europe and beyond
- Contribute to European integration and values
- Engage in international negotiations and diplomacy

## Becoming an EU Official: Permanent Staff

### Overview of Permanent Staff Categories

EU permanent officials (fonctionnaires) are recruited through **open competitions** organised by EPSO. There are three main categories:

#### 1. Administrators (AD)

**Grades:** AD 5 to AD 16
- **AD 5**: Entry level for university graduates
- **AD 6-7**: Specialist profiles with several years of experience
- **AD 15-16**: Director General level

**Typical Duties:**
- Policy development and analysis
- Drafting EU legislation and legal texts
- Regulatory tasks and compliance
- International negotiations
- Stakeholder engagement
- Managing EU-wide programmes
- Economic and financial analysis
- Communication and public affairs
- Scientific research coordination

**Qualifications Required:**
- University degree (minimum 3 years of higher education - Bachelor's level)
- For specialist profiles (AD 6-7): additional experience required
- Proficiency in at least 2 EU official languages

**Starting Salary (2025):**
- AD 5, Step 1: approximately €5,300 gross per month (basic salary)
- With allowances: €6,500-8,000+ depending on personal situation

#### 2. Assistants (AST)

**Grades:** AST 1 to AST 11
- **AST 1**: Entry level with secondary education
- **AST 3**: Entry level with vocational qualification and/or experience

**Typical Duties:**
- Executive and technical support roles
- Finance and budgetary support
- IT support and systems administration
- Research assistance
- Policy implementation support
- Administrative coordination
- Human resources support

**Qualifications Required:**
- **AST 1**: Secondary education + relevant experience OR vocational qualification
- **AST 3**: Secondary education + vocational qualification and/or several years' experience
- Proficiency in at least 2 EU official languages

**Starting Salary (2025):**
- AST 1, Step 1: approximately €3,400 gross per month (basic salary)
- AST 3, Step 1: approximately €4,400 gross per month (basic salary)

#### 3. Secretaries/Clerks (AST/SC)

**Grades:** AST/SC 1 to AST/SC 6
- **AST/SC 1-2**: Entry levels

**Typical Duties:**
- Office management and administrative support
- Document preparation and filing
- Meeting coordination and minute-taking
- Diary management
- Database management and information compilation
- General secretarial work

**Qualifications Required:**
- **AST/SC 1**: Secondary education + relevant experience OR vocational qualification
- **AST/SC 2**: For more experienced candidates
- Proficiency in at least 2 EU official languages

**Starting Salary (2025):**
- AST/SC 1, Step 1: approximately €3,000 gross per month (basic salary)

### Career Progression

**Promotion System:**
- Based on merit and performance evaluations
- Average progression from AD5 to AD14 (Director level): 35-40 years
- Regular step increases within grades (every 2 years typically)
- Opportunities for internal mobility across services and institutions

**Reality Check:**
- Promotion prospects can be slow, especially at higher levels
- Many officials spend years at the same grade
- Internal competitions available for career advancement
- Management experience harder to gain than in private sector

## EPSO Exam Structure and Selection Process

### Competition Types

EPSO organises two main types of selections:

1. **Open Competitions** - For permanent officials (AD, AST, AST/SC)
2. **CAST Permanent** - For contract agents (covered in separate section)

### EPSO Selection Process for Permanent Officials

#### Stage 1: Application Phase

**Notice of Competition (NoC):**
- Published on EU Careers website and Official Journal
- Contains all essential information:
  - Job profile and duties
  - Number of reserve list places
  - Qualifications and experience required
  - Selection procedure and test format
  - Eligibility criteria
  - Language requirements
  - Application deadlines

**Application System:**
- **New 2025:** Single Candidate Portal for competitions published after 1 January 2025
- For older competitions: EPSO Account still required
- Create account and complete detailed application form
- Upload required documents (CV, diplomas, certificates)

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- EU citizenship (for most positions)
- Full rights as EU citizen
- Fulfilment of military service obligations
- Character requirements met
- Specific educational and professional requirements per competition

#### Stage 2: Computer-Based Tests (CBT)

**Test Components:**

**1. Reasoning Skills Tests** (Multiple-choice)
- **Verbal Reasoning**: Text comprehension, logical deduction from written information
- **Numerical Reasoning**: Data interpretation, mathematical problem-solving
- **Abstract Reasoning**: Pattern recognition, logical thinking

**Format:**
- Timed tests with strict limits
- Pass/fail threshold (typically 50% correct answers minimum)
- Results used for ranking candidates

**2. Situational Judgement Test (SJT)** (For some competitions)
- Assesses how you handle workplace scenarios
- Evaluates competencies relevant to the role

**3. Field-Specific Knowledge Test** (For specialist competitions)
- **EU Knowledge Test**: EU institutions, legislative procedures, policy areas (for Generalist AD5)
- **Specialist Tests**: Technical knowledge in specific fields (law, economics, science, etc.)
- Format varies: multiple-choice or ranking questions

**Testing Method (2025):**
- **Remote proctored exams** from home (since 2023)
- Automated proctoring system
- Candidates responsible for their setup (hardware, network, environment)
- Alternative: Test centre options in some cases

**Important Note:**
- You must pass the minimum threshold on ALL tests to proceed
- High scores are crucial as results determine ranking on reserve list

#### Stage 3: Written Test (EUFTE or Field-Specific)

**Two Weeks Before Exam:**
- EPSO publishes background document on general EU matters (EUFTE) or field-specific topic
- Candidates study the document thoroughly

**On Exam Day:**
- Receive assignment/task based on the background document
- **Time Limit**: Typically 90 minutes
- Must write a structured response (briefing note, analysis, policy paper, etc.)

**Assessment Criteria:**
1. **Clear Structure**: Logical organisation with introduction, body, conclusion
2. **Factual Support**: Use of information from background document
3. **Concise Writing**: Direct, efficient communication
4. **Appropriate Tone**: Professional and suitable for the audience
5. **Effective Use of Information**: Relevant selection and application of provided data

**Competencies Assessed:**
- Written Communication Skills
- Analysis and Problem-Solving
- Prioritising and Organising
- Delivering Quality & Results

**Pass Mark:**
- Must reach minimum threshold to continue
- Score contributes to final ranking

#### Stage 4: Assessment Centre (For some competitions)

**Components:**
- **Structured Interview**: Competency-based questions about experience and skills
- **Oral Presentation**: Present on a topic, demonstrate communication skills
- **Group Exercise**: Collaborative problem-solving with other candidates
- **In-Tray/E-Tray Exercise**: Prioritise and respond to workplace scenarios

**Competencies Assessed:**
- Leadership
- Analysis and Problem Solving
- Communicating
- Delivering Quality and Results
- Working with Others
- Resilience
- Prioritising and Organising
- Learning and Development

**Note:** Assessment Centre stage depends on the competition type. Many AD5 competitions since 2023 do NOT include Assessment Centre.

#### Stage 5: Reserve List

**Success:**
- Candidates who pass all stages are placed on a **Reserve List**
- Listed in order of merit (highest scores first)
- Reserve lists typically valid for **1-3 years** (with possible extension)
- Published on EPSO website

**Recruitment:**
- EU institutions recruit directly from the reserve list
- Being on reserve list does NOT guarantee a job
- Higher ranking = better chances of being recruited quickly
- Candidates can remain on reserve list until it expires

**Statistics:**
- Thousands apply per competition
- Typically only 5-10% make it to reserve list
- Most successful candidates need 2-3 attempts before getting on a list

### EPSO's Competency Framework

EPSO assesses candidates on 8 core competencies (as of 2025):

1. **Analysis and Problem Solving**
2. **Communicating**
3. **Delivering Quality and Results**
4. **Learning and Development**
5. **Prioritising and Organising**
6. **Resilience**
7. **Working with Others**
8. **Leadership** (for management positions)

### 2025 EPSO Generalist Administrators (AD5) Competition

**The Big One:**
- Most anticipated competition - first since 2019
- Originally scheduled for July 2025, published early 2026
- Open to graduates from any academic background
- Gateway to EU administrator careers

**Test Structure (2025 Format):**
1. **Reasoning Skills**: Verbal, Numerical, Abstract
2. **EU Knowledge Test**: Institutions, procedures, policies (NEW format - ranking test)
3. **Written Test (EUFTE)**: Based on background document about general EU matters

**No Assessment Centre** for AD5 Generalist 2025

**Preparation Time Recommended:**
- **Minimum**: 3-6 months of regular practice
- **Ideal**: 1 year with consistent preparation
- 1 hour daily or 10 hours weekly recommended

### Key Changes in 2025

**New Test Provider:**
- EPSO announced new test provider (OAT) in June 2025
- Transitional period for implementation
- May affect test format and delivery

**Single Candidate Portal:**
- All competitions from January 2025 onwards use new application system
- More streamlined and user-friendly
- Created with support from DG HR and DIGIT

**Remote Proctoring:**
- Standard testing method since 2023
- No need to travel to test centres
- Automated proctoring for security

## Traineeship Programmes

### Blue Book Traineeship Programme (European Commission)

#### Overview

The **Blue Book Traineeship**, established in 1960, is the European Commission's flagship traineeship programme. It offers approximately **1,000 positions twice per year** for five-month paid internships in EU policymaking and administration.

**Key Features:**
- **Duration**: 5 months (March-July or October-February sessions)
- **Paid**: Yes, with monthly grant
- **Location**: Mainly Brussels and Luxembourg, some positions in Member State representations
- **Highly Competitive**: ~2,500 applicants shortlisted from tens of thousands

#### Traineeship Types

**1. Administrative Traineeship**
- Work across Commission's Directorate-Generals (DGs), services, and agencies
- Fields include:
  - Competition law
  - Environmental policy
  - Human resources
  - Digital policy
  - Agriculture and rural development
  - External relations
  - Economic and financial affairs
  - Research and innovation
  - Justice and home affairs
  - All other EU policy areas

**2. Translation Traineeship (DGT)**
- With Directorate-General for Translation
- Specialised language work
- Translation projects in various policy areas

**3. Scientific Traineeship (JRC)**
- At Joint Research Centre
- Research-based positions
- Scientific and technical fields

#### Eligibility Criteria

**Who Can Apply:**
- **All EU citizens** (no age limit)
- **Limited number of non-EU nationals** from:
  - Candidate countries
  - Other countries (limited spots, including Eastern Partnership)

**Educational Requirements:**
- **Completed** 3-year university degree (minimum Bachelor's/EQF Level 6)
- Certificate or official confirmation from university required
- Degree must be awarded BEFORE application deadline

**Language Requirements:**
- Proficiency in **at least 2 EU official languages**
- One must be **English, French, or German**
- Non-EU nationals: Very good knowledge of English, French, or German

**Experience Requirements:**
- **No prior work experience** in EU institutions exceeding 6 weeks
- Recent graduates and early-career professionals ideal candidates

**Other Requirements:**
- Open-minded approach to European issues
- Interest in Commission's work
- Proactive attitude
- Willingness to work in multicultural environment

#### Application Process

**Application Period:**
- **March Session (1 March - 31 July):** Applications open July-August (previous year)
  - Next session: Applications 15 July - 30 August 2025
- **October Session (1 October - 28/29 February):** Applications open February-March
  - Next session: Applications 16 February - 16 March 2026 (at 10:00 Brussels time)

**Steps:**

1. **Create EU Login Account**
   - European Commission Authentication Service
   - Required for all applications

2. **Complete Application Form**
   - Personal information
   - Educational background
   - Language skills
   - Motivation
   - Preferences for DG/service placement

3. **Upload Supporting Documents** (PDF format, with table of contents):
   - **Valid passport or ID card**
   - **University degrees** and transcripts from ALL universities attended
   - **Proof of language skills** (excluding mother tongue):
     - University exchange programmes
     - EU/international programme participation
     - Seminars or workshops
     - Language certificates (not required but beneficial)
   - Additional supporting documents (optional):
     - Work certificates
     - Recommendation letters
     - Other relevant qualifications

**Important:**
- No IELTS or TOEFL required
- All documents can be in any EU official language
- **Recommended**: CV in English or French for better selection chances
- Documents cannot be added after deadline

#### Selection Process

**Stage 1: Application Evaluation**
- Pre-selection committee evaluates all applications
- Assessment based on:
  - Academic qualifications
  - Language skills
  - Motivation
  - International experience
  - Extracurricular activities

**Stage 2: Virtual Blue Book**
- **Top ~2,500-3,000 candidates** selected into Virtual Blue Book
- Official CV database accessible to all Commission services

**Stage 3: Final Selection**
- Individual DGs and services select trainees from Virtual Blue Book
- **Approximately 650-700 trainees per session** ultimately selected
- Selection rate: **5-6% of total applicants**

**Stage 4: Admission**
- Selected candidates receive official Admission Letter via email
- Must confirm acceptance
- Provide additional documents (medical certificate, criminal record)

#### Benefits and Grant

**Monthly Grant (2025):**
- **€1,418.20 - €1,490** per month (approximately 25% of entry-level EU official salary)
- Intended to cover living expenses
- Amount may vary by location

**Additional Benefits:**
- **Travel reimbursement**: Travel costs to/from place of work
- **Visa costs reimbursement**: If applicable for non-EU nationals
- **Medical expenses coverage**: Reimbursement of medical fees
- **Social security coverage**: Paid during traineeship
- **Certificate**: Official certificate from European Commission upon completion

**Important Notes:**
- Trainees must arrange own accommodation
- Grant is not taxable at EU level (check national tax rules)
- 40-hour work week (standard Commission hours: 8:00-19:00 with flexible arrangements)

#### Traineeship Experience

**Daily Work Includes:**
- Attending and organising meetings
- Working groups and public hearings
- Researching documents and policy papers
- Answering citizens' inquiries
- Supporting project management
- Drafting briefing notes and reports
- Participating in policy development

**Professional Development:**
- Welcome Conference and orientation sessions
- Networking events with other trainees
- Access to Commission training programmes
- Exposure to EU policymaking processes
- Mentoring from experienced staff

**Networking:**
- Meet trainees from all EU Member States and beyond
- Connect with Commission officials
- Attend inter-institutional events
- Build career network in EU affairs

#### Tips for Success

1. **Start Early**: Prepare application well before deadline
2. **Strong Motivation**: Clearly articulate why you want to work for the Commission
3. **Highlight EU Interest**: Demonstrate knowledge of EU policies and commitment to European values
4. **Language Skills**: Emphasize multilingual abilities
5. **International Experience**: Study abroad, volunteering, or international projects are assets
6. **Tailor Application**: Show genuine interest in specific DG or policy area
7. **Europass CV**: Use Europass format for CV
8. **Check Technical Issues**: Apply early to avoid last-minute server crashes (reported near deadlines)

### Schuman Traineeship Programme (European Parliament)

#### Overview

Named after **Robert Schuman**, one of the founding fathers of European integration, the Schuman Traineeships are five-month paid internships in the Secretariat of the European Parliament. Approximately **800 trainees per year** (400 per session) are selected.

**Key Features:**
- **Duration**: 5 months
- **Paid**: Yes, monthly grant varies by location
- **Location**: Brussels, Luxembourg, Strasbourg, or Parliament Liaison Offices in Member States
- **Two Sessions Per Year**:
  - **March Session** (1 March - 31 July)
  - **October Session** (1 October - 28/29 February)

#### Fields of Work

Schuman traineeships cover diverse areas:
- EU internal and external policies
- Finance and budgetary affairs
- Law and legal affairs
- Multilingualism and translation
- Administration
- Infrastructure and logistics
- Communication and press
- IT and digital services
- Research and analysis (EPRS - European Parliamentary Research Service)

**Special Traineeships:**
- **Ján Kuciak Traineeships**: In memory of murdered Slovak journalist, offered by DG Communication
- **EPRS Traineeships**: Research and analytical support positions

#### Eligibility Criteria

**Who Can Apply:**
- **EU Member State nationals** (priority)
- **Candidate/accession country nationals**
- **10% of positions** available to nationals from other countries

**Basic Requirements:**
- **Age**: At least 18 years old (no maximum age)
- **Education**: Full university degree of at least 3 years (Bachelor's level) - **must be completed BEFORE application deadline**
- **Language**: 
  - **EU citizens**: Thorough knowledge of one official EU language + very good knowledge of another EU language
  - **Non-EU citizens**: Very good knowledge of English, French, OR German
- **Criminal Record**: Clean criminal record (issued within 6 months before traineeship start)

**Exclusions:**
- Cannot have worked or benefited from traineeship in EU institution/body/agency for more than 2 consecutive months
- Cannot have completed study visit at Parliament Secretariat within 6 months before traineeship

#### Application Process

**Application Periods:**
- **March Session:** Applications 1 October - 31 October (previous year)
  - For 1 March 2026 - 31 July 2026
- **October Session:** Applications 1 May - 31 May
  - For 1 October 2025 - 28/29 February 2026

**Application Steps:**

1. **Access Application Portal**
   - Visit Parliament's traineeship application page
   - Create account or log in

2. **Select Traineeships**
   - Browse available positions
   - Apply for **maximum of 3 traineeships**
   - Prioritise your choices

3. **Upload Documents (if shortlisted)**
   - University degree(s) and transcripts
   - CV (Europass format recommended in English or French)
   - Motivation letter (in language of target position)
   - Language certificates
   - Criminal record (valid for 6 months)
   - Any additional supporting documents

**Important:**
- Documents can be in any EU official language
- Link to upload documents expires after **14 days** - prepare in advance!
- No spontaneous applications accepted

#### Selection Process

**Stage 1: Initial Screening**
- Applications reviewed by relevant Parliament services
- Assessment based on academic qualifications, languages, experience, motivation

**Stage 2: Shortlisting**
- Suitable candidates shortlisted
- Email invitation to provide supporting documents
- 14-day deadline to submit

**Stage 3: Final Selection**
- Individual services make final decisions
- Approximately **800 trainees selected per year** (400 per session)

**Stage 4: Admission**
- Selected candidates receive official Admission Letter via email
- Confirm acceptance
- Attend Welcome Session

#### Positive Action Scheme for Trainees with Disabilities

**Special Programme:**
- Priority for persons with long-term disability (physical, mental, intellectual, sensory) of **at least 20%**
- Greater opportunity to participate

**Benefits:**
- Additional payment of **up to 50% of monthly grant** to compensate for disability-related costs
- Reasonable accommodation measures
- Full access and participation support

**Application:**
- Apply through standard process
- Request consideration for Positive Action Scheme
- Provide medical documentation confirming disability

#### Monthly Grant (2025)

**Brussels:**
- **€1,339.40** per month (full-time)

**Luxembourg:**
- **€1,339.40** per month (full-time)

**Strasbourg:**
- Grant amount aligned with Brussels/Luxembourg

**Plus:**
- **Travel Allowance**: Covers expenses from home to place of work
- Grant not subject to EU tax (check national rules)
- Amount calculated based on location

#### Traineeship Experience

**Working Hours:**
- Full-time: 40 hours per week
- Flexible working hours within Parliament's core time

**Welcome and Orientation:**
- **Welcome Days**: Organised for all Schuman trainees in Brussels and Luxembourg
- Web-streamed for trainees in Strasbourg and Liaison Offices
- **European Commission Welcome Conference**: Also open to Schuman trainees

**Rights and Obligations:**
- Clear traineeship supervisor and coordinator assigned
- Regular feedback and guidance
- Confidentiality and ethical obligations
- Professional conduct expected

**Experience:**
- Participate in committee meetings and plenary sessions
- Research and policy analysis
- Support MEPs and Parliament services
- Attend workshops and seminars
- Network with Parliament staff and other trainees

#### Recruitment Pathway

**Schuman Recruitment and Development Programme:**
- High-performing trainees may be considered for this programme
- Potential pathway to employment in European Parliament
- Not guaranteed but possible for outstanding candidates

#### Tips for Application

1. **Complete Your Degree**: Must be finished BEFORE deadline - no exceptions
2. **Check Language Requirements**: Ensure you meet the language criteria
3. **Be Specific**: Tailor motivation letter to specific service/field
4. **European Engagement**: Show commitment to European affairs
5. **Prepare Documents Early**: Have all documents ready before shortlisting
6. **Multiple Applications**: Apply for 3 positions to increase chances
7. **Research Parliament Work**: Understand EP's role and current priorities

### Traineeships with Members of the European Parliament (MEPs)

**Alternative Traineeship:**
- Directly with MEP offices in Brussels or Strasbourg
- Duration: 6 weeks to 5 consecutive months
- Payment depends on individual MEP arrangement
- Contact MEPs directly - not through EPSO

**How to Apply:**
- Identify MEPs whose work interests you
- Contact their offices directly

## Contract Agents (CAST)

### Overview of Contract Agents

**Contract Agents** are non-permanent EU staff employed on **fixed-term contracts** for up to **6 years maximum**. They represent more than **one-fifth of European Commission staff** and are increasingly employed across all EU institutions and agencies.

**CAST stands for**: Contract Agent Selection Tool
**"CAST Permanent"**: Open-ended selection procedure (no application deadline) introduced in 2017

### Why CAST?

**Advantages:**
- **Open-ended registration**: Apply anytime, no competition deadlines
- **Faster entry** into EU institutions than open competitions
- **Good stepping stone**: Gain EU experience while preparing for permanent positions
- **Multiple opportunities**: Register for multiple profiles
- **Experience diverse roles**: Possibility to explore different function groups

**Realities:**
- **Not permanent**: Contracts limited to 6 years maximum
- **No guarantee**: Registration doesn't guarantee job offer
- **Competitive**: Still need to pass reasoning tests when invited
- **Career progression limited**: Difficult to advance to permanent official status without competition

### Function Groups (FG)

Contract agents are organised into **4 Function Groups** based on education and duties:

#### FG I - Manual and Administrative Support
- **Typical Roles**: Maintenance, drivers, security, basic admin tasks
- **Qualification**: Secondary education
- **Salary Range (2025)**: €2,200-3,200 gross monthly (approximate)

#### FG II - Clerical, Secretarial, Office Management
- **Typical Roles**: Office support, secretarial work, data entry, reception
- **Qualification**: Secondary education + vocational training/experience
- **Salary Range (2025)**: €2,600-3,800 gross monthly (approximate)

#### FG III - Executive and Technical Duties
- **Typical Roles**: Technical support, specialised admin, IT support, finance support
- **Qualification**: Post-secondary vocational training OR secondary education + relevant experience
- **Salary Range (2025)**: €3,200-4,800 gross monthly (approximate)

#### FG IV - Administrative, Advisory, Linguistic, Technical Tasks
- **Typical Roles**: Policy support, project management, communication, analysis, advisory roles, translation/interpretation support
- **Qualification**: University degree (3 years minimum)
- **Duties**: Equivalent to AD work but under supervision
- **Salary Range (2025)**: €4,200-6,500 gross monthly (approximate)

**Tip:** If eligible, register for **FG IV** (highest level) even if not immediately seeking a role - keeps options open.

### CAST Selection Procedure

#### Step 1: Registration in CAST Database

**How to Register:**
1. Visit [EU Careers CAST page](https://eu-careers.europa.eu/en)
2. Go to "Work for the EU" → "Contract staff selection procedures"
3. Select relevant CAST profile(s)
4. Complete online registration form
5. Upload CV and supporting documents

**Multiple Profiles:**
- Register for **as many profiles as you want**
- Each profile represents different field/specialisation
- Examples: Finance, IT, Communication, Policy, Project Management, HR, Legal, etc.

**What You Receive:**
- **CAST ID number**: Save this! Required for all job applications
- Confirmation email
- Profile stored in CAST database

**Important:**
- **Update profile every 6 months** to keep it active and searchable
- Otherwise, profile gets buried in database
- Outdated profiles = lower visibility to recruiters

#### Step 2: CV Selection by Recruiting Services

**How It Works:**
- When vacancy arises, EU institutions search CAST database
- HR professionals filter by relevant skills, experience, and qualifications
- **Only shortlisted candidates invited to take tests**
- **No invitation = no test opportunity**

**Key Factors for Selection:**
- Relevant qualifications
- Specific experience matching vacancy needs
- Language skills
- Europass CV format (recommended)
- Profile completeness and updates

**Reality:**
- You may wait weeks, months, or longer for invitation
- You may receive multiple invitations
- You may never be contacted if profile not matching needs
- Being in database ≠ automatic testing opportunity

#### Step 3: Invitation to Computer-Based Tests (CBT)

**When Invited:**
- Email from recruiting service
- Invitation to book testing slot
- Preparation time before test date

**Test Booking:**
- Select available testing window
- Remote proctored exam from home (since September 2023)
- Responsible for own setup (hardware, stable internet, quiet environment)

#### Step 4: EPSO CAST Reasoning Tests

**Test Components (as of 2025):**

**1. Verbal Reasoning**
- Text comprehension
- Logical deduction from written passages
- Multiple-choice format

**2. Numerical and Abstract Reasoning (Combined)**
- Data interpretation
- Pattern recognition
- Mathematical problem-solving
- Multiple-choice format

**Pass Requirements:**
- **Minimum required scores** on each test component
- Typically around 50% correct answers
- Exact thresholds published in each Call for Expression of Interest

**Important Changes (May 2023):**
- **Field-related competency tests removed** from EPSO testing
- EPSO now only handles reasoning skills
- Recruiting agencies may conduct separate field tests during recruitment phase

**Test Format (2025):**
| Test Component | Number of Questions | Time Limit | Pass Mark |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------|-----------|
| Verbal Reasoning | Varies by FG | Varies | ~50% |
| Numerical & Abstract | Varies by FG | Varies | ~50% |

(Exact numbers published in each specific call)

#### Step 5: Results

**Pass:**
- Results sent via email
- **Valid for 10 years** from test date
- Profile marked as "passed tests" in database
- Available for recruitment interviews

**Fail:**
- Can retake tests when invited again for another vacancy
- No limit on number of attempts
- Prepare better for next opportunity

#### Step 6: Recruitment Phase

**After Passing Tests:**
- Recruiting service reviews passed candidates
- Shortlist for interviews
- **Field-specific competency testing** (if required by recruiting agency)
- **Structured interviews**
- Reference checks

**Job Offer:**
- Contract offer if selected
- Negotiate start date and terms
- Medical examination
- Security clearance

### Language Requirements for CAST

## Temporary Agents and Other Staff Categories

### Temporary Agents (TA)

**Overview:**
- Fixed-term contracts for **specialised or temporary tasks**
- **Maximum duration**: 6 years
- Employed directly by EU institutions
- Aligned with AD/AST salary grades

**Typical Positions:**
- Highly specialised roles requiring specific expertise
- Temporary projects with defined end date
- Replacement for staff on leave
- Urgent operational needs

**Recruitment:**
- **NOT through EPSO open competitions**
- Individual institution vacancy notices
- Published on EU Careers website
- Sent to Member States' Permanent Representations

**Qualifications:**
- Depends on position
- Usually requires significant experience and expertise
- May require advanced degrees for specialised roles

**Pathway to Permanent Status:**
- TAs can apply for internal competitions
- Still must pass formal EPSO selection
- Experience counts but not automatic conversion

**Salary:**
- Equivalent to permanent officials (AD/AST grades)
- Same allowances and benefits
- Pension rights accumulated

### Seconded National Experts (SNE)

**Overview:**
- National or international civil servants
- Public sector employees working temporarily for EU institutions
- Typically 6 months to 4 years

**Recruitment:**
- Contact your country's **Permanent Representation to the EU**
- Information about opportunities through national channels
- Application through diplomatic/government channels

**Conditions:**
- Remain employed by home country/organisation
- Receive daily subsistence allowance from EU
- Home salary continues to be paid
- Return to home position after secondment

**Benefits:**
- Gain EU experience
- Maintain home country employment status
- Network building
- Policy influence

### Interim Staff

**Overview:**
- Short-term positions (weeks to several months)
- Cover urgent needs or temporary gaps
- Usually administrative or secretarial support

**Recruitment:**
- **NOT employed directly by EU institutions**
- Hired through **private staffing agencies**
- Contact local temping agencies in Brussels/Luxembourg
- Different employment conditions than EU staff

**Typical Roles:**
- Clerical work
- Administrative support
- Reception duties
- Data entry
- Short-term project support

**Limitations:**
- No EU staff status
- Limited benefits
- No career progression within EU
- Agency employment terms apply

**Can Lead To:**
- Experience working in EU environment
- Networking opportunities
- Potential transition to Contract Agent position
- Understanding of EU institution work

### Freelance Interpreters

**Overview:**
- Work for European Parliament, Commission, and Court of Justice
- Joint selection process
- Accreditation required

**How to Become Accredited:**
- Pass **accreditation test**
- Organised by DG Interpretation services
- Must demonstrate high-level interpreting skills
- Multiple EU languages required

**Work:**
- Conference interpreting
- Simultaneous and consecutive interpretation
- EU meetings, conferences, official events
- Paid per day worked

**Information:**
- Contact interpretation services of institutions
- Regular calls for new languages
- Competitive and demanding

### Trainers and Consultants

**Overview:**
- External experts providing services to EU institutions
- Training programmes
- Consultancy projects
- Short-term missions

**Recruitment:**
- Open calls for proposals
- Framework contracts
- Tender procedures
- Individual expert profiles

**Fields:**
- Training and capacity building
- Technical expertise
- Policy advice
- Evaluation and assessment

### Methodology: The Key to Success

**Why Methodology Matters:**
- Understanding the **logic behind questions** more important than just practicing
- EPSO tests have specific patterns and structures
- Knowing methodology improves speed and accuracy
- Practice alone insufficient without understanding

**How to Learn Methodology:**
1. **Study detailed explanations** in preparation materials
2. **Attend methodology webinars** (EU Training, others)
3. **Read strategy sections** in test books
4. **Analyze mistakes** to understand patterns
5. **Apply systematic approaches** to question types

### Practice Strategy

**Effective Practice:**
- **Consistency over intensity**: 1 hour daily better than 10 hours weekly
- **Spread over weeks/months**: Not last-minute cramming
- **Simulate real conditions**: Timed practice crucial
- **Review mistakes thoroughly**: Learn from errors
- **Track progress**: Use analytics to identify weak areas

**What to Avoid:**
- Passive reading without practice
- Practicing without understanding methodology
- Last-minute intensive cramming
- Ignoring weak areas
- Not simulating exam conditions

### Expert Tips from András Baneth (EU Training)

1. **Understand methodology first** before extensive practice
2. **Practice consistently**: Minimum 30 minutes daily
3. **Stay persistent** through motivation dips
4. **Form study groups** for accountability
5. **Simulate real exam conditions** regularly
6. **Establish baseline** through full simulations
7. **Frequency matters** more than total hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: What is the age limit for applying to EPSO competitions?**
A: There is NO age limit. The only requirement is that you meet the educational and professional criteria. Pensionable age for new recruits is 66.

**Q: Do I need to be an EU citizen?**
A: For most permanent official positions, YES - EU citizenship required. Some positions open to candidate country nationals. Limited traineeship spots for non-EU nationals. Contract Agent positions primarily for EU citizens.

**Q: How many languages do I need?**
A: Minimum TWO EU official languages. Requirements vary by competition. For most AD/AST positions: thorough knowledge of one EU language + very good knowledge of another. One language typically must be English, French, or German.

**Q: Can I apply if I'm finishing my degree this year?**
A: NO. Degree must be COMPLETED before application deadline. Even graduating one day after deadline means waiting for next competition.

**Q: I don't see my field - when will there be a competition?**
A: EPSO publishes upcoming opportunities page. Competitions depend on institutional needs. Timing unpredictable. Register for CAST in the meantime.

### EPSO Exams

**Q: How difficult are EPSO exams?**
A: Very competitive. Pass rates vary but typically only 5-10% make it to reserve list. Proper preparation essential. With good preparation, success is achievable.

**Q: How long should I prepare?**
A: Minimum 3-6 months with consistent practice (1 hour daily). Many successful candidates prepare for 6-12 months. Earlier start = better chances.

**Q: Can I retake EPSO exams if I fail?**
A: YES. No limit on number of attempts. Each competition is separate. Many successful candidates needed 2-3 attempts. Learn from experience and try again.

**Q: Are the tests in my language?**
A: Tests available in all EU official languages. You choose Language 1 for tests. EPSO ensures rigorous translation quality control.

**Q: What happens if I have technical issues during remote exams?**
A: Contact EPSO immediately. Document issues. EPSO has procedures for handling technical problems. May offer retests in valid cases.

### Reserve List and Recruitment

**Q: If I'm on the reserve list, am I guaranteed a job?**
A: NO. Reserve list means you're qualified and available for recruitment. Institutions recruit based on needs. Higher ranking = better chances. Some on reserve lists never get recruited.

**Q: How long are reserve lists valid?**
A: Typically 1-3 years, with possible extension. Validity period specified in Notice of Competition.

**Q: Can I be on multiple reserve lists?**
A: YES. You can apply for multiple competitions and be on several reserve lists simultaneously.

**Q: How long does recruitment take after being on reserve list?**
A: Highly variable. Some candidates recruited within weeks, others wait months or years. Some never recruited before list expires.

### Traineeships

**Q: Are traineeships paid?**
A: YES. Blue Book: ~€1,418-1,490/month. Schuman: ~€1,339/month. Plus travel and other allowances.

**Q: Can I do more than one EU traineeship?**
A: Generally NO. Rules limit multiple traineeships. Blue Book explicitly states no more than 6 weeks prior EU experience.

**Q: Will a traineeship lead to a job?**
A: Not automatically. But excellent networking opportunity. Many EU staff started as trainees. Experience helps in competitions. Some trainees recruited as Contract Agents.

**Q: Can I apply for both Blue Book and Schuman?**
A: YES. Separate programmes, can apply to both. Different institutions, different deadlines.

### CAST

**Q: How long does CAST take?**
A: Unpredictable. Registration is instant. Invitation to test can take days, months, or never. If invited and pass tests, recruitment depends on vacancies.

**Q: Do Contract Agent positions lead to permanent roles?**
A: Not automatically. Contract Agents can apply for EPSO competitions like anyone else. Experience valuable but still must pass formal selection.

**Q: Can I register for multiple CAST profiles?**
A: YES. Recommended to register for all relevant profiles to maximise opportunities.

### Salaries and Benefits

**Q: How much will I earn as an EU official?**
A: Varies by grade, step, and personal situation. AD5 Step 1: ~€5,300 basic + allowances = €6,500-8,000+ total gross. See Salaries section for details.

**Q: Are salaries taxed?**
A: YES, but by EU tax system, not national tax. Generally lower than national income tax. Rates 8-45% progressive, average 15-25% effective.

**Q: Do I get a pension?**
A: YES. Generous defined benefit pension scheme. 1.9% of reference salary per year of service. Maximum 70% after 36.75 years.

**Q: What about health insurance?**
A: Excellent coverage through JSIS. 80-85% reimbursement (100% for serious illness). Covers family members.

### Career

**Q: How is career progression in EU institutions?**
A: Promotion based on merit and performance. Can be slow at higher levels. Average AD5 to AD14: 35-40 years. Internal mobility across services possible.

**Q: Can I move between EU institutions?**
A: YES. Internal mobility encouraged. Staff can transfer between Commission, Parliament, Council, agencies, etc.

**Q: Is there job security?**
A: Excellent for permanent officials (fonctionnaires). Very stable career. Contract Agents have fixed-term contracts (up to 6 years).

### Living in Brussels/Luxembourg

**Q: What is the cost of living like?**
A: Brussels and Luxembourg are relatively expensive. Housing costs significant. EU salaries account for this. Allowances help expatriates.

**Q: Do I need to relocate?**
A: Usually YES for permanent positions. Most jobs based in Brussels, Luxembourg, or Strasbourg. Some agency positions throughout EU.

**Q: What about schools for my children?**
A: EU schools available in Brussels and Luxembourg. Excellent multilingual education. Education allowance helps with costs.

### Application Process

**Q: Can I apply for multiple competitions simultaneously?**
A: YES. Recommended to apply for all competitions where you meet criteria.

**Q: Can I update my application after submission?**
A: NO. Once submitted, no changes possible. Review very carefully before submitting.

**Q: What if I miss the deadline?**
A: No late applications accepted. Deadlines strictly enforced. Must wait for next competition.

## Conclusion

Working for the EU institutions offers a unique opportunity to contribute to European integration, work in a truly multicultural environment, and build a meaningful career in public service. While the selection process is highly competitive and demanding, with proper preparation, persistence, and genuine motivation, success is achievable.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Multiple Pathways**: Permanent officials, contract agents, temporary agents, traineeships - various entry points exist
2. **Preparation is Essential**: EPSO exams require serious preparation - start early, practice consistently, understand methodology
3. **Be Patient**: Processes take time (12-18 months typical for competitions)
4. **Be Persistent**: Many successful candidates needed 2-3 attempts
5. **Stay Informed**: Regularly check EU Careers website for updates and opportunities
6. **Network**: Connect with current and former EU staff, join preparation groups
7. **Start Now**: Whether through CAST registration, traineeship application, or competition preparation

1. **Register for CAST** if eligible - it's free and keeps options open
