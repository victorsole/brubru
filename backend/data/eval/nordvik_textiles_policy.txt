NORDVIK TEXTILES AB
INTERNAL SUSTAINABILITY AND PRODUCT COMPLIANCE POLICY
Version 4.2 — reviewed January 2026
Applies to: all apparel and footwear lines placed on the EU market

1. SCOPE AND ORGANISATION

Nordvik Textiles AB is a large enterprise within the meaning of Directive 2013/34/EU,
employing 1,340 staff with an annual turnover of EUR 310 million. We design, source and
sell knitted and woven apparel (CN chapters 61 and 62), leather accessories (CN 4203) and
footwear (CN 6403, 6404) through our own retail network, our webshop, and third-party
online marketplaces. Manufacturing is contracted to suppliers in Portugal, Turkey and
Bangladesh. We import into the Union through our Rotterdam warehouse and hold an EORI
number. Product compliance is owned by the Head of Product Integrity, who reports to the
Chief Operating Officer.

2. EXTENDED PRODUCER RESPONSIBILITY

We are registered as a producer in the national producer register in Sweden and in the
Netherlands, and we pay annual fees to the producer responsibility organisation in both
markets. Registration in France, Germany, Spain and Italy is in progress and expected to
complete during 2026. We report tonnage placed on the market annually to each PRO, split
by material category, based on the finished-goods weight recorded in our ERP.

We have not yet appointed authorised representatives in the Member States where we sell by
distance contract but are not established. Legal is scoping this work for the second half
of 2026.

Our fee contributions are calculated on total weight placed on the market. We do not
currently apply eco-modulation criteria such as durability, recyclability, or recycled
content, because our PROs have not yet published modulation schedules in the markets where
we operate.

3. UNSOLD STOCK

Unsold seasonal stock is handled in the following order: (a) transfer to our outlet
channel, (b) sale to off-price wholesale partners, (c) donation to registered charities,
(d) destruction. In 2025 we destroyed 41,200 units, principally damaged returns and items
withdrawn for safety reasons following a drawstring recall.

We do not currently publish figures on discarded unsold products. Our annual sustainability
report, prepared under Article 19a of Directive 2013/34/EU, discusses waste in general
terms but does not break down units or weight of unsold goods discarded, nor the reasons
for discarding, nor the split across waste treatment operations.

Where products are destroyed for intellectual property reasons — principally counterfeit
seizures and licensed collaborations whose licence term has expired — the decision is taken
by Brand Protection and recorded in a case file retained for three years.

We do not operate a formal donation-offer step before destruction. Charity donation is
attempted informally by the warehouse team where time allows.

4. PRODUCT INFORMATION AND TRACEABILITY

Every style carries a QR code on the care label linking to our product microsite, which
shows fibre composition, country of final assembly, care instructions and our repair
service. The microsite is maintained in our PIM system. Data is held at style (model)
level; we do not currently generate batch-level or item-level identifiers, and our QR codes
resolve to a marketing URL rather than to a persistent unique product identifier.

We have begun a Digital Product Passport readiness project with an external consortium. No
data carrier standard has been selected and we have not registered any product in any
Commission registry. We have not obtained a qualified electronic seal or qualified
electronic signature under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014.

5. SUBSTANCES AND SAFETY

All suppliers sign our Restricted Substances List, which is aligned to REACH Annex XVII and
updated twice yearly. Third-party laboratory testing is carried out on 8% of styles per
season on a risk basis. Products found non-compliant are withdrawn and destroyed, and a
test report stating the non-compliant chemical and the applicable law is retained
indefinitely.

6. SEPARATE COLLECTION AND REUSE

We operate in-store take-back bins in 40 stores. Collected textiles are passed to a sorting
partner in Poland. We receive an annual tonnage statement from the sorting partner but do
not receive a breakdown between preparing for reuse, recycling, and disposal, and we do not
audit the partner's downstream flows.

Shipments of used goods assessed as fit for reuse are sent to our sorting partner
accompanied by a commercial invoice and a transport document. We do not currently include a
declaration of fitness for reuse.

7. RECORD KEEPING

Compliance documentation is retained for five years in our document management system,
with the exception of Brand Protection case files noted above. Records are retrievable on
request by competent authorities; our target response time is 30 days.

8. GOVERNANCE

The Product Integrity Committee meets quarterly. Compliance obligations are tracked in a
spreadsheet maintained by the Head of Product Integrity. There is no automated horizon
scanning for new or amended EU legislation; changes are identified through our trade
association newsletter and our external counsel.
