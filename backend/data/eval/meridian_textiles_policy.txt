MERIDIAN APPAREL GROUP NV
EU PRODUCT COMPLIANCE AND CIRCULARITY MANUAL
Version 11.0 — approved by the Board 14 January 2026
Scope: all apparel, clothing accessories and footwear placed on the EU market

1. SCOPE AND ORGANISATION

Meridian Apparel Group NV is a large enterprise within the meaning of Directive
2013/34/EU, with 4,120 employees and consolidated turnover of EUR 1.16 billion. We design
and sell knitted and woven apparel (CN chapters 61 and 62), leather goods (CN 4203),
headgear (CN 6504 and 6505) and footwear (CN 6403 and 6404). We sell through 610 own
stores, our webshop, and third-party online marketplaces in all 27 Member States.
Accountability sits with the Group Director of Product Compliance, who reports to the
Chief Executive and presents to the Audit Committee quarterly.

2. EXTENDED PRODUCER RESPONSIBILITY

We are registered in the national register of producers of textile, textile-related and
footwear products in every Member State in which we make Annex IVc products available on
the market. Registration numbers for all 27 registers are held in our regulatory register
and reconciled quarterly against our sales ledger; a market may not be opened by Commercial
until registration is confirmed.

In each of those Member States we have entrusted an authorised producer responsibility
organisation to discharge our extended producer responsibility obligations on our behalf,
under written contract. We report tonnage placed on the market to each organisation
quarterly, split by Annex IVc category and by CN heading, derived from finished-goods
weights captured at SKU level in our ERP.

For each Member State in which we sell by distance contract without being established, we
have appointed an authorised representative by written mandate, and the mandate is filed
with the competent national authority. The current list covers eleven Member States and is
reviewed whenever a new distance-selling market is opened.

Our financial contributions are calculated on weight and quantity and are eco-modulated.
We apply the modulation criteria published by each producer responsibility organisation,
covering durability, repairability, recycled fibre content and the presence of substances
of concern, and we hold the supporting technical file for each modulated SKU.

We cover the costs of separate collection of used and waste products, of transport to
sorting, of sorting operations, and of the awareness campaigns run by our producer
responsibility organisations, in accordance with the cost-coverage scope agreed with each
organisation.

3. UNSOLD STOCK

We do not destroy unsold consumer apparel, clothing accessories or footwear. Since 1 January
2026 the destruction of these products has been prohibited by Group policy irrespective of
volume, ahead of the statutory date, and the prohibition is enforced by a system control:
no disposal movement can be booked in the warehouse management system for a CN code within
chapters 61 or 62, or headings 4203, 6504, 6505 or 6401 to 6405, without a derogation case
number issued by Legal.

Where a derogation is relied upon, the applicable ground under Commission Delegated
Regulation (EU) 2026/296 is recorded in the case file, together with the evidence required
by Article 3 of that Regulation. Case files are retained in electronic form for five years
from the date of destruction and can be produced to a competent authority within 30 days of
a request. In 2025 we relied on derogations 41 times, in every case for products found to
be dangerous or non-compliant following laboratory testing, and the corresponding test
reports naming the non-compliant substance and the applicable Union law are held with each
case.

Before any product may be destroyed on a ground other than safety, non-compliance or an
intellectual property infringement, it must first be offered for donation. Our process
offers the stock directly to at least three social economy entities established in the
Union and, in parallel, lists it on a publicly accessible donation page of our corporate
website for a minimum of eight weeks. Only if no acceptance is received within that window
may the case proceed, and the offer records are retained with the case file.

We disclose annually, on our corporate website, the number and total weight of unsold
consumer products discarded in the previous financial year, the reasons for discarding
them, the derogation relied on where applicable, the proportion delivered to each waste
treatment operation, and the measures taken and planned to prevent destruction. The
disclosure follows the format set out in Annex I to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU)
2026/2, is presented per Combined Nomenclature category at four-digit level for the
headings listed in Annex II to that Regulation, and is cross-referenced from the
sustainability statement in our management report.

We retain the information and documentation demonstrating the delivery and reception of
discarded unsold consumer products, including the statements provided by our waste
treatment operators, for five years following the disclosure.

4. PRODUCT INFORMATION, IDENTIFIERS AND THE DIGITAL PRODUCT PASSPORT

Every product carries a data carrier linked to a persistent unique product identifier.
Identifiers are issued at item level, and each item identifier is linked to its batch
identifier and to its model identifier in our product information management system, so
that a passport can be resolved at whichever granularity a delegated act requires.

Meridian Apparel Group NV is registered as a verified economic operator. Our identity and
establishment were evidenced by a qualified electronic seal supported by a qualified
certificate issued by a qualified trust service provider under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014.
The seal is held by Group Legal and renewed on a calendared cycle.

For every product group for which a digital product passport is required, we register the
passport in the Commission registry before the product is placed on the market, at the
granularity specified in the applicable delegated act, and where several instruments apply
we register at the most granular level required by any of them. We generate and archive the
proof of registration for each passport version, and we treat the accuracy, completeness
and currency of the registered data as an owner obligation of the Group Director of Product
Compliance. Where a service provider acts in the registry on our behalf, that provider is
itself verified and our responsibility for compliance is unchanged.

5. SEPARATE COLLECTION, SORTING AND SHIPMENTS

We operate take-back in all 610 stores. Collected products pass to sorting operators
contracted through our producer responsibility organisations. We receive quarterly reporting
from each operator broken down between preparing for re-use, recycling, other recovery and
disposal, and we audit two operators per year against that reporting.

Shipments arranged on a professional basis of used products assessed as fit for re-use are
accompanied by a copy of the invoice and the contract stating that the products are
destined for and fit for re-use, together with a declaration of fitness for re-use signed
by the assessing operator.

6. KNOWN GAPS AND WORK IN PROGRESS

Two matters are open and are reported to the Audit Committee each quarter.

First, our take-back bins currently accept only Meridian-branded products in 94 of the 610
stores, because those stores operate under a franchise agreement that has not yet been
amended. Collection systems are required to accept products regardless of brand.
Renegotiation is scheduled to conclude in Q4 2026.

Second, we do not yet publish the annual figures required of producer responsibility
organisations on their own websites, because two of the organisations we use have not
published theirs; we have written to both and are tracking the matter.
