TALENTYX - AI GOVERNANCE AND RESPONSIBLE AI STATEMENT
Version 3.0, adopted February 2026

1. ABOUT TALENTYX
Talentyx GmbH is a 55-person company registered in Berlin. We build and sell
"Talentyx Screen", a SaaS product that helps mid-sized employers shortlist job
applicants. The product ranks candidates against a role description and returns
a scored list with short natural-language explanations. We fine-tune a
third-party open-weights language model on our own annotated dataset of
anonymised historical hiring decisions provided by customers under a data
processing agreement. The product is sold under the Talentyx name and trade
mark. We operate on AWS in the eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) region.

2. HOW WE SEE OUR ROLE
We recognise that candidate screening for employment is one of the uses listed
in Annex III of the EU AI Act, and we treat Talentyx Screen as a high-risk AI
system. We have documented this classification and the reasons for it. We have
not separately analysed whether our fine-tuning of the base model changes our
legal role from deployer to provider, and our terms currently describe us as a
"technology supplier".

3. PROHIBITED USES
Talentyx Screen does not use subliminal or manipulative techniques, does not
perform social scoring, does not scrape facial images, and does not infer
emotions. We do not generate images, audio or video of any kind. Our terms
prohibit customers from using the product for anything other than assessing
job applications.

4. TRANSPARENCY
Every candidate-facing page carries a persistent banner reading "Screening
assisted by AI", and recruiters see a tooltip explaining that the scores are
generated by a model. Our privacy notice describes the logic of the scoring in
general terms.

5. AI LITERACY
All engineering and customer-success staff completed a four-hour internal
training on the EU AI Act and on the limitations of our model in January 2026.
New joiners complete it during onboarding, and we repeat it annually. Records
are held in our HR system.

6. DATA PROTECTION
We completed a data protection impact assessment in November 2025 covering the
training pipeline and the live product. Our lawful basis for processing
candidate data is the legitimate interests of the employer, recorded in a
balancing test annexed to the DPIA. We do not process special categories of
personal data. Candidates can request access to and deletion of their data via
privacy@talentyx.example, and deletion requests are executed against our
production database within 30 days.

7. HUMAN OVERSIGHT AND DECISIONS
The product does not make hiring decisions. Recruiters review the ranked list
and decide whom to interview. Our terms of service require the customer to keep
a human in the loop, and we assign our own product specialists to advise
customers on interpreting scores.

8. MODEL DEVELOPMENT AND TESTING
We maintain an internal model card describing the training data, evaluation
results and known limitations. We measure selection-rate differences across
gender and age bands quarterly and retrain if the difference exceeds our
internal threshold. We keep a changelog of model versions.

9. SECURITY
We hold ISO 27001 certification, renewed October 2025. Data is encrypted at
rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Access to the training corpus
and model weights is restricted to nine named engineers via SSO with hardware
keys. We run an annual external penetration test.

10. INCIDENTS
Our incident response plan defines severity levels and an on-call rota. For
security incidents affecting customer data we notify the affected customer's
administrator by email. We do not currently have a procedure for reporting
serious incidents to a market surveillance authority.

11. WHAT WE HAVE NOT DONE
We have not drawn up the Annex IV technical documentation, we have not carried
out a conformity assessment, we have not affixed a CE marking, and we have not
registered the product in any EU database. We rely on our upstream model
provider's published documentation for the base model.
