Second Call for Applications to the BSC AI Factory Incubation Space Program
The BSC AI Factory is pleased to announce the second call for applications to its Incubation Space Program. This program is designed to support startups that are either building AI-driven solutions or working intensively with AI technologies to create innovative, scalable, and high-impact applications.

What We Offer

Selected beneficiaries will have the opportunity to:

Dedicated incubation space within the BSC AI Factory’s four incubation spaces in Barcelona, Guimarães, Lisbon, or Bucharest, from 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026
Access to AI expertise and technical guidance
Participation in a structured incubation track, including tailored sessions, workshops, and visits from relevant members of the ecosystem
Networking opportunities within a unique ecosystem of AI experts, researchers, industrial partners, investors, and fellow startups
The program provides a dynamic and collaborative environment designed to strengthen both the technological and business dimensions of AI-driven startups.

Who Should Apply

Early-stage startups and teams developing AI-based products or services with strong technological foundations and growth potential.

Key Dates

Application Deadline: 12 March 2026
Program period (call 2): 1 April 2026 to 31 September 2026

1. Quality and Maturity of the AI Solution (max. 30 points)*
Description of the AI solution, including the technologies used or developed (models, algorithms, datasets, infrastructure). Applicants must indicate the current level of development, validation status, and technological readiness of the solution.

Brubru is a fully built, production-ready AI platform comprising 213,000+ lines of code across a React 18 + TypeScript frontend and a FastAPI + SQLAlchemy 2.0 backend, backed by PostgreSQL. The AI engine orchestrates a four-provider fallback chain (Mistral as primary, Claude Sonnet, GPT-4 Turbo, and Gemini 1.5 Pro) through 18 specialised AI services covering conversational Q&A, document generation, compliance gap analysis, legislative outcome predictions, and amendment drafting. The platform ingests data from 15+ official EU institutional sources via 32 custom-built scrapers (EUR-Lex, OEIL, EP committees, Commission document register, public consultations, Legislative Train, EPRS Think Tank, TED procurement, and more), feeding into a hybrid retrieval system that combines BM25 keyword search with ChromaDB semantic vector search for context injection. Each user query triggers a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline that pulls real-time legislative context from 25+ database models and a curated knowledge base of 17 policy reports and 7 live monitors. The solution is fully deployed: the frontend is hosted on SiteGround, the backend on Railway.app, and the database on Supabase PostgreSQL. All eight core features (chatbot, dashboard, amendment editor, compliance checker, document generator, predictions engine, tender service, and admin panel) are live and functional. The technology readiness level is TRL 7-8: the system has been validated in an operational environment with 13 test users across two subscription tiers and is ready for commercial launch.

2. Disruptiveness, strategic relevance and use case fit (max. 25 points)*
Description of the project's use case and the problem or need it addresses. Applicants must explain the relevance and clarity of the problem, the suitability of the AI-based solution for the proposed use case, the degree of disruptiveness or innovation introduced, and the applicability of the solution to real-world scenarios within the context of the Incubation Program.

EU policy professionals currently spend 2-4 hours per day manually monitoring 15+ institutional websites, 3-5 days drafting a single position paper (costing EUR 5,000-15,000), and 2-4 weeks on compliance gap analyses (EUR 20,000-50,000). There is no AI-native platform purpose-built for this workflow: generic tools like ChatGPT and Claude lack institutional context and hallucinate on procedural details, while incumbents like POLITICO Pro (EUR 7,000-50,000/year) and FiscalNote offer news monitoring without AI capabilities. Brubru is disruptive because it is the first platform to unify the entire EU advocacy cycle (monitor, analyse, draft, comply, predict, bid) in a single AI-powered service. The Amendator, for instance, parses official Akoma Ntoso XML from EUR-Lex and generates properly formatted legislative amendments with legal justifications, a capability no existing tool offers outside the EU institutions' own internal LEOS system. The predictions engine delivers AI-powered legislative outcome forecasts, EP plenary vote simulations, and Council QMV calculations, turning political intelligence into quantifiable data. The timing is ideal: an unprecedented wave of EU regulation (AI Act, MiCA, CSRD, NIS2, DORA, Packaging Regulation) is creating compliance deadlines in 2026-2028, driving massive demand for exactly the kind of rapid, expert legislative analysis that Brubru delivers. The incubation period (April-September 2026) coincides precisely with our commercial launch and first customer acquisition phase.

The incubation period is therefore perfectly aligned with the transition from solo founder to a small, focused team.

4. Scalability and impact potential (max. 15 points)*
Description of the project's potential to scale beyond the incubation phase, including growth opportunities, market expansion, and expected economic, social, or technological impact.

The EU public affairs market is valued at EUR 3-5 billion, encompassing lobbying firms, consultancies, corporate government affairs teams, in-house policy departments, law firms, trade associations, and NGOs. Our serviceable market (the portion addressable by software) is EUR 1.2-2 billion, of which Brubru targets a conservative EUR 130,000 in year one (0.01% of SAM), leaving enormous headroom for growth. The SaaS model (EUR 79/month Yellow tier, EUR 599/month Blue tier) delivers 90-94% gross margins, with break-even at just 23 subscribers. Financial projections show EUR 10,000 MRR by month 12, with a seed round target of EUR 500,000-1,000,000 at EUR 3-5M pre-money valuation by month 12-18. Beyond the Brussels bubble, the platform is architected for multi-level governance: national parliament monitoring (starting with France and Germany) is on the roadmap for Q4 2026, with expansion to all 27 Member States by 2028. The i18n infrastructure already supports 23 EU languages. Comparable companies in adjacent verticals have achieved significant scale: FiscalNote (US political intelligence) reached a USD 1.5 billion IPO, and Harvey AI (legal AI) is valued at USD 3 billion+. The social impact is equally meaningful: by democratising access to EU legislative intelligence, Brubru levels the playing field between well-resourced corporations and smaller organisations like NGOs, SMEs, and independent consultants who cannot afford EUR 50,000/year monitoring subscriptions.

5. Relevance and added value of the BSC AI Factory environment (max. 10 points)*
Description of how the project fits the BSC AI Factory environment. Applicants must explain what the startup brings to the space, how it plans to use and occupy the Incubation Space, and which AI Factory services, infrastructure, or expertise are relevant for the development of the solution.

Beresol brings to the BSC AI Factory a production-ready, revenue-stage AI product operating in the high-value intersection of natural language processing, information retrieval, and public policy. Our presence enriches the ecosystem with a concrete use case of applied AI in the govtech/regtech domain, an area underrepresented among typical AI startups. We plan to occupy the Barcelona incubation space as our primary development hub during the programme, using it for daily product development, user testing, and investor preparation. The BSC AI Factory's technical infrastructure and expertise are directly relevant to three of our key engineering challenges: first, scaling our hybrid retrieval pipeline (BM25 + semantic vector search across 25+ database models) where access to HPC resources and guidance on efficient embedding models would significantly accelerate performance; second, fine-tuning domain-specific language models on EU legislative corpora, where BSC's expertise in large-scale model training and its compute infrastructure could help us move from API-dependent models to proprietary ones, reducing costs and increasing differentiation; and third, optimising our multi-provider AI orchestration and prediction engine (11 forecasting services), where BSC's research in machine learning could help us refine our legislative outcome prediction algorithms with more sophisticated approaches. The networking dimension is equally valuable: access to investors, industrial partners, and the broader Barcelona AI ecosystem would support our seed fundraising timeline (targeting month 12-18) and help establish partnerships for our planned expansion beyond Brussels.
