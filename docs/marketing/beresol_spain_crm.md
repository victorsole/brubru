## How to use

This file is a plain-text companion to the Excel CRM. Edit it directly in VS Code. 
When you update a contact's pipeline stage, last contact date, or next action, mirror the change into the Excel file (or ask Claude Code to do it for you).

Ask Claude Code things like:

- *"Update all Telefónica contacts to pipeline stage '3. Outreach 1' with next action date today + 4 days."*
- *"Show me all Priority A contacts in Banking & Finance whose pipeline stage is still '1. Identified'."*
- *"Draft a Spanish-language cold email to Ana Fernández Manrique at BBVA based on the Regulatory template."*
- *"Enrich Mercadona contacts with LinkedIn URLs using web search."*

## Scoring methodology

Lead score (0–100) is the sum of four components:

| Component | Weighting | Bands |
|---|---|---|
| **Company size** | 0–30 | >€10B = 30 · €5–10B = 25 · €1–5B = 20 · €500M–1B = 15 · <€500M = 10 |
| **Sector fit** | 0–25 | Banking/Energy/Telecom/Defence = 25 · Pharma/Auto/Tech = 20 · Construction/Retail/Insurance = 15 · Food/Transport/Real Estate = 10 · Hospitality/Media/Other = 8 |
| **Role seniority** | 0–25 | PA/Regulatory = 25 · Compliance/Legal = 22 · CDO/CTO/AI/Innovation = 20 · ESG = 18 · CSO = 15 · CEO = 12 |
| **EU exposure** | 0–20 | Brussels presence = 20 · No Brussels = 10 · Unknown = 8 |

Priority tiers: **A** ≥ 80 · **B** 60–79 · **C** 40–59 · **D** < 40

## Pipeline stages

- **1. Identified — Contact added to list**
- **2. Researched — Details verified, sales angle defined**
- **3. Outreach 1 — Initial cold email or LinkedIn sent**
- **4. Outreach 2 — Follow-up via different channel or angle**
- **5. Engaged — Contact has responded or interacted**
- **6. Discovery call — Qualifying call completed**
- **7. Proposal — Formal proposal or demo delivered**
- **8. Negotiation — Terms under discussion**
- **9. Closed Won — Deal signed**
- **10. Closed Lost — Deal rejected or stalled**

## Outreach cadence

| Day | Action | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial cold email (sector-specific template) | Email |
| 2 | View LinkedIn profile (triggers notification) | LinkedIn |
| 4 | Personalised connection request | LinkedIn |
| 8 | Follow-up email with different angle | Email |
| 12 | Direct message or InMail | LinkedIn |
| 18 | Break-up email + value resource | Email |
| 25 | Move to nurture / quarterly list | CRM |

Stop the sequence immediately on any response. 'Not now' → 90-day reminder. Referral → new contact record.
