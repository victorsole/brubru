The Metric
One number to rule them all
10
Month 3 Target

Weekly Active Paid Users (WAPU)

Not MRR. Not subscriber count. Not raw active users. The intersection of money AND usage — the only honest signal of product-market fit.

A paid subscriber who performs at least one core action in the past 7 days.
MRR
Trailing indicator. Subscriber x price. Doesn't tell you if the product is working. A EUR 99/mo user who hasn't logged in is a churn event waiting to happen.
Subscriber Count
A subscriber who signed up but doesn't use the product is a vanishing subscriber. Inflates the dashboard, masks retention problems.
Raw Active Users
Free trial users who never convert are noise. Measuring paid + active is the Slack "2,000 messages" signal — the productivity rubicon.
Core Actions
Any one of these in a 7-day window = active

Spaak Competitive Posture
Not "match their monitoring breadth" but "make our features irreplaceable"

  • VC-backed, dedicated sales team
  • 100+ public affairs teams as clients
  • Brussels office, events presence
  • Webinars, content marketing, network effects
  • Multi-level governance monitoring
Brubru Strengths
  • Amendator — Only non-institutional Akoma Ntoso editor
  • Predictions — EP group vote, QMV calculator, resolution indicators
  • EU Law Comply — Compliance gap analysis (high switching cost)
  • Document Generator — Position papers in 2 minutes vs 4 hours
  • Brubru Chat — AI advisor with 35 scrapers of context
Orbit Strategy: Build feature depth, maintain LinkedIn visibility, let Spaak come to you from a position of strength. The CEO is connected on LinkedIn — build the relationship from a position of product superiority. Spaak's distribution advantage is real but can't be replicated by a solo founder. Feature depth can.
