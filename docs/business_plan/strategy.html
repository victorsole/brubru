Weekly Active Paid Users (WAPU)

Not MRR. Not subscriber count. Not raw active users. The intersection of money AND usage, the only honest signal of product-market fit.

A paid subscriber who performs at least one core action in the past 7 days.
MRR
Trailing indicator. Subscriber x price. Doesn't tell you if the product is working. A EUR 99/mo user who hasn't logged in is a churn event waiting to happen.
Subscriber Count
A subscriber who signed up but doesn't use the product is a vanishing subscriber. Inflates the dashboard, masks retention problems.
Raw Active Users
Free trial users who never convert are noise. Measuring paid + active is the Slack "2,000 messages" signal, the productivity rubicon.

Spaak competitive posture
Not "match their monitoring breadth" but "make our features irreplaceable"
Spaak Strengths
  • Danish-origin, proven national PMF funding EU expansion
  • VC-backed (EUR 1.5M pre-seed), dedicated sales team
  • 100+ public affairs teams as clients
  • Brussels office, events presence, aggressive hiring
  • Webinars, content marketing, network effects
  • Multi-level governance monitoring (698+ sources)

Key weakness: Core team comes from Danish political monitoring, lacks deep EU institutional knowledge (EP procedure, trilogue dynamics, Akoma Ntoso, committee structures).

Brubru Strengths
  • Amendator: only non-institutional Akoma Ntoso editor
  • Predictions: EP group vote, QMV calculator, resolution indicators
  • EU Law Comply: compliance gap analysis (high switching cost)
  • Document Generator: position papers in 2 minutes vs 4 hours
  • Brubru Chat: AI advisor with 35+ scrapers, 102 knowledge guides, 2,798 triggers
  • Deep Dives: 7 legislative explainers in 6 languages (pharma, telecoms, biotech, etc.)
  • Catalan translation: only entity translating EU legislation into Catalan
Orbit Strategy: Build feature depth, maintain LinkedIn visibility, let Spaak come to you from a position of strength. The CEO is connected on LinkedIn. Build the relationship from a position of product superiority. Spaak's distribution advantage is real but can't be replicated by a solo founder. Feature depth can.
Prismos (Brussels): the Belgian monitoring newcomer

Founded 2024, pre-seed US$ 162.5K from imec.istart. 2-10 employees. CEO Pepijn Mores (data/AI engineering). Won EU FinTech Pitch Battle 2025. Covers EU + Belgian parliament with real-time debate transcription and AI-powered analysis.

Overlap: monitoring + alerts + chat (same space as Spaak). Gap: no legislative tools (no Amendator, Comply, Document Generator, Predictions, Calendar). No curated knowledge base. No institutional founder background.

What Brubru should watch: their real-time transcription capability is a genuine differentiator. Brubru's CRE transcripts have a 3-5 day delay. Their "AI Prisms" (personalised relevance filtering) is a strong concept worth emulating. Their Belgian parliament coverage shows national-level expansion potential.

Threat level: medium. Same competitive category as Spaak (monitoring-first). Does not compete on Brubru's core tools. Multiple well-funded startups entering EU policy tech (Spaak, Prismos, Dixit) validates the market timing.

EU Matrix (Brussels): political foresight analytics

Founded by Doru P. Frantescu (recognised by Politico as top EU affairs influencer). MEP voting pattern analysis, coalition mapping, 62,000+ MEP-stakeholder meetings tracked, 22,000+ Commissioner meetings, MEP Influence Index by policy domain. Gen AI in beta (March 2026).

Overlap: MEP analysis (voting, influence). Gap: no AI chat, no amendment tools, no compliance, no calendar, no document generation, no knowledge guides, no deep dives. Analytics platform, not workflow tool.

Threat level: low-medium. Different category (foresight vs. workflow). Their behavioural data (who meets whom) is valuable but complementary, not competitive. Potential future data partnership.

Lobium: AI-powered amendment and consultation analysis

EU Datathon 2022 winner. 5 tools: Amendment Analyser (filter, visualise, export), Stakeholder-Issue Matrix (consultation navigator, coalition detection), Policy Reports Insight Engine (impact assessment analysis), EU Radar (personalised tracker), Tailored AI Solutions. Clients: Bernstein Group, Pharma Deutschland, Article 19, EU DisinfoLab.

Overlap: amendment analysis + monitoring (EU Radar). Gap: no Akoma Ntoso editor (analysis only, not drafting), no compliance, no predictions, no document generator, no calendar, no deep dives, no multilingual support, no curated knowledge base.

Threat level: medium. Closest direct competitor in amendment AI. But analyses amendments, doesn't draft them. Brubru's Amendator is a legislative editor; Lobium's is a legislative reader. The distinction matters: action vs. observation.

The category distinction: dashboards vs. agents

Spaak sells dashboards to look at. Brubru sells an agent to work with. This is a fundamentally different product category.

Observation Platform (Spaak) Action Platform (Brubru)
Aggregates feeds from 698+ sources Aggregates feeds AND drafts amendments from them
Tracks politician social media posts Predicts EP group vote outcomes with QMV calculation
AI assistant answers questions AI generates position papers, MEP briefings, talking points
Monitors consultations Runs automated compliance gap analysis

The Agent Maestro framing (Calacanis & Sacks, All In Podcast, Mar 2026): The hottest emerging role is the person who orchestrates AI agents to execute business processes, not a developer, but an operations person who understands workflows. EU policy professionals are exactly this profile. Brubru is the agent they maestro. Every feature that makes Brubru more trainable to a user's specific dossiers (tracked files, notifications, dossier workspaces) deepens the maestro experience.

The Cherny Paradigm (internal development principle): "You are part of a workforce, not just an assistant. The human orchestrates multiple AI instances in parallel: think fleet commander, not typist." This is how Brubru is built (founder orchestrating AI coding agents with institutional memory), and it is also how Brubru's users will work: orchestrating AI policy agents within their advocacy workflow.
