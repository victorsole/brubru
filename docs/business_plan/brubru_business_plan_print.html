1. Executive Summary

Brubru is an AI-powered strategic advocacy platform purpose-built for EU policy professionals. It combines a conversational AI assistant with specialised legislative tools -- amendment drafting, compliance analysis, tender intelligence, legislative monitoring, predictive analytics, and a closed-loop feedback system -- to replace the fragmented workflow of lobbyists, consultants, trade associations, and government affairs teams operating within the Brussels ecosystem.

The EU public affairs market is valued at EUR 3-5 billion annually. Today, this work is done by expensive human consultants charging EUR 250-500/hour, using generic tools that have no understanding of EU institutional procedures. Brubru encodes deep procedural knowledge from 15+ EU institutional data sources into an AI platform that produces analyst-grade work at software costs.

Vision: From AI-powered advocacy platform to the system of record for EU affairs -- the single workspace where a professional's entire dossier lives: tracked legislation, drafted amendments, MEP relationships, stakeholder positions, predictions, documents, and campaign history. Then expand nationally: 27 Member States, every level of governance, one platform per country. Nobody in EU affairs has a dossier management system powered by real-time institutional data and AI. That's the product.

2. The Problem

EU policy professionals face a uniquely complex working environment: fragmented information across 15+ institutional sources, arcane procedural knowledge that takes years to develop, expensive manual deliverables, and no purpose-built tools. US political intelligence has FiscalNote ($1.5B), Quorum, and Bloomberg Government. Legal AI has Harvey ($3B+). EU policy has nothing.

3. The Solution

Brubru replaces fragmented workflows with a single AI-powered platform that understands EU institutional procedures natively. From Workflow Platform to System of Record

Brubru has built an exceptionally strong vertical workflow platform (Layer 3) with 10 feature verticals, 35 scrapers, and 11 prediction services. The next strategic step is the transition to system of record (Layer 4): tying all features together around the concept of a user-owned dossier -- a workspace per legislative file where tracking, amendments, contacts, predictions, and documents accumulate into an asset the user cannot replicate elsewhere.

LayerDescriptionBrubru Status
Layer 1-2Foundation models + ChatDone (4-provider chain, context-aware chat)
Layer 3Vertical workflow platformSubstantially built (10 verticals, 35 scrapers)
Layer 4System of recordNext phase (dossier workspaces, contacts, accumulated user data)

Today's system of record for EU affairs is fragmented across Outlook, Excel, shared drives, and mental models. Nobody owns this. A lobbyist with 50 annotated dossiers, 200 MEP contacts, and 18 months of prediction history inside Brubru will never leave -- regardless of what foundation models do.

Competitive Advantages

1. Workflow completeness vs. Dixit/SAVOIRR. Full advocacy cycle: Monitor (My EU Bubble, EU Calendar) > Analyse (Chat, MEP Amendments) > Compare (AI alignment scoring, best allies, coverage gaps) > Draft (Amendator, Document Generator) > Comply > Predict > Bid. Dixit covers monitoring+analysis but not creation. SAVOIRR lacks drafting and predictions.

2. Akoma Ntoso XML + MEP Intelligence vs. FiscalNote/LEOS. Standards-compliant legislative amendments plus real MEP amendment data with AI comparative scoring. Draft your amendments, see what MEPs proposed, identify allies, spot gaps. FiscalNote drafts generic text with no MEP analysis. LEOS uses AKN but is institutional-only with no AI.

3. 10x price advantage vs. POLITICO Pro/FiscalNote. EUR 948-7,188/yr vs EUR 7K-50K+ (POLITICO) or EUR 10K-30K (FiscalNote). Mistral primary = 15x cost advantage, 90%+ margins.

4. Domain depth vs. generic AI. 35 scrapers, 15+ institutional data pipelines, structured procedural knowledge -- 18 months of data engineering that generic AI cannot replicate.

5. Founder-market fit vs. all. 7+ years EP + 10+ years EU advisory. Built by a parliamentary insider who personally produced every deliverable the platform automates.

6. Structural defensibility through accumulated user data. Long-term defensibility comes from switching costs, not feature checklists. When a professional has 50+ dossiers, 200+ MEP contacts, 18 months of amendment history, and 30+ position papers inside the platform, the cost of switching becomes prohibitive. This is the difference between a workflow tool and a system of record.

7. Defence against foundation model plugins. The risk is not another SaaS competitor -- it is ChatGPT/Claude adding EU policy plugins. Defence: (1) depth of structured pipelines that general plugins cannot match, (2) domain engineering (Akoma Ntoso, QMV, compliance gap analysis) beyond data access, and (3) the system-of-record layer where user-accumulated dossiers, contacts, and documents live inside Brubru and nowhere else.
