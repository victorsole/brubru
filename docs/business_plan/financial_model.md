## Executive Summary

1. **Financial Viability** - Cash, burn, runway
2. **Structural Goals** - North star metrics and OKRs
3. **Unit Economics** - LTV:CAC analysis

## Risk Analysis

### Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Free trial costs too much | Medium | High | 14-day trial limit; cap at 500 concurrent trials |
| Low conversion rate | Medium | High | Improve onboarding; add feature gates |
| AI costs spike | Low | Medium | Multi-provider fallback; usage caps |
| Single founder dependency | High | High | Document processes; build recurring revenue |
| EU regulation changes | Low | Medium | Core value prop is regulatory intelligence |
