What slows down a public affairs team like yours

  • Tracking six live files simultaneously Pharma reform, HTA Regulation, compulsory licensing, Critical Medicines Act, Biotech Act, FAST-EU pilot. Each has its own OEIL procedure, rapporteur, committee timeline, and trilogue calendar. Keeping them synchronised is a part-time job on its own.
  • Drafting briefings the same day a text drops When a compromise text emerges from trilogue, your members expect a clear summary of what changed on data protection, clawback, and paediatric incentives within hours. Brubru drafts it from the source text in minutes.
  • Building the amendment picture for MEPs Knowing which EP groups are aligned with EFPIA's position on regulatory data protection, and which MEPs to brief first, requires scanning hundreds of tabled amendments. Brubru's Best Allies ranking surfaces the right fifteen names instantly.
  • Monitoring the Council in parallel Council working party discussions on the pharma reform are harder to track than EP committee work. Brubru surfaces member state positions and signals as soon as they enter the public record.
  • Turning W.A.I.T. data into advocacy arguments The 532-day median and the country-level spread (Romania over 1,100 days, Italy under 450 days) are powerful evidence. Brubru keeps this evidence base at your fingertips and wires it into the documents it generates for you.
  • Producing documents in multiple working languages EFPIA communicates across six working languages. Brubru supports EN, FR, NL, ES, CA, and IT natively, so a position paper drafted in English can be adapted for a French or Dutch MEP briefing without leaving the platform.

Live priority files

Pharma Reform (lead file)
EU Pharmaceutical Legislation Revision

Regulation and Directive (procedures 2023/0131 and 2023/0132). Provisional trilogue agreement reached December 2025; formal adoption expected autumn 2026; application date approximately 2028.

EFPIA headline concerns: cut in regulatory data protection from 8 to 6 years; clawback mechanisms; incentive structures for medicines in areas of unmet need. Ask Brubru: "What did the December 2025 trilogue agree on data protection and clawbacks?"

HTA Regulation
Joint Clinical Assessments (Regulation 2021/2282)

The HTA Regulation is in application from January 2025 for oncology and ATMPs, expanding progressively. Joint Clinical Assessments are now producing binding scientific conclusions that national HTA bodies must take into account.

Brubru tracks every EUnetHTA 21 JCA output and maps it to the underlying procedure, so your team knows exactly which assessments are live and which are pending.

Compulsory Licensing
Crisis Compulsory Licensing Regulation (2023/0129)

Proposal for a regulation on compulsory licensing for crisis management. Trilogue ongoing as of mid-2026.

Brubru monitors every trilogue session record and committee vote, and can instantly produce a briefing on the current state of play including EFPIA's positions on the scope and safeguards.

Critical Medicines Act
Critical Medicines Act (provisional deal May 2026)

Provisional trilogue deal reached May 2026. The Act addresses supply chain resilience and strategic autonomy for critical medicines, with particular relevance for API sourcing and manufacturing diversification.

Ask Brubru: "What does the Critical Medicines Act provisional deal mean for pharmaceutical manufacturers?" to get a sourced summary immediately.

Innovation agenda
European Biotech Act

The European Biotech Act is an EFPIA-supported initiative to strengthen EU competitiveness in biopharmaceutical innovation. Brubru tracks the Commission's preparatory work and any related legislative vehicles in the 2024-2029 work programme.

Brubru surfaces relevant Commission communications, DG SANTE consultations, and European Parliament own-initiative reports as they are published.

Clinical trials
FAST-EU Pilot and Vaccines Europe

The FAST-EU pilot aims to accelerate clinical trial authorisation across member states. Vaccines Europe monitors specific regulatory and procurement aspects of the EU vaccine ecosystem.

Brubru tracks the pilot's rollout schedule, participating member states, and any related Commission delegated or implementing acts that affect trial sponsors.
