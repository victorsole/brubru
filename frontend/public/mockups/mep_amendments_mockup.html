AM 35 EPP Modification
Axel Voss, Jens Gieseke, Angelika Niebler
on behalf of the EPP Group
Article 3 -- paragraph 2
Text proposed by the Commission
2. The Agency shall carry out its tasks in relation to the registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals with the aim of ensuring a high level of protection of human health and the environment.
Amendment
2. The Agency shall carry out its tasks in relation to the registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals with the aim of ensuring a high level of protection of human health and the environment as well as the free movement of substances on the internal market while enhancing competitiveness and innovation.
Or. en Justification
Justification
The mandate of the Agency should explicitly include promoting the competitiveness of European industry and innovation, in line with the broader objectives of the European Green Deal and Industrial Strategy. A high level of protection should not come at the expense of European competitiveness.
AM 42 S&D Modification
Marina Kaljurand, Tiemo Woelken
on behalf of the S&D Group
Article 3 -- paragraph 2
Text proposed by the Commission
2. The Agency shall carry out its tasks in relation to the registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals with the aim of ensuring a high level of protection of human health and the environment.
Amendment
2. The Agency shall carry out its tasks in relation to the registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals with the aim of ensuring the highest possible level of protection of human health and the environment, in particular for vulnerable groups including children, pregnant women, and workers exposed to hazardous substances.
Or. en Justification
Justification
The level of ambition must match the scale of the chemicals crisis. Vulnerable populations face disproportionate exposure to harmful substances and deserve explicit mention in the Agency's core mandate to ensure targeted protection measures.
AM 48 Greens/EFA Addition
Jutta Paulus
Article 3 -- paragraph 2a (new)
Text proposed by the Commission
(new paragraph)
Amendment
2a. The Agency shall apply the precautionary principle when assessing risks posed by chemicals for which scientific data is incomplete or inconclusive. Where there are reasonable grounds for concern, the Agency shall recommend preventive action without waiting for full scientific certainty.
Or. en Justification
Justification
The precautionary principle is enshrined in Article 191(2) TFEU and must be made operational in the Agency's mandate. Past failures to act on early warnings -- such as with PFAS, asbestos, and endocrine disruptors -- demonstrate the need for explicit precautionary obligations.

AM 39 EPP Modification
Axel Voss
Article 7 -- paragraph 1
Text proposed by the Commission
1. The Agency shall provide the Member States and the institutions of the Union with the best possible scientific and technical advice on questions relating to chemicals.
Amendment
1. The Agency shall provide the Member States and the institutions of the Union with the best possible independent, scientific and technical advice on questions relating to chemicals, based on the most recent peer-reviewed scientific evidence.
Or. en Justification
Justification
The Agency's advice must be grounded in independent, peer-reviewed science to maintain credibility and avoid undue influence from any stakeholder group. This aligns with the European Food Safety Authority's (EFSA) mandate and ensures consistency across EU regulatory agencies.
