What is the EU Canon?

A reference library for anyone trying to understand what a specific EU law actually requires.

The EU produces hundreds of binding regulations and directives every year. Many of them are long, technically dense, and written for practitioners who already know the surrounding framework. The EU Canon exists to fix that. Each entry takes a single law and explains it in full: what it covers, who it applies to, what its key obligations are, and how it connects to the broader EU legal corpus.

This is not a news digest or a policy commentary. It is a reference resource. If you are a policy professional, a compliance officer, a researcher, or simply a curious citizen who landed on a specific regulation and wants to understand what it says, this library is for you. Currently it covers four laws. More canon entries land weekly or biweekly as the library grows.

The live legislative tracker, AI chat, and compliance gap analysis live inside the Brubru product at brubru.beresol.eu. The Canon library is the free, publicly accessible companion to that platform.

How each explainer is built

Primary sources, no shortcuts.

Primary sources

Each explainer is built from the Formex V4 XML of the law itself, fetched from EUR-Lex and the EU Publications Office. We read the act in full, not a press release or a summary of a summary.

Article-by-article

We walk through scope, definitions, key obligations, and operative parts article by article. No skimming. The full structure of the act is preserved in the explainer so you can navigate it as a reference document.

Plain language

Written for non-specialists. Legal terms get glossed on first use. Tables, ratios, and timelines replace paragraph walls wherever the structure of the law permits. The goal is comprehension, not simplification.

Family tree

Each law links upstream to the framework it implements and downstream to delegated acts, technical standards, and related case law. No EU law exists in isolation; the explainer shows where it sits in the wider corpus.
