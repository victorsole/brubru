Coverage at a glance

  • 28,505 adopted laws indexed and full-text searchable.
  • 2,080 legislative files tracked from Commission proposal to adoption.
  • 16,521 EU officials from 75 organigrammes (Commission DGs, EP services, Council Secretariat, Ombudsman, agencies).
  • 58,399 EP amendments, plus reports, resolutions, opinions, votes and parliamentary questions.
  • 528 Commission Register documents (COM/SWD/JOIN/C-series) with EUR-Lex CELEX-derived PDF URLs.
  • 210 research publications across EPRS, STOA, JRC, ART and ECA.
  • 386 tenders from TED.
  • 206 curated knowledge guides covering every major EU law in plain English.
  • 8 calendar sources aggregated into /calendar/events (EP, Council, EC College, agencies, euagenda, plus 3 more).

What we don't yet have

  • EU Official Journal full-text feed. We have OJ references per law but not the OJ as a daily-bulletin stream. On roadmap.
  • National parliaments. We don't track Member State legislatures. Pair us with GovClipping (Spain), NordLex (Nordics), etc.
  • Multilingual structured responses. Some law texts are available in all 24 EU languages via the Cellar fallback but the JSON envelopes are English-only. September 2026 roadmap.
  • Webhooks / push notifications. Polling on our refresh cadence is the integration pattern today. Webhooks September 2026.

Ingestion pipeline

Behind the scenes, three tiers:

  1. Structured APIs (15 sources) — EUR-Lex SPARQL, OEIL XML, EP Open Data v2, data.europa.eu CKAN, ECB SDMX, etc. Hand-crafted clients.
  2. RSS / Atom feeds (60+ sources) — generic YAML-configured ingester. Adding a new source is a config file, not code.
  3. HTML scraping (select sources) — Scrapling + BeautifulSoup with auto-selector relocation. For sites without APIs or feeds.

Full discovery process is documented in data/europa_eu/europa.eu_all_sources.md (519 validated europa.eu domains inventoried). Our roadmap takes this from ~55 actively-ingested to ~200 by end of 2026.
